# The Pact Primeval: The Blueprint of the Iron Spindle

In the wake of the **Shattering of the Loom**, the multiverse exists in a state of precarious, "fluid" instability—a condition the **Nine Hells** views as a catastrophic "Legacy Bug" in the fabric of existence. While the **Pale Lodge** seeks to mend the original Loom and the **Abyss** seeks to unravel it entirely, **Asmodeus** has proposed a third path: **The Pact Primeval**.

The Pact Primeval is not merely a legal document or a collection of infernal laws; it is the **Master Blueprint** for a "New Loom"—the **Iron Spindle** (also known as the Infernal Spindle). It is the foundational contract that defines the transition from Telara's "Fluid Balance" to Asmodeus's "Absolute Law."

---

## 1. The Philosophy of the Rigid Weave
Asmodeus’s core thesis is that the original Loom failed because it allowed for "slack"—the unpredictability of free will and the "wobble" of infinite potential. He believes that the **Shattering** was the inevitable result of a design that was too flexible to withstand the entropy of the **Great Dreamer**.

The Pact Primeval outlines a reality where:
*   **Order is Absolute**: Every thread is locked into a fixed, unchanging position.
*   **Predictability is Total**: Probability is replaced by Mandate.
*   **Stasis is Safety**: By "freezing" reality into a state of absolute rigidity, the Abyss can no longer find purchase to pull at loose threads.

---

## 2. The Soul-Thread Mechanism
In the Shattered Loom, souls are the literal raw material of reality. The Pact Primeval establishes the legal and metaphysical framework for the harvest of **Soul-Threads**.

*   **The Signature as a Vote**: When a mortal signs an infernal contract, they are not just bartering their afterlife; they are "voting" for Asmodeus’s version of reality. Each signature provides a "Code-Update" to the local weave, slowly overwriting Telara’s original design with the logic of the Iron Spindle.
*   **Hardening the Thread**: A soul claimed by the Hells is "reformatted." Its "slack" (chaotic potential) is smelted away in the foundries of **Phlegethos**, turning it into a "Standardized Thread" of infernal iron, ready to be woven into the new Spindle.
*   **The Accumulation of Weight**: As more souls are bound to the Pact, the "Gravitational Law" of the Hells increases, allowing the Iron Spindle in **Nessus** to exert more control over the surrounding **Fractured Nexus**.

---

## 3. The Pacts of Stabilization
The primary method of implementing the Pact Primeval across the planes is through **Pacts of Stabilization**. As Material Plane shards face the "Great Unraveling" (Abyssal entropy), devils arrive as "Technicians of Order."

They offer a desperate bargain:
> *"We will stitch your world back together. We will stop the crumbling of your mountains and the fraying of your skies. In exchange, you will grant us the 'Future-Threads' of your civilization."*

By accepting, a world is saved from the Abyss but is "Hard-Locked" into the Hells' hierarchy. Its inhabitants become cogs in the Infernal Machine, their destinies permanently fixed by the mandates of the Pact Primeval.

---

## 4. The Architecture of the Spindle
The Pact Primeval is physically manifested in two forms:
1.  **The Etched Skin**: The full text of the Pact is etched into the very skin of **Asmodeus**. He is the living embodiment of the contract; as long as he exists, the blueprint remains "active."
2.  **The Infernal Spindle**: Located in the depths of **Nessus**, this massive, jagged artifact acts as a counterfeit Loom. It broadcasts the "Signal of Law," a harmonic frequency that attempts to force all nearby threads into perfect alignment.

---

## 5. The Ethical Conflict: Peace vs. Grave
The Pact Primeval presents a terrifyingly seductive argument: **Safety through Tyranny**.

*   **The Infernal View**: The multiverse is dying. The Loom is broken. The only way to survive the "Great Dreamer" is to build a cage so strong that even a god of entropy cannot break the bars. A "Frozen Reality" is better than non-existence.
*   **The Pale Lodge’s View**: The Pact Primeval is a "Planar Cancer." It seeks to solve the problem of death by killing everything and preserving the corpses in stasis. To the Lodge, the "Fluidity" of the Loom is what makes life worth living.
*   **The Weaver’s Perspective (Telara)**: If any echoes of Telara remain, they view the Pact as a betrayal of her "Thread of Infinite Potential." The Pact Primeval is the ultimate "Binding"—the final end of all new stories.

---

## 6. Known "Codexes" of the Pact
The Pact is subdivided into several "Codexes," each managed by an Archdevil:
*   **The Codex of Displacement (Dispater)**: Rules for the realignment of physical geometry.
*   **The Codex of Valuation (Mammon)**: Rules for the conversion of abstract destiny into material wealth.
*   **The Codex of Stasis (Levistus)**: Rules for the preservation of "Legacy Data" from the Unbroken Tapestry.
*   **The Codex of Culling (Glasya)**: Rules for the "Pruning" of threads that cannot be standardized.
