## 🧭 Planar Navigation & Shipboard Physics

Every Spelljammer in the **Project Shattered Loom** multiverse operates upon distinct physical and magical axioms governed by planar wake physics:

### 1. The Gravity Plane
Every spelljammer vessel projects a flat, invisible plane of selective gravity that slices horizontally through the center of the ship, usually level with the main deck. 
- Anything above the gravity plane falls **down** toward the deck.
- Anything below the gravity plane falls **up** toward the underbelly of the hull.
- Passing through the gravity plane results in a momentarily disorienting shift in subjective down, requiring a **DC 10 Dexterity (Acrobatics)** check for unfamiliar crew or boarders to avoid tumbling prone.

### 2. The Atmospheric Envelope
When a spelljammer leaves an atmosphere or planar rift, it carries an envelope of breathable air that clings to its hull in an egg-shaped field proportional to the ship's tonnage.
* **Fresh Air**: Provides clean breathing for a standard crew for roughly **120 days**.
* **Foul Air**: When air reaches 50% capacity, all creatures aboard suffer disadvantage on Constitution checks and saving throws.
* **Deadly Air**: Exhausted atmosphere causes suffocating rules after 1d4 hours unless renewed by planar plants, air crystals, or returning to an atmospheric body.

### 3. Planetary & Astral Propulsion
* **Tactical Speed**: In local combat and planetary atmospheres, ships move at tactical speeds (30–90 ft. per round) driven by their specialized propulsion fins, sails, or thrusters.
* **Planar Slipstream Speed**: In the frictionless currents of the Astral Sea or deep Ether, the ship's helm bends the fractured threads of the Loom, propelling the vessel at thousands of leagues per day without physical acceleration felt by those inside the air bubble.

## ⚙️ 4. The Starbound Colossus (The Clockwork Titan-Leviathan)

*“A flying fortress of burnished starmetal and clockwork gears, plowing through astral tides with the elemental fury of creation roaring in its belly.”*

![[starbound_colossus_deckplan.jpg]]

*Figure 4.1: The Starbound Colossus — Exterior dreadnought war-galleon profile and architectural cross-section of all five clockwork deck levels.*

The **Starbound Colossus** is an awe-inspiring, dreadnought-class spelljammer war-galleon designed by planar artificers and clockwork guilds. Built as a colossal multi-deck astral battle-galleon forged from interlocking plates of burnished starmetal, brass piping, and adamantine gears, its prow is crowned by a monumental heroic knight figurehead that functions as a structural ram. Instead of mundane canvas sails, the Colossus spreads articulated celestial solar vanes and gear-driven rudders along three towering brass masts that capture planar gravitational currents. At its heart spins the **Tetrarch Engine**, a catastrophic gyro-furnace capable of housing four different primordial elemental essences.

### Tactical & Vessel Profile
* **Size**: Colossal Spelljammer War-Galleon (200 ft. long, 80 ft. beam, 110 ft. height including sterncastle and masts)
* **Armor Class**: 22 (Axiomatic Starmetal Carapace)
* **Hit Points**: 480 (Damage Threshold 25)
* **Tactical Speed**: Flying 40 ft. (Turn 45°)
* **Planar Slipstream Speed**: 80,000 miles per day
* **Air Envelope**: 150 days for a crew of 40 (Ventilated by giant brass bellows and pneumatic air-recyclers)
* **Crew**: 15 minimum, 35 standard, 60 maximum (often supplemented by clockwork automatons)
* **Armament**:
  * **2× Heavy Siege Thunder-Cannons** (Forecastle and sterncastle swivel mounts; +8 to hit, range 300/1,200 ft. *Hit*: 44 (8d10) thunder and bludgeoning damage. Double damage to structures).
  * **4× Broadside Arcane Lightning Ballistas** (Two per flank; +7 to hit, range 150/600 ft. *Hit*: 22 (4d8 + 4) piercing plus 14 (3d8) lightning damage).
  * **1× Prow Knight-Ram** (Adamantine ram formed by the figurehead's outstretched blade; +9 to hit, *Hit*: 55 (10d10) bludgeoning damage and pushes the target ship 40 ft.).
* **Fuel Source: Primordial Cores**
  * Condensed spherical cores harvested from elemental lords or natural planar disasters. The Colossus can socket up to four cores simultaneously:
    * **Air Core**: Tactical speed increases to 70 ft.; can hover in place.
    * **Fire Core**: Weapons deal an extra 2d8 fire damage; adds thermal shield.
    * **Water Core**: Ship can submerge into planar seas; hull self-cleans and resists corrosion.
    * **Earth Core**: Damage threshold increases to 30; immune to forced movement.
  * **Fuel Perk**: *Elemental Overcharge*. The pilot can vent one core's total power to unleash an elemental catastrophe (e.g. 60-ft. cone of 10d10 fire, lightning, or bludgeoning damage).
  * **Drawback**: *Core Destabilization*. Attempting to run opposing cores (Fire + Water or Earth + Air) without an attuned Master Elementalist requires a **DC 17 Arcana check** every 24 hours. Failure causes a Core Meltdown dealing 10d10 force damage to the engine bay.
* **Required Attunement**: **Master Elementalist or Artificer**.

## 💀 5. The Whispering Wraith (The Ossuary Void-Stalker)

![[whispering_wraith_deckplan.jpg]]

*Figure 5.1: The Whispering Wraith — Exterior skeletal void profile and architectural cutaway view of all five ossuary deck tiers.*

### Tactical & Vessel Profile
* **Size**: Huge / Gargantuan Spelljammer (125 ft. long, 40 ft. beam, 50 ft. height)
* **Armor Class**: 18 (Shadow-Glass & Void-Hardened Bone)
* **Hit Points**: 310 (Damage Threshold 15)
* **Tactical Speed**: Flying 65 ft. (Turn 90°)
* **Planar Slipstream Speed**: 110,000 miles per day (Silent Running)
* **Air Envelope**: 90 days for a crew of 15 (Air is unnaturally cold, crisp, and smells of dry stone and grave dust)
* **Crew**: 5 minimum, 12 standard, 20 maximum
* **Armament**:
  * **2× Soul-Leech Projectors** (Prow-mounted; +8 to hit, range 180/720 ft. *Hit*: 28 (5d10) necrotic damage, and the target ship's pilot must make a DC 15 Wisdom saving throw or take 1 level of mental exhaustion).

  * Harvested from the shores of the River Styx, the Shadowfell's Gloom-Sinks, or astral drift-shores. A single urn of soul-vapors fuels the ship for 10 days.

  * **Drawback**: *Mutiny of the Restless Dead*. If the souls are mistreated or fuel runs out, the spirits revolt. Every creature on board must make a **DC 15 Wisdom saving throw (or Lucidity Check)** or suffer psychic trauma, hallucinations, and attacks from 1d4 **Specters** erupting from the engine vents.
* **Required Attunement**: **Soulbinder** (A Necromancer Wizard, Death/Grave Cleric, or Shadow Psion).

* **Room 2D: The Pale Ledger Room & Spool Archives**
  * *Visual & Atmosphere*: Stacks of vellum ledgers bound in black leather documenting names, dates of death, and debts of souls purchased across the multiverse.
