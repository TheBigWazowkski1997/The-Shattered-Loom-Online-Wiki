### **The Librarian: Keeper of the Infinite Archive**

The **Librarian** is an eldritch entity born from the multiverse's collective knowledge and the shattering of the Loom. It is the architect and sole master of the **Grand Archive**, existing as a neutral observer of all that was, is, and will be.

---

## **Lore & Origins**

### **The Shadow of Telara**
A persistent legend among the **Pale Lodge** suggests that when **Telara** (the Loom's creator) wove the first thread, she realized that to create, one must also observe. She supposedly cast off her own "Inquisitive Shadow"—her pure desire to know and record—which coalesced into the Librarian. If true, the Librarian is literally the discarded curiosity of a goddess, bound to watch the Loom but unable to mend it.

### **The Pact of the Silver Thread**
The Librarian maintains a symbiotic relationship with the **Pale Lodge**. While the Librarian provides the "Blueprints of Reality" (records of the Loom's original state), the Lodge acts as its field agents, recovering lost knowledge and defending the Archive from those who would erase history.

---

## **Nature & Appearance**

### **The Ever-Shifting Form**
The Librarian possesses no single true body. It manifests as a **living paradox of information density**, cycling through three primary aspects depending on the nature of the knowledge being accessed—or the mood of the entity:

* **The Ever-Expanding Tome (Default Manifestation)**: A colossal, floating grimoire easily thirty feet tall, bound in covers of pale, living vellum that feels like skin warmed by a fever. Its pages turn of their own accord, each leaf inscribed with text that rewrites itself in real-time—languages that predate the Loom, mathematical proofs of futures that never happened, and the true names of dead gods. The spine is a rod of solidified starlight, and the book "breathes," exhaling motes of silver dust that taste of ozone and old paper. When it speaks in this form, the sound emanates from the text itself, syllables forming and dissolving in the air like ink in water.

* **The Living Constellation (Cosmic Manifestation)**: The Librarian unfolds into a three-dimensional star map suspended in the Archive's central atrium. Its "body" is a lattice of golden filaments connecting thousands of luminous nodes—each node a memory, a secret, or a possible future. It appears as a vaguely humanoid silhouette woven from starlight and shadow, with joints marked by pulsating quasars. When it moves, constellations rearrange themselves; its gestures rewrite the night sky of a thousand worlds simultaneously. In this form, it does not walk—it *edits* its position into reality.

* **The Loom of Eyes (Judgment Manifestation)**: A vertical architecture of interlocking rings, each studded with millions of irises of every conceivable color and shape—human, draconic, insectoid, and geometries that hurt to perceive. The rings rotate in counter-spin, creating a constant soft *chime* like crystal struck by light. There is no mouth; the Loom of Eyes speaks by *selecting* which eyes open, the pattern of dilation and contraction forming a visual language older than speech. To stand before it is to feel the weight of a billion gazes, each one reading a different chapter of your soul.

### **Sensory Signature**
Regardless of form, the Librarian carries a distinct sensory profile:
*   **Scent**: Vellum, ozone, petrichor (rain on dry stone), and the metallic tang of blood—specifically, the blood of those who died keeping secrets.
*   **Sound**: A sub-audible harmonic hum, like a violin bow drawn across a taut thread of fate. When it focuses, the hum resolves into whispers in languages the listener *almost* understands.
*   **Temperature**: The air around it drops precisely 7 degrees Fahrenheit, and written text within 60 feet begins to blur and rearrange into more "accurate" versions of itself.

### **Multi-Temporal Existence**
The Librarian perceives time simultaneously across multiple eras. It may answer questions you haven't asked yet or refer to you by a title you haven't earned.

### **The Collector of Silence**
It values things that *cannot* be spoken, often trading information for the "Silence" of an event, effectively erasing its vocal memory from the multiverse.

## **Personality & Quirks**

* **Obsessive Curator**: Will not tolerate the destruction or theft of knowledge.
* **Aloof and Alien**: Operates on a cosmic scale; lacks mortal empathy.
* **The Tithe of Secrets**: Requires a "Tithe" (a personal memory or a unique truth) for interaction.

### **Traits**

#### **Archivist’s Omniscience**
The Librarian automatically succeeds on all Intelligence checks related to identifying or understanding artifacts, spells, or creatures. It also knows any creature’s alignment, current emotional state, and intentions within 120 feet.

#### **Manifest Secrets (Aura)**
Creatures within 60 feet of the Librarian must succeed on a Wisdom saving throw at the start of their turn or become overwhelmed by revelations of their own forgotten memories or hidden truths. A creature that fails is **stunned** for 1 round.

#### **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**
If the Librarian fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

---

### **Actions**

#### **Multiattack**
The Librarian makes three attacks: two with **Threads of Knowledge** and one with **Mind Rend**.

#### **Threads of Knowledge (Melee Spell Attack)**
_Range 30 ft., one target._
* **Hit**: 28 (4d10 + 9) force damage, and the target must make a Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, the target cannot cast spells until the end of its next turn.

#### **Mind Rend (Recharge 5–6)**
_Range 120 ft., one target._ The Librarian attacks the mind of a creature, forcing it to confront an unbearable truth. The target must make a Intelligence saving throw or take 72 (16d8) psychic damage and become paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns.

---

### **Legendary Actions**
The Librarian can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below.

#### **Reconfigure Archive (Costs 1 Action)**
The Librarian shifts the space around a creature within the Grand Archive. The target must make a Dexterity saving throw or be teleported to another part of the Archive (chosen by the Librarian).

#### **Summon Spectral Guardian (Costs 2 Actions)**
The Librarian summons a **Spectral Guardian** (CR 5), which lasts for 1 minute or until destroyed.

#### **Unveil Knowledge (Costs 3 Actions)**
The Librarian forces all creatures within 60 feet to make a Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, a creature becomes **frightened** for 1 minute, seeing visions of truths they cannot comprehend.
