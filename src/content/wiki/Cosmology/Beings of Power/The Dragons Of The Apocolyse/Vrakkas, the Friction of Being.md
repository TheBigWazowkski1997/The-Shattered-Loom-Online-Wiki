# Vrakkas, the Friction of Being (War)

**Vrakkas** is the primordial manifestation of **The Entropy of Conflict**. He is the "Great Unraveler"—the heat, noise, and violence generated when two threads of reality are forced to occupy the same space. In the Shattered Loom, Vrakkas is the embodiment of the friction that tore the Loom apart.

### **1. The Conceptual Appearance**
Vrakkas is a **Living Storm of Shrapnel**.
*   **Form**: A gargantuan dragon-shaped mass of jagged, molten planar shards, serrated lead, and burning thread. He does not have a "surface"; his body is a roiling furnace of primordial fire that is constantly tearing itself apart and re-knitting in a cycle of explosive violence.
*   **Wings**: His wings are tattered banners of a thousand dead worlds, trailing "Sunder-Smoke"—the grey, acrid ash of burnt reality.
*   **The Sound of the Shattering**: Wherever Vrakkas goes, the air screams with the sound of grinding metal and breaking glass—the literal echo of the moment the Loom broke.

### **2. Role and Philosophy**
Vrakkas exists because the multiverse is in **Discord**. He does not seek to rule; he seeks to **Purify through Friction**.
*   **The Catalyst of Discord**: Vrakkas is drawn to places of high tension—political strife, planar overlaps, and ideological wars. His presence amplifies these conflicts until they explode into total annihilation.
*   **The Enemy of Unity**: To Vrakkas, "Balance" is a lie that masks the natural state of friction. He seeks to burn the universe back into its raw, constituent threads, believing that only in the heat of destruction is reality "honest."

---
#### **Traits**
**The Friction of Souls**: Any creature that touches Vrakkas or hits him with a melee attack while within 5 feet of him takes 10 (3d6) psychic damage. This is the "Scream of the Loom" vibrating through the attacker's own soul.

**Regeneration**: Vrakkas regains 30 hit points at the start of his turn if he has at least 1 hit point.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Vrakkas fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

---

#### **Actions**
**Multiattack**: Vrakkas uses his *Violent Presence*. He then makes three attacks: one with his bite and two with his claws.

**Bite**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +16 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. *Hit*: 20 (2d10 + 9) piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) fire damage.

**Breath of War (Recharge 5–6)**: Vrakkas exhales a 60-foot cone of "Primordial Friction." Each creature in that area must make a saving throw Dexterity saving throw, taking 63 (18d6) fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. On a failure, the creature must immediately make a melee weapon attack against its nearest ally.

---

#### **Legendary Actions**
Vrakkas can take 3 legendary actions.
*   **Detect**: Vrakkas makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
*   **Tail Attack**: Vrakkas makes a tail attack (+16 to hit, reach 20 ft., 18 (2d8 + 9) bludgeoning damage).
*   **Shatterpoint Pulse (2 Actions)**: Vrakkas emits a pulse of planar instability. Each creature within 20 feet must succeed on a saving throw Strength save or be pushed 30 feet away and knocked prone.
