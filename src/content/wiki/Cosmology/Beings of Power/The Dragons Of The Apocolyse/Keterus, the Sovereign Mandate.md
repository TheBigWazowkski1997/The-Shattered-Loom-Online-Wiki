# Keterus, the Sovereign Mandate (Conquest)

**Keterus** is the primordial manifestation of **Absolute Narrative Control**. In the Shattered Loom, he represents the "Tyranny of the Single Thread"—the cosmic force that seeks to erase all divergent paths and force every soul into one predetermined, unyielding destiny. He is the ultimate "Law" taken to a horrific, stagnant extreme.

### **1. The Conceptual Appearance**
Keterus is not a creature of flesh and blood, but a **Living Singularity of Logic**.
*   **Form**: A gargantuan dragon composed of interlocking, diamond-shaped shards of the Loom itself. These "scales" do not reflect the environment; instead, they show "The One True Future"—a vision of a perfectly ordered, silent multiverse.
*   **Wings**: His wings are massive, unmoving planes of golden geometric force. They do not flap; they "overwrite" the space they occupy, forcing reality to conform to his presence.
*   **The Crown of Mandates**: Floating above his head are halos of burning Draconic script—laws of physics that he enforces simply by existing.
*   **Sound**: His voice is not a sound, but a "Correction." When he speaks, it feels as though the listener's own thoughts are being edited in real-time.

### **2. Role and Philosophy**
Keterus serves the cold logic of the **Silent Spindle**. He believes that the Shattering was a failure of "choice" and that the only way to save reality is to remove choice entirely.
*   **The Enforcer of Stasis**: He hunts those who create "New Threads"—heroes, explorers, and agents of chaos. To Keterus, a new destiny is a "glitch" in the weave that must be smoothed over.
*   **The Sovereign Will**: He does not conquer lands; he conquers **possibility**. Where Keterus walks, gravity becomes constant, time becomes linear, and the "Thread of Potential" in all living things is pulled taut until it can no longer move.

---
#### **Traits**
**The Inevitable Path**: Keterus ignores resistance to psychic damage and fear. Any creature frightened by Keterus has its speed reduced to 0, as their destiny is "locked" in place.

**Narrative Weight**: Any creature that starts its turn within 120 feet of Keterus must succeed on a saving throw Charisma saving throw or take 10 (3d6) psychic damage as their personal timeline is compressed by his presence.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Keterus fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

---

#### **Actions**
**Multiattack**: Keterus uses his *Conquering Presence*. He then makes three attacks: one with his bite and two with his claws.

**Bite**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +12 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 17 (2d10 + 6) piercing damage plus 4 (1d8) cold damage. The target is knocked prone if it is afraid of Keterus.

**Breath of the Conqueror (Recharge 5–6)**: Keterus exhales a blast of "Frozen Logic" in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw Constitution saving throw, taking 54 (12d8) cold damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one. On a failure, the creature has disadvantage on its next saving throw against being frightened.

---

#### **Legendary Actions**
Keterus can take 3 legendary actions.
*   **Detect**: Keterus makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
*   **Tail Attack**: Keterus makes a tail attack (+12 to hit, reach 15 ft., 15 (2d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage).
*   **Command Reality (2 Actions)**: Keterus targets one creature he can see. The target must succeed on a saving throw Charisma save or be forced to use its reaction to move its full speed in a direction of Keterus's choosing.
