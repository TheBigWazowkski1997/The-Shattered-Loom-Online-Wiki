# Mortha-Vahl, the Blight of Meaning (Pestilence)

**Mortha-Vahl** is the primordial manifestation of **The Corruption of Information**. He is the "Frayed Edge"—the metaphysical rot that occurs when a thread of reality is infected by the **Great Dreamer’s** nonsensical logic. He is the cancer of the weave, turning meaning into madness and substance into shadow.

### **1. The Conceptual Appearance**
Mortha-Vahl is a **Hollow Shroud**.
*   **Form**: He is a dragon-shaped swarm of "Loom-Moths" and grey, mildewed threads. He has no solid heart; instead, his chest cavity is a localized "Void-Pocket" that leaks "Anti-Information"—a grey, ink-like smoke that erases the color and detail of anything it touches.
*   **Wings**: His wings are tattered, mildewed tapestries that pulse with the rhythm of a dying heart. When they flap, they release a dust of "Forgotten Names."
*   **Sound**: His "voice" is a cacophony of overlapping whispers—every lie ever told and every promise ever broken, played at once.

### **2. Role and Philosophy**
Mortha-Vahl is a direct manifestation of the **Great Dreamer’s** influence. He seeks to "un-weave" the universe into a state of pure, meaningless potential.
*   **The Consumer of Meaning**: Mortha-Vahl hunts "Planar Knots" and Loom Fragments, not to use them, but to **defile** them. He turns the "logic" of a plane into a "dream," making gravity optional, time cyclical, and memory unreliable.
*   **The Herald of the Void**: To Mortha-Vahl, the Loom was a "Cage of Definition." He believes that "Pestilence" (the breakdown of order) is the first step toward the ultimate freedom of the Great Dreamer’s embrace.

---
#### **Traits**
**Plague of Information**: Mortha-Vahl ignores resistance to the poisoned condition and treats immunity to poison as resistance.

**Rotting Agony**: Creatures within 120 feet of Mortha-Vahl that are poisoned suffer disadvantage on all saving throws.

**Undead Fortitude**: If damage reduces Mortha-Vahl to 0 hit points, he must make a Constitution saving throw with a DC of 5 + the damage taken, unless the damage is radiant or from a critical hit. On a success, he drops to 1 hit point instead.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Mortha-Vahl fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

---

#### **Actions**
**Multiattack**: Mortha-Vahl uses his *Toxic Miasma*. He then makes three attacks: one with his bite and two with his claws.

**Bite**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +13 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 17 (2d10 + 7) piercing damage plus 13 (3d8) acid damage.

**Pestilent Breath (Recharge 5–6)**: Mortha-Vahl exhales "The Fog of Unraveling" in a 60-foot line that is 10 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a saving throw Constitution saving throw, taking 27 (6d8) poison damage and 27 (6d8) acid damage on a failed save and the creature is poisoned and reeling. On a success, it takes half damage and is not poisoned.

---

#### **Legendary Actions**
Mortha-Vahl can take 3 legendary actions.
*   **Detect**: Mortha-Vahl makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
*   **Tail Attack**: Mortha-Vahl makes a tail attack (+13 to hit, reach 15 ft., 16 (2d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage).
*   **Cast a Spell (2 Actions)**: Mortha-Vahl casts *Cloudkill* or *Contagion* (no material components).
