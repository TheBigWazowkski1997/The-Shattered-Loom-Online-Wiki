# The Loom Warden: The Silent Singularity

**The Loom Warden** is not a person, a god, or a ghost. It is a **Cosmic Constant**—a living scar in the fabric of the multiverse that remains from the moment Telara’s Loom was shattered. It serves as the impartial, eldritch custodian of the **Fractured Nexus**, ensuring that while the multiverse is broken, it does not completely unravel into the void.

### **1. The Eldritch Appearance**
To look upon the Loom Warden is to invite a migraine of cosmic proportions. It does not possess a "body" in the traditional sense. Instead, it manifests as:

*   **The Hollow Loom**: A vertical tear in reality, thirty feet tall, that bleeds golden light and obsidian smoke.
*   **The Crown of Spindles**: Hovering around this tear are twelve massive, obsidian spindles that spin at impossible speeds. They are connected to the central rift by thousands of humming, vibrating filaments of pure probability.
*   **The Thousand-Fold Gaze**: There are no eyes, yet the Warden "sees" through the vibration of every thread in the Nexus. When it focuses on a creature, the golden filaments in the air tighten, and the creature feels the weight of their own destiny pressing against their skin.
*   **Sound**: It does not speak. It communicates through **The Harmonic Shift**—a change in the background hum of the Nexus that sounds like a choir singing in reverse, accompanied by the smell of ozone and wet stone.

### **2. Role and Philosophy**
The Loom Warden is strictly **Neutral**. It does not care for good, evil, law, or chaos. It only cares for the **Integrity of the Weave**.

*   **The Great Balancer**: If a plane is growing too stable and threatens to "heal" the Loom prematurely (which could cause a cosmic collapse), the Warden will send its Riftborn to fray its edges. If a plane is collapsing too fast, it will stitch it back together.
*   **The Gatekeeper**: It allows travelers to use the Nexus because their movement creates "New Threads," which strengthen the overall tapestry of existence. However, it will ruthlessly "prune" any individual who threatens the Nexus itself.
*   **The Relationship with Telara**: It is often debated whether the Warden is a fragment of Telara’s shadow, a safeguard she left behind, or the very force that betrayed her. The Warden offers no answers.

#### **Traits**
**Aura of the Unraveling (120 ft.)**: Any creature that starts its turn within 120 feet of the Warden must succeed on a saving throw Wisdom saving throw or take 21 (6d6) psychic damage and be **Confused** (as the spell) until the start of their next turn as their personal timeline begins to blur.

**Reactive Spindles**: The Loom Warden has twelve spindles. Each time it takes more than 50 damage in a single turn, one spindle shatters, dealing 35 (10d6) force damage to all creatures within 30 feet. When all spindles are gone, the Warden’s AC drops to 20, but it gains an additional Action on each of its turns.

**Singularity Core**: The Warden cannot be moved, banished, or teleported against its will. Any spell of 6th level or lower cast within 60 feet of the Warden has a 50% chance of being absorbed, granting the Warden 20 temporary hit points.

**Legendary Resistance (5/Day)**: If the Warden fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

---

#### **Actions**
**Multiattack**: The Warden uses its *Harmonic Resonance* and makes four *Spindle Lash* attacks.

**Spindle Lash**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +18 to hit, reach 60 ft., one target. *Hit*: 32 (4d10 + 10) force damage. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a saving throw Strength saving throw or be pulled 30 feet toward the Warden's central rift.

**Harmonic Resonance (Recharge 5–6)**: The Warden emits a pulse of pure probability. Every creature within 90 feet must make a saving throw Charisma saving throw. On a failure, the creature takes 77 (22d6) radiant damage and is **Banished** to a pocket dimension of white light until the end of the Warden's next turn. On a success, they take half damage and are not banished.

**Sever the Frayed Thread (1/Day)**: The Warden chooses one creature it can see. The creature must make a saving throw Constitution saving throw. If it fails, its maximum hit points are reduced by 100. If this reduces the creature's maximum HP to 0, it is instantly erased from history. Only a *Wish* spell can restore a creature erased this way.

---

#### **Legendary Actions**
The Warden can take 3 legendary actions, choosing from the options below.

*   **Thread-Twist (1 Action)**: The Warden targets one creature. The target must succeed on a saving throw Charisma save or be swapped in space with another creature the Warden can see within 120 feet.
*   **Resonate Spindle (2 Actions)**: One of the Warden’s spindles emits a beam of energy. *Ranged Spell Attack*: +19 to hit, range 300 ft., one target. *Hit*: 45 (10d8) force damage.
*   **Rewind (3 Actions)**: The Warden "undoes" the last action taken by a creature it can see. The creature regains any hit points lost during that action, any spent spell slots or abilities are restored, and it is moved back to its previous position. The Warden then regains 50 hit points.
