## **1. The Conceptual Appearance**

The Great Dreamer is a **Walking Paradox of Slumber**—a entity that exists in the space between the dreamer and the dream. It does not occupy space so much as it *implies* space, a localized collapse of the boundary between the Material Plane and the Realm of Dreams.

*   **Form: The Somnolent Colossus**: A vast, recumbent figure of indeterminate biology, easily 120 feet in length when fully extended. Its "skin" is not flesh but a **tapestry of sleeping minds**—countless faces, human and inhuman, pressed against the surface from within, their expressions shifting through the spectrum of dream-emotions: ecstasy, terror, boredom, the strange logic of nightmare. The faces are not attached; they *are* the surface, rising and falling like bubbles in tar. When the Dreamer moves, the faces slide across one another in wet, silent waves.
*   **The Crown of Unspooled Thought**: Instead of a head, the Dreamer's "anterior" blooms into a halo of **floating neural architecture**—dendrites and axons made of solidified moonlight, connecting to nothing and everything. Memories, fears, and fantasies drift through this crown like jellyfish in a dark ocean, occasionally surfacing as fully formed illusions that play out around the entity before dissolving back into potentiality.
*   **Wings: The Veils of Unmaking**: Four great membranes unfurl from its back, each woven from a different category of dream:
    *   *The Veil of Desire* (pale rose, smelling of jasmine and copper)
    *   *The Veil of Terror* (bruised violet, cold as grave-earth)
    *   *The Veil of Nostalgia* (sepia-gold, warm and sticky as honey)
    *   *The Veil of the Prophetic* (mirror-silver, reflecting futures that hurt to witness)
    These wings do not beat; they *undulate*, rewriting the local laws of physics with each ripple. Gravity becomes subjective. Time flows backward in eddies. Colors trade places.
*   **Eyes: The Thousand Lids**: The Dreamer has no fixed eyes. Instead, **eyelids** open and close across its entire form—on its flanks, its wings, the crown, even the empty air around it. Each lid reveals a different pupil: a human eye, a goat's horizontal slit, a compound insect facet, a camera aperture, a geometric impossibility. When the Dreamer "looks" at you, a dozen lids open simultaneously, and you experience your own memories from the perspective of everyone else who was there.

### **Sensory Signature**
*   **Scent**: Varies by proximity and the Dreamer's current dream-cycle. Common notes: dried lavender, burning hair, wet copper, ozone before a storm, the specific perfume of your childhood home, the smell of a hospital corridor at 3 AM.
*   **Sound**: A **polyphonic lullaby**—thousands of voices singing different melodies in different languages, somehow harmonizing. Underneath: the wet sound of a brain folding itself. When the Dreamer speaks, it doesn't use its "mouth"; the lullaby *arranges itself* into words inside your skull.
*   **Touch**: The air within 60 feet becomes **viscous with narrative weight**. Moving feels like swimming through honey. Thoughts take on physical texture—sharp ideas cut, heavy regrets drag, fleeting joys float upward like bubbles.

### **The Sleeping Posture**
The Great Dreamer is almost always encountered in a state of **recumbent torpor**, curled upon a "bed" of compressed possibility (resembling a mountain range of pale, soft stone). It does not stand, walk, or fly in the conventional sense—it *dreams itself elsewhere*. When it relocates, its form simply *dissolves into narrative smoke* and *condenses* at the destination, the transition taking exactly the time it takes to forget a dream upon waking.

### **Traits**

#### **Dreamer’s Presence (Aura)**

Creatures within 60 feet of the Great Dreamer must make a Wisdom saving throw at the start of their turn or take 5d10 psychic damage and become **stunned** until the end of their next turn.

---

#### **Warp Reality**

The Great Dreamer can reshape its immediate surroundings. As a bonus action, it creates one of the following effects within a 120-foot radius:

* **Gravity Reversal**: All creatures must succeed on a Dexterity saving throw or fall upward 30 feet, taking 3d6 bludgeoning damage if they collide with the ceiling.
* **Shifting Terrain**: The ground becomes difficult terrain, and creatures have disadvantage on Dexterity saving throws.

---

#### **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**

If the Great Dreamer fails a saving throw, it can choose to succeed instead.

---

### **Actions**

#### **Multiattack**

The Great Dreamer makes two **Tendrils of Madness** attacks and uses **Psychic Burst** if available.

#### **Tendrils of Madness (Melee Weapon Attack)**

_Range 30 ft., one target._

* **Hit**: 25 (4d8 + 7) psychic damage, and the target must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or become **confused** for 1 minute.

#### **Psychic Burst (Recharge 5–6)**

_120-foot radius._

* All creatures in range must make a Intelligence saving throw, taking 60 (10d12) psychic damage on a failed save or half as much on a success.

---

### **Legendary Actions**

#### **Mind Rend (Costs 1 Action)**

One creature within 60 feet must succeed on a Intelligence saving throw or take 30 (6d10) psychic damage and have disadvantage on Intelligence and Wisdom checks for 1 minute.

#### **Astral Spawn (Costs 2 Actions)**

The Great Dreamer summons 1d4 **Astral Spawn** (CR 3 aberrations) to aid it in combat.

#### **Manifest Nightmare (Costs 3 Actions)**

The Great Dreamer causes each creature within 60 feet to see its worst fear. Each creature must make a Wisdom saving throw or become **frightened** for 1 minute. While frightened, the creature takes 3d10 psychic damage at the start of its turn.
