# The Elder Evils: Manifestations of the Un-Woven

In the **Shattered Loom Cosmology**, the "Elder Evils" are not gods, nor are they typical monsters. They are **Primal Glitches**—sentient fragments of the Far Realm or "Legacy Errors" from the original Loom that have gained catastrophic power. They represent the **Ultimate Unraveling**, entities whose mere presence causes the threads of reality to dissolve into static.

While the **Blood War** is a conflict over the *state* of the weave (Rigidity vs. Entropy), the Elder Evils are the **End of the Weave** itself.

---

## 1. The Signs of the Unraveling
As an Elder Evil approaches a Material Plane shard or begins to "wake" within the **Waking Void**, the local weave begins to fray. These are known as the **Signs of the Unraveling** (adapted from the *Signs of Apocalypse*).

*   **Faint (The Fraying)**: Minor glitches in reality. Shadows move independently; magic occasionally flickers or produces "Abyssal Static."
*   **Moderate (The Tangle)**: Gravity becomes inconsistent. Commoners suffer from "The Dreamer's Fever" (collective nightmares). Divine magic requires a "Connection Check" as the Shroud thins.
*   **Strong (The Knot)**: The sun might turn a bruised purple (The Dead Sun) or the dead may spontaneously rise as "Un-Woven Echoes" (The Restless Dead). The laws of physics begin to fail.
*   **Overwhelming (The Snap)**: The shard begins to physically dissolve into the Astral Sea. The local Loom is gone; only the presence of the Elder Evil remains.

---

## 2. Key Elder Evils in the Shattered Loom

### **Father Llymic: The Frozen Nightmare**
*   **Origin**: A direct manifestation of the **Great Dreamer’s** cold indifference.
*   **Setting Adaptation**: Llymic is a "Dead-Thread" from a reality that was never woven. He seeks to plunge the multiverse into the **Permanent Stasis of the Void**. His sign is the **Dead Sun**, which siphons the "Heat" (Life-Energy) from the Loom’s threads.

### **Ragnorra: The Mother of Monsters**
*   **Origin**: A surge of "Corrupted Positive Energy" from a ruptured Loom-core.
*   **Setting Adaptation**: Ragnorra represents "Uncontrolled Growth"—threads that multiply without a pattern. She turns the ordered life of a plane into a cancerous, pulsing mass of **Dreamspawn**. Her sign is **Twisted Life**, causing creatures to mutate into horrific versions of their "Potential Selves."

### **Atropus: The World Born Dead**
*   **Origin**: The "Stillborn" remains of a plane that failed to weave during the Age of the Unbroken Tapestry.
*   **Setting Adaptation**: Atropus is a moon-sized **Knot of Necrotic Static**. He drifts through the Astral Sea, seeking to "Silence" the music of the Loom. His sign is the **Restless Dead**, where the threads of the deceased refuse to release, binding them to their rotting forms as shambling glitches.

### **Pandorym: The God-Slayer**
*   **Origin**: An ancient "Anti-Thread" weapon created during the war between the **Primordial Lords** and Telara.
*   **Setting Adaptation**: Pandorym is a sentient "Void in the Weave." It was designed to sever the connection between the Weaver and her threads. Its sign is the **Seal of Binding**, which effectively cuts off a plane from all divine and planar influence.

---

## 3. The Relationship to the Great Dreamer
Most scholars of the **Pale Lodge** believe that the Elder Evils are the **"Nightmare-Architects"** of the Great Dreamer. While the Dreamer sleeps, these entities act as its immune system—or its active malice—seeking out "Stable Shards" of reality to consume and return to the Far Realm.

---

## 4. Using Elder Evils in a Campaign
An Elder Evil campaign in the Shattered Loom is a race against **The Snap**. 
*   **The Goal**: Players must find "Needles of Telara" or "Stabilizing Shards" to anchor their world while they journey to the heart of the anomaly to "Suture" the Elder Evil back into the void.
*   **The Price**: Engaging an Elder Evil often scars a character’s own **Soul-Thread**, leading to permanent madness or "Planar Bleed" as they become partially un-woven themselves.
