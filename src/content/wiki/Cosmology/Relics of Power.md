# Relics of Power

The **Relics of Power** are artifacts of singular importance in the Shattered Loom multiverse — objects that have absorbed divine energy, historical trauma, or fragments of Telara's Loom over ages of existence. Each is intrinsically linked to a major faction who guards, wields, or desperately seeks to recover it. These are not merely magical items. They are **cornerstones of faction identity**, objects around which wars have been fought and ideologies have crystallised.

> *"Every relic is a story frozen in matter. To wield one is to carry the weight of everything it has witnessed."*

---

## I. The Needle of Eternity
**Held by:** [[../Factions/The Pale Lodge/The Pale Lodge|The Pale Lodge]]

*Wondrous Artifact — Legendary*

### Description
The Needle of Eternity is a colossal implement — roughly the length of a spear, its shaft a seamless column of pale, translucent crystal shot through with veins of living gold thread that pulse slowly, as though breathing. Its tip is impossibly sharp, able to pierce planar fabric as easily as cloth, and yet it leaves no scar when it mends rather than tears. When grasped with intent to repair, the Needle emits a low harmonic tone audible only to its wielder — a sound many Pale Lodge Spindle Lords have described as *the Loom remembering itself*.

It is kept at the Nexus Spire when not in active use, suspended upright in a cradle of woven planar energy, oriented always toward the nearest active Loom fracture.

### History
The Needle predates the Pale Lodge itself. It was found embedded in the crystalline walls of the Fractured Nexus by the Lodge's founders — driven there, they believe, at the moment the Loom shattered, as if Telara made one last desperate stitch before falling. Some scholars of the Keepers of the Sacred Weft believe it is not a tool at all, but **a fragment of the original Loom's frame** — the needle-arm of the device itself, torn free and thrown across the planes.

### Properties
- **Planar Mending (Ritual, 1 hour)**: When used during the Mending Vigil or in response to an active planar breach, the Needle can seal a rift of up to 100 feet in diameter permanently. Each use temporarily bonds the wielder's soul to the repaired section of the Loom; they cannot move more than 1 mile from the mended location for 24 hours.
- **Thread Detection**: The wielder is constantly aware of Loom fractures within 10 miles, sensing their severity and direction as a faint vibration in the hand.
- **Rift Sight**: As an action, the wielder can see through any planar barrier within 60 feet for 1 minute.
- **Alignment Pull**: The Needle subtly resists being wielded for destructive purposes. Any attempt to use it as a weapon imposes disadvantage on attack rolls and requires a saving throw Wisdom saving throw or the wielder experiences a vision of the Loom's shattering — 5d10 psychic damage, no save on a failed check.

### Lore Hooks
- The Cult of the Broken Thread has made three documented attempts to steal or destroy the Needle. Each attempt ended catastrophically for the perpetrators, though not through the Lodge's direct intervention — as if the Needle itself arranged their failure.
- Archivist Knothe of the Cult is obsessed with the Needle, believing that if one were to thread it with **unraveled Loom fragments** in reverse sequence, it could be used to *undo* a specific moment in the Loom's history, not repair it.

## II. The Threadkeeper's Gauntlet
**Held by:** [[../Factions/The Pale Lodge/The Pale Lodge|The Pale Lodge]]

*Wondrous Artifact — Very Rare*

### Description
A gauntlet of pale silver, articulated perfectly for any hand that wears it, its surface etched with thousands of hairline grooves in which golden thread is perpetually running — never knotting, never stopping. When the wearer reaches out with it, the threads extend visibly into the air, briefly touching and connecting things: people, objects, ideas. Those sensitive to magic describe it as "seeing the shape of causality, for just a moment."

### History
The Threadkeeper's Gauntlet was crafted by **Threadwarden Kaelthar** in the Lodge's early centuries, forged from alloy purchased from the Plane of Eternal Craft and woven through with threads recovered from the first sealed planar rift. It was designed as a command instrument — allowing the Warden to direct Spindle Guard units across vast distances by tugging at the threads of fate connecting individual soldiers.

Over time its use became increasingly dangerous as the Loom's degradation intensified. Today it is issued only to Spindle Lords and only when the Lodge is engaged in multi-planar operations.

### Properties
- **Threads of Alliance (3/day)**: The wearer grants up to six allies advantage on all ability checks and saving throws for 1 minute by briefly seizing the threads of fate running between them.
- **Severance (1/day)**: The wearer attempts to sever a creature's connection to fate. The target must succeed on a saving throw Constitution saving throw or be reduced to 0 hit points. On a success, they take 10d10 force damage instead.
- **Fate Reading**: Once per long rest, the wearer can ask the DM one yes/no question about the likely outcome of an action they are about to take. The answer reflects the most probable outcome, not a certain one.
- **Attunement Consequence**: The gauntlet begins pulling the wearer's own fate threads taut. Each month of attunement, the wearer's history becomes subtly more *fixed* — the DM may introduce one element of their past that was previously ambiguous as established, immutable fact.

## III. The Loomspindle Relic
**Held by:** [[../Factions/The Pale Lodge/The Pale Lodge|The Pale Lodge]]

*Wondrous Artifact — Rare*

### Description
A shard of the original Loom's spindle — roughly the size of a human fist, teardrop-shaped, made of a material that is simultaneously crystalline and fibrous. It is warm to the touch regardless of ambient temperature. Its glow intensifies as it nears other Loom fragments. In complete darkness it illuminates in soft, silver-gold light. Those who hold it for extended periods report hearing a faint weaving rhythm — like a loom operating somewhere just beyond hearing.

### History
This was the first Loom fragment the Pale Lodge ever recovered, found by the Lodge's founders during the initial survey of the Fractured Nexus. It is the founding artifact of the Lodge and considered sacred beyond its mechanical utility. Some members of the Lodge believe it is not merely *of* the Loom, but that the Loom's fragmented consciousness dwells within it — that The Silent Spool's strange behaviour around it supports this.

### Properties
- **Loom Compass**: The Relic glows brighter the closer it is to another Loom fragment, Loom shard, or active planar rift. It pulses when within 1 mile, shines when within 300 feet, and blazes when within 30 feet.
- **Fragment Resonance**: When placed in contact with another Loom shard, both objects emit a harmonic tone and the wielder receives a brief vision of where in the multiverse the *nearest* other shard is located.
- **Stabilise Rift (1/day)**: The Relic can stabilise a planar rift of up to 30 feet in diameter, preventing it from expanding for 24 hours. This does not close the rift.

## IV. The Gilded Standard
**Held by:** [[../Factions/The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper|The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper]]

*Wondrous Artifact — Legendary*

### Description
Not a weapon but a banner — a masterwork of craft so exquisite it has accrued divine properties simply through the perfection of its making. The Gilded Standard is a floor-to-ceiling pole of enchanted adamantine topped with a miniature golden caliper set with a sapphire at each of its three measurement points. Suspended from the pole is a banner of impossible textile — woven from thread drawn from the Prism Flow of Crystalis, the Ore Flow of Metallos, and the Heart Flow of Timberforge simultaneously. It depicts, in perfect relief, a map of the Plane of Eternal Craft's three cities as they existed before the Loom's shattering — more accurate and more detailed than any surveyor's chart.

The Standard is carried at the head of every formal Consortium delegation. In its presence, trade negotiations do not fail. Arbitration always reaches a resolution. This is not a metaphor.

### History
The Gilded Standard was the first object ever formally ratified under the Threefold Treaty. It was crafted jointly by the founders of all three city chapters — Metallos, Crystalis, and Timberforge — taking thirty-three years to complete, each city contributing one element. When it was finished, the Grand Calipers placed it beneath the Colossal Forge's heat for a single night as an offering to Kaldryn. In the morning it had gained its properties. The Consortium has never quite agreed on whether this was divine blessing or simply the logical outcome of perfect craft.

### Properties
- **Aura of Good Faith (Passive)**: All creatures within 60 feet of the Standard who are engaged in negotiation, trade, or arbitration are unable to knowingly make a false statement. They can refuse to speak, but cannot lie while the Standard is present and displayed.
- **Threefold Authority**: Once per long rest, the bearer of the Standard can invoke the authority of the Threefold Treaty. All creatures within 120 feet who have ever benefited from the Consortium's trade or protection must succeed on a saving throw Wisdom saving throw or cease hostile action for 1 minute, compelled to seek resolution rather than conflict.
- **Masterwork Resonance**: Any crafting attempt made within 60 feet of the Standard is treated as if the crafter has proficiency in the relevant tools, and proficient crafters make their roll with advantage.
- **The Unfinished Map**: The map on the Standard occasionally shows features that do not yet exist — structures, roads, and settlements that will be built. A saving throw Arcana check can identify these as prophetic inclusions, not decorative ones.

## V. The Fraying Spindle
**Held by:** [[../Factions/The Cult of The Broken Thread|The Cult of the Broken Thread]]

*Wondrous Artifact — Legendary (Cursed)*

### Description
Where the Lodge's Needle gleams and mends, the Fraying Spindle *unravels*. It is a short, black rod of indeterminate material — not metal, not bone, not wood, something in between all three — wrapped in the remnants of charred silver thread. Its surface is fractured along hairline fault lines, and when tilted in bright light, the cracks reveal a depth that seems to go inward much further than its dimensions should allow. It is cold. Always cold. It does not absorb heat; it seems to refuse it.

Vaelis Unspun carries it always, wrapped in a length of unfinished tapestry against their chest.

### History
The Fraying Spindle is not a made thing. The Cult believes it is a **fragment of the Loom's break-point** — a piece of the Loom's own spindle arm that shattered at the moment of the catastrophe, carrying within it the exact vibrational signature of the Shattering itself. Whether this makes it a piece of history or a *cause* of it is a theological question the Frayed Conclave has debated for centuries without resolution.

The Keepers of the Sacred Weft believe, based on a single corrupted page of the Telarian Codices, that the Fraying Spindle is capable of **reigniting** the Shattering — replaying the catastrophic unraveling not as an echo but as a new event, if placed in contact with a sufficient number of assembled Loom fragments simultaneously. Vaelis believes this is the point.

### Properties
- **Aura of Unmaking (30 ft.)**: Non-magical crafted objects within 30 feet of the Spindle that are not held or worn slowly degrade — losing 1 point of structural integrity per hour until they crumble. Magical objects must succeed on a saving throw Constitution saving throw (DM assigns stat) or suffer the same.
- **Thread-Echo Disruption**: Any attempt to use divination magic, Loom-reading, or fate-based magic within 60 feet of the Spindle has a 50% chance of producing a false or inverted result.
- **Unravel (3/day)**: As an action, the wielder targets one spell effect or magical construct within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a saving throw Charisma saving throw or the magic unravels — spell effects end, constructs take 8d10 force damage, magical bonds dissolve.
- **Curse — The Fraying Touch**: Each week of attunement, the attuned creature loses one connection from their past — a relationship, a memory, or a deeply held belief — which simply ceases to feel real or meaningful to them. This is permanent. It cannot be reversed by *Remove Curse*; only a *Wish* or divine intervention can restore what is lost.
- **Resonance Warning**: Any Pale Lodge artifact within 120 feet of the Fraying Spindle vibrates intensely and emits an audible harmonic alarm.

## VI. The Telarian Codex Fragment
**Held by:** [[../Factions/The Keepers of the Sacred Weft|The Keepers of the Sacred Weft]]

*Wondrous Artifact — Legendary*

### Description
Not a single book but a **collection of surviving pages** — thirty-seven sheets of material that is neither paper nor vellum, dense with Telara's own inscription in a script that shifts language depending on the reader's native tongue, written in ink of condensed starlight that remains perpetually luminous. They are housed in a case of warded crystal in the Skein Chamber beneath the Monastery of the Quiet Loom, arranged on a sloped lectern so they can be read without being touched.

Each page is a fragment of the divine blueprint Telara wrote before beginning to weave the multiverse. Together they represent perhaps four percent of the original document. The Keepers have spent two centuries identifying which sections they have and mapping the gaps — the gaps that the Master Weavers believe, if incorrectly filled, would cause a restored Loom to produce something monstrous.

### History
The Codex pages were not found by the Keepers — they were *given* to the order's founder by an entity that identified itself only as **"the last shuttle,"** a being the founder described as neither alive nor dead, neither mortal nor divine, but shaped entirely like the instrument of a loom. This entity has not appeared since. The Keepers believe it will return when all pages have been gathered. Some fear what it will ask for in exchange.

### Properties
- **Blueprint Reading (Ritual, 1 hour)**: A creature of Intelligence 18 or higher can study the pages for one hour and ask up to three questions about the *correct* way to restore a specific section of the Loom. The answers are always accurate but often maddeningly incomplete — a correct answer about method, but not about location, for example.
- **Pattern Recognition**: Any creature attuned to the Fragment gains advantage on all Intelligence (Arcana) and Wisdom (Insight) checks related to Loom shards, planar rifts, or restoration magic.
- **Mending Guidance**: Once per tenday, a spell cast using restoration, abjuration, or conjuration school magic within sight of the open pages has its spell save DC increased by 3 and its duration doubled.
- **Incompletion Toll**: The gaps in the Codex are not merely informational absences — they exert a subtle pull on those who study them too long. A creature that studies the pages for more than 4 hours without a long rest must succeed on a saving throw Wisdom saving throw or develop an obsessive compulsion to find the missing pages, taking priority above nearly all other goals for 1d6 days.

## VII. The Ironscript Prime
**Held by:** [[../Factions/The Ironbound Covenant|The Ironbound Covenant]]

*Wondrous Artifact — Legendary*

### Description
The oldest of the Ironscript Leaves — **the first** — is distinct from all others in the Bound Archive. Where other Leaves are silver-grey and pragmatic in their inscription, the Ironscript Prime is a plate of obsidian-dark alloy that no Forge Judge has been able to identify as any known metal from the Plane of Eternal Craft. Its runes glow amber at rest, white-hot when read aloud, and go entirely cold and invisible when Vorzath's essence is within 1 mile. It is kept in the Bound Archive's innermost vault, accessible only to the Sealed Conclave.

### History
The Ironscript Prime was not forged by Kaldryn's artisans. It was discovered embedded in the deepest wall of the Eternal Bastion when the Covenant first began construction — already there, as if the mountain had always held it. The inscription was penned in the same divine notation Kaldryn uses, but the Conclave's best scholars believe it predates even Kaldryn's first recorded act of creation on the plane. The oldest Conclave members — those who remember the plane's early centuries — are deeply unsettled by this.

Its most controversial line, the one the Conclave guards above all else:

> *"The Maw was made before the first soldier. The soldier was made to know the Maw. Whether they were made to defeat it, the maker has not said."*

### Properties
- **Void Proximity Sense (Passive)**: Any creature holding the Ironscript Prime is immediately aware of Vorzath's Maw-Spawn within 1 mile, sensing their number and rough direction as a wordless certainty.
- **Divine Notation Reading**: Once per long rest, a Forge Judge or creature with at least 5 levels in a divine spellcasting class can spend 10 minutes reading the Prime and receive an answer — not words, but an overwhelming certainty — to one yes/no question about Kaldryn's intent or Vorzath's nature. The DM determines the answer based on the cosmology.
- **Soulcore Anchor**: A Warforged who carries the Prime cannot have their Soulcore forcibly removed or drained by any means short of divine intervention. Their Soulcore also automatically stabilises at 1 hp when they would otherwise reach 0, once per long rest.
- **The Unanswered Question**: Any creature that reads the Prime's core inscription must succeed on a saving throw Wisdom saving throw or spend the next 24 hours unable to stop thinking about the question it raises. During this time they have disadvantage on Wisdom (Perception) checks and cannot take the Ready action.

## VIII. The Gilded Caliper (The Original Instrument)
**Held by:** [[../Factions/The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper|The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper]]

*Wondrous Artifact — Very Rare*

### Description
Not the symbol, but the **actual instrument** from which the Consortium takes its name. The original Caliper is a tool of measurement approximately eighteen inches from tip to tip, crafted from a gold-platinum alloy that does not exist in standard metallurgical catalogues. Its pivot is set with a single, flawless sapphire. When opened to measure something, the Caliper hums at a frequency just below audible range — and whatever it measures, it measures *perfectly*: not just dimension, but quality, magical integrity, material purity, and structural soundness.

It is displayed in the Hall of the Iron Gavel in Metallos, behind a case of reinforced crystal, and brought out only for formal certification ceremonies of exceptional items.

### History
The Gilded Caliper was crafted by the Consortium's founding Grand Caliper — a smith whose name was deliberately excluded from official records as a gesture of institutional humility. According to founding doctrine, the tool is greater than the maker; to name the maker is to diminish the tool. The Caliper was used to certify the first item ever ratified under the Threefold Treaty and has certified every masterwork of exceptional standing on the plane since.

### Properties
- **Perfect Measurement (Passive)**: When used to measure any object, the Caliper reveals its exact dimensions, weight, material composition, magical aura strength, and any structural flaws or hidden compartments. This requires no check.
- **Masterwork Certification**: An item measured and certified by the Caliper gains the **Mark of the Guild** permanently — a subtle golden glow under examination. Certified items cannot be counterfeited or magically disguised as something else.
- **Quality Arbitration (1/day)**: When two parties disagree on the value or quality of a crafted object, the Caliper can be applied to both parties' claims simultaneously. Both must succeed on a saving throw Wisdom saving throw or find themselves compelled to accept the Caliper's reading as definitive truth.
- **Detect Counterfeit**: Automatically identifies any counterfeit, magical forgery, or deliberately flawed item placed within 5 feet of it, without requiring activation.
