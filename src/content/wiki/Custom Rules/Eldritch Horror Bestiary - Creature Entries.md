# 🐙 Eldritch Horror Bestiary: Comprehensive Creature Entries
### *The Anatomical & Combat Field Guide of the Order of the Severed Eye and the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor*

> *"To butcher a beast is honest labor. To butcher an aberration is psychological warfare. When you plunge your scalpel into its thoracic fold, do not look into the cavity; look only at your hands. If the cavity blinks at you, recite the Third Litany of the Severed Eye and cut deeper before your pulse betrays you."*  
> — **Doctor Vaelen Malcor**, Chief Void-Surgeon of the Order of the Severed Eye

## 📖 Anatomical Classification & Tactical Overview

The creatures detailed within this bestiary represent aberrations, cosmic parasites, and non-Euclidean entities that have breached the Loom from the **Far Realm**, the abyssal drowned depths of **Ry'leh**, or shattered planar rifts. 

### Special Eldritch Mechanics in Combat
1. **The Reciprocal Gaze**: Whenever a player character targets an eldritch creature with an attack, spell, or ability that requires sight, they momentarily witness the creature's true multi-dimensional geometry. Critical hits or natural 1s on attack rolls against the creature trigger a DC-specific Wisdom saving throw or incur immediate **Lucidity Drain (Module II)**.
2. **Alien Cognition**: Eldritch aberrations are immune to psychic damage and cannot be charmed, frightened, or put to sleep by mundane or fey magic. Any attempt to read the creature's thoughts (*Detect Thoughts*, telepathy) forces the caster to make an immediate Intelligence saving throw or take 3d8 psychic damage and gain a level of Madness.
3. **Non-Euclidean Physiology**: Attacks from beyond 30 feet suffer disadvantage against these creatures unless the attacker possesses blindsight, truesight, or the *Silver-Filament Blindfold*, as the creature's physical form constantly folds through impossible spatial dimensions.
