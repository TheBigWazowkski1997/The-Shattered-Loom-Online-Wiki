# 🏹 Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium
### *The Master Directory of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor across 22 Planes of Existence (66 Apex Monsters)*

> *"There is no shard of reality so desolate that something hasn't grown claws to rule it. And where there are claws, there is an Iron Spoor lodge watching the tree-line, sharpening the harpoons, and keeping the stew warm."*  
> — **Grand Huntmaster Torvald Blood-Tracer**, Opening Address at the Hearth of the Spoor

## 🏛️ Directory Overview

The **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** maintains twenty-two permanent branch lodges across the planes of the Shattered multiverse. Each lodge acts as:
1. **A Regional Sanctuary**: Safe refuge from ambient planar hazards (hellfire, abyssal decay, non-Euclidean vertigo).
2. **A Processing Hub**: Equipped with salting vats, tanning racks, and master trophy forges to process monster carcasses before planar bleed ruins them.
3. **A Commerce Center**: Where hunters redeem **Spoor Marks**, buy specialized local tracking gear, and contract with master trophy-smiths.

Below is the complete regional breakdown of all **22 Planar Lodges**, each featuring **Three Thematic Apex Quarry Monsters** with complete harvest yields and trophy-forge recipes (**66 Monsters Total**).
