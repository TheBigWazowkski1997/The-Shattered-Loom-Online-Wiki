## **Opening Scene – Morning in the Citadel**

Describe the eerie “normalcy” of daily life for someone who lives in Castle Avernus:

* **The sky**: a constant twilight above the citadel, streaked with green lightning.
* **The halls**: shifting corridors that “settle” only when the necromancer wills them.
* **The neighbors**: intelligent undead going about business, apprentices whispering incantations, vendors calling from shadowy stalls.

**Prompt to Player:** “How does your necromancer start the day? What does their home look like inside the walls of this living citadel?”

* * *

## **Step 1 – The Messenger**

A servant (perhaps a skeletal courier with a sealed parchment) delivers a message.
**The Message (Quest Hook):**

> “Your talents are required beyond these halls. A disturbance brews in the Mists, and the remnants of Azalin’s will hunger for release. Prepare yourself. Seek the Bazaar. What you claim will decide your fate.”

This sets the quest in motion: the necromancer must **gather supplies from the Shadowed Bazaar** before leaving Castle Avernus.

* * *

## **Step 2 – The Shadowed Bazaar**

The necromancer descends into the Bazaar, weaving through shops and familiar NPCs where they come face to face with THE SILENT SPOOL. Encourage **roleplay with merchants**, and let the player make **choices about which items they’ll buy or barter for.**

* * *

## **Step 3 – Farewells**

The necromancer visits people they know in the citadel before departing. Encourage personal roleplay here:

* **An undead friend or ally** (a ghost tutor, skeletal servant, or stitched homunculus) offers a final gift—maybe a charm or a whispered secret.
* **Archivist Rhaegis** may press a forbidden tome into their hands, hissing: _“Knowledge is a heavier burden than steel. Carry it wisely.”_

**Prompt to Player:** “Who in Darkon would you say goodbye to, and what words pass between you?”

* * *

## **Step 4 – The Departure**

As the necromancer returns to their chambers to pack, the citadel **reacts**. The walls close in, shadows stretch unnaturally, and the echo of Azalin’s voice whispers through the stone:

> “You cannot escape me. Even beyond these halls… you carry my legacy.”

## **Opening Scene – Chains & Ashes**

_Location: A memory within Kaelen’s mind, half-dream, half-nightmare._
The session begins with Kaelen reliving his **first great failure**: the cave where Sorren Velak betrayed his people. The DM describes echoes of screams, shadows flickering unnaturally, and Kaelen holding the sharpened stake to his own chest.

* Dream of Telara
* **Choice Point:** Before Xal’thorak’s voice enters, ask Kaelen what he would have done if no pact was offered. This sets tone for how the player imagines his willpower and despair.
* **Xal’thorak’s Bargain:** The infernal voice slithers in—smooth, patient, inevitable. Play this conversation out. Make Kaelen _state aloud_ what vengeance means to him. Once he agrees, the **Bloomfang seed** manifests in his palm, pulsing faintly with hunger.

* * *

## **Act I – The Fractured Streets of Axiom**

_Location: The City of Broken Symmetry._
Kaelen awakens on one of the **floating marble shards** of Axiom, years later. He’s no longer the boy from the cave but the rogue forged in shadows. The city is alive around him—bridges flicker in and out, people walk sideways across walls, time stutters (an hourglass runs backward mid-conversation).

* **Encounter:** Kaelen is summoned to the **Citadel of Broken Codes**, where the **Wardens of the Fractured Code** question his presence. Rumors of his pact (and murders) have reached them.

* * *

## **Act II – The Demon’s Demand**

_Location: A forgotten alley in Axiom’s shifting streets._
As night falls, Kaelen feels the pull of Xal’thorak. The **Bloomfang** whispers for nourishment. If he resists, he suffers psychic torment; if he obeys, he must take a life.

* **Choice Point:** The DM presents two possible victims:

A dying beggar who begs for release.
A young thief who tried to steal Kaelen’s coin purse.
No matter what Kaelen chooses, he feels the plane itself **react**—bridges collapse, shards tremble, whispers echo that "law bends beneath the blade."

* * *

## **Act III – Shadows of the Past**

_Location: The Shattered Plaza, a place where fragments of time overlap._
Kaelen encounters a **familiar face**: Hadrik, the old soldier who once raised him. But this version is wrong—an echo pulled from the fractured plane, warped and bitter. Hadrik condemns Kaelen for the deaths he caused, accusing him of betraying his mother’s memory.

* **Social Encounter:** Hadrik’s echo wields spectral chains and tries to bind Kaelen, demanding he “submit to order.” Kaelen can fight him or attempt to talk him down.
