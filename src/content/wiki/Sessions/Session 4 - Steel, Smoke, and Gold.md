# Session Plan: Session 4 — Steel, Smoke, and Gold

**Location:** The Fractured Nexus → Plane of Eternal Craft (Metallos)
**Primary Goal:** Secure access to the **Colossal Forge** to purify the *Loom Fragment of the Crimson Weave*, and navigate the gang politics of Metallos to resolve the industrial lockdown.

---

## **Scene I: The Reforging Directive**

*   **Setting:** The Atrium of Reflection in the **Pale Lodge** or a quiet corner of the **Frayed End Tavern**.
*   **The Problem:** The party has returned from Barovia with the **Loom Fragment of the Crimson Weave (The Crimson Thread)**. However, when **"The Stitcher" (Vaelen Corryn)** inspects it, his hands tremble.
    *   The fragment is heavily corrupted by Strahd’s blood-magic and the dark mists of the Shadowfell. It vibrates with a predatory, cold hunger.
    *   *"If we try to stitch this into the Spire as it is, the resonance will overwrite the Nexus with a curse of blood and decay. It must be purified."*
*   **The Directive:** Vaelen explains that the only heat capable of burning away this ancient planar corruption is the **Colossal Forge** in **Metallos**, the Great Foundry City on the **Plane of Eternal Craft**.
*   **Transition:** Vaelen activates a planar portal key, opening a rift that smells of hot iron, sulfur, and coal smoke. The party steps through, leaving the violet hues of the Nexus behind.

---

## **Scene II: Family Business in the Dwarven District**

*   **Setting:** The **Emberflow Quarter** of Metallos. Rhythmic clanging of giant steam-hammers, rivers of molten brass flowing beneath iron grates, and soot-stained stone buildings.
*   **The Welcome:** Since **Daine** was raised by the **Ironhand Brotherhood**, the party heads straight to their headquarters: a massive, fortified brewery and tavern in the Dwarven District.
    *   *Publicly:* A roaring, crowded alehouse famous for its heavy stouts and boisterous patrons.
    *   *Privately:* Carved stone vaults, secure ledgers, and escape tunnels leading to the docks and fighting pits.
*   **Key NPCs:**
    *   **Kazrik “The Auditor” Goldmantle:** Calm, immaculate, soft-spoken, and ruthless. Daine's savior and father-figure. He greets Daine with a rare, warm nod. *"You look well, son. But you return at a difficult hour."*
    *   **Merric “Quicklock” Bramblefoot:** Daine’s halfling safecracking partner, fast-talking and currently snacking on honey-roasted nuts. He eagerly claps Daine on the shoulder.
    *   **Tivren Gearquill:** The meticulous gnome bookkeeper who calmly adjusts his ledger, noting the interest on a few overdue contracts.
    *   **Rurik Stonejaw:** The boastful dwarven pit champion, drinking heavy stout and itching for a good brawl.
*   **The Situation:** Kazrik explains that the **Consortium of the Gilded Caliper** (specifically Arbiter Valerie Sterling) has locked down the Colossal Forge due to severe sabotage in the **Molten Veins**. The merchant lords are blaming the outer districts and local gangs for the incident, threatening a bloody purge.

---

## **Scene III: The Shadow Alliance**

*   **The Meeting:** **Seraphaine "Silvertongue" Vale**, the tiefling leader of the **Gilded Crucible** (a fluid burglary and fence syndicate), slipstream-slides into the brewery's private meeting vault. 
*   **The Alliance:** The Brotherhood and the Crucible have a long-standing alliance (Ironhand provides muscle and money laundering; the Crucible provides espionage and stealth). 
*   **The Intelligence:** Seraphaine reveals that the lockdown is a setup. The rival syndicates—the **Moonveil Accord** (elf-led political manipulators) and the **Bloodchain Horde** (orc/goblin expansionists)—are using the crisis to dismantle the Brotherhood's control over the smelting contracts and the docks.
    *   *“The Moonveil Accord has been smuggling unstable magic-cores into the lower districts, and the Bloodchain Horde is instigating violent dock riots to frame our alliance for the sabotage. If the Caliper lords believe them, we're all dead or in chains.”*
*   **The Plan:** The party must secure the trust of the **Gilded Caliper** by proving the rival gangs' framing plot. In return, the gang alliance will use their political sway to grant the party access to the Colossal Forge once the lockdown is lifted.

---

## **Scene IV: The Merchant's Court**

*   **Location:** The **Hall of the Iron Gavel**, a massive brass-domed fortress.
*   **The Confrontation:** The party enters the court of **Arbiter Valerie Sterling** (the stocky, Braided-copper-haired dwarf chapter head). She is surrounded by angry guild merchants and inspectors demanding action.
*   **The Triumvirate Gridlock:** The three supreme rulers of the plane are deadlocked:
    *   **Hesperus Vane (Metallos):** Wants to deploy clockwork automatons to purge the lower districts and enforce martial law.
    *   **Lady Seraphina Aurum (Crystalis):** Distracted, studying the emotional resonance of the crisis.
    *   **Elder Oakhaven (Timberforge):** Strongly opposing Vane’s industrial militarization.
*   **The Negotiation:** The party must present the evidence gathered (using Daine's reputation, Tivren's financial records, or Seraphaine's stolen blueprints). 
    *   **Checks:** A **DC 15 Charisma (Persuasion or Deception)** check is required to convince Valerie and the council that the sabotage is a frame-up. 
    *   Valerie is skeptical but pragmatic. She makes a deal: *"The Bloodchain Horde has challenged the Brotherhood's authority in the public arena. The trade guilds need to see strength and order. Win the crowd, humiliate the rivals, and I will delay Vane's clockwork sweep. Prove who did the sabotage, and the forge is yours."*

---

## **Scene V: Showdown at the Ironhand Pits**

*   **Setting:** A roaring, circular stone fighting arena beneath the Dwarven District, packed to the brim with screaming miners, artisans, and gang members. Molten metal glows in trenches surrounding the sand-covered pit.
*   **The Challenge:** To resolve the gang dispute over smelting rights and prove the Horde's instigations, Daine and the party must face a champion in a high-stakes match.
*   **The Enemy:** **[[Dorrim Ashvein]]** (dwarf, a dirty-fighting defector who left the Ironhand Brotherhood for the Gilded Crucible's gold and now serves as their champion). He is backed by 8x Bandit Captain
*   **Combat Encounter: The Smelting Pit**
    *   Dorrim taunts Daine mercilessly. He uses pockets of blinding soot, spiked knuckles, and attempts to push players into the molten trenches.
    *   **Spectator Mechanics (The Crowd):**
        *   The crowd reacts to flashy, honorable, or brutal actions. A character can use a bonus action to make a **DC 14 Charisma (Performance or Intimidation) check** to sway the crowd.
        *   *Crowd Favor:* Whichever side has the crowd's favor gains a +1 bonus to attack rolls and saving throws as the arena echoes with their name.
*   **The Traitor’s Defeat:** Once Dorrim is beaten (reduced to 0 HP or yields), he spits blood and laughs. Daine has the choice to spare him or execute him for his treachery. Sparing or forcing him to talk reveals the final clue.

---

## **Scene VI: The Cult Exposed (Cliffhanger)**

*   **The Reveal:** Dorrim or a captured Bloodchain lieutenant confesses under pressure:
    *   Both the Moonveil Accord and the Bloodchain Horde were secretly manipulated and funded by a shadowy faction within the city—the **Cult of the Broken Thread**.
    *   The Cult didn't just want a trade war; they wanted to snuff out the plane's core. They have seized the subterranean **Molten Veins** directly beneath the Colossal Forge, preparing to flood the conduits with void-magic to permanently break the Loom's anchor.
*   **The Cliffhanger:** The party races back to the Hall of the Iron Gavel. Arbiter Valerie Sterling, realizing the gravity of the situation, points them to the central elevator shaft.
    *   *"The security gates are locked, but they've cut the steam lines. Go down there. Stop them before they freeze the heart of Metallos. If you succeed, my personal smiths will heat the forge for whatever you carry."*
    *   The session ends as the party steps onto the heavy iron elevator platform, the chains grinding as they begin their dark descent into the steam-choked abyss of the Molten Veins.
