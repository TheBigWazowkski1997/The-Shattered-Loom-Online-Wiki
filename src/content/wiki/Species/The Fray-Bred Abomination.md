## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Fray-Bred Abomination (Goristro-Hydra)** (Huge Fiend (Demon, Aberration), Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Abyss**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Broken-Bone Redoubt**.

A towering, multi-headed beast spawned in the entropic pits of the 88th layer. It combines the massive bulk of a minotaur-goristro with four writhing hydra maws that spew acidic bile and scream in discordant frequencies.
