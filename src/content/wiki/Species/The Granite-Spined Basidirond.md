# 🦁 The Granite-Spined Basidirond
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “The bedrock has teeth here. When you hear the amethyst geodes singing beneath your boots, draw adamantine steel or be crushed into gravel.”
— Grumm Stone-Shoulder, Deep-Delver Master

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Granite-Spined Basidirond** (Huge Plant (Elemental), Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Earth**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Iron-Root Delve**.

An inverted umbrella-shaped fungal monstrosity that roots in adamantine veins, launching hallucinogenic spores that cause victims to see the cavern walls collapsing upon them.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Earth|Plane of Earth]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Iron-Root Delve]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Granite-Spined Basidirond establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Granite-Spined Basidirond decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
