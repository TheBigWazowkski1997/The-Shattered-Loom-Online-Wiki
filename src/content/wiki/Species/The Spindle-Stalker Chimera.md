# 🦁 The Spindle-Stalker Chimera
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Mortal hunters think claws and teeth are all a beast needs. In the Shattered Loom, even the wolves have threads of astral force stitched between their ribs.”

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

![The Spindle-Stalker Chimera prowling through the pine forests of the Material Plane with owl wings spread and dual heads alert.](/The-Shattered-Loom-Online-Wiki/images/spindle_stalker_chimera.jpg)


A **The Spindle-Stalker Chimera** (Large Monstrosity, Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Material Plane**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Timber-Weft Lodge**.

A predatory chimera warped by ambient Loom filaments. It has the body of a scarred shadow-panther, the wings of a gloom-owl, and twin heads—one a lioness with prismatic glass fangs, the other a serpent spitting thread-dissolving acid.

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Material Plane|The Material Plane]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Timber-Weft Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Spindle-Stalker Chimera establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Spindle-Stalker Chimera decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
