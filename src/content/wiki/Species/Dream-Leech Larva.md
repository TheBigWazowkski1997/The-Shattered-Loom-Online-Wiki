# 🧬 Dream-Leech Larva
### *Multiversal Species & Apex Predator Dossier*

> “It did not burrow into the flesh of our scout; it burrowed into the memory of his mother's voice. When we pulled the pale slug from his nape, he could no longer remember why he wept.”
— Brother Thaddeus, Chronicler of the Severed Eye

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **Dream-Leech Larva** is a parasitic aberration originating from the psychically saturated astral currents bordering the Far Realm and the City of Ry'leh. Appearing as a three-foot-long, translucent, slug-like creature ribbed with pulsing bioluminescent eyes, it slithers through oneiric rifts to feed upon the subconscious thoughts and lifespans of sleeping sentient humanoids.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

Dream-Leech larvae cannot survive long in mundane three-dimensional atmosphere without a psychic anchor. When an astral traveler sleeps near a planar tear or shatterpoint, the larva detects the beacon of their dream-frequencies. Slipping across the planar veil, it attaches itself to the brainstem. While attached, it secretes an anaesthetic psychic slime that induces paralyzing nightmares, siphoning memories and leaving its host perpetually exhausted until the mind collapses into complete catatonia.
