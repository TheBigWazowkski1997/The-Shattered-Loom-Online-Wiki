# 🦁 The Abyssal Kraken-Spawn
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 14)*

> “The deep trench doesn't forgive. When a leviathan rises from the sapphire abyss, you hold your breath and pray the harpoon winch holds.”
— Captain Seris Vael, Void-Corsair

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Abyssal Kraken-Spawn** (Huge Monstrosity (Aberration), Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Water**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Coral-Spire Haven**.

A predatory juvenile titan of the lightless trenches, featuring eight barbed tentacles lined with lamprey-like mouths and a central beak of serrated black coral. It emits a bioluminescent ink that suffocates magic and light.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Water|Plane of Water]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Coral-Spire Haven]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Abyssal Kraken-Spawn establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Abyssal Kraken-Spawn decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 17 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 180 (18d12 + 90)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft., swim 50 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 22 (+6) | DEX 16 (+3) | CON 20 (+5) | INT 20 (+5) | WIS 16 (+3) | CHA 16 (+3)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +10, Wis +8
  * **Senses**: truesight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 18
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 14 (14,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +5
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Abyssal Kraken-Spawn has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+5) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Abyssal Kraken-Spawn makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 15 (3d10 + 6) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Abyssal Kraken-Spawn unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 17 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 49 (14d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
