# 🧬 Mind-Leviathan Harbinger
### *Multiversal Species & Apex Predator Dossier*

> “The sky did not darken because of storm clouds. It darkened because a floating brain the size of a war galley drifted over our spires, singing in frequencies that made our clerics gouge out their ears.”
— Inquisitor Sylas the Blind

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **Mind-Leviathan Harbinger** is an ancient, floating titan of aberrant consciousness. Suspended by self-generated gravitational fields and enveloped in halos of crackling cerulean psychic flame, this cephalopod behemoth features six massive golden eyes that observe the weave across past, present, and parallel realities. From its belly hang dozens of barbed nerve-cables capable of crushing war galleys or rewriting minds.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

Harbingers act as heralds for Elder Evils and Primordial Sleepers. They drift along the boundaries between the Astral Sea and the Far Realm, feeding on the collective unconsciousness of entire civilizations. Their presence causes mass somnambulism, spontaneous amnesia, and spontaneous generation of aberrant tumors among terrestrial wildlife.
