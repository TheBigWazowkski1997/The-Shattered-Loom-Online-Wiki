# 🦁 The Leviathan Bone-Whale
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 13)*

> “The deep trench doesn't forgive. When a leviathan rises from the sapphire abyss, you hold your breath and pray the harpoon winch holds.”
— Captain Seris Vael, Void-Corsair

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Leviathan Bone-Whale** (Gargantuan Beast (Monstrosity), Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Water**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Coral-Spire Haven**.

An ancient cetacean measuring over 90 feet in length, armored in external bone scaffolding formed from calcified coral. Its echolocating breach can shatter ship hulls and rupture the lungs of divers.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Water|Plane of Water]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Coral-Spire Haven]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Leviathan Bone-Whale establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Leviathan Bone-Whale decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 16 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 153 (17d20 + 68)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft., swim 50 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 20 (+5) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 18 (+4) | INT 10 (+0) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +9, Wis +7
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 17
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 13 (13,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +5
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Leviathan Bone-Whale has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+5) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Leviathan Bone-Whale makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 14 (3d10 + 5) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Leviathan Bone-Whale unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 16 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 45 (13d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
