# 🧬 Dimensional Warp-Stalker
### *Multiversal Species & Apex Predator Dossier*

> “It did not run through the trees. It folded the forest like a paper fan, stepping from one horizon to the next between the blinks of an eye.”
— Captain Seris Vael, Void-Corsair

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **Dimensional Warp-Stalker** is a predatory horror born of shattered space-time. Resembling an eight-limbed chimeric panther coated in interlocking indigo chitin plates, its body folds constantly across four spatial dimensions. Four scythe-like mantis blades extend from its shoulder ridges, shearing reality with crisp, glass-like detonations.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

Native to the unstable periphery of the Far Realm and the tears of the Fractured Nexus, warp-stalkers hunt creatures with high psionic potential or magical resonance. They establish territories across planar rifts, stalking prey by sensing the gravitational ripples of active spellcasting. When cornered, they collapse localized geometry, forcing their quarry into pocket rifts where oxygen and sanity quickly evaporate.
