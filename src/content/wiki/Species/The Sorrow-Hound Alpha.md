# 🦁 The Sorrow-Hound Alpha
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 8)*

> “In the darkness of the gloom, things do not merely hunt you for meat; they feed upon the memory of what you loved before you crossed the mist.”
— Alanik Ray, Investigator of the Dark Realms

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Sorrow-Hound Alpha** (Large Monstrosity (Shadow), Neutral Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Shadowfell**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Cinder-Lantern Lodge**.

A canine shadow-predator whose fur absorbs all ambient light. It stalks those in deep grief, amplifying feelings of hopelessness until its prey lays down in the snow to die.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Shadowfell|The Shadowfell]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Cinder-Lantern Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Sorrow-Hound Alpha establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Sorrow-Hound Alpha decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 14 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 84 (12d10 + 24)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 16 (+3) | DEX 16 (+3) | CON 14 (+2) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +5, Wis +5
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 15
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 8 (8,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +3
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Sorrow-Hound Alpha has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+3) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Sorrow-Hound Alpha makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +6 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 12 (2d10 + 3) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Sorrow-Hound Alpha unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 13 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 28 (8d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
