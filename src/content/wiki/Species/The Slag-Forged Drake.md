# 🦁 The Slag-Forged Drake
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Forged in the heart of celestial crucibles, these beasts are living engines of adamantine and perpetual flame.”
— Master Smith Thoradin

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Slag-Forged Drake** (Huge Dragon (Elemental), Neutral Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Eternal Craft**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Anvil-Hearth Lodge**.

A wingless quadrupedal drake born from the runoff slag of divine anvils. Its body is composed of layered Damascus-folded steel and glowing magma veins, exhaling a torrent of white-hot molten metal.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Eternal Craft|Plane of Eternal Craft]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Anvil-Hearth Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Slag-Forged Drake establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Slag-Forged Drake decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
