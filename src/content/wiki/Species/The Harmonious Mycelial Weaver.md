# 🦁 The Harmonious Mycelial Weaver
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “To fight one is to fight the entire ecosystem. Its mycelial threads connect every root, leaf, and hive in a single sovereign mind.”
— Arch-Weaver Thelar

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Harmonious Mycelial Weaver** (Huge Plant (Monstrosity), Lawful Good) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Unity**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Threaded Haven**.

A colossal entity of ancient fungal threads, living bark, and luminous spore-mushrooms. It communicates through spores, absorbing fallen beasts into its shared root-consciousness while ensnaring intruders in empathic webs.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Unity|Plane of Unity]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Threaded Haven]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Harmonious Mycelial Weaver establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Harmonious Mycelial Weaver decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
