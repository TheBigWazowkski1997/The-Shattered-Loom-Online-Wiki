# 🦁 The Tempering Flame-Salamander
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Forged in the heart of celestial crucibles, these beasts are living engines of adamantine and perpetual flame.”
— Master Smith Thoradin

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Tempering Flame-Salamander** (Large Elemental (Monstrosity), Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Eternal Craft**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Anvil-Hearth Lodge**.

A quick, predatory lizard that nests inside the cooling flumes of Kaldryn's forges. Its scales quench molten metals instantly, absorbing thermal energy and redirecting it as concussive shockwaves.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Eternal Craft|Plane of Eternal Craft]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Anvil-Hearth Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Tempering Flame-Salamander establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Tempering Flame-Salamander decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
