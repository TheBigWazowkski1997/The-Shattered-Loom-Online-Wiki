# 🦁 The Tomb-Gorged Corpse-Wyrm
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “In the darkness of the gloom, things do not merely hunt you for meat; they feed upon the memory of what you loved before you crossed the mist.”
— Alanik Ray, Investigator of the Dark Realms

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Tomb-Gorged Corpse-Wyrm** (Huge Undead (Monstrosity), Neutral Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Shadowfell**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Cinder-Lantern Lodge**.

A terrifying, segmented scavenger that burrows through the grave-silt of Darkon and the Svalich Woods. Its carapace is made of fused mausoleum stones and bone meal, exhaling a vapor of tomb-chill.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Shadowfell|The Shadowfell]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Cinder-Lantern Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Tomb-Gorged Corpse-Wyrm establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Tomb-Gorged Corpse-Wyrm decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
