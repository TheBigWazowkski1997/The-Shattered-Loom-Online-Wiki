# 🦁 The Echo-Mirage Stalker
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 12)*

> “It mimics the face of your dead companion, weeping with their voice while its glass spines prepare to pierce your lungs.”
— Memory-Keeper Selene

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Echo-Mirage Stalker** (Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Fragmented Memories**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Shard-Cairn Outpost**.

A feline predator composed of broken mirror-prisms that stalks travelers across the Shardlands. It does not attack directly; it projects illusions of the target's most painful memories, feeding on mental anguish before shattering into a whirlwind of razor glass.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Fragmented Memories|Plane of Fragmented Memories]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Shard-Cairn Outpost]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Echo-Mirage Stalker establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Echo-Mirage Stalker decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 16 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 144 (16d10 + 64)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 20 (+5) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 18 (+4) | INT 10 (+0) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +8, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 12 (12,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Echo-Mirage Stalker has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Echo-Mirage Stalker makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 14 (2d10 + 5) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Echo-Mirage Stalker unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 16 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 42 (12d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
