# 🧬 Primordial Sleeper Scion
### *Multiversal Species & Apex Predator Dossier*

> “It is not a beast that walks upon the earth; it is the nightmare of a god that has taken flesh and stone to trample cities into dust.”
— Doctor Vaelen Malcor, Void-Surgeon

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **Primordial Sleeper Scion** is a titan of cosmic cataclysm, spawned directly from the dreaming psyche of the slumbering Great Dreamer beneath Ry'leh. Standing forty feet tall with a torso constructed from non-Euclidean black stone and obsidian living flesh, it is crowned by a ring of weeping green stars and ten titanic tentacles tipped with lamprey maws.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

Awakening only when cosmic alignments or shatterpoints tear wide open, the Sleeper Scion does not seek sustenance—it seeks the dissolution of waking reality. Landscapes crushed under its tread become permanent salt-marshes of psychic brine, and whole towns exposed to its somnolence aura fall into perpetual sleep, their collective consciousness absorbed into the Sleeper's core.
