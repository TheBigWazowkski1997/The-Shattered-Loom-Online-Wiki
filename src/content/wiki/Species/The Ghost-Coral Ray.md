# 🦁 The Ghost-Coral Ray
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “It swims through oceans of whispered truths. When you slay it, its dying breath reveals a secret that will break your heart.”
— Sister Elyse

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Ghost-Coral Ray** (Huge Beast (Undead), Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Secrets**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Silent Pearl Pavilion**.

A spectral manta ray whose wings are composed of translucent coral filigree. It glides noiselessly through the Ocean of Whispers, passing through solid objects like phantom mist.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Secrets|Plane of Secrets]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Silent Pearl Pavilion]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Ghost-Coral Ray establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Ghost-Coral Ray decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
