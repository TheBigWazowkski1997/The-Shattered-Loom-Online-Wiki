# 🦁 The Moonhorn Stag-Behemoth
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 11)*

> “Never trust a beast that shines like silver. The more beautiful its antlers, the deeper the thorny roots it will bury into your ribcage.”
— Lady Vaelen Sylph-Treader, Silver-Bough Lodge

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

![The Moonhorn Stag-Behemoth standing regally in an enchanted fey glade, crowned by starlight-crystalline antlers.](/The-Shattered-Loom-Online-Wiki/images/moonhorn_stag_behemoth.jpg)


A **The Moonhorn Stag-Behemoth** (Huge Fey, Chaotic Good) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Feywild**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Silver-Bough Lodge**.

A colossal silver-white stag standing 18 feet at the shoulder, crowned by sweeping crystalline antlers that chime with celestial harmonies. Its hooves leave trails of luminous morning dew that cause thorny roses to erupt in its wake.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Feywild|The Feywild]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Silver-Bough Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Moonhorn Stag-Behemoth establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Moonhorn Stag-Behemoth decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 15 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 120 (15d12 + 45)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 18 (+4) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 16 (+3) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 14 (+2)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +7, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: truesight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 11 (11,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Moonhorn Stag-Behemoth has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Moonhorn Stag-Behemoth makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 13 (3d10 + 4) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Moonhorn Stag-Behemoth unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 15 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 38 (11d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
