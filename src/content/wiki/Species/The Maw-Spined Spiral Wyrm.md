# 🦁 The Maw-Spined Spiral Wyrm
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “An endless gullet lined with spinning teeth. It swallows reality whole, leaving only hollow vacuum in its wake.”
— Scout Kaelen the Scarred

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Maw-Spined Spiral Wyrm** (Huge Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Infinite Hunger**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Maw-Rim Camp**.

A terrifying burrower that spirals along the chasm walls of Vorzath's domain. Its body is surrounded by five rotating rings of serrated bone-plates that grind stone and flesh into digestible slurry.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Infinite Hunger|Plane of Infinite Hunger]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Maw-Rim Camp]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Maw-Spined Spiral Wyrm establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Maw-Spined Spiral Wyrm decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
