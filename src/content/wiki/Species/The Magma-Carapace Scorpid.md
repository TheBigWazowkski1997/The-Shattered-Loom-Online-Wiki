# 🦁 The Magma-Carapace Scorpid
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “If its blood doesn't melt your spearhead, you haven't struck deep enough into its magma core.”
— Torvald Blood-Tracer, Grand Huntmaster

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

![The Magma-Carapace Scorpid poised atop an obsidian precipice in the Plane of Fire, armor veins burning with liquid magma.](/The-Shattered-Loom-Online-Wiki/images/magma_carapace_scorpid.jpg)


A **The Magma-Carapace Scorpid** (Huge Elemental (Monstrosity), Neutral Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Fire**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Cinder-Spur Bastion**.

A hulking arachnid armored in interlocking plates of blackened volcanic glass. Its tail drips boiling sulfur-venom, and its claws can shear through solid iron while superheating the surrounding air to kiln temperatures.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Fire|Plane of Fire]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Cinder-Spur Bastion]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Magma-Carapace Scorpid establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Magma-Carapace Scorpid decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
