# 🦁 The Tidal Sea-Drake
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 11)*

> “The deep trench doesn't forgive. When a leviathan rises from the sapphire abyss, you hold your breath and pray the harpoon winch holds.”
— Captain Seris Vael, Void-Corsair

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Tidal Sea-Drake** (Huge Dragon (Elemental), Chaotic Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Water**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Coral-Spire Haven**.

A sleek, finned dragon with scales like polished sea-glass. It rides oceanic whirlpools, firing high-pressure jets of water capable of boring through foot-thick iron bulkheads.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Water|Plane of Water]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Coral-Spire Haven]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Tidal Sea-Drake establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Tidal Sea-Drake decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 15 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 120 (15d12 + 45)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft., fly 60 ft., swim 50 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 18 (+4) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 16 (+3) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 14 (+2)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +7, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: truesight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 11 (11,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Tidal Sea-Drake has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Tidal Sea-Drake makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 13 (3d10 + 4) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Tidal Sea-Drake unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 15 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 38 (11d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
