## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Tidal Sea-Drake** (Huge Dragon (Elemental), Chaotic Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Water**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Coral-Spire Haven**.

A sleek, finned dragon with scales like polished sea-glass. It rides oceanic whirlpools, firing high-pressure jets of water capable of boring through foot-thick iron bulkheads.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Water|Plane of Water]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Coral-Spire Haven]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Tidal Sea-Drake establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Tidal Sea-Drake decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
