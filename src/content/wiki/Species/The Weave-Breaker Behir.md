# 🦁 The Weave-Breaker Behir
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 11)*

> “Mortal hunters think claws and teeth are all a beast needs. In the Shattered Loom, even the wolves have threads of astral force stitched between their ribs.”
— Huntsman Roderic Reed, Timber-Weft Lodge Master

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Weave-Breaker Behir** (Huge Monstrosity, Neutral Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Material Plane**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Timber-Weft Lodge**.

A twelve-legged serpentine titan whose azure scales are cross-hatched with golden Loom-scars. It hunts magical ley lines, disgorging lightning that shatters spell wards and unwinds active enchantments.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Material Plane|The Material Plane]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Timber-Weft Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Weave-Breaker Behir establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Weave-Breaker Behir decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 15 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 120 (15d12 + 45)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 18 (+4) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +7, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 11 (11,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Weave-Breaker Behir has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Weave-Breaker Behir makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 13 (3d10 + 4) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Weave-Breaker Behir unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 15 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 38 (11d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
