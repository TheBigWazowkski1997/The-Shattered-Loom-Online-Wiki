# 🦁 The Primal Apex Chimera-Panther
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 13)*

> “Primal perfection without civilized mercy. It is the apex predator of the ancient wild, untouched by mortal gods or planar decay.”
— Hunter Bran of the Verdant Canopy

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Primal Apex Chimera-Panther** (Huge Beast (Monstrosity), Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Wild Balance**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Verdant Canopy Lodge**.

The undisputed apex hunter of the Verdant Nexus. It features the muscular chassis of a dire sabertooth panther, the sweeping horned mantle of a ram, and four barbed scorpion tails that lash with neuromuscular venom.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Wild Balance|Plane of Wild Balance]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Verdant Canopy Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Primal Apex Chimera-Panther establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Primal Apex Chimera-Panther decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 16 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 153 (17d12 + 68)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft., climb 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 20 (+5) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 18 (+4) | INT 10 (+0) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +9, Wis +7
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 17
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 13 (13,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +5
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Primal Apex Chimera-Panther has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+5) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Primal Apex Chimera-Panther makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 14 (3d10 + 5) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Primal Apex Chimera-Panther unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 16 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 45 (13d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
