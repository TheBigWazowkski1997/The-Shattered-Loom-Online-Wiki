# 🦁 The Living Vellum Cockatrice
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 9)*

> “A beast with pages for scales and ink for venom. It does not eat your flesh; it rewrites your past until you were never born at all.”
— Scribe Justinian, Custodian of the Vaults

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Living Vellum Cockatrice** (Medium Monstrosity, Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Grand Archive**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Scribe's Quill Lodge**.

A chimeric rooster-serpent composed of compressed illuminated manuscript pages and quill-like feathers that inject a fast-acting calcifying ink into intruders.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Grand Archive|The Grand Archive]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Scribe's Quill Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Living Vellum Cockatrice establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Living Vellum Cockatrice decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 14 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 91 (13d8 + 26)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 16 (+3) | DEX 16 (+3) | CON 14 (+2) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +6, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 9 (9,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Living Vellum Cockatrice has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Living Vellum Cockatrice makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 12 (2d8 + 3) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Living Vellum Cockatrice unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 14 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 31 (9d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
