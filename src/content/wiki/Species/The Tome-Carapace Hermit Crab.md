# 🦁 The Tome-Carapace Hermit Crab
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “A beast with pages for scales and ink for venom. It does not eat your flesh; it rewrites your past until you were never born at all.”
— Scribe Justinian, Custodian of the Vaults

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Tome-Carapace Hermit Crab** (Large Beast (Construct), Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Grand Archive**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Scribe's Quill Lodge**.

A crustacean that inhabits abandoned spellbook bindings from dead archmages, using the hardbound iron covers as an impervious shell while its pincers slice paper and steel alike.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Grand Archive|The Grand Archive]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Scribe's Quill Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Tome-Carapace Hermit Crab establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Tome-Carapace Hermit Crab decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
