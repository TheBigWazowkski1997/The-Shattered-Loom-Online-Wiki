# 🦁 The Living Crucible Golem-Behemoth
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 14)*

> “Forged in the heart of celestial crucibles, these beasts are living engines of adamantine and perpetual flame.”
— Master Smith Thoradin

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Living Crucible Golem-Behemoth** (Huge Construct (Elemental), Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Eternal Craft**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Anvil-Hearth Lodge**.

A walking foundry furnace constructed from divine brass and slag. It shovels ore and broken weaponry into its central chest-furnace, exhaling showers of white-hot steel pellets from twin chimney-shoulders.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Eternal Craft|Plane of Eternal Craft]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Anvil-Hearth Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Living Crucible Golem-Behemoth establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Living Crucible Golem-Behemoth decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 17 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 180 (18d12 + 90)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 22 (+6) | DEX 16 (+3) | CON 20 (+5) | INT 10 (+0) | WIS 16 (+3) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +10, Wis +8
  * **Senses**: truesight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 18
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 14 (14,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +5
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Living Crucible Golem-Behemoth has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+5) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Living Crucible Golem-Behemoth makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 15 (3d10 + 6) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Living Crucible Golem-Behemoth unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 17 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 49 (14d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
