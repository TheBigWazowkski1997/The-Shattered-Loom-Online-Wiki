# 🦁 The Shackle-Carapace Arthropod
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Iron chains that hunt like serpents, and hounds that damp out the sound of your screams before they bite through your throat.”
— Warden-Adjutant Corryn

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Shackle-Carapace Arthropod** (Large Aberration (Construct), Lawful Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Prison Plane (The 32 Chambers)**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Shackle-Watch Post**.

A scuttling, crab-like construct formed from the melted chains of condemned sorcerers. It moves silently across the iron ceilings of the Prison Plane, dropping iron clamps onto spellcasters.

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Prison Plane (The 32 Chambers)|The Prison Plane (The 32 Chambers)]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Shackle-Watch Post]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Shackle-Carapace Arthropod establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Shackle-Carapace Arthropod decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
