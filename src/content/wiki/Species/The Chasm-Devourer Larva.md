# 🦁 The Chasm-Devourer Larva
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “An endless gullet lined with spinning teeth. It swallows reality whole, leaving only hollow vacuum in its wake.”
— Scout Kaelen the Scarred

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Chasm-Devourer Larva** (Huge Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Infinite Hunger**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Maw-Rim Camp**.

A segmented, burrowing worm 50 feet in length, possessing three concentric rings of rotating teeth and multiple acid glands. It bores through solid planar stone at terrifying speed, leaving tunnels lined with corrosive digestive fluids.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Infinite Hunger|Plane of Infinite Hunger]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Maw-Rim Camp]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Chasm-Devourer Larva establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Chasm-Devourer Larva decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
