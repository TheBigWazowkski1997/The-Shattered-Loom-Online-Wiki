# 🦁 The Blood-Tide Vrock Matriarch
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 13)*

> “Chaos does not breed pure strains. The things that crawl out of the abyssal pits are amalgams of horn, slime, and endless hunger.”
— Forge-Mistress Vespera Quick-Flint

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Blood-Tide Vrock Matriarch** (Huge Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Abyss**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Broken-Bone Redoubt**.

A diseased vulture-demon four times the size of a standard vrock. Its screech shatters physical glass and mortal eardrums, while its rotten plumage rains acidic toxic spores over entire battlefields.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Abyss|The Abyss]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Broken-Bone Redoubt]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Blood-Tide Vrock Matriarch establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Blood-Tide Vrock Matriarch decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 16 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 153 (17d12 + 68)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft., fly 60 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 20 (+5) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 18 (+4) | INT 10 (+0) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +9, Wis +7
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 17
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 13 (13,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +5
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Blood-Tide Vrock Matriarch has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+5) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Blood-Tide Vrock Matriarch makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +10 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 14 (3d10 + 5) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Blood-Tide Vrock Matriarch unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 16 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 45 (13d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
