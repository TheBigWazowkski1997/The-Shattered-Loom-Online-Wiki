# 🦁 The Mirage-Winged Gossamer Manticore
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Never trust a beast that shines like silver. The more beautiful its antlers, the deeper the thorny roots it will bury into your ribcage.”
— Lady Vaelen Sylph-Treader, Silver-Bough Lodge

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Mirage-Winged Gossamer Manticore** (Large Monstrosity (Fey), Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Feywild**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Silver-Bough Lodge**.

A graceful yet sadistic predator with the body of an emerald lion, wings of stained glass, and a tail crowned with crystalline needles that project disorienting auditory glamour before striking.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Feywild|The Feywild]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Silver-Bough Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Mirage-Winged Gossamer Manticore establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Mirage-Winged Gossamer Manticore decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
