# 🦁 The Gear-Toothed Inevitable Hound
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 11)*

> “Clockwork precision turned sadistic. It calculates your death to the microsecond before its pendulum blades ever swing.”
— Justiciar Marcus

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Gear-Toothed Inevitable Hound** (Large Construct, Lawful Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Shattered Law**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Measured Compass Guildhouse**.

A quadrupedal tracking automaton forged from brass and iron. It is programmed to hunt law-breakers across the Spire, calculating attack trajectories with cold mathematical perfection.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Shattered Law|Plane of Shattered Law]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Measured Compass Guildhouse]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Gear-Toothed Inevitable Hound establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Gear-Toothed Inevitable Hound decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 15 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 120 (15d10 + 45)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 18 (+4) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +7, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 11 (11,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Gear-Toothed Inevitable Hound has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Gear-Toothed Inevitable Hound makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 13 (2d10 + 4) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Gear-Toothed Inevitable Hound unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 15 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 38 (11d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
