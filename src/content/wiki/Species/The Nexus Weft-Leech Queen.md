# 🧬 The Nexus Weft-Leech Queen
### *Multiversal Species & Apex Predator Dossier*

> “She does not eat meat. She eats the golden strands holding the sky together.”
— Aelira the Weftkeeper

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A titanic, iridescent serpentine aberration that nests within the fractured rifts of the Nexus. Armed with maw-rings of diamond-sharp filament teeth, it feeds directly on ambient Loom-threads, unraveling spells and severing teleportation networks.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

Living along major planar leyline junctures, the Weft-Leech Queen acts as an ecosystem predator of magical energy. When magic users cast powerful spells in her presence, she senses the ripple, hunting down the caster to devour their magical essence.

---

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

*Huge Aberration, Neutral Evil*
___
* **Armor Class**: 17 (thread-woven armor)
* **Hit Points**: 175 (14d12 + 84)
* **Speed**: 30 ft., fly 40 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 20 (+5) | 18 (+4) | 22 (+6) | 16 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) |

* **Saving Throws**: Dex +8, Con +10, Wis +7
* **Damage Immunities**: force, psychic
* **Senses**: truesight 60 ft., blindsight 120 ft., passive Perception 17
* **Challenge**: 12 (8,400 XP)

#### Traits
* **Weft-Sever Aura**: Spells cast within 30 ft require DC 16 spellcasting check or spell slot is wasted with no effect, healing the queen for 10 HP.

#### Actions
* **Multiattack**: Two Filament-Bite attacks and one Thread-Lash.
* **Filament-Bite**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit*: 18 (3d8 + 5) piercing + 9 (2d8) force damage. Dispels one active spell on target.
* **Thread-Lash**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 20 ft. *Hit*: 14 (2d8 + 5) slashing damage, and target is tethered (cannot move more than 20 ft away).
