# 🦁 The Nexus Weft-Leech Queen
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 12)*

> “Where the tapestry broke, predators learned to eat the threads. If a beast severs your soul-thread, no resurrection spell in the multiverse will find you.”
— Kaelthar the Threadwarden

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Nexus Weft-Leech Queen** (Huge Aberration (Monstrosity), Neutral Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Fractured Nexus**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Hearth of the Spoor (Grand Central Lodge)**.

A bloated, lamprey-jawed horror that attaches itself to the underside of the Nexus Spire, siphoning the golden thread energy holding the city together and birthing swarms of hungry thread-mites.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Fractured Nexus|The Fractured Nexus]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Hearth of the Spoor (Grand Central Lodge)]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Nexus Weft-Leech Queen establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Nexus Weft-Leech Queen decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 16 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 144 (16d12 + 64)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 20 (+5) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 18 (+4) | INT 18 (+4) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 14 (+2)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +8, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: truesight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 12 (12,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Nexus Weft-Leech Queen has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Nexus Weft-Leech Queen makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 14 (3d10 + 5) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Nexus Weft-Leech Queen unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 16 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 42 (12d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
