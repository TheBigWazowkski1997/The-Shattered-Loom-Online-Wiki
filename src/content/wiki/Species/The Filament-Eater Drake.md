# 🧬 The Filament-Eater Drake
### *Multiversal Species & Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Its scales shimmer like spun gold, but touch them and your hand turns to paradoxical dust.”
— Kaelthar the Threadwarden

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A wingless drake native to the Fractured Nexus whose scales are formed from interwoven loom-filaments. It stalks magical conduits, exhaling paradoxical pulses that unravel physical armor and mystical barriers alike.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

Drakes hunt in pairs along planar drift currents. They prey on spelljammers and celestial creatures, using their disruptive breath to collapse astral sails and shield hulls.

---

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

*Large Aberration, Unaligned*
___
* **Armor Class**: 16 (woven scale)
* **Hit Points**: 142 (15d10 + 60)
* **Speed**: 40 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 20 (+5) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 8 (-1) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

* **Saving Throws**: Str +9, Con +8
* **Damage Resistances**: force; bludgeoning, piercing, and slashing from nonmagical attacks
* **Damage Immunities**: psychic
* **Challenge**: 10 (5,900 XP)

#### Actions
* **Multiattack**: One Bite and two Claws.
* **Bite**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 16 (2d10 + 5) piercing + 7 (2d6) force damage.
* **Claw**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft. *Hit*: 12 (2d6 + 5) slashing damage.
* **Paradox Breath (Recharge 5–6)**: 30-foot cone of unraveling energy. DC 16 Dex save: 45 (10d8) force damage (half on success).
