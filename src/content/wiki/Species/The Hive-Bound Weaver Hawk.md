# 🦁 The Hive-Bound Weaver Hawk
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 9)*

> “To fight one is to fight the entire ecosystem. Its mycelial threads connect every root, leaf, and hive in a single sovereign mind.”
— Arch-Weaver Thelar

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Hive-Bound Weaver Hawk** (Large Beast (Monstrosity), Lawful Good) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Unity**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Threaded Haven**.

A raptor that hunts in synchronized flocks connected by an empathic psychic network. Its feathers shimmer with harmonic golden filaments, blinding prey with coordinated aerial maneuvers.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Unity|Plane of Unity]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Threaded Haven]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Hive-Bound Weaver Hawk establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Hive-Bound Weaver Hawk decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 14 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 91 (13d10 + 26)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft., fly 60 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 16 (+3) | DEX 16 (+3) | CON 14 (+2) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +6, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 9 (9,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Hive-Bound Weaver Hawk has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Hive-Bound Weaver Hawk makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +7 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 12 (2d10 + 3) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Hive-Bound Weaver Hawk unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 14 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 31 (9d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
