# 🦁 The Midas-Carapace Scorpion
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “A beast whose scales are pure molten gold will tempt any mortal fool. But touch its hide and your hands will turn to heavy, frozen bullion.”
— Merchant-Baron Vane

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Midas-Carapace Scorpion** (Large Monstrosity, Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Avarice**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Golden-Snare Salon**.

A predatory scorpion that crawls through Grelthor's vault vaults. Its claws are heavy ingots of gold that crush plate armor, and its stinger injects an auric venom that calcifies blood vessels into pure gold wire.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Avarice|Plane of Avarice]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Golden-Snare Salon]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Midas-Carapace Scorpion establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Midas-Carapace Scorpion decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
