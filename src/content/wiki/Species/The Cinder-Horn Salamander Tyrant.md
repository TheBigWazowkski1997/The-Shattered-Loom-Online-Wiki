# 🦁 The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “If its blood doesn't melt your spearhead, you haven't struck deep enough into its magma core.”
— Torvald Blood-Tracer, Grand Huntmaster

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant** (Huge Elemental (Monstrosity), Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Fire**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Cinder-Spur Bastion**.

An ancient, crested fire-lizard that swims through the molten rivers of the Everblaze Expanse. Its horns are superheated rods of natural tungsten, and its tongue can snare prey from 30 feet away, cooking them in seconds.

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Fire|Plane of Fire]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Cinder-Spur Bastion]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
