# 🦁 The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 12)*

> “If its blood doesn't melt your spearhead, you haven't struck deep enough into its magma core.”
— Torvald Blood-Tracer, Grand Huntmaster

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant** (Huge Elemental (Monstrosity), Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Fire**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Cinder-Spur Bastion**.

An ancient, crested fire-lizard that swims through the molten rivers of the Everblaze Expanse. Its horns are superheated rods of natural tungsten, and its tongue can snare prey from 30 feet away, cooking them in seconds.

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Fire|Plane of Fire]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Cinder-Spur Bastion]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 16 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 144 (16d12 + 64)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft., swim 50 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 20 (+5) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 18 (+4) | INT 10 (+0) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +8, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 12 (12,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 14 (3d10 + 5) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Cinder-Horn Salamander Tyrant unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 16 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 42 (12d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
