# 🧬 The Non-Euclidean Trench-Stalker
### *Multiversal Species & Apex Predator Dossier*

> “You strike where its chest appears to be, and your blade passes through empty air. It strikes from behind you without turning its head.”
— Forge-Mistress Vespera

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A predatory monstrosity with five multi-jointed legs that intersect three-dimensional space at impossible angles. It phases effortlessly through solid stone and underwater trenches, bending local reality to ensnare explorers in paradoxical loops.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

Prowling the architectural faults of sunken Ry'leh, the Trench-Stalker ambushes prey by stepping through structural angles less than 90 degrees. It drags victims into non-Euclidean voids where Euclidean direction ceases to exist.

---

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

*Large Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
___
* **Armor Class**: 17 (spatial distortion)
* **Hit Points**: 168 (16d10 + 80)
* **Speed**: 45 ft., climb 45 ft., swim 45 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 20 (+5) | 18 (+4) | 20 (+5) | 15 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 10 (+0) |

* **Saving Throws**: Dex +8, Con +9
* **Damage Immunities**: psychic, force
* **Condition Immunities**: grappled, restrained, prone
* **Senses**: blindsight 90 ft., passive Perception 16
* **Languages**: Deep Speech
* **Challenge**: 12 (8,400 XP)

#### Traits
* **Incorporeal Spatial Phase**: Can move through creatures and objects as difficult terrain. Takes 5 (1d10) force damage if ending turn inside solid matter.
* **Paradox Angles**: Attack rolls against it have disadvantage unless attacker uses blindsight.

#### Actions
* **Multiattack**: Three Spatial Talon strikes.
* **Spatial Talon**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 10 ft. *Hit*: 15 (2d10 + 4) slashing + 9 (2d8) force damage.
* **Reality Fracture (Recharge 5–6)**: 20-foot cube. DC 17 Cha save: 36 (8d8) force damage, teleported 30 ft in random direction, knocked prone.
