# 🦁 The Hellfire Brimstone Juggernaut
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 15)*

> “Every beast here has a contract stamped into its hide and brimstone in its lungs. Kill it cleanly, or the debt passes to your descendants.”
— Inquisitor Sylas the Blind

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Hellfire Brimstone Juggernaut** (Huge Fiend (Devil), Lawful Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Nine Hells**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Iron-Chain Keep**.

A terrifying war-beast bred in the armories of Dis. It resembles a quadrupedal rhinoceros clad in thick iron-and-bone plate that burns with hellfire, exhaling clouds of choking sulfur and molten copper.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells|The Nine Hells]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Iron-Chain Keep]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Hellfire Brimstone Juggernaut establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Hellfire Brimstone Juggernaut decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 17 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 190 (19d12 + 95)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 22 (+6) | DEX 16 (+3) | CON 20 (+5) | INT 10 (+0) | WIS 16 (+3) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +10, Wis +8
  * **Senses**: truesight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 18
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 15 (15,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +5
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Hellfire Brimstone Juggernaut has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+5) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Hellfire Brimstone Juggernaut makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 15 (3d10 + 6) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Hellfire Brimstone Juggernaut unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 17 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 52 (15d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
