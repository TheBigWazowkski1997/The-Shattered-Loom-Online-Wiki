# 🦁 The Star-Spawn Spore-Calamity
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 15)*

> “It is not a beast that walks upon the earth; it is a tumor of living thought that has breached the Loom to unravel existence itself.”
— Grand Inquisitor Sylas the Blind

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Star-Spawn Spore-Calamity** (Gargantuan Aberration, Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Far Realm**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Edge of Sanity Outpost**.

A catastrophic fungal-astronomic horror that drifts through deep void space. Its body is pitted with meteor craters that discharge cosmic spores, distorting gravity and decomposing biological tissue into gelatinous sludge.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Far Realm|The Far Realm]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Edge of Sanity Outpost]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Star-Spawn Spore-Calamity establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Star-Spawn Spore-Calamity decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 17 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 190 (19d20 + 95)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 22 (+6) | DEX 16 (+3) | CON 20 (+5) | INT 20 (+5) | WIS 16 (+3) | CHA 16 (+3)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +10, Wis +8
  * **Senses**: truesight 60 ft., darkvision 120 ft., passive Perception 18
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 15 (15,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +5
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Star-Spawn Spore-Calamity has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+5) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Star-Spawn Spore-Calamity makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +11 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 15 (3d10 + 6) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Star-Spawn Spore-Calamity unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 17 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 52 (15d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
