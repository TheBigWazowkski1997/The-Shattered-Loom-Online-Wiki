# 🧬 The Star-Spawn Spore-Calamity
### *Multiversal Species & Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Do not inhale near the violet bell-stalks. The spores do not rot your lungs—they grow new eyes inside them.”
— Huntsman Torvald Blood-Tracer

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A grotesque, bell-shaped fungal aberration native to the alien landscapes of the Far Realm. Covered in weeping spore vents that release choking violet dust, it calcifies living nervous tissue into brittle alien coral.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

Spore-Calamities grow at the impact sites of meteors from the deep void. They transform terrestrial biomes into fungal wastelands, colonizing local beasts and turning them into mindless spore-thralls.

---

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

*Huge Aberration, Chaotic Evil*
___
* **Armor Class**: 16 (rubbery fibrous flesh)
* **Hit Points**: 241 (21d12 + 105)
* **Speed**: 30 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 22 (+6) | 10 (+0) | 20 (+5) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 10 (+0) |

* **Saving Throws**: Con +10, Wis +9
* **Damage Immunities**: poison, psychic
* **Condition Immunities**: charmed, frightened, poisoned
* **Senses**: blindsight 120 ft., passive Perception 19
* **Challenge**: 15 (13,000 XP)

#### Traits
* **Alien Spore Cloud**: Creatures starting turn within 20 ft must make DC 18 Con save. Failed save: 14 (4d6) poison + 7 (2d6) psychic damage; infected with spore-blight (lose 1d6 Lucidity hourly).

#### Actions
* **Multiattack**: Three Spore-Lash attacks.
* **Spore-Lash**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +11 to hit, reach 15 ft. *Hit*: 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning + 9 (2d8) poison damage.
* **Cataclysmic Spore Eruption (Recharge 5–6)**: 50-foot radius burst. DC 18 Con save: 54 (12d8) poison + 18 (4d8) psychic damage. Target blinded and incapacitated for 1 round.
