# 🦁 The Cyclopean Starlight Octopod
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Do not look into the angles of its skull. The geometry here does not conform to three dimensions, and your sanity will spill into the brine.”

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Cyclopean Starlight Octopod** (Huge Aberration, Chaotic Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **City of Ry'leh**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Drowned Lantern Lodge**.

A predatory cephalopod that crawls through the cyclopean arches of the Dreaming Spires. Its skin is studded with thirty-six functioning human eyes, each weeping glowing starlight brine that burns mortal minds.

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/City of Ry'leh|City of Ry'leh]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Drowned Lantern Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Cyclopean Starlight Octopod establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Cyclopean Starlight Octopod decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
