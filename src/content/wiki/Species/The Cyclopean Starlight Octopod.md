# 🧬 The Cyclopean Starlight Octopod
### *Multiversal Species & Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Its skin does not reflect the ocean; it reflects stars that burned out before the gods were born.”
— Grumm Stone-Shoulder

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A colossal cephalopod monstrosity whose skin mimics an inverted, frozen starscape. Instead of a natural beak, its core opens into a miniature gravitational vacuum that extinguishes all light and tears physical objects apart at the subatomic level.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

Stalking the black trenches surrounding the submerged ruins of Ry'leh, this octopod uses its light-devouring aura to plunge entire dive expeditions into absolute sensory deprivation before dragging divers into its singularity maw.

---

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

*Huge Aberration, Neutral Evil*
___
* **Armor Class**: 16 (natural armor)
* **Hit Points**: 195 (17d12 + 85)
* **Speed**: 20 ft., swim 50 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|:---:|
| 22 (+6) | 16 (+3) | 20 (+5) | 17 (+3) | 16 (+3) | 12 (+1) |

* **Saving Throws**: Dex +8, Con +10, Wis +8
* **Damage Resistances**: radiant, necrotic; bludgeoning and slashing from nonmagical attacks
* **Damage Immunities**: psychic
* **Senses**: darkvision 120 ft., truesight 30 ft., passive Perception 18
* **Languages**: Deep Speech, telepathy 120 ft.
* **Challenge**: 13 (10,000 XP)

#### Traits
* **Light-Devouring Aura**: Nonmagical light is extinguished within 40 feet. Magical light of 3rd level or lower is dispelled.
* **Constricting Grasp**: Has ten arms; can grapple up to four targets simultaneously.

#### Actions
* **Multiattack**: Three Barbed Arm strikes and one Singularity Maw pull.
* **Barbed Arm**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +11 to hit, reach 20 ft. *Hit*: 17 (2d10 + 6) slashing + 7 (2d6) cold damage.
* **Singularity Maw**: Draws grappled target to center. Deals 28 (4d10 + 6) force damage (DC 18 Str save or gear crushed).
* **Star-Cold Ink Cloud (Recharge 5–6)**: 40-foot cloud. DC 18 Con save: 33 (6d10) cold damage and blinded.
