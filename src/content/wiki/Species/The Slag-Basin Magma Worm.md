# 🦁 The Slag-Basin Magma Worm
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 11)*

> “If its blood doesn't melt your spearhead, you haven't struck deep enough into its magma core.”
— Torvald Blood-Tracer, Grand Huntmaster

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Slag-Basin Magma Worm** (Huge Monstrosity, Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Fire**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Cinder-Spur Bastion**.

A thick, armored annelid that bores through the volcanic crust near the City of Brass, digesting raw basalt and excreting rivers of liquified copper and gold.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Fire|Plane of Fire]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Cinder-Spur Bastion]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Slag-Basin Magma Worm establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Slag-Basin Magma Worm decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 15 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 120 (15d12 + 45)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft., burrow 30 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 18 (+4) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +7, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 11 (11,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Slag-Basin Magma Worm has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Slag-Basin Magma Worm makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 13 (3d10 + 4) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Slag-Basin Magma Worm unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 15 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 38 (11d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
