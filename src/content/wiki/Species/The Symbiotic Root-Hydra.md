# 🦁 The Symbiotic Root-Hydra
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “To fight one is to fight the entire ecosystem. Its mycelial threads connect every root, leaf, and hive in a single sovereign mind.”
— Arch-Weaver Thelar

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Symbiotic Root-Hydra** (Huge Plant (Monstrosity), Lawful Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Unity**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Threaded Haven**.

A multi-headed serpentine entity grown from ancient oak roots and living vines. When one head is severed, two new root-tendrils weave together in seconds through shared cellular resonance.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Unity|Plane of Unity]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Threaded Haven]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Symbiotic Root-Hydra establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Symbiotic Root-Hydra decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
