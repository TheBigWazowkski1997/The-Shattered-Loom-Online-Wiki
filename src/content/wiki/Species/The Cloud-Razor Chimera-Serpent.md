# 🦁 The Cloud-Razor Chimera-Serpent
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “The sky here has no bottom. If you miss your harpoon strike, you have four thousand feet of freefall to contemplate your error.”
— Zephyr-Captain Joshua Drake, Cloud-Anchor Eyrie

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Cloud-Razor Chimera-Serpent** (Huge Elemental (Monstrosity), Chaotic Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Air**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Cloud-Anchor Eyrie**.

A winged aerial viper that blends invisibly with cumulus clouds. Its scales are edged like surgical razors, slashing diving prey while discharging static bursts from its dual forked tongues.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Air|Plane of Air]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Cloud-Anchor Eyrie]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Cloud-Razor Chimera-Serpent establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Cloud-Razor Chimera-Serpent decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
