# 🦁 The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 11)*

> “A beast whose scales are pure molten gold will tempt any mortal fool. But touch its hide and your hands will turn to heavy, frozen bullion.”
— Merchant-Baron Vane

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk** (Large Monstrosity, Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Avarice**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Golden-Snare Salon**.

An eight-legged reptilian beast whose scales have absorbed centuries of pure auric pressure, turning into solid, overlapping gold plates. Its gaze does not turn flesh to stone, but transmutes it into solid, screaming gold.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Avarice|Plane of Avarice]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Golden-Snare Salon]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 15 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 120 (15d10 + 45)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 18 (+4) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +7, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 11 (11,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 13 (2d10 + 4) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 15 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 38 (11d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
