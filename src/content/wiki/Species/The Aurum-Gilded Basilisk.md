# 🦁 The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “A beast whose scales are pure molten gold will tempt any mortal fool. But touch its hide and your hands will turn to heavy, frozen bullion.”
— Merchant-Baron Vane

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk** (Large Monstrosity, Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Avarice**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Golden-Snare Salon**.

An eight-legged reptilian beast whose scales have absorbed centuries of pure auric pressure, turning into solid, overlapping gold plates. Its gaze does not turn flesh to stone, but transmutes it into solid, screaming gold.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Avarice|Plane of Avarice]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Golden-Snare Salon]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Aurum-Gilded Basilisk decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
