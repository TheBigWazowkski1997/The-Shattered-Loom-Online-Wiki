# 🦁 The Regret-Gorged Behemoth
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “It mimics the face of your dead companion, weeping with their voice while its glass spines prepare to pierce your lungs.”
— Memory-Keeper Selene

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Regret-Gorged Behemoth** (Huge Monstrosity, Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Fragmented Memories**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Shard-Cairn Outpost**.

A four-legged armored beast that consumes the petrified memories scattered across the Shardlands. Its hide is an impenetrable shell of compressed mirror-quartz that reflects physical attacks back at attackers.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Fragmented Memories|Plane of Fragmented Memories]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Shard-Cairn Outpost]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Regret-Gorged Behemoth establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Regret-Gorged Behemoth decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
