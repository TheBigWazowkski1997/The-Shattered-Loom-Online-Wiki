# 🦁 The Thorn-Horned Primeval Moose
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Primal perfection without civilized mercy. It is the apex predator of the ancient wild, untouched by mortal gods or planar decay.”
— Hunter Bran of the Verdant Canopy

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Thorn-Horned Primeval Moose** (Huge Beast (Monstrosity), Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Wild Balance**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Verdant Canopy Lodge**.

A colossal cervid standing 22 feet tall, crowned with sweeping antlers composed of petrified blackthorn branches. Its hooves shake the ground, and its roar causes predatory vines to strangle pursuers.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Wild Balance|Plane of Wild Balance]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Verdant Canopy Lodge]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Thorn-Horned Primeval Moose establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Thorn-Horned Primeval Moose decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
