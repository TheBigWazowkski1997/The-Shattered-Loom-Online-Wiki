# 🦁 The Axiom-Fracture Clockwork Chimera
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Clockwork precision turned sadistic. It calculates your death to the microsecond before its pendulum blades ever swing.”
— Justiciar Marcus

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Axiom-Fracture Clockwork Chimera** (Large Construct, Lawful Neutral) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Shattered Law**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Measured Compass Guildhouse**.

A biomechanical chimera forged from polished brass, interlocking clockwork springs, and fractured law-stones. Its gears tick with rhythmic precision, accelerating its speed or freezing local time when it pounces.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Shattered Law|Plane of Shattered Law]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Measured Compass Guildhouse]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Axiom-Fracture Clockwork Chimera establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Axiom-Fracture Clockwork Chimera decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
