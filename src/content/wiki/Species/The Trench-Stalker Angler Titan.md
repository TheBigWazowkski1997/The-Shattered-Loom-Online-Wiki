# 🦁 The Trench-Stalker Angler Titan
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Trench-Stalker Angler Titan** (Huge Monstrosity (Aberration), Neutral Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Secrets**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Silent Pearl Pavilion**.

A terrifying deep-sea behemoth with a bioluminescent lure that mimics the voices of drowned kings and long-dead confidants, drawing travelers into its cavernous, needle-lined gullet.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Secrets|Plane of Secrets]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Silent Pearl Pavilion]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Trench-Stalker Angler Titan establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Trench-Stalker Angler Titan decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
