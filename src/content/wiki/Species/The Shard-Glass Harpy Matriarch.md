# 🦁 The Shard-Glass Harpy Matriarch
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “It mimics the face of your dead companion, weeping with their voice while its glass spines prepare to pierce your lungs.”
— Memory-Keeper Selene

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Shard-Glass Harpy Matriarch** (Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Fragmented Memories**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Shard-Cairn Outpost**.

A winged predator with plumage of jagged mirror-glass and talons of honed quartz. Her screech carries the voices of a victim's lost loved ones, paralyzing them with regret before she dives.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Fragmented Memories|Plane of Fragmented Memories]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Shard-Cairn Outpost]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Shard-Glass Harpy Matriarch establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Shard-Glass Harpy Matriarch decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
