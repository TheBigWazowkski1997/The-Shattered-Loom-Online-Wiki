# 🦁 The Carrion-Gorged Chasm Hyena Alpha
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “An endless gullet lined with spinning teeth. It swallows reality whole, leaving only hollow vacuum in its wake.”
— Scout Kaelen the Scarred

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Carrion-Gorged Chasm Hyena Alpha** (Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Infinite Hunger**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Maw-Rim Camp**.

A mutated pack-leader that stalks the chasm edges, feasting on whatever falls into the spiral. Its jaws can crack adamantine bones, and its laughing howl sends tremors of panic through entire hunting expeditions.

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Infinite Hunger|Plane of Infinite Hunger]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Maw-Rim Camp]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Carrion-Gorged Chasm Hyena Alpha establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Carrion-Gorged Chasm Hyena Alpha decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
