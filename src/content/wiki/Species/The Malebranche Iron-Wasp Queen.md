# 🦁 The Malebranche Iron-Wasp Queen
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier*

> “Every beast here has a contract stamped into its hide and brimstone in its lungs. Kill it cleanly, or the debt passes to your descendants.”

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

A **The Malebranche Iron-Wasp Queen** (Huge Fiend (Devil), Lawful Evil) is an apex quarry monster native to **The Nine Hells**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Iron-Chain Keep**.

A terrifying, mechanized insectoid devil that nests inside the furnace-towers of Dis. Its stinger is an iron needle inscribed with binding legal damnations that paralyze victims before dragging them to the hives.

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells|The Nine Hells]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Iron-Chain Keep]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Malebranche Iron-Wasp Queen establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Malebranche Iron-Wasp Queen decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.
