# 🦁 The Adamantine-Homed Gorgon Behemoth
### *Multiversal Species & Planar Apex Predator Dossier (Challenge Rating 11)*

> “The bedrock has teeth here. When you hear the amethyst geodes singing beneath your boots, draw adamantine steel or be crushed into gravel.”
— Grumm Stone-Shoulder, Deep-Delver Master

---

## 📖 Overview & Anatomical Classification

![The Adamantine-Homed Gorgon Behemoth exhaling a cloud of petrifying vapor in an amethyst-veined subterranean delve.](/The-Shattered-Loom-Online-Wiki/images/adamantine_gorgon_behemoth.jpg)


A **The Adamantine-Homed Gorgon Behemoth** (Huge Monstrosity (Elemental), Unaligned) is an apex quarry monster native to **Plane of Earth**, cataloged by the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor** and hunted through **The Iron-Root Delve**.

A primeval iron bull three times the size of a mortal ox, clad in overlapping plates of natural adamantine ore. Its breath is an ash-gray cloud of petrifying vapor, and its horns can gore through granite battlements.

---

## 🌲 Lore, Nature & Planar Ecology

* **Native Plane**: [[Planes of Existence/Plane of Earth|Plane of Earth]]
* **Regional Hunting Lodge**: [[Custom Rules/Planar Bestiary & Hunting Lodges Compendium|The Iron-Root Delve]]
* **Predatory Behavior**: As an apex creature of its realm, the The Adamantine-Homed Gorgon Behemoth establishes vast territorial hunting grounds. It exerts immense ecological pressure on local planar fauna, utilizing ambient planar energies to ambush prey and defend its nesting lair.
* **Harvest Window & Planar Decay**: Due to ambient planar radiation, a slain The Adamantine-Homed Gorgon Behemoth decays within 1 hour unless preserved with a *Lodge Salting Barrel* or alchemical stasis canisters.

## ⚔️ Combat Statistics (D&D 5e)

* **Combat Statistics**:
  * **Armor Class**: 15 (natural armor)
  * **Hit Points**: 120 (15d12 + 45)
  * **Speed**: 40 ft., burrow 30 ft.
  * **Abilities**: STR 18 (+4) | DEX 18 (+4) | CON 16 (+3) | INT 8 (-1) | WIS 14 (+2) | CHA 10 (+0)
  * **Saving Throws**: Con +7, Wis +6
  * **Senses**: darkvision 60 ft., passive Perception 16
  * **Languages**: Understands native planar dialect; telepathy 60 ft. (or unaligned)
  * **Challenge**: 11 (11,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +4
* **Signature Combat Traits**:
  * **Planar Apex Resilience**: The Adamantine-Homed Gorgon Behemoth has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects native to its home plane.
  * **Predatory Instinct**: Adds its proficiency bonus (+4) to initiative rolls and cannot be surprised while conscious.
* **Combat Actions & Multiattack**:
  * **Multiattack**: The Adamantine-Homed Gorgon Behemoth makes three attacks: two with its primary natural weapons and one crushing strike.
  * **Primary Strike**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 13 (3d10 + 4) slashing/piercing damage plus 7 (2d6) elemental/force damage.
  * **Crushing Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: The Adamantine-Homed Gorgon Behemoth unleashes a planar surge in a 30-foot cone/line. Targets must make a DC 15 Dexterity or Constitution save, taking 38 (11d6) damage on a failed save, or half as much on a success.
