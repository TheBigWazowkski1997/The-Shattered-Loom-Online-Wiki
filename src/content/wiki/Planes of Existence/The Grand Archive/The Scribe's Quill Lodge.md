# 🏡 The Scribe's Quill Lodge
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — The Grand Archive*

> *"The wilderness of The Grand Archive will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Archivist Kaelen the Tracker (Kalashtar Hunter-Scholar, CR 11)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Scribe's Quill Lodge** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/The Grand Archive/The Grand Archive|The Grand Archive]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Outer perimeter of the Whispering Stacks, Tier IV
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Archivist Kaelen the Tracker (Kalashtar Hunter-Scholar, CR 11)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A quiet, book-lined lodge where weapons are muffled in velvet and monster hide is tanned on stone slates using ancient parchment presses. Trackers speak in hushed whispers to avoid alerting living spell-beasts.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of The Grand Archive:

* **Processing & Salting**: Scriptorium Tannery, Memory-Drying Shelves, Ink-Blood Press, Silence Wards.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
