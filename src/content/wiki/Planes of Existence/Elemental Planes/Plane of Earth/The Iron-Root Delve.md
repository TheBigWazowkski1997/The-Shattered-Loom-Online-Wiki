# 🏡 The Iron-Root Delve
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Earth*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Earth will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Grumm Stone-Shoulder (Dwarf Deep-Stalker, CR 11)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Iron-Root Delve** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane of Earth/Plane of Earth The Unyielding Depths|Plane of Earth]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Subterranean geode cavern 3 miles beneath the Obsidian Cradle
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Grumm Stone-Shoulder (Dwarf Deep-Stalker, CR 11)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: Hewn directly into a cavern of purple amethyst crystals and adamantine veins. Heavy steam hammers echo rhythmically as goliath trackers crush monster scales into armor rivets.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Earth:

* **Processing & Salting**: Pneumatic Stone Crushers, Petrified Bone Tannery, Geode Vault, Tremor-Compass Workshop.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
