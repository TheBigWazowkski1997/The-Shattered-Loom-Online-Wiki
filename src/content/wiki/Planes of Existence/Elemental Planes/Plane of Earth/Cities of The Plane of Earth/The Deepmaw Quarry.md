Deepmaw Quarry
**Description**:A mining city perched on the edge of a massive chasm that seems to descend infinitely. It is a dangerous but lucrative hub for miners and treasure hunters.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Chasm’s Edge**: A series of mining platforms that descend into the abyss.
	* **The Shadowforged Guild**: A group specializing in crafting items from abyssal stone.
	* **The Maw Shrine**: A temple dedicated to appeasing the spirits of the deep.
	

	

**Deepmaw Quarry** is a vast, labyrinthine mining settlement deep within the **Plane of Earth**, carved into a colossal fissure that plunges miles into the heart of the plane. Named for its resemblance to a massive stone maw gaping into the depths, Deepmaw is both a place of unrelenting labor and untold danger. Rich veins of rare gems, elemental cores, and ancient crystalline formations make the quarry one of the most coveted—and contested—locations in the plane.
However, the depths of **Deepmaw** hide more than treasure. As miners delve further, they uncover remnants of forgotten civilizations, sentient stone creatures, and ominous forces that slumber far below. The deeper one descends into the quarry, the thinner the boundary between the **Plane of Earth** and other, darker realms becomes, warping the stone and bringing forth strange anomalies.
Deepmaw is a place of grit, greed, and secrets—a harsh and unforgiving settlement where survival and fortune hinge on both skill and luck.

---

## **Overview**

* **Population**: ~25,000 (Earth Genasi 30%, Dwarves 20%, Salamanders 15%, Xorn 10%, Gargoyles 10%, Azer 5%, Other 10%).
* **Government**: Deepmaw Quarry is governed by a **Mining Collective**, a coalition of guild leaders, merchants, and overseers. The most powerful faction is the **Deepmaw Syndicate**, a ruthless organization that controls access to the richest veins and enforces its rule with an iron fist.
* **Alignment**: Neutral with strong Lawful Evil tendencies, reflecting its focus on profit, control, and survival.
* **Exports**: Raw gemstones, refined magical crystals, elemental cores, precious metals, and enchanted obsidian.
* **Imports**: Food, tools, enchanted mining equipment, magical wards, and planar mercenaries for protection.

---

## **Geography and Layout**

Deepmaw Quarry is structured in layers, carved downward into the colossal fissure that gives the quarry its name. Each level—called a **"Ring"**—is a mix of mining operations, industrial facilities, and residential areas, connected by **stone elevators**, spiraling tunnels, and narrow switchback paths. The deeper levels are treacherous, unstable, and plagued by both natural and unnatural threats.
The quarry is divided into **five major Rings**, descending progressively deeper into the fissure.

---

### **1\. The Stone’s Cradle (Surface Ring)**

* **Description**: The **Stone’s Cradle** is the uppermost layer of Deepmaw Quarry, located at the fissure’s surface. This level serves as the hub of trade, administration, and access to the outside world. Massive lifts and reinforced ramps lead into the quarry’s depths, while merchants and laborers bustle about the stone-paved streets.
* **Architecture**: Structures are made of dark basalt and enchanted stone, built to withstand the constant tremors of the fissure. Sturdy walls surround the surface entrance, and massive ramps lead into the depths.
* **Unique Features**:
	* **The Maw Gate**: A fortified stone gate that serves as the primary entrance to the quarry. It is guarded day and night by mercenaries and golems.
	* **The Overseer’s Hold**: Headquarters of the **Mining Collective**, a towering fortress-like building carved from obsidian. Guild leaders and syndicate enforcers operate from here.
	* **The Smelter’s Row**: A line of massive forges and smelting facilities where raw ore and gems are refined for trade. The air is thick with heat and the clang of industry.
	* **The Dust Market**: A sprawling marketplace where miners, merchants, and travelers trade in tools, raw materials, and forbidden goods smuggled up from the depths.

---

### **2\. The Ember Ring (Industrial Layer)**

* **Description**: The **Ember Ring** houses Deepmaw’s industrial and smithing facilities, where Azer and dwarven artisans craft tools, weapons, and enchanted mining equipment. It is lit by glowing magma channels and humming crystal lanterns, with constant noise from forges and workshops.
* **Architecture**: Broad tunnels and chambers reinforced with obsidian and metal. Open magma pits serve as both fuel and light sources, with bridges crisscrossing the glowing channels.
* **Unique Features**:
	* **The Molten Core Forge**: The largest forge in the Ember Ring, powered by a bound fire elemental. It produces enchanted tools capable of cutting through nearly any material.
	* **The Ember Foundries**: A series of workshops where machinery, golems, and mining constructs are built and repaired. Sparks and molten slag fill the air.
	* **The Chain Lifts**: Enormous, enchanted elevators made of steel and stone that carry workers and materials between the quarry’s levels. They creak ominously under heavy loads.
	* **The Red Guildhall**: Headquarters of the **Deepmaw Syndicate**, where enforcers oversee operations and impose ruthless order on the workers.

---

### **3\. The Vein Tunnels (Middle Mines)**

* **Description**: The **Vein Tunnels** are the heart of Deepmaw’s mining operations, where massive veins of rare gems and precious metals are extracted. This layer is a sprawling network of tunnels, shafts, and quarries that echo with the sounds of pickaxes, steam-driven drills, and heavy machinery.
* **Architecture**: Rough-hewn tunnels supported by stone beams and glowing crystal sconces. Some areas have been smoothed into massive chambers where machinery operates, while others remain wild and unstable.
* **Unique Features**:
	* **The Crystal Veins**: Massive seams of glowing gems that pulse faintly with elemental energy. Miners harvest these for use in enchantments and magical artifacts.
	* **The Collapsing Caverns**: Dangerous, partially mined tunnels prone to cave-ins and tremors. Many miners go missing here, blamed on **Xorn scavengers** and earth-shaping elementals.
	* **The Shardfall Reservoir**: A chamber where loose gems and rubble collect in massive piles. Scavengers and thieves often raid this area, looking for overlooked treasures.
	* **The Glowstone Guilds**: A coalition of miner clans and artificers who oversee operations, though their control is often contested by the **Deepmaw Syndicate**.

---

### **4\. The Shattered Maw (Lower Depths)**

* **Description**: The **Shattered Maw** is a chaotic, dangerous layer where mining operations have breached into unstable territory. Strange crystalline growths twist through the stone, warping tunnels and creating shimmering anomalies. Elementals and hostile creatures emerge from fissures, attacking miners and patrols.
* **Architecture**: The tunnels here are fractured and irregular, with jagged crystal formations jutting from the walls. Some chambers glow eerily with unnatural light.
* **Unique Features**:
	* **The Fracture Rift**: A massive crack in the quarry’s center, said to descend endlessly into the earth. It radiates strange energy, and miners report hearing whispers when near it.
	* **The Echo Crystals**: Veins of luminous crystals that hum with a dissonant, resonant sound. Interacting with them can trigger dangerous magical surges.
	* **The Hollow Warrens**: Labyrinthine tunnels overrun with earth elementals, xorn, and gargoyles. Gangs of scavengers compete to claim these areas.
	* **The Stonebound Relics**: Ancient ruins carved into the lower tunnels, predating the quarry. Scholars believe these ruins are tied to an extinct planar civilization.

---

### **5\. The Abyssal Roots (The Deep Dark)**

* **Description**: The **Abyssal Roots** are the lowest and least explored layer of Deepmaw Quarry, a pitch-black void of stone, anomalies, and unfathomable depths. It is said to be where the **Plane of Earth** touches realms of darker, corrupted energies. No proper mining occurs here—only daring explorers, mercenaries, and cultists venture into its maw.
* **Architecture**: Gaping fissures and caverns stretch into darkness, illuminated only by faint crystal glows and magma flows far below. Strange, alien structures made of black stone dot the deepest chambers.
* **Unique Features**:
	* **The Howling Fissure**: A massive chasm that seems to breathe, exhaling winds laced with whispers of madness.
	* **The Obsidian Idol**: A colossal, alien statue buried in stone, radiating dark energy. Cults have begun to form around it.
	* **The Rooted Spires**: Towering stone formations that seem to pulse with life. Strange creatures and elemental horrors nest within them.
	* **The Abyssal Gate**: A massive, rune-covered portal partially buried in the cavern wall. It flickers faintly, as if waiting to be opened.

* **Role**: Mysterious entity within the Abyssal Roots

## **The Dao Gem-Mines & The Dismal Monopolies**

While the Mining Collective nominally governs surface trade, the richest and most perilous subterranean veins in the lower rings are claimed by the **Dao**—the cruel, avaricious earth genies who have expanded their tentacles from the Great Dismal Delve into Deepmaw Quarry. 

The Dao operate through sovereign mining enclaves known as the **Seven-Vein Concessions**, utilizing enchanted stone-shaping magic, bound earth elementals, and indebted mortal labor to strip mountains of diamonds, jacinth, and raw elemental earth. The undisputed mistress of these operations is **Khanum Zoraya of the Seven Veins**, a noble Dao warlord whose gem-cutting monopolies dictate the price of precious stones across three planes.

## **Shops of Deepmaw Quarry**

### **1. The Geode Vault**

**Specialty**: Raw & Cut Planar Gemstones, Elemental Earth Cores, and Ioun Stones  
**Location**: The Stone’s Cradle, set into a reinforced granite bunker near the Maw Gate.  
**Owner**: **Thurgan Stone-Eye**, an elderly mountain dwarf whose right eye was replaced with a rotating faceted sapphire that detects gem purity.  
**Description**: The vault sparkles with hypnotic brilliance. Shelves of velvet-lined stone trays hold uncut diamonds the size of fists, pulsing fire-rubies, and deep green emeralds glowing with earth-magic. Thurgan sits behind an iron-banded desk, weighing gems on enchanted brass scales.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Stone of Good Luck (Luckstone)** (*Uncommon*, 800 gp): As per the DMG. Adds +1 to ability checks and saving throws.
* **Elemental Core (Earth)** (*Rare*, 1,200 gp): A dense geode vibrating with elemental earth; used to craft constructs or reinforce magical foundations.
* **Diamond (Revivify/Resurrection grade)** (500 gp to 1,000 gp): Flawless spell components harvested from the Vein Tunnels.
* **Gem of Brightness** (*Uncommon*, 750 gp): As per the DMG (50 charges).
* **Ioun Stone of Protection** (*Rare*, 3,500 gp): Grants +1 to AC while orbiting the head.

**Unique Services**:

* **Gem Resonance Attunement**: Thurgan can attune a raw gemstone to an elemental damage type (acid, bludgeoning, or thunder) for 250 gp, allowing it to be socketed into a weapon for +1d6 elemental damage for 30 days.
* **Appraisal & Purity Testing**: Identifies cursed or illusion-masked gems for 20 gp.

---

### **2. Flint & Fissure Heavy Outfitters**

**Specialty**: Adamantine Mining Equipment, Breaching Tools, and Heavy Armor  
**Location**: The Ember Ring, adjoining the Molten Core Forge.  
**Owner**: **Grelka Flintforge**, an azer master-smith with a beard of glowing embers and calloused, bronze-scaled hands.  
**Description**: The shop is a warehouse of heavy-duty excavation gear. Wall racks bristle with reinforced adamantine pickaxes, hydraulic rock-splitters, and spiked climbing boots designed for vertical sheer-face mining.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Adamantine Pickaxe (+1 War Pick)** (*Uncommon*, 600 gp): Automatically deals critical hits against non-magical stone, crystal, and structures.
* **Earth Tremor Hammer (+1 Warhammer)** (*Rare*, 1,800 gp): Once per short rest, the wielder can slam the hammer into stone to cast *Earth Tremor* (saving throw Dexterity save).
* **Climber's Kit (Pneumatic Pitons)** (50 gp): Grants advantage on Athletics checks to scale stone walls and chasm faces.
* **Boots of Striding and Springing** (*Uncommon*, 500 gp): As per the DMG; heavily favored by deep-chasm scouts.
* **Plate Armor of the Earthbound (+1 Plate)** (*Rare*, 2,800 gp): Heavy armor forged from black granite and adamantine. The wearer cannot be knocked prone or moved against their will while standing on solid stone.

**Unique Services**:

* **Adamantine Edge-Tempering**: Coats existing mundane weapons or armor in adamantine, granting structural critical hit immunity or damage (500 gp).
* **Seismic Stabilization**: Enchants mining stakes to prevent tunnel cave-ins in a 30-foot radius for 48 hours (150 gp).

---

### **3. The Stalactite Apothecary**

**Specialty**: Stone Giant Elixirs, Petrification Antidotes, and Spore Salves  
**Location**: The Vein Tunnels, built into an alcove formed by weeping limestone stalactites.  
**Owner**: **Xylar the Blind**, a deep gnome hermit who lost his sight in a cave-in and navigates by acoustic tongue-clicking.  
**Description**: Smelling of damp cave earth, lichen tea, and vinegar, the shop is lined with clay flasks nestled into damp moss beds. Rare subterranean mushrooms grow in hanging baskets from the ceiling, shedding faint greenish bioluminescence.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Potion of Stone Giant Strength** (*Rare*, 1,200 gp): Sets Strength to 23 for 1 hour.
* **Elixir of the Basilisksbane** (*Rare*, 600 gp): Grants immunity to the petrified condition for 1 hour; cures ongoing petrification if administered within 3 rounds of turning to stone.
* **Potion of Healing** (50 gp) & **Potion of Greater Healing** (150 gp): Distilled using mineral-rich cavern springs.
* **Glowstone Dust (Vial)** (25 gp): Thrown into the air; sheds bright light in a 20-foot radius for 1 hour, clinging to invisible creatures and revealing their location.
* **Tears of the Earth (Salve)** (100 gp): When smeared on boots, muffles footsteps on stone, granting advantage on Stealth checks for 1 hour.

---

### **4. The Chasm Cartographer**

**Specialty**: Seismic Maps, Deep Fissure Surveys, and Resonance Chimes  
**Location**: The Stone’s Cradle, in a circular stone tower overlooking the fissure edge.  
**Owner**: **Kaelen Crag-Strider**, an earth genasi surveyor with skin like cracked shale and a calm, quiet demeanor.  
**Description**: Long parchment scrolls and engraved slate tablets cover massive drafting tables. Brass seismographs tick and swing in the corners, measuring the heartbeat of the Plane of Earth.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Surveyed Fissure Map (Rings 1–4)** (100 gp): Grants advantage on Survival checks to navigate Deepmaw Quarry and prevents getting lost in the upper tunnels.
* **Resonance Chime of Cavity Detection** (*Uncommon*, 450 gp): Struck against a stone wall; vibrates to reveal hollow chambers, secret passages, or gas pockets within 30 feet.
* **Driftglobe (Amber)** (*Uncommon*, 500 gp): Levitates and illuminates dark mines without requiring a free hand.

## **Taverns of Deepmaw Quarry**

### **1. The Hanging Basin**

**Theme**: High-Altitude Chasm Tavern & Miner Hall  
**Location**: Suspended directly over the center of the colossal fissure by four immense titan-chains.  
**Owner**: **"Crag-Breaker" Durna**, a boisterous female mountain dwarf with braided red hair and arms as thick as tree trunks.  
**Description**: The Hanging Basin is not for the faint of heart. Built upon a circular, reinforced iron platform 200 feet across, it hangs over a two-mile drop into the pitch-black chasm. Giant iron braziers heat the platform, while draft beer is delivered via overhead cable cars. The entire tavern sways gently in the thermal drafts rising from the deep fissure, accompanied by the hearty roar of hundreds of miners slamming stone tankards together.  

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **Deep-Vein Ale** *(House Special)*: A thick, dark, mineral-heavy stout brewed in chilled cavern vaults; leaves a spicy, earthy burn in the gut (3 sp).
* **Glow-Spore Mead**: Pale, slightly luminescent mead brewed from cave mushrooms and clover honey (4 sp).
* **Roasted Rock-Mole Haunch**: Slow-cooked gamey meat seasoned with mountain pepper and roasted cavern root tubers (5 sp).
* **Granite Biscuits & Marrow Gravy**: Dense, heavy biscuits served with a hot, peppery bone-marrow gravy (2 sp).

**Entertainment**:

* **The Chain Walk**: Daring miners wager on walking the suspension chains out to the rock face blindfolded (saving throw Acrobatics check; safety ropes are optional and ridiculed).
* **Quarry Dice**: Fast-paced gambling played on smooth slate slabs with carved bone dice.

---

### **2. The Shattered Geode**

**Theme**: Luxurious Crystal Lounge & High-Stakes Exchange  
**Location**: The Ember Ring, carved directly inside a gargantuan, hollowed-out amethyst geode.  
**Owner**: **Lord Baelin Amberfall**, an impeccably dressed earth genasi aristocrat who acts as a broker between the Mining Collective and outside planar merchants.  
**Description**: Entering The Shattered Geode is like stepping into a dream of purple and violet light. Every wall is an angled crystal facet that reflects and amplifies the soft music of enchanted harps. Private booths are cushioned in velvet and silk, illuminated by floating magical motes. Here, wealthy mine owners, Dao emissaries, and visiting planar dignitaries negotiate million-gold contracts over crystal flutes of aged planar wine.  

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **Amethyst Reserve** *(House Special)*: A vintage wine aged in crystal barrels; causes the drinker to feel a pleasant, weightless euphoria (1 gp).
* **Spiced Cave-Pheasant**: Delicate smoked fowl stuffed with truffles and wild rice (8 sp).
* **Crystalline Fondue**: Melted pungent cheeses blended with white wine, served with toasted sourdough and pickled root vegetables (6 sp).

## **The Black Market: The Dust Market & Sump Warrens**

Beneath the regulated stalls of the surface Dust Market lies **The Sump Warrens**, a clandestine network of abandoned mining galleries, secret hoists, and drainage caves where the **Deepmaw Syndicate** and rogue Dao cartels trade in illicit contraband.

### **Black Market Shops**

### **1. The Chasm Fence**

**Specialty**: Stolen Uncut Diamonds, Unregistered Elemental Cores, and Scraped Ingots  
**Location**: Deep in a dead-end tunnel off the Vein Ring, behind a revolving boulder door.  
**Owner**: **"Quick-Picks" Skrag**, a wiry goblin rogue with an eye for cut stones and a network of street informants.  
**Description**: Stacks of iron lockboxes and burlap sacks filled with raw, unrefined minerals line the damp walls. Skrag buys stolen gems and planar loot for 50–60% of their street value with zero questions asked, paying in unmarked trade platinum or cut gemstones.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Dagger of Venom** (*Rare*, 2,000 gp): As per the DMG.
* **Stolen Dao Mining Claim Deed** (300 gp): Grants legitimate-looking legal rights to a high-yield emerald vein in Ring 3 (until the Dao discover the forgery).
* **Chime of Opening** (*Rare*, 1,600 gp): Heavily restricted tool used to pop open sealed strongboxes.
* **Glove of Stone-Softening** (*Rare*, 1,400 gp): Once per day, touch allows the user to soften up to 5 cubic feet of stone into soft clay for 1 minute.

---

### **2. Venom & Fissure Apothecary**

**Specialty**: D&D 5e Ingested and Injury Poisons, Cave-Spore Tinctures, and Petrification Acids  
**Location**: An unmapped sewer alcove where sulfur-water drains into the fissure.  
**Owner**: **Morgra the Claw**, a half-medusa alchemist veiled in thick grey gauze.  
**Description**: The air burns the eyes. Clay amphorae hold foul bubbling acids, while cages of cave vipers and cave scorpions hang from iron chains.  

**Notable Items for Sale (Poisons & Contraband)**:

* **Pale Tincture (Ingested)** (*DMG p. 258*, 250 gp): saving throw Constitution save or take 1d6 poison damage and be poisoned; repeat save every 24 hours.
* **Torpor (Ingested)** (*DMG p. 258*, 600 gp): Incapacitates victim for 4d6 hours on failed saving throw Constitution save.
* **Purple Worm Poison (Injury)** (*DMG p. 258*, 2,000 gp): 42 (12d6) poison damage on failed saving throw save.
* **Crawler Mucus (Contact)** (*DMG p. 257*, 200 gp): Paralyzes victim for 1 minute on failed saving throw save.
* **Cave-Nox Spores (Inhaled)** (120 gp): Thrown glass bulb. Recreates *Stinking Cloud* in a 15-foot radius for 1 minute.
* **Grave-Dust Snuff** (80 gp): Addictive black powder; grants temporary advantage on Constitution saving throws for 1 hour, followed by a saving throw Con save or 1 level of exhaustion.
