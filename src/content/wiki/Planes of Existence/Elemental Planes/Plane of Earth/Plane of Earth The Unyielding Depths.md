The Plane of Earth is a realm of solid stone, endless caves, and immense mineral wealth. It represents strength, endurance, and stability but is also suffocatingly heavy and claustrophobic.

### **Geography and Features**

1. **The Shattered Caverns**: An expansive underground labyrinth of twisting tunnels and echoing chambers.
2. **The Crystal Mountains**: Gigantic peaks made entirely of glowing crystals, with magical properties.
3. **The Magma Veins**: Rivers of molten rock that flow through the plane, often mined for rare minerals.

### **Key Locations**

* **Gemspire Citadel**:
	
	A city carved from a massive geode, its walls encrusted with gemstones that glow with their own light.
	
* **The Deepmaw Quarry**:
	
	A mining hub perched on the edge of an infinite chasm, home to the Shadowforged Guild.
	
* **Obsidian Cradle**:
	
	A fortress of volcanic rock, housing Kaldryn’s most devout followers and craftsmen.
	

### **Unique Characteristics**

* **Immovable Ground**: The earth here is nearly indestructible without powerful magic.
* **Resonant Magic**: Earth-based spells are enhanced, while air-based magic struggles to function.

### **Inhabitants**

* **Earth Elementals**: Hulking beings of stone and crystal that patrol the plane.
* **Dao**: Wealth-obsessed earth genies who oversee vast mining operations.
* **Xorn**: Strange, three-legged creatures that consume precious metals and gemstones.
