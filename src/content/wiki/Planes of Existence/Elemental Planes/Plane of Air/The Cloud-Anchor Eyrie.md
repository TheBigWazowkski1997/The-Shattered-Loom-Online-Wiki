# 🏡 The Cloud-Anchor Eyrie
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Air*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Air will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Captain Zephira Cloud-Dancer (Aasimar Sky-Ranger, CR 11)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Cloud-Anchor Eyrie** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane of Air/Plane of Air The Skybound Expanse|Plane of Air]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Tethered floating island near Zephyros, suspended by golden chains
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Captain Zephira Cloud-Dancer (Aasimar Sky-Ranger, CR 11)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: An open-air pavilion buffeted by warm thermals, constructed from polished driftwood and sailcloth. Giant gliders and harpoon ballistas sit mounted along the launch decks overlooking infinite blue sky.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Air:

* **Processing & Salting**: Thermal Launch Catapult, Feather-Tanning Frames, Lightning-Rod Forge, Sky-Sled Anchorage.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.

## 🦁 Primary Apex Quarry: The Gale-Rider Roc (CR 12)

The primary monster tracked and harvested from this lodge is the feared **The Gale-Rider Roc**.

* **Creature Profile**: *A tempestuous thunderbird spanning 100 feet from wingtip to wingtip. Its feathers crackle with sapphire lightning, and its piercing screech generates gale-force shockwaves capable of capsizing spelljammers.*
