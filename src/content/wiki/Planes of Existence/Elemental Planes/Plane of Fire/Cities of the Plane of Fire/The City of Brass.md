# The City of Brass: Jewel of the Sea of Fire

![[attachments/city_of_brass.jpg]]

The **City of Brass** is the undisputed jewel and imperial capital of the **Elemental Plane of Fire**. Resting upon a colossal, floating hemisphere of enchanted brass dozens of miles across, the city drifts upon the churning, molten waves of the **Sea of Fire**. Towering minarets of red copper, domes of gleaming gold, and obsidian palaces pierce the smoky, amber sky. It is a metropolis of supreme luxury, merciless cruelty, and unbridled elemental power—a nexus of planar trade where an empire of noble **Efreeti**, azer smiths, salamander warlords, and planar travelers meet.

Within its blazing walls, anything in the multiverse can be bought or sold—for the right price in gold, souls, or elemental servitude. The city operates under rigid, despotic laws enforced by the **Brass Janissaries**, where contracts are sacred, honor is measured in subservience, and the weak are enslaved without mercy.

---

## **Overview**

* **Government**: Despotic imperial monarchy ruled by the **Grand Sultan of the Efreet** from the **Charcoal Palace**, advised by the **Court of Embers** and grand viziers.
* **Population**: ~500,000 (Efreeti 15%, Fire Genasi 25%, Salamanders 20%, Azer 15%, Mortals/Slaves 20%, Other Planar Races 5%).
* **Alignment**: Lawful Evil with strict legalistic structures and planar contract enforcement.
* **Exports**: Legendary fire-forged weaponry, elemental wish-contracts, enslaved planar labor, refined volcanic glass, and elemental cores.
* **Imports**: Fresh water, ice from the Plane of Water, food, rare metals from the Plane of Earth, exotic terrestrial luxuries.

---

## **Geography and Layout**

The city sits within an immense brass hemisphere whose underside glows white-hot from contact with the molten sea. Massive defensive walls of burnished brass rise hundreds of feet above the lava line, crowned with flame-spitting cannons and watchtowers.

The City of Brass is divided into **four grand districts**:

---

### **1. The Charcoal Palace & High Spires (Noble Quarter)**

* **Description**: The elevated imperial core of the city, suspended on obsidian pillars above lush hanging gardens of crystallized fire-lilies and burning terraces. Here dwell the noble Efreeti sultans, emirs, and grand viziers in palaces carved from basalt and sheeted in pure gold.
* **Unique Features**:
  * **The Charcoal Palace**: Seat of the Grand Sultan; a sprawling fortress of black volcanic stone surrounded by moats of liquid sunlight.
  * **The Hall of Burning Oaths**: The high court where planar contracts and wish-covenants are legally ratified under eternal flame wards.
  * **The Sultan's Menagerie**: Enclosures holding captured planar beasts, phoenixes, and astral leviathans.

---

### **2. The Pyrefield Bazaar (The Great Market)**

* **Description**: The largest open marketplace in the Inner Planes. Streets paved with polished red granite wind between silk-draped brass stalls and grand trading halls. Scents of roasted meats, sulfurous perfumes, and exotic spices fill the air.
* **Unique Features**:
  * **The Pillar of Merchants**: A colossal, glowing brass obelisk where currency exchange rates across the multiverse are updated by divination daily.
  * **The Brass Arcade**: Endless rows of luxury shops selling legendary weapons, enchanted silks, and planar relics.
  * **The Fountain of Molten Gold**: A grand plaza fountain that recirculates molten gold enchantedly cooled to the touch.

---

### **3. The Foundry Spires (Craft & Industrial District)**

* **Description**: The roaring industrial heart where Azer clans and enslaved salamander smiths labor over titanic magma forges, tempering weapons and crafting elemental siege-engines for planar wars.
* **Unique Features**:
  * **The Great Basin Sluice**: Magma canals diverted directly from the Sea of Fire to power blast-furnaces and metal presses.
  * **The Azer Redoubt**: A fortified quarter of free azer artisans who maintain uneasy independence through their irreplaceable metallurgy skills.

---

### **4. The Ash Docks & Sump Slums (Lower Ward)**

* **Description**: Clinging to the lower rim of the brass basin near the lava-gates. It is crowded, smoky, and lawless compared to the glittering upper spires. Planar skiffs, magma-crawlers, and fire-galleons dock here to unload cargo and raw ore.
* **Unique Features**:
  * **The Obsidian Slipways**: Massive launchways where skyships and magma-barges moor.
  * **The Ash Warrens**: The labyrinthine underbelly of the city beneath the blast pipes where the black market thrives.

---

## **Key Figures**

### **1. Grand Vizier Jazeel al-Zahr**

* **Role**: Chief Advisor to the Grand Sultan, Master of Wish-Contracts
* **Appearance**: A noble Efreet with crimson skin, curling horns plated in filigreed gold, and eyes of white-hot fire. He wears robes of woven cinder-silk and carries a scepter of smoking brimstone.
* **Personality**: Cunning, smooth-tongued, and calculating. Jazeel treats all living beings as chess pieces in his grand planar maneuvers.

---

### **2. Malikah Soraya of the Cinder Veil**

* **Role**: High Merchant of the Pyrefield Bazaar and Consortium Ambassador
* **Appearance**: A charismatic fire genasi noblewoman with copper skin, molten gold eyes, and dark hair interwoven with glowing embers. She dresses in shifting fiery brocade.
* **Personality**: Ambitious, diplomatic, and pragmatic. She maintains close economic ties with [[../../../../Factions/The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper|The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper]].

---

### **3. General Voltan Ashcaller**

* **Role**: Supreme Commander of the Brass Janissaries
* **Appearance**: A massive salamander warlord armored in spiked black adamantine, wielding a flaming two-handed glaive.
* **Personality**: Harsh, unyielding, and obsessed with martial discipline.

## **Shops of the City of Brass**

### **1. The Furnace & Anvil**

**Specialty**: Masterwork Fire-Forged Weapons, Adamantine Plate, and Elemental Cores  
**Location**: In the Foundry Spires, overlooking the Great Basin Sluice.  
**Owner**: **Master Durnik Flamebeard**, an azer master-smith renowned for crafting blades that never lose their heat.  
**Description**: A fortress of a smithy with four roaring blast furnaces. The ring of hammers is deafening, and cooling racks display glowing broadswords, barbed polearms, and interlocking brass plate mail.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Flame Tongue (Greatsword, Longsword, or Scimitar)** (*Rare*, 2,500 gp): As per the DMG. Ignites in flame, dealing +2d6 fire damage.
* **Adamantine Plate Armor** (*Uncommon*, 1,500 gp): As per the DMG. Turns critical hits into normal hits.
* **Shield of the Fire-Ward** (*Rare*, 1,800 gp): Grants +2 AC and resistance to fire damage; once per day can absorb a fire spell of 4th level or lower.
* **Cinder-Forged Dagger (+1 Dagger)** (*Uncommon*, 500 gp): Deals an extra 1d4 fire damage and sheds bright light in a 10-foot radius on command.
* **Elemental Core (Fire)** (*Rare*, 1,200 gp): A sealed crystal housing pure planar fire, used for powering constructs or forge engines.

**Unique Services**:

* **Flame-Hardening**: Durnik can infuse non-magical steel with magma-temper, granting it immunity to acid and rust effects (200 gp).
* **Azer Rune-Etching**: Adds a glowing protective rune to armor (+1 bonus against elemental damage) for 400 gp.

---

### **2. The Cinder Bazaar Arcana**

**Specialty**: Fire Magic Foci, Rings of Fire Resistance, Spell Scrolls, and Wands  
**Location**: The Pyrefield Bazaar, beneath a tent of woven golden wire.  
**Owner**: **Zuleika the Red**, a tiefling pyromancer with curling ram horns and hands that constantly smoke with faint sparks.  
**Description**: Glass pedestals hold floating orbs of bottled lightning, swirling embers, and spellbooks bound in dragon-scale. The shop is enchanted to maintain a comfortable temperature for mortal visitors, complete with cooling fountains of perfumed rosewater.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Ring of Fire Resistance** (*Rare*, 1,800 gp): As per the DMG. Grants resistance to fire damage.
* **Circlet of Blasting** (*Uncommon*, 600 gp): As per the DMG. Can cast *Scorching Ray* once per long rest.
* **Wand of Fireballs** (*Rare*, 4,500 gp): As per the DMG (7 charges).
* **Ruby Arcane Focus** (*Uncommon*, 250 gp): A flawless volcanic ruby that adds +1 to damage rolls for fire spells.
* **Spell Scroll of Fireball** (300 gp) & **Spell Scroll of Wall of Fire** (500 gp): Scribed on sheet brass.

**Unique Services**:

* **Wish-Contract Drafting**: Zuleika acts as an accredited scribe for drawing up legally binding planar contracts recognized by the Court of Embers (300 gp).

---

### **3. Al-Zahr's Spicery & Alchemy**

**Specialty**: Fire-Breath Potions, Fire Giant Strength Elixirs, and Volcanic Salves  
**Location**: Adjacent to the Pillar of Merchants in the Pyrefield Bazaar.  
**Owner**: **Qasim al-Zahr**, a distant cousin of the Grand Vizier and an expert in high-temperature planar distillation.  
**Description**: A luxurious, shaded storefront scented with crushed cinnamon, frankincense, and burning sulfur. Hundreds of sealed alabaster jars and glass vials rest in cooled niches along the walls.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Potion of Fire Breath** (*Uncommon*, 150 gp): As per the DMG.
* **Potion of Fire Giant Strength** (*Rare*, 1,200 gp): Sets Strength to 25 for 1 hour.
* **Potion of Healing** (50 gp) & **Potion of Superior Healing** (500 gp): Formulated with infused fire-orchid nectar.
* **Oil of Slipperiness** (*Uncommon*, 200 gp): As per the DMG.
* **Volcanic Heat-Salve** (100 gp): Applied to clothing or skin; allows comfortable survival in temperatures up to 200°F for 8 hours.

---

### **4. The Gilded Menagerie**

**Specialty**: Elemental Mounts, Fire-Resistant Beasts, and Combat Familiars  
**Location**: The outer perimeter of the Pyrefield Bazaar.  
**Owner**: **Jaluk the Beast-Binder**, a scarred salamander handler who communicates through harsh clicks and hisses.  
**Description**: Vast iron pens holding smoking, flame-maned steeds and exotic planar beasts. Handlers wield brass gaffs and frost-wands to keep the more volatile creatures in check.  

**Notable Creatures & Items for Sale**:

* **Nightmare Steed** (*Special/Very Rare*, 8,000 gp): Loyal planar mount capable of planar travel with its master.
* **Magmin Familiar Token** (*Uncommon*, 800 gp): Once per long rest, summons a loyal Magmin servant for 1 hour.
* **Fire Snake (Juvenile Mount/Pet)** (1,500 gp): Fast and venomous aquatic/magma crawler.
* **Horseshoes of Speed** (*Rare*, 2,000 gp): Increases a mount's speed by 30 feet.

## **Taverns of the City of Brass**

### **1. The Gilded Scimitar**

**Theme**: Opulent Efreeti Hookah Lounge & Planar Banquet Hall  
**Location**: The High Spires, overlooking the Sea of Fire.  
**Owner**: **Emir Rashad al-Din**, an extravagant noble Efreeti who delights in the company of witty conversationalists and planar diplomats.  
**Description**: The tavern is a palace of sensory indulgence. Silken divans, gold-embroidered cushions, and bubbling waterpipes filled with fragrant, spiced embers line the sprawling terrace. The ceiling is a dome of stained glass that filters the harsh light of the Plane of Fire into deep violet and ruby tones. Music from brass lutes and enchanted finger-cymbals floats through the perfumed air.  

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **Sultan's Nectar** *(House Special)*: A honeyed liqueur infused with gold flakes and distilled flame-orchid nectar; pleasantly warms the veins and grants temporary darkvision (1 gp).
* **Cinder-Smoked Lamb**: Succulent spiced lamb slow-roasted over volcanic embers with pomegranate reduction (8 sp).
* **Scorched Saffron Rice**: Fragrant yellow rice studded with almonds, currants, and spicy fire-peppers (4 sp).
* **Hell-Boar Ribs**: Glazed in spicy plum sauce and seared to smoky perfection (12 sp).

**Entertainment**:

* **Fire-Dance Contests**: Dancers clad in fire-immune silks perform mesmerizing acrobatics with flaming blades.
* **The High Stake Chess**: Patrons play *Three-Dragon Ante* and celestial chess on boards of pure ivory and jade.

---

### **2. The Ash & Ember**

**Theme**: Gritty Planar Mercenary & Smelter Dive  
**Location**: In the Ash Docks, built into an iron warehouse overlooking the lava-ship slips.  
**Owner**: **"Iron-Jaw" Bronn**, a retired azer brawler with a prosthetic molten-iron jaw.  
**Description**: Hot, smoky, and dangerously loud. The tavern floor is made of iron grates positioned over a slow-moving channel of molten slag that provides both light and heating. Battered mercenaries, corsair sailors, and soot-covered foundry workers pound heavy mugs of fortified ale on scarred basalt tables. Fights break out regularly, usually resolved when Bronn douses the brawlers with a bucket of sulfur-water.  

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **Slag-Heater Rum** *(House Special)*: Extremely potent rum distilled from fermented ash-sugar; burns like liquid fire (3 sp).
* **Basalt Stout**: A heavy, opaque dark ale with notes of chicory and roasted malt (2 sp).
* **Spicy Fire-Skewer Feast**: Chunks of goat meat, bell peppers, and volcanic onions grilled on glowing skewers (5 sp).
* **Black-Crust Loaf**: Dense bread baked directly on hot basalt stones, served with spicy tallow butter (1 sp).

**Special Features**:

* **The Mercenary Roster**: An iron board where bounty hunters and privateers post contracts and recruit muscle for expeditions on the Sea of Fire.

## **The Black Market: The Ash Warrens**

Beneath the paved basalt avenues and blast gates of the Ash Docks lies **The Ash Warrens**, a subterranean labyrinth of ventilation shafts, drainage flues, and forgotten catacombs. Here, beyond the gaze of the Brass Janissaries, an illegal economy thrives.

### **Black Market Shops**

### **1. The Cursed Lamp**

**Specialty**: Stolen Wish-Mist, Fenced Imperial Relics, and Smuggled Astral Shards  
**Location**: Deep within an unmapped volcanic conduit behind the docks.  
**Owner**: **Nadir the Faceless**, a shadowy doppelganger who dresses in the garb of an Efreeti merchant.  
**Description**: A dimly lit vault stacked with lead-lined chests and warded urns. Nadir buys and sells goods stolen from noble estates and intercepted caravans.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Dagger of Venom** (*Rare*, 2,000 gp): As per the DMG.
* **Flask of Stolen Wish-Mist** (*Very Rare*, 5,000 gp): A swirling vial of unformed planar wish-energy. Can be used as an action to duplicate the effect of any spell of 5th level or lower without components.
* **Chime of Opening** (*Rare*, 1,600 gp): As per the DMG.
* **Robe of Scintillating Colors** (*Very Rare*, 6,000 gp): As per the DMG.
* **Forged Imperial Transit Seals** (100 gp each): Counterfeit passes granting safe conduct through city gates without search.

---

### **2. The Brimstone Apothecary**

**Specialty**: D&D 5e Poisons, Volatile Planar Narcotics, and Caustic Acids  
**Location**: The Gutter-Pipes beneath the Foundry Spires.  
**Owner**: **Marzanna Black-Ash**, a tiefling poisoner whose fingertips are permanently stained violet.  
**Description**: Boiling glassware drips noxious, glowing fluids into lead flasks. The air is choking with fumes of sulfur, arsenic, and dried belladonna.  

**Notable Items for Sale (Poisons & Contraband)**:

* **Midnight Tears (Ingested)** (*DMG p. 258*, 1,500 gp): saving throw Con save at midnight; 31 (9d6) poison damage on failure.
* **Burnt Othur Fumes (Inhaled)** (*DMG p. 258*, 500 gp): saving throw Con save or take 3d6 poison damage each round until save succeeds.
* **Purple Worm Poison (Injury)** (*DMG p. 258*, 2,000 gp): 42 (12d6) poison damage on failed saving throw save.
* **Cinderdust Snuff (Planar Narcotic)** (75 gp): Illegal battle stimulant; grants fire resistance and frenzy, but risks exhaustion.
* **Acid of Disincorporation** (100 gp per flask): Dissolves up to 1 cubic foot of metal or organic matter within 1 minute.

---

### **3. The Broken Shackle**

**Theme**: Underworld Refuge & Rebel Haven  
**Location**: An abandoned lava pumping chamber beneath the Ash Docks.  
**Owner**: **Tariq the Unchained**, a former tiefling gladiator who bought his freedom in blood.  
**Description**: The tavern is a sanctuary for runaway slaves, rogue elementals, and smugglers. Entry requires knowing the secret phrase ("The flame burns brightest when free"). Tariq provides safehouse rooms, medical aid, and underground railroad networks to help escaped slaves reach planar portals.  

---

## **The City of Brass Notice Board**

The City of Brass Notice Board stands in the center of the Pyrefield Bazaar—a monumental brass slab flanked by continually burning torches. Bounties, trade proclamations, and judicial execution warrants are stamped into bronze placards by order of the Grand Vizier and the Brass Janissaries.

---

### **Mission 1: The Magma-Titan’s Wake**

**Posted by**: Commander Voltan Ashcaller (Brass Janissaries)  
**Reward**: 600 gp and an *Adamantine Breastplate*  
**Description**: "A colossal, rogue Magma Titan has emerged from the deep thermal vents of the Sea of Fire and is currently drifting toward the southern shipping docks. Two patrol galleons have already been crushed under its advance. We require elite combatants to board a military skiff, intercept the beast, and destroy its elemental core before it breaches the outer harbor walls."  

---

### **Mission 2: Heist in the Pyrefield**

**Posted by**: Malikah Soraya of the Cinder Veil  
**Reward**: 500 gp and a *Ring of Fire Resistance*  
**Description**: "An illicit team of planar infiltrators broke into my private vault in the Brass Arcade last evening, escaping with a sealed *Flask of Bound Wish-Mist* intended for the Grand Sultan's tribute. The thieves were traced to an abandoned foundry flue in the Ash Warrens. Recover the flask unopened, and eliminate the thieves."  

---

### **Mission 3: The Foundry Strike**

**Posted by**: Grand Vizier Jazeel al-Zahr  
**Reward**: 450 gp and one *Flame Tongue Longsword*  
**Description**: "An unsanctioned strike by azer metalworkers in Foundry Tower Nine has halted military production for the Grand Sultan's legions. The workers have barricaded the blast doors and threaten to vent the furnace cooling reservoirs, which would trigger a volcanic explosion across the district. Quell this insurrection—either through forceful disarmament or decisive negotiation."
