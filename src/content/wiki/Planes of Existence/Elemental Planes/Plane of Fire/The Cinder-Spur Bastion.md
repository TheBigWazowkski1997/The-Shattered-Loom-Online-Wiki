# 🏡 The Cinder-Spur Bastion
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Fire*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Fire will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Ignis Iron-Skin (Fire Genasi Forge-Hunter, CR 12)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Cinder-Spur Bastion** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane of Fire/Plane Of Fire The Everblaze Expanse|Plane of Fire]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Basalt outcrop overlooking the Sea of Ash, near the City of Brass
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Ignis Iron-Skin (Fire Genasi Forge-Hunter, CR 12)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A volcanic fortress cooled internally by salamander-powered water heat-sinks. The hearth is a pool of bubbling magma used to quench monster-bone blades and melt slag-carapaces.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Fire:

* **Processing & Salting**: Magma Quench-Trough, Cooling Cellar, Ash-Resistant Salting Vats, Crucible Forge.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
