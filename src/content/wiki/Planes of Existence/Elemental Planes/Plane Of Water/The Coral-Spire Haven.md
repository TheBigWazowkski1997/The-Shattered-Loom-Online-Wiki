# 🏡 The Coral-Spire Haven
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Water*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Water will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Tide-Stalker Nerina (Triton Leviathan-Hunter, CR 12)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Coral-Spire Haven** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane of Water/Plane of Water The Azure Expanse|Plane of Water]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Pressurized air-dome anchored to a coral atoll near Tidehaven
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Tide-Stalker Nerina (Triton Leviathan-Hunter, CR 12)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A bioluminescent domed sanctuary where water is kept at bay by ancient kinetic wards. Harpooners mend nets of kelp-fiber while deep-sea leviathan skulls hang like trophies in the sapphire glow.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Water:

* **Processing & Salting**: Decompression Chamber, Ink-Distillery, Whale-Bone Forge, Submersible Dry-Dock.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
