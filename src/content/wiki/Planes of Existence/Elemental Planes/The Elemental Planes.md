The Inner Planes are remnants of the primordial war, chaotic and ever-changing realms of raw matter and elemental energy:

### **The Four Elemental Realms & Major Hubs**

1. **[[Plane of Fire/Plane Of Fire The Everblaze Expanse|The Plane of Fire (The Everblaze Expanse)]]**
   * **[[Plane of Fire/Cities of the Plane of Fire/The City of Brass|The City of Brass]]**: Imperial capital of the Noble Efreet, featuring the Pyrefield Bazaar, the Charcoal Palace, the Ash Warrens black market, and the Court of Embers.
   * **[[Plane of Fire/Cities of the Plane of Fire/Cinderhold|Cinderhold]]**: Resilient volcanic fortress city carved into Mount Khaldrath.
   * **[[Plane of Fire/Cities of the Plane of Fire/Blazeward Keep|Blazeward Keep]]** & **[[Plane of Fire/Cities of the Plane of Fire/Emberspire|Emberspire]]**.

2. **[[Plane Of Water/Plane of Water The Azure Expanse|The Plane of Water (The Azure Expanse)]]**
   * **[[Plane Of Water/Cities of Plane of Water/Tidehaven|Tidehaven]]**: Semi-aquatic drifting metropolis of living coral, featuring the Saltspire Anchorage, the Kraken's Hold black market, and the Council of Waves.
   * **[[Plane Of Water/Cities of Plane of Water/Pearldome|Pearldome]]** & **[[Plane Of Water/Cities of Plane of Water/The Sapphire Abyss|The Sapphire Abyss]]**.

3. **[[Plane of Earth/Plane of Earth The Unyielding Depths|The Plane of Earth (The Unyielding Depths)]]**
   * **[[Plane of Earth/Cities of The Plane of Earth/The Deepmaw Quarry|The Deepmaw Quarry]]**: Colossal chasm-settlement and Dao gem-mining hub, featuring the Five Rings, the Hanging Basin, the Sump Warrens, and the Seven-Vein Concessions.
   * **[[Plane of Earth/Cities of The Plane of Earth/Gemspire Cradle|Gemspire Cradle]]** & **[[Plane of Earth/Cities of The Plane of Earth/Obsidian Cradle|Obsidian Cradle]]**.

4. **[[Plane of Air/Plane of Air The Skybound Expanse|The Plane of Air (The Skybound Expanse)]]**
   * **[[Plane of Air/Cities of the Plane of Air/Zephyros|Zephyros]]**: Floating metropolis of drifting islands and Sylph Sky-Ports, featuring the Skyspire Citadel, Gale Market, the Slipstream Undercroft, and the Concord of Winds.
   * **[[Plane of Air/Cities of the Plane of Air/SkySong Isles|SkySong Isles]]** & **[[Plane of Air/Cities of the Plane of Air/Stormhold|Stormhold]]**.

* **Para-Elemental Planes**: Planar rifts where elements merge or clash, creating unstable hybrid zones (e.g., swamps of mud, fields of glass, clouds of scald-steam).
