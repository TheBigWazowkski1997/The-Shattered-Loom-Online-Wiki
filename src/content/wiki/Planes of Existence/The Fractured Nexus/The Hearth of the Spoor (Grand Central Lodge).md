# 🏡 The Hearth of the Spoor (Grand Central Lodge)
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — The Fractured Nexus*

> *"The wilderness of The Fractured Nexus will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Grand Huntmaster Torvald Blood-Tracer (Goliath Master Stalker, CR 16)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Hearth of the Spoor (Grand Central Lodge)** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/The Fractured Nexus/The Fractured Nexus|The Fractured Nexus]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Floating central shard anchored by three massive Golden Filaments, Nexus Spire district
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Grand Huntmaster Torvald Blood-Tracer (Goliath Master Stalker, CR 16)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: The global headquarters of the Iron Spoor Guild. A colossal hall with twenty-two vaulted hearths, each burning wood native to one of the 22 planes. Hunters from across the multiverse trade stories, exchange Spoor Marks, and receive apex bounties.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of The Fractured Nexus:

* **Processing & Salting**: The High Master Forge, Universal Salting Tanks, Guild Armory, Apex Bounty Board, Mount Stables.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.

## 🦁 Primary Apex Quarry: The Filament-Eater Drake (CR 10)

The primary monster tracked and harvested from this lodge is the feared **The Filament-Eater Drake**.

* **Creature Profile**: *A slender, sinuous drake with scales of spun golden thread that feeds on the cosmic filaments connecting drifting shards. It breathes a cone of kinetic unraveling that unties magic and destabilizes spatial orientation.*
* **Type & Alignment**: `Large Dragon (Monstrosity), Unaligned`
