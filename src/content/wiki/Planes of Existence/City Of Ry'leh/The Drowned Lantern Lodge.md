# 🏡 The Drowned Lantern Lodge
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — City of Ry'leh*

> *"The wilderness of City of Ry'leh will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Harpooner Kael (Mutated Deep-Human Ranger, CR 12)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Drowned Lantern Lodge** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/City Of Ry'leh/City Of Ry'leh|City of Ry'leh]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Submerged vault accessible through the Starlight Harbor piers
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Harpooner Kael (Mutated Deep-Human Ranger, CR 12)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A sunken, air-pocket tavern constructed from petrified memory-stone. Green witchlight illuminates tanks of glowing starlight brine where hunters store the alien pelts of creatures dragged from the Abyssal Trench.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of City of Ry'leh:

* **Processing & Salting**: Thought-Brine Salting Vats, Non-Euclidean Workbench, Sanity Recovery Bunkhouse, Harpoon Forge.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
