# **The Abyss: The Great Unraveling**

If the Nine Hells are the "Tension Weights" seeking to bind the multiverse into an unyielding cage of iron law, **The Abyss** is the **Static of the Unwoven**. It is not merely a plane, but a cataclysmic cosmic process—the primal force of boundless entropy that pre-dates the Loom and seeks to return every spun thread of reality to pure, chaotic potential. 

In the cosmological architecture of the Shattered Loom, the Abyss is the **Entropic Wake** carved out by the **Great Dreamer** as it stirs within the Astral depths, gnawing at the drifting remnants of the Golden Tapestry. Where the Loom broke, planar shards were formed; but where the Loom **unravels completely**, the Abyss yawns open.

![The Abyss Planar Atlas](/The-Shattered-Loom-Online-Wiki/images/the_abyss_map.jpg)

***

## **Core Characteristics & Loom Metaphysics**

1. **The Frayed Weave**: Reality within the Abyss is non-linear and unstable. Physical laws, time vectors, and identity strands actively fray. Mortal travelers frequently experience "glitching"—temporarily duplicating across adjacent space, shedding fragments of memory, or hearing their spoken words transform into screaming static.
2. **The Hunger of the Void (The Abyssal Sunder)**: The Abyss does not rest. It exerts a constant metaphysical pull on drifting Astral shards and neighboring planes, tearing at their mooring threads and dragging entire cities into its infinite Fracture-Zones to be chewed into raw thread-debris.
3. **Sentient Chaos (The Fray-Fiends)**: Unlike the devils of Baator, who covet legal contracts and mortal souls to forge cosmic machines, the demons of the Abyss—the **Fray-Fiends**—are living embodiments of the unraveling. They do not desire dominion; they crave the tearing of every bound knot until nothing remains but silence and static.
4. **The Blood War (The Weaver's Paradox)**: The eternal clash between Baator and the Abyss is fought over reality's foundational architecture. If the devils triumph, existence becomes a frozen prison of absolute tyranny. If the demons prevail, the Loom dissolves forever into entropic oblivion.

## **1. Layer 1: Pazunia (The Plain of Infinite Portals)**

> *"A gale of rusted red grit screams across an endless, jagged limestone plateau. Beneath a sunless, ochre sky, the ground is punched with thousands of yawning, bottomless pits. From the dark depths of each pit rises a distinct cosmic groan—the howling of blizzards, the hiss of boiling bile, or the distant screech of a dying world being shredded alive."*

### **Atmosphere & Read-Aloud**
Pazunia is the reception chamber of damnation and the primary threshold where astral skiffs, fallen meteors, and planar wanderers crash. The air smells of sulfur, baked iron, and ozone. Above, the sky is not atmospheric gas, but a turbulent ceiling of cosmic static where distant planar shards drift like burning embers. Pits ranging from ten feet across to canyon-sized chasms riddle the ground—each one a one-way or two-way conduit descending to one of the infinite lower layers of the Abyss.

### **Environmental Dynamics & Loom Metaphysics**
* **The Void-Pits**: Over ten thousand sinkholes puncture the plains. Dropping into a pit hurls the traveler into a specific lower fracture-zone (e.g., Pit 45 descends to Azzagrat; Pit 88 to the Gaping Maw).
* **Rust-Wind Gale**: Fierce winds loaded with pulverized planar debris scour the surface. Every hour exposed to the open gale requires a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or creatures take 2d6 slashing damage and nonmagical metal gear gains a cumulative -1 penalty to AC or attack rolls (rusting away at -5).
* **Spatial Vertigo**: Horizon lines bend unnaturally upward. Navigating without planar instruments requires a DC 15 Wisdom (Survival) check; failure results in traveling in an entropic loop.

### **Social Order & Outposts**
There is no central throne on Pazunia. The ancient winged lord **Pazuzu** rules the toxic skies, demanding obedience from swarms of Vrocks and Chasme. On the ground, opportunistic scavengers, Tiefling salvagers, and deserters from the Blood War inhabit **Fort Fray**, a precarious fortress built from the wreckage of crashed astral spelljammers.

### **Key Landmarks**
* **Fort Fray (The Scavenger's Redoubt)**: A walled bastion perched on the lip of the Grand Chasm, where unaligned planar wanderers buy maps, sell salvage, and negotiate guide contracts.
* **The Grand Chasm of Shards**: A mile-wide canyon where debris from recently unraveled mortal cities continuously rains down from the Astral Sea.
* **The Conduit Well-Field**: An iron-fenced region containing hundreds of mapped, semi-stable portal conduits guarded by mercenary tanar'ri gatekeepers.

### **Regional Commerce: Unique Shops of Pazunia**
1. **The Iron Scavenger's Exchange** *(Proprietor: Vrek the Rust-Picker, Tiefling Tinker)*

   * *Rust-Shield Grease (Flask)*: Coating applied to weapons or armor that prevents corrosive damage and abyssal rust for 24 hours. (Cost: 45 gp).
   * *Salvaged Loom-Strand Anchor*: A small piton that pulses with faint golden light. When driven into abyssal ground, it negates gravity glitches in a 20-foot radius for 8 hours. (Cost: 250 gp).
2. **The Styx-Flask Depot** *(Proprietor: Marra the Blind, Drow Alchemist)*
   * *Sealed Vial of Styx Sludge*: Caustic fluid that can be splashed (range 20 ft) to deal 3d10 psychic damage and force a DC 14 Int save or inflict short-term amnesia. (Cost: 180 gp).
   * *Amnesia Sponge*: A dry, porous fungal nodule that absorbs psychic backlash; grants advantage on saves against abyssal madness for 4 hours. (Cost: 75 gp).
   * *Conduit Chalk*: Runed chalk used to mark portal rims, identifying the destination layer number for 7 days. (Cost: 30 gp).
3. **Pazuzu's Feather Reliquary** *(Proprietor: Scribe Kaen, Corrupted Aasimar)*
   * *Gale-Strider Boots*: Worn boots that allow the wearer to ignore the movement penalties and damage of the Rust-Wind Gale. (Cost: 500 gp).

### **Regional Taverns: Dens of Pazunia**
1. **The Broken Skiff**: Built inside the inverted hull of a shattered nautiloid vessel balanced over the lip of a conduit pit. Serves fermented cactus bile and blood-wine to Doomguard veterans and desperate planar couriers.
2. **The Rust & Bone**: A subterranean dugout beneath Fort Fray smelling of boiled marrow and pipeweed. Gamblers bet soul-shards and salvage scrap on pit-fighting dretches.

## **2. Layers 45, 46, 47: Azzagrat (The Triple-City of Glitches)**

> *"A sprawling, impossible metropolis of dark obsidian towers and iridescent marble spans across three overlapping layers simultaneously. The sky burns in a deep, bruised violet, and light here has tangible physical weight. In the grand markets of Zelatar, every indulgence imaginable is peddled with exquisite courtesy, even as the walls of buildings flicker between opulence and slaughterhouse gore."*

### **Atmosphere & Read-Aloud**
Azzagrat is the crown jewel of abyssal civilization—a breathtaking, terrifying empire ruled by **Graz'zt, the Dark Prince**. The realm encompasses three interconnected layers (45: Rauvryk, 46: Barazbul, and 47: Ztissz), all unified by the magnificent metropolis of **Zelatar**. Here, demons dress in fine silks, dark elves stroll tree-lined avenues of serpentine vipers, and travelers from across the multiverse come to buy what is forbidden everywhere else.

### **Environmental Dynamics & Loom Metaphysics**
* **Weight of Light**: Illumination has physical pressure. Bright light slows movement by 10 feet unless the creature succeeds on a DC 14 Strength saving throw or carries a Graz'zt sigil.
* **The Triple-Dimensional Shift**: Zelatar exists in three layers at once. Crossing certain street intersections without speaking the password shifts a character to a different layer, separating parties who walk more than 30 feet apart.
* **Ego-Dissolution Aura**: Graz'zt’s intoxicating presence makes travelers feel safe and hedonistically reckless. Finishing a long rest requires a DC 15 Charisma saving throw; failure inflicts disadvantage on Insight checks and an obsession with staying in the city.

### **Social Order & Leadership**
Graz'zt governs from the **Argent Palace**, maintaining strict laws of etiquette and decadent commerce. Unlike the mindless slaughter of other abyssal realms, violence in Zelatar is an art form—assassinations are legal if preceded by a written invitation, and betrayal is considered high culture.

### **Key Landmarks**
* **The Argent Palace**: A colossal castle of sixty-six towers built of mirrors, obsidian, and ivory, where Graz'zt presides over orgiastic revels and cosmic conspiracies.
* **The River of Salt**: A roaring white waterway of razor-sharp salt brine dividing the city's three dimensional districts.
* **The Viper Forest**: An eerie woodland outside the walls where the trees are giant living pythons whose breath lulls trespassers to sleep.

### **Regional Commerce: Unique Shops of Azzagrat**
1. **The Silken Serpent Atelier** *(Proprietor: Madame Xelise, Cambion Couturière)*
   * *Cloak of the Six Fingers*: A dark velvet cape granting advantage on Dexterity (Sleight of Hand) and Charisma (Deception) checks. Once per day, you can cast *Charm Person* (DC 15). (Cost: 650 gp).
   * *Veil of Distorted Truth*: A face veil woven from mirrored thread. When speaking a lie, magical lie-detection spells detect your words as absolute truth. (Cost: 400 gp).
   * *Shadow-Silk Rope (50 ft)*: Weightless black rope that climbs surfaces autonomously upon command and can hold up to 3,000 lbs. (Cost: 200 gp).
2. **The Confectionery of Sins** *(Proprietor: Master Malakor, Tiefling Chocolatier)*
   * *Oblivion Truffles (Box of 3)*: Eating a truffle cures one level of exhaustion and erases the memory of the most painful thing experienced in the last 24 hours. (Cost: 150 gp).
   * *Viper-Venom Cordial*: A sweet liqueur that grants resistance to poison damage for 8 hours but inflicts 1d4 psychic damage whenever you tell a truth. (Cost: 100 gp).
   * *Sensory Spice Powder*: Inhaling this dust grants truesight out to 30 feet for 10 minutes, followed by 1 hour of blinded darkness. (Cost: 300 gp).
3. **The Argent Forge of Graz'zt** *(Proprietor: Smith-Baron Kael, Incubus Artificer)*
   * *Wavy Greatsword of Azzagrat (+2)*: A blackened blade with a serpentine edge. Deals an extra 2d6 acid damage on hit and can emit a 15-foot cone of blinding violet smoke once per short rest. (Cost: 2,500 gp).
   * *Argent Mirror Shield*: A mirrored steel shield. When targeted by a spell that requires a ranged spell attack, you can use your reaction to impose disadvantage on the attack; if it misses, the ray reflects at another creature within 30 feet. (Cost: 1,800 gp).
   * *Soul-Coin Signet Ring*: Allows the wearer to store up to 3 Soul-Coins in an extradimensional pocket and spend them to cast *Misty Step*. (Cost: 750 gp).

### **Regional Taverns: Dens of Azzagrat**
1. **The Lustrous Mirror**: A tri-level tavern where the floors, ceilings, and bar counters are polished mirrors. Patrons drink shimmering indigo liqueurs while shapeshifting succubi perform aerial acrobatics suspended from silver chains.
2. **The Gilded Scorpion**: A smoky speakeasy located in the lower Salt District where interplanar smugglers, mercenary captains, and planar outcasts broker black market soul-trade contracts away from Graz'zt's tax-collectors.

### **Notice Board / Fray Contracts**
* **[Contract: The Argent Thief]**: "A cambion courtier has fled the Argent Palace carrying Graz'zt's private diplomatic correspondence with the Cult of the Broken Thread. Slay him and recover the scroll tube unopened. Reward: 1,500 gp + a letter of safe passage in Zelatar."
* **[Harvest: Viper Tree Sap]**: "Collect five flasks of sap from the Great Anaconda tree in the heart of the Viper Forest. Beware the hypnotic pollen. Bounty: 900 gp."
* **[Investigation: The Vanishing Alley]**: "Three caravans disappeared near the Intersection of the Triple Arch. Determine if this was a spatial glitch or an illegal teleportation trap set by Lamashtu cultists. Reward: 1,100 gp."

## **3. Layer 66: The Demonweb Pits (The Tangled Web & False Loom)**

> *"A bottomless, lightless gulf of cold cosmic void is spanned by billions of interlocking, glistening black silk cables. Trapped within this colossal web drift fractured city quarters, ruined temples, and frozen meteorites. At the geometric hub of the web hangs a colossal iron arachnid fortress that moves upon eight massive legs, its multifaceted ruby eyes burning with divine spite."*

### **Atmosphere & Read-Aloud**
The Demonweb Pits are the domain of **Lolth, the Queen of the Demonweb Pits (The False Weaver)**. Here, the Void is not empty, but thick with predatory silk woven from the corrupted threads of unraveled worlds. Entire fragments of the Material Plane—castles, villages, cathedrals—hang suspended in the dark like trapped flies, wrapped in suffocating web-shrouds while giant hunting spiders and chitinous Bebiliths stalk between the strands.

### **Environmental Dynamics & Loom Metaphysics**
* **The Sticky Corrupted Weave**: Moving across web strands requires a DC 14 Dexterity (Acrobatics) check. On a failure, a creature is restrained by sticky silk (escape DC 15 Strength). Slashing the silk releases acidic black bile (2d8 acid damage to the attacker).
* **The Willpower Drain**: Lolth’s webs siphon psychic vitality. For every hour spent navigating the webs, non-evil creatures must make a DC 14 Wisdom saving throw or lose 1 spell slot of their highest level or suffer disadvantage on all mental ability checks until resting on solid ground.
* **Arachnid Tremorsense**: Any movement or spellcasting that causes vibration along the web strands instantly alerts hunting Bebiliths within 1 mile.

### **Social Order & Outposts**
Lolth rules with tyrannical cruelty through her high priestesses and Yochlol handmaidens. Drow outcasts, driders, and enslaved mortal artisans labor in the hanging iron sanctuaries. Unlike the chaotic slaughter elsewhere in the Abyss, Lolth demands absolute devotion, intricate court intrigue, and sacrificial offerings of captured Loom fragments.

### **Key Landmarks**
* **The Web-Fortress of Lolth**: A mobile citadel shaped like a colossal iron spider that crawls across the web network, carrying the Dark Mother's throne room and eggs.
* **The Hanging City of Malshym**: A suspended district built upon twenty captured planar shards lashed together with demon-silk cables, housing Lolth's mortal cultists and drow emissaries.
* **The Cocoon Vaults**: Billions of mummified victims hanging in the void, their memories and soul-threads being slowly distilled into black silk for the False Weaver's counterfeit tapestry.

### **Regional Commerce: Unique Shops of the Demonweb Pits**
1. **The Silkweaver's Fang** *(Proprietor: Matron Vriss, Drider Artificer)*
   * *Demonweb Silk Cloak*: A shimmering gossamer cloak that grants climbing speed equal to walking speed and advantage on stealth checks in shadows or webs. (Cost: 700 gp).
   * *Spider-Eye Circlet*: A silver brow-band set with eight ruby lenses. Grants 60 ft darkvision, advantage on Perception checks, and the ability to see through magical darkness. (Cost: 850 gp).
   * *Bebilith Chitin Shield (+1)*: Shield forged from demonic carapace. When struck in melee, you can use a reaction to spray venom, dealing 2d8 poison damage (DC 15 Con save halves). (Cost: 1,200 gp).
2. **The Cocooned Secrets Emporium** *(Proprietor: Yochlol Zephyra, Handmaiden of Lolth)*
   * *Distilled Memory Vial*: A crystal vial containing the extracted memory of a dead sage or wizard; drinking it imparts proficiency in one skill or tool for 24 hours. (Cost: 350 gp).
   * *Web-Snare Grenade (x2)*: Glass spheres filled with compressed demon-silk. When thrown (range 30 ft), explodes in a 15-foot radius web (as the *Web* spell, DC 15). (Cost: 180 gp).
   * *Poisoner's Needle (+1)*: Dagger coated with desiccating venom; on hit, target must succeed on a DC 14 Con save or take 3d6 poison damage and be paralyzed for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn). (Cost: 950 gp).
3. **The Eight-Fold Reliquary** *(Proprietor: Arch-Inquisitor Vaelin, Drow Warlock)*
   * *Ring of Arachnid Command*: Allows the wearer to speak telepathically with arachnids and cast *Giant Spider Summon* once per day. (Cost: 1,000 gp).
   * *Corrupted Loom Needle*: An iron needle that can stitch a wound instantly (heals 4d8+4 HP), but the healed creature gains glowing spider-markings and vulnerability to radiant damage for 24 hours. (Cost: 450 gp).
   * *Black Silk Thread (100 ft)*: Unbreakable thread that cannot be cut by nonmagical blades; holds up to 5,000 lbs. (Cost: 250 gp).

### **Regional Taverns: Dens of the Demonweb**
1. **The Suspended Carapace**: A tavern hollowed out of the petrified exoskeleton of a gargantuan abyssal arachnid hanging by eight iron chains over the bottomless void. Serves fermented venom-wine and roasted cave grubs to drows, fiends, and captured planar explorers.
2. **The Eight-Eyed Gaze**: A quiet, candle-lit parlor in the Hanging City where drow noble houses plot assassinations over tea brewed from midnight-lotus and dream-fungus.

### **Notice Board / Fray Contracts**
* **[Contract: The Severed Cable]**: "A stray spelljammer collided with Anchor Cable 42, severing the connection to the Cocoon Vaults. Repair the cable using demon-silk before the vault drifts into the void. Payment: 1,200 gp."
* **[Bounty: Rogue Drider Hunt]**: "A drider champion who abandoned Lolth’s faith is hiding among the debris of a ruined elven monastery. Slay him and bring his fangs to Matron Vriss. Bounty: 850 gp + an enchanted spider-silk tunic."
* **[Infiltration: The Counterfeit Loom]**: "The Pale Lodge seeks a fragment of the corrupted black silk currently being spun inside the Web-Fortress. Extract the sample without alerting the Yochlol guards. Reward: 2,000 gp."

## **4. Layer 88: The Gaping Maw (The Dual-Fracture & Primeval Madness)**

> *"A boundless, boiling black-and-purple brine ocean crashes violently against primeval, jungle-smothered shores. The sky above is torn asunder, illuminated by two diseased, flickering suns that cast shifting, contradictory shadows. From the roaring sea rise the Twin Spires of Abysm—two monstrous fangs of black volcanic stone crowned by gargantuan thrones where the Prince of Demons roars at his own reflection."*

### **Atmosphere & Read-Aloud**
The Gaping Maw is the seat of **Demogorgon, the Prince of Demons (The Living Contradiction)**. This realm is a nightmarish primal wilderness where evolution runs backward into monstrous savagery. Flesh-eating ferns, titanic aboleths, two-headed sea serpents, and feral ixitxachitl wage endless, bloody war. The air is thick with the stench of rotting seaweed, electrical ozone, and the coppery tang of spilled blood.

### **Environmental Dynamics & Loom Metaphysics**
* **The Duality Logic Error**: Demogorgon’s presence splits reality into two contradictory truths. Any creature within 10 miles of the Twin Spires must succeed on a DC 16 Wisdom saving throw each dawn or roll a d6: on 1-3, they believe they have two competing identities that argue internally (disadvantage on concentration checks and initiative); on 4-6, physical attacks made against the creature have a 20% miss chance as their body visibly flickers into a duplicate phantom image.
* **The Screaming Reefs**: Coral reefs formed from the calcified skeletons of drowned armies wail with the rise and fall of the tides. Creatures within 100 feet of a reef take 2d6 psychic damage at the start of each turn unless deafened.
* **Caustic Brine Waves**: The ocean water deals 1d6 acid and 1d6 poison damage per round of immersion, eroding nonmagical boats and dissolving mortal skin.

### **Social Order & Demonic Clashes**
Demogorgon rules through raw, crushing terror. His two heads, **Aameul** (the schemer) and **Hethradiah** (the berserker), are locked in an eternal, seething feud, each constantly plotting the destruction of the other while remaining inextricably fused to the same titanic body. Cults of sea-devils, Kuo-toa, and primal humanoids worship the twin beast as a god of inevitable ruin.

### **Key Landmarks**
* **The Twin Spires of Abysm**: Two jagged mountains rising from the ocean, connected by dripping bridges of bone. Each spire contains a throne room for one of Demogorgon’s heads.
* **Lemoriax (City of the Twin Spires)**: A flooded, half-sunken metropolis of cyclopean stone carved with maddening spirals, where aquatic demons and sahuagin zealots offer screaming sacrifices to the tides.
* **The Maw of the Sunder**: A titanic oceanic whirlpool that drags unraveled planar matter down into the deepest, unmapped trenches of the entropic void.

   * *Bifurcated Halberd (+2)*: A polearm that branches into two parallel blades. On a hit, target takes an extra 1d8 slashing and 1d8 psychic damage. (Cost: 2,200 gp).
   * *Abyssal Shark-Tooth Armor*: Scale mail forged from petrified megalodon teeth. Grants resistance to cold and acid damage and a swimming speed equal to walking speed. (Cost: 1,500 gp).
   * *Amulet of Contradiction*: Once per day, when you fail a saving throw, you can choose to succeed instead by accepting 4d8 psychic damage as your mind temporarily fractures. (Cost: 1,800 gp).
2. **The Brine-Rot Apothecary** *(Proprietor: Hagra of the Maw, Sea Hag Coven Mother)*
   * *Elixir of the Twofold Mind*: Grants advantage on Intelligence and Wisdom saving throws for 1 hour, but you must speak all sentences twice in different voices. (Cost: 250 gp).
   * *Kraken-Bile Flask*: Corrosive oil that burns underwater; deals 4d10 acid damage to structures or hulls. (Cost: 300 gp).
   * *Screaming Coral Charm*: A necklace that emits a wailing shriek when an aberration or demon approaches within 60 feet. (Cost: 200 gp).
3. **The Deep Trench Scavenger** *(Proprietor: Qorlax the Deep-Drow, Warlock of the Sunder)*
   * *Tide-Breaker Ring*: Grants water breathing and immunity to the pressure and cold of oceanic trenches. (Cost: 600 gp).

   * *Pearl of Abyssal Madness*: When crushed, forces all creatures within 30 feet to make a DC 16 Wisdom save or suffer confusion for 1 minute. (Cost: 800 gp).

### **Regional Taverns: Dens of the Maw**
1. **The Two-Headed Eel**: A tavern built inside the dried ribcage of a leviathan beached on the black shores of Lemoriax. Patrons drink briny rum that numbs all feeling and listen to the frantic prophecies of mad shipwreck survivors.
2. **The Drowned Caravel**: The intact wreckage of a grand merchant galleon suspended forty feet underwater by glowing buoy-gems. Air bubbles enchanted by sea-hags allow air-breathing planar corsairs and aquatic fiends to carouse in eerie, buoyant comfort.

### **Notice Board / Fray Contracts**

* **[Beast Hunt: The Abyssal Leviathan]**: "A mutated, three-eyed megalodon is attacking transport barges crossing the Maw of the Sunder. Slay the beast and bring back its dorsal fin. Reward: 2,200 gp."

## **5. Layer 113: Thanatos (The Void-Grave of Unmoving Threads)**

> *"A sunless, midnight-black sky is dusted with frozen, dead stars that never twinkle. Beneath this cosmic shroud lies a frozen desert of pulverized bone and gray tomb-ash, crossed by the sluggish, frost-rimmed waters of the River Styx. Far in the distance looms Naratyr—the city of sunken skeletons—overshadowed by a mountain-sized obsidian skull wreathed in roaring, sickly-green necrotic fire."*

### **Atmosphere & Read-Aloud**
Thanatos is the silent necropolis of **Orcus, the Prince of Undeath (The Tatter-King)**. In Orcus’s grim vision, the agony of the Shattering can only be solved by halting all motion, life, and destiny forever. The plane is bitterly cold, smelling of embalming fluid, crypt dust, and dry decay. Armies of skeletal legionnaires, wights, and liches march in lockstep silence, enforcing an unnatural, absolute stasis.

### **Environmental Dynamics & Loom Metaphysics**
* **The Stagnant Knot**: In Thanatos, the threads of fate do not unravel—they freeze solid. Spells of the School of Necromancy have their save DCs increased by 2. Conversely, magical healing spells restore only half their normal hit points.
* **Ghost-Static Aura**: The air is heavy with the psychic screaming of dead souls whose threads were severed before their time. Finishing a short or long rest requires a DC 15 Constitution saving throw; failure inflicts 1 level of exhaustion as the chill of the grave seeps into the soul.
* **The Frozen Styx**: The river here is thick with ice floes composed of frozen, weeping memories. Touching the water deals 4d10 cold damage and forces a DC 16 Wisdom save against permanent memory loss.

### **Social Order & Undead Hierarchy**
Orcus reigns from his **Obsidian Skull Citadel**, cared for by undead high priests and vampire princes. Living creatures are viewed strictly as raw material to be harvested and reanimated into the grand army of universal stillness. The only living mortals permitted in Naratyr are necromancers and death-cultists who bring tributes of stolen corpses and severed Loom fragments.

### **Key Landmarks**
* **The Obsidian Skull Citadel**: A mountain carved into the likeness of a colossal horned ram skull, whose hollow eyes burn with necrotic fire visible across the entire layer.
* **Naratyr (The City of Sunken Skeletons)**: A majestic necropolis built on a shelf of black bone projecting into a lake of frozen tears, interconnected by aqueducts carrying Styx waters.
* **The Hall of the Cut Threads**: A subterranean vault beneath Orcus’s throne where millions of severed golden soul-threads are pinned to stone slabs, frozen into brittle gray needles.

1. **The Ossuary Smithy** *(Proprietor: Master Karr, Wight Weaponsmith)*
   * *Bone-Forged Scythe (+2)*: A two-handed scythe carved from titan bone. Deals an extra 2d6 cold and 2d6 necrotic damage on a hit; on a critical hit, target cannot regain hit points until the start of your next turn. (Cost: 2,400 gp).
   * *Grave-Dust Armor*: Full plate crafted from compacted tomb-ash and grave-steel. Grants immunity to necrotic damage and vulnerability to radiant damage. (Cost: 2,000 gp).
   * *Crypt-Bone Wand*: An arcane focus granting +1 to spell attack rolls and spell save DCs for necromancy spells. (Cost: 750 gp).
2. **The Tatter-Shroud Tailor** *(Proprietor: Lady Morbida, Vampire Seamstress)*
   * *Shroud of the Unmoving Thread*: A burial shroud that can be donned as an action. While wearing it, you register as an undead creature to divination spells and mindless undead will ignore you unless attacked. (Cost: 600 gp).
   * *Wraith-Silk Gloves*: Dark translucent gloves that allow the wearer to physically touch and manipulate intangible ghosts and spectral creatures. (Cost: 450 gp).
   * *Casket-Key of Thanatos*: An iron skeleton key that can unlock any mundane or magical lock once, turning the lock mechanism into cold dust. (Cost: 350 gp).
