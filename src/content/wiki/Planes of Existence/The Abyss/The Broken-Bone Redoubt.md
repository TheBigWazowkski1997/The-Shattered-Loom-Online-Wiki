# 🏡 The Broken-Bone Redoubt
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — The Abyss*

> *"The wilderness of The Abyss will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Vrakesh the Scarred (Gorgon-Blooded Orc Ranger, CR 13)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Broken-Bone Redoubt** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/The Abyss/The Abyss|The Abyss]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Barricaded basalt gorge overlooking the Viper Forest, Pazunia edge
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Vrakesh the Scarred (Gorgon-Blooded Orc Ranger, CR 13)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A crude, brutal war-camp cobbled from the ribcages of dead demon lords and iron spikes. The atmosphere is loud, violent, and relentless—hunters must defend their kills from scavenging dretches and chasme.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of The Abyss:

* **Processing & Salting**: Acid-Neutralizing Vats, Bone-Splinter Forge, Demon-Gland Distillery, Chaos Ward Generator.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.

## 🦁 Primary Apex Quarry: The Fray-Bred Abomination (Goristro-Hydra) (CR 15)

The primary monster tracked and harvested from this lodge is the feared **The Fray-Bred Abomination (Goristro-Hydra)**.

* **Creature Profile**: *A towering, multi-headed beast spawned in the entropic pits of the 88th layer. It combines the massive bulk of a minotaur-goristro with four writhing hydra maws that spew acidic bile and scream in discordant frequencies.*
* **Type & Alignment**: `Huge Fiend (Demon, Aberration), Chaotic Evil`
* **Harvest Check DC**: **DC 25**

## 🪙 Lodge Store: Specialized Regional Goods

Hunters can redeem their **Spoor Marks** or trade gold and monster components for specialized supplies:
