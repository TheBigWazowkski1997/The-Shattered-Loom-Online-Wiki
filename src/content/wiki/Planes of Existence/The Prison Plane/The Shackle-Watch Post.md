# 🏡 The Shackle-Watch Post
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — The Prison Plane (The 32 Chambers)*

> *"The wilderness of The Prison Plane (The 32 Chambers) will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Watchman Gideon (Githzerai Inquisitor, CR 12)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Shackle-Watch Post** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/The Prison Plane/The 32 Chambers|The Prison Plane (The 32 Chambers)]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Observation ledge above Tier XVII, The Silent Spindle
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Watchman Gideon (Githzerai Inquisitor, CR 12)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: An austere, solemn watch-post carved from lead-lined stone. No alcohol or rowdy banter is permitted; hunters meditate on sensory discipline to prevent attracting the acoustic wards of the prison.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of The Prison Plane (The 32 Chambers):

* **Processing & Salting**: Lead-Lined Salting Boxes, Sound-Dampened Anvil, Anti-Magic Cleansing Basin, Inscription Desk.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.

## 🦁 Primary Apex Quarry: The Silent Spindle Warden-Hound (CR 11)

The primary monster tracked and harvested from this lodge is the feared **The Silent Spindle Warden-Hound**.

* **Creature Profile**: *A headless, canine-shaped construct woven from null-magic adamantine filaments and dense lead plating. It hunts sound and somatic casting, draining spell slots with every bite and radiating an aura of total silence.*
* **Type & Alignment**: `Large Aberration (Construct), Lawful Neutral`
