# 🏡 The Edge of Sanity Outpost
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — The Far Realm*

> *"The wilderness of The Far Realm will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **The One-Eyed Hermit (Astral Githzerai Slayer, CR 14)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Edge of Sanity Outpost** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/The Far Realm/The Far Realm|The Far Realm]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Anchored to the threshold rift dividing the Grand Traverse from the Far Void
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **The One-Eyed Hermit (Astral Githzerai Slayer, CR 14)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: An unsettling outpost anchored by reality-reinforcing lead cables. The walls hum with counter-harmonic frequencies to prevent visitors' bones from turning into glass or liquid.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of The Far Realm:

* **Processing & Salting**: Spatial Anchor Forge, Paradox Dissection Slab, Anti-Maddening Bath, Reality-Suture Bench.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.

## 🦁 Primary Apex Quarry: The Void-Folded Polyhedral Horror (CR 16)

* **Creature Profile**: *An entity with twelve angular facets that rotate through non-existent fourth-dimensional directions. It attacks by unfolding its surfaces, trapping victims in localized geometric paradoxes that invert anatomy.*
