# 🏡 The Threaded Haven
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Unity*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Unity will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Aelith Tree-Bonder (Dryad-Elf Tracker, CR 11)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Threaded Haven** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Unity/Plane of UnityThe Woven Citadel|Plane of Unity]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: The Sacred Grove within the Woven Citadel, branch 7
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Aelith Tree-Bonder (Dryad-Elf Tracker, CR 11)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A living pavilion grown from interwoven branches of telara-oak. The air hums with harmonic unity; guild members share sensory thoughts through communal honey-wine and hunt only what disturbs the collective balance.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Unity:

* **Processing & Salting**: Root-Grown Tannery, Symbiotic Weave Loom, Communal Broth Cauldron, Sap-Refining Still.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.

## 🦁 Primary Apex Quarry: The Harmonious Mycelial Weaver (CR 10)

The primary monster tracked and harvested from this lodge is the feared **The Harmonious Mycelial Weaver**.

* **Creature Profile**: *A colossal entity of ancient fungal threads, living bark, and luminous spore-mushrooms. It communicates through spores, absorbing fallen beasts into its shared root-consciousness while ensnaring intruders in empathic webs.*
* **Type & Alignment**: `Huge Plant (Monstrosity), Lawful Good`
