**Description:** A dark, infinite ocean where the waters echo with the whispers of hidden truths. The plane tempts travelers with forbidden knowledge but often drives them mad with its revelations.The **Plane of Secrets** is a shadowy, enigmatic realm where hidden knowledge, unspoken truths, and forgotten memories are locked away. It is a plane defined by veils, whispers, and layers of concealment, where nothing is as it seems. The Plane of Secrets is both a sanctuary for forbidden truths and a prison for the knowledge too dangerous to exist elsewhere. Those who enter often find themselves entangled in webs of lies and illusions, lured by the promise of forbidden insight.
The landscape of the plane is surreal and disorienting, shifting with the perspective of those who walk it. Paths vanish into shadow, doors open to impossible vistas, and the very air is thick with hushed murmurs. It is a plane of discovery and danger, where secrets have a price, and the curious must tread carefully.

#### **1\. The Whispering Labyrinth**

The **Whispering Labyrinth** is an ever-shifting maze of shadowy corridors, veiled pathways, and mirrored chambers. This labyrinth is the heart of the plane, where countless secrets are stored, guarded by illusions and traps.

* **Appearance**:The labyrinth is a seemingly infinite structure of black stone, shimmering glass, and swirling mist. The walls are inscribed with runes that glow faintly when approached, emitting whispers that reveal fragments of hidden knowledge. Shadows dance along the corridors, often taking on humanoid forms.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Hall of Reflections**: A chamber filled with mirrored walls that show not the viewer’s reflection, but their **deepest secrets** and unspoken thoughts.
	* **The Ever-Locked Door**: A massive iron door with no visible keyhole. It is said to lead to the **Vault of the First Secret**, but it only opens to those who can answer its riddle.
	* **The Shadow Guardians**: Ethereal beings of living shadow patrol the labyrinth, punishing those who seek secrets without proper intent.

---

#### **2\. The Shrouded Spires**

The **Shrouded Spires** are towering, jagged formations that pierce the sky of the plane. Each spire holds a powerful secret locked within its core, protected by illusions, puzzles, and guardians.

* **Appearance**:The spires are colossal black obelisks that rise into swirling mists, their surfaces etched with glowing, shifting glyphs. Wisps of light spiral around the spires, whispering incomprehensible phrases.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Glyph Chambers**: Hidden chambers within the spires contain glowing glyphs that must be deciphered to access the secrets they guard.
	* **The Vault Keepers**: Mysterious constructs shaped like humanoid owls with crystalline eyes, who guard the spires and test intruders' worthiness.
	* **The Obsidian Observatory**: The highest point of the tallest spire, where one can glimpse fragments of forgotten knowledge about the cosmos.

---

#### **3\. The Abyss of Whispers**

The **Abyss of Whispers** is a vast, echoing void where voices of the past and future converge. It is said to be the resting place of secrets too dangerous to speak, their power muffled by the endless darkness.

* **Appearance**:A sprawling, black abyss filled with shifting fog and faint, glowing motes of light. The air is heavy with overlapping whispers, each carrying fragments of hidden truths. The ground is uneven, often giving way to bottomless pits.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Whispering Pools**: Small, reflective pools scattered throughout the abyss that reveal memories or hidden thoughts when gazed into.
	* **The Silent Bridge**: A narrow, ethereal bridge that spans the abyss. Walking across it without succumbing to the whispers requires immense focus.
	* **The Forbidden Echo**: A massive, swirling vortex of sound in the abyss’s depths. It is said to contain a secret capable of unmaking reality.

---

#### **4\. The Hidden Gardens**

The **Hidden Gardens** are an illusory paradise within the Plane of Secrets, where truths bloom as flowers and lies take root as twisted, thorny vines. The gardens serve as a test of intent, revealing the true desires and motivations of those who wander through them.

* **Appearance**:The gardens are lush, with vibrant flowers that glow faintly and release calming scents. However, shadows lurk beneath the surface, and the gardens shift to reflect the emotions and thoughts of those who enter.
* **Key Features**:
	* **Truthblooms**: Radiant flowers that bloom when someone speaks an honest truth.
	* **Thorned Vines**: Dark, creeping plants that lash out at those who lie or conceal their intentions.
	* **The Fountain of Revelation**: A glowing fountain at the garden’s center that shows glimpses of the future or hidden truths when one drinks from it.

---

#### **5\. The Vault of the Forgotten**

The **Vault of the Forgotten** is a massive, impenetrable structure buried deep within the plane. It holds the most dangerous and forbidden secrets, locked away to prevent them from ever being uncovered.

* **Appearance**:The Vault is a towering, featureless monolith made of dark, unbreakable stone. It hums faintly with suppressed energy, and intricate runes of binding glow softly across its surface.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Sealed Gates**: Massive doors that require three keys—representing **truth**, **sacrifice**, and **understanding**—to unlock.
	* **The Binding Wards**: Magical seals that punish those who attempt to open the Vault without proper intent.
	* **The Guardian of Silence**: A colossal, faceless entity that protects the Vault, erasing intruders from existence.

### **1\. Whisperhold: The City of Echoes**

**Whisperhold** is a sprawling, twilight city hidden within an immense, mist-filled cavern beneath the surface of the Plane of Secrets. The city is constructed entirely from shadowed stone and dimly glowing crystal, which hums faintly with the countless secrets etched into its structure. Every wall, every step, and every surface within Whisperhold is a silent witness, recording the thoughts, deeds, and whispers of those who pass through.
The city is a haven for spies, informants, and keepers of hidden lore, as well as those who seek forbidden truths. Its streets twist and turn like a maze, seemingly rearranging themselves to lead wanderers to—or away from—what they seek. The only constant is **The Vault of Murmurs**, a towering black spire at the city's center that is said to hold the most guarded secrets in existence.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Vault of Murmurs**
	* A colossal obsidian tower whose exterior is covered in shifting glyphs that only reveal fragments of themselves when stared at directly. The Vault is said to contain every secret whispered, written, or thought within the multiverse, locked behind impenetrable wards. Only the **Silent Order**, a sect of monks who have severed themselves from speech, are allowed to enter.
	* **Lore**: It is whispered that those who force their way into the Vault and steal a secret will find that secret burning their mind, consuming their sanity until they return it.
2. **The Echo Market**
	* A sprawling bazaar where merchants sell information, rumors, and fragments of knowledge. Transactions are conducted in strange currencies, such as memories, promises, or shadows.
	* **Lore**: The most powerful merchant in the Echo Market is **Chimera Veil**, an ancient being who takes on the appearance of whoever looks at her but always speaks with her true voice, which reverberates with unsettling truth.
3. **The Labyrinth of Whispers**
	* A vast network of tunnels beneath Whisperhold that hum with the faint echoes of voices. The Labyrinth is said to hold the discarded secrets of gods and civilizations long forgotten, but it is also a trap for the unwary, who may wander forever among its echoing halls.
	* **Lore**: Those who listen too closely to the whispers in the labyrinth sometimes find their own secrets stolen, leaving them hollow and fragmented.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **The Silent Order**: Monks who take vows of silence and guard the Vault of Murmurs. They communicate only through gestures and their presence alone deters those who would seek to steal secrets.
* **Shadowkind**: Beings formed of shadow and memory, who act as guides or spies for those who can pay their price.
* **Knowledge Seekers**: Adventurers, scholars, and rogues who come to Whisperhold in search of forbidden truths, though many lose themselves in the process.

---
### **2\. Veilscar: The Shattered Citadel**

**Veilscar** is a broken city, built upon the ruins of an ancient stronghold that once belonged to the **Keepers of Revelation**, a forgotten order dedicated to cataloging and protecting dangerous truths. Long ago, Veilscar was shattered by its own knowledge, its foundations ripped apart by the release of a forbidden secret. Now, the city floats as a fragmented series of islands connected by glowing bridges of thought and memory.
The inhabitants of Veilscar are wanderers, scavengers, and hermits who search the shattered remnants of the city for fragments of lost wisdom. Some seek enlightenment, while others aim to harness the power of forgotten truths for their own ends. However, the ruins are fraught with danger, as the **Echoing Fracture**, the wound left by the city’s destruction, emanates waves of chaos that warp reality itself.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Fractured Archives**
	* The remnants of a grand library that once held the accumulated knowledge of countless worlds. Scattered and broken, its books now float between the islands, protected by sentient, semi-hostile glyphs that lash out at those who cannot decipher them.
	* **Lore**: The Fractured Archives hold the secret of what destroyed Veilscar, though finding it means confronting the same truth that ruined the city.
2. **The Radiant Bridges**
	* Networks of glowing, ephemeral pathways that connect the floating fragments of Veilscar. These bridges shift and shimmer, changing their destinations and sometimes collapsing entirely.
3. **The Echoing Fracture**
	* A gaping void in the center of the city that radiates chaotic energy. The Fracture occasionally whispers half-truths to those who approach, though its words can drive listeners mad.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **Shard Seekers**: Adventurers and scholars searching for pieces of knowledge in the ruins. Many become obsessed with their search, unwilling to leave.
* **Wounded Constructs**: Guardians of the Fractured Archives, mechanical and magical beings warped by the destruction of the city.
* **The Scarborn**: Mutated survivors of Veilscar’s destruction, who now act as guides and scavengers.

---
### **3\. Umbralith: The Shadowed City**

**Umbralith** is a city built entirely from **solidified shadows**, its streets and buildings a seamless blend of darkness and form. The city has no light of its own, instead illuminated by distant, pale orbs that hang in the sky like watchful eyes. Umbralith is a city of secrecy and conspiracy, where every alleyway hums with whispers, and every shadow may conceal a hidden door, a lurking figure, or a watching eye.
The city is governed by the **Cabal of Veils**, a shadowy council that manipulates the Plane of Secrets from behind the scenes. Umbralith is a haven for spies, assassins, and conspirators, and many of its inhabitants wear masks or conceal their true forms. However, the city’s shadows seem alive, moving of their own accord and feeding on the secrets of those who dwell there.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Veiled Spire**
	* A central tower that seems to fade into the sky. The Veiled Spire is the headquarters of the **Cabal of Veils**, though no one is certain who—or what—truly resides at its summit.
2. **The Cloaked Bazaar**
	* A labyrinthine market where goods are sold under strict anonymity. Buyers and sellers conceal their identities, and even speaking aloud is forbidden. Transactions are carried out through gestures and whispers.
3. **The Living Shadows**
	* Portions of the city where the shadows themselves come alive, forming semi-sentient creatures that watch, follow, or attack those who trespass.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **The Cabal of Veils**: A council of faceless beings who govern Umbralith and manipulate the flow of secrets across the plane.
* **Shadecallers**: Mystics who can control the living shadows, often serving as spies or assassins.
* **Masked Merchants**: Traders and artisans who hide their true identities, fearing the city’s watchful nature.

## **Taverns of Whisperhold**

### **1. The Muffled Tongue**

**Theme**: Exclusive Speakeasy & High-Stakes Intrigue Salon  
**Location**: Built into a hollowed crystalline stalactite hanging above the Echo Market.  
**Owner**: **Countess Sylphira**, an elegant drow diplomat with a silver fox familiar that senses telepathic eavesdroppers.  
**Description**: The tavern is under a permanent *Zone of Dampened Sound*; across tables, words are clear, but sound dissipates within 5 feet, making eavesdropping impossible. Velvet-curtained booths offer complete visual privacy. Waiters glide silently across thick, plush rugs, serving vintage spirits and cold appetizers.  

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **The Silent Vow** *(House Special)*: An obsidian-colored liqueur that tastes of blackberries and mint; while under its influence for 2 hours, the drinker cannot be compelled to speak an untruth or reveal a chosen designated secret even under magical coercion (1 gp).
* **Night-Veil Wine**: Rich, dark red vintage infused with shadow-moss; induces a relaxing trance and resistance to psychic damage for 4 hours (8 sp).
* **Glazed Shadow-Figs**: Sweet, dark figs filled with almond paste and dusted with edible silver sugar (5 sp).
* **Smoked Cold-Water Eel**: Crisp, salted smoked delicacy served on dark rye toast (6 sp).

**Entertainment**:

* **Silent Chess**: Patrons play games of chess using pieces that whisper subtle philosophical riddles when moved.
* **The Secret Auction**: At midnight, a masked master of ceremonies auctions off three sealed envelopes containing verified high-value planar intelligence to the highest bidder.
