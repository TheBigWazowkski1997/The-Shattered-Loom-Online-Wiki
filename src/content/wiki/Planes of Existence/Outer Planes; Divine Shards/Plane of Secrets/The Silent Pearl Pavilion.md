# 🏡 The Silent Pearl Pavilion
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Secrets*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Secrets will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Lady Mara Deep-Ear (Merfolk Inquisitor, CR 12)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Silent Pearl Pavilion** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Secrets/Plane of Secrets Ocean of Whispers|Plane of Secrets]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Concealed coral atoll beneath the singing tides of Zathara
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Lady Mara Deep-Ear (Merfolk Inquisitor, CR 12)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: An elegant pavilion constructed of pearl shells and iridescent sea-glass. Water runs silently across the floors, and hunters communicate exclusively through telepathic shells to avoid disturbing the singing secrets in the deep.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Secrets:

* **Processing & Salting**: Secret-Press Distillery, Shell-Weaving Loom, Telepathic Pearl Basin, Submerged Vault.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
