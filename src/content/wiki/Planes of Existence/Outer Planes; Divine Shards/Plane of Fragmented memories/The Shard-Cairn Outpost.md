# 🏡 The Shard-Cairn Outpost
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Fragmented Memories*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Fragmented Memories will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Selene the Mnemonist (Half-Elf Echo-Tracker, CR 11)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Shard-Cairn Outpost** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Fragmented memories/Plane of Fragmented Memories - The Shardlands|Plane of Fragmented Memories]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Cairn of the First Regret, Shardlands perimeter
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Selene the Mnemonist (Half-Elf Echo-Tracker, CR 11)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: Constructed from stacked mirror-stones that catch and repeat reflections of long-dead travelers. The hearth fire crackles with whispered words instead of sparks, and hunters preserve their kills inside ice-clear memory crystals.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Fragmented Memories:

* **Processing & Salting**: Mirror-Glass Tannery, Regret-Still, Echo-Sounding Chamber, Memory-Vault.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.

## 🦁 Primary Apex Quarry: The Echo-Mirage Stalker (CR 12)

The primary monster tracked and harvested from this lodge is the feared **The Echo-Mirage Stalker**.

* **Creature Profile**: *A feline predator composed of broken mirror-prisms that stalks travelers across the Shardlands. It does not attack directly; it projects illusions of the target's most painful memories, feeding on mental anguish before shattering into a whirlwind of razor glass.*
* **Type & Alignment**: `Large Monstrosity, Chaotic Neutral`
* **Harvest Check DC**: **DC 22**
