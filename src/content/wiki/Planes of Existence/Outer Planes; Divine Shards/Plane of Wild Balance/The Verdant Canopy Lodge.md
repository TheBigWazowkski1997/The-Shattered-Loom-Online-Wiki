# 🏡 The Verdant Canopy Lodge
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Wild Balance*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Wild Balance will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Ranger Fenris Green-Eye (Wild-Elf Beast-Lord, CR 13)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Verdant Canopy Lodge** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Wild Balance/Plane of Wild Balance Verdant Nexus|Plane of Wild Balance]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Crowned boughs of the Primal World-Oak, Verdant Nexus
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Ranger Fenris Green-Eye (Wild-Elf Beast-Lord, CR 13)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: Suspended hundreds of feet in the emerald canopy, open to the rain and sky. Hunters train with dire hawks and sabertooth companions while treating animal hide with herbal tinctures that smell of wild moss and crushed mint.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Wild Balance:

* **Processing & Salting**: Canopy Skinning Racks, Beast-Taming Aviary, Herbal Fermenting Vats, Bone-Carver's Bower.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.

## 🦁 Primary Apex Quarry: The Primal Apex Chimera-Panther (CR 13)

The primary monster tracked and harvested from this lodge is the feared **The Primal Apex Chimera-Panther**.

* **Creature Profile**: *The undisputed apex hunter of the Verdant Nexus. It features the muscular chassis of a dire sabertooth panther, the sweeping horned mantle of a ram, and four barbed scorpion tails that lash with neuromuscular venom.*
* **Type & Alignment**: `Huge Beast (Monstrosity), Unaligned`
