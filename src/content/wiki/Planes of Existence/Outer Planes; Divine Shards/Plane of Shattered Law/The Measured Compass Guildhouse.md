# 🏡 The Measured Compass Guildhouse
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Shattered Law*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Shattered Law will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Calibrator Vane (Clockwork Tiefling Engineer-Tracker, CR 12)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Measured Compass Guildhouse** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Shattered Law/Plane of Shattered Law The Fractured Spire|Plane of Shattered Law]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Platform 14 at the base of the Fractured Spire, overlooking the gear-valleys
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Calibrator Vane (Clockwork Tiefling Engineer-Tracker, CR 12)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A spotless guildhouse where every weapon is precisely weighed and numbered on brass scales. Trackers use pendulum compasses and chronometers to calculate monster migration angles down to the second.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Shattered Law:

* **Processing & Salting**: Precision Calibration Bench, Gear-Cutting Lathe, Spring-Tension Tannery, Mathematical Registry.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
