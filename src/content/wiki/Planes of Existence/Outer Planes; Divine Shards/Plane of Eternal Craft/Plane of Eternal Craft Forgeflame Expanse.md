# The Plane of Eternal Craft (Forgeflame Expanse)

![[plane_of_eternal_craft_map.jpg]]

> **Cartographic Atlas**: See full geography, travel routes, villages, and regional details in [[Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Eternal Craft/Map of the Plane of Eternal Craft|Map & Geography of the Plane of Eternal Craft]].

### **General Overview**

The **Plane of Eternal Craft** is an awe-inspiring expanse of diverse landscapes, each tailored to a specific form of creation. Towering mountain ranges sparkle with veins of magical ore, while vast oceans churn with molten metal. Floating islands serve as mobile workshops, drifting above rivers of flowing energy. The air hums with the sound of hammering, carving, weaving, and other acts of craftsmanship. Every element of the plane is alive with potential, its resources and terrain designed to support an infinite variety of creative endeavors.

While the plane is beautiful and inspiring, it is also **demanding and relentless**. The pursuit of perfection here can consume its denizens, turning their creativity into obsession. Many who come seeking inspiration or mastery find themselves lost to their craft, becoming one with the plane’s unyielding rhythm.

Commerce, quality control, and cooperation between the plane's scattered cities are governed by [[Factions/The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper]], a plane-wide artisan guild that maintains balance between the metalworkers, crystal-shapers, and living-wood crafters.

* * *

### **Key Features**

1. **The Colossal Forge**:
	* At the center of the plane lies the **Colossal Forge**, a massive, blazing structure surrounded by an ever-burning flame. This forge is said to be the heart of the plane, created by a primordial force of crafting. It can produce items of unimaginable power but demands equal sacrifice in skill, effort, or material to unlock its true potential.
2. **The Artisan's Fields**:
	* Surrounding the Colossal Forge are vast expanses of raw materials: forests of living wood, rivers of liquid crystal, and plains of gleaming metal. These fields are enchanted, constantly replenishing themselves, ensuring that the act of creation is never hindered by a lack of resources.
3. **Clockwork Heavens**:
	* Above the Colassal Forge, the skies are filled with a **vast network of gears, cogs, and machinery**, endlessly turning and clicking in perfect unison. This clockwork network is said to regulate time within the plane, ensuring that creators have as much as they need to complete their work.
4. **The Whispering Stones**:
	* In the far reaches of the plane lie mountains whose stones are said to hold the memories of every item ever crafted. Those who meditate here claim to hear whispers of ancient knowledge, techniques, and inspiration.
5. **The Living Workshops**:
	* Scattered throughout the plane are **sentient workshops**, structures that adapt and reshape themselves to suit the needs of their inhabitants. These workshops have personalities of their own, often testing their users before granting access to their full potential.

### **1\. Metallos: The Great Foundry City**

**Metallos** is a sprawling metropolis built around a central **Colossal Forge**, a divine forge said to have been ignited by the first act of creation itself. The city is an industrial marvel, its streets humming with the sound of hammers, roaring furnaces, and the rhythmic ticking of immense clockwork mechanisms. Rivers of molten metal and glowing energy flow through channels beneath the streets, powering the city and feeding its ever-burning forges.
Metallos is the beating heart of the Plane of Eternal Craft, a hub for metalworkers, inventors, and smiths from across the multiverse. Its structures are composed of gleaming brass, enchanted steel, and adamantine, all polished to a mirror-like shine. While the city’s beauty is undeniable, its relentless pace of work often drives its inhabitants to obsession, pushing them to their limits in pursuit of perfection.

* * *

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Colossal Forge**
	* The central forge of Metallos, a towering structure that burns with an eternal flame. It is said that any item forged within its fires is imbued with perfection and a spark of the divine. The **Forge Masters**, a council of legendary artisans, govern Metallos from this site.
	* **Lore**: The Colossal Forge was created by the Primordial **Arkonos, the Shaper of All**, who left the plane to allow others to carry on his work. It is whispered that Arkonos’s hammer still lies within the forge’s depths, waiting to be wielded by a true master.
2. **The Gearspire**
	* A towering clockwork tower at the city’s edge, filled with workshops, laboratories, and experimental chambers. Inventors and artificers gather here to test their designs and collaborate on grand projects.
	* **Lore**: The Gearspire’s topmost chamber holds the **Ethereal Loom**, a device capable of weaving reality itself into physical form, though its use is highly restricted due to the risks of imbalance.
3. **The Molten Veins**
	* A network of glowing channels that carry molten metal and raw magical energy throughout the city, providing power to its forges and workshops.
	* **Lore**: The veins are rumored to be alive, carrying the "lifeblood" of the plane itself. Disrupting their flow can lead to catastrophic consequences.

* * *

#### **Inhabitants**

* **Forge Masters**: Legendary smiths and artisans who govern Metallos and oversee the Colossal Forge.
* **Clockwork Servants**: Mechanical constructs designed to assist in crafting and city maintenance.
* **Obsessive Crafters**: Mortals and planar beings who have dedicated themselves entirely to their work, often losing their sense of time and self.

### **The Clockwork Curio: Companions for the Courageous**

The **Clockwork Curio** is a renowned workshop and store located in a bustling city or magical hub, catering to adventurers, scholars, and explorers seeking reliable, customized **clockwork companions**. Owned by the eccentric and ingenious **Master Tinkerer Cylix Gremlincoil**, this shop is a wonderland of mechanical marvels, filled with the soft whirring of gears, the ticking of intricate devices, and the occasional chirp or squeak of a newly crafted automaton.
From loyal guardians to utility-focused assistants, Cylix designs each companion to fit the specific needs of their clients, making the Clockwork Curio a favorite among adventurers who desire something unique to aid them on their journeys.

* * *

### **The Storefront**

The exterior of the Clockwork Curio is a spectacle that draws the eye of any passerby. The building itself is an elaborate piece of craftsmanship, featuring:

* **Gleaming brass-and-steel architecture** with clockwork gears embedded into its walls, constantly turning in mesmerizing patterns.
* A **rotating sign** depicting a mechanical owl holding a gear in its talons, with the store’s motto: “**Precision. Loyalty. Ingenuity.**”
* Large glass windows showcasing an array of small clockwork creatures in motion—mechanical birds hopping on perches, tiny automaton dogs wagging their tails, and intricate arachnid scouts weaving webs of copper thread.

* * *

### **Inside the Store**

The interior of the Clockwork Curio is a chaotic-yet-cozy workshop, combining retail space with a working laboratory. Visitors are greeted by:

* **Rows of display cases** filled with miniature and medium-sized clockwork creations, each labeled with their name, purpose, and features.
* **Shelves stacked with components**: brass gears, crystal cores, enchanted screws, and shimmering metal plates etched with runes.
* A **central counter** cluttered with tools, half-finished projects, and small mechanical arms that occasionally pass Cylix a needed tool or clean up the workspace.
* **Chirping and buzzing companions** scuttle and hover throughout the shop, performing tasks or simply observing the customers.

* * *

### **The Owner: Cylix Gremlincoil**

* **Appearance**:
	
	Cylix is a gnome with wild, soot-streaked hair and an infectious enthusiasm for their craft. They wear a leather apron bristling with tools and magnifying goggles that constantly whir and shift as they examine intricate details.
	
* **Personality**:
	
	Cylix is friendly, fast-talking, and deeply passionate about their creations. They are always eager to show off their latest inventions and take great pride in crafting companions that perfectly suit their clients’ needs. However, they can be somewhat scatterbrained and easily distracted by new ideas.
	
* **Backstory**:
	
	Cylix grew up in a city known for its magical innovation and developed an early fascination with blending **magic and machinery**. They opened the Clockwork Curio to fund their larger dream of creating a sentient clockwork city one day.

### **The Under-Foundry: The Slag Warrens (Metallos Black Market)**

Beneath the paved brass avenues and regulated smokestacks of Metallos lies **The Slag Warrens**, also known as the **Under-Foundry**. Spanning a labyrinthine network of decommissioned drainage conduits, abandoned casting flues, and catacombs beneath the Smokestack Alleys and the Dwarven District, this subterranean black market caters to those whose trades violate [[Factions/The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper|The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper]] regulations.

Here, illicit planar contraband, black-market schematics, unregistered automatons, and deadly poisons flow freely. Officially, the Forge Masters and Consortium deny the Warrens exist; unofficially, city watch marshals and guild calibers are bribed with purified platinum and unrefined starmetal to patrol elsewhere. Access is gained through coal chutes in the Smokestack Alleys, hidden trapdoors beneath the lower cargo docks, secret vaults under the Ironhand Brotherhood's fortified brewery in the Dwarven District, or a concealed water-sluice elevator behind [[Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Eternal Craft/Plane of Eternal Craft Forgeflame Expanse#3. The Rusted Chalice|The Rusted Chalice]].

### **The Fighting Pits: The Smelting Pits (The Ironhand Pits)**

Deep within the subterranean bedrock beneath the Dwarven District and the Slag Warrens lies **The Smelting Pits** (widely known as the **Ironhand Pits**), as chronicled in [[Sessions/Session 4 - Steel, Smoke, and Gold|Session 4 — Steel, Smoke, and Gold]]. Carved out of an ancient subterranean foundry vault, this roaring, multi-tiered circular colosseum is the beating heart of Metallos's criminal bloodsport, high-stakes wagering, and gang diplomacy. 

When the merchant lords and inspectors of the Consortium deadlock in the Hall of the Iron Gavel, or when the four major criminal syndicates of Metallos clash over territory, smelting contracts, or smuggling routes, disputes are settled in the sand with fists, blades, and fire.

#### **The Arena Environment & Hazards**

* **The Sand Ring**: A wide, circular pit covered in thick, dark sand soaked with centuries of spilled blood, heavy stout, and forge soot.
* **The Molten Trenches**: A 10-foot-wide, glowing trench of churning molten metal rings the entire perimeter of the combat floor, just below the reinforced spectator railings.
  * *Hazard*: Any creature shoved, thrown, or falling into the molten trench takes **4d10 fire damage** at the start of its turn (or **2d10 fire damage** on a successful DC 14 Dexterity saving throw if it catches the lip and pulls itself up).
* **Soot Pockets**: The pit floor is littered with mounds of hot foundry soot and brass shavings. Any combatant can use a bonus action to fling a handful of blinding soot into an opponent's eyes (range 5 ft.), forcing a DC 13 Constitution saving throw or blinding the target until the end of its next turn.
* **The Spectator Gantries & Crowd Favor**:
  * Suspended iron gantries loom overhead, packed to the brim with screaming dwarven miners, mercenary fixers, artisans, and syndicate enforcers tossing copper and cheering blood.
  * **Crowd Favor Mechanic**: Once per round on their turn, a combatant can use a bonus action to play to the crowd by making a **DC 14 Charisma (Performance or Intimidation) check**. If successful, their side wins the roaring support of the arena. Whichever combatant or team holds **Crowd Favor** gains a **+1 bonus to attack rolls and saving throws** as the colosseum echoes with their name.

#### **Key Figures & Syndicate Politics**

The Smelting Pits are the battleground for the four underworld syndicates that shape the Metallos black market:

1. **The Ironhand Brotherhood (Dwarven-led Custodians)**:
   * **Insignia**: A black iron gauntlet gripping a gold coin over an anvil.
   * **Role**: The Brotherhood operates out of a massive fortified brewery and tavern in the Dwarven District. They maintain the official betting ledgers, referee contract duels, and manage the pit bank.
   * **Key NPCs**:
     * **Kazrik “The Auditor” Goldmantle**: The calm, immaculate, soft-spoken mastermind who oversees debt, ledgers, and house takes.
     * **Rurik Stonejaw**: The boastful, heavy-drinking dwarven pit champion whose hammer blows are legendary in the arena.
     * **Tivren Gearquill**: The meticulous gnome bookkeeper tracking odds, contracts, and interest.
     * **Merric “Quicklock” Bramblefoot**: Fast-talking halfling scout and safecracker who spots rigged bets and spies.
2. **The Gilded Crucible (Opportunity & Profit Syndicate)**:
   * **Leader**: **Seraphaine "Silvertongue" Vale**, a fluid tiefling mastermind who operates from velvet-draped private booths overlooking the ring. The Crucible coordinates high-stakes wagers, fences exotic relics won in the pits, and maintains a strategic alliance with the Ironhand Brotherhood.
3. **The Crucible Champion: [[NPC's/Dorrim Ashvein|Dorrim Ashvein]]**:
   * A formidable mountain dwarf (*CR 6 Champion*) who notoriously defected from the Ironhand Brotherhood for the Gilded Crucible's gold. Bald, scarred, and covered in glowing runic tattoos, Dorrim is infamous for fighting dirty—blinding opponents with soot, slashing with his *Runic Battleaxe*, battering with bleeding *Spiked Knuckles*, and using his *Shove to the Brim* bonus action to heave rivals directly into the molten trenches.
4. **The Bloodchain Horde (Brutal Expansionists)**:
   * An aggressive orc and goblin gang operating out of the lower docks. They routinely field savage war beasts, berserkers, and pit gladiators in the Smelting Pits to violently challenge the Brotherhood's control over the city's smelting contracts and dock rights.
5. **The Moonveil Accord (Elegant Saboteurs)**:
   * Aloof elven conspirators who disdain dwarven brawling as crude, but use the pit's chaos to broker high-society assassinations, smuggle volatile magic-cores into rival corners, and place poison-tipped bets.

#### **Pit Bounties & Wagers**

* **Contract by Blood**: Merchants deposit disputed smelting quotas and property deeds with Kazrik Goldmantle. Each faction fields a champion; the victor wins the legal rights indisputably.
* **The Gauntlet of Slag**: Open challenge bouts where outside adventurers can battle waves of pit beasts, rogue constructs, and Horde berserkers. Surviving three consecutive rounds pays out **500 gp to 1,000 gp**, plus an honorary Ironhand coin recognized across all underbelly establishments.
* **Champion's Purse**: Defeating a ranked champion like Rurik Stonejaw or Dorrim Ashvein awards up to **2,000 gp**, a *Soul-Forged Weapon* from the Flayed Crucible, and immense underworld renown throughout Metallos.

The atmosphere is hushed and reverent. Magical lights drift through the air like fireflies, and the walls occasionally show brief visions of past magical events—echoes of the spells preserved within.

The **Grove Codex** is Timberforge's most revered and unusual institution. Located within the **Grandmother Root**—the ancient, enormous tree at the center of the Seedling Grove—the Grove Codex offers adventurers, scholars, and artisans access to the collective memory of Timberforge's trees. Because living wood remembers everything it witnesses, the oldest trees of the city hold centuries of accumulated knowledge: battle plans, lost recipes, hidden secrets, and whispered confessions.

The service is tended by **Keeper Aldris of the Deep Root**, an ancient Barkborn being of indeterminate age whose body is partially fused with the Grandmother Root itself.

At the center stands the **Speaking Knot**—a gnarled formation where seven roots converge, and where a supplicant can place their hands to commune with the tree's memory.

The air inside is warm and smells of loam and old rain. Time feels slower here. Many visitors report feeling watched—but not threatened.

* * *

#### **The Keeper: Aldris of the Deep Root**

* **Backstory**: Aldris was once a wood elf artisan who chose to fuse with the Grandmother Root rather than age and die, a practice now forbidden by the Verdant Shapers. They have been Keeper for approximately 400 years.

1. **Living-Wood Blessing**: Aldris can perform a ritual that enchants a wooden weapon or tool, granting it a +1 bonus for one month. Cost: 100 gp and a full day's rest in the Grove.
2. **Commune with Nature (Ritual)**: For 50 gp and an hour's time, Aldris can channel the spell *Commune with Nature* on a supplicant's behalf, answering up to three questions about the natural environment of a specific region.
3. **Seed of Remembrance**: For 500 gp, Aldris will crystallize a significant memory or final words from a dying person into a living seed. When planted, the seed grows into a small tree that whispers the preserved memory to those who place their ear against its bark.

### Taverns

### **1\. The Hollow Hearth**

**Theme**: Cozy Canopy Tavern
**Location**: High in the canopy, built into the hollowed trunk of a centuries-old oak. Accessible by vine-lift or a spiraling staircase carved into the trunk.
**Owner**: **Bryndis Rootale**, a female dwarf who came to Timberforge as a lumberjack and stayed when she realized she loved trees more than she'd expected.
**Description**: The Hollow Hearth is exactly what it sounds like—a warm, enclosed space carved from living wood, where the walls breathe softly and glowing moss provides gentle light. Every table is a cross-section of a great tree, polished smooth and showing its rings. The air smells of woodsmoke, pine, and something sweet Bryndis won't identify. The sounds of the city barely penetrate the thick walls, making this the quietest and most peaceful tavern on the plane.

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **Sapwine** *(House Special)*: A golden, sweet wine pressed from the sap of the Heartwood Forge's outer trees. Mildly intoxicating and surprisingly warming.
* **Rootbrew Ale**: A dark, earthy ale with notes of mushroom and bark. Popular with the Barkborn laborers who gather here after shifts.
* **Green Mead**: A pale, herb-infused mead that tastes faintly of mint and fresh rain. Light and refreshing.
* **Ember-Roasted Haunch**: A haunch of forest animal roasted over enchanted heartwood charcoal, served with braised root vegetables.
* **Canopy Pudding**: A dense, sweet steamed pudding made from harvested berries and preserved sap, served warm with cream.

**Entertainment**:

* **The Ring Count**: Patrons bet on which cross-section table has the most tree rings. Bryndis keeps tally and the winner gets a free round.
* **Carver's Night**: Every tenday, a local artisan brings a work-in-progress and carves at a central table while patrons watch and comment. The finished piece is auctioned when complete.

**Special Features**:

* **The Listening Walls**: The tavern's wood is old enough to remember. On certain nights, Bryndis swears the walls whisper old conversations back—nothing intelligible, just the warm murmur of remembered voices.
* **The Hammock Loft**: An upper level filled with rope hammocks where travelers can rent overnight sleeping space for 3 sp a night. Cheaper than any proper inn and significantly more comfortable than the forest floor.

**Rumor**: Bryndis found a hollow in the trunk that leads somewhere she won't talk about. She sealed it with a carved panel and has a standing rule that no one touches the south wall.

* * *

### **2\. The Tangled Vine**

**Theme**: Lively Social Tavern and Community Hub
**Location**: At the Root Market, at ground level, with tables spilling out onto a broad wooden platform.
**Owner**: **Moss Wickerfen**, a cheerful firbolg of enormous size and even more enormous hospitality.
**Description**: The Tangled Vine is the most social establishment in Timberforge. It is loud, chaotic, and endlessly welcoming. Tables are packed close, the menu is enormous, and Moss himself moves between them like a benevolent force of nature, refilling cups before they are empty and introducing strangers to each other with total confidence. The walls are covered in carved signatures—the traditional way visitors mark their stay in Timberforge.

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **The Verdant Pour** *(House Special)*: A large wooden tankard filled with a rotating selection of whatever the brewers are most proud of that week. Never the same twice.
* **Bark and Bitter**: A dark lager brewed with bark extract and wild hops. Strongly bitter, loved by regulars.
* **Sporecloud Cider**: A slightly fizzy cider infused with beneficial spores that, Moss claims, give the drinker clarity of thought. (This effect is unconfirmed by any healer in the city.)
* **Wanderer's Platter**: A large shared plate of preserved meats, smoked fish, pickled vegetables, and dense travel bread. Enough for four people.
* **The Big Stump**: A massive roasted vegetable dish served in a hollowed-out stump bowl, filled with roots, tubers, mushrooms, and herbs in a rich broth.

**Entertainment**:

* **The Vine-Pull**: A rope-pull competition using actual enchanted vines, conducted in the open-air section. The vine fights back, making it a three-way contest between the two teams and the plant.
* **Open Floor Storytelling**: Moss keeps a carved chair at the center of the tavern reserved for storytellers. Anyone can take it; he rings a small bell to announce the next teller. No interruptions allowed.

**Special Features**:

* **The Signature Wall**: A carved stretch of wall where visitors leave their name, profession, and home plane. It has been growing for over a century. Finding a familiar name on the wall is a DC 12 Investigation check.
* **Moss's Garden Table**: A table in the corner where Moss tends small potted plants between serving rounds. He offers cuttings from them for free to anyone who asks. The plants are completely mundane, but he is very proud of them.

**Rumor**: Moss has never left Timberforge. Not once. Not even to the city outskirts. No one knows why, and he deflects every question about it with a laugh and a refill.

The Timberforge Notice Board is a living entity—a broad slab of the Grandmother Root’s outer bark where notices are pinned with ironwood thorns and expired postings are slowly subsumed back into the tree's living fiber. Monitored by the Verdant Shapers and coordinated by Moss Wickerfen of the Tangled Vine, it is the primary contract exchange for planar woodsmen, rangers, and hunters.
