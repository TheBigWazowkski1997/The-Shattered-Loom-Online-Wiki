# Map & Geography of the Plane of Eternal Craft (Forgeflame Expanse)

![[attachments/plane_of_eternal_craft_map.jpg]]

> _"To walk the Forgeflame Expanse is to witness creation in its rawest, most unyielding forms: where mountains are hewn by divine hammers, rivers flow with liquid brass, forests breathe with conscious intent, and the sky itself turns on adamantine cogs."_
> — Journal of Vexiryn the Unbowed

## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Plane of Eternal Craft Forgeflame Expanse#1. Metallos: The Great Foundry City|Metallos: The Great Foundry City]]
* **Location**: Exact center of the plane.
* **Role**: The industrial heart and economic core of the Expanse.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Colossal Forge**: A towering volcanic structure ignited by Kaldryn's first hammerstrike. The heartbeat of the plane.
  * **The Gearspire**: An immense clockwork spire housing the artificer academies and the mysterious Ethereal Loom.
  * **The Molten Veins (River Ignis)**: Glowing, navigable conduits of liquid metal channeling power throughout the metropolis.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Triumvirate's Iron Caliper, [[../../../NPCs/Hesperus Vane|Hesperus Vane]], alongside [[../../../NPCs/Arbiter Valerie Sterling|Arbiter Valerie Sterling]] of the Hall of the Iron Gavel.

### 2. [[Plane of Eternal Craft Forgeflame Expanse#2. Crystalis: The City of Prismatic Perfection|Crystalis: The City of Prismatic Perfection]]
* **Location**: Far eastern plateau, suspended over the bottomless Luminous Rift Chasm.
* **Role**: Capital of crystal shaping, enchanting, optical science, and gemcraft.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Prism Cathedral**: Colossal translucent edifice refracting subterranean void-light into dazzling auroras.
  * **The Void Terrace**: The lowest hanging platform, suspended directly above the chasm where master weavers channel raw planar light.
  * **The Reflecting Pool**: A basin of liquid quartz that reflects inner memories and emotional resonance.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: [[../../../NPCs/Lady Seraphina Aurum|Lady Seraphina Aurum]], Grand Caliper of Light & Prism.

### 3. [[Plane of Eternal Craft Forgeflame Expanse#3. Timberforge: The Living Workshop|Timberforge: The Living Workshop]]
* **Location**: Southern reaches, situated within the boundless enchanted rainforest.
* **Role**: Center of organic craft, living wood architecture, druidic tailoring, and vine-golem horticulture.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Heartwood Forge**: A colossal living hollow tree powered by superheated burning sap.
  * **The Grandmother Root**: The elder primeval tree housing the living memories of the Grove Codex.
  * **The Canopy Walks**: Interconnected suspension bridges spanning miles through the forest canopy.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: [[../../../NPCs/Elder Oakhaven|Elder Oakhaven]], Grand Caliper of Sap & Sinew, and the Council of Verdant Shapers.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Cinderbrook (The Ingot Wells)
* **Location**: Southwestern riverbanks of River Ignis, halfway between Metallos and the coast.
* **Population**: ~1,800 (Dwarves, Fire Genasi, Warforged).
* **Description**: A soot-cloaked boomtown built around natural cascading sluices of liquid iron. The villagers divert the molten river into stepped stone pools to skim slag and harvest rare alchemical dross.
* **Specialty**: Pure pig iron ingots, refractory crucible bricks, and thermal cooling salts.
* **Notable Feature — The Quench Wheel**: A four-story water wheel made of cast basalt that turns through a river of liquid zinc, generating steam power for the surrounding smelters.

### 2. Slagshore & Smelter's Bay
* **Location**: The western jagged coastline, bordering the Molten Oceans of Liquid Brass & Copper.
* **Population**: ~2,400 (Dwarves, Tieflings, Dragonborn, Githzerai).
* **Description**: A rugged port built atop black obsidian cliffs. Its docks are built from fire-hardened volcanic stone, where heat-shielded ironclad barges deploy heavy ceramic drag-nets into the molten sea to trawl for floating crusts of meteoric gold, celestial brass, and living cinder-coral.
* **Specialty**: Molten-sea navigation charts, heavy heat-resistant diving rigs, and rare volcanic glass.
* **Notable Feature — The Obsidian Lighthouse**: A towering beacon of black glass housing an imprisoned fire titan's spark, warning ships away from boiling copper whirlpools.

### 3. Prismfall
* **Location**: The jagged eastern canyon rim overlooking the northern branch of the Luminous Rift Chasm.
* **Population**: ~1,200 (Elves, Gnomes, Aasimar).
* **Description**: Terraced vertically along sheer crystal cliffs, Prismfall is named for the waterfalls of glowing, bioluminescent dust that spill over the rim like liquid starlight into the abyss below. The village is built on cantilevered glass platforms anchored by adamantine guy-wires.
* **Specialty**: Resonant tuning forks, prismatic lenses, luminescent inks, and geode mining.
* **Notable Feature — The Harmonic Harp**: An enormous array of singing crystal filaments strung across a gorge, played by the chasm's updrafts to forecast planar weather.

### 4. Glassvein
* **Location**: The shimmering Salt-Glass Flats, northeast of Metallos.
* **Population**: ~850 (Gnomes, Humans, Hermit Artificers).
* **Description**: A strange, quiet community situated in a basin of fine white silica sand where frequent planar lightning strikes (discharged from the Clockwork Heavens) fuse the ground into wild, twisting fulgurite glass structures. Artisans here "steer" the lightning strikes using copper lightning towers to sculpt hollow glass monoliths.
* **Specialty**: Lightning-infused glass flasks, optic mirrors for telescopes, and insulating crystal insulators.
* **Notable Feature — The Fulgurite Forest**: A sprawling maze of glassy spires created by millennia of guided lightning.

### 5. Briarhollow (Root-Haven)
* **Location**: The transitional frontier where the southern Artisan's Fields blend into the deep jungle of Timberforge.
* **Population**: ~1,500 (Wood Elves, Firbolgs, Barkborn, Halflings).
* **Description**: Built entirely within the hollowed trunks and entwined branches of massive weeping willows and ironwood trees. The ground is carpeted in soft, glow-in-the-dark moss. Briarhollow acts as the primary agricultural and silvicultural feeder for Timberforge, cultivating enchanted graft-cuttings and living bark.
* **Specialty**: Living seeds, medicinal bark tea, flexible silkwood timber, and tame vine-beasts.
* **Notable Feature — The Nursery Cistern**: A subterranean root chamber holding pure dew gathered from the canopy, used to accelerate the growth of sentient treant saplings.

### 6. Sprocket-in-the-Mist (Cogford)
* **Location**: A geyser-filled volcanic basin southwest of Metallos.
* **Population**: ~1,100 (Rock Gnomes, Autognomes, Kobold Engineers).
* **Description**: A dizzying engineering marvel: an entire town built atop three interlocking, revolving gear platforms suspended over boiling geothermal geysers. The town rotates constantly, aligning different workshop gates with bridges as the gears turn.
* **Specialty**: Clockwork chronometers, pressure valves, miniaturized steam engines, and pneumatic transport tubes.
* **Notable Feature — The Great Escapement**: A massive grandfather-clock pendulum that swings across the town's central chasm every sixty seconds, regulating the town's rotation speed.

### 7. Bastion-Under-Spine (Forge-Hold)
* **Location**: The southern foothills of the Ironspine Range, guarding the primary pass to the Eternal Bastion.
* **Population**: ~3,000 (Warforged of the Covenant, Dwarven Armorsmiths, Planar Mercenaries).
* **Description**: A fortress-village of austere black stone and heavy iron battlements. It serves as the primary waystation, supply cache, and military staging point for pilgrims, trade convoys, and Covenant crusaders preparing to enter the frozen, high-altitude passes of the Ironspine Range.
* **Specialty**: Forgeplate repair, consecrated holy symbols of Kaldryn, rations of condensed soul-cinder, and alpine siege gear.
* **Notable Feature — The Pilgrim's Gate**: A massive gatehouse flanked by two 80-foot statues of Warforged Grand Paladins holding crossed hammers, beneath which all visitors must submit their Soulcores or weapons for inspection.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Clockwork Heavens
* **Description**: Instead of an open astronomical void, the sky of the plane is an interlocking dome of titanic bronze, gold, and iron cogs turning in eternal, silent harmony.
* **Planar Trait**: The turning of the gears regulates time, gravity, and the flow of planar leylines. Crafters can attune to the celestial tempo; those who succeed on a saving throw Arcana check can enter a "Temporal Flow State," doubling their crafting progress without suffering exhaustion.

### 2. The Ironspine Range & [[../../../Factions/The Ironbound Covenant#The Eternal Bastion (Primary Fortress — Plane of Eternal Craft)|The Eternal Bastion]]
* **Description**: A jagged, razor-sharp mountain range of raw iron ore, granite, and magnetic lodestone in the northern hemisphere.
* **The Eternal Bastion**: The supreme fortress-monastery of [[../../../Factions/The Ironbound Covenant|The Ironbound Covenant]], carved directly into the heart of Mount Dreadnought. It houses the **Vigil Flame** (kindled directly from Kaldryn's hearth), the **Bound Archive** (holding the sacred Ironscript Leaves and Vault 7), and the living command of Forge-Herald [[../../../NPCs/Urdakael, the Unbroken|Urdakael, the Unbroken]].

### 3. The Whispering Stones
* **Location**: An ancient standing ring of megaliths nestled in an alpine plateau east of the Eternal Bastion.
* **Mystical Property**: Every item, weapon, or construct ever hammered or forged on any plane echoes through these basalt monoliths. Artificers who meditate here during a long rest can cast *Legend Lore* or *Identify* at will on any masterwork item by listening to the stone's vibrations.

### 4. The Luminous Rift Chasm
* **Location**: The bottomless abyss beneath Crystalis.
* **Mystical Property**: A tear in the planar crust that exposes raw, primordial light-energy trailing from the Shattering of the Loom. While it provides boundless power for prism-shapers and enchanters, aberrant creatures drawn to entropic fractures occasionally crawl up from its lightless depths.

### 5. The Molten Oceans of Liquid Brass and Copper
* **Location**: The entire western edge of the known expanse.
* **Mystical Property**: Churning seas of molten metallics heated by subterranean planar mantles. The seas have their own ecosystem of magma-crabs, fire elementals, and cinder-wyrms. Crossing requires reinforced obsidian-clad steam-trawlers or planar flight.

### 6. The Artisan's Fields
* **Location**: The vast tracts of territory buffering Metallos, Crystalis, and Timberforge.
* **Mystical Property**: Enchanted self-regenerating terrain. Stone outcroppings naturally regrow into geometric marble blocks, felled ironwood saplings regrow in tendays, and mineral veins replenish their gold and adamantine reserves overnight.

### 7. Floating Workshop Atolls
* **Location**: Mobile; drifting across the thermals between Metallos and Crystalis.
* **Mystical Property**: Massive floating chunks of buoyant lodestone outfitted with windmills, brass balloons, and private master-workshops. Wealthy inventors and reclusive hermits build independent research laboratories here to avoid guild taxes.

| Route Name | From / To | Distance / Travel Time | Hazards / Features |
|---|---|---|---|
| **The Great Crucible Way** | Metallos $\rightarrow$ Bastion-Under-Spine | 120 miles (4 days on foot, 1.5 days by Cog-Rail) | Volcanic ash storms, magnetic anomalies, Covenant patrols |
| **The Prismatic Traverse** | Metallos $\rightarrow$ Crystalis | 95 miles (3 days on foot, 1 day by Light-Skiff) | Blinding sun glares, crystal refraction mirages, rogue glass elementals |
| **The Sunken Root Causeway** | Metallos $\rightarrow$ Timberforge | 80 miles (2.5 days on foot, 1 day by Barge) | Overgrowing vines, quick-moss pits, awakened briar beasts |
