# 🏡 The Anvil-Hearth Lodge
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Eternal Craft*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Eternal Craft will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Forge-Mistress Danielle (Azer-Dwarf Master Artisan, CR 12)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Anvil-Hearth Lodge** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Eternal Craft/Plane of Eternal Craft Forgeflame Expanse|Plane of Eternal Craft]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Base of the Great Crucible, near the Foundry Citadels of Kaldryn
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Forge-Mistress Danielle (Azer-Dwarf Master Artisan, CR 12)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A fortress built around a thermal magma flume where the sound of hammers never ceases. Hunters forge their own weapons alongside celestial dwarven smiths while tasting smoked meats cooked over crucible flames.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Eternal Craft:

* **Processing & Salting**: Master Crucible Forge, Slag-Separation Sump, Tempering Oil Pools, Blueprints Library.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
