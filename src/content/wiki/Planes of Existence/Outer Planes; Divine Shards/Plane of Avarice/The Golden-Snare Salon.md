# 🏡 The Golden-Snare Salon
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Avarice*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Avarice will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Lord Victor Vance (Avaricious Human Slayer, CR 12)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Golden-Snare Salon** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Avarice/Plane of Avarice The Gilded Eternity|Plane of Avarice]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Auris Spire, overlooking the sea of molten coinage in Grelthor's domain
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Lord Victor Vance (Avaricious Human Slayer, CR 12)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A lavish hunting salon with silk divans, gold-plated taxidermy, and crystal decanters of spiced nectar. Guild members pay exorbitant entry fees to pursue beasts whose bones are literal platinum and gold.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Avarice:

* **Processing & Salting**: Jeweled Skinning Tables, Assay Office, Gilding Forge, Trophy Auction Room.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
