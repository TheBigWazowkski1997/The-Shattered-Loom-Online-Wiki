# 🏡 The Maw-Rim Camp
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — Plane of Infinite Hunger*

> *"The wilderness of Plane of Infinite Hunger will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Grok Bone-Chewer (Minotaur Beast-Slayer, CR 13)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Maw-Rim Camp** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Infinite Hunger/Plane of Infinite Hunger The Spiral Abyss|Plane of Infinite Hunger]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Upper rim of the great maw vortex, Tier 3 of the Spiral
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Grok Bone-Chewer (Minotaur Beast-Slayer, CR 13)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A grim encampment anchored by heavy pitons to the crumbling rim of the hungry vortex. Massive iron spires roast entire carcass sides over bonfires of petrified teeth, while hunters compete in feats of raw physical endurance.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of Plane of Infinite Hunger:

* **Processing & Salting**: Bone-Marrow Press, Acid-Hardened Anvil, Meat-Smoking Spits, Heavy Winch Station.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.

## 🦁 Primary Apex Quarry: The Chasm-Devourer Larva (CR 13)

The primary monster tracked and harvested from this lodge is the feared **The Chasm-Devourer Larva**.

* **Creature Profile**: *A segmented, burrowing worm 50 feet in length, possessing three concentric rings of rotating teeth and multiple acid glands. It bores through solid planar stone at terrifying speed, leaving tunnels lined with corrosive digestive fluids.*
* **Type & Alignment**: `Huge Monstrosity, Chaotic Evil`
* **Harvest Check DC**: **DC 23**
