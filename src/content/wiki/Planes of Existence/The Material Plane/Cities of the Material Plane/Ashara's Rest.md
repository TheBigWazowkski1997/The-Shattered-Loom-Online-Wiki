# Cities

### **1. Ashara’s Rest**

- **Description**: A bustling port city built atop towering cliffs overlooking a stormy sea. Known for its vibrant trade and naval strength, it serves as a hub for adventurers and merchants.
    
- **Government**: Ruled by the **Council of Tides**, a group of merchants, adventurers, and scholars.
    
- **Primary Exports**: Fish, rare oceanic gems, enchanted maritime artifacts.
    
- **Primary Imports**: Exotic spices, wood, and magical goods from far-off lands.
    

---

## **Geography and Layout**

Ashara’s Landing sprawls across three major tiers carved into the cliffs and cliffsides. Each tier has distinct districts and serves different functions for the city.

### **1. The Harbor Tier (Lower City)**

- Located at the base of the cliffs, the Harbor Tier is the bustling heart of Ashara’s maritime trade.
    
- **Notable Features**:
    

- **The Iron Lighthouse**: A towering structure of enchanted iron and crystal, powered by ancient arcane energy. It emits a brilliant beam of light that cuts through even the densest storms, protecting ships from the jagged reefs surrounding the harbor.
    
- **The Tidefall Market**: A sprawling bazaar where merchants sell exotic goods, including enchanted fishing nets, storm glass (magical crystal that stores lightning energy), and rare sea creatures.
    
- **The Docks**: A maze of piers and warehouses constantly alive with the movement of sailors, cargo, and adventurers.
    

---

### **2. The Clifftop Promenade (Middle City)**

- The middle tier houses most of the city’s residents, businesses, and administrative buildings. Its architecture features sturdy stone buildings and narrow streets that curve with the cliffs.
    
- **Notable Features**:
    

- **The Sailor’s Rest**: An enormous inn and tavern that serves as a meeting point for adventurers, sailors, and mercenaries. Its walls are covered with maps, old ship wheels, and trophies from past expeditions.
    
- **Council Hall of Tides**: A grand hall carved into the cliffside, where the ruling council meets. The building’s interior is decorated with murals depicting Ashara’s founding and maritime victories.
    
- **The Shrine of the Tides**: A temple dedicated to **Nereia, the Depthmother**, goddess of water and protection. Pilgrims often pray here before venturing out to sea.
    

---

### **3. The Upper Crags (High City)**

- The highest tier is reserved for the wealthy elite and important institutions, providing breathtaking views of the ocean and the city below.
    
- **Notable Features**:
    

- **Skyspire Academy**: A renowned magical college specializing in weather and water-based magic, training mages to navigate and manipulate storms.
    
- **The Stormcaller Observatory**: A tower where astronomers and storm mages study the weather patterns of the Storm-Touched Seas. The Observatory houses powerful artifacts used to predict storms and channel lightning energy.
    
- **The Reefborn Estate**: A mansion owned by the **Reefborn Guild**, a powerful faction of sailors and adventurers who specialize in salvaging wrecked ships and sunken treasures.
    

Shops

### **1. The Gilded Gear** — Artificer & Clockwork Shop

**Location/Description:** Brass gears line the walls, steam hisses from hidden pipes, and tiny clockwork creatures scuttle along shelves. **Shopkeeper:** **Tivren Cogspinner**, a gnome artificer with goggles perpetually fogged from experiments. Cheerful but prone to distraction.

**Items:**

**Mechanical Owl Familiar** _(Rare)_ — A small clockwork owl that can fly, scout, and deliver messages within 60 ft. Returns to owner automatically after 1 hour.

**Spring-Loaded Boots** _(Uncommon)_ — Once per short rest, add 10 ft to movement and jump distance.

**Tinker’s Quick-Fuse** _(Uncommon)_ — 3 uses per day: instantly repair or modify one mechanical device or trap within 1 minute.

**Rumors & Hooks:**

- A clockwork hummingbird has been spotted **outside town, moving on its own**, but no one knows who built it. Tivren may know.
    
- Some say Tivren is **searching for a “missing gear of infinite power”**, which could make his inventions sentient.
    
- Side NPC: **Petra Fizzbang**, Tivren’s apprentice, is secretly trying to make a **clockwork dragon**.
    

---

### **2. Shadow & Silk** — Enchanter & Curiosities

**Location/Description:** Dimly lit boutique of black velvet and silver mirrors; items shimmer with faint enchantment. **Shopkeeper:** **Elaria Moonveil**, an elf sorceress who speaks softly but sees everything. Highly secretive.

**Items:**

1. **Veil of Whispered Secrets** _(Rare)_ — Grants advantage on Deception and Stealth checks while worn. Once per long rest, you may cast _Suggestion_ (saving throw).
    
2. **Ring of the Phantom Step** _(Very Rare)_ — Once per long rest, cast _Misty Step_ without expending a spell slot.
    
3. **Mirror Dust** _(Uncommon)_ — Sprinkle on a reflective surface to reveal invisible creatures within 20 ft.
    

**Rumors & Hooks:**

- A wealthy noble recently **disappeared after purchasing a mirror from the shop**; some claim the mirrors trap souls.
    
- Elaria is rumored to be **collecting whispers from across the city** — anyone who speaks secrets to her may regret it.
    
- Side NPC: **Lirien, the Mirror Sprites’ Guardian**, small fey creatures hiding in the shop, who can provide tips or tricks for stealthy adventurers.
    

---

### **3. Ember & Ash** — Pyromancer’s Supplies

**Location/Description:** Warm, cozy shop with constantly glowing embers and flame dancing along enchanted braziers. **Shopkeeper:** **Korbin Firehand**, a human pyromancer with singed hair and a booming laugh. Loves teaching sparks to children.

**Items:**

1. **Flamejar** _(Uncommon)_ — A glass jar containing a small, controllable flame. Can cast _Firebolt_ 1/day (1d10 fire damage).
    
2. **Ember Cloak** _(Rare)_ — Grants resistance to fire damage; once per short rest, you may surround yourself in flames for 1 turn, dealing 1d6 fire damage to attackers who hit you.
    
3. **Spark Gloves** _(Uncommon)_ — Allows you to ignite small flammable objects at will; once per long rest, cast _Burning Hands_ (1st level).
    

**Rumors & Hooks:**

- Villagers say a **small fire demon** has been seen dancing in the nearby woods, possibly linked to Ember & Ash’s experiments.
    
- Korbin is rumored to be **training a child prodigy to harness fire magic**, but the prodigy has gone missing.
    
- Side NPC: **Cinder**, a tiny flame spirit that Korbin accidentally bound to a teacup; mischievous but helpful.
    

---

### **4. The Verdant Nook** — Herbalist & Alchemy

**Location/Description:** Shelves of dried plants, glowing fungi, and bubbling vials; fragrant with earth and flowers. **Shopkeeper:** **Mira Leafwhisper**, a halfling druid with a pet chameleon, gentle and motherly.

**Items:**

1. **Vial of Verdant Growth** _(Uncommon)_ — Pour on dead soil to instantly grow 5 ft radius of plants. Once per day, cast _Entangle_.
    
2. **Healing Tea of the Moon** _(Rare)_ — Brewed tea restores 2d8 HP and removes one condition (poisoned, frightened, or paralyzed).
    
3. **Chameleon Cloak** _(Uncommon)_ — Grants advantage on Stealth checks in natural environments; once per day, you may blend perfectly with surroundings for 1 minute.
    

**Rumors & Hooks:**

- Strange **glowing mushrooms** are appearing in the town sewers — Mira may know their origin.
    
- A group of **poachers seeks a rare plant** that Mira keeps hidden; she may hire adventurers for protection.
    
- Side NPC: **Thistle**, Mira’s chameleon familiar, can spy and deliver messages unnoticed.
    

---

### **5. Iron & Bone** — Necromancer’s Curiosity Shop

**Location/Description:** Dark, eerie, with bone ornaments and skeletal displays. Candles flicker constantly. **Shopkeeper:** **Tharivol Duskwalker**, a reclusive elf necromancer; polite but unnervingly quiet.

**Items:**

1. **Ring of Bone Ward** _(Rare)_ — Grants +1 AC; once per long rest, cast _False Life_ on yourself.
    
2. **Wand of Spectral Grasp** _(Very Rare)_ — Once per day, summon spectral hands to grapple or restrain creatures within 30 ft (Strength saving throw to resist).
    
3. **Necrotic Vial** _(Uncommon)_ — Splash on a weapon to deal +1d4 necrotic damage for 1 hour.
    

**Rumors & Hooks:**

- A **graveyard near town has been moving its tombstones**, and villagers are afraid; Tharivol may know why.
    
- Tharivol is rumored to **seek a lost tome of forbidden necromancy**, possibly hidden in the city.
    
- Side NPC: **Rattik**, a skeletal raven that speaks cryptic warnings, may guide adventurers to hidden secrets.
    

---

### **6. The Astral Compass** — Explorer & Planar Gear

**Location/Description:** Maps, telescopes, and glowing orreries; faint hum of planar energy in the air. **Shopkeeper:** **Jax Talemapper**, a half-elf ranger with a patch over one eye, constantly scribbling in a notebook.

**Items:**

1. **Planar Compass** _(Rare)_ — Points toward the nearest planar portal or waypoint within 1 mile.
    
2. **Traveler’s Boots** _(Uncommon)_ — Move across difficult terrain without penalty; once per day, cast _Feather Fall_ on self.
    
3. **Starforged Rope** _(Uncommon)_ — A 50 ft rope that can support up to 1000 lbs; once per day, can animate to climb or tie itself.
    

**Rumors & Hooks:**

- Travelers swear they saw **a glowing portal appear in the woods** near town; Jax might know where it leads.
    
- Jax has been **mapping an uncharted section of the Astral Plane** and seeks adventurers to scout it.
    
- Side NPC: **Oris**, a tiny elemental familiar that can phase through solid matter, helps locate hidden paths.
    

---

### **7. Crystal & Coil** — Magic Item & Trinket Emporium

**Location/Description:** Glittering with crystals, gemstones, and trinkets that hum faintly. **Shopkeeper:** **Liora Gemwhisper**, a tiefling with crystalline horns and a mischievous grin.

**Items:**

1. **Orb of Captured Sound** _(Uncommon)_ — Record and replay up to 1 minute of sound per long rest.
    
2. **Gem of Foresight** _(Rare)_ — Once per long rest, gain advantage on one attack, saving throw, or ability check.
    
3. **Cloak of Prismed Light** _(Very Rare)_ — As a reaction when hit, emit dazzling light; creatures within 10 ft must succeed on a saving throw Dex save or be blinded until the start of your next turn.
    

**Rumors & Hooks:**

- Some say Liora has **a gem that can show the future**, but its visions sometimes drive people mad.
    
- Liora is rumored to **trade with planar merchants**; some items in her shop may have unusual effects.
    
- Side NPC: **Shimmer**, a mischievous imp bound to the shop, can provide tips or cause harmless chaos if persuaded.

## **Notable NPCs**

### **1. Captain Lyara Stormborn**

- **Role**: Leader of the Reefborn Guild
    
- **Appearance**: A half-elf with sea-green eyes and hair braided with pearls and shark teeth. She wields a magical cutlass called **Stormbite**, which crackles with lightning.
    
- **Personality**: Bold, brash, and fiercely protective of her crew.
    
- **Hook**: Captain Lyara seeks adventurers to retrieve a powerful artifact called the **Heart of the Maelstrom**, rumored to be hidden within a storm-wracked island.
    

---

### **2. Elder Orrick Deepwake**

- **Role**: High Priest of the Shrine of the Tides
    
- **Appearance**: An elderly dwarf with a flowing white beard and robes adorned with seashells.
    
- **Personality**: Calm and wise, with a deep reverence for the ocean.
    
- **Hook**: Orrick believes a strange storm is a sign of Nereia’s displeasure. He tasks the party with investigating the source of the disturbance.
    

---

### **3. Professor Sarya Wavetouch**

- **Role**: Head of Skyspire Academy
    
- **Appearance**: A human woman with flowing blue robes and hair streaked with silver, resembling waves.
    
- **Personality**: Brilliant and eccentric, with a tendency to get lost in her research.
    
- **Hook**: Sarya hires the party to test a new magical artifact—a **Storm Engine**—designed to calm storms, but her experiments draw the ire of elemental beings.
    

---

## **Factions**

### **1. The Reefborn Guild**

- A powerful collective of sailors, divers, and salvagers who specialize in recovering treasures from shipwrecks and lost civilizations beneath the sea.
    
- **Goal**: Expand their influence by controlling the trade of rare goods salvaged from the depths.
    
- **Hook**: The guild discovers a cursed artifact in a sunken ship, and its presence threatens the city.
    

---

### **2. The Skycallers**

- A loose alliance of storm sorcerers, druids, and scholars dedicated to mastering weather magic and understanding the Storm-Touched Seas.
    
- **Goal**: Harness the power of storms to defend Ashara’s Landing from invaders and natural disasters.
    
- **Hook**: The Skycallers suspect a rival faction of sabotaging their efforts to stabilize a massive storm threatening the harbor.
    

---

### **3. The Black Tide**

- A shadowy network of smugglers and pirates who operate under the guise of legitimate merchants.
    
- **Goal**: Disrupt trade routes and establish dominance over the Tidefall Market.
    
- **Hook**: The Black Tide is rumored to have acquired an artifact capable of summoning a kraken, and the party must stop them before they unleash it.
