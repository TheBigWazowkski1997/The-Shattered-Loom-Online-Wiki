# 🏡 The Silver-Bough Lodge
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — The Feywild*

> *"The wilderness of The Feywild will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Lady Vaelen Sylph-Treader (Eladrin Ranger, CR 10)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Silver-Bough Lodge** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild|The Feywild]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Canopy threshold of the Moonlit Glade, border of Senaliesse
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Lady Vaelen Sylph-Treader (Eladrin Ranger, CR 10)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: Woven directly into living silver-bark willow trees connected by rope bridges of spun starlight. Bioluminescent butterflies illuminate tables where hunters sip elderflower mead and trade enchanted pelts.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of The Feywild:

* **Processing & Salting**: Living-Wood Fletchery, Fey-Mist Salting Cellar, Moon-Water Scrying Basin, Velvet Tannery.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
