# 🏡 The Cinder-Lantern Lodge
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — The Shadowfell*

> *"The wilderness of The Shadowfell will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Jarek the Blind Tracker (Shadar-kai Slayer, CR 11)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Cinder-Lantern Lodge** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell|The Shadowfell]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: The Mist-Gorged Ravine between Barovia and Darkon
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Jarek the Blind Tracker (Shadar-kai Slayer, CR 11)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A grim, basalt fortress surrounded by iron lanterns burning lavender corpse-oil to repel mist-wraiths. The Great Hall is hung with dried sorrowsworn claws and preserved gargoyle wings.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of The Shadowfell:

* **Processing & Salting**: Anti-Gloom Salt Vats, Shadow-Forge, Memory-Stripping Still, Raven-Perch Messenger Eyrie.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.

## 🦁 Primary Apex Quarry: The Gloom-Weaver Arachnid (CR 10)

The primary monster tracked and harvested from this lodge is the feared **The Gloom-Weaver Arachnid**.

* **Creature Profile**: *A grotesque, eight-legged predator whose carapace resembles cracked obsidian tombstones. It weaves webs not of silk, but of condensed shadow and mournful memories that drain heat and life from ensnared prey.*
* **Type & Alignment**: `Huge Monstrosity (Undead), Neutral Evil`

## 🪙 Lodge Store: Specialized Regional Goods

Hunters can redeem their **Spoor Marks** or trade gold and monster components for specialized supplies:

* **Regional Specialties**: Lantern of Warded Mists (disperses planar choking fog in 30 ft), Sorrow-Ward Oil (advantage vs despair effects), Ash-Salt Rations.
* **Standard Hunt Provisions**: *Lodge Salting Barrel* (25 gp), *Planar Tracking Kit* (50 gp), *Trapper's Spoor Snuff* (15 gp).
