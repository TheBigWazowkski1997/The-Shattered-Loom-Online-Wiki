# Map & Geography of the Shadowfell (Realm of Shadow & Domains of Dread)

![[shadowfell_map.jpg]]

From the blood-mills of Barovia and the shifting eldritch corridors of Darkon, to the galvanic laboratories of Lamordia and the gilded masquerades of Dementlieu, each domain is an inescapable prison where time rots, soul-threads bleed, and the Dark Powers feed on the vanity of the damned."_

## D&D 5e Adaptation & Canon Map References

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### Adapted Directly from D&D 5e Canon
> The Shadowfell within **Project Shattered Loom** integrates the canonical gothic horror setting of **Ravenloft** (*Curse of Strahd* & *Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft*), seamlessly adapted into the cosmic architecture of the Shattered Loom:
> 
> * **The Valley of Barovia**: Uses the canonical **Map of Barovia** by Mike Schley (*Curse of Strahd*), covering Castle Ravenloft, the Svalich Woods, Lake Zarovich, Mount Baratok, Mount Ghakis, the Old Bonegrinder, and the Amber Temple. In this campaign, the castle's **Heart of Sorrow** anchors the **Loom Fragment of the Crimson Weave**.
> * **Darkon & Castle Avernus**: Uses canonical cartography of Darkon (Necropolis, Martira Bay, Rivalis, Nartok) and the shifting non-Euclidean blueprints of Castle Avernus.
> * **Dementlieu & Port-a-Lucine**: Sourced from *Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft*, featuring the gaslit canals of Port-a-Lucine, the Grand Chateaux, and the treacherous Glass Gardens.
> * **Lamordia & Ludendorf**: Features the Isle of Agony, Schloss Mordenheim, the industrial port of Ludendorf, and the frozen heights of Mount Grysl.
> * **Har'Akir**: Features the Oasis of Muhar, the Black Pyramid of Pharaoh Ankhtepot, and the sunken catacombs of Sekhet.
> * **Falkovnia & Lekar**: Features the fortress city of Lekar, the spiked stockades, Morfenzi, and the sprawling zombie warzones of the Dead Fields.
> * **Borca**: Features the opulent twin estates of Levkarest and Lechberg, the Poison Gardens of Misericordia, and the Great Cathedral of Ezra.
> * **Valachan**: Features the stilt village of Oselo, the stepped pyramid of Shuaran, and the untamed wilderness of the Twilight Jungle.
> * **The Fortress of Memories**: Sourced from *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes*, the obsidian throne where the Raven Queen reaps memories sheared from the Loom.

## The Nine Great Realms & Strongholds

### 1. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell#2. Castle Ravenloft — The Predator’s Eye|Barovia: Castle Ravenloft & Vallaki]]
* **Location**: Central misty valley flanked by Mount Baratok and Mount Ghakis.
* **Role**: The gothic heart of dread, harnessing the **Crimson Weave (Loom Fragment)** to defy entropy.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **Castle Ravenloft**: A predatory citadel perched atop a 1,000-foot pillar of black stone, housing the Heart of Sorrow.
  * **Vallaki**: The walled bastion of forced cheer, governed by Baron Vallakovich and contested by Lady Fiona Wachter.
  * **The Amber Temple**: Ancient mountain vault holding the amber sarcophagi of the Dark Powers.
* **Darklord**: **Count Strahd von Zarovich**, supreme master of the valley and vampire lord.

### 2. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell#2. Darkon|Darkon: Castle Avernus & Necropolis]]
* **Location**: Northern expanse of obsidian ridges and mist-bound coasts.
* **Role**: The sprawling empire of the intelligent dead, crumbling under the mysterious absence of its Lich-King.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **Castle Avernus**: Shifting, non-Euclidean doomspire pulsing with wild magic rifts and ancient grimoires.
  * **Necropolis (Il Aluk)**: Metropolis ruled entirely by undead patricians, centered around the Ebon Market.
  * **Martira Bay**: Salt-crusted port city and headquarters of the Kargat secret police.
* **Darklord / Leadership**: **Azalin Rex** (absent sovereign), governed de facto by Vhorkas the Unshackled and the Kargat.

### 3. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell#3. Dementlieu: The Masquerade of Gilded Rot|Dementlieu: Port-a-Lucine & The Grand Chateaux]]
* **Location**: Western coastline overlooking the Grand Canal and the misty bay.
* **Role**: Capital of courtly intrigue, psychological terror, and high-society masquerades.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Grand Chateaux**: Duchess Saidra's palatial residence, hosting the compulsory weekly Grand Masquerade.
  * **The Glass Gardens**: A conservatory of razor-sharp glass flora and toxic floral vapors.
  * **The Opera of Whispers**: Grand theater where treason and scandalous secrets are performed in song.
* **Darklord**: **Duchess Saidra d’Honaire**, tormented by fatal impostor syndrome.

### 4. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell#4. Lamordia: The Galvanic Frost of Hubris|Lamordia: Ludendorf & Schloss Mordenheim]]
* **Location**: Sub-arctic northern fjords battered by perpetual blizzards and galvanic storms.
* **Role**: Hub of gothic science, galvanic engineering, and unethical fleshcrafting.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **Schloss Mordenheim**: Windswept fortress on the Isle of Agony bristling with colossal lightning towers.
  * **Ludendorf University**: Sprawling medical and engineering academy with vast anatomical vaults.
  * **Mount Grysl**: Jagged peak carrying ancient galvanic lightning pylons.
* **Darklord**: **Dr. Viktra Mordenheim**, obsessed with reclaiming her synthetic creation, Elise.

### 5. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell#5. Har’Akir: The Sunless Sands of Eternity|Har’Akir: The Black Pyramid & Muhar]]
* **Location**: Vast arid desert of shifting red and black dunes trapped in permanent twilight.
* **Role**: Ancient realm of desiccating heat, mummy curses, and forgotten burial dynasties.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Black Pyramid**: Monolithic granite tomb radiating freezing necrotic power, resting place of Ankhtepot.
  * **The Oasis of Muhar**: Fortified mud-brick city sustained by deep subterranean cisterns.
  * **The Sunken City of Sekhet**: Buried ancient capital emerging periodically during violent sandstorms.
* **Darklord**: **Pharaoh Ankhtepot**, the desiccated god-king trapped in eternal preservation without worship.

### 6. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell#6. Falkovnia: The Spiked Bastion of Endless Siege|Falkovnia: Lekar & The Dead Fields]]
* **Location**: Ash-choked warzone surrounded by concentric spiked stockades and muddy trenches.
* **Role**: Brutal military dictatorship under constant, unceasing siege by the walking dead.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **Lekar (The Citadel)**: Impregnable fortress capital bristling with ironwood pikes and pitch cauldrons.
  * **The Dead Fields**: Scorched wasteland churning with millions of buried corpses that rise during the New Moon.
  * **Morfenzi Watch-Citadel**: Northern mountain bastion guarding the primary pass through the Razor Range.
* **Darklord**: **General Vladeska Drakov**, doomed to fight an unwinnable war against the grave.

### 7. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell#7. Borca: The Decadence of Poison and Petals|Borca: Levkarest & Misericordia]]
* **Location**: Picturesque alpine valley of terraced vineyards, glacial streams, and Renaissance estates.
* **Role**: Epicenter of Machiavellian political intrigue, lethal perfumes, and cold-war dynastic feuds.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Great Cathedral of Ezra**: Massive marble basilica and supreme seat of the Church of Ezra.
  * **Misericordia & The Poison Gardens**: Lady Ivana Boritsi's palatial estate and toxic botanic laboratories.
  * **Lechberg Fortress**: Ivan Dilisnya's mountain estate filled with lethal clockwork toys and poisoned pastries.
* **Darklords**: **Ivana Boritsi & Ivan Dilisnya**, estranged cousins locked in a permanent war of assassination.

### 8. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell#8. Valachan: The Law of the Apex Hunt|Valachan: Oselo & The Temple of Shuaran]]
* **Location**: Dense, suffocating tropical rainforest trapped in eternal twilight beneath a triple canopy.
* **Role**: Primitive survival arena where civilized law is supplanted by the law of predator and prey.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Stilt-Village of Oselo**: Fortified village raised on 20-foot ironwood pilings over crocodile rivers.
  * **The Pyramid of Shuaran**: Stepped obsidian ziggurat dedicated to forgotten panther gods, seat of Chakuna.
  * **The Twilight Jungle**: Uncharted wilderness filled with carnivorous flora, displacement beasts, and quicksand.
* **Darklord**: **Chakuna the Blood Huntress**, alpha werepanther cursed to constantly hunt or suffer necrosis.

### 9. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell#The Raven Queen|The Fortress of Memories: The Obsidian Spire]]
* **Location**: Far western perimeter of the Shadowfell, carved into jagged peaks jutting out over the Shadowsea.
* **Role**: Seat of the Raven Queen, collecting extracted memories and soul-threads from across the multiverse.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Memory Scriptorium**: Vast chambers where Shadar-kai catalog memories sheared from mortal minds before death.
  * **The Shadow Lattice**: Ethereal webs connecting the fortress to shadow-crossings throughout the Material Plane.
  * **The Pool of Black Ice**: A freezing basin where petitioners surrender traumatic memories in exchange for dark boons.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **The Raven Queen**, assisted by her Shadar-kai death-clerics and shadow dragons.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. The Village of Barovia (The Blood-Farm)
* **Location**: Eastern Svalich Valley beneath the cliff of Castle Ravenloft.
* **Population**: ~700 (Humans under Thread-Staple bondage, Vampire Spawn overseers).
* **Description**: A bleak hamlet where residents are treated as living cellars. The sound of rhythmic Blood-Mills echoes day and night.
* **Specialty**: Blood-wine barrels, preserved funeral vestments, and silver-plated wolf charms.
* **Notable Feature — The Tax-Gallows**: Central square platform where debtors who fail the Vein-Levee are exsanguinated.

### 2. Krezk (The Abbey Sanctuary)
* **Location**: Western foothills of Mount Baratok, protected by massive stone walls.
* **Population**: ~400 (Humans, Mongrelfolk).
* **Description**: An austere, isolationist commune built beneath the Abbey of Saint Markovia, sustained by a miraculous blessed spring.
* **Specialty**: Pure mountain well-water, defensive masonry, and herbal sheep-salves.
* **Notable Feature — The Abbot's Menagerie**: The cliff-side sanitarium holding flesh-sculpted chimerae.

### 3. Martira Bay (The Shadowed Port)
* **Location**: The cold, jagged northern coast of Darkon.
* **Population**: ~8,500 (Living mortals, Ghouls, Kargat Inquisitors, Shadar-kai traders).
* **Description**: A salt-caked, foggy port city filled with stone warehouses, petrified wood docks, and iron merchant cogges.
* **Specialty**: Salt-cured fish, embalming resins, forged travel papers, and shadow-skiff charters.
* **Notable Feature — The Black Watchtower**: Citadel where the Kargat secret police interrogate planar travelers.

### 4. Chateaufaux (The Oblivion Vineyards)
* **Location**: Inland hills of Dementlieu, bordering the southern mists.
* **Population**: ~1,800 (Human vintners, Masked retainers).
* **Description**: A picturesque village surrounded by manicured grapevines, where wine is distilled with memory-suppressing floral draughts.
* **Specialty**: Oblivion vintage wines, decorative porcelain masks, and candied glass violets.
* **Notable Feature — The Wine Cisterns**: Underground cellars where aging wine is infused with whispered secrets.

### 5. Neufurchtenburg (The Glacier Foundry)
* **Location**: Southern alpine foothills of Mount Grysl in Lamordia.
* **Population**: ~2,200 (Miners, Dwarf ironworkers, University field researchers).
* **Description**: A soot-stained mining town clustered around deep glacial shafts where miners unearth prehistoric frozen behemoths.
* **Specialty**: Anthracite coal, raw iron ore, cryo-preserved anatomical specimens, and heavy steam drills.
* **Notable Feature — The Great Sluice**: A geothermal steam pipeline melting ice bridges to keep transit open.

### 6. Morfenzi (The Watch-Citadel)
* **Location**: Northern mountain pass guarding the border of Falkovnia.
* **Population**: ~3,000 (Iron Talon legionnaires, Conscript battalions).
* **Description**: A forbidding stone redoubt bristling with ballistas, ironwood stakes, and heliograph signaling towers.
* **Specialty**: Anti-undead siege bolts, trench pikes, heavy armor repair, and field triage medicine.
* **Notable Feature — The Beacon of Cinders**: A colossal iron brazier that burns day and night to spot approaching zombie waves.

### 7. Stenger (The River Mint)
* **Location**: The banks of the River Luna in southern Borca.
* **Population**: ~4,500 (Merchant bankers, Tasters Guild apprentices, Alchemists).
* **Description**: A bustling commercial town famous for banking houses, fine porcelain workshops, and ruthless debt courts.
* **Specialty**: Silver bullion, engraved poison-testing cutlery, promissory bonds, and scented parchment.
* **Notable Feature — The Luna Vaults**: Subterranean river vaults guarded by water-elemental locks.

### 8. Shuaran (The Ziggurat Outpost)
* **Location**: Deep river junction in eastern Valachan.
* **Population**: ~950 (Tribal scouts, Free hunters, Werepanther initiates).
* **Description**: A jungle outpost clustered around the base of an ancient obsidian pyramid choked with strangler vines.
* **Specialty**: Curare venoms, chameleon cloaks, dugout river canoes, and obsidian weapons.
* **Notable Feature — The Blood-Carved Altar**: Where hunters present trophies to earn Chakuna's temporary favor.

### 9. Berez (The Drowned Ruins)
* **Location**: Southern marsh basin along the River Ivlis in Barovia.
* **Population**: ~120 (Scarecrow Constructs, Baba Lysaga's Coven, Giant Toads).
* **Description**: An abandoned village sunken into muddy bog-water, cursed after its burgomaster executed Marina.
* **Specialty**: Creeping hut totems, swamp poisons, and corrupted magic gems.
* **Notable Feature — The Creeping Hut**: Baba Lysaga’s mobile walking cottage powered by a stolen vineyard gem.

### 10. Argynvostholt (The Dragon's Ruin)
* **Location**: South-central mountain ridge in Barovia.
* **Population**: ~80 (Spectral Knights of the Silver Dragon, Revenants).
* **Description**: A fortified gothic mansion built by the silver dragon Argynvost, now a melancholy fortress of fallen paladins.
* **Specialty**: Consecrated silver blades, dragon-scale shields, and historical tapestries.
* **Notable Feature — The High Beacon**: The lighthouse tower awaiting the skull of Argynvost to bathe the valley in holy sunlight.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Crimson Weave (Loom Fragment)
* **Location**: Suspended within the Heart of Sorrow in Castle Ravenloft.
* **Mystical Property**: A primary relic of the Shattered Loom, stolen by Strahd. It grants him atmospheric control over Barovia's mists and absorbs the first 100 points of damage dealt to him each day.

### 2. The Mists of Ravenloft
* **Location**: Enveloping every Domain of Dread and the outer borders of the plane.
* **Mystical Property**: Sentient, impenetrable grey fog that enforces planar isolation. Entering causes choking suffocation (DC 20 Con save each round or 1 level of exhaustion) and recursively loops travelers back into the domain.

### 3. The Shadowsea
* **Location**: An endless ocean of liquid shadow and cold mist connecting disparate domains.
* **Mystical Property**: Non-native creatures immersed in its waters take 4d6 cold and 4d6 necrotic damage per round. Traversed safely only via Shadar-kai shadow-skiffs or specialized ironclads.

### 4. The Amber Temple
* **Location**: Carved into the frozen crags of Mount Ghakis.
* **Mystical Property**: Holds dozens of amber sarcophagi holding ancient Dark Powers. Touching a sarcophagus offers a Dark Gift, forcing a DC 15 Charisma saving throw against permanent moral corruption.

### 5. The Black Pyramid of Ankhtepot
* **Location**: Rising from the dunes of Har'Akir.
* **Mystical Property**: An ancient necrotic engine constructed from seamless black granite. It stabilizes the desert's eternal twilight and channels soul-energy to sustain Ankhtepot's embalmed court.

### 6. The Mount Grysl Lightning Pylons
* **Location**: The summit of Mount Grysl in Lamordia.
* **Mystical Property**: An immense array of copper and basalt monoliths that channels planar lightning into galvanic storage batteries, providing boundless power for fleshcrafting experiments.

### 7. The Glass Gardens of Lucine
* **Location**: The heart of Port-a-Lucine in Dementlieu.
* **Mystical Property**: A botanical conservatory of razor-sharp living crystal. The flowers generate psychoactive pollen that can induce euphoria, memory loss, or lethal respiratory paralysis.

### 8. The Poison Gardens of Misericordia
* **Location**: The terraces of Ivana Boritsi's villa in Borca.
* **Mystical Property**: Choked with exotic flora engineered to produce venoms capable of bypassing magical immunity and divine resistance.

### 9. The Dead Fields
* **Location**: The wastelands surrounding Lekar in Falkovnia.
* **Mystical Property**: A necrotic leyline sinkhole where corpses buried for centuries animate spontaneously under the influence of the New Moon.

### 10. The Pool of Black Ice
* **Location**: The deepest sanctum of the Fortress of Memories.
* **Mystical Property**: A freezing basin overseen by the Raven Queen. Mortals can surrender traumatic memories or planar grief in exchange for divination secrets and dark boons.

## Hazards & Planar Threats

| Hazard | Description | 5e Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **The Shadow Despair** | Metaphysical melancholy eroding the will to survive. | DC 12 Wisdom save each dawn; failure imposes Shadowfell Despair (disadvantage on checks/attacks). |
| **Amnesia of Darkon** | The fading of memories for non-native mortals. | Staying over 1d4+1 tendays requires a DC 14 Wisdom save each week or lose memories of homeland. |
| **Blood-Mist Surge** | Crimson mist carrying vampiric hunger in Barovia. | 20-ft radius; DC 14 Con save or take 3d8 necrotic damage and be blinded for 1 minute. |
| **Galvanic Lightning Arc** | Supercharged electrical discharge in Lamordia. | DC 15 Dexterity save or take 6d8 lightning damage; metal armor grants disadvantage on save. |
| **Desert Natron Storm** | Desiccating sandstorm stripping moisture from flesh in Har'Akir. | DC 14 Constitution save every 10 minutes or take 2d8 necrotic damage and gain 1 level of exhaustion. |
| **The New Moon Horde** | Relentless wave of undead in Falkovnia. | Encounter with 1 Ogre Zombie, 4 Wights, 8 Ghouls, and 16 Zombies. |
| **Belladonna Spore Bloom** | Toxic pollen cloud in Borca. | 15-ft cloud; DC 15 Constitution save or take 4d6 poison damage and be poisoned/hallucinating for 1 hour. |
| **The Apex Hunt Pursuit** | Chakuna's pack tracking marked prey in Valachan. | DC 16 Survival check to mask tracks; failure results in an ambush by 2 Werepanthers and 3 Dire Wolves. |
