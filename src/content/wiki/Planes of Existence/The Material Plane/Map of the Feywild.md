## D&D 5e Adaptation & Canon Map References

> [!NOTE]
> ### Adapted Directly from D&D 5e Canon
> * **The Feywild / Plane of Faerie**: Adapted directly from official D&D 5e cosmology (*Dungeon Master's Guide*, pp. 49–50, *The Wild Beyond the Witchlight*, and *Mordenkainen's Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse*).
> * **Canon Map Reference**: The Courts of the Feywild—including the Summer Court of Queen Titania, the Autumn and Winter domains of the Unseelie Court under the Queen of Air and Darkness, and the Domain of Prismeer—provide canonical foundations for Feywild exploration and capricious planar rules.
> * **Loom Integration**: In the Shattered Loom, the Feywild represents the **Emotional Warp** of the cosmic tapestry—where the threads of reality are so supercharged with primal vitality that thought and feeling instantly reshape geography.

## The Ten Great Realms & Sovereign Courts

### 1. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild#1. The Seelie Court: The Verdant Spire & The Summerlands|The Verdant Spire: The Court of Light]]
* **Location**: North-central Vibrant Summerlands, set amidst eternal meadows of blooming gold and emerald orchards.
* **Role**: Seat of the Seelie Court, capital of summer revels, divine artistry, passion, and natural growth.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Verdant Spire**: A living palace of woven sunlight, flowering silverwood, and sparkling morning dew.
  * **The Hall of the Eternal Revel**: Sprawling terraces where feasts, poetry duels, and grand masquerades have continued unbroken for millennia.
  * **The Sun-Well**: A sacred spring of pure radiant water that restores youth and inspires genius in mortal artists.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Titania, Queen of Light**, alongside her consort **King Oberon, the Green Lord**.

### 2. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild#2. The Unseelie Court: The Gloaming Cradle & Duskwood|The Gloaming Cradle: The Court of Shadows]]
* **Location**: Southern frozen frontier within the Duskwood, shrouded in perpetual midnight and chilling rime.
* **Role**: Seat of the Unseelie Court, redoubt of winter intrigue, dark pacts, predatory hunts, and shadow magic.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Gloaming Cradle**: A brooding, gothic fortress carved from blue glacial ice and petrified black thorn-wood.
  * **The Hall of Broken Mirrors**: High court where the Queen of Air and Darkness receives petitioners through illusions and whispers.
  * **The Hounds' Kennels**: The starting gate for the Wild Hunt, housing shadow mastiffs and winter wolves.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **The Queen of Air and Darkness**, enforced by **Prince Varyl, the Night Stalker**.

### 3. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild#3. The Twilight Glade & The Court of Stars|The Twilight Glade: The Court of Stars]]
* **Location**: Eastern highlands of indigo birch groves beneath an eternal velvet starlight canopy.
* **Role**: Neutral sanctuary of celestial divination, dream-weaving, astral charting, and cosmic arbitration.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Starlight Grove**: Concentric rings of singing silver birches where Archfey meet in neutral parliament.
  * **The Moondrop Pools**: Seven stepped crystalline ponds capturing liquid starlight used for multiversal scrying.
  * **The Spire of Constellations**: Crystal observatory housing the Great Planar Orrery.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Queen Serelune, the Twilight Monarch**, supported by **Lunaris** and **Stellara**.

### 4. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild#4. The Blooming Vale: The Nectar Fields & The Floral Labyrinth|The Blooming Vale: Realm of Lord Faelthorn]]
* **Location**: Southwestern lowlands overflowing with gargantuan eighty-foot blossoms and wild clover seas.
* **Role**: Capital of hedonistic satyr revels, botanical hyper-growth, honey cultivation, and sylvan music.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Colossal Blossom**: Lord Faelthorn's palace grown inside the petals of a legendary world-hibiscus.
  * **The Hive Spires**: Towering honeycombed cities of golden beeswax built by Queen Melissia's giant bumblebees.
  * **The Nectar Vats**: Fermentation cisterns aging millions of gallons of lotus wine and wild honey.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Lord Faelthorn, the Bloom King**, alongside **Queen Melissia**.

### 5. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild#5. The Gloaming Mire: The Rot-Weavers & The Sinking Causeway|The Gloaming Mire: Realm of Lady Marrowshade]]
* **Location**: Western stagnant wetlands choked with weeping willows, Spanish moss, and tea-colored peat bogs.
* **Role**: Domain of grief, necessary decay, bog-witchcraft, lost memories, and spectral mourning.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Hollow Tree**: An eighty-pace-wide petrified ironwood trunk serving as Lady Marrowshade's throne room.
  * **The Whispering Reeds**: Wetland maze that captures and repeats the final sighs of mortals across the planes.
  * **The Sinking Causeway**: Dangerous causeway of petrified logs traversing quicksand morasses.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Lady Marrowshade, the Wailing Widow**, alongside the Coven of Auntie Blackspit.

### 6. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild#6. The Embergrove: The Cinder Wilds & The Burning Copse|The Embergrove: Realm of Cinderheart]]
* **Location**: Central-eastern volcanic highlands covered in perpetual, smokeless autumn conflagration.
* **Role**: Domain of transformation, martial rebirth, passion, and sylvan weaponsmithing.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Burning Copse**: Living redwood grove burning with immortal, sentient crimson fire that never turns to ash.
  * **The Hearth of Renewal**: Basalt amphitheater with white-hot planar flames that incinerate curses.
  * **The Chimney-Rock Spires**: Towering sandstone pillars venting cinnamon smoke, home to wild phoenixes.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Cinderheart, the Autumn Flame**, supported by **Pyrewarden Ignis**.

### 7. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild#7. The Frostwild Tundra: The Winterspire & The Glacier of Frozen Voices|The Frostwild Tundra: Realm of Sovereign Kaelthas]]
* **Location**: Far northern glacial rim under raging emerald and violet auroras.
* **Role**: Realm of stoic endurance, permafrost survival, ancient preservation, and frozen arcana.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Icebound Keep**: Monolithic sapphire ice fortress carved from a living glacial peak.
  * **The Glacier of Frozen Voices**: Five-mile ice field trapping sound waves and songs from forgotten eras.
  * **The Crevasse of Forgotten Snow**: Bottomless fissure holding primordial entities in eternal cryogenic slumber.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Kaelthas, the Winter Sovereign**, advised by **Rime-Caller Isolda**.

### 8. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild#8. The Thornmaze Court: The Briar Vaults & The Labyrinth of Thorns|The Thornmaze Court: Realm of Thalassia]]
* **Location**: Shifting eastern border hedges of black briars, dagger-thorns, and strangler ivy.
* **Role**: Natural fortification, botanical puzzle-gauntlet, repository of dangerous relics, and defensive bulwark.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Vinebound Gate**: Living archway of briars and white roses where travelers must pay the Blood-Tithe.
  * **The Center of Petals**: Open-air rose courtyard housing Thalassia's throne of black brambles.
  * **The Briar Vaults**: Root-crypts beneath the labyrinth storing forgotten treasures of lost planar explorers.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Thalassia, the Briar Matron**, assisted by **Hedge-Master Thorne**.

### 9. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild#9. The Wild Hunt: The Moonlit Tracks & The Ancient Horn-Ring|The Wild Hunt: Tracks of Cernunnos]]
* **Location**: Nomadic wanderers moving through the primeval deep woods, crags, and planar crossings.
* **Role**: Primal apex predators, punishers of sylvan oath-breakers, guardians of virgin wildlands.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Ancient Horn-Ring**: Megalithic standing stones where the Hunt gathers at the dark of each moon.
  * **The Trophy Caverns**: Subterranean vaults preserving the skulls and antlers of mythical planar beasts.
  * **The Stalker's Grove**: Mobile bivouac of mammoth-hide tents and roasting bonfires.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Cernunnos, Master of the Wild Hunt**, riding with **Hunt-Mistress Valerie**.

### 10. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild#10. Bramble-Cross (The Goblin Market) & The Lake of Shimmering Dreams|Bramble-Cross & The Lake of Shimmering Dreams]]
* **Location**: Neutral hedge-border between Seelie and Unseelie territories, alongside the vast crystalline lake.
* **Role**: Lawless black-market bazaar, interplanar smuggling hub, dream-distillery, and neutral trade fair.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Central Hag's Cauldron**: Law-pillar of the market where the Three Grannies enforce trade rules.
  * **The Auction Ring of Promises**: Amphitheater where True Names, luck, and lifespan are traded.
  * **The Floating Lily-Piers (Moonpetal Village)**: Whimsical village of giant lily pads harvesting dream-water.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Merchant Council, **Grimgog the Broker**, and **Grandmother Yaga-Maeve**.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Moonpetal Village
* **Location**: On the southeastern shore of the Lake of Shimmering Dreams.
* **Population**: ~1,200 (Eladrin, Pixies, Satyrs, Displaced Artists).
* **Description**: Whimsical settlement of hollowed bioluminescent mushroom towers, silk cocoons, and floating blossom-piers.
* **Specialty**: Moonpetal wine, dream-catching gossamer nets, and memory-woven cloaks.

### 2. Wistful Hollow
* **Location**: Western twilight woods along the River Starlight.
* **Population**: ~850 (Centaur herds, Dryads, Displaced Mortals).
* **Description**: Melancholic refuge under weeping willows where mortals who lost their way in the Feywild live without fear of aging or court intrigues.
* **Specialty**: Memory-preserving amber, herbal soothing teas, and flute carvings.

### 3. Bramble-Cross (The Goblin Market)
* **Location**: The border hedge where the Golden Leaf Forests meet the Unseelie Duskwood.
* **Population**: ~1,600 (Quicklings, Boggles, Hags, Redcaps, Goblins).
* **Description**: Lawless, neon-lit carnival trading in oddities, stolen mortal years, memories, and cursed trinkets.
* **Specialty**: True names, bottled luck, riddle keys, and counterfeit contracts.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Lake of Shimmering Dreams
* **Description**: An immense body of crystal-clear water reflecting alien constellations that do not exist in the mortal sky.
* **Planar Mechanics**: Bathing in the lake restores all spent spell slots up to 5th level, but requires a DC 14 Charisma save to avoid falling into a blissful 24-hour magical slumber.

### 2. The Golden Leaf Forests (Eternal Autumn)
* **Description**: Endless woodlands where the leaves are perpetually gold, scarlet, and amber, slowly drifting to carpet the forest floor in silence.
* **Planar Mechanics**: Tracking or navigating through the falling leaves has disadvantage unless guided by a native fey or possessing the Fey Ancestry trait.

### 3. Faerie Rings (Cross-Planar Crossings)
* **Description**: Concentric circles of bioluminescent mushrooms found throughout the meadows and groves.
* **Planar Mechanics**: Stepping into a ring under a full moon transports creatures directly to a corresponding ring on the Material Plane; stepping in during a new moon leads to the Shadowfell.

### 4. Feywild Time Warp & Memory Loss
* **Description**: The fundamental temporal distortion of Faerie.
* **Planar Mechanics**: Upon leaving the Feywild, creatures must roll on the *Feywild Time Warp* and *Memory Loss* tables (*DMG* p. 50), risking forgetting their experiences or discovering decades have passed.

## Hazards & Planar Threats

| Hazard | Description | 5e Mechanics |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Emotional Euphoria / Despair** | Extreme mood amplification affecting rational judgment. | DC 14 Wis save; failure causes the creature to be Charmed or Frightened by nearby fey for 1 hour. |
| **Wild Magic Surges** | Spells cast in the Feywild trigger unstable reality fluctuations. | Any leveled spell cast triggers a roll on the Wild Magic Surge table (d100). |
| **Redcap Blood-Hunts** | Vicious fey killers hunting lone travelers to dye their iron boots. | Encounter with 3 Redcaps and 2 Displacer Beasts stalking through brush. |
| **Capricious Pacts** | Words spoken with fey nobles bind as literal, magical curses. | Any spoken promise counts as under the effects of a 7th-level *Geas* spell. |
