# 🏡 The Timber-Weft Lodge
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — The Material Plane*

> *"The wilderness of The Material Plane will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Huntsman Roderic Reed (Human Master Ranger, CR 8)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Timber-Weft Lodge** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/The Material Plane|The Material Plane]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: The Whispering Pines, east of Verdent Hollow
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Huntsman Roderic Reed (Human Master Ranger, CR 8)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A sturdy timber fortress built from ironwood logs and riverstone, smelling of cedar smoke, pine resin, and roasting venison. Antlers of massive dire stags and fangs of chimeras line the vaulted rafters.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of The Material Plane:

* **Processing & Salting**: Riverstone Butchery Vat, Feather & Fletching Shop, Hearthside Bounty Board, Tanning Racks.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
