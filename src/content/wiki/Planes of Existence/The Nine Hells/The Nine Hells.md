# The Nine Hells: The Spindle of Absolute Tyranny

![[the_nine_hells_map.jpg]]

> _"In the Unbroken Tapestry of Telara, the Nine Hells served as the Tension Weights of the Loom. They were the absolute foundation of Law, ensuring that the threads of existence remained straight, rigid, and unchanging. When the Shattering of the Loom occurred, the Nine Hells did not break into fragments like other planes; instead, they hardened. Today, the Nine Hells exist as a planar scar of absolute order—a series of nine compressed reality-shards where the laws of the multiverse are enforced with terrifying, infernal precision. It is a realm of cold calculation, where every soul is a Soul-Thread and every contract is a binding weave designed to eventually replace Telara's failed design with an Infernal Spindle."_
> — Journal of Vexiryn the Unbowed, Planar Cartographer

* * *

## **Characteristics of the Nine Hells**

1. **The Rigid Weave**: Magic in the Hells is highly structured. Chaos-based magic (Wild Magic, fey glamours) is severely suppressed and volatile, while Law-based magic (Abjuration, Divination) is amplified but taxed by the plane itself, requiring small sacrifices of order, blood, or soul-energy to cast.
2. **The Soul-Thread Economy**: In the Shattered Loom, devils do not merely collect souls for torture; they harvest **Soul-Threads**—the literal strands of destiny that once connected a mortal to the Loom. By claiming these threads through voluntary, legally binding contracts, the Archdevils accumulate the raw metaphysical material to reweave a "New Loom"—one founded on absolute tyranny and immutable hierarchy rather than dynamic balance.
3. **The Eternal Siege (The Blood War)**: The Hells are the primary cosmic bulwark against the **Great Unraveling** (the infinite Abyss). While the Abyss seeks to tear every thread of creation apart into entropic void, the Hells seek to bind them so tightly they can never move. Avernus is the apocalyptic "Shatterpoint" where these two existential forces clash for eternity.
4. **The Nine Layers of the Spindle**: The Hells are structured as a descending vertical spiral of increasingly dense, rigid reality. The **River Styx**, a corrupted, greasy memory-thread, flows through all nine layers, siphoning the identities of those who enter so they can be processed and reformatted into the infernal hierarchy.

## **1. Avernus: The Shattered Frontier & The Bronze Citadel**

### **Atmosphere & Nature: "The Blood-Mud, The Fire-Rains, and The Screaming Pistons"**
Avernus is the apocalyptic front-line of the multiverse, where the debris of the Shattered Loom crashed into the planar bedrock. The sky is an unending, blood-red tempest of boiling clouds, crisscrossed by streaks of golden lightning and meteor showers that detonate upon the cracked plains. The ground is a bruised wasteland of basalt, dried gore, and jagged scrap-metal from shattered cities dragged down from the mortal world. The air tastes of burning brimstone, copper, and diesel exhaust from infernal war machines roaring across the dunes. The River Styx cuts through the waste like a thrombosed black vein, carrying the screaming psychic residue of dissolved souls. Here, war is neither noble nor temporary: it is a permanent, industrialized grinder where Zariel's legions stitch reality shut against the infinite, howling horrors of the Abyss.

### **Read-Aloud: Approach to the Blood Plains & The Bronze Citadel**
> _"The burning gale hits you like the draft from a blast furnace, reeking of scorched oil and old blood. Below your vantage point, the red plains of Avernus stretch into a haze of sulfurous smoke. In the distance rises the Bronze Citadel—not a static fortress, but a colossal, multi-tiered battlement of jagged iron plates and bronze bastions spanning ten miles along the bluffs of the River Styx. Towering siege engines fling spheres of concentrated hellfire into the boiling waters, while war machines with spiked tires and smoke-belching smokestacks tear across the flats, their drivers shouting battle-curses in Infernal. Above it all, suspended upon wings of fire and blackened bone, the silhouette of Archduchess Zariel circles like an eagle of divine wrath, her blazing blade cutting through the gloom."_

### **Key Features & Regional Mechanics**
* **The Soul-Coin Economy**: In Avernus, mundane gold is treated as scrap metal. Trade operates on **Soul Coins**—dense disks of infernal iron each containing the screaming soul-thread of a mortal. A soul coin can power an infernal war machine for 24 hours, cast *Drain Soul*, or be bargained for passage.
* **Fireball Storms**: At the top of every hour, roll 1d20. On a roll of 18–20, a meteor shower rains upon the party's sector; creatures without cover must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or take 28 (8d6) fire damage.
* **The Static of Severance**: The ambient planar energy suppresses teleportation magic. Any creature attempting to cast *Misty Step*, *Dimension Door*, or *Teleport* must succeed on a DC 14 Constitution save or take 3d6 force damage and have their destination randomly displaced by 10d10 feet.

### **The Social Order of Avernus**
Avernus operates under martial law and the harsh chain of command of the Blood War:
1. **The Archduchess (Zariel)**: Supreme commander of all military forces, holding the power of instantaneous execution over any devil who retreats or questions an order.
2. **The Warlords & Warmachine Captains**: High-ranking pit fiends, erinyes commanders, and mortal warlords who captain armored war machine warbands.
3. **The Scavenger Barons (Fort Knucklebone & The Outposts)**: Night hags, rogue artificers, and mechanics who salvage war-scrap, tune engines, and barter soul coins.
4. **The Conscript Legions**: Bearded devils, spined devils, and damned mortal mercenaries marched into the meat-grinder against demon incursions.
5. **The Sludge-Pawn Debtors (Lemures & Nupperibos)**: The raw, mindless biological clay of the Hells, shoveled into the furnaces or used as living bridge-paving.

### **Important NPCs**
* **Archduchess Zariel, The Fallen Weaver**: The blazing, scarred angel-turned-archdevil who refused to watch the Loom unravel. She is tragic, furious, unyielding, and consumed by her single-minded crusade to save the multiverse by annihilating the Abyss through absolute martial discipline.
* **Bel, the Forge-Master**: The former Archduke of Avernus, now chief military engineer in the Bronze Citadel. He harbors cold, calculating resentment toward Zariel and secretly aids adventurers who can weaken her standing without compromising the war effort.
* **Mad Maggie**: An eccentric night hag who rules Fort Knucklebone from a rocking chair made of giant bones. She is obsessed with mortal drama, redemption stories, and retrofitting hot-rod war machines.
* **Mahadi, Master of the Wandering Emporium**: A suave, feline rakshasa who runs a mobile luxury bazaar under the personal protection of Lord Asmodeus. He provides five-star dining, infernal contracts, and spa treatments amidst the horrors of the blood plains.

### **Districts & Key Locations**
* **The Bronze Citadel**: The colossal military headquarters of the Nine Hells, featuring thirty concentric ring-walls, warmachine hangars, and the High Command Spire.
* **Fort Knucklebone**: A salvage garage built into the curl of four colossal petrified skeletal fingers, home to goblin grease-monkeys and redcap mechanics.
* **The Wandering Emporium**: A moving convoy of luxurious silk tents, hookah lounges, and bathhouses protected by a divine infernal truce.
* **The Styx Dockyards**: Fortified river gates where infernal ironclad galleys are constructed and launched against demonic barges.

## **2. Dis: The Iron Spindle & The Geometric Bastion**

### **Atmosphere & Nature: "The Screaming Metal, The Red-Hot Walls, and The Sovereign Straight Line"**
Dis is the second layer of the Hells, a sprawling subterranean megalopolis of oppressive, suffocating iron geometry. Here, nature has been entirely eradicated and replaced by a machine-grid of terrifying precision. The sky is a ceiling of smoked green-black granite hung with thick smog from thousands of smelters and forges. The streets are perfectly straight chasms flanked by monolithic iron towers whose rust-coated walls radiate intense, skin-scorching heat. The ambient temperature is an unbearable 110°F; touching any bare metal without enchanted gloves causes immediate second-degree burns. The air smells of burning machine oil, sulfur, scorched iron, and dry legal parchment. Silence is broken only by the rhythmic clang of iron hammers, the marching footsteps of barbed devil patrols, and the eerie, high-pitched screech of Dispater's automated measuring compasses constantly verifying that no building deviates from an exact 90-degree angle.

### **Read-Aloud: Entering the City of Dis**
> _"You pass beneath the towering archway of the Red Iron Gates, where the iron stones hum with a low, bone-jarring vibration. The heat hits you instantly—a dry, suffocating blast that makes the eyes burn and metal armor feel like an oven. Before you stretches the City of Dis: an endless sea of black iron tenements, soaring bastions, and labyrinthine streets laid out with geometric obsession. Every street is a straight, unrelenting corridor; every corner a sharp right angle. At the heart of the city rises the Iron Tower—a gargantuan, needle-sharp spire that pierces the smoky cavern roof miles above. Floating iron measuring tapes and glowing red compasses drift through the air like predatory birds, screaming whenever a stray shadow or mortal footprint deviates from the prescribed straight lines."_

### **Key Features & Regional Mechanics**
* **The Searing Iron**: Any creature touching bare metal in Dis without fire resistance or insulated clothing takes 1d6 fire damage per round of contact. Metal armor becomes uncomfortable after 10 minutes and inflicts 1 level of exhaustion every 2 hours unless cooled by magical means.
* **The Law of the Straight Line**: Deviation from designated transit avenues is illegal. Turning down an unpermitted alleyway or cutting corners across a plaza triggers an immediate alarm; within 1d4 rounds, a patrol of 3 Barbed Devils and 1 Chain Devil arrives to administer an on-the-spot legal audit.
* **Dispater's Paranoia Ward**: Within 5 miles of the Iron Tower, no creature can benefit from the *Invisible* condition or cast illusion spells of 5th level or lower. Every whisper spoken in the streets is mechanically transcribed by clockwork imp-scriveners onto rolls of brass parchment.

### **The Social Order of Dis**
Dis is governed by the strictest, most suffocating legal bureaucracy in the multiverse:
1. **The Iron Duke (Archduke Dispater)**: The paranoid sovereign who never leaves the inner sanctum of the Iron Tower, governing through edicts stamped with his Great Iron Seal.
2. **The Surveyors of Deviance**: Elder pit fiends and inquisitors tasked with auditing the physical geometry and ideological conformity of the city.
3. **The Iron Scribes & Notaries**: Horned devils and legalist imps who draft, catalog, and archive the trillions of contracts that bind mortals and devils to the Hells.
4. **The Chain Wardens**: Kytons and chain devils who manage the city's vast prison vaults, keeping political dissidents in suspended iron cages.
5. **The Laborer Guilds (Duergar, Tieflings & Damned Petitioners)**: Slaves and artisans who continuously scrape rust from the city walls and forge iron plates to replace decaying sections.

### **Important NPCs**
* **Archduke Dispater, Surveyor of Deviance**: The aristocratic, deeply paranoid ruler of Dis. He believes the entire multiverse is a flawed blueprint full of chaos-leaks and geometry errors. He trusts no one—least of all his fellow Archdevils—and communicates exclusively through iron homunculi and enchanted mirrors.
* **Bael, the General of the Citadel**: Dispater’s chief general and master tactician. Unlike his paranoid master, Bael is a brilliant, bold military strategist who longs to lead the iron legions into the Blood War to crush the demon hordes.
* **Master Notary Vexath**: Chief legal auditor of the High Court of Dis. He carries a ten-foot iron scroll containing the names of every mortal who has breached an infernal bargain in the past thousand years.
* **Kallista the Unforged**: A rebellious tiefling artificer operating in the rusted slums of the Outlying Ring, who crafts insulated stealth gear for planar thieves and escapees.

### **Districts & Key Locations**
* **The Iron Tower**: Dispater’s impenetrable needle-spire, featuring the Hall of Geometries, the Vault of Cosmic Legalisms, and the personal redoubts of the Duke.
* **The Hall of Geometries**: The central calculating room where automated brass mechanisms track dimensional rifts, planar shifts, and Loom fraying across the planes.
* **The Prison of Decrees**: Subterranean catacombs beneath the iron streets where souls that violated interplanar compacts are imprisoned inside iron maidens that whisper their legal infractions in perpetuity.
* **The Red Iron Market**: The only legal commercial district in Dis, where interplanar merchants can trade iron, soul-ingots, and legal dispensations under heavy guard.

## **3. Minauros: The Bog of Sinking Destinies & The Sinking City**

### **Atmosphere & Nature: "The Greasy Mud, The Rusted Chains, and The Weight of Hoarded Gold"**
Minauros is the third layer of the Hells, a foul, bottomless morass of rotting sludge, toxic marsh gas, and acidic rain where the multiverse's discarded garbage and failed destinies accumulate. In the cosmology of the Shattered Loom, Minauros serves as the "Trash-Compactor of Reality." The sky is an oily, sickly yellow-grey smog that perpetually weeps lukewarm, greasy rain. The ground is a treacherous, sucking swamp composed of dissolving memories, liquidated gold, and broken vows. Heavy chains the size of galleon anchors drape from monolithic basalt pillars, groaning under the impossible weight of stone citadels that are constantly sinking into the mire. The air smells intensely of stagnant water, decaying flesh, wet copper, and the foul sulfur of open sewer vaults. Here, Mammon rules from a palace of hoarded Loom-Shards and counterfeit coin, believing that whoever owns the greatest weight of physical gold and destiny-threads will anchor the multiverse to his will.

### **Read-Aloud: Approach to Jangling Hiter & The Sinking City**
> _"Your boots sink ankle-deep into foul, warm mud that squelches with the sound of muffled weeping. Overhead, gargantuan iron chains—each link thick as an ox—stretch through the yellow smog, tethering stone towers and jagged ramparts to prevent them from slipping into the fathomless swamp. This is Jangling Hiter, the City of Chains: a metropolis suspended above an ocean of toxic slime. Rusted cages sway in the wind, packed with petitioners whose debts have come due, their skin encrusted with verdigris and dried salt. Down below, bloated swamp-devils and barbed scavengers dredge the sludge with iron nets, dragging up fragments of broken armor, dead mortal crowns, and shimmering threads of dissolved destinies. In the center of the quagmire towers Mammon’s Sinking Palace, adorned with tons of tarnished gold plate that slowly drags the fortress deeper into the muck with every tick of the clock."_

### **Key Features & Regional Mechanics**
* **The Sinking Sludge**: Moving across the mud of Minauros without checking footing requires a DC 14 Dexterity or Survival check; on a failure, the creature sinks 2 feet into the mire (speed is reduced to 0; escaping requires a DC 15 Athletics check). Unattended metal objects sink completely within 10 minutes.
* **The Acidic Smog**: The rain in Minauros is mildly acidic. Non-magical leather, cloth, and iron equipment not treated with preservative oil degrades over 24 hours (-1 to armor AC or weapon damage per day until destroyed).
* **The Weight of Greed**: Creatures carrying more than 100 gp worth of coinage or gems feel an unnatural metaphysical gravity. Their movement speed is reduced by 10 feet, and they have disadvantage on saving throws against being knocked prone or submerged in the mud.

### **The Social Order of Minauros**
Minauros is governed by usury, predatory debt, and the hoarding of material weight:
1. **The Lord of Avarice (Archduke Mammon)**: A bloated, serpentine Archdevil covered in tarnished gold scales and jewel-encrusted pustules. He spends eternity counting coin, weighing soul-threads, and foreclosing on mortal kingdoms.
2. **The Chain Masters (The Kytons of Jangling Hiter)**: Sadistic masters of physical and psychic bondage who craft the great chains that hold the city above the slime.
3. **The Gilded Bailiffs & Debt-Collectors**: Barbed devils and osyluths who enforce repayment schedules across the planes, tracking debtors to the ends of the multiverse.
4. **The Dredge-Scavengers**: Imps, boggles, and damned souls who dive into the bottomless sludge with dredges, seeking lost Loom-shards and sunken artifacts.
5. **The Bankrupt Petitioners**: Those who traded their souls for mortal wealth, now chained to the towers or forced to pump bilge water out of the foundations until their debts are cleared.

### **Important NPCs**
* **Archduke Mammon, The Gilded Miser**: Repulsive, cunning, and insatiable. He appears as a colossal humanoid serpent adorned with crowns, necklaces, and melted coins. He views the Shattered Loom as a galactic bankruptcy auction: whoever buys the most broken threads gains ownership of reality. He will betray anyone for a sufficient margin of profit.
* **Master Chain-Smith Morvath**: The supreme architect of Jangling Hiter. A kyton whose body is intertwined with thirty living chains that move like vipers. He knows the breaking strain of every link holding the city aloft.
* **Gluttonous Balthazar**: A morbidly obese erinyes who serves as Mammon's chief appraiser, able to determine the exact value of a mortal soul-thread down to the copper penny with a single glance.
* **Skiff-Captain Oras**: A tiefling river-pilot with a flat-bottomed skiff who smuggles escapees across the mire in exchange for pre-Shattering silver coins.

### **Districts & Key Locations**
* **Jangling Hiter (The City of Chains)**: The suspended metropolis of iron towers, bridges, and hanging cages, held above the swamp by thousands of massive chains anchored to the cavern ceiling.
* **The Sinking Palace of Mammon**: A monolithic fortress of stone and gilded brass built atop a foundation of crushed gold coins, perpetually sinking and being rebuilt.
* **The Vault of Defaulted Pacts**: Subterranean vaults beneath the sludge where the deeds to kingdoms, castles, and bloodlines that defaulted on infernal loans are preserved in lead caskets.
* **The Quagmire of Discarded Dreams**: A vast expanse of the swamp where the failed ambitions and unfulfilled promises of the multiverse gather as a thick, phosphorescent sludge.

### **Unique Shops of Jangling Hiter**

#### **1. The Pawnshop of Eternity — Defaulted Relics & Curios**
**Proprietor**: Appraiser Balthazar, weighing a severed mortal ear against an ounce of gold.
* **Notable Items for Sale**:
  * *Coin of the Greedy Merchant* (Wondrous item, Rare — 3,200 gp or 3 Soul Coins): When flipped, lands on the side the user desires. While in the owner's purse, grants advantage on Charisma (Deception and Persuasion) checks involving financial transactions.

* **Current Rumor**: "An expedition of wizards from the Red Wizards of Thay entered the Chasm of Nessus three days ago. Only their frozen fingers were returned, pinned to an iron warning plaque."

## **9. Nessus: The Spindle of the Dark Weaver & The Citadel of Malsheem**

### **Atmosphere & Nature: "The Fathomless Chasm, The Adamantine Filaments, and The Counter-Loom of Absolute Law"**
Nessus is the ninth, deepest, and most terrifying layer of the Hells—a shattered, subterranean abyss of sheer canyons, rivers of liquid obsidian, and petrified adamantine bridges spanning thousands of leagues. In the cosmology of the Shattered Loom, Nessus serves as the "Root Directory of Reality and The Counter-Spindle." It is where reality is so compressed, dense, and rigid that time itself moves with mechanical, heavy ticks like an iron grandfather clock. The sky is an impenetrable ceiling of jagged black basalt miles above, split by glowing fissures of unholy ruby light. There is no weather here, no wind, no natural warmth or cold—only the crushing, suffocating gravitational weight of absolute divine order. In the center of the greatest chasm—the Serpent's Coil—floats **Malsheem**, the Citadel of the Infernal Spindle: a fortress carved from a single mountain of black diamond. Here, Lord Asmodeus operates the **Infernal Spindle**, a counterfeit Loom that transmits a signal of absolute law throughout the Fractured Nexus, slowly overriding the remnants of Telara’s work to reweave the multiverse in his own unyielding image.

### **Read-Aloud: Approach to the Serpent’s Coil & Malsheem**
> _"You step onto the Bridge of Severed Oaths, an adamantine span as wide as a highway that arches over a canyon so deep that not even magical light can reach the bottom. The silence here is absolute, terrifying, and unnatural—the silence of a courtroom before the sentence of execution is delivered. Suspended in the void ahead floats Malsheem: a mountain of black diamond sculpted into soaring spires, ramparts, and flying buttresses that defy all mortal geometry. Glowing red filaments of concentrated soul-energy pulse through the rock like veins of liquid ruby, converging upon the central spire. There, the Infernal Spindle spins—a gargantuan, clockwork engine of blackened iron and celestial gold, turning with a low, earth-shattering hum that vibrates directly in your teeth and bones. Sitting upon the Ruby Throne at the heart of the engine is Lord Asmodeus: impossibly handsome, aristocratic, and composed, his ruby eyes examining you as if you were a minor calculation in an equation he solved before the gods were born."_

### **Key Features & Regional Mechanics**
* **The Gravitational Weight of Law**: The dense planar weave of Nessus imposes immense metaphysical gravity. Non-lawful creatures have their walking speed reduced by 15 feet and must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw every hour or gain 1 level of exhaustion as their life-force is pressurized.
* **The Infernal Spindle's Pulse**: Every 6 hours, the Spindle emits a cosmic resonance pulse. All creatures on Nessus must make a DC 18 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, a creature cannot take actions that violate a lawful command, contract, or oath for the next 24 hours.
* **The Absolute Contract Wards**: No contract signed or approved by Asmodeus can be broken, undone by *Wish*, or altered by divine intervention. The penalty for breach of a Nessus pact is immediate, irrevocable death and the absorption of the violator's soul into the Spindle.

### **The Social Order of Nessus**
Nessus is governed with autocratic perfection and supreme detachment:
1. **The Dark Architect (Lord Asmodeus)**: The supreme ruler of the Nine Hells, Master of the Pact Primeval, and Prime Weaver of the Infernal Spindle. He is the ultimate chess-master of the cosmos, viewing gods, mortals, and demons alike as predictable variables in his master theorem.
2. **The High Council of Nessus**: An inner circle of ancient pit fiends, arch-devils, and infernal cardinals who manage interplanar diplomacy and oversee the execution of universal treaties.
3. **The Ruby Inquisitors**: The supreme legalists and truth-seekers of the Hells, carrying rods of faceted ruby that can compel absolute obedience from lesser fiends.
4. **The Spindle-Engineers**: Diabolical artificers and fallen celestial weavers who calibrate the tension of the soul-threads feeding into the Infernal Spindle.
5. **The Guard of the Serpent’s Coil**: Legions of horned devils and pit fiends in black adamantine plate who guard the bridges and subterranean descents to Malsheem.

### **Important NPCs**
* **Lord Asmodeus, Supreme Master of the Nine Hells**: Impossibly charismatic, polite, soft-spoken, and terrifying beyond measure. He appears as a tall, flawlessly aristocratic figure with ruby-red skin, sleek dark hair, elegant curving horns, and eyes like smoldering stars. He carries the *Ruby Rod of Asmodeus*—a scepter of celestial power that commands absolute subjugation. He does not yell or threaten; he simply explains the inevitability of his triumph with effortless, horrifying logic.
* **Adramalech, Chancellor of Hell**: The Grand Chancellor who keeps the Master Records of Nessus. A towering, fire-breathing devil who wears robes of blackened gold and oversees the ceremonial presentations before the Ruby Throne.
* **Glasya-Vael, The Keeper of the Ruby Seal**: A high erinyes notary who stamps all universal treaties that cross interplanar thresholds.
* **Martinet, The Constable of Malsheem**: A horned devil who commands the household guard of Malsheem, famous for his immaculate etiquette and instantaneous execution of security threats.

### **Districts & Key Locations**
* **Malsheem (The Citadel of the Spindle)**: The floating black diamond fortress, housing the Ruby Throne, the High Council Chamber, and the Great Archive of the Pact Primeval.
* **The Infernal Spindle**: The colossal, counter-loom mechanism that spins stolen soul-threads into the rigid framework of the New Universe.
* **The Archive of the Pact Primeval**: The sacred subterranean vault where the original cosmic treaty that granted devils the right to harvest mortal souls is inscribed on tablets of celestial ruby.
* **The Serpent’s Coil**: The fathomless trench beneath Malsheem where Asmodeus’s true, cosmic serpentine form is said to lie wounded from the fall from the heavens.

### **Unique Shops & Vaults of Malsheem**

#### **1. The Treasury of the Prime Weaver — Cosmic Artifacts & Decrees**
**Proprietor**: Chancellor Adramalech, inspecting ledgers bound in gold thread.
* **Notable Items for Sale**:
  * *Shard of the Ruby Rod* (Wondrous item, Legendary — 25,000 gp or *a sovereign kingdom's unconditional surrender*): When held, grants immunity to all enchantment spells, advantage on spell attack rolls, and allows the wielder to cast *Dominate Monster* (DC 20) once per long rest.
  * *Parchment of the Pact Primeval* (Wondrous item, Legendary — 20,000 gp): A certified copy of the original celestial compact; grants the bearer immunity to being attacked by devils unless the bearer initiates combat.
  * *Adamantine Spindle-Thread* (Wondrous item, Very Rare — 8,500 gp): A 50-foot cord of unbreakable black adamantine. Can bind any creature up to Gargantuan size (DC 22 Strength check to break); the bound creature cannot cast spells, teleport, or use extra-planar movement.

#### **2. The Scribe of the Ninth Core — Universal Contracts & Warrants**
**Proprietor**: Notary Vane, a pit fiend wearing spectacles of faceted sapphire.
* **Notable Items for Sale**:
  * *Writ of Planar Sovereignty* (Scroll, Legendary — 30,000 gp): Bears Asmodeus’s personal blood-seal; grants the bearer lawful authority to claim and govern an entire Material Plane territory or demiplane without divine interference.
  * *Ink of the Prime Weaver* (Wondrous item, Very Rare — 5,000 gp): Any contract written in this ink cannot be destroyed by fire, acid, or planar collapse; even if the parchment is destroyed, the contract's terms remain spiritually active.
  * *Ring of the Sovereign Mandate* (Wondrous item, Very Rare — 9,000 gp): Wearer can cast *Command* at 5th level at will (DC 18) against any creature that has ever signed an agreement with the wearer.

#### **3. The Armory of the Black Diamond — Regalia of Absolute Law**
**Proprietor**: Constable Martinet, inspecting rows of polished adamantine armor.
* **Notable Items for Sale**:
  * *Armor of the Black Diamond* (Armor [Plate], Legendary — 22,000 gp): +2 armor. Grants resistance to all damage types except radiant. The wearer cannot be moved against their will, knocked prone, or teleported.
  * *Glaive of the Infernal Executioner* (Weapon [Glaive], Very Rare — 7,500 gp): +2 weapon. Deals an extra 2d10 force damage. On hit, severs one magical bond, pact, or active buff spell on the target.
  * *Talisman of the Malsheem Guard* (Wondrous item, Rare — 3,500 gp): Allows the wearer to teleport directly to the Bridge of Severed Oaths from any layer of the Hells once per year.

### **Nessian Sanctuaries & Council Halls**

#### **1. The Chamber of Decrees (Malsheem)**
* **Atmosphere**: A vast, silent rotunda of black marble and red velvet. Archdevils and planar ambassadors stand in designated circles of bronze, awaiting their summons before the Ruby Throne. No voice is raised; only the slow, rhythmic turning of the Infernal Spindle fills the space.
* **Signature Drink**: *The Wine of the Ninth Circle* (Pitch-black vintage aged for ten thousand years; grants the drinker truesight out to 120 feet and immunity to mind-reading for 24 hours, but turns their blood black).
* **Current Rumor**: "Asmodeus has already begun spinning the threads for the Material Plane's final alignment. He is waiting for the mortals to fracture the Loom's remaining anchor stones before issuing the Final Mandate."

#### **2. The Bastion of the Bridge**
* **Atmosphere**: An austere military fortress guarding the entrance to Malsheem across the Bridge of Severed Oaths. Elite horned devil sentinels inspect every traveler down to their soul-threads.
* **Signature Fare**: Cold smoked roast spiced with celestial salts and black pepper.
* **Current Rumor**: "Mephistopheles attempted to send a hidden scrying-shard into Malsheem disguised as a diplomatic gift. Asmodeus placed the shard on his mantelpiece and smiled, letting Mephistopheles watch him sign the order demoting Canian troops in the Blood War."
