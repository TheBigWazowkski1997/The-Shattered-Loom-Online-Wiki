# 🏡 The Iron-Chain Keep
### *Branch Outpost of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor — The Nine Hells*

> *"The wilderness of The Nine Hells will swallow you in minutes if you don't respect its tracks. Step inside, shake the dust from your cloak, and let's see what you brought back to salt."*  
> — **Malakor the Devil-Flayer (Tiefling Slayer, CR 13)**

---

## 🏛️ Lodge Overview & Geography

The **The Iron-Chain Keep** serves as the primary sanctuary and resource exchange for planar hunters, trackers, and trophy-smiths operating within **[[Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/The Nine Hells|The Nine Hells]]**.

* **Precise Geographic Location**: Fortified basalt bridge spanning the Styx ravine between Avernus and Dis
* **Presiding Lodge Master**: **Malakor the Devil-Flayer (Tiefling Slayer, CR 13)**
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor|The Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor]]
* **Atmosphere & Architecture**: A jagged bastion constructed of black infernal iron and bound by chains inscribed with broken pacts. Hell Hound pelts dry above sulfur pits while hunters drink blood-wine and clean serrated poleaxes.

---

## 🛠️ Specialized Lodge Facilities

Every hunter passing through the lodge has access to specialized equipment designed to counteract the unique environmental physics of The Nine Hells:

* **Processing & Salting**: Infernal Quench Basin, Devil-Chain Tannery, Contract Bribe Vault, Styx-Filter Still.
* **Trophy Forge Access**: Fully equipped for processing regional pelts, chitin, and essence cores under the [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
* **Bunkhouse & Rations**: Secure lodging immune to local planar hazards, featuring food and drink tailored to mortal digestion.
