## D&D 5e Adaptation & Canon Map References

> [!NOTE]
> ### Adapted Directly from D&D 5e Canon
> The Nine Hells within **Project Shattered Loom** directly integrates the official D&D 5e cosmology of **Baator** (*Dungeon Master's Guide*, pp. 64–66, *Baldur's Gate: Descent into Avernus*, and *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes*):
> 
> * **The Nine Layers of Baator**:
>   1. **Avernus**: Uses the official **Map of Avernus** by Jared Blando (*Descent into Avernus*), including the Bronze Citadel, the River Styx, Fort Knucklebone, the High Hall of Elturel, and the Wandering Emporium.
>   2. **Dis**: The iron geometric labyrinth city of Dispater (*Manual of the Planes* / *Fires of Dis*).
>   3. **Minauros**: The endless sinking swamp and city of Mammon.
>   4. **Phlegethos**: The volcanic foundries and city of Abriymoch (Belial & Fierna).
>   5. **Stygia**: The frozen sea and icebergs encasing Levistus; the port of Tantlin.
>   6. **Malbolge**: The sloping, mountainous avalanche prison of Glasya.
>   7. **Maladomini**: The ruined architectural monuments and quarries of Baalzebul; the city of Grenpoli.
>   8. **Cania**: The frozen glaciers and citadel of Mephistar (Mephistopheles).
>   9. **Nessus**: The deepest rift and citadel of Malsheem (Asmodeus).
> * **Loom Integration**: In this campaign, the Hells are the "Tension Weights" of reality. Devils do not merely torture souls; they harvest **Soul-Threads** to reforge reality into the **Infernal Spindle**—a mechanical counter-loom of absolute tyranny.

## The Three Great Citadels & Strongholds

### 1. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/The Nine Hells#1. Avernus: The Shattered Frontier & The Bronze Citadel|The Bronze Citadel: Fortress of the First Front]]
* **Location**: Avernus, spanning a series of low basalt ridges overlooking the blood plains of the River Styx.
* **Role**: The massive headquarters of the infernal war effort in the Blood War, commanded by Archduchess Zariel.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Command Spire**: Zariel’s war council chamber, hung with tactical threat-maps tracking Abyssal breaches.
  * **The Warmachine Hangars**: Enormous hangars housing heavy infernal war machines fueled by distilled soul coins.
  * **The Styx Dockyards**: Fortified river gates where infernal ironclad galleys are built and launched against demon hordes.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Archduchess Zariel**, the Fallen Weaver.

### 2. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/The Nine Hells#2. Dis: The Iron Spindle & The Geometric Bastion|The Iron Tower of Dis: The Sovereign Geometric Bastion]]
* **Location**: Dis (Layer 2), rising at the center of the labyrinthine iron city.
* **Role**: The bureaucratic data-vault of cosmic order, where universal legal statutes and divine infractions are codified.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Hall of Geometries**: Dispater’s inner sanctum, where floating red-hot iron measuring tapes scream at the slightest deviation from straight lines.
  * **The Prison of Decrees**: Subterranean vaults housing entities who violated planar compacts.
  * **The Red Iron Gates**: Massive city gates that heat to glowing white when non-lawful creatures approach.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Archduke Dispater**, Surveyor of Deviance.

### 3. [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/The Nine Hells#9. Nessus: The Spindle of the Dark Weaver & The Citadel of Malsheem|Malsheem: Citadel of the Infernal Spindle]]
* **Location**: Nessus (Layer 9), suspended above the yawning chasm of the Serpent's Coil.
* **Role**: The ultimate command throne of the Nine Hells and the site of Asmodeus’s counter-loom.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Infernal Spindle**: A gargantuan machine of blackened adamantine and cold-iron, endlessly spinning stolen mortal soul-threads into a rigid framework of absolute law.
  * **The Archive of the Pact Primeval**: The original cosmic contract inscribed on tablets of celestial ruby.
  * **The Serpent's Abyss**: The fathomless trench beneath Malsheem where Asmodeus’s true serpentine avatar slumbers.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Lord Asmodeus**, Supreme Master of the Nine Hells.

## Fortresses, Encampments & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Fort Knucklebone (The Scavenger's Garage)
* **Location**: Avernus, nestled within the protective curl of four colossal petrified skeletal fingers.
* **Population**: ~80 (Mad Maggie’s gang of redcaps, kenku mechanics, and mad scavengers).
* **Description**: A bustling salvage yard and repair shop where infernal war machines are built, customized, and traded.
* **Specialty**: War machine tuning, soul coin trading, and dream-divinations.

### 2. The Wandering Emporium (Mahadi's Travelling Sanctuary)
* **Location**: Avernus (Mobile nomadic encampment).
* **Population**: ~200 (Merchants, succubi entertainers, salamander cooks, and planar wanderers).
* **Description**: A luxurious oasis of silk tents, fine food, and calm baths that travels the wastes under the protection of Asmodeus's personal seal.
* **Specialty**: Rare planar delicacies, magical loans, safe lodging, and infernal bargains.

### 3. Tantlin: The Floating Ice-Port
* **Location**: Stygia (Layer 5), constructed atop a cluster of frozen ice floes on the River Styx.
* **Population**: ~1,500 (Ice devils, planar smugglers, and frost-bitten tieflings).
* **Description**: A lawless port town perpetually shifting with the tides of ice, serving as the sole trading post on the frozen layer.
* **Specialty**: Preserved ancient texts extracted from Stygian ice, Styx-skiff rentals, and cold-resistant iron gear.

### 4. Abriymoch: City of Caldera Forges
* **Location**: Phlegethos (Layer 4), built into the interior walls of a massive volcanic crater.
* **Population**: ~4,000 (Barbed devils, fire elementals, infernal smiths, and petitioners).
* **Description**: An obsidian metropolis lit by perpetual rivers of magma, renowned as the chief industrial engine of infernal armaments.
* **Specialty**: Hellfire-tempered plate armor, soul-branded shackles, and law tablets.

### 5. Grenpoli: The City of Diplomatic Truce
* **Location**: Maladomini (Layer 7), a pristine white marble city protected by an absolute non-violence mythal.
* **Population**: ~6,000 (Planar diplomats, legalists, erinyes judges, and merchant-ambassadors).
* **Description**: A sanctuary of debate and political arbitration where physical conflict is impossible; disputes are settled entirely by rhetoric and contract.
* **Specialty**: Interplanar arbitration, diplomatic seals, and forbidden blueprints.

## The Nine Layers & Key Citadels

1. **Avernus**: The Planar Junkyard and front line of the Blood War; ruled by Zariel.
2. **Dis**: The Data-Vault of Order; iron geometric city ruled by Dispater.
3. **Minauros**: The cosmic waste compactor where discarded destinies dissolve into mud; ruled by Mammon.
4. **Phlegethos**: Obsidian foundries and magma lakes where lawless souls are melted into infernal iron; ruled by Belial & Fierna.
5. **Stygia**: The frozen sea and ice-bound archive encasing Levistus; port of Tantlin.
6. **Malbolge**: The shifting vertical logic-trap mountain prison; ruled by Glasya.
7. **Maladomini**: Decaying quarries and ruins of failed architectural drafts; ruled by Baalzebul.
8. **Cania**: The frozen void heat-sink buffering the Hells from cosmic entropy; ruled by Mephistopheles.
9. **Nessus**: The deepest core housing the Infernal Spindle and Malsheem; ruled by Asmodeus.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The River Styx
* **Description**: A dark, greasy torrent flowing through all nine layers, carrying the dissolved memories and identities of mortal souls.
* **Planar Mechanics**: Touching the water forces a DC 15 Intelligence saving throw; failure results in the effects of a permanent *Feeblemind* spell and complete memory loss.

### 2. Infernal Soul-Thread Contracts
* **Description**: Binding metaphysical documents signed in blood that transfer the mortal's thread directly to the devil's spindle.
* **Planar Mechanics**: Immune to dispelling; breaking an infernal contract triggers immediate soul-forfeit, damning the creature to Avernus upon death.

### 3. Soul Coins & War Engines
* **Description**: Heavy coins forged from infernal iron containing the compressed soul-thread of a single mortal, used as currency and fuel for infernal war machines.
* **Planar Mechanics**: Burning a soul coin in an infernal engine grants 24 hours of operation, but slowly burns away the trapped soul's consciousness.
