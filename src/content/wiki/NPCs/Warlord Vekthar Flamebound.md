# Warlord Vekthar Flamebound (The Basalt Iron-Lord)

![[attachments/vekthar_flamebound.jpg]]

* **Race**: Salamander (Magma-Born Noble)
* **Faction**: The Council of Stone and Flame / Stoneheart Bastion
* **Role**: High Warlord of the Obsidian Cradle, Commander of the Basalt Vanguard
* **Appearance**: An eight-foot-tall, muscular salamander whose charcoal scales are veined with glowing molten gold. His chest houses an eternal volcanic ember that radiates intense heat through his black iron breastplate. In his clawed hands he wields the *Obsidian Sunder-Glaive*, a terrifying polearm carved from a single slab of razor-sharp volcanic glass.
* **Personality**: Grim, authoritarian, and fiercely pragmatic. Unlike the decadent Efreet or reckless fire elementals, Vekthar believes true power is forged at the collision of stone and magma. He values unwavering loyalty, unyielding discipline, and brutal battlefield efficiency.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Master of the Bastion:** Vekthar maintains the military defense of the Obsidian Cradle, standing between the peaceful geode cities of the plane and rogue magma titans rising from the border with Fire.
* **The Shattered Loom:** Vekthar's tremorsense has detected unnatural rhythmic pulses passing through the continental bedrock, suspecting an extraplanar anchor has rooted beneath the Obsidian Throne.
* **Dialogue & Demeanor:** *"Stone does not ask for mercy, and magma does not offer it. Speak your purpose before I grind your bones into mortar for the walls."*
