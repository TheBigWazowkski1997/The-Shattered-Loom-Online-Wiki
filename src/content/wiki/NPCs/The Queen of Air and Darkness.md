# The Queen of Air and Darkness (Sovereign of Night & Mistress of the Unseelie)

![[the_queen_of_air_and_darkness.jpg]]

* **Race**: Archfey (Primordial Fallen Fey Demigod of Darkness, Winter, and Forbidden Arcana)
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Unseelie Court|The Unseelie Court]] (Absolute Sovereign)
* **Role**: Ruler of the Gloaming Cradle / Empress of the Duskwood / Mistress of Broken Mirrors
* **Appearance**: A figure of chilling, terrifying, and fathomless majesty. The Queen has no permanent physical face—she appears as a tall, imperious silhouette draped in voluminous, floor-length robes woven of midnight-black velvet, swirling mist, and dying stars. Her face is concealed behind a mask of polished black obsidian that reflects the viewer's greatest fear or darkest secret. In her pale, ring-bedecked hands she holds the *Black Scepter of the Eclipse*, topped with a faceted diamond of absolute, light-devouring void. When she speaks, her voice is a chorus of falling ice needles and the sound of someone whispering your secret sins into your ear.
* **Personality**: Cold, calculating, utterly brilliant, and mercilessly pragmatic. She was once Titania's radiant sister, corrupted when she gazed upon the Black Crystal in the abyssal chasms beneath Faerie. She views emotion as a tool for manipulation and compassion as a lethal flaw. Yet she is not mindlessly destructive: she values discipline, contracts, contracts upheld to the letter, and the grim, elegant beauty of rot and winter.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

The Queen of Air and Darkness rarely battles directly, preferring to crush opponents through illusions, psychological disintegration, and curses. If forced to manifest her true might, she commands void magic, absolute permafrost, and the shattering of reflections.

---

## Roleplay & Lore Notes

* **The Shattered Loom Ambition**: The Queen seeks to unravel the Loom entirely, believing that mortal existence is a flawed, chaotic nightmare that must be plunged into the peaceful stillness of absolute zero and eternal dark.
* **Master of Pacts**: She will negotiate with mortals who bring her secrets from other planes—especially knowledge stolen from the Shadowfell or the divine shard planes. Her contracts are impeccably worded: they will never contain an outright lie, but the penalty for failure will always be the signer's eternal soul.
* **Rivalry with Titania**: Her war with Titania is deeply personal. Any mortal who strikes a humiliating blow against the Seelie Court can earn the Queen's cold, calculating protection.
