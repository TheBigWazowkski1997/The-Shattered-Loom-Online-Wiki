# The Queen of Air and Darkness (Sovereign of Night & Mistress of the Unseelie)

![[the_queen_of_air_and_darkness.jpg]]

* **Race**: Archfey (Primordial Fallen Fey Demigod of Darkness, Winter, and Forbidden Arcana)
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Unseelie Court|The Unseelie Court]] (Absolute Sovereign)
* **Role**: Ruler of the Gloaming Cradle / Empress of the Duskwood / Mistress of Broken Mirrors
* **Appearance**: A figure of chilling, terrifying, and fathomless majesty. The Queen has no permanent physical face—she appears as a tall, imperious silhouette draped in voluminous, floor-length robes woven of midnight-black velvet, swirling mist, and dying stars. Her face is concealed behind a mask of polished black obsidian that reflects the viewer's greatest fear or darkest secret. In her pale, ring-bedecked hands she holds the *Black Scepter of the Eclipse*, topped with a faceted diamond of absolute, light-devouring void. When she speaks, her voice is a chorus of falling ice needles and the sound of someone whispering your secret sins into your ear.
* **Personality**: Cold, calculating, utterly brilliant, and mercilessly pragmatic. She was once Titania's radiant sister, corrupted when she gazed upon the Black Crystal in the abyssal chasms beneath Faerie. She views emotion as a tool for manipulation and compassion as a lethal flaw. Yet she is not mindlessly destructive: she values discipline, contracts, contracts upheld to the letter, and the grim, elegant beauty of rot and winter.

---

## Combat Profile

The Queen of Air and Darkness rarely battles directly, preferring to crush opponents through illusions, psychological disintegration, and curses. If forced to manifest her true might, she commands void magic, absolute permafrost, and the shattering of reflections.

### **The Queen of Air and Darkness**
*Medium Fey (Archfey), Chaotic Evil*
* **Armor Class**: 22 (Obsidian Void Ward)
* **Hit Points**: 325 (30d8 + 190)
* **Speed**: 40 ft., fly 90 ft. (hover)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 14 (+2) | 24 (+7) | 22 (+6) | 28 (+9) | 26 (+8) | 30 (+10) |

* **Saving Throws**: Dex +15, Con +14, Int +17, Wis +16, Cha +18
* **Skills**: Arcana +25, Deception +26, History +25, Insight +24, Intimidation +26, Perception +24, Stealth +23
* **Damage Resistances**: Acid, Fire, Lightning; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
* **Damage Immunities**: Cold, Necrotic, Poison
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned, Stunned
* **Senses**: Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 34
* **Languages**: All languages, Telepathy 1,000 ft.
* **Challenge**: 24 (62,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +8

#### **Traits**
* **Aura of the Black Eclipse**: The Queen sheds an aura of magical darkness out to 30 feet that dispels non-magical light and magical light created by spells of 8th level or lower. Within this aura, enemy creatures have disadvantage on all saving throws.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If the Queen fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
* **Mirror-Walk**: As a bonus action, the Queen can step into any reflective surface (mirror, ice, still pool) within 60 feet and emerge from another reflective surface anywhere within her domain.
* **Innate Spellcasting**: The Queen's spellcasting ability is Charisma (Spell Save DC 26, +18 to hit with spell attacks). Prepared spells:
  * At will: *Darkness, Invisibility, Misty Step, Phantasmal Force, Ray of Frost*
  * 3/day each: *Cone of Cold, Maddening Darkness, Shadow of Moil, Disintegrate*
  * 1/day each: *Power Word Kill, Time Stop, Weird, Maze*

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: The Queen makes three attacks with her Scepter of the Eclipse or casts one spell and makes one scepter attack.
* **Scepter of the Eclipse**: *Melee Spell Attack*: +18 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 19 (2d8 + 10) force damage plus 22 (4d10) cold damage. The target must succeed on a DC 24 Wisdom saving throw or have its shadow ripped away, becoming vulnerable to necrotic damage until the shadow is restored.
* **Shatter the Reflection (Recharge 5–6)**: The Queen shatters the psychic reflections of all enemies within 60 feet. Each creature must make a DC 24 Intelligence saving throw, taking 65 (10d12) psychic damage and being stunned for 1 minute on a failed save, or half damage on a success.

#### **Legendary Actions**
The Queen can take 3 legendary actions:
* **Shadow Fade**: The Queen turns invisible and teleports up to 60 feet to an unoccupied space in dim light or darkness.
* **Whisper of Regret**: One creature within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 24 Wisdom save or take 22 (4d10) psychic damage and use its reaction to drop whatever it is holding.
* **Glacial Spikes (Costs 2 Actions)**: Pillars of black ice erupt beneath up to three creatures within 90 feet. Each target takes 35 (10d6) cold damage and is knocked prone (DC 24 Dex save for half).

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Shattered Loom Ambition**: The Queen seeks to unravel the Loom entirely, believing that mortal existence is a flawed, chaotic nightmare that must be plunged into the peaceful stillness of absolute zero and eternal dark.
* **Master of Pacts**: She will negotiate with mortals who bring her secrets from other planes—especially knowledge stolen from the Shadowfell or the divine shard planes. Her contracts are impeccably worded: they will never contain an outright lie, but the penalty for failure will always be the signer's eternal soul.
* **Rivalry with Titania**: Her war with Titania is deeply personal. Any mortal who strikes a humiliating blow against the Seelie Court can earn the Queen's cold, calculating protection.
