## 👤 Secondary Personages of Note

### 1. Archivist Mauren Coldsteel
* **Role**: Head of the Planar Surveyors (Former Clockwork Guild of Mechanus).
* **Location**: The Fractured District (Refuge of the Clear Mind).
* **Personality**: Meticulous, paranoid, constantly checks his chronometer and pinches his arms to verify he is awake.
* **Services**: Sells *Phials of Loom-Sap* (restores Lucidity) and offers safe sanctuary inside an anti-psionic lead-lined cellar.
* **Quote**: *"Do not trust the stairs here. If you count thirteen steps going down, you will find thirty-one coming back up."*

### 2. Scribe Kath'un
* **Role**: Antiquarian and Memory-Broker.
* **Location**: Starlight Harbor (*The Scribed Nautilus*).
* **Appearance**: Tall, cloaked humanoid whose lower face has dissolved into four articulate, violet tentacles.
* **Trade**: Trades in *Astral Compass-Orbs* and *Memory Crystals*. Accepts payment in unexpended 5th-level spell slots or memories of childhood.

### 3. Captain Vaelen Dreadtide
* **Role**: Astral Smuggler and Tide-Captain.
* **Location**: *The Drowned Reflection* Tavern, Starlight Harbor.
* **Appearance**: Spectral sailor whose spectral uniform drips phantom brine that evaporates into indigo smoke.
* **Vessel**: *The Somnolent Wake*, an astral caravel that sails the Tides of Thought without being crushed by mental pressure.
