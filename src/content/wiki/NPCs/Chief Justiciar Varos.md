# Chief Justiciar Varos (The Axiomatic Hammer)

![[attachments/chief_justiciar_varos.jpg]]

* **Race**: Githzerai (Axiom-sworn)
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Shattered Law/Plane of Shattered Law The Fractured Spire|Wardens of the Fractured Code]] / Axiom High Command
* **Role**: Supreme War-Magistrate of Axiom, Master of the Axiomatic Armory
* **Appearance**: A tall, weathered githzerai with skin the color of dry parchment, bearing glowing geometric brand-tattoos across his shaved scalp. He wears rigid plate armor forged from black star-iron and trimmed with inlaid gold circuit-like glyphs. At his hip hangs *Equation's Edge*, a flawless greatsword that hums with an unnervingly flat, steady acoustic pitch.
* **Personality**: Dogmatic, unyielding, and utterly devoid of humor. Varos sees the multiverse as a grand, broken calculation that can only be saved through the absolute enforcement of cosmic order. He has no personal malice toward chaotic beings; he views them simply as errors in the equation that must be systematically factored out.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Code Above All**: Varos does not accept bribes, flattery, or emotional appeals. To gain his favor or aid, parties must appeal to strict logic, precedent, or present a legal charter signed by planar authorities.
* **The Hunt for Unit Secundus**: He secretly fears that Unit Secundus was deliberately sabotaged by anarchists within the Fractured Spire, and will reward adventurers who uncover the truth without causing panic.
