# Fray, the Unraveler (The Discordant Champion)

![[fray_the_unraveler.jpg]]

* **Race**: Riftborn (Renegade Starlight Entity)
* **Faction**: [[The Frayed Ones]] / Champion of the Spindle Ring
* **Role**: Pit Champion / Undercroft Warlord / Filament Saboteur
* **Appearance**: Unlike the harmonious, golden-white geometry of lawful Riftborn, Fray's body is composed of jagged, shifting planes of violet and fractured crimson starlight. Sharp polygon edges orbit their torso like floating obsidian blades, sparking with dark static whenever they move. They do not possess a humanoid mouth; instead, aggressive geometric fracture-patterns flicker across their diamond-shaped face, and their voice reverberates through the air like grinding crystal and tearing silk. Across their back are strapped two curved, jagged *Filament-Shearing Scythes* forged from meteoric void-iron.
* **Personality**: Fierce, cynical, and uncompromisingly rebellious. Fray views the entire concept of the "Divine Blueprint" as cosmic enslavement. Having spent decades repairing filaments for the Pale Lodge before their defection, Fray knows every structural weakness of the Nexus. In the arena, they fight with cruel, unpredictable brilliance, playing to the undercroft crowd and mocking lawful authority.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Fray is an erratic, highly mobile skirmisher who dominates three-dimensional battlefields. In the Spindle Ring fighting pits, they utilize the arena's iron grates and subjective gravity to attack from impossible angles, shearing through defensive magic, blinding foes with fractured geometric flares, and hurling enemies into void trenches. Their discordant form causes ranged strikes and spells to refract harmlessly through space, while their void-shearing scythes strip away magical barriers with devastating speed.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Defection from the Spire:** Fray was originally a high-ranking Riftborn artisan known as *Facet*, tasked with maintaining the primary anchor filaments of the Spire Ward. After witnessing the Spindle Lords quietly sacrifice outer refugee shards to preserve the Spire's stability, Fray snapped, severing their oath and renaming themselves Fray the Unraveler.
* **The Arena Showdown (Spindle Ring):** When the party encounters Fray in the Void Pits, Fray taunts them as "puppets on golden strings." Fray fights with reckless swagger, using the arena's iron grates, subjective gravity drops, and void-bile slicks to gain the crowd's roaring favor.
* **Spare or Execute:** If defeated and spared, Fray laughs bitterly, their fractured starlight dimming to a deep violet. They reveal that the **Entropic Hand** is being covertly supplied with void-iron by a corrupted Justiciar inside the Threadbearer Spindle Council, and offers the party their secret cipher to navigate the Folding Alleys without triggering traps.
