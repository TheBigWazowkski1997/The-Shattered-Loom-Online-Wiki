# Matron Varis Gloomweaver (Hand of the Raven Queen)

![[attachments/matron_varis_gloomweaver.jpg]]

* **Race**: Shadar-kai (Shadow Elf)
* **Faction**: The Shadar-kai Memory-Sworn / Disciples of the Fortress
* **Role**: High Inquisitor of Memories / Planar Emissary of the Raven Queen
* **Appearance**: An ageless, striking figure whose porcelain skin flickers between serene elven youth and hollow, skeletal decay. She has stark white hair that flows like smoke, eyes entirely black without whites or pupils, and robes of woven liquid shadow pinned by silver raven talons. A delicate silver spindle hangs from her sash, softly humming with captured memory light.
* **Personality**: Detached, enigmatic, coldly dignified, yet deeply compassionate toward souls suffering meaningless torment. She does not fear death, regarding it as the tranquil unknotting of a long, tangled thread. She speaks in quiet, resonant whispers that echo with the fluttering of thousands of raven wings.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Matron Varis is a deadly gloom-arcanist and skirmisher who slips through shadow as easily as air. She uses necrotic spells, memory-severing daggers, and teleportation to flank foes, plucking the memories of spells and abilities straight from their minds in mid-combat.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Raven Queen's Will Across Domains:** Varis moves freely through the Mists of Ravenloft using shadow-paths granted by her mistress. She maintains active covert alliances with the [[../Factions/The Keepers of the Feather|Keepers of the Feather]] in Barovia and the [[../Factions/The Ulmist Inquisition|Ulmist Inquisition]] in Darkon.
* **Interaction with the Party:** Varis recognizes the party as bearers of Loom destiny. Following their recovery of the corrupted Crimson Weave from Berez, she may approach them in a quiet tavern corner or misty crossroad, offering planar guidance, warnings about the Darklords, or holy raven feather charms to ward off madness.
* **The Cosmic Balance:** Varis despises both the Dark Powers and the Cult of the Broken Thread. She will actively assist the party if their actions prevent a Darklord from unmaking reality or feeding a Loom fragment to entropic horrors.
