## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Baphomet is a savage juggernaut who combines labyrinthine cunning with brutal physical violence. Armed with the cleaving glaive Heartcleaver and vicious horns, he excels at hunting down prey in winding subterranean corridors and overwhelming opponents with reckless momentum.

---

## **Roleplay & Lore Notes**
* **Personality**: Primal, brutal, calculating behind his bestial exterior. He despises Yeenoghu with a burning, personal hatred, waging constant wars along their intersecting fracture-zones.
* **Loom Integration**: Baphomet carves mathematical knots into the fabric of space, trapping planar wanderers in recursive loop-traps where time dilates and reason unravels.
