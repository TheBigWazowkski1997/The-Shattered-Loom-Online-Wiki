# Maerith the Whisperer (High Priestess of the Dreambound)

![[attachments/maerith_the_whisperer.jpeg]]

* **Race**: High Elf (corrupted by Far Realm exposure)
* **Faction**: The Dreambound Cult
* **Role**: High Priestess — spiritual leader and ritual architect of the Dreambound Cult
* **Appearance**: A pale elf with glowing, pupilless silver eyes that reflect light in the wrong direction. Tendrils of shadow-like energy trail from her movements like smoke clinging to a dying flame. Her voice carries a subtle echo — as if a thousand voices speak the same words a half-second behind her, just below the threshold of hearing.
* **Personality**: Charismatic, fanatical, and deeply reverent of the Great Dreamer. She speaks with the quiet confidence of someone who has seen the truth and pities those who haven't. She is not cruel — she genuinely believes awakening the Dreamer will end all mortal suffering by dissolving it.

---
## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Maerith fights with divine shadow power, blending cleric channeling with dream-inflicted madness. She uses *Dominate Person* and *Dream* to turn enemies against each other before committing her own power. In close combat, her shadow tendrils strike with chilling necrotic energy.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Ritual of Awakening:** Maerith's central goal is performing the Ritual — a complex ceremony requiring rare artifacts (at least one Loom Shard) and the willing or unwilling sacrifice of a powerful soul. She will spend months in patient preparation rather than rush it.
* **The Compassionate Villain:** Unlike the Cult of the Broken Thread, Maerith does not want to destroy. She wants to *release*. In her mind, awakening the Dreamer will dissolve the separation between all consciousness, ending loneliness, fear, and suffering forever. She may attempt to *convert* the players rather than fight them.
* **The Whispering Network:** The Dreambound cult has cells across three planes. Maerith communicates with them through *Dream* and *Sending*, and the whispers of the Great Dreamer himself guide her strategic decisions in ways she does not always consciously understand.
