# Khanum Zoraya of the Seven Veins (Mistress of the Dismal Delve)

![[attachments/khanum_zoraya.jpg]]

* **Race**: Dao (Noble Earth Genie)
* **Faction**: The Seven-Vein Concessions / The Great Dismal Delve
* **Role**: Sovereign Gem-Magnate of Deepmaw Quarry, High Slavelord
* **Appearance**: A colossal, imposing noble dao standing nearly ten feet tall, with polished bronze-brown skin and long hair adorned with strands of woven diamonds, emeralds, and gold chains. Her thick, muscular frame is draped in heavy cloth-of-gold and layered velvet embroidered with powdered gemstones. She wears a torc of uncut star sapphires and wields an ornate two-handed maul fashioned from a single gigantic dark amethyst.
* **Personality**: Greedy, imperious, cruel, and boastful. Zoraya loves precious gems with an all-consuming passion, viewing mortals and elementals alike merely as tools to unearth more treasures for her vaults. She is easily flattered by displays of opulence and deference, but swift to petrify anyone who cheats or disrespects her contracts.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Gem Hoard**: Zoraya measures respect by the carat. She can be bribed or placated by exceptionally rare or foreign gemstones (especially those from the Plane of Eternal Craft or the Astral Sea).
* **Ruthless Employer**: She frequently hires outside adventurers to hunt down runaway indentured workers, seize rogue mines, or assassinate rival Dao cartel agents who infringe on her concessions.
* **Quests**: She will pay enormous sums in diamonds and Ioun stones to any party that retrieves ancient pre-Shattering relics from the Abyssal Roots or eliminates rival cartels in Deepmaw Quarry.
