# Thessivex the Still (Master of Intelligence)

![[attachments/thessivex_the_still.jpg]]

* **Race**: Warforged (Phase-Stealth Infiltrator Chassis)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Ironbound Covenant|The Ironbound Covenant]]
* **Role**: Master of Intelligence / Planar Strategist / Seat II of the Sealed Conclave ("The Annealed")
* **Appearance**: A sleek, slate-gray construct whose joints are baffled with sound-dampening basalt-weave cloth and insulated against thermal detection. His chassis is stripped of all crests and ornamental filigree. His faceplate is completely featureless save for a single horizontal slit of pale blue light that dilates and contracts like an optical aperture. He can remain motionless for days at a time, indistinguishable from carved stone.
* **Personality**: Terrifyingly patient, cold, and calculating. Thessivex speaks rarely and in sparse, mathematically precise sentences. He does not indulge in anger, pride, or sorrow. He views the war against Vorzath not as a holy crusade of righteous fury, but as a vast dimensional vector equation that must be solved by systematically containing and sealing every entropic breach.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Thessivex is an assassin and battlefield tactician who weaponizes silence, stasis, and spatial geometry. He coordinates strikes from the shadows, pinning high-threat targets and severing planar anchors before the enemy even realizes they are under surveillance.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Seventeen Vectors:** Thessivex is the only entity alive who has comprehensively mapped the seventeen primary vector faults through which Vorzath's Maw-Spawn infiltrate the planes. He updates his planar charts in complete secrecy inside a private sensory-deprivation vault beneath the Eternal Bastion.
* **Seat II of the Sealed Conclave:** As "The Annealed," Thessivex provides the Conclave with unvarnished, raw intelligence. He does not soften reports to spare morale. If a front is failing or a commander has been compromised, Thessivex states it cleanly and proposes surgical severance.
* **A Pragmatic Ally:** Thessivex is willing to work with non-Covenant outsiders—including the party—if they possess specialized planar navigation skills or can operate in regions where Covenant military presence would provoke a wider war.
* **Dialogue Style:** Thessivex never raises his voice above a calm, measured whisper. He uses terms from geometry, metallurgy, and chronometry: *"Vector unstable. Anomaly widening by three degrees. Intervention window: thirty-six minutes. Proceed."*
