# Pharaoh Ankhtepot (The Sunless Sovereign)

![[pharaoh_ankhtepot.jpg]]

* **Race**: Mummy Lord (Undead Humanoid)
* **Faction**: The Jackal Priesthood of Har'Akir / The First Dynasty
* **Role**: Darklord of Har'Akir / Master of the Black Pyramid
* **Appearance**: A desiccated, gaunt titan of royal majesty wrapped in yellowed, natron-stained linens embroidered with gold hieroglyphs. He wears the Nemes headdress of lapis lazuli and gold, the ceremonial beard of divine kingship, and an ornate collar studded with turquoise and carnelians. In his clawed, dried hands, he holds the golden *Crook of Desiccation* and the bronze *Flail of Pestilence*. When he opens his cracked mouth, sand spills from his lips and his hollow eyes burn with sullen amber fire.
* **Personality**: Bitter, grandiose, despairing, and vengeful. Ankhtepot sacrificed his own people's souls in an ancient ritual to achieve godlike immortality, only to be dragged into the Shadowfell by the Dark Powers. He hungers for genuine worship as a living god, yet every prayer uttered by his subjects is choked with terror and hatred, which tastes like ash upon his tongue.

---

## Combat Profile

Pharaoh Ankhtepot fights with the crushing necrotic weight of ancient curses, sandstorm control, and insect plagues. He relies on his extreme resilience, high spellcasting potency, and the claustrophobic stone traps of his Black Pyramid to entomb intruders alive.

### **Pharaoh Ankhtepot**
*Medium Undead, Lawful Evil*
* **Armor Class**: 18 (Natural Armor)
* **Hit Points**: 161 (19d8 + 76)
* **Speed**: 30 ft., burrow 30 ft. (through sand)

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 19 (+4) | 12 (+1) | 18 (+4) | 16 (+3) | 20 (+5) | 18 (+4) |

* **Saving Throws**: Con +9, Int +8, Wis +10, Cha +9
* **Skills**: History +8, Insight +10, Perception +10, Religion +8
* **Damage Vulnerabilities**: Fire
* **Damage Immunities**: Necrotic, Poison; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Petrified, Poisoned
* **Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 20
* **Languages**: Ancient Akirian, Celestial, Common
* **Challenge**: 16 (15,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +5

#### **Traits**
* **Magic Resistance**: Ankhtepot has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects.
* **Rejuvenation**: A destroyed Ankhtepot gains a new body in 24 hours if his heart remains preserved in its canopic jar inside the Black Pyramid, regaining all hit points.
* **Sunless Desiccation**: Water within 30 feet of Ankhtepot evaporates instantly. Creatures that hit Ankhtepot with a melee attack while within 5 feet must make a DC 17 Constitution saving throw or take 10 (3d6) necrotic damage as moisture is sucked from their flesh.
* **Spellcasting**: Ankhtepot is a 12th-level spellcaster (Spell Save DC 18, +10 to hit with spell attacks). Prepared spells:
  * Cantrips (at will): *Sacred Flame, Thaumaturgy, Toll the Dead*
  * 1st level (4 slots): *Command, Guiding Bolt, Inflict Wounds, Shield of Faith*
  * 2nd level (3 slots): *Hold Person, Silence, Spiritual Weapon*
  * 3rd level (3 slots): *Animate Dead, Dispel Magic, Spirit Guardians*
  * 4th level (3 slots): *Banishment, Blight, Guardian of Faith*
  * 5th level (2 slots): *Contagion, Flame Strike, Insect Plague*
  * 6th level (1 slot): *Harm*

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Ankhtepot can use his Dreadful Glare and makes two melee attacks (one with his Crook and one with his Flail).
* **Crook of Desiccation**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 11 (2d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 14 (4d6) necrotic damage. Target must succeed on a DC 17 Con save or be infected with Mummy Rot.
* **Flail of Pestilence**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 10 (3d6) poison damage.
* **Dreadful Glare**: Ankhtepot targets one creature within 60 feet. If the target can see him, it must succeed on a DC 17 Wisdom save or be paralyzed with fear until the end of Ankhtepot's next turn.

#### **Legendary Actions**
Ankhtepot can take 3 legendary actions:
* **Attack**: Ankhtepot makes one attack with his Crook or Flail.
* **Dust Step**: Ankhtepot dissolves into a cloud of red sand and teleports up to 30 feet.
* **Whirlwind of Sand (Costs 2 Actions)**: Ankhtepot magically unleashes a blinding sandstorm in a 20-foot radius. Each creature must make a DC 17 Con save, taking 21 (6d6) slashing damage and being blinded for 1 round on a failed save.

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Ancient Solar Fragment:** Deep beneath the Black Pyramid, in the Sunken Sanctuary of Sekhet, lies an ancient fragment of the Shattered Loom—a solar crystal that once nourished Har'Akir's lush golden grain fields before the domain was ripped into the Shadowfell. Ankhtepot guards this fragment jealously, hoping to reignite it to burn away the Mists.
* **The Curse of False Prayers:** If the party flatters Ankhtepot or offers genuine-sounding divine praise, he is intrigued, pausing hostilities to test their sincerity. However, if he detects mockery or deception, his wrath is absolute.
* **The Desert Trade:** Ankhtepot tolerates the Water Cartel in Muhar only because their extortion drives mortals to despair, feeding the dark energies that sustain his pyramid.
