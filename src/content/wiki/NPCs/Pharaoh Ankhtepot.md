# Pharaoh Ankhtepot (The Sunless Sovereign)

![[pharaoh_ankhtepot.jpg]]

* **Race**: Mummy Lord (Undead Humanoid)
* **Faction**: The Jackal Priesthood of Har'Akir / The First Dynasty
* **Role**: Darklord of Har'Akir / Master of the Black Pyramid
* **Appearance**: A desiccated, gaunt titan of royal majesty wrapped in yellowed, natron-stained linens embroidered with gold hieroglyphs. He wears the Nemes headdress of lapis lazuli and gold, the ceremonial beard of divine kingship, and an ornate collar studded with turquoise and carnelians. In his clawed, dried hands, he holds the golden *Crook of Desiccation* and the bronze *Flail of Pestilence*. When he opens his cracked mouth, sand spills from his lips and his hollow eyes burn with sullen amber fire.
* **Personality**: Bitter, grandiose, despairing, and vengeful. Ankhtepot sacrificed his own people's souls in an ancient ritual to achieve godlike immortality, only to be dragged into the Shadowfell by the Dark Powers. He hungers for genuine worship as a living god, yet every prayer uttered by his subjects is choked with terror and hatred, which tastes like ash upon his tongue.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Pharaoh Ankhtepot fights with the crushing necrotic weight of ancient curses, sandstorm control, and insect plagues. He relies on his extreme resilience, high spellcasting potency, and the claustrophobic stone traps of his Black Pyramid to entomb intruders alive.

---

## Roleplay & Lore Notes

* **The Ancient Solar Fragment:** Deep beneath the Black Pyramid, in the Sunken Sanctuary of Sekhet, lies an ancient fragment of the Shattered Loom—a solar crystal that once nourished Har'Akir's lush golden grain fields before the domain was ripped into the Shadowfell. Ankhtepot guards this fragment jealously, hoping to reignite it to burn away the Mists.
* **The Curse of False Prayers:** If the party flatters Ankhtepot or offers genuine-sounding divine praise, he is intrigued, pausing hostilities to test their sincerity. However, if he detects mockery or deception, his wrath is absolute.
* **The Desert Trade:** Ankhtepot tolerates the Water Cartel in Muhar only because their extortion drives mortals to despair, feeding the dark energies that sustain his pyramid.
