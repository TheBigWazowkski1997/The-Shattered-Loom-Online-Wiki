# Zariel, Archduchess of Avernus

![[zariel.jpg]]

* **Race**: Fallen Solar Angel / Archdevil of Avernus
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Legions of the Blood War|The Legions of the Blood War]] (Supreme Military Commander)
* **Role**: Ruler of Avernus / Supreme Commander of the Stitch-Legions / Guardian of the First Front
* **Appearance**: A towering, battle-scarred figure of awe-inspiring martial fury. Zariel possesses cracked alabaster skin veined with molten gold and searing hellfire. Her eyes burn like twin miniature suns beneath a blackened blindfold or battle-helm. From her back spread two magnificent wings: one composed of charred black feathers and skeletal iron spurs, the other blazing with roaring crimson hellfire and smoke. She wears spiked infernal plate armor engraved with celestial runes and wields a colossal greatsword wreathed in white-hot divine fire.
* **Personality**: Fierce, uncompromising, tragic, and consumed by relentless martial fury. Zariel did not fall because she was evil; she fell because she refused to stand by and watch the multiverse unravel while the gods debated. She views the Blood War as the only thing standing between existence and the infinite void of the Abyss. She despises cowardice, political scheming, and hesitation, respecting only martial valor and unwavering resolve.

---

## Combat Profile

Archduchess Zariel is a relentless martial engine. She charges directly into the thickest ranks of the enemy, cleaving through fiendish hordes with her flaming greatsword and commanding her war machine legions with tactical brilliance.

### **Zariel, Archduchess of Avernus**
*Large Fiend (Archdevil), Lawful Evil*
* **Armor Class**: 21 (Natural Armor & Infernal Plate)
* **Hit Points**: 420 (29d10 + 261)
* **Speed**: 50 ft., fly 150 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 27 (+8) | 24 (+7) | 28 (+9) | 26 (+8) | 27 (+8) | 30 (+10) |

* **Saving Throws**: Str +16, Dex +15, Con +17, Wis +16, Cha +18
* **Skills**: Athletics +16, Insight +16, Intimidation +18, Perception +16
* **Damage Resistances**: Cold; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
* **Damage Immunities**: Fire, Necrotic, Poison
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
* **Senses**: Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 26
* **Languages**: All languages, Telepathy 1,000 ft.
* **Challenge**: 26 (90,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +8

#### **Traits**
* **Aura of Hellfire Wrath**: Any enemy creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of Zariel must make a DC 26 Wisdom saving throw or take 14 (4d6) fire damage plus 14 (4d6) radiant damage and be frightened until the start of its next turn.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Zariel fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
* **Magic Weapons**: Zariel's weapon attacks are magical and deal an extra 36 (8d8) fire damage (included in the attacks).
* **Innate Spellcasting**: Spellcasting ability is Charisma (Spell Save DC 26, +18 to hit with spell attacks). Prepared spells:
  * At will: *Alter Self, Detect Evil and Good, Fireball, Invisibility (self only), Wall of Fire*
  * 3/day each: *Blade Barrier, Dispel Evil and Good, Finger of Death*
  * 1/day each: *Holy Aura, Meteor Swarm*

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Zariel makes three attacks with her Greatsword of Blazing Wrath or two ranged javelin attacks.
* **Greatsword of Blazing Wrath**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +16 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 15 (2d6 + 8) slashing damage plus 36 (8d8) fire damage. If the target is a fiend (demon), it takes an extra 18 (4d8) radiant damage.
* **Hellfire Javelin**: *Ranged Weapon Attack*: +16 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. *Hit*: 15 (2d6 + 8) piercing damage plus 36 (8d8) fire damage.
* **Horrid Touch (Recharge 5–6)**: Zariel touches one creature within 10 feet. The target must make a DC 26 Constitution save, taking 44 (8d10) necrotic damage and being poisoned for 1 minute on a failed save, or half damage on a success.

#### **Legendary Actions**
Zariel can take 3 legendary actions:
* **Teleport**: Zariel teleports up to 120 feet to an unoccupied space she can see.
* **Immolating Gaze**: One creature within 60 feet must make a DC 26 Wisdom save or take 22 (4d10) fire damage and be blinded until the end of its next turn.
* **Blazing Cleave (Costs 2 Actions)**: Zariel sweeps her blade in a 15-foot cone. Each creature must make a DC 26 Dexterity save, taking 35 (10d6) fire damage on a failed save.

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Tragedy of Avernus**: Zariel views her damnation as a noble sacrifice. Mortals who seek her favor must demonstrate unwavering courage and willingness to fight the Abyssal hordes.
* **Redemption or War**: A party carrying her lost celestial sword (or deep memories of her pre-fallen grace) can potentially unlock the path to her redemption, altering the balance of the Blood War forever.
