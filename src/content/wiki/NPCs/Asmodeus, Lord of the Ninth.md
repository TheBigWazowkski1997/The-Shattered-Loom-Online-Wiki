# Lord Asmodeus, Supreme Master of the Nine Hells

![[asmodeus.jpg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity / Primordial Archdevil (The Dark Architect & Lord of the Ninth)
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Infernal Hierarchy of Baator|The Infernal Hierarchy of Baator]] (Supreme Monarch)
* **Role**: Ruler of Nessus / Sovereign Master of Malsheem / Prime Weaver of the Infernal Spindle
* **Appearance**: An imposing, terrifyingly handsome, and impeccably aristocratic devil god. Asmodeus possesses deep crimson-ruby skin, piercing glowing golden eyes, and sleek black swept-back hair framed by elegant, curving obsidian horns. His skin is faintly etched with the master blueprints of the cosmos, glowing with faint reddish-gold light. He wears imperial robes of midnight-black velvet, crimson silk, and gold-trimmed infernal filigree that absorb nearby light. In his hand he holds the legendary *Ruby Rod of Asmodeus*—a scepter of faceted blood-red crystal topped with a crowned dark star that crackles with divine authority and red planar lightning.
* **Personality**: Supremely composed, chillingly polite, fathomlessly patient, and mercilessly brilliant. Asmodeus never raises his voice, never acts in anger, and never shows fear. He views the entire multiverse as a complex, flawed calculation drafted by Telara—one that he is systematically rewriting into a state of permanent, immutable order. He considers all other gods, archdevils, and mortals to be mere variables in his master theorem.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Lord Asmodeus rarely engages in physical combat directly, preferring to subjugate opponents with the absolute legal authority of the Pact Primeval and the reality-warping power of the Ruby Rod. If directly challenged in his sanctum in Malsheem, he commands the primal laws of absolute tyranny and cosmic subjugation.

---

## Roleplay & Lore Notes

* **The Counter-Loom Mandate**: Asmodeus views the Shattered Loom not as a tragedy, but as proof of Telara's cosmic incompetence. He is actively spinning the **Infernal Spindle** to impose absolute, unbending order across the multiverse. He will bargain with mortals, offering divine favors in exchange for their participation in his grand legal treaties.
* **The Master Chess-Player**: Asmodeus never threatens or blusters. When interacting with player characters, he speaks with the warm, reassuring cadence of an elder statesman offering reasonable solutions to difficult cosmic problems.
* **Detente with the Gods**: The upper gods tolerate Asmodeus because his Blood War legions prevent the Abyss from drowning all existence in madness.
