# 💉 Doctor Vaelen Malcor, Void-Surgeon
### *Chief Anatomist of the Severed Eye, Master of Non-Euclidean Pathology, Suture of the Un-Woven*

> *"Hand me the cold-iron clamp and hold the lead shield steady. When I slice into the third ventricle of this star-spawn's neural lobe, it will scream in four languages you don't know, and your teeth will feel loose. Ignore the teeth. Watch the bile."*  
> — **Doctor Vaelen Malcor**, during a field autopsy

---

## 📜 Identity & Background

**Doctor Vaelen Malcor** is an eccentric, brilliant human-tiefling anatomist whose medical textbooks on aberrant pathology are studied throughout the Grand Archive and the Order. Based in the Dissection Cellars of the Sanctorum, Malcor is responsible for discovering how to harvest living psionic glands without causing spontaneous brain liquefaction in the harvester.

* **Title**: Chief Void-Surgeon, Master Anatomist of the Severed Eye.
* **Habitat**: Dissection Vaults, Sanctorum of the Blind Needle, The Fractured Nexus.
* **Creature Type**: Medium Humanoid (Tiefling), Neutral Good.
* **Services**: Performs master void-harvesting autopsies, crafts *Phials of Lucid Brine*, and implants anti-psionic bone sutures.
