# Alanik Ray (The Great Inquisitive)

![[alanik_ray.jpg]]

* **Race**: Elf (High Elf Investigator)
* **Faction**: Consulting Detectives of Lucine / Independent
* **Role**: Master Consulting Inquisitive / Associate of Dr. Arthur Sedgwick
* **Appearance**: An immaculate, slender elven gentleman in his late centuries, possessing silver-streaked dark hair, high arched cheekbones, and piercing hazel eyes that seem to register every micro-expression and speck of mud in a room. He dresses in tailored charcoal wool coats, silk cravats, and carries an engraved mahogany walking stick concealing a silver rapier.
* **Personality**: Methodical, razor-sharp, emotionally guarded, and profoundly observant. Ray lives by logic, observation, and deductive forensic science in a realm where truth is considered an unforgivable crime. Alongside his stalwart friend Dr. Arthur Sedgwick, he has solved hundreds of impossible murders across Dementlieu, Borca, and Darkon, making him a dangerous thorn in the side of the Darklords.

---

## Combat Profile

Alanik Ray fights with analytical precision. He does not engage in wild brawls; he studies an opponent's footwork, center of gravity, and armor seams before striking with lethal accuracy, utilizing dirty tricks and forensic flashes to incapacitate attackers.

*Medium Humanoid (Elf, Rogue/Inquisitive), Neutral Good*
* **Armor Class**: 17 (+1 Studded Leather)
* **Hit Points**: 91 (14d8 + 28)
* **Speed**: 30 ft.

#### **Traits**
* **Eye for Detail**: Ray can use a bonus action to make a Wisdom (Perception) check to spot a hidden creature or an Intelligence (Investigation) check to uncover clues.
* **Insightful Fighting**: As a bonus action, Ray can make a Wisdom (Insight) check contested by a target's Charisma (Deception) check. If he succeeds, he can use his Sneak Attack against that target even without advantage for 1 minute.
* **Sneak Attack (1/Turn)**: Ray deals an extra 17 (5d6) damage when he hits a target with a weapon attack and has advantage on the attack roll, or when his Insightful Fighting applies.

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Ray makes two attacks with his Cane-Rapier or fires two shots with his Pepperbox Pistol.
* **Cane-Rapier (+1 Rapier)**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +8 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 9 (1d8 + 5) piercing damage.
* **Concealed Pepperbox Pistol**: *Ranged Weapon Attack*: +7 to hit, range 40/120 ft., one target. *Hit*: 9 (1d10 + 4) piercing damage.
* **Flash-Powder Cane (3/Day)**: Ray strikes the ground with his cane, igniting magnesium flash-powder. Each creature within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 16 Constitution saving throw or be blinded until the end of Ray's next turn.

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Impostor File:** Alanik Ray possesses an encrypted notebook locked in his study safe detailing conclusive evidence that Duchess Saidra d'Honaire is a fraud who rose from the servant class. He has not revealed this because he knows exposing her publicly would trigger a catastrophic slaughter of everyone in Port-a-Lucine.
* **Partnering with the Party:** Ray serves as the ultimate patron for mystery, investigation, and espionage quests in Dementlieu and the wider Shadowfell. He will hire the party to extract witnesses, break into noble vaults, or protect whistleblowers.
* **Understanding the Loom:** Ray has deduced that the Mists of Ravenloft are not natural weather, but an entropic weave defect. He tracks "Loom Stitches"—anomalies where planar fabric tears and spills foreign travelers into the domains.
