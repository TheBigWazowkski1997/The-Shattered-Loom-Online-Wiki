## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Graz'zt fights with elegant malice and aristocratic cruelty. Preferring to dominate minds, charm combatants, and teleport effortlessly through the shadows, he wields his acidic greatsword Wave of Sorrow with lethal finesse while bending reality to punish anyone daring to challenge his absolute sovereignty.

---

## **Roleplay & Lore Notes**
* **Personality**: Charming, seductive, aristocratic, deeply narcissistic. He never shouts; his voice is a warm, velvety purr that makes horrific cruelty sound like reasonable compromise.
* **Complex Ties**: Graz'zt shares a passionate, volatile history with Tasha (Iggwilv) and fathers numerous cambions across the planes (including Iuz). He seeks to lure threadwalkers into surrendering their Loom fragments in exchange for endless sensory delights.
