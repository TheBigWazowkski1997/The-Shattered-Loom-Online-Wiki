## **D&D 5e Stat Block Profile (CR 24)**

*Large Fiend (Demon), Chaotic Evil*

* **Armor Class**: 20 (Natural Armor)
* **Hit Points**: 346 (29d10 + 187)
* **Speed**: 40 ft., fly 40 ft.
* **Saving Throws**: Dex +11, Con +14, Wis +13, Cha +17
* **Skills**: Deception +17, Insight +13, Perception +13, Persuasion +17
* **Damage Resistances**: Cold, Fire, Lightning
* **Damage Immunities**: Poison; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
* **Senses**: Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 23
* **Languages**: All, Telepathy 120 ft.
* **Challenge**: 24 (62,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +7

### **Special Traits**
* **Shapechanger**: Graz'zt can use an action to polymorph into any humanoid form of Small, Medium, or Large size, retaining his statistics.
* **Innate Spellcasting**: Charisma-based (DC 25, +17 to hit). At will: *Charm Person*, *Detect Magic*, *Dispel Magic*, *Dissonant Whispers*, *Sanctuary*. 3/day each: *Dominate Person*, *Teleport*, *Hold Monster*. 1/day: *Feeblemind*.
* **Joy from Pain**: Whenever Graz'zt suffers a critical hit, he gains advantage on all attack rolls and saving throws until the end of his next turn.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: Can choose to succeed on a failed saving throw.

### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Graz'zt makes two Greatsword attacks or casts a spell and makes one Greatsword attack.
* **Wave-Bladed Greatsword (+2)**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +16 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 22 (4d6 + 8) slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) acid damage.
* **Seductive Teleport**: Graz'zt teleports up to 60 feet. Each creature within 10 feet of his destination space must make a DC 25 Wisdom save or be charmed by Graz'zt until the end of its next turn.

### **Legendary Actions (3/Round)**
* **Attack**: Graz'zt makes one Greatsword attack.
* **Dance of Seduction**: Graz'zt targets one creature he can see within 60 feet. The target must succeed on a DC 25 Wisdom save or become charmed until the end of Graz'zt's next turn.
* **Abyssal Command (Costs 2 Actions)**: Graz'zt issues a command to a charmed creature, which must immediately use its reaction to follow the command.

***

## **Roleplay & DM Notes**
* **Personality**: Charming, seductive, aristocratic, deeply narcissistic. He never shouts; his voice is a warm, velvety purr that makes horrific cruelty sound like reasonable compromise.
* **Complex Ties**: Graz'zt shares a passionate, volatile history with Tasha (Iggwilv) and fathers numerous cambions across the planes (including Iuz). He seeks to lure threadwalkers into surrendering their Loom fragments in exchange for endless sensory delights.
