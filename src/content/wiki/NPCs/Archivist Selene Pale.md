# Archivist Selene Pale (Keeper of Thoughts)

![[attachments/archivist_selene_pale.jpg]]

* **Race**: Kalashtar
* **Faction**: The Veiled Archive / Mnemosyne Apothecary
* **Role**: Master Mentalist, Memory Restorer, Apothecary Keeper
* **Appearance**: A graceful, dignified kalashtar woman with hair the color of spun liquid silver and serene, pupil-less opalescent eyes that seem to perceive thought waves. She wears layered scholar's robes of pale twilight silk embroidered with runic mandalas, and carries a delicate silver quill capable of drawing radiant memory threads directly from the air.
* **Personality**: Calm, empathetic, and profoundly philosophical. Having seen the deepest joys and darkest horrors of thousands of minds, Selene judges no one for their past. She approaches psychic wounds with the solemn care of a master surgeon and considers mental consent paramount.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Selene is a formidable psychic spellcaster and mental warder capable of confounding enemies, pacifying aggression, and retaliating with overwhelming mental force.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Restoration Services**: Parties suffering from planar amnesia, *Feeblemind*, madness, or memory tampering come to Selene. She can reconstruct lost clues, quest memories, or unravel mental curses for a fee or exchange of rare planar lore.
* **The Forbidden Reliquary**: Selene knows the danger of the "First Sorrow" reliquary and will aid parties attempting to keep it out of the hands of abyssal cultists.
