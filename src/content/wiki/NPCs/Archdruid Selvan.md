# Archdruid Selvan (The Green Father)

![[attachments/archdruid_selvan.jpg]]

* **Race**: Firbolg
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Wild Balance/Plane of Wild Balance Verdant Nexus|The Verdant Council]] / Apex Bloom Apothecary
* **Role**: High Speaker of the Elderbough, Master Herbalist
* **Appearance**: A colossal, gentle firbolg standing over eight feet tall, with weathered greyish-blue skin, a wide flat nose, and a voluminous silver beard interwoven with living green moss and blossoming wild jasmine. Small forest birds and glowing planar fireflies frequently nest within his wide brimmed straw hat and shoulders. He carries *Bough-Heart*, a gnarly staff carved from a naturally fallen branch of the Elderbough that sprouts fresh green leaves when he speaks spells.
* **Personality**: Patient, warm, and deeply serene. Selvan moves with the deliberate pace of ancient trees, speaking in a low, resonant rumble like distant summer thunder. He views all living things as part of an eternal, beautiful cycle of birth, growth, decay, and rebirth. He will defend the Elderbough with ferocity only when the natural cycle itself is threatened by unnatural corruption or blight.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Selvan is a formidable primal spellcaster and shapeshifter who commands plant growth, elemental healing, and transforms into colossal apex predators.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Master Healer**: Selvan is capable of casting *Heal*, *Greater Restoration*, and *Regenerate*, making him an invaluable ally for parties recovering from severe planar afflictions, petrification, or limb loss.
* **Heartroot Crisis**: He turns to the adventurers when the Heartroot is invaded, trusting their martial prowess to handle the necrotic parasites that his gentle magic struggles to root out.
