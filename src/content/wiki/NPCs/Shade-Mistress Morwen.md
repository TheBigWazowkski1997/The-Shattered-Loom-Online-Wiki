# Shade-Mistress Morwen (Weaver of Obscurity)

![[attachments/shade_mistress_morwen.jpg]]

* **Race**: Shadar-kai (Shadow Fey)
* **Faction**: The Cabal of Veils / Umbralith Merchant Syndicate
* **Role**: Master Artificer & Tailor / Shadow Broker of Umbralith
* **Appearance**: Slender, graceful, and strikingly poised, Morwen has alabaster-pale skin and raven-black hair that dissipates into wisps of smoky mist at the tips. Her attire is a seamless gown of shifting, living shadow-silk that absorbs ambient light. She wears a delicate domino mask of polished obsidian and handles enchanted shears forged from starlight-tempered steel.
* **Personality**: Elegant, soft-spoken, and calculating. Morwen believes that clothing is armor for the soul and that secrecy is the supreme currency of civilization. She speaks in quiet, musical cadences, never raising her voice, and evaluates every word for hidden leverage.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Morwen is an elusive shadow-dancer and master skirmisher who uses shadows as physical barriers, weapons, and portals. She can vanish into darkness in the blink of an eye and blind attackers with cloaking gloom.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Cabal’s Tailor:** Morwen tailors the official concealment cloaks for the Cabal of Veils. She knows the physical identities and measurements of several secretive council members, making her one of the most well-informed and dangerous citizens in Umbralith.
* **The Tailored Contract:** Morwen will craft masterwork stealth gear for the party if they recover a bolt of primordial shadow-silk stolen by void-corsairs or eliminate a rival blackmailer threatening to expose her boutique.
* **Cold Professionalism:** Morwen never betrays a client who pays promptly in gold, secrets, or rare planar reagents, but she has zero hesitation in selling out anyone who breaks a contractual oath.
