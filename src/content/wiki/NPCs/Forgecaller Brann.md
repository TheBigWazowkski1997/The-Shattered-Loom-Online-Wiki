# Forgecaller Brann (Master of the Emberforges)

![[attachments/forgecaller_brann.jpg]]

* **Race**: Mountain Dwarf (Earth & Fire Elementalist)
* **Faction**: The Stonekeepers & Emberforges Guild of Stonehollow
* **Role**: High Forgecaller / Master of the Subterranean Nexus
* **Appearance**: Broad, muscular, and barrel-chested, Brann has skin like fire-hardened terracotta and a thick, braided auburn beard threaded with burning embers and cooled basalt beads. His eyes burn like orange coal fires. He wears an apron of dragon-leather over an adamantine-studded cuirass, and his calloused hands bear runic brands of the four elemental wells. In battle or at the anvil, he wields *Earth-Shatterer*, a massive rectangular warhammer forged from lodestone and cooled magma.
* **Personality**: Gruff, boisterous, pragmatic, and passionately dedicated to the art of elemental equilibrium. Brann respects honesty, hard physical labor, and fine metallurgical craftsmanship. He has zero patience for bureaucrats, cowardly politicians, or anyone who disrespects the raw, dangerous power of the subterranean earth.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Brann combines traditional dwarven martial grit with primal elemental magic. He channels magma through his strikes, summons earthquakes that stagger entire groups of enemies, and commands stone walls to erupt around his foes.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Balance of the Four:** Brann maintains the Emberforges not just to make weapons, but to vent excess pressure from the Elemental Wellspring. If the forges stop burning, the entire mountain could detonate in a cataclysmic volcanic eruption.
* **The Master's Challenge:** Brann refuses to forge for sellswords or cowards. To earn a custom weapon, adventurers must journey into the cooling magma flumes and extract raw meteoric ore with their bare hands or endure his crucible trials.
* **Respect for the Canopy:** Unlike dwarven strip-miners on other planes, Brann fiercely respects the forests of Viridspire, understanding that the roots of the Elderbough anchor the subterranean mountain caverns of Stonehollow.
