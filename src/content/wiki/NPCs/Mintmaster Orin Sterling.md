# Mintmaster Orin Sterling (The Silver Warden)

![[attachments/orin_sterling.jpg]]

* **Race**: Dwarf (Duergar-blooded Mountain Dwarf)
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Avarice/Plane of Avarice The Gilded Eternity#3. Argentum Abyss: The City Beneath the Hoard|The Argentum Syndicate]] / The Silver Spire Mint
* **Role**: Chief Mintmaster of Argentum Abyss, Ghost-Bane Armorsmith
* **Appearance**: An austere, muscular dwarf with a braided steel-grey beard dipped in silver leaf. His heavy leather smithing apron is branded with the scales of the Syndicate, and his skin is pale from centuries living beneath the mountain of gold. His hands are calloused and stained with acid testing solutions and liquid silver nitrate.
* **Personality**: Methodical, incorruptible, and grimly cautious. In a city driven mad by gold, Orin values pure silver for its sacred warding properties against the undead. He views wealth as an existential weight rather than a blessing, constantly warning younger miners of the "Crushing Ceiling" that threatens to bury them all.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Orin is a formidable combatant who excels in subterranean defense and anti-spectral warfare, wielding pure alchemical silver against undead and planar thieves.

* **Dwarven Resilience**: Orin has advantage on saving throws against poison, and resistance against poison damage.
* **Ghost-Bane Master**: Orin's weapon attacks are considered magical and silvered, and ignore an incorporeal creature's damage resistances.
* **Anchor of the Vault**: Orin has advantage on ability checks and saving throws against being knocked prone, shoved, or moved against his will.
* **Multiattack**: Orin makes three melee attacks with *Silver-Sunder* or two ranged attacks with his *Alchemical Silver Sprayer*.
* **Silver-Sunder (Warhammer)**: one target. If the target is undead or an incorporeal creature, it takes an extra significant damage.
* **Alchemical Silver Sprayer**: *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, range 30 ft. line, 5 ft. wide. Each creature in the line must make a saving throw, taking 18 (4d8) acid and radiant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. Incorporeal creatures make this save with disadvantage.
* **Quench Blast (Recharge 5–6)**: Orin vents pressurized superheated silver steam in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw or take significant damage and be blinded until the end of its next turn.
* **Vault Clamp**: Orin slams his magnetic boots into the stone, granting himself advantage on all Strength saving throws and +2 to AC until the start of his next turn.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Stamped Token:** Orin can mint custom Syndicate bullion tokens for the party, granting them diplomatic immunity in the upper sectors of Argentum Abyss.
* **Silvering Service:** He will coat the party's weapons in pure alchemical silver at half price if they bring him uncorrupted silver ore from the lower catacombs.
* **The Syndicate Rebellion:** If the party sides with the free miners against Auditor Malakar, Orin secretly supplies them with ghost-bane weapons, confessing that Malakar's greed has jeopardized the structural safety of the entire city.
