# Xelthar, Keeper of the Ziggurat (Aberrant Guardian)

![[attachments/xelthar_ziggurat.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Aberration (Far Realm-touched humanoid, original form lost)
* **Faction**: The Dreambound / The Great Dreamer (direct servant)
* **Role**: Guardian of the Ziggurat of Ry'leh, Tester of Intruder Worthiness
* **Appearance**: A humanoid figure with a perfectly smooth, featureless head that shifts like a disturbed oil slick. Its body is covered in chitinous armor plates the color of deep ocean darkness, interlocking over a form that is *almost* human in proportion. Dozens of small, unblinking eyes of varying sizes are distributed across its torso, each moving independently. It moves in complete silence.
* **Personality**: Loyal exclusively to the Great Dreamer. It does not experience loyalty as devotion — it experiences it as physics. It speaks cryptically when it speaks at all, and "tests" intruders not out of cruelty but out of a genuine drive to determine whether they are worthy to proceed deeper into the Ziggurat.

---
## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Xelthar is a powerful aberrant guardian based on a Mind Flayer chassis, enhanced by legendary resistances and tied to the Ziggurat's lair actions. It combines mind blasts with chitinous strikes, using its many eyes to track multiple targets simultaneously.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Test:** When the party enters the Ziggurat, Xelthar does not attack immediately. It observes. It may manifest before them and issue a cryptic task or riddle — solve it, and it steps aside. Fail, or attack without engaging, and it defends the Dreamer with lethal force.
* **Communicating the Alien:** Describe Xelthar's speech as fragmented, reversed, or as concepts that arrive before the words do. It does not understand human social cues. It may tilt its head and ask why players are afraid of it — not mockingly, but with genuine incomprehension.
* **Working with the Party:** If the players prove themselves worthy AND have a goal that aligns with protecting the Dreamer's current state (not waking it), Xelthar may become a reluctant guide within the Ziggurat.
