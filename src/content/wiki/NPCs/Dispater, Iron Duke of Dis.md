# Archduke Dispater, Iron Duke of Dis

![[dispater.jpg]]

* **Race**: Archdevil (The Surveyor of Deviance & Lord of the Second)
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Infernal Hierarchy of Baator|The Infernal Hierarchy of Baator]] (Lord of the Iron Spindle)
* **Role**: Ruler of Dis / Grand Archivist of Cosmic Deviance / Master of the Hall of Geometries
* **Appearance**: A tall, aristocratic, and intensely paranoid devil statesman with pale grey ash skin, cold analytical ice-blue eyes, and sleek silver-black hair framed by sharp curving iron horns. Clad in heavy polished rusted iron and dark steel plate armor, draped in a high-collared mantle of scorched parchment covered in endless mathematical geometric diagrams and legal statutes. In his hands he holds an iron measuring rod and a heavy iron key rod.
* **Personality**: Deeply paranoid, cold, calculating, and obsessed with the straight line. Dispater views the multiverse as an endless series of geometry errors and chaos-leaks. He trusts no one, never leaves the Iron Tower, and communicates exclusively through iron homunculi and enchanted mirrors. He respects only absolute mathematical precision and immutable legal statutes.

---

## Combat Profile

Dispater fights with defensive mastery and mathematical calculation. He utilizes his iron rods to deflect spells, alter physical space, and summon legions of barbed and chain devils to overwhelm attackers.

### **Dispater, Iron Duke of Dis**
*Medium Fiend (Archdevil), Lawful Evil*
* **Armor Class**: 22 (+3 Iron Plate & Deflection Ward)
* **Hit Points**: 360 (28d8 + 234)
* **Speed**: 40 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 24 (+7) | 20 (+5) | 26 (+8) | 28 (+9) | 26 (+8) | 26 (+8) |

* **Saving Throws**: Str +15, Con +16, Int +17, Wis +16
* **Skills**: Arcana +25, Deception +16, History +25, Insight +24, Perception +24
* **Damage Resistances**: Cold; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
* **Damage Immunities**: Fire, Poison
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
* **Senses**: Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 34
* **Languages**: All languages, Telepathy 500 ft.
* **Challenge**: 25 (75,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +8

#### **Traits**
* **Iron Paranoia**: Dispater cannot be surprised, cannot be targeted by divination magic, and has advantage on all saving throws against spells.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Dispater fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
* **Geometric Absolute**: Within 60 feet of Dispater, all non-magical and magical difficult terrain is suppressed; creatures cannot benefit from cover.
* **Innate Spellcasting**: Spellcasting ability is Intelligence (Spell Save DC 25, +17 to hit with spell attacks). Prepared spells:
  * At will: *Detect Thoughts, Dispel Magic, Hold Person, Wall of Iron*
  * 3/day each: *Forcecage, Maze, Globe of Invulnerability*
  * 1/day each: *Imprisonment, Prismatic Wall*

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Dispater makes three attacks with his Iron Rod of Deviance or casts one spell and makes one attack.
* **Iron Rod of Deviance**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 16 (2d8 + 7) bludgeoning damage plus 22 (4d10) force damage. Target must succeed on a DC 25 Constitution save or have its speed reduced to 0 until the end of its next turn.
* **Iron Shackle Pulse (Recharge 5–6)**: Chains of red-hot iron erupt from the ground in a 40-foot radius. Each creature must make a DC 25 Strength saving throw, taking 45 (10d8) fire damage and being restrained on a failed save.

#### **Legendary Actions**
Dispater can take 3 legendary actions:
* **Measure Distance**: Dispater teleports up to 40 feet in a straight line.
* **Iron Strike**: Dispater makes one attack with his Iron Rod.
* **Realign Architecture (Costs 2 Actions)**: Dispater shifts the walls within 60 feet, creating a *Wall of Iron* or opening a 20-foot chasm beneath up to three enemies.

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Data-Vault Sovereign**: Dispater holds the legal records of every planar infraction. Adventurers seeking to invalidate a devil's contract or find a loophole in infernal law must negotiate with Dispater.
* **Paranoid Bargains**: He will only grant audiences if supplicants leave all weapons, magic items, and spell foci outside the Iron Tower.
