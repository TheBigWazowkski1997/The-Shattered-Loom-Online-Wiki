# Archduke Dispater, Iron Duke of Dis

![[dispater.jpg]]

* **Race**: Archdevil (The Surveyor of Deviance & Lord of the Second)
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Infernal Hierarchy of Baator|The Infernal Hierarchy of Baator]] (Lord of the Iron Spindle)
* **Role**: Ruler of Dis / Grand Archivist of Cosmic Deviance / Master of the Hall of Geometries
* **Appearance**: A tall, aristocratic, and intensely paranoid devil statesman with pale grey ash skin, cold analytical ice-blue eyes, and sleek silver-black hair framed by sharp curving iron horns. Clad in heavy polished rusted iron and dark steel plate armor, draped in a high-collared mantle of scorched parchment covered in endless mathematical geometric diagrams and legal statutes. In his hands he holds an iron measuring rod and a heavy iron key rod.
* **Personality**: Deeply paranoid, cold, calculating, and obsessed with the straight line. Dispater views the multiverse as an endless series of geometry errors and chaos-leaks. He trusts no one, never leaves the Iron Tower, and communicates exclusively through iron homunculi and enchanted mirrors. He respects only absolute mathematical precision and immutable legal statutes.

---

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Dispater fights with defensive mastery and mathematical calculation. He utilizes his iron rods to deflect spells, alter physical space, and summon legions of barbed and chain devils to overwhelm attackers.

---

## Roleplay & Lore Notes

* **The Data-Vault Sovereign**: Dispater holds the legal records of every planar infraction. Adventurers seeking to invalidate a devil's contract or find a loophole in infernal law must negotiate with Dispater.
* **Paranoid Bargains**: He will only grant audiences if supplicants leave all weapons, magic items, and spell foci outside the Iron Tower.
