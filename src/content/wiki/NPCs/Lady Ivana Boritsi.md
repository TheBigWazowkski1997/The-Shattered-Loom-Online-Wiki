# Lady Ivana Boritsi (The Black Widow of Borca)

![[attachments/ivana_boritsi.jpg]]

* **Race**: Human (Poison-Blooded Noble)
* **Faction**: The Noble House of Boritsi / Court of Lechberg
* **Role**: Co-Darklord of Borca / Master of the Poison Gardens of Misericordia
* **Appearance**: A breathtakingly beautiful woman in her early thirties with raven-black curls swept with jeweled silver combs, emerald eyes, and skin as pale as crushed alabaster. She wears dresses of deep emerald and black velvet with lace chokers that hide the delicate purple venom-veins pulsing at her throat. A faint, intoxicating perfume of night-blooming belladonna and crushed almonds surrounds her.
* **Personality**: Elegant, cold, profoundly vindictive, and consumed by paranoid obsession. Her curse is that her very touch and fluids are deadly poison to anyone she desires to love, rendering true intimacy utterly impossible. She treats politics, romance, and warfare as games of gradual botanical assassination.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Ivana avoids physical combat whenever possible, relying on poisoned darts, enchanted perfume atomizers, and charmed noble bodyguards. Her body is saturated with centuries of rare planar poisons; weapons coated in her blood deal catastrophic toxic damage, and anyone who makes contact with her skin risks immediate paralysis.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Bitter Feud with Ivan Dilisnya:** Borca is co-ruled with her hateful cousin Ivan Dilisnya. The two despise each other with burning passion, spending their days sending poisoned candies, mechanical assassin-toys, and cursed floral arrangements back and forth while maintaining polite aristocratic smiles.
* **Interactions with the Party:** Ivana welcomes foreign adventurers with magnificent banquets where every course is subtly poisoned with non-lethal paralytics, testing their resilience and negotiating alliances against her cousin.
* **Connection to the Shadowfell Black Market:** Ivana is the primary planar exporter of rare toxins, including *Heartstop Tincture*, *Widow's Kiss*, and distilled essence of the *Black Beast of Bedlam*.
