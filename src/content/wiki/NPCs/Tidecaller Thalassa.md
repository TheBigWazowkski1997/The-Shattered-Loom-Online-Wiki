# Tidecaller Thalassa (Voice of the Prismreef)

![[attachments/tidecaller_thalassa.jpg]]

* **Race**: Water Genasi (Archdruid of the Tides)
* **Faction**: The Tidecallers of Tidehearth / Protectors of the Coral Crown
* **Role**: High Tidecaller / Guardian of the Prismreef
* **Appearance**: An ethereal water genasi whose skin shimmers with iridescent turquoise and mother-of-pearl tones. Her translucent aquamarine hair flows weightlessly around her head like sea anemone tendrils, and her eyes resemble deep ocean glass. She is draped in robes woven from living kelp silk and delicate fire-coral filigree. She wields the *Spire-Conch*, a spiraling nautilus staff crowned with a glowing ocean pearl.
* **Personality**: Fluid, patient, yet fierce when the equilibrium of the tides is disturbed. Thalassa views life as an eternal ebb and flow, where joy and sorrow, growth and decay are merely phases of the same ocean swell. She speaks in soft, rhythmic whispers reminiscent of gentle surf, but her wrath can summon crushing tidal waves and oceanic fury.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Thalassa is a potent master of water and marine life. She commands ocean currents, summons whirlpools, shapes living coral into lethal spikes, and can transform into a living water elemental.

* **Amphibious**: Thalassa can breathe air and water effortlessly.
* **Child of the Tide**: While submerged in water, Thalassa cannot be knocked prone, grappled, or restrained, and her spell attack rolls and DCs have a +1 bonus.
* **Call to the Deep (1/Day)**: As a bonus action, Thalassa summons a loyal Water Elemental (*MM p. 125*) to fight alongside her for 1 hour or until dismissed.
* **Multiattack**: Thalassa makes two attacks with her *Spire-Conch* or casts one spell and makes one staff attack.
* **Spire-Conch**: Notable combat maneuver and signature technique.
* **Tidal Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: Thalassa unleashes a crushing wave of pressurized seawater in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes significant damage, is pushed 20 feet away, and is knocked prone. On a success, it takes half damage and is neither pushed nor knocked prone.
* **Spellcasting**: Thalassa is a 12th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save saving throw, +9 to hit with spell attacks). She has the following druid spells prepared:.
* **Undertow**: When a creature misses Thalassa with a melee attack, she can force the attacker to make a saving throw or be dragged 10 feet into the nearest body of water and knocked prone.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Tide’s Will:** Thalassa reads the health of the entire plane by observing the subtle coloration changes in the Coral Crown. She knows that damage to the forests of Viridspire eventually washes into the oceans as toxic sediment.
* **Undersea Guide:** If the party must explore the lightless abyssal trenches or seek sunken artifacts from the Shattering, Thalassa can bestow enduring blessings of water breathing and pressure immunity—if they cleanse a sahuagin blood-temple.
* **Philosophical Calm:** Thalassa never panics. Even when monstrous leviathans approach the reef, she listens to the ocean's heartbeat, believing that every storm eventually runs out of rain.
