# King Oberon (The Green Lord & Sovereign of Beasts)

![[king_oberon.jpg]]

* **Race**: Archfey (Primordial Fey Demigod of the Hunt, Beasts, and Ancient Forests)
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Seelie Court|The Seelie Court]] (Consort King & Master of the Wild Sylvan Borders)
* **Role**: Lord of the Sylvan Wardens / Guardian of the Primeval Groves / Master of Beasts
* **Appearance**: A towering, powerfully muscled figure of raw primeval majesty. Oberon possesses rugged, aristocratic eladrin features framed by thick braided dark-moss hair and a short, bristling beard. From his brow sprout magnificent stag antlers spanning seven feet, hung with autumn leaves and ancient druidic rune-tokens. He wears masterwork plate armor carved from petrified ironwood and living green dragonhide, draped in a heavy mantle of forest ivy and wolf pelts. In his hand he holds *Gwyllion*, a massive longsword of meteoric silver whose blade hums with the heartbeat of the forest.
* **Personality**: Proud, fierce, honorable, yet quick-tempered and fiercely territorial. Unlike the capricious, theatrical whimsy of Titania, Oberon respects martial skill, direct speech, physical courage, and unyielding loyalty. He rules the beasts and treants of Faerie with a stern but paternal hand. He despises deceit, cowardice, and industrial exploitation of wild places.

---

## Combat Profile

King Oberon is a devastating front-line warrior and divine commander. He enters battle mounted on his gargantuan emerald-scaled sylvan wyrm, flanked by legendary displacer beasts, dire stags, and ancient treants. In single combat, his blade cleaves through magical wards and armor with relentless martial precision.

### **King Oberon, The Green Lord**
*Medium Fey (Archfey), Chaotic Good*
* **Armor Class**: 21 (Petrified Ironwood Plate & Sylvan Ward)
* **Hit Points**: 290 (24d8 + 168)
* **Speed**: 40 ft., climb 30 ft.

#### **Traits**
* **Master of Beasts**: All beasts and monstrosities of good or neutral alignment within 120 feet of Oberon consider him their rightful king and will not attack him unless magically compelled (DC 23 to resist).
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Oberon fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
* **Sylvan Cleave**: Once per turn, when Oberon hits a creature with a melee attack, he can deal the same damage to an adjacent enemy within 5 feet.
* **Innate Spellcasting**: Oberon's spellcasting ability is Wisdom (Spell Save DC 23, +15 to hit with spell attacks). Prepared spells:
  * At will: *Animal Friendship, Commune with Nature, Hunter's Mark, Misty Step, Speak with Plants*
  * 3/day each: *Conjure Animals (at 7th level), Wrath of Nature, Steel Wind Strike, Earthquake*
  * 1/day each: *Tsunami, Foresight, Animal Shapes*

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Oberon makes three attacks with his greatsword *Gwyllion* or two attacks with his Sylvan Longbow.
* **Gwyllion (Meteoric Greatsword)**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +16 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 22 (4d6 + 8) slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) force damage. If the target is concentrating on a spell, the DC to maintain concentration is equal to the damage dealt.
* **Sylvan Longbow**: *Ranged Weapon Attack*: +13 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit*: 18 (2d8 + 5) piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) radiant damage. The target's speed is halved until the start of Oberon's next turn.
* **Horn of the Wild Lord (Recharge 5–6)**: Oberon sounds his ancient horn. Up to 4 Sylvan Beast Guardians (stat block: Saber-Toothed Tigers with maximum HP) materialize in unoccupied spaces within 60 feet, acting on Oberon's initiative.

#### **Legendary Actions**
Oberon can take 3 legendary actions:
* **Primal Stride**: Oberon moves up to his speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
* **Strike of the Forest**: Oberon makes one attack with *Gwyllion*.
* **Roar of the Apex (Costs 2 Actions)**: Oberon roars with the fury of a thousand predators. Each enemy within 30 feet must succeed on a DC 23 Constitution save or take 28 (8d6) thunder damage and be stunned until the end of its next turn.

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Protector of the Tapestry**: Oberon views the Shattered Loom through the lens of primal ecology. He sees the cosmic threads as living roots that sustain the multiverse. Any entity seeking to tear or burn the threads is a rabid beast that must be put down with clean iron and steel.
* **Bargains of Strength**: Oberon has zero patience for riddles, deceit, or flattery. To win his favor or borrow his troops, adventurers must prove their worth through a trial of arms, a hunting expedition against a rampaging chimera, or by defending a sacred grove from defilers.
* **Alliance with Cernunnos**: While Cernunnos wanders the wild without courts, Oberon considers the Horned Hunter a spiritual brother and frequently rides alongside the Wild Hunt when great monsters threaten the plane.
