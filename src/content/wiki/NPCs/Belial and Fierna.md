# Belial and Fierna, The Diarchy of Phlegethos

* **Race**: Archdevils (The Smelters of Mandates & Lords of the Fourth)
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Infernal Hierarchy of Baator|The Infernal Hierarchy of Baator]] (Lords of the Caldera Forges)
* **Role**: Rulers of Phlegethos / Overseers of the Obsidian Anvils / Smelters of Lawless Souls
* **Appearance**: A striking, contrasting diarchy. Belial appears as a tall, aristocratic devil with ashen-grey skin, refined obsidian horns, and eyes like smoldering embers, clad in dark tailored plate. Lady Fierna is breathtakingly seductive, with glowing golden-crimson skin, wild hair of leaping orange flame, and robes of living crimson smoke. Together they represent the forge: Fierna is the heat of passion, Belial is the rigid hammer and mold.
* **Personality**: Belial is patient, calculating, and cynical, content to work behind the scenes while directing the industrial machinery of the Hells. Fierna is theatrical, magnetic, and fiercely passionate, convinced that total obedience through love and security is the ultimate cosmic truth.

---

## Combat Profile

Belial and Fierna fight as a synchronized team. Fierna enchants and blinds foes with radiant charm, while Belial locks them down with obsidian chains and crushing hammer-strikes.

### **Belial, The Old Sculptor**
*Medium Fiend (Archdevil), Lawful Evil*
* **Armor Class**: 20 (Obsidian Plate) | **Hit Points**: 310 (24d8 + 192) | **Challenge**: 23

### **Fierna, The Seductive Flame**
*Medium Fiend (Archdevil), Lawful Evil*
* **Armor Class**: 20 (Flame Ward) | **Hit Points**: 280 (22d8 + 176) | **Challenge**: 22

---

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Smelter’s Deal**: Mortals seeking unbreakable weapons or legal immunity can bargain at Abriymoch. Fierna appeals to their vanity, while Belial ensures the contract binds them forever.
