# Duchess Saidra d'Honaire (The Gilded Pretender)

![[attachments/saidra_dhonaire.jpg]]

* **Race**: Human (Dread Apparition / Mirror-Weaver)
* **Faction**: The Council of Brilliance / The Masquerade of Port-a-Lucine
* **Role**: Darklord of Dementlieu / Host of the Grand Masquerade
* **Appearance**: An exquisite noblewoman adorned in shimmering powdered satin gowns embroidered with spun glass, pearl chokers, and an ornate Venetian porcelain mask decorated with gilded feathers. Beneath the porcelain mask and opulent makeup, however, lies the withered, corpse-pale visage of an unacknowledged scullery maid whose flesh cracks like brittle porcelain whenever her lies are challenged.
* **Personality**: Insecure, obsessively vain, manipulative, and pathologically terrified of social rejection. Saidra is trapped in an eternal grand ball where she is revered as high royalty, yet lives in constant dread that her guests will see through her illusions and expose her lowly origins.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Saidra fights through psychic glamour, animated glass, and devastating illusions. She manipulates the grand mirrors of the Grand Masquerade, stepping through reflective surfaces to evade harm, turning her enemies' own delusions and social insecurities into lethal psychic trauma, and transforming those who commit faux pas into lifeless mannequins.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Masquerade's Illusion:** In Port-a-Lucine, all citizens and visitors are forced to attend Saidra’s weekly masquerade ball at the Palais de la Joie. Those who fail to dress properly, lack an invitation, or display bad manners are publicly ostracized and transformed into decorative garden statues.
* **Interactions with the Loom:** Saidra possesses a handheld folding fan woven from gossamer glass threads—a minor resonance filament of the Shattered Loom. The fan allows her to rewrite how people perceive reality within her ballroom.
* **Encountering the Party:** Saidra welcomes high-class planar travelers with hollow enthusiasm, testing their etiquette while probing their minds for weaknesses she can exploit.
