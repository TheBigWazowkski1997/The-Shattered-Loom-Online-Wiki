# Prince Varyl, the Night Stalker

![[prince_varyl.jpg]]

* **Race**: Eladrin (Winter Aspect) / Shadow Archfey
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Unseelie Court|The Unseelie Court]] (High Inquisitor & Master of the Gloaming Guard)
* **Role**: Chief Enforcer of the Queen of Air and Darkness / Crown Stalker of the Duskwood
* **Appearance**: Lithe, strikingly handsome, and dangerously predatory. Prince Varyl possesses pale alabaster skin, aristocratic angular cheekbones, and piercing glowing violet eyes that see through magical deceptions. His long obsidian-black hair is adorned with woven bramble-thorns and iridescent raven feathers. He wears tailored midnight-blue velvet and blackened chainmail reinforced with raven-wing pauldrons. In his hands he wields twin curved stygian daggers—*Gloom-Fang* and *Frost-Bite*—wreathed in shifting violet mist.
* **Personality**: Cunning, sadistic, ruthlessly efficient, yet possessed of an aristocratic, dark elegance. Varyl views the hunt as high art. He does not simply slaughter his quarry; he stalks them, psychologically dismantles their hope, and forces them to make impossible moral choices before delivering the final strike. He is fanatically loyal to the Queen of Air and Darkness, viewing her will as absolute law.

---

## Combat Profile

Prince Varyl is a master assassin and skirmisher. He moves through shadows with instantaneous speed, poisoning weapons with stygian venom and striking from blind spots before slipping back into the dark.

### **Prince Varyl, the Night Stalker**
*Medium Fey (Eladrin), Neutral Evil*
* **Armor Class**: 20 (+2 Shadow Leather & Dex)
* **Hit Points**: 210 (28d8 + 84)
* **Speed**: 45 ft., climb 30 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 16 (+3) | 26 (+8) | 16 (+3) | 18 (+4) | 20 (+5) | 22 (+6) |

* **Saving Throws**: Dex +14, Con +9, Wis +11, Cha +12
* **Skills**: Acrobatics +14, Deception +12, Insight +11, Intimidation +12, Perception +17, Stealth +20, Survival +17
* **Damage Resistances**: Cold, Necrotic; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned
* **Senses**: Darkvision 120 ft., Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 27
* **Languages**: Sylvan, Elvish, Common, Undercommon
* **Challenge**: 18 (20,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +6

#### **Traits**
* **Assassinate**: During his first turn, Varyl has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't taken a turn. Any hit he scores against a surprised creature is a critical hit.
* **Shadow Stalker**: While in dim light or darkness, Varyl can take the Hide action as a bonus action and has advantage on all Dexterity (Stealth) checks.
* **Sneak Attack (1/Turn)**: Varyl deals an extra 28 (8d6) damage when he hits a target with a weapon attack and has advantage on the attack roll.
* **Fey Step (Recharge 4–6)**: As a bonus action, Varyl can teleport up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see. When he arrives, up to two creatures within 10 feet must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom saving throw or be frightened until the end of their next turn.

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Varyl makes three melee attacks with his stygian daggers or three ranged attacks with his Shadow-Bramble Bow.
* **Gloom-Fang (Stygian Dagger)**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 10 (1d4 + 8) piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) necrotic damage. Target must succeed on a DC 20 Constitution save or be blinded until the end of its next turn.
* **Frost-Bite (Stygian Dagger)**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 10 (1d4 + 8) piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) cold damage. Target's speed is reduced by 15 feet until the end of its next turn.
* **Shadow-Bramble Bow**: *Ranged Weapon Attack*: +14 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit*: 12 (1d8 + 8) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) cold damage.

#### **Legendary Actions**
Varyl can take 3 legendary actions:
* **Vanish into Fog**: Varyl teleports up to 30 feet into dim light or darkness and makes a Dexterity (Stealth) check.
* **Dagger Strike**: Varyl makes one attack with *Gloom-Fang*.
* **Predator's Lunge (Costs 2 Actions)**: Varyl moves up to his speed and makes two dagger attacks against a frightened target.

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Queen's Bloodhound**: When a high-value prisoner escapes the Gloaming Cradle or a mortal warlock violates an Unseelie pact, the Queen sends Varyl. He will follow his quarry across planar boundaries, leaving a trail of frozen corpses in his wake.
* **Rivalry with Cernunnos**: Varyl respects Cernunnos's martial prowess but finds the Wild Hunt's code of primal honor laughably primitive. He prefers to hunt with poisons, psychological traps, and sudden betrayals.
