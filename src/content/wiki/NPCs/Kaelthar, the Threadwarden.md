# Kaelthar, the Threadwarden (Leader of the Order of Severance)

![[attachments/kaelthar_threadwarden.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Shadar-kai
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Pale Lodge/The Pale Lodge|The Pale Lodge]]
* **Role**: Second of the Spindle Lords — Leader of the Order of Severance, Head of Military Operations
* **Appearance**: A pale, scarred warrior in black plate armor etched with silver runes that shift when he moves. His shadow often moves independently — not a trick of the light, but a genuine second presence that mimics the act of cutting something with invisible shears. He carries a two-handed silver-etched blade at his back and moves with the efficiency of someone who has never taken an unnecessary step.
* **Personality**: Stern, pragmatic, and brutally honest. He treats the players like raw recruits who have not yet earned the right to be informed. He pushes them through "tests" — genuinely dangerous situations — with the conviction that survival is the only proof of worthiness.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Kaelthar is a shadow-warlock/fighter hybrid who combines devastating melee strikes with necrotic shadow magic. He uses his independent shadow as a flanking partner and can teleport through darkness as a reaction. He fights with military precision — every move has a tactical purpose.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Lifeboat Plane:** Kaelthar has secretly hollowed out a hidden pocket dimension inside the Nexus Spire — a "Lifeboat Plane" that can house several hundred chosen survivors. He is using the players' missions to harvest the loom energy needed to fuel this exodus engine. He has identified them as the best possible "fuel source" without telling them.
* **The Genuine Fear:** Unlike Aelira's cold calculation, Kaelthar is genuinely terrified. He has run the numbers and does not believe the Loom can be fixed. He sees the "Lifeboat" as the only honest solution. If confronted with evidence that restoration is possible, he becomes destabilized — his entire worldview cracks.
* **Relationship with Players:** He respects capability and despises sentimentality. If a player impresses him in combat or tactical planning, he will say: *"You'll survive long enough to be useful. That's more than most."*
