## **Frontier Figures, Outcasts & Guides**

* **Vrek the Rust-Picker** (Tiefling Scavenger, Fort Fray): Veteran salvager who sells portal-conduit charts and void-grappling hooks to expeditions entering Pazunia.
* **Madame Xelise** (Cambion Couturière, Zelatar): Operates the Silken Serpent Atelier in Azzagrat, weaving gowns from mirrored thread for interplanar nobles.
* **Matron Vriss** (Drider Artificer, Malshym): High priestess who crafts enchanted spider-silk cloaks and chitin shields from fallen Bebiliths.
* **Hagra of the Maw** (Sea Hag Coven Mother, Lemoriax): Ancient crone brewing sanity-restoring tinctures in exchange for severed tongues.
* **Master Karr** (Wight Weaponsmith, Naratyr): Forges bone scythes and grave-steel armor for Orcus's skeletal officers.
* **Oozemaster Sliss** (Slime-Touched Cultist, Glistening Bastion): Extracts dissolving resins and sentient acid vials from Juiblex's morass.
* **Flind-Warlord Garsh** (Veteran of the Blood War, Seeping Woods): Gnoll champion who trades bone flails and hyena-hide harnesses for iron weapons.
* **Lady Seraphina Noir** (Succubus Courtesan, Shendilavri): Master spy in Malcanthet's court, specializing in extracting secrets from charmed planar kings.
* **Master Korus** (Blind Tiefling Geometer, Endless Maze): Blind cartographer who maps the non-Euclidean loop-knots of Lyktion using brass sextants.
