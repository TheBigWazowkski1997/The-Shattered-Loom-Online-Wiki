# Chakuna (The Blood-Spur Huntress)

![[attachments/chakuna.jpg]]

* **Race**: Weretiger (Shapechanger Humanoid)
* **Faction**: The Pack of the Blood Moon / Valachan Hunter Guild
* **Role**: Darklord of Valachan / Sovereign of the Twilight Jungle
* **Appearance**: A tall, lithe, athletic warrior with sun-bronzed skin decorated with ritual crimson scarification, intense amber feline eyes that glow in pitch darkness, and braided black hair adorned with bone fetishes and predator fangs. In hybrid form, she transforms into a terrifying, nine-foot-tall weretiger with golden-black striped fur, massive obsidian claws, and sabered fangs dripping with jungle venom.
* **Personality**: Savage, cunning, fiercely territorial, and relentless. Chakuna deposed the cruel vampire lord Baron von Kharkov through sheer feral cunning, only to be cursed by the Dark Powers to become the eternal hunter—and the eternal prey—of Valachan’s deadly Yester-Beasts and trapped mortals.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Chakuna is a master ambush predator who uses the jungle canopy, rain squalls, and deep shadows to stalk her prey. She uses traps, poisoned blowguns, and pouncing strikes that snap spines in seconds, never engaging head-on if an ambush or guerrilla hit-and-run tactic is available.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Trial of the Hunt:** Mortals swept into Valachan are declared "The Quarry." Chakuna grants them a single head-start before releasing her packs. Survivors who reach the ancient ziggurat of Ocotlan are granted temporary freedom—until the next full moon rises.
* **The Loom's Predator:** Chakuna instinctively hunts any planar travelers carrying Loom shards or fragments, viewing the humming resonance as a beacon of cosmic power to be consumed to bolster her strength.
* **Attitude Toward Adventurers:** She respects pure combat capability and martial prowess. A fighter or barbarian who duels her to a standstill can earn her begrudging respect and safe passage out of the jungle.
