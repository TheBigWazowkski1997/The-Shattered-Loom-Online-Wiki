# General Vladeska Drakov (The Iron Falcon)

![[vladeska_drakov.jpg]]

* **Race**: Human (Cursed Warlord)
* **Faction**: The Iron Talons of Falkovnia / Supreme Military Directorate
* **Role**: Darklord of Falkovnia / Supreme Commander of Lekar
* **Appearance**: A formidable, muscular woman in her late forties with severe, weather-beaten features, short steel-grey hair, and cold grey eyes hardened by unceasing warfare. Across her face runs a jagged scar from a ghoul's talons. She is perpetually encased in full plate armor of blackened steel, adorned with spiked pauldrons and the embossed Falcon crest of Falkovnia. At her waist hangs *Talon's Wrath*, an enormous serrated greatsword that ignites with searing alchemical flame.
* **Personality**: Unyielding, authoritarian, pragmatic to the point of atrocity, and consumed by relentless paranoia. Drakov believes in nothing except force, discipline, and fortified perimeters. Her curse is that no matter how many fortifications she constructs, how many conscripts she brands, or how many bodies she impales, the zombie hordes rising from the Dead Fields will inevitably breach the perimeter, forcing her into an eternal cycle of desperate last stands.

---

## Combat Profile

Drakov is an unstoppable frontline juggernaut and military commander. She fights in the thick of melee, bellowing tactical commands that grant temporary hit points and attack bonuses to her allied legionnaires, while cutting down undead and intruders with her flaming greatsword.

### **General Vladeska Drakov**
*Medium Humanoid (Human, Fighter), Lawful Evil*
* **Armor Class**: 20 (Masterwork Falcon Plate & Armored Spikes)
* **Hit Points**: 178 (21d8 + 84)
* **Speed**: 30 ft.

* **Saving Throws**: Str +10, Con +9, Wis +7
* **Skills**: Athletics +10, History +8, Insight +7, Intimidation +9, Perception +7, Survival +7
* **Damage Resistances**: Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
* **Condition Immunities**: Frightened, Stunned
* **Senses**: Passive Perception 17
* **Languages**: Common, Dwarvish, Falkovnian
* **Challenge**: 14 (11,500 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +5

#### **Traits**
* **Aura of Iron Command**: Allied creatures within 30 feet of Drakov have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened, and deal an extra 1d6 damage on weapon attacks.
* **Spiked Armor Retaliation**: Any creature that grapples Drakov or hits her with an unarmed strike takes 7 (2d6) piercing damage.
* **Relentless Juggernaut (Recharges on Long Rest)**: When Drakov drops to 0 hit points, she drops to 1 hit point instead and gains 30 temporary hit points.

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Drakov makes three attacks with *Talon's Wrath* or two attacks with her Heavy Siege Crossbow.
* **Talon's Wrath (+2 Greatsword)**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +12 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 14 (2d6 + 7) slashing damage plus 9 (2d8) fire damage. Against undead, this attack deals an extra 9 (2d8) radiant damage.
* **Heavy Siege Crossbow**: *Ranged Weapon Attack*: +7 to hit, range 100/400 ft., one target. *Hit*: 13 (2d10 + 2) piercing damage.
* **War Horn of Lekar (1/Day)**: Drakov sounds her master war horn. Up to 6 allied **Iron Talon Legionnaires** (Veterans) appear within 60 feet, acting immediately on her initiative count.

#### **Legendary Actions**
Drakov can take 3 legendary actions:
* **Tactical Reposition**: Drakov moves up to her speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
* **Command Strike**: Drakov directs an ally within 30 feet to make one weapon attack as a reaction.
* **Furious Cleave (Costs 2 Actions)**: Drakov swings *Talon's Wrath* in a wide arc. Each creature within 5 feet must make a DC 18 Dexterity saving throw, taking 18 (4d6 + 4) slashing damage on a failed save.

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Stolen Siege Core:** Drakov recently recovered an intact **Foundry Core** scavenged from a planar breach linked to the Plane of Eternal Craft. She has installed it beneath Lekar's inner citadel, using its thermal power to run automated flame-throwers and smelters—unaware that the core is emitting a chronal beacon that draws even more monstrosities to her walls.
* **Dealing with the Party:** Drakov does not negotiate from weakness. She views adventurers as mercenaries to be conscripted into service. If they prove their combat mettle by clearing an undead breach, she will offer bounties, masterwork arms, or safe conduct through Falkovnia.
* **The Fear of Impairment:** Drakov's greatest secret is that she refuses to ever sleep more than two hours at a time, taking alchemical stimulants from Captain Irina Voss to keep watch against the New Moon.
