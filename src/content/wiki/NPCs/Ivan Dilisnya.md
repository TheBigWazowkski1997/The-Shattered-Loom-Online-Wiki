# Ivan Dilisnya (The Gilded Child)

![[ivan_dilisnya.jpg]]

* **Race**: Human (Cursed Nobleman / Toy-Artificer)
* **Faction**: House Dilisnya / The Court of Lechberg
* **Role**: Co-Darklord of Borca / Master of Fortress Lechberg
* **Appearance**: A plump, petulant man in his forties with flushed cheeks, thinning dark curls, and watery blue eyes that gleam with childish cruelty. He dresses extravagantly in velvet sailor suits, lace ruffs, and diamond-buckled shoes, resembling a grotesque, oversized toddler. He constantly nibbles on poisoned sugarplums and marzipan, carrying a miniature mechanical songbird or a wind-up jester in his soft, manicured hands.
* **Personality**: Immature, spiteful, paranoid, and sadistic. Ivan views the world as his toybox and other living souls as playthings to be broken, repaired, or discarded. He loathes his cousin **Ivana Boritsi** with a consuming fury, blaming her for his exile to Lechberg, yet he is so terrified of her contact poisons that he refuses to ever meet her face-to-face, waging his war entirely through clockwork assassins, forged wills, and poisoned confections.

---

## Combat Profile

Ivan is a cowardly combatant who avoids direct confrontation. He surrounds himself with life-sized clockwork automatons, mechanical soldiers, and toy cannons. If cornered, he throws poisoned marzipan bombs, fires dart-blowers concealed in his cane, and screams for his constructs to shield his body.

*Medium Humanoid (Human, Artificer), Chaotic Evil*
* **Armor Class**: 15 (Silk Doublet with Concealed Mithral Links)
* **Hit Points**: 84 (13d8 + 26)
* **Speed**: 30 ft.

#### **Traits**
* **Child of Venom**: Ivan has spent decades ingesting small quantities of lethal toxins. He is immune to all nonmagical and magical poisons, and whenever a creature bites or ingests his blood, the creature takes 14 (4d6) poison damage.
* **Construct Master**: While Ivan is within 10 feet of at least one allied construct, all attacks directed at Ivan have disadvantage, as the constructs interpose themselves.
* **Toymaker's Retreat**: When Ivan takes damage, he can use his reaction to swap places with an allied construct within 30 feet, which takes the damage instead.

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Ivan makes two attacks: one with his Concealed Dart-Cane and one with a Marzipan Bomb.
* **Concealed Dart-Cane**: *Ranged Weapon Attack*: +6 to hit, range 30/90 ft., one target. *Hit*: 5 (1d4 + 3) piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) poison damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 15 Constitution saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute (save ends at end of turn).
* **Marzipan Bomb (3/Day)**: Ivan throws an explosive confection at a point within 30 feet. Each creature within a 10-foot radius must make a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw, taking 14 (4d6) fire damage and 14 (4d6) poison damage on a failed save, or half on a success.
* **Activate Toys (Recharge 5–6)**: Ivan winds a master silver key. 1d4 **Animated Armors** (refluffed as porcelain clockwork soldiers) spring to life within 30 feet and obey his mental commands.

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Clockwork Cold War:** Ivan is desperate to assassinate Ivana Boritsi. He will enthusiastically hire the party to deliver a "diplomatic gift" to Misericordia—an exquisite music box that secretly conceals an alchemical neurotoxin detonator.
* **The Loom's Gears:** Ivan's clockwork creations are powered by miniature, salvaged chronal springs smuggled from the Plane of Eternal Craft through the Gilded Caliper black market.
* **Tantrum Mechanics:** When Ivan loses a battle or has a prized toy broken, he flies into a screaming rage, ordering all traps in Lechberg to seal shut and flood the halls with sweet-smelling sleeping gas.
