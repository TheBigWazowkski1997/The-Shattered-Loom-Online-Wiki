# High Glutton Malakor (Hierophant of Fleshspire)

![[attachments/high_glutton_malakor.jpg]]

* **Race**: Tiefling (Flesh-Warped Abyssal Scion)
* **Faction**: The Glutton Priesthood of Fleshspire
* **Role**: High Priest / Hierophant of the Living Spire
* **Appearance**: An imposing, swollen figure clad in robes sewn from living, twitching flesh and adorned with scrimshaw talismans of carved bone. His skin is pale, translucent, and veined with pulsing black bile, while his crown is formed from four obsidian ram horns that coil tightly around his brow. In his right hand, he wields the *Crozius of Devouring*, a jagged bone staff capped with a constantly chewing maw.
* **Personality**: Sanctimonious, ravenous, and disturbingly eloquent. He views mortal life not with malice, but with a reverent desire to incorporate its vitality into the divine bulk of the Spire. He treats ritual sacrifice as an act of cosmic union and holy elevation.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Malakor commands grotesque biomantic magic, altering flesh, expelling caustic bile, and siphoning the life energy of any who defy the hunger of the Spire. He is rarely encountered without several devoted Flesh Servitors acting as a living wall of meat around him.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Spire’s Voice:** Malakor does not consider himself a ruler, but an ear. He listens to the subtle throbbing within the marrow-walls of Fleshspire and translates its desires into public mandates and sacrificial lotteries.
* **The Harvest Bargain:** If the party seeks passage through the low-spiral Bile Oceans or desires rare alchemical flesh-grafts, Malakor will negotiate—demanding either a piece of a party member's vitality (a permanent loss of 1 Hit Die until cured by *Greater Restoration*) or a live celestial or fey captive to feed the Everbirth vats.
* **Secret Ambition:** Malakor secretly fears that Fleshspire's hunger will one day outstrip its supply and turn upon him. He covertly finances exploratory expeditions into the Material Plane to identify large mortal settlements suitable for planar harvesting.
