# Gildmaster Vane Karr (The Gilded Master)

![[attachments/gildmaster_vane_karr.jpg]]

* **Race**: Gold Dwarf (Curse of Avarice-touched)
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Avarice/Plane of Avarice The Gilded Eternity|The Council of Chains]] / Midas Touch Guild
* **Role**: Proprietor of The Midas Touch Armory, Master Bullion-Smith
* **Appearance**: A stout, broad-shouldered dwarf whose flesh has slowly metamorphosed into lustrous 24-karat gold from decades of inhaling enchanted bullion vapors and planar avarice. His braided beard is threaded with uncut emeralds and fine platinum wires. He wears ornate plate armor crafted from tempered bullion alloys, etched with the runic ledgers of all who owe him debts.
* **Personality**: Shrewd, exacting, and unapologetically materialistic. Vane believes that tangible wealth is the only objective measure of a mortal's worth. Despite his ruthless commercial acumen, he has an intense pride of craftsmanship; he will never sell an inferior blade or flawed plate, considering bad craftsmanship an insult to wealth itself.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Transactional Trust**: Vane treats every interaction as a contract. He will never betray a contract that has been signed and paid for in bullion or true gems, but he will exploit any missing clause to maximize his profit.
* **The Living Vault Crisis**: He contracts the party to exterminate the mutated treasure mimic infesting his lower armory vault before his rivals on the Council of Chains discover his vulnerability.
* **Curse Progression**: His gold petrification is advancing; he secretly seeks rare reagents (such as primordial starlight or liquid silver from the Astral Sea) to halt the spread before his heart turns to solid bullion.
