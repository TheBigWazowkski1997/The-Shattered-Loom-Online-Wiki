# Chainlord Vane Shadow-Drift (Warlord of the Hollow Expanse)

![[attachments/chainlord_vane_shadow_drift.jpg]]

* **Race**: Shadar-kai (Shadow Elf Voidfarer)
* **Faction**: The Chainlord Flotilla / Free Corsairs of the Endless Maw
* **Role**: Fleet Admiral / Pirate Warlord of the Hollow Expanse
* **Appearance**: Tall, lean, and athletic, Vane wears a long leather duster lined with void-silk and adorned with severed anchor rings and grappling spikes. His face is pale ash with dark violet tattoos tracing the paths of stellar constellations, and his left arm has been amputated and replaced with a barbed, shadowy harpoon-prosthetic tethered directly to a spool of ghostly chain at his hip.
* **Personality**: Daring, cynical, and relentlessly pragmatic. Vane views existence as a perpetual tug-of-war against gravity and oblivion. He laughs in the teeth of abyssal gales, values personal loyalty above written laws, and despises the dogmatic zealotry of both the Glutton Priests and the celestial courts.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Vane is an aerial and zero-gravity skirmisher of unmatched skill. He uses shadow tethers and spatial teleports to drag foes off walkways, reel in targets into hazardous blades, and leap effortlessly across yawning voids.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Chains of Freedom:** Vane forged the Chainspire out of captured slaver ships and imperial wreckage. He offers safe haven to anyone willing to keep the tethers tight and fight off void-beasts, regardless of their past crimes.
* **The Endless Maw Gamble:** Vane knows the secrets of navigating the turbulent updrafts over the Endless Maw. If the party needs to reach sunken shards or escape pursuing factions, he is the only captain bold enough to pilot a ship into the vortex's eye—for a cut of the salvage.
* **Tense Rivalry:** Vane despises [[High Glutton Malakor|High Glutton Malakor]], considering the Glutton Priests insane cattle worshiping their own butcher. He frequently hijacks supply caravans intended for the Maw Gardens.
