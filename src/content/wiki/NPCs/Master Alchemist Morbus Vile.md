# Master Alchemist Morbus Vile (The Satiety Lord)

![[attachments/master_alchemist_morbus.jpg]]

* **Race**: Tiefling (Alchemically-Augmented)
* **Faction**: Satiety Vault / Mawhollow Merchant Enclave
* **Role**: Chief Alchemist of Mawhollow, Purveyor of Feaststones
* **Appearance**: An unnervingly tall, gaunt tiefling with obsidian skin and curling ram horns capped in brass. Where his stomach and liver once sat, an intricate glass and brass apparatus is integrated into his torso, containing swirling amber liquids and glowing crimson catalytic crystals that hum with faint digestive energy. He wears pristine velvet scholar robes trimmed in silver thread, contrasting sharply with the bleak canyon.
* **Personality**: Coldly intellectual, calculating, and courteous. Morbus views hunger not as an existential curse, but as an energetic equation to be balanced through science and alchemy. He speaks in measured, soothing cadences, always analyzing the biological vulnerabilities of those he converses with.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Morbus is a formidable alchemical spellcaster who manipulates acids, digestive enzymes, and necrotic life-siphoning fields.

* **Alchemical Infusions**: Morbus's spell attacks deal an extra 7 (2d6) acid or necrotic damage (included in the attacks).
* **Internal Catalyst**: When Morbus takes damage from an attack, his torso apparatus flashes; he can use his reaction to gain 15 temporary hit points and spray caustic digestive vapor in a 10-foot sphere around him (saving throw or take significant damage).
* **Spellcasting**: Morbus is a 9th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save saving throw, +9 to hit with spell attacks). He has the following wizard spells prepared:.
* **Multiattack**: Morbus makes two attacks with his *Vitriolic Cane* or casts a spell and makes one attack.
* **Vitriolic Cane**: Notable combat maneuver and signature technique.
* **Hunger Syringe**: *Melee Spell Attack:* +9 to hit, reach 5 ft., one creature. The target must succeed on a saving throw or gain 1 level of exhaustion.
* **Alchemical Bomb (3/Day)**: Morbus throws an unstable flask of concentrated digestive acid up to 40 feet. It explodes in a 15-foot radius. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw, taking significant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Monopolist of Satiety**: Morbus holds immense political sway in Mawhollow because he controls the supply of Feaststones. Even the Gorgekeepers must treat him with respect.
* **Ethical Flexibility**: He has no qualms about testing new anti-hunger serums on desperate debtors or condemned outlaws, viewing their deaths as acceptable research costs.
* **Party Interaction**: If the party brings him samples of divine marrow or rare bile from the heart of the Maw Eternal, he can synthesize permanent immunity elixirs against starvation and planar exhaustion.
