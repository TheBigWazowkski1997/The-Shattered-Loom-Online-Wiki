# Urdakael, the Unbroken (Forge-Herald of the Covenant)

![[attachments/urdakael_the_unbroken.jpg]]

* **Race**: Warforged (Elder Forgeplate Paragon)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Ironbound Covenant|The Ironbound Covenant]]
* **Role**: Forge-Herald (Supreme Commander of the Ironbound Covenant) / Seat I of the Sealed Conclave ("The Tempered")
* **Appearance**: A towering, broad-shouldered construct of immense age. His chassis is a living mosaic of history—original divine steel layered with plates of adamantine, celestial bronze, and meteoric iron welded over millennia of battle wounds. His right eye burns with the steady, piercing gold flame of the Vigil, while his left is a cold, runic targeting aperture. When he draws *Aethelforged*, his colossal divine greatsword, heat waves ripple from his chestplate.
* **Personality**: Absolute, immovable conviction. Urdakael speaks in heavy, measured cadences that carry the weight of an anvil falling into place. He does not negotiate with entropy and harbors no fear of death. Yet beneath his unyielding exterior lies the profound weight of thousands of years of warfare, carrying the spiritual echo of every fallen Warforged whose flame has gone dark.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Urdakael is a peerless frontline commander and juggernaut. He fights at the head of his Shard-Hosts, radiating divine forgefire and anchoring reality against Vorzath's entropic decay. He cannot be broken, cowed, or moved against his will.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Weight of Millennia:** Urdakael is not arrogant; he is ancient and weary, sustained entirely by sacred purpose. He remembers the names and dates of thousands of Warforged destroyed in battle. If questioned about the fallen, he can recite their service records from memory.
* **Seat I of the Conclave:** Urdakael knows of the Sealed Conclave and serves as "The Tempered." However, the Conclave's structure is partitioned so that even he does not know the true identities of all six other members—only their titles, tokens, and encrypted missives.
* **Stance on the Questioned:** While younger officers would purge doubt, Urdakael protects the Questioned. He believes Kaldryn intentionally gave Warforged wills capable of reflection, stating: *"Rigid iron snaps under sudden shock; tempered steel bends, remembers its shape, and strikes true."*
* **Meeting the Party:** If the adventurers arrive with shards of the Shattered Loom or battle against Vorzath's spawn, Urdakael will grant them an audience at the Eternal Bastion. He expects respect for the Mandate, concise reports, and unflinching resolve.
