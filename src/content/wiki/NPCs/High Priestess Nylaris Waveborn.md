# High Priestess Nylaris Waveborn (Voice of the Eternal Current)

![[attachments/high_priestess_nylaris.jpg]]

* **Race**: Merfolk
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane Of Water/Cities of Plane of Water/Tidehaven|The Tidal Spire]] / The Council of Waves
* **Role**: Spiritual Leader of the Tidal Spire, High Priestess of Hydrion
* **Appearance**: An ethereal merfolk with pearlescent, opalescent scales that shimmer with rainbow hues when catching light. Her hair resembles pale silver sea-grass, swaying weightlessly around her shoulders. She wears robes woven from consecrated silk-kelp and carries the *Staff of the Deep Spring*, a sacred relic carved from fossilized living coral tipped with a pulsing blue water-crystal.
* **Personality**: Serene, deeply contemplative, and compassionate. Nylaris speaks in a melodic, hypnotic cadence reminiscent of distant surf. Despite her gentle demeanor, she possesses an unyielding resolve when protecting the sacred waters and people of Tidehaven.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

High Priestess Nylaris is a 12th-level divine spellcaster who commands oceanic fury, healing waters, and abjuration shields to safeguard her allies.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Guardian of the Gates**: Nylaris is deeply troubled by the weakening wards of the **Abyssal Gate** in the deep caverns beneath Tidehaven. She suspects an aberrant power from the Far Realm is corrupting the deep trenches.
* **Diplomatic Anchor**: Nylaris acts as the moral conscience of the Council of Waves, frequently mediating disputes between Captain Lysara's corsairs and Tolaris Shellsong's ambitious mages.
* **Quests**: She tasks righteous parties with cleansing cursed marine temples, gathering holy pearl essences from dangerous trenches, and averting demonic or aberrant incursions.
