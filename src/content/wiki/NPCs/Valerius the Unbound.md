# Valerius the Unbound (The Chrono-Curator)

![[valerius_unbound.jpg]]

* **Race**: High Elf (Time-Displaced)
* **Faction**: [[The Fractured Nexus#2. The Gossamer Gallery|The Gossamer Gallery]] / Independent Collector
* **Role**: Curator / Planar Historian / Chronomancer
* **Appearance**: An exceptionally tall, elegant high elf with silver-white hair cascading past his shoulders, dressed in high-collared velvet robes dyed in deep astral midnight and embroidered with golden constellation charts. His most striking feature is metaphysical: **his shadow visibly lags two seconds behind his physical body**, moving in slow, fluid echoes of his past movements. His eyes are entirely silver, reflecting distant dying stars rather than ambient light.
* **Personality**: Aloof, nostalgic, and melancholic. Valerius was alive when Telara’s Loom was whole and witnessed the horrifying moment of the Great Betrayal firsthand. Having outlived his entire native plane, he views the present multiverse as an echo chamber of ghosts. He speaks with quiet, poetic gravitas, treating the artifacts in his gallery not as merchandise, but as the preserved final words of murdered civilizations.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Valerius is a formidable temporal skirmisher and chronomantic controller. Because his physical existence is desynchronized from the current flow of time, attacks routinely strike where he was a second ago, and he can rip localized pockets of time to slow, age, or displace attackers into future seconds. In danger, he can instantly swap places with his lagged shadow, evading deadly blows entirely.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Stolen Memory Vial:** Valerius’s primary campaign hook involves the theft of his prized *Memory Vial of the Aurelian System* by operatives of [[The Frayed Ones]]. If the party recovers it without breaking the crystal seal, he will reward them with an authentic *Driftglobe of the Loom* and grant them full research access to his private collection of dead-world codices.
* **Witness to the Betrayal:** Valerius is one of the few living beings who saw the **Silent Spindle** turn against Telara with their own eyes. If asked about the cataclysm, he grows deeply somber, recounting how the Spindle declared that "the script must burn so mortals may choose their own lines" before snapping the Thread of Infinite Potential.
* **Identifying Lost Relics:** Valerius will happily identify and appraise any mysterious or ancient relic the party recovers from planar ruins for a modest fee—or completely free if the item belonged to a world he once knew.
