# Chimera Veil (The Many-Faced Merchant)

![[attachments/chimera_veil.jpg]]

* **Race**: Planar Shapeshifter / Aberrant Entity
* **Faction**: The Echo Market / The Silenced Reliquary
* **Role**: Proprietor of The Silenced Reliquary, Master of Masks
* **Appearance**: An enigmatic entity whose physical form subtly shifts into the likeness, race, and gender of whomever observes them directly, yet always clothed in sweeping robes of sound-absorbing twilight silk that seems to drink the surrounding light. When multiple people view Chimera Veil at once, each person perceives someone from their own past whom they have wronged or lost.
* **Personality**: Cryptic, all-knowing, and serenely disquieting. Chimera Veil speaks with a layered voice of multiple overlapping tones that reverberate with undeniable truth. They value secrets, true names, and suppressed emotions far more than physical currency, and treat mortal subterfuge as an amusing art form.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The True Name Broker**: Chimera Veil can obtain almost any secret in the multiverse, but the price is never gold alone. Chimera Veil demands a secret that could destroy the buyer if revealed.
* **Ambiguous Ally**: Chimera Veil plays all sides in Whisperhold, helping heroes only when it disrupts stale planar stalemates or reveals grand hypocrisies.
