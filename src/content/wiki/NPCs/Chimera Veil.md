# Chimera Veil (The Many-Faced Merchant)

![[attachments/chimera_veil.jpg]]

* **Race**: Planar Shapeshifter / Aberrant Entity
* **Faction**: The Echo Market / The Silenced Reliquary
* **Role**: Proprietor of The Silenced Reliquary, Master of Masks
* **Appearance**: An enigmatic entity whose physical form subtly shifts into the likeness, race, and gender of whomever observes them directly, yet always clothed in sweeping robes of sound-absorbing twilight silk that seems to drink the surrounding light. When multiple people view Chimera Veil at once, each person perceives someone from their own past whom they have wronged or lost.
* **Personality**: Cryptic, all-knowing, and serenely disquieting. Chimera Veil speaks with a layered voice of multiple overlapping tones that reverberate with undeniable truth. They value secrets, true names, and suppressed emotions far more than physical currency, and treat mortal subterfuge as an amusing art form.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Chimera Veil is a formidable illusory assassin and reality-distorting entity that steps between shadows and strikes with silenced, lethal precision.

* **Shapechanger**: Chimera Veil can use an action to polymorph into a Small or Medium humanoid it has seen, or back into its true shadowy form. Its statistics remain the same.
* **Aura of Silence**: Chimera Veil can emit a 20-foot aura of magical silence at will (as per the *Silence* spell), choosing which creatures or objects within the aura are affected.
* **Shadow Step**: While in dim light or darkness, Chimera Veil can teleport up to 60 feet as a bonus action.
* **Sneak Attack (1/Turn)**: Deals an extra 14 (4d6) damage when hitting a target with a weapon attack while having advantage, or when the target is within 5 feet of an ally of Chimera Veil.
* **Multiattack**: Chimera Veil makes three attacks with their *Whisper-Edge Blades*.
* **Whisper-Edge Blade**: one target. If the attack hits, the target cannot make any vocal sound until the end of its next turn.
* **Mirror Nightmare (Recharge 5–6)**: Chimera Veil reveals the terrifying truth of a creature's deepest secret. One creature within 60 feet must make a saving throw. On a failure, the creature takes significant damage and is frightened and paralyzed for 1 minute. The target can repeat the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success. On a successful save, it takes half damage and is not paralyzed or frightened.
* **Flicker Away**: When hit by an attack, Chimera Veil turns into a swirl of smoke and teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space, halving the triggering attack's damage.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The True Name Broker**: Chimera Veil can obtain almost any secret in the multiverse, but the price is never gold alone. Chimera Veil demands a secret that could destroy the buyer if revealed.
* **Ambiguous Ally**: Chimera Veil plays all sides in Whisperhold, helping heroes only when it disrupts stale planar stalemates or reveals grand hypocrisies.
