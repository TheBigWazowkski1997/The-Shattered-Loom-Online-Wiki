# Garrick the Frayed (The Infiltrator)

![[attachments/garrick_the_frayed.jpg]]

* **Race**: Tiefling
* **Faction**: Cult of the Broken Thread (corrupted; formerly a senior forge-wright of Metallos)
* **Role**: Forge-wright / Corrupted Occultist / Subterranean Saboteur
* **Appearance**: A tall, slender tiefling with ash-grey skin, sharp curved horns, and glowing purple eyes overflowing with void energy. His clothing is scorched, torn, and frayed from both planar forge fires and entropic magic. Whisps of dark purple void energy ripple around his hands as he works.
* **Personality**: Tormented, fanatical, and obsessive. He once sought perfection in metal and craft, but the Cult of the Broken Thread convinced him that the Loom is a cage holding back true creation. He now views unraveling the Loom as the ultimate act of forging.

---
## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Garrick is a powerful corrupted spellcaster and martial hybrid (an MCDM Illrigger) who uses baleful seals to lock down opponents, siphons health with infernal conduits, and casts disruptive magic to control the battlefield. In Session 5, he is found sabotaging the Molten Veins.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Fall of the Forge-Wright:** Garrick was once one of the most respected senior forge-wrights working under Arbiter Valerie Sterling. His mind frayed under the pressure of trying to repair the planar anchors of Metallos, making him easy prey for the whispers of the Cult of the Broken Thread.
* **The Sabotage :** In Scene I of Session 5, Garrick is found at the central console of the Molten Veins, using void-filaments to unravel the stabilizing gears. He is fanatical, claiming that the Loom is a cage and the multiverse must be "melted down and reforged."
* **Death Throes:** When Garrick is defeated, he dissolves into grey ash, whispering his final cryptic words: *"The Loom is a cage... we only wanted to melt the bars..."*
