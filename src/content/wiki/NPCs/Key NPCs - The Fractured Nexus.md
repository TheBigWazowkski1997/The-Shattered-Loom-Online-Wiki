# Key NPCs - The Fractured Nexus

The following figures make up the political leadership, commercial elite, underworld syndicates, and cosmic guardians of [[The Fractured Nexus|The Fractured Nexus (The City of Broken Threads)]].

* * *

## 1. Cosmic Entities & Neutral Arbiters

* [[The Loom Warden]] — The Silent Singularity, eldritch custodian of the Nexus and living scar of Telara’s Loom (CR 28 Cosmic Aberration).
* [[Stitch, the Frayed End Barkeep]] — Multi-limbed planar amalgam construct and neutral arbiter of the Frayed End Tavern (CR 12 Construct).
* [[Malphas the Broker]] — Master of the Ossuary of Rebirth, infernal surgeon, and architect of bodily grafts (CR 12 Fiend).

* * *

## 2. The Spire Ward (Pale Lodge & Threadbearers)

* [[Aelira, the Weftkeeper]] — First of the Spindle Lords, leader of the Way of Harmony (CR 14 High Elf Archmage).
* [[Kaelthar, the Threadwarden]] — Second of the Spindle Lords, commander of the Order of Severance (CR 12 Shadar-kai Warrior).
* [[The Silent Spool]] — Third of the Spindle Lords, oracle of the Nexus Spire (CR 18 Eldritch Construct).
* **High Justiciar Vaelen Drake** — Lead magistrate and civic spokesman of [[The Threadbearers]].
* **Arch-Architect Kaelis Vorn** — Chief structural engineer overseeing the Loomforge and Golden Filaments.
* **The Chronicler** — Living manuscript proprietor of *The Thread-Bound Archive*.
* [[Valerius the Unbound]] — Time-displaced high elf curator of *The Gossamer Gallery* (CR 6 Chronomancer).
* **Lyraea Dawnbreeze** — Eladrin bard and proprietor of *The Singing Spindle* tavern.

* * *

## 3. The Sanctorum of the Blind Needle (Order of the Severed Eye)

* [[Grand Inquisitor Sylas the Blind]] — Supreme Inquisitor of the Severed Eye, master of blind psionic tracking (CR 16 Kalashtar).
* [[Doctor Vaelen Malcor, Void-Surgeon]] — Chief Void-Surgeon and master of non-Euclidean pathology (CR 13 Tiefling).
* **Sister Morwenna of the Pale Thread** — Chief Astral Seer and Diviner of the Three Needles (CR 11 Kalashtar Seer).

* * *

## 4. The Drift District (The Rift Market)

* [[Knot]] — Dry-witted Riftborn merchant and survival specialist of *The Warp & Weft* (CR 5 Riftborn).
* [[Hook, the Thread-Cartographer]] — Manic changeling scholar and planar cartographer (CR 3 Support Scholar).
* **Bramble** — Sentient moss-covered Shambling Mound and proprietor of *The Menagerie of Misfit Shards*.
* **Master Caelis** — Githzerai telekinetic glass-shaper of *The Star-Glass Refinery*.
* **Overseer Zorath** — Dwarven banker of *The Planar Exchange & Memory Bank*.
* [[Grand Huntmaster Torvald Blood-Tracer]] — Supreme commander of [[The Hearth of the Spoor (Grand Central Lodge)]] (CR 16 Goliath Master Stalker).

* * *

## 5. The Frayed Undercroft & The Void-Side

* [[Fray, the Unraveler]] — Renegade Riftborn warlord and reigning champion of the Spindle Ring fighting pits (CR 8 Aberration).
* **Madame Vespera** — Night Hag mastermind of the Cartel of the Severed Seam.
* [[Garrick the Frayed]] — Corrupted tiefling forge-wright and subterranean saboteur (CR 7 Illrigger).
* **Kaelen the Black-Smith** — Disgraced duergar weaponsmith of *The Severed Spool*.
* **Morwen the Pale** — Shadar-kai poisoner of *The Ashen Filament Apothecary*.
* **"Quick-Fingers" Jax** — Kenku fence and information broker of *The Lock & Shroud*.
* **Dr. Varkus Slag-Eye** — Goblin unlicensed graft surgeon of *The Stitch-Ripper Chop-Shop*.
