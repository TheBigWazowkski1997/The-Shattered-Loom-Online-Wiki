# Kallian the Abyssal (Lord of the Sinking Market)

![[attachments/kallian_the_abyssal.jpg]]

* **Race**: Triton (Deep-Trench Dweller)
* **Faction**: The Deepcurrent Syndicate / The Sinking Market
* **Role**: Master Scavenger, Black Market Boss, Sunken Salvage Baron
* **Appearance**: A weathered, scarred triton with dark indigo skin patterned like a tiger shark, sharp silver fins, and eyes that glow with eerie pale bioluminescence. He wears coat-plates fashioned from black kraken-carapace, adorned with salvaged brass gears and barnacled medals from sunken planar ships. At his side rests *Drowned-Bite*, a barbed abyssal harpoon forged from cold starmetal.
* **Personality**: Cautious, ruthless, and opportunistic. Kallian regards the surface worlds and celestial planes as naive, fragile toys that inevitably sink into his domain. He respects only leverage, secrets, and raw survival instinct.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Master of the Trenches:** Kallian controls every scrap of valuable wreckage that tumbles down the Sapphire Abyss. Anyone wishing to explore the deeper trenches or salvage astral wrecks must pay him homage or face his deep-sea cutthroats.
* **The Loom's Debris:** Kallian recently salvaged a broken clockwork conduit that hums with starlight and refuses to rust. He does not fully understand that it is a direct loom-filament, but he knows its power is drawing dangerous entities to his market.
* **Dialogue & Demeanor:** *"Down here, gold doesn't float, and neither will your excuses. Pay the toll, or feed the trench."*
