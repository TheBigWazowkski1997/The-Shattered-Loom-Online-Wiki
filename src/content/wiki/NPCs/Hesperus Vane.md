# Hesperus Vane (Grand Caliper of Iron & Gear)

![[attachments/hesperus_vane.jpg]]

* **Race**: Human (Cyborg-Artificer)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper|The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper]]
* **Role**: Grand Caliper of Iron & Gear — Metallos Representative of the Triumvirate
* **Appearance**: A tall, thin man in pristine dark leather coats lined with ticking clockwork brass. His left eye has been replaced by a brass-plated magnifying monocle with multiple clicking lenses that focus and refocus on their own. His left hand is a clockwork prosthetic of incredible precision — fine brass fingers that can pick a lock, draft a schematic, or crush an opponent's wrist with equal efficiency.
* **Personality**: Cold, analytical, and highly impatient. He views biological life as a series of inefficient designs that can be improved with mechanical augmentation. He speaks in clipped, precise sentences and finds emotional reasoning to be a critical design flaw.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Hesperus does not lower himself to physical combat if avoidable, but is a formidable tactician who deploys clockwork constructs, lightning capacitors, and precise mechanical strikes. If cornered, his prosthetic hand can extend hidden blades and release electrical discharges.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Automation Agenda:** Hesperus is actively lobbying the Triumvirate to authorize a fleet of fully autonomous clockwork trade-servants to replace dockworkers, miners, and courier guilds across the Plane of Eternal Craft. He believes this will make the plane indestructible.
* **The Secret Research:** In the sub-vaults below the Hall of the Iron Gavel, he secretly funds research into *sentient* constructs — beings that can think, adapt, and evolve without organic input. This is in direct violation of Gilded Caliper safety laws.
* **Views on the Players:** If the players impress him with efficient, tactically sound solutions to the Metallos crisis, he offers a cold, backhanded compliment: *"Marginally less inefficient than expected."* He will never fully trust them — they are biological and therefore unpredictable variables.
