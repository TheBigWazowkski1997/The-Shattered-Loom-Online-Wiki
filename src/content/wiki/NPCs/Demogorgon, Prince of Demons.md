## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Demogorgon is a titan of primal contradiction and abyssal fury. In battle, he fights with terrifying reach and hydrodynamic mobility, crushing mortal frontlines with life-draining tentacles while his dual heads project conflicting gazes of madness and beguilement that turn allies against one another.

---

## **Roleplay & Lore Notes**
* **Personality**: Volatile, arrogant, constantly arguing between his two heads. Aameul speaks in calculating, icy whispers; Hethradiah roars with bloodthirsty hysteria.
* **Tactics**: In combat, Demogorgon uses reach and mobility in the water to isolate targets, crippling enemy frontlines with his HP-reducing tentacles while using his twin gazes to turn spellcasters against their companions.
