# Captain Sariss Voryn (The Salamander Vanguard)

![[attachments/sariss_voryn.jpg]]

* **Race**: Salamander (Noble Bloodline)
* **Faction**: The Emberwatch of Blazeward Keep
* **Role**: High Commander of the Outer Garrison / Master of the Crucible Bastion
* **Appearance**: An imposing, seven-foot-tall serpentine warrior with iridescent crimson-and-charcoal scales that glow from within like superheated embers. Her sinuous lower serpent body is coiled beneath heavy obsidian lamellar armor, and she wields the *Hellfire Glaive*, a massive serrated polearm that leaves trails of blue fire with every swing.
* **Personality**: Fierce, uncompromising, and strictly martial. Unlike many of her chaotic kin, Sariss respects military discipline, duty, and honor above all else. She despises cowardice and tests outsiders through tests of physical grit.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Military Bastion:** Sariss coordinates the defense of the eastern caldera, standing as Cinderhold’s first line of defense against rogue fire titans and extraplanar infiltrators.
* **Loyalty to Cinderhold:** Despite tempting offers from Efreet sultans in the City of Brass, Sariss remains fiercely loyal to Cinderhold, valuing the freedom and meritocracy of the mountain over imperial slavery.
* **Dialogue & Demeanor:** *"Words are like ash—they drift and disappear. Show me what your steel can do against the heat of Mount Khaldrath, or clear out of my fortress."*
