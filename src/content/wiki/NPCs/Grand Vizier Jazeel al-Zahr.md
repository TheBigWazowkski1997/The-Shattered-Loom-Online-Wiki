# Grand Vizier Jazeel al-Zahr (The Cinder Sovereign)

![[attachments/grand_vizier_jazeel.jpg]]

* **Race**: Efreet (Noble Genie)
* **Faction**: Court of Embers / The Charcoal Palace
* **Role**: Chief Advisor to the Grand Sultan of the Efreet, Keeper of the Burning Oaths
* **Appearance**: An imposing noble efreet standing nine feet tall, with polished obsidian-red skin, curling horns tipped with beaten gold, and eyes of incandescent white fire. He is draped in gossamer robes of spun flame-silk and cloth-of-gold that billow on phantom thermal updrafts. His fingers are adorned with signet rings cut from captured stars, and he bears a curved scepter of smoking black iron that leaks tiny glowing sparks.
* **Personality**: Supremely arrogant, impeccably polite, and ruthlessly calculating. Jazeel treats the multiverse as an intricate ledger of debts, obligations, and contracts. He never loses his temper openly; a soft, elegant smile from Jazeel is far more dangerous than a dragon’s roar. He views mortals as brief, fragile sparks to be directed toward imperial dominance.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Jazeel is a Noble Efreeti archmage (formidable) capable of innate wish-magic, destructive pyromancy, and planar command. He floats above the fray, hurling searing rays while commanding bound fire elementals to crush his enemies.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Web of Oaths**: Jazeel holds hundreds of planar contracts. If the party owes a debt or is entangled with planar courts, Jazeel likely has purchased or audited the paperwork.
* **Manipulative Patron**: He employs powerful mortal parties to undermine rival emirs or recover stolen relics from other planes, always offering tempting rewards that come with subtle contractual clauses.
* **Quests**: He will hire the party to expose a traitor in the Court of Embers, secure a prime elemental core from the Plane of Earth, or sabotage a rival trade cartel from the Plane of Water.
