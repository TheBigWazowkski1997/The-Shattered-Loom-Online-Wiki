# Captain Lysara Driftwave (The Corsair of Waves)

![[attachments/captain_lysara_driftwave.jpg]]

* **Race**: Water Genasi
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane Of Water/Cities of Plane of Water/Tidehaven|Council of Waves]] (Commander of the Corsairs)
* **Role**: Pirate Queen, Council Member of Tidehaven, and Admiral of the Free Flotilla
* **Appearance**: A striking water genasi woman with aquamarine skin that glimmers like sunlight through clear ocean shallows. Her hair moves as if suspended in water, flowing in deep indigo waves adorned with small pearls and shark-tooth charms. She wears a longcoat tailored from enchanted bioluminescent kelp, sea-leather boots, and carries *Tide-Cleaver*, an ancestral crystalline rapier that hums with oceanic thunder.
* **Personality**: Bold, sharp-witted, and relentlessly independent. Lysara despises tyranny and rigid bureaucracy, valuing individual freedom, nautical cunning, and fierce loyalty. While charming and playful in negotiation, she is ruthlessly efficient in shipboard battle and does not tolerate treason.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Captain Lysara is a master swashbuckler who uses the water itself as her ally. She is exceptionally agile, slipping past frontline defenders to duel enemy leaders while commanding ocean currents to displace her foes.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Pirate Queen's Agenda**: Lysara believes Tidehaven must maintain complete autonomy from outside planar empires like the Consortium of the Gilded Caliper and the Efreeti of the City of Brass. She is willing to ally with adventurers who share her vision of free planar navigation.
* **Tidehaven Politics**: She clashes constantly with Master Arcanist Tolaris Shellsong over the Abyssal Gate. While Tolaris wants to study and harness its power, Lysara views it as an existential threat that must be sealed before an eldritch incursion swallows the city.
* **Quests**: She will hire the party to escort high-risk convoys, intercept hostile Sahuagin war-barges, or scout rogue derelicts drifting from the shadow borders.
