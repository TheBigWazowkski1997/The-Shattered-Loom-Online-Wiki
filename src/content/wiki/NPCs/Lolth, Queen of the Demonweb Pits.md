## **D&D 5e Stat Block Profile (CR 24)**

*Large Fiend (Demon, Shapechanger), Chaotic Evil*

* **Armor Class**: 21 (Natural Armor)
* **Hit Points**: 324 (24d10 + 192)
* **Speed**: 40 ft., climb 40 ft.
* **Saving Throws**: Dex +12, Con +14, Wis +13, Cha +16
* **Skills**: Stealth +19, Deception +16, Insight +13, Perception +13
* **Damage Resistances**: Cold, Fire, Lightning
* **Damage Immunities**: Poison; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned, Prone
* **Senses**: Truesight 120 ft., Web Sense 300 ft., Passive Perception 23
* **Languages**: All, Telepathy 120 ft.
* **Challenge**: 24 (62,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +7

### **Special Traits**
* **Spider Climb & Web Walker**: Lolth can climb difficult surfaces without making an ability check and ignores movement restrictions caused by webbing.
* **Innate Spellcasting**: Charisma-based (DC 24, +16 to hit). At will: *Darkness*, *Dispel Magic*, *Faerie Fire*, *Web*. 3/day each: *Divine Word*, *Harm*, *Heal*, *Insect Plague*. 1/day: *Mass Suggestion*.
* **Sunlight Sensitivity**: While in direct sunlight, Lolth has disadvantage on attack rolls and Perception checks relying on sight.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: Can choose to succeed on a failed saving throw.

### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Lolth makes three attacks: two with her Claws and one with her Venomous Bite.
* **Claws**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 18 (3d6 + 8) slashing damage plus 14 (4d6) poison damage.
* **Venomous Bite**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +15 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 24 (3d10 + 8) piercing damage plus 36 (8d8) poison damage (DC 22 Con save halves).
* **Web Spray (Recharge 5–6)**: Lolth sprays sticky, acidic web in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 22 Dex save or be restrained and take 28 (8d6) acid damage.

### **Legendary Actions (3/Round)**
* **Web Strider**: Lolth moves up to her speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
* **Bite Attack**: Lolth makes one Venomous Bite attack.
* **Cast Spell (Costs 2 Actions)**: Lolth casts one of her at-will or 3/day spells.

***

## **Roleplay & DM Notes**
* **Personality**: Paranoic, sadistic, infinitely cunning. She demands constant sacrifices and tests her followers' loyalty by forcing them to betray one another.
* **Loom Integration**: Lolth operates a counterfeit loom inside her mobile Web-Citadel, capturing drifting Astral shards and re-weaving mortal souls into black spider-silk to construct an unassailable cosmic web.
