## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Lolth is an apex terror of shadows, venom, and divine trickery. Wielding black divine magic and venomous fangs, she ensnares enemies in unbreakable silk webs, summons swarms of demonic spiders, and exploits paranoia to turn companions against each other.

---

## **Roleplay & Lore Notes**
* **Personality**: Paranoic, sadistic, infinitely cunning. She demands constant sacrifices and tests her followers' loyalty by forcing them to betray one another.
* **Loom Integration**: Lolth operates a counterfeit loom inside her mobile Web-Citadel, capturing drifting Astral shards and re-weaving mortal souls into black spider-silk to construct an unassailable cosmic web.
