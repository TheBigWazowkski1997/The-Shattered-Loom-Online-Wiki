# The Raven Queen (Mistress of Fate, Death, and Memory)

![[raven_queen.jpg]]

* **Race**: Divine Entity / Planar Sovereign (formerly Elf Sorceress-Queen)
* **Faction**: The Shadar-kai Memory-Sworn / Sovereign of the Shadowfell
* **Role**: Ruler of the Fortress of Memories / Shepherd of Severed Soul-Threads
* **Appearance**: An enigmatic, ethereal figure of shifting shadows and frost. She appears as a tall, poised woman wrapped in a voluminous mantle of midnight-black raven feathers that seem to dissolve into mist at the hem. Her face is concealed behind a polished mask of dark porcelain, silver filigree, or an ivory raven's skull. When she speaks, her voice echoes with the dry whisper of autumn leaves and the tolling of distant funeral bells.
* **Personality**: Cold, aloof, dispassionate, yet intensely observant. She does not harvest souls to torment them; she collects memories, emotions, and fateful tragedies to preserve them against the total unraveling of the multiverse. She regards the Darklords not as rulers, but as pathetic, squirming insects trapped in jars of their own making.

---

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

The Raven Queen rarely descends to engage in physical combat, acting through her shadow dragons, Shadar-kai gloomweavers, and flocks of spectral ravens. If challenged in her sanctum or when an avatar manifests, she commands the primal forces of absolute cold, shadow magic, and the shearing of soul-threads.

---

## Roleplay & Lore Notes

* **The Weaver's Collector:** The Raven Queen recognizes the Shattered Loom as the true origin of mortal fate. Her Shadar-kai scavenge the Planes of Existence to collect fraying threads, bringing them to the Fortress of Memories where they are woven into the Tapestry of the Unforgotten.
* **Patron of Mortals:** Adventurers seeking to repair or claim Loom Fragments can bargain with the Raven Queen. She will exchange crucial planar lore or resurrect fallen allies—demanding in return a traumatic personal memory or the elimination of a Darklord who hoards stolen weave fragments (such as Strahd's Crimson Weave).
* **The Dark Powers' Detente:** The Dark Powers tolerate the Raven Queen's presence because her harvesting of sorrow complements their eternal torment of the Darklords, creating a grim planar equilibrium.
