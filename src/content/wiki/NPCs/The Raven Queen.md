# The Raven Queen (Mistress of Fate, Death, and Memory)

![[raven_queen.jpg]]

* **Race**: Divine Entity / Planar Sovereign (formerly Elf Sorceress-Queen)
* **Faction**: The Shadar-kai Memory-Sworn / Sovereign of the Shadowfell
* **Role**: Ruler of the Fortress of Memories / Shepherd of Severed Soul-Threads
* **Appearance**: An enigmatic, ethereal figure of shifting shadows and frost. She appears as a tall, poised woman wrapped in a voluminous mantle of midnight-black raven feathers that seem to dissolve into mist at the hem. Her face is concealed behind a polished mask of dark porcelain, silver filigree, or an ivory raven's skull. When she speaks, her voice echoes with the dry whisper of autumn leaves and the tolling of distant funeral bells.
* **Personality**: Cold, aloof, dispassionate, yet intensely observant. She does not harvest souls to torment them; she collects memories, emotions, and fateful tragedies to preserve them against the total unraveling of the multiverse. She regards the Darklords not as rulers, but as pathetic, squirming insects trapped in jars of their own making.

---

## Combat Profile

The Raven Queen rarely descends to engage in physical combat, acting through her shadow dragons, Shadar-kai gloomweavers, and flocks of spectral ravens. If challenged in her sanctum or when an avatar manifests, she commands the primal forces of absolute cold, shadow magic, and the shearing of soul-threads.

*Medium Celestial (Deity Avatar), True Neutral*
* **Armor Class**: 20 (Natural Armor & Shadow Ward)
* **Hit Points**: 285 (30d8 + 150)
* **Speed**: 40 ft., fly 60 ft. (hover)

#### **Traits**
* **Incorporeal Movement**: The Raven Queen can move through other creatures and objects as if they were difficult terrain. She takes 5 (1d10) force damage if she ends her turn inside an object.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If the Raven Queen fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
* **Aura of the Winter Veil**: Any enemy creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the Raven Queen takes 14 (4d6) cold damage and its speed is reduced by 10 feet until the start of its next turn.
* **Memory Shear**: When the Raven Queen hits a creature with a spell or attack, the target must make a DC 22 Wisdom saving throw or lose one memory or prepared spell slot (highest available level).
* **Spellcasting**: The Raven Queen is a 20th-level spellcaster (Spell Save DC 23, +15 to hit with spell attacks). Prepared spells:
  * Cantrips (at will): *Chill Touch, Mage Hand, Minor Illusion, Toll the Dead*
  * 1st level (4 slots): *Detect Magic, Fog Cloud, Shield, Ray of Sickness*
  * 2nd level (3 slots): *Blindness/Deafness, Darkness, Misty Step, Silence*
  * 3rd level (3 slots): *Animate Dead, Counterspell, Dispel Magic, Fear*
  * 4th level (3 slots): *Blight, Dimension Door, Shadow of Moil*
  * 5th level (3 slots): *Cone of Cold, Danse Macabre, Scrying*
  * 6th level (2 slots): *Circle of Death, Soul Cage*
  * 7th level (2 slots): *Finger of Death, Plane Shift*
  * 8th level (1 slot): *Demiplane, Maddening Darkness*
  * 9th level (1 slot): *Time Stop, Power Word Kill*

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: The Raven Queen makes three attacks with her Scythe of Severed Threads or casts one spell and makes one scythe attack.
* **Scythe of Severed Threads**: *Melee Spell Attack*: +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 17 (2d8 + 8) slashing damage plus 18 (4d8) necrotic damage. If the target is a creature, its hit point maximum is reduced by an amount equal to the necrotic damage taken.
* **Flock of Memories (Recharge 5–6)**: The Raven Queen dissolves into a storm of spectral ravens and teleports up to 60 feet. Each creature within 20 feet of the origin and destination points must make a DC 22 Constitution saving throw, taking 45 (10d8) cold damage and being blinded for 1 minute on a failed save, or half damage on a success.

#### **Legendary Actions**
The Raven Queen can take 3 legendary actions:
* **Shadow Stride**: The Raven Queen teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space she can see in dim light or darkness.
* **Raven's Glare**: One creature within 60 feet must make a DC 22 Wisdom save or be frightened until the end of its next turn.
* **Thread Shear (Costs 2 Actions)**: The Raven Queen targets one creature she can see within 60 feet. The target takes 28 (8d6) necrotic damage and cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn.

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Weaver's Collector:** The Raven Queen recognizes the Shattered Loom as the true origin of mortal fate. Her Shadar-kai scavenge the Planes of Existence to collect fraying threads, bringing them to the Fortress of Memories where they are woven into the Tapestry of the Unforgotten.
* **Patron of Mortals:** Adventurers seeking to repair or claim Loom Fragments can bargain with the Raven Queen. She will exchange crucial planar lore or resurrect fallen allies—demanding in return a traumatic personal memory or the elimination of a Darklord who hoards stolen weave fragments (such as Strahd's Crimson Weave).
* **The Dark Powers' Detente:** The Dark Powers tolerate the Raven Queen's presence because her harvesting of sorrow complements their eternal torment of the Darklords, creating a grim planar equilibrium.
