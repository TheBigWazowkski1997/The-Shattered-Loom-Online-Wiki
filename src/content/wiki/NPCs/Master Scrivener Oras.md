# Master Scrivener Oras (The Silent Scribe)

![[attachments/master_scrivener_oras.jpg]]

* **Race**: Tiefling
* **Faction**: The Encrypted Codex / Whisperhold Scribes
* **Role**: Master Cryptographer, Archival Scribe, Dealer in Secrets
* **Appearance**: An elderly, frail tiefling with ash-grey skin, curved ebony horns, and lips ritually stitched shut with fine silver wire. He communicates exclusively through floating letters of pale silver light that trace in the air with his enchanted quill. He wears midnight-blue velvet robes with deep pockets filled with parchment rolls, decoding lenses, and brass cipher wheels.
* **Personality**: Meticulous, discreet, and deeply bound by professional honor. Oras believes that secrets are sacred trusts—dangerous when mishandled, but powerful tools of survival when kept inviolate. He will never voluntarily disclose a client's secrets, even under the threat of death or torture.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Deciphering Ancient Texts**: Oras is the premier NPC for decoding ancient forgotten scripts, lost planar cartography, or mysterious letters found by the party.
* **The Stolen Vault Key**: Oras holds the only cipher capable of unlocking the second gate of the Vault of Murmurs, making him a prime target for ambitious planar cabals.
