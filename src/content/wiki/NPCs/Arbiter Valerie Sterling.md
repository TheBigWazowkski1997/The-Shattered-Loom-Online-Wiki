# Arbiter Valerie Sterling (Metallos Chapter Head)

![[attachments/arbiter_valerie_sterling.jpg]]

* **Race**: Dwarf (Forge-Master)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper|The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper]]
* **Role**: Arbiter of the Brass Gavel — Metallos Chapter Head
* **Appearance**: A stocky, soot-stained dwarf with braided copper hair adorned with iron beads. She wears heavy smith's aprons over fine velvet robes, and a massive brass gavel — the symbol of her office — hangs prominently at her hip. Her hands are scarred from forge-work, and her eyes burn with the intensity of someone who has personally resolved a hundred disputes.
* **Personality**: Practical, loud, and fiercely protective of her city's artisans. She has no tolerance for political pleasantries and prefers direct action. She is blunt, fair, and deeply principled — though her principles are rooted firmly in Metallos pragmatism.

## Spellcasting

Valerie is a 9th-level Forge Cleric. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (Spell Save saving throw, +7 to hit with spell attacks).

* **Cantrips (at will):** *Sacred Flame*, *Thaumaturgy*, *Mending*
* **1st Level (4 slots):** *Searing Smite*, *Shield of Faith*, *Cure Wounds*
* **2nd Level (3 slots):** *Heat Metal*, *Spiritual Weapon*, *Lesser Restoration*
* **3rd Level (3 slots):** *Elemental Weapon*, *Protection from Energy*
* **4th Level (3 slots):** *Wall of Fire*, *Fabricate*
* **5th Level (1 slot):** *Flame Strike*

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Valerie fights as a hammer-and-shield warrior-cleric, using her legendary Brass Gavel to stun and suppress, and channeling forge-deity power to empower her allies or rain molten sparks on her enemies. She rarely initiates violence, but once committed, she fights to end the problem decisively.

* **Dwarven Resilience**: Valerie has advantage on saving throws against poison and resistance to poison damage.
* **Forge-Touched**: Valerie's weapon attacks deal an extra significant damage (included in attacks below).
* **Gavel Authority**: When Valerie crits with her Brass Gavel, the target must succeed on a saving throw or be stunned until the end of their next turn.
* **Multiattack**: Valerie makes two attacks with her Brass Gavel and uses Forgefire Burst if available.
* **Brass Gavel**: one target. The target must succeed on a saving throw or be knocked prone.
* **Forgefire Burst (Recharge 5–6)**: Valerie slams her gavel into the ground, sending a shockwave of molten sparks in a 15-foot radius. Each creature in the area must succeed on a saving throw, taking significant damage on a failure, or half on a success.
* **Rally the Artisans (1/Day)**: Valerie calls out to her allies. Up to 6 creatures of her choice within 60 feet regain 2d6 + 3 hit points and gain advantage on their next attack roll or saving throw.
* **Parry**: When a creature hits Valerie with a melee attack, she can add +4 to her AC against that attack (potentially turning it into a miss).

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Primary Quest-Giver:** Valerie is the party's main institutional contact in Metallos. She handles permits, arbitrates contract disputes, and is the one who ultimately unlocks access to the Colossal Forge. She respects results and despises excuses.
* **The Hidden Titan:** In the sub-vaults beneath the Hall of the Iron Gavel, she has secretly stashed a deactivated clockwork titan — an illegal Level-9 construct she found in a collapsed rift. She is studying its engine core before Hesperus Vane discovers it and claims it for the Triumvirate.
* **Relationship with Daine:** She knows Daine's reputation from the Brotherhood and respects the work ethic, if not the life choices. If Daine impresses her in the arena, she gives a rare genuine compliment.
