# High Chancellor Aeris Windveil (Voice of the Concord)

![[attachments/high_chancellor_aeris.jpg]]

* **Race**: Air Genasi
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane of Air/Cities of the Plane of Air/Zephyros|The Concord of Winds]]
* **Role**: High Chancellor of Zephyros, Diplomatic Head of the Floating Metropolis
* **Appearance**: An exceptionally tall, poised air genasi woman with opalescent, translucent skin that glows with a faint pale-blue inner luminescence. Her hair moves constantly as if caught in a gentle breeze, shifting from sky blue to wispy cirrus-white. She wears layered robes of spun cloud-silk embroidered with silver runes, and carries the *Staff of the Zephyr*, a crystalline spire topped with an eternally spinning wind-core.
* **Personality**: Visionary, diplomatic, calm, and articulate. Aeris believes that Zephyros's true strength lies in its openness to outside planar cultures and free intellectual pursuit. While patient in council debates, she possesses a razor-sharp intellect and does not hesitate to unleash tempestuous authority when the sovereignty of the city is threatened.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Aeris is a 13th-level aeromantic sorcerer/evoker (formidable) who manipulates wind currents to evade attacks, deflect projectiles, and displace enemies with sonic concussions.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Concord's Fragility**: Aeris constantly balances the competing interests of the Cloudforged Artisans, the Skyguard military faction, and visiting planar emissaries.
* **Planar Diplomacy**: She has open diplomatic channels with both Arbiter Valerie Sterling of the Gilded Caliper and the Court of Embers in the City of Brass, striving to prevent inter-elemental wars from boiling over into Zephyros's skies.
* **Quests**: She hires adventurers to foil espionage plots from the Skyward Cabal, recover stolen skyships, and investigate tears in the atmospheric fabric of the Plane of Air.
