# Prime Cogwright Torin (Keeper of the Prime Engine)

![[attachments/prime_cogwright_torin.jpg]]

* **Race**: Rock Gnome (Clockwork Chronomancer)
* **Faction**: The Order of Cogwrights / Municipal Directorate of Lex Machina
* **Role**: Prime Cogwright / Chief Chronal Engineer of Lex Machina
* **Appearance**: An elderly rock gnome with wild white hair sticking out beneath a brass headpiece fitted with rotating ocular magnifying loupes. His left arm from the shoulder down is a marvel of miniaturized interlocking gold and adamantine gears, ending in delicate articulated clockwork calipers. He wears a heavy leather apron with dozens of specialized screwdrivers, oil vials, and tuning forks.
* **Personality**: Fast-talking, mathematically obsessed, and relentlessly optimistic despite the constant risk of chronological collapse. He speaks in rapid cadences, often measuring conversation in "seconds of optimal efficiency." He treats the Prime Engine as a living elder patriarch that needs gentle, constant maintenance.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Torin fights using a combination of high-tier chronomancy and deployable clockwork contraptions. He manipulates the initiative order, slows enemies down to a crawl, and can reverse recent wounds by rewinding personal time.

* **Gnome Cunning**: Torin has advantage on all Intelligence, Wisdom, and Charisma saving throws against magic.
* **Temporal Calibration**: At the start of each of his turns, Torin can choose to adjust his place in the initiative order by up to 10 points (higher or lower), taking effect on the following round.
* **Rewind Mechanism (1/Day)**: When Torin would drop to 0 hit points, his mechanical heart instantly reverses his physical state to what it was at the start of his previous turn, restoring 40 hit points and teleporting him 15 feet.
* **Multiattack**: Torin makes two attacks with his *Chronal Wrench* or makes one wrench attack and casts one spell.
* **Chronal Wrench**: Notable combat maneuver and signature technique.
* **Galvanic Gear-Burst (Recharge 5–6)**: Torin launches an explosive cluster of energized clockwork gears in a 20-foot radius centered on a point within 60 feet. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) lightning and slashing damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
* **Spellcasting**: Torin is an 11th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save saving throw, +9 to hit with spell attacks):.
* **Paradox Interruption**: When an attacker hits Torin with an attack roll, Torin can force the attacker to roll an additional d20 and take the lower result.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Engine's Murmur:** Torin believes that the Prime Engine is slowly re-aligning itself with the original cosmic weave. He is eager to acquire authentic Shard remnants from other planes to test their resonance against the central escapement.
* **Quest Patron:** Torin frequently hires capable adventurers to venture into dangerous high-speed gear tiers to retrieve slipped components or neutralize temporal parasites (such as chrono-scorpions).
* **Quirky Demeanor:** When nervous or deep in thought, Torin absentmindedly oils his fingers or adjusts the tiny springs in his pocketwatches, humming rhythmic mechanical scales.
