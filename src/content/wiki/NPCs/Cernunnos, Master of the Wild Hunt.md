# Cernunnos, Master of the Wild Hunt

![[cernunnos_wild_hunt.jpg]]

* **Race**: Archfey (Primordial Horned Sovereign of the Wild Hunt & Ancient Beasts)
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Wild Hunt|The Wild Hunt]] (Supreme Master)
* **Role**: Sovereign Huntsman / Lord of the Moonlit Chase / Warden of Primal Law
* **Appearance**: A towering, primeval titan of the wild woods, standing eight feet tall. Cernunnos possesses weathered, ruggedly handsome features framed by a braided moss-brown beard and mane. From his brow erupt sweeping stag antlers spanning nine feet, entwined with dry autumn oak leaves, mistletoe, and faintly glowing starlight runes. He is clad in armor fashioned of mammoth bone, dire wolf pelts, and hardened leather engraved with primordial runes. At his side run three spectral faerie hounds with glowing green eyes. In his massive hands he grips *Sylvan-Bane*, a nine-foot barbed hunting spear carved from ancient bone and wreathed in crackling green sylvan lightning.
* **Personality**: Primal, fierce, unyielding, yet bound by the oldest and purest code of honor in the multiverse. Cernunnos laughs at court politics, silks, and honeyed lies; he respects only the truth of the chase, the courage of the quarry, and the sacred balance between life, predation, and death. He is never needlessly cruel, but he shows zero mercy to those who break sacred oaths or defile the ancient sanctums of nature.

---

## Combat Profile

Cernunnos is a devastating physical predator and battlefield commander. He leads from the front, charging with his phantom hounds and hurling lightning-charged spears that impale legendary beasts and planar titans alike.

### **Cernunnos, Master of the Wild Hunt**
*Large Fey (Archfey), Chaotic Neutral*
* **Armor Class**: 21 (Bone-Forged Armor & Primal Hide)
* **Hit Points**: 320 (25d10 + 175)
* **Speed**: 50 ft.

#### **Traits**
* **Apex Predator**: Cernunnos cannot be surprised, ignores difficult terrain, and can track any marked quarry across planar boundaries.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Cernunnos fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
* **Aura of the Hunt**: Allies within 60 feet of Cernunnos have advantage on attack rolls against any creature that has taken damage since the end of its last turn.
* **Innate Spellcasting**: Spellcasting ability is Wisdom (Spell Save DC 23, +15 to hit with spell attacks). Prepared spells:
  * At will: *Hunter's Mark (at 5th level), Pass without Trace, Misty Step, Speak with Animals*
  * 3/day each: *Conjure Fey (at 7th level), Destructive Wave, Freedom of Movement, Lightning Bolt*
  * 1/day each: *Storm of Vengeance, Earthquake, Foresight*

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Cernunnos makes three attacks with his Great Hunting Spear *Sylvan-Bane* or casts one spell and makes one spear attack.
* **Sylvan-Bane (Barbed Spear)**: *Melee or Ranged Weapon Attack*: +16 to hit, reach 10 ft. or range 60/180 ft., one target. *Hit*: 22 (3d8 + 9) piercing damage plus 18 (4d8) lightning damage. If thrown, the spear instantly teleports back into Cernunnos's hand.
* **Call of the Horn (Recharge 5–6)**: Cernunnos sounds his hunting horn. A concussive blast of sonic force tears through a 60-foot cone. Each creature must make a DC 23 Constitution saving throw, taking 54 (12d8) thunder damage and being knocked prone and deafened for 1 minute on a failed save, or half damage on a success. In addition, 2 Spectral Faerie Hounds appear in unoccupied spaces within 30 feet.

#### **Legendary Actions**
Cernunnos can take 3 legendary actions:
* **Hunter's Leap**: Cernunnos leaps up to 40 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.
* **Thrust of Sylvan-Bane**: Cernunnos makes one melee attack with his spear.
* **Command the Pack (Costs 2 Actions)**: All hounds of the Wild Hunt within 60 feet can immediately make one bite attack against an enemy within reach.

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Code of the Quarry**: If a party or mortal NPC is marked as the Quarry, Cernunnos will not assassinate them in their sleep. He gives them a head start of one hour, sounds his horn three times, and begins the chase. If the quarry survives until sunrise, Cernunnos honors their triumph and rewards them with a legendary sylvan boon.
* **Guardian of the Ancient Tapestry**: Cernunnos views the Shattered Loom as the cosmic heart of the great forest. Those who try to unravel it are poaching the life of the universe, and will find themselves pursued by the full fury of the Wild Hunt.
