# 👁️ The Watcher in the Deep
### *High Oracle of the Submerged Ziggurat, Matriarch of the Dreambound, Voice of the Slumbering Eye*

> *"You speak of truth as though it were a coin to be counted. Truth in Ry'leh is a liquid that drowns all who attempt to drink it. Open your gills, dry-lander, or choke on the dream."*  
> — **The Watcher in the Deep, addressing interplanar pilgrims**

---

## 📜 Identity & History

The **Watcher in the Deep** is an ancient, aberrant cephaloid matriarch who serves as the living conduit between the slumbering **Great Dreamer** and the mortal sects dwelling within the **City of Ry'leh**. Resting within the **Chamber of Eyes (Tier 2 of the Submerged Ziggurat)**, she orchestrates the collection of Starlight Brine, interprets the nocturnal convulsions of the entity below, and guards the lower gates against those seeking to disrupt the Somnambulic Loom.

* **Title**: High Oracle of the Ziggurat, Matriarch of the Dreambound.
* **Habitat**: The Chamber of Eyes, The Submerged Ziggurat, City of Ry'leh.
* **Creature Type**: Huge Aberration (Psion), Neutral Evil.
* **Faction Role**: Supreme Prophet of [[Factions/The Dreambound of Ry'leh|The Dreambound of Ry'leh]].
* **Associated Rules**: [[Custom Rules/The Eldritch Madness & Dream-Fracture Ruleset|Lucidity Index & Dream-Fracture Mechanics]].

---

## 🎨 Appearance & Aura

The Watcher appears as an immense, semi-translucent cephalopoid entity over 25 feet across, adorned with iridescent chitin plates that shimmer with shifting violet and deep turquoise bio-luminescence. In place of standard eyes, her domed mantle is studded with forty-nine human-like eyes that weep glowing astral oil into the brine around her. Six muscular primary tentacles terminate in segmented, delicate appendages capable of scribing complex arcane equations or tearing iron plating apart.

A perpetual cloud of sparkling **Starlight Brine** surrounds her, refracting light into impossible angles and whispering fragments of lost planar memories to all within hearing range.

### Traits

* **Amphibious**: The Watcher can breathe both air and water.
* **Aura of the Many-Eyed**: Any hostile creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of the Watcher must make a DC 19 Wisdom saving throw. On a failure, the creature takes 16 (3d10) psychic damage and suffers disadvantage on attack rolls against the Watcher until the start of its next turn.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If the Watcher fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
* **Psionic Mastery**: The Watcher's spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save DC 21, +13 to hit with spell attacks). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:
  * At will: *detect thoughts*, *levitate*, *mage hand* (invisible), *mind sliver* (4d6)
  * 3/day each: *synaptic static*, *telekinesis*, *hold monster*, *counterspell* (at 5th level)
  * 1/day each: *feeblemind*, *plane shift* (self and 4 willing allies only)

### Actions

* **Multiattack**: The Watcher uses *Mind Lash* and makes three Tentacle strikes.
* **Tentacle**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +12 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. *Hit*: 19 (3d8 + 6) bludgeoning damage plus 11 (2d10) psychic damage. The target is grappled (escape DC 18). The Watcher can grapple up to four targets simultaneously.
* **Mind Lash**: *Ranged Spell Attack*: +13 to hit, range 120 ft., one creature. *Hit*: 29 (4d10 + 7) psychic damage, and the target must succeed on a DC 21 Intelligence saving throw or lose **10 Lucidity** (under [[Custom Rules/The Eldritch Madness & Dream-Fracture Ruleset|Dream-Fracture Rules]]) and be stunned until the end of its next turn.
* **Bile of the Dream (Recharge 5–6)**: The Watcher expels a 60-foot cone of bioluminescent psionic brine. Each creature in that area must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes 54 (12d8) acid plus psychic damage, is blinded for 1 minute, and loses **15 Lucidity**. On a successful save, it takes half damage and is not blinded.

### Legendary Actions (3 Actions/Round)

* **Tentacle Swipe**: The Watcher makes one Tentacle attack.
* **Psionic Jaunt (Costs 2 Actions)**: The Watcher teleports up to 40 feet to an unoccupied space she can see, releasing a psychic burst. Each creature within 10 feet of her destination must succeed on a DC 19 Wisdom save or take 14 (4d6) psychic damage.
* **Gaze of the Oracle (Costs 2 Actions)**: One creature within 60 feet must make a DC 21 Wisdom saving throw or reveal its innermost thoughts and vulnerabilities to the Watcher, granting all Dreambound cultists advantage on attack rolls against it for 1 minute.

## 🎭 Roleplay & Interaction

* **Motivation**: Maintain the delicate equilibrium of the Great Dreamer's slumber. She views waking the entity as the ultimate tragedy, but welcomes those who offer psychic tribute or ancient relics of the Shattered Loom.
* **Voice**: Echoing, multidirectional telepathy that sounds like multiple voices speaking the same sentence with subtle harmonic delays.
* **Allies & Enmities**: Commands the [[Factions/The Dreambound of Ry'leh|Dreambound of Ry'leh]]; regards dry-land empires as fragile daydreams destined to dissolve into the thought-sea.
