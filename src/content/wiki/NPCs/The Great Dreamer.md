# 🐙 The Great Dreamer
### *The Somnolent Colossus, Weaver of the Un-Woven, He Who Slumbers in the Non-Euclidean Depths*

> *"Do not fear that the Dreamer will awaken to destroy our world. Fear rather that when he awakes, our world will simply cease to have ever been dreamed at all."*  
> — **Archivist Mauren Coldsteel, Fragments of the Starlight Ledger**

---

## 👁️ Overview & Primordial Identity

The **Great Dreamer** is the metaphysical keystone of the extradimensional city of **Ry'leh**. Neither fully awake nor entirely deceased, this titanic primordial aberration rests in the deepest abyss beneath the **Submerged Ziggurat**. Its consciousness does not occupy three-dimensional space so much as it emanates reality itself; the sprawling stone spires, non-Euclidean angles, and thought-seas of Ry'leh are nothing more than physical calcifications of its slumbering neural architecture.

* **Epithets**: The Somnolent Colossus, The Sleeping Sovereign, The Eye in the Brine, The Unmaker of Loom-Threads.
* **Habitat & Domain**: Sanctum of the Sleeper (Tier 5, The Submerged Ziggurat), City of Ry'leh.
* **Alignment**: Chaotic Neutral (Operates entirely beyond conventional mortal morality).
* **Affiliated Factions**: [[Factions/The Dreambound of Ry'leh|The Dreambound of Ry'leh]], The Starlight Syndicate, The Deep Slumber Priests.
* **Relevant Ruleset**: [[Custom Rules/The Eldritch Madness & Dream-Fracture Ruleset|The Eldritch Madness & Dream-Fracture Ruleset (Lucidity System)]].

## 🎨 Visual & Conceptual Manifestation

### The Somnolent Colossus
The Great Dreamer measures over 120 feet in recumbent length, curled within a caldera of pale, soft memory-stone. Its flesh is not organic skin, but a shifting **tapestry of sleeping minds**—millions of translucent humanoid and inhuman faces pressed against the surface from within. Their expressions drift through ecstasy, horror, and delirium. When the Dreamer shifts in its torpor, the faces slide across one another in wet, silent currents.

### The Crown of Unspooled Thought
Instead of a cranial skull, the Dreamer's apex blooms into an intricate halo of **solidified moonlight neural architecture**—dendrites, axons, and glowing Loom-filaments spanning hundreds of feet. Memories, forgotten prophecies, and suppressed nightmares manifest as bioluminescent jellyfish within this crown before bursting into ephemeral physical reality.

### The Veils of Unmaking
Four titanic, undulating wings unfurl from its dorsal ridge, each woven from a fundamental category of mortal dreaming:
1. **The Veil of Desire**: Pale rose silk, smelling of jasmine and warm copper.
2. **The Veil of Terror**: Bruised violet chitin, freezing the air with the chill of grave-earth.
3. **The Veil of Nostalgia**: Sepia-gold filament, heavy and sweet like melted amber.
4. **The Veil of Prophecy**: Polished mercury, reflecting futures that inflict psychic trauma upon those who gaze upon them.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

The Great Dreamer is a primordial slumbering consciousness whose dormant thoughts distort the geometry of reality and warp the sanity of all who draw near. It defends itself through reality-folding psychic waves, sleep-induction fields, and spontaneous aberrant manifestations.

---

## 🎭 Roleplaying The Great Dreamer

* **Tone & Persona**: Detached, colossal, poetic, and terrifyingly indifferent. The Dreamer does not hate mortals; it is simply dreaming them.
* **Communication**: Never uses mundane words. Speaks through synesthetic sensations—the scent of rain before a lightning strike, the memory of childhood guilt, or geometric equations echoing behind the eyelids.
* **Tactical Behavior**: The Dreamer rarely focuses on a single foe; it treats combat as a restless sleeper swatting at bothersome gnats. It constantly alters the battlefield using *Warp Reality* to test the party's adaptability.
