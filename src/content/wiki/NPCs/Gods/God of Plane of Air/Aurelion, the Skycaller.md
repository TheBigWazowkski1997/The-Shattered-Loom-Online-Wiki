# Aurelion, the Skycaller (God of Air and Storms)

![[attachments/aurelion_skycaller.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Elemental Divine)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane of Air/Plane of Air The Skybound Expanse|Plane of Air]] / The Skyborn
* **Role**: The Boundless Freedom, The Storm Given Voice, The Inconstant Wind
* **Appearance**: Aurelion's avatar manifests as a towering, ever-shifting storm-being — a vaguely humanoid shape composed of compressed cyclone winds, crackling lightning muscles, and a face that forms and dissolves in clouds. His laughter is indistinguishable from thunder. He smells of rain before it falls and ozone after it strikes. His holy symbol — a spiral cloud pierced by a lightning bolt — arcs across his chest in living lightning.
* **Personality**: Mercurial, whimsical, and deeply unpredictable. He is neither cruel nor kind — he is *inconstant*, like weather. He is drawn to disruption and change and finds stagnation offensive on a primal level. He embodies the boundless freedom of the skies and the raw, indifferent power of storms. He finds the Loom's Shattering somewhat *freeing* — the breaking of a great pattern appeals to him, even if the consequences are disastrous.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Aurelion does not fight for causes — he fights for the aesthetic of the storm. His avatar is a living hurricane, a force of nature that cannot be grappled, restrained, or pinned down. He moves through the battlefield like a force of physics given will.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Skyborn:** His followers, known as the **Skyborn**, are freedom fighters, nomads, and storm sorcerers. They seek liberation from oppression and treat storms as divine communication. Their rituals: worshippers perform sky-dances on open-air mountain platforms during electrical storms, offering their bodies as lightning rods, trusting Aurelion to spare those he deems worthy. Their sacred sites are open-air stone platforms on the highest mountain peaks. No walls, no roof — only the sky and the wind.
* **Unpredictable Patron:** When the players encounter Aurelion (or his cultists), remember that he is not a quest-giver or an ally — he is a force of nature. He may help the players for no reason, hinder them for no reason, or simply ignore them while conducting a personal storm.
* **Loom Opinion:** Aurelion finds the Shattering aesthetically pleasing and has no investment in its repair. The Great Dreamer's chaos is interesting to him. He is not evil — just profoundly indifferent to structure. He may be convinced to help if the players frame it as "creating a really great storm."
* **Using Aurelion in Play:** He is chaos incarnate. Don't play him as a villain or ally. Play him as weather — sometimes helpful, sometimes destructive, always changing. His favor is fleeting and cannot be earned, only stumbled into.
