# Grelthor, the Golden Hoard (God of Avarice)

![[attachments/grelthor_golden_hoard.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Draconic Divine)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Avarice/Plane of Avarice The Gilded Eternity|Plane of Avarice]] / The Gilded Hands
* **Role**: The First Transaction, The Ultimate Merchant, The Dragon Who Owns the Vault
* **Appearance**: Grelthor's avatar is a grotesque amalgam of dragon and plutocrat — a massive reptilian form with scales made of overlapping gold coins, claws of compressed treasure, and a belly that glows with the light of a million hoarded gems. He wears the ruined crowns of every king who ever worshipped him as trophies on a bandolier. His holy symbol — a hand clutching a coin so tightly it bleeds gold — is branded into his forehead.
* **Personality**: Charming, acquisitive, and utterly transactional. He does not want destruction — he wants *acquisition*. Everything has a price. Everyone has a price. He is delighted to discover what yours is. Grelthor was born from the first transaction made in the multiverse — the moment one being gave something to another in exchange for something else. Over eons, the weight of all accumulated greed coalesced into his form. He views the Shattering as an enormous business opportunity — with the Loom broken, the value of stability has skyrocketed, and he intends to own it.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Grelthor is the ultimate merchant — even in combat, he calculates the value of every action. His avatar is a dragon of living treasure, breathing molten gold and dealing in souls. He prefers to negotiate, but when forced to fight, he buys victory with overwhelming force.

* **Price of Everything**: At the start of combat, Grelthor identifies the single thing each creature values most (automatically, no roll). He can use this knowledge as a bonus action to impose disadvantage on that creature's attack rolls for 1 round as he threatens what they value.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Grelthor fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
* **Multiattack**: Grelthor makes two Claw attacks and one Bite attack.
* **Claw**: Notable combat maneuver and signature technique.
* **Bite**: Notable combat maneuver and signature technique.
* **Gold Breath (Recharge 5–6)**: Grelthor breathes a torrent of molten gold in a 60-foot cone. Each creature must succeed on a saving throw, taking significant damage on a failure, or half on a success. On a failure, the creature is also encased in rapidly cooling gold — restrained until a effort (action) breaks free.
* **Appraise (1 Action)**: Grelthor names a price for one creature's soul. The creature must make a saving throw or be charmed for 1 minute, unable to attack Grelthor.
* **Coin Storm (2 Actions)**: Grelthor flings golden coins in a 30-foot radius. Each creature must succeed on saving throw or take significant damage.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Ultimate Merchant:** Grelthor will deal with the players — but everything has a cost. He will never be gratuitously hostile unless attacked. He prefers to negotiate. He may offer to sell them information about the Loom Fragments — at the cost of something deeply personal (a memory, a relationship, a piece of their soul).
* **The Gilded Hands:** His worshippers are merchants and bankers who pray to Grelthor for profit and offer him a tithe of their gains. They believe wealth is the only real form of power. Their sacred sites are great treasure vaults and commodity exchanges. The central artifact is the **Scale of Infinite Worth** — a balance that weighs the "true value" of any object or soul.
* **The Shattering as a Market:** He has stockpiled Loom Fragments in his vaults as commodities. He is waiting for the right moment to auction them to the highest bidder. Players who can outbid everyone else — or offer something he hasn't already acquired — may walk away with a Shard.
* **Using Grelthor in Play:** He is a patron, a fence, a quest-giver — not a villain. He wants the Loom repaired because a stable multiverse has better trade routes. But he'll charge for every thread rewoven.
