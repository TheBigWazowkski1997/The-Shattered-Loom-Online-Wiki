# Terridon, the Unyielding Pillar (God of Earth and Endurance)

![[attachments/terridon_unyielding_pillar.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Primordial Elemental)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane of Earth/Plane of Earth The Unyielding Depths|Plane of Earth]] / The Deep Keepers
* **Role**: The Principle of Persistence, First Architect, The Mountain Given Will
* **Appearance**: Terridon's avatar is not a humanoid form — he is a mountain given will. A towering mass of basalt and granite, 30 feet tall, with veins of molten iron running through him like blood. His "face" is a fractured cliff face whose geological strata record millions of years of history. His voice is seismic — felt in the chest before it is heard with the ears. His holy symbol, a single standing stone pillar on an unbroken plain, is etched into his chest in veins of gold.
* **Personality**: Immovable, patient, and deeply traditional. He does not rush, he does not change his mind, and he does not forgive easily. He is not cruel — he is simply geological in his time scale. He views the Shattering as a geological event — regrettable, but ultimately temporary on his timescale. He values permanence above all; a vow made in his presence is held to for eternity.

Terridon is one of the oldest beings in the multiverse — possibly predating the Loom itself. He is the principle of *persistence*: the idea that matter, pressed hard enough and long enough, becomes permanent. ## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Slow Ally:** Terridon is not hostile unless his plane is directly threatened. Earning his trust requires demonstrating patience — waiting him out, speaking plainly, not trying to rush him. He values permanence; if the players make a vow in his presence, he will hold them to it for eternity.
* **The Deep Keepers:** His worshippers are dwarves, gnomes, and earth-shapers who revere Terridon as the first architect. They carve their prayers into stone and leave them forever. Their sacred sites are ancient standing stone circles and deep underground cathedrals of natural crystal formation.
* **The Anti-Chaos Force:** He is a natural enemy of the Great Dreamer and Pale Night. The dissolution of reality is, to him, a personal offense. He views the Loom's Shattering as a fracture that will eventually heal through pressure and time — if the fragments are properly aligned.
* **Using Terridon in Play:** He should feel like a force of nature, not a person. Conversations with him take place over days (his perception of time). He may give a quest that takes generations to complete, expecting the players to lay the groundwork for their descendants.
