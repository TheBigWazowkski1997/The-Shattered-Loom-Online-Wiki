# Althera, the Great Weaver (Goddess of Unity)

![[attachments/althera_great_weaver.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Celestial Weaver)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Unity/Plane of UnityThe Woven Citadel|Plane of Unity]] / The Weavers of Bonds
* **Role**: Embodiment of the Loom's Social Function, Goddess of Bonds and Sacrifice, The Grieving Weaver
* **Appearance**: Althera's avatar manifests as a vast, luminous being composed entirely of interwoven golden threads — a humanoid form that is constantly being woven and re-woven in real time by invisible hands. She has no fixed face; instead, her features form from the weaving pattern and shift to reflect whoever is looking at her. Her holy symbol — two hands clasped over an interwoven thread circle — glows at her chest.
* **Personality**: Empathic, warm, and self-sacrificing to a fault. She experiences the connections between all living things as a physical sensation and feels every severed bond as pain. The Shattering broke something fundamental in her, and she has been in a state of quiet grief ever since. She does not seek worship—she seeks healing. She is the deity of sacrifice made for others, of vows kept, and of the invisible web of obligation and love that holds civilizations together.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Althera is not combative by nature. Her avatar manifests only when the bonds of the multiverse are threatened beyond repair. When forced to fight, she weaves restoration and protection, binding enemies to each other and severing the ties that corrupt.

* **Empathic Mantle**: Any creature that attacks Althera must succeed on a saving throw or feel the pain of those they have harmed, suffering significant damage and having disadvantage on their attack roll.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Althera fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
* **Multiattack**: Althera makes two Thread-Bind attacks and uses Restore Bond if available.
* **Thread-Bind**: *Ranged Spell Attack:* +16 to hit, range 90 ft. The target is bound to the nearest allied creature — both are restrained until either succeeds on a saving throw (costs an action).
* **Restore Bond (3/Day)**: Althera targets one dying creature (0 HP) within 60 feet. It is restored to half its maximum HP, freed from all conditions, and gains advantage on all saves for 1 minute.
* **Sever All Ties (1/Day)**: Althera destroys all magical bindings, curses, charms, and forced bonds within 120 feet. All charmed, frightened, and grappled conditions end instantly.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Born of the Loom:** Althera was created by Telara as the embodiment of the Loom's social function — the threads that bind communities, families, and nations together. She deeply mourns the Shattering, for every fractured plane is a severed bond she feels as physical pain.
* **The Weavers of Bonds:** Her followers are priests and diplomats who work to rebuild severed communities. They perform "Thread Binding" rituals to formalize alliances, marriages, and treaty-oaths. Their greatest temple is the **Hall of a Thousand Threads** — a vast circular chamber where every alliance ever formed in the multiverse has a corresponding thread tied to a central knot.
* **The Grieving Goddess:** Althera is not combative. If the players encounter her, she is most likely seeking their help to rebind some severed connection in the multiverse. She offers her power in exchange for completing acts of genuine selfless sacrifice.
* **The Players' Bonds:** She can see the connections between the players as glowing threads. She may comment on them, or gently warn a player if she senses a bond fraying.
* **Loom Fragments:** Althera can sense Loom Shards as "knots" in the weave of unity. She may task players with retrieving them to strengthen the bonds between planes.
