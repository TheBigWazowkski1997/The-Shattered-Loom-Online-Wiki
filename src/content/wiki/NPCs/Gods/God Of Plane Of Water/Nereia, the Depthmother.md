# Nereia, the Depthmother (Goddess of Water and Memory)

![[attachments/nereia_depthmother.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Primordial Elemental)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane Of Water/Plane of Water The Azure Expanse|Plane of Water]] / The Tidecallers
* **Role**: Keeper of the Primal Deep, Mother of All Waters, The Memory Keeper
* **Appearance**: Nereia's avatar is a colossal being of living water and bioluminescent deep-sea life. She has the upper body of an impossibly beautiful woman made of rushing water, and below the waist her form dissolves into a vast spiral of ocean current. Her hair is a living reef of coral and luminous deep-sea creatures. Her voice sounds like water over stones and the deep resonant groans of whale song. Her holy symbol — a cresting wave encircling a glowing pearl — floats beside her, pulsing with the tides.
* **Personality**: Ancient, patient, and deeply maternal. She views all living things as children of the ocean's first waters, and feels responsible for their wellbeing in the way that a great mother feels responsible for her youngest children finding their way home. She existed before the Loom, in the primal deep. When Telara wove the first threads, they floated upon Nereia's waters, and she recognized the Weaver as kin. She is one of the few deities who genuinely mourns the Shattering not for power reasons but for personal ones — she felt every living thing's connection to the ocean fray when the Loom broke.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Nereia prefers not to fight. She is a healer, a preserver, a listener. But when the waters of her plane are threatened — when the Loom's fracturing poisons the deep — she rises as the Depthmother, and the ocean itself becomes her weapon.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Memory Keeper:** Nereia preserves the memories of the Loom before the Shattering in the waters of her plane. Players who dive into the Abyssal Pearl's waters might experience visions of the pre-Shattering multiverse.
* **The Tidecallers:** Her worshippers are priests and sailors who communicate with Nereia through the rhythms of tides and dream-visions that come during deep sleep near water. Their greatest temple is the **Abyssal Pearl** — a luminous structure at the bottom of the plane's endless sea.
* **The Gentle Giant:** Unlike many gods, Nereia will not fight if she can avoid it. She prefers to talk, to listen, and to understand. A player who sits with her and speaks truthfully about their fears will receive something of genuine value — a memory, a vision, a blessing of the deep.
* **Relationship with Telara:** She recognized Telara as kin when the Weaver first spun the Loom upon her waters. She mourns Telara's fragmentation as a personal loss. She is one of the few gods who might know where the largest fragments of Telara's consciousness reside.
* **Using Nereia in Play:** She is a sanctuary, not a boss. Players who seek her out find healing, wisdom, and a moment of peace in a shattered multiverse. Combat with her should only occur if players attack her or desecrate her plane.
