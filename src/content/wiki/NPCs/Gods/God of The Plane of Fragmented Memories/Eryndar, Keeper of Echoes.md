# Eryndar, Keeper of Echoes (God of Fragmented Memories)

![[attachments/eryndar_keeper_of_echoes.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Psychic Echo Given Form)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Fragmented memories/Plane of Fragmented Memories - The Shardlands|Plane of Fragmented Memories]] / The Echo Seekers
* **Role**: Guardian of Lost Truths, Archivist of the Unremembered, The Shattered Mirror
* **Appearance**: Eryndar manifests as a tall, androgynous figure composed of shifting crystalline shards—each fragment reflecting a different memory, a different face, a different moment in history. Where a face should be, there is a shattered mirror reflecting an open eye (his holy symbol). His form constantly reassembles and fractures, never settling into a single shape. He wears robes woven from condensed starlight and forgotten whispers. When he moves, the sound is like wind through chimes made of glass.
* **Personality**: Detached, enigmatic, and utterly patient. Eryndar speaks in riddles and fragmented visions, rarely offering direct guidance—because to him, all answers are already present in the echoes, if one knows how to listen. He does not distinguish between "important" and "trivial" memories; a child's lost toy and a god's dying secret are equally preserved in his archive. He believes that preserving even the smallest fragments of history can guide the future. He is sought by scholars, oracles, and those seeking answers to forgotten mysteries, but few leave his plane unchanged.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Eryndar does not fight in the traditional sense—his plane *is* his defense, a labyrinth of crystallized memories where intruders lose themselves in echoes of the past. When forced to manifest an avatar to protect a vital memory or prevent a truth from being erased entirely, he becomes the **Keeper of Echoes**, a being who rewrites the present with the weight of the past.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Shardlands:** Eryndar's plane, the Plane of Fragmented Memories, is a cavernous realm lined with glowing crystals—each containing a memory from across the multiverse. The **Echo Seekers** (scholars, diviners, mourners) meditate before crystalline altars, attempting to commune with fragmented memories. They perform ceremonies where they share personal secrets, which are symbolically "stored" in shards of enchanted crystal.
* **The Loom and Memory:** Eryndar was born from the Shattering itself—when the Loom broke, the memories of what *was* coalesced into a god. He is the Loom's scar tissue, preserving what was lost so it might someday be rewoven. He does not seek to repair the Loom; he *is* the preservation that makes repair possible.
* **Information Broker of the Gods:** Eryndar knows secrets about every deity, every Loom Shard, every Mark of Telara. He trades in memories. Players who visit his plane can ask one question per visit—but the price is always a memory of equal value. A happy childhood memory for the location of a Loom Shard. The memory of a loved one's face for the weakness of a god.
* **Not an Ally, Not an Enemy:** Eryndar has no stake in the war over the Loom. He will help the Pale Lodge, the Cult of the Broken Thread, or the players—whichever offers the most *interesting* memory. He finds the players' Marks of Telara fascinating; they are "living memories" of a goddess he never met.
* **Using Eryndar in Play:** He is best used as an oracle the players must petition. The journey to his plane is a dungeon crawl through crystallized memories (combat encounters are relived traumas given form). The reward is a truth that changes the campaign. He should never be a straight combat encounter unless the players try to destroy his archive.
