# Kaldryn, the Eternal Artisan (God of Craft and Creation)

![[attachments/kaldryn_eternal_artisan.jpeg]]



* **Race**: Greater Deity (Celestial Artificer)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Eternal Craft/Plane of Eternal Craft Forgeflame Expanse|Plane of Eternal Craft Forgeflame Expanse]] / The Forgemasters
* **Role**: Patron of Endless Creation, The Living Forge, The Divine Craftsman
* **Appearance**: Kaldryn's avatar manifests as a colossal dwarf-like figure standing twenty feet tall, composed entirely of molten metal and living fire contained within plates of impossibly perfect alloy. His beard is a cascade of cooling metal filaments that glow cherry-red at their tips. His eyes are furnaces, and when he speaks, his words leave visible heat distortions in the air. His holy symbol — a blazing forge with crossed hammers over an unfinished masterwork — burns at the center of his chest.
* **Personality**: Meticulous, demanding, and relentlessly driven. He does not acknowledge failure — only "iterations toward perfection." He views every imperfect creation as a personal insult to the divine gift of craft. He was one of the first entities to embrace Telara's Loom, viewing its threads as the ultimate pattern to be woven into physical form. When the Loom shattered, his response was not despair but fury — he set his entire plane to work, believing that enough perfect craftsmanship can physically reconstruct the Loom's framework from the material plane upward.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Kaldryn does not intervene directly in mortal affairs unless the Colossal Forge or the plane itself is threatened. If summoned or attacked, he manifests as the Living Forge — a devastating martial and elemental force.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Loom and the Forge:** Kaldryn believes physical creation is the only true path to repairing the Loom. He funds the Colossal Forge and views the Gilded Caliper as his mortal instrument. If the players are trying to use the Forge for purification, he approves — but demands it be done with precision and respect.
* **The Forgemasters:** His worshippers are elite artisan-devotees who dedicate their lives to crafting legendary objects. They believe each masterwork object strengthens the Loom's fraying threads by adding a new, stable pattern to reality. They perform "Forge Vigils" — working tirelessly for days without sleep to create a single flawless piece of art. The finished object is offered to Kaldryn's flame and consumed; its "pattern" is said to join the Loom directly.
* **Displeasure at Sabotage:** The Cult of the Broken Thread's sabotage of the Molten Veins is, to Kaldryn, a personal attack. He may communicate through the Forge fires, leaving soot-written commands on the walls of the Molten Veins.
* **Conflict with Pale Night:** Kaldryn is actively hostile to the concept of the Great Dreamer and the Cult of the Broken Thread. He views entropy as a creative failure.
* **Sacred Sites:** Massive forges powered by divine fire serve as Kaldryn's temples. Each has a central **Eternal Anvil** where items of immense power are forged. The greatest of these — the **Colossal Forge** — is the anchor point of the entire Plane of Eternal Craft.
