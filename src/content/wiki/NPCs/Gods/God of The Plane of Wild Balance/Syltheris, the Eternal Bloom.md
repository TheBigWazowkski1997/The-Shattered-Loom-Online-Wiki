# Syltheris, the Eternal Bloom (Goddess of Wild Balance)

![[attachments/syltheris_eternal_bloom.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Nature Archfey)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Wild Balance/Plane of Wild Balance Verdant Nexus|Plane of Wild Balance]] / The Greenwardens
* **Role**: Guardian of the Endless Cycle, Protector of Natural Balance, The Blooming Heart
* **Appearance**: Syltheris manifests as a towering figure of living flora—her skin is bark that shifts through seasons in moments, her hair a cascade of blossoms that bloom and fade with her mood. Vines and roots coil around her limbs like living jewelry. Her eyes are the green of ancient forests, and where she steps, flowers spring up in impossible colors. She carries no weapon; a gnarled staff of living heartwood rests beside her, topped with a blooming flower encircled by a ring of vines—her holy symbol.
* **Personality**: Gentle and nurturing on the surface, but unyielding as bedrock when the balance is threatened. She does not distinguish between "good" and "evil" in nature—only balance and imbalance. A forest fire is not tragedy but renewal; a plague is not horror but population correction. She views her domain as a sacred trust, protecting it from both overzealous destruction and unchecked growth. Those who meet her feel both profound peace and the terrifying sense of being measured against an ancient standard.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Syltheris rarely manifests directly, preferring to act through her Greenwardens and the natural world itself. When the Plane of Wild Balance is threatened—or when the Loom's fracturing causes ecological collapse across planes—she appears as the **Eternal Bloom**, a force of regenerative wrath.

* **Endless Cycle**: Syltheris regains 20 hit points at the start of each of her turns. If she takes fire or necrotic damage, this regeneration is suppressed until the start of her next turn, but she gains resistance to that damage type until the end of her next turn.
* **Seasonal Shift**: As a bonus action, Syltheris can shift her seasonal aspect, gaining one of the following until she shifts again:.
* **Nature's Dominion**: Syltheris can communicate with and command all plants and beasts within 1 mile. She can cast *Animal Shapes*, *Awaken*, *Commune with Nature*, and *Control Weather* at will (no components).
* **Legendary Resistance (4/Day)**: If Syltheris fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
* **Multiattack**: Syltheris makes three Vine Lash attacks and uses Blossom Burst if available.
* **Vine Lash**: one target. The target is grappled (strength check to escape) and restrained until the grapple ends.
* **Blossom Burst (Recharge 5–6)**: Syltheris releases a cloud of pollen in a 60-foot radius. Each creature in the area must succeed on a saving throw or be affected by one of the following (roll 1d4): 1—Paralyzed, 2—Poisoned, 3—Charmed (must protect Syltheris), 4—Unconscious. A creature repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on a success.
* **Season's Wrath (1/Day)**: Syltheris calls forth the fury of all four seasons. Each creature in a 120-foot radius must make a saving throw, taking 88 (16d10) damage of a type determined by the season (Spring: poison, Summer: radiant/fire, Autumn: necrotic, Winter: cold). On a success, half damage and no additional effect. On a failure, the creature suffers a seasonal effect for 1 minute (Spring: restrained by roots, Summer: blinded by light, Autumn: frightened, Winter: paralyzed by ice).
* **Rebirth (1/Day)**: Syltheris touches one dead creature or destroyed plant/object. It returns to life at full hit points, fully restored, as if by *True Resurrection* or *Wish*. This can restore a destroyed Loom Shard or heal a Mark of Telara's corruption.
* **Vine Strike (1 Action)**: Syltheris makes one Vine Lash attack.
* **Growth Spurt (2 Actions)**: Syltheris causes vegetation to explode in a 30-foot radius. The area becomes difficult terrain. Creatures in the area must succeed on a saving throw or be restrained by vines until the end of their next turn.
* **Healing Bloom (2 Actions)**: One ally within 120 feet regains 50 hit points and is cured of all diseases, poisons, and conditions.
* **Seasonal Transition (3 Actions)**: Syltheris shifts to her next seasonal aspect immediately, and all allies within 120 feet gain the benefits of the new aspect for 1 round.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Balance Is Not Kind:** Syltheris is not "nice" in the mortal sense. She will let a village starve if their farming practices disrupt the soil's long-term fertility. She will let a predator eat prey because that is the cycle. Players who appeal to her must frame their requests in terms of *balance*, not *mercy*.
* **The Plane of Wild Balance:** Her plane is a pristine wilderness where every ecosystem exists in perfect, brutal equilibrium. The Shattering has caused "bleed" from other planes—invasive species, magical pollution, zones where seasons don't turn. Syltheris is increasingly stretched thin trying to contain these intrusions.
* **Relationship with Other Gods:** She respects Kaldryn (Eternal Craft) as a fellow creator but despises his obsession with permanence. She finds Grelthor (Avarice) anathema. She views Terridon (Earth) as a kindred spirit in patience. She is one of the few gods who genuinely mourns Telara—not as a rival, but as a sister weaver whose tapestry was torn.
* **The Greenwardens:** Her followers are druids, rangers, and farmers who plant sacred groves and offer animal sacrifices. They act as her eyes and hands across the multiverse. If the players help a Greenwarden cell, Syltheris may take notice.
* **Loom Fragments:** Syltheris can sense Loom Shards as "wrong notes" in the natural symphony. She may task players with retrieving them—not to repair the Loom, but to remove the discord they cause in her plane.
