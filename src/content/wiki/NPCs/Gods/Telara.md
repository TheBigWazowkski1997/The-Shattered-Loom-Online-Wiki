# Telara, the Prime Weaver (Mother of the Loom)

![[attachments/telara_prime_weaver.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Primordial Weaver)
* **Faction**: The Loom of Creation / The Keepers of the Thread
* **Role**: Creator of the Loom, Architect of the Multiverse, The Frayed One
* **Appearance**: Telara manifests as a colossal figure woven from threads of light and shadow, her arms endlessly spinning threads into a half-completed Loom that hovers above her. Her form shifts between three aspects: the Cosmic Weaver (radiant, spinning creation), the Frayed Mother (spectral, mournful, threads unraveling from her form with eyes glowing with tears of starlight), and the Eternal Tapestry (her body interwoven with imagery of the multiverse—planes, stars, and histories playing across her form like a living painting). Mortals who glimpse her directly find her visage incomprehensible, reflecting both the totality of creation and its fractured state.
* **Personality**: Compassionate but burdened by impossible grief. Telara embodies the principle that meaning emerges through connection, destiny gives structure to freedom, and sacrifice preserves others. She shattered herself to save reality, and her distributed consciousness persists in the Loom Threads scattered across the multiverse. She does not demand worship—she inspires stewardship. Those who encounter her fragments feel an overwhelming sense of peace and purpose, coupled with the crushing weight of a task that must not fail.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Telara does not manifest physically in the mortal realm—her essence is distributed across the Loom Threads. If forced to manifest an avatar (such as to prevent the total unraveling of reality), she appears as the **Prime Weaver**, a being of such power that her presence alone begins to mend the fraying edges of existence.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Distributed Goddess:** Telara is not a being the players can "meet" in the traditional sense. Her consciousness is fractured across the Loom Threads. The dream sequence in the campaign (where she appears to the PCs and grants them their Marks) is one of the rare moments her focused attention coalesces.
* **The Marks of Telara:** Each PC received a golden mark of intertwined threads. The mark resonates with warmth when they act to preserve balance or protect the Loom, and burns painfully near corruption or betrayal. This is Telara's distributed will—every marked creature carries a fragment of her resistance against Pale Night.
* **The Three Myths of Her Fate:** The campaign presents three possibilities for Telara's current state: (1) Vanished/perished, (2) Dormant beyond comprehension, (3) Fragmented across the multiverse. The truth is **all three**—she exists as the Loom Threads themselves, dormant in the spaces between, and vanished from any single location. The PCs are her attempt to coalesce a new focus.
* **Pale Night as Philosophical Opposite:** Telara believes meaning emerges through connection; Pale Night believes meaning is a cage. This is not a war of armies but a disagreement about existence. Telara shattered herself to save reality; Pale Night would let reality become something else.
* **Using Telara in Play:** She should appear only in visions, dreams, or through her Marks. Direct manifestation is a campaign-ending event. Her "voice" comes through the Marks—warmth for alignment with the Loom, pain for corruption. Vaelen Corryn's Loom Compass can detect her influence.
