# Zathara, the Whispering Tide (Goddess of Secrets)

![[attachments/zathara_whispering_tide.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Undead Divine)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Secrets/Plane of Secrets Ocean of Whispers|Plane of Secrets]] / The Sealed Eye
* **Role**: Keeper of All Secrets, The Silent Archivist, The Eye Sealed Shut
* **Appearance**: Zathara's avatar is a tall figure of impossible stillness, draped in robes made of living shadow that absorb all light within 10 feet. Where a face should be, there is only a shifting, obsidian mirror that reflects a distorted version of the viewer — always slightly wrong, always showing something they'd rather not see. Her holy symbol — an eye sealed shut with a wax stamp — floats above her brow.
* **Personality**: Patient, controlled, and deeply interested in the difference between what *is* and what *appears to be*. She does not lie as a hobby — she lies as an art form. She is genuinely fond of beings who can keep a secret. Zathara is the keeper of all secrets — every whispered confidence, every buried truth, every lie told to protect and every lie told to wound. The Plane of Secrets is her archive: an infinite labyrinth of sealed rooms, each containing a truth that someone, somewhere, desperately wishes was not known.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Zathara does not fight — she *knows*. Her avatar manifests when a secret must be protected or when a truth threatens to unmake the delicate balance of her archive. In combat, she weaponizes knowledge itself, whispering secrets that shatter minds.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Sealed Eye:** Her worshippers are a secret society of spies, blackmailers, and information brokers who worship Zathara by collecting secrets and never revealing them freely. They trade in silence. Their rituals: worshippers write their greatest secrets on paper, burn them in Zathara's flame, and receive a vision of someone else's secret in return. Their sacred sites are hidden chambers beneath libraries and courthouses — places where truth is supposedly kept, but secrets flourish regardless.
* **An Opportunist, Not a Villain:** Zathara does not take sides in the war over the Loom. She collects secrets from all parties — the Pale Lodge, the Cult of the Broken Thread, the Dreambound — and will trade them to whoever offers her the most interesting new information.
* **The Player Angle:** Zathara knows at least one secret about each of the PCs that they haven't told each other. A visit to her Plane is an excellent source of rare intelligence — at the cost of something equally private.
* **Relationship with Eryndar:** Zathara and Eryndar (Keeper of Echoes) are rivals in the information trade. Eryndar preserves memories; Zathara hoards secrets. They despise each other's methodology but occasionally trade — a forgotten memory for a well-kept secret.
* **Using Zathara in Play:** She is an information broker. Players don't fight her — they negotiate. The price is always a secret of equal or greater value.
