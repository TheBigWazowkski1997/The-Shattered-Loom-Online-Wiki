# Halverak the Shorn (The Conclave Enforcer)

![[attachments/halverak_the_shorn.jpg]]

* **Race**: Warforged (Heavy Dreadnought Vanguard)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Ironbound Covenant|The Ironbound Covenant]]
* **Role**: Grand Paladin of the Mandate / Conclave Enforcer / Seat V of the Sealed Conclave ("The Drawn")
* **Appearance**: A walking fortress of blackened, blast-pitted dreadnought plate. Halverak's massive frame bears the deep, scarred pitting of void-acid from past battles against Maw-Spawn. Most striking are his arms: severed at the mid-bicep during a cataclysmic breach, they were replaced with experimental, oversized hydraulic Forgeplate limbs powered by internal combustion pistons and runic pressure coils. He carries no weapons, sheaths, or shields; his massive alloyed fists *are* his weapons.
* **Personality**: Grim, ascetic, and brutally uncompromising. Halverak views weapons as artificial intermediaries between a warrior's resolve and the enemy's unmaking. He speaks with blunt, clipped economy, expressing no pleasure in violence but treating its execution with the absolute precision of a smith striking red-hot iron. He does not hesitate to track down deserters, corrupted constructs, or rogue elements of the crusade.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Halverak is an unstoppable shock-trooper and unarmed brawler. He utilizes explosive hydraulic bursts from his mechanical arms to shatter armor, crush bone and stone, and discharge concussive kinetic shockwaves that pulverize multiple foes simultaneously.

* **Siege Engine**: Halverak deals double damage to objects and structures. His unarmed strikes count as adamantine magical weapons.
* **Relentless Anchor**: Halverak cannot be moved against his will, shoved, or knocked prone by any physical or magical effect unless he chooses to be.
* **Meteoric Kinetic Reservoir**: Whenever Halverak takes 20 or more damage in a single turn, his hydraulic arm coils charge with kinetic energy, giving him advantage on his next attack roll and adding significant damage to the hit.
* **Multiattack**: Halverak makes three unarmed strikes with his *Piston-Forged Fists*, or makes two unarmed strikes and uses *Sunder Ground*.
* **Sunder Ground (Recharge 5–6)**: Halverak slams both hydraulic gauntlets into the ground with catastrophic force. A tremor erupts in a 20-foot radius centered on him. Each creature on the ground in that area must make a saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes significant damage and is knocked prone. On a success, it takes half damage and is not knocked prone. The ground within the area becomes difficult terrain.
* **Hydraulic Crush**: one creature grappled by Halverak. The target must succeed on a saving throw or be incapacitated until the end of its next turn.
* **Piston Overdrive (3/Day)**: Halverak releases safety limiters on his arms. For the next minute, his movement speed increases by 10 feet, and his unarmed strikes deal an additional significant damage from superheated exhaust vents.
* **Ironclad Intercept**: When a hostile creature moves within 5 feet of Halverak, he can make one unarmed opportunity attack. If the attack hits, the target's speed becomes 0 for the remainder of the turn.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Seat V of the Sealed Conclave ("The Drawn"):** Halverak serves as the muscle and executor of the Conclave. When the Conclave issues a covert mandate that cannot be known to the rank-and-file or the wider public, Halverak ensures it is carried out without leaving loose ends.
* **The Hunt for the Deserter (Current Investigation):** Halverak is currently tracking a senior Questioned Warforged officer who broke ranks after claiming to have uncovered proof that Maw-incursions along the Covenant's eastern front are being secretly provoked or staged to distract the military from deep-vault excavations. If the party encounters the deserter first, Halverak will track them down relentlessly.
* **Contempt for Weapons:** Halverak genuinely believes that wielding manufactured swords or axes is an admission of personal inadequacy: *"A blade dulls, a shaft snaps, a hilt slips. The body Kaldryn hammered from raw flame and iron has no seams to fracture. Fight with what you are."*
* **DM Tips:** Halverak is an intimidating presence who should feel like an implacable juggernaut. He rarely opens with diplomacy, but if beaten in a trial of pure strength, he offers genuine martial respect.
