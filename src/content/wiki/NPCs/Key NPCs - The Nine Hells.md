# Key NPCs - The Nine Hells of Baator

The following legendary figures make up the supreme Archdevils, fallen angels, paranoid surveyors, and infernal commanders who govern the Spindle of Absolute Tyranny across [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Nine Hells/The Nine Hells|The Nine Hells]].

* * *

## The Supreme Sovereign of the Ninth

* [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Asmodeus, Lord of the Ninth|Lord Asmodeus, Supreme Master of the Nine Hells]] — The Dark Architect, Master of the Pact Primeval, and Prime Weaver of the Infernal Spindle

* * *

## The Sovereign Archdevils of Baator

1. **Avernus**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Zariel, Archduchess of Avernus|Archduchess Zariel]] — The Fallen Weaver & Supreme Commander of the Blood War
2. **Dis**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Dispater, Iron Duke of Dis|Archduke Dispater]] — The Paranoid Archivist & Surveyor of Deviance
3. **Minauros**: **Archduke Mammon** — The Lord of Avarice & Scavenger of Loom-Shards
4. **Phlegethos**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Belial and Fierna|Belial and Fierna]] — The Smelters of Mandates & Lords of the Caldera
5. **Stygia**: **Archduke Levistus** — The Frozen Echo & Lord of the Pre-Shattered Archive
6. **Malbolge**: **Archduchess Glasya** — The Princess of the Hells & The Weaver's Scalpel
7. **Maladomini**: **Archduke Baalzebul** — The Lord of Flies & Architect of the False Weave
8. **Cania**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Mephistopheles, Lord of Cania|Lord Mephistopheles]] — Master of Hellfire Alchemy & The Void of Negation

* * *

## Key Military Generals, Outcasts & Brokers

* **Bel, the Forge-Master** — Former Archduke of Avernus; Chief War Machine Engineer of the Bronze Citadel
* **Bael, General of the Citadel** — Supreme Tactician and Marshal of Dis
* **Mad Maggie** — Night Hag Scavenger Queen of Fort Knucklebone
* **Mahadi, Master of the Wandering Emporium** — Rakshasa Merchant Prince and Sanctuary Keeper
* **Chancellor Adramalech** — Grand Chancellor of Malsheem and Record Keeper of Nessus
* **Lady Ariadne of Grenpoli** — Mayor of the City of Diplomatic Truce in Maladomini
