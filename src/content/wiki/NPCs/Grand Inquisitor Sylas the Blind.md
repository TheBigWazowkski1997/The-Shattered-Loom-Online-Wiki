# 👁️ Grand Inquisitor Sylas the Blind
### *Supreme Inquisitor of the Severed Eye, The Un-Blinded Seer, Exorcist of the Far Void*

> *"They pitied me when I took the needle to my eyes. But when the Void-Weavers descended upon our monastery, their eyes melted in their skulls while I saw the exact silver thread connecting each beast to the dark. Sight is a prison; clarity is a weapon."*  
> — **Sylas the Blind**, addressing the Council of Needles

---

## 📜 Identity & Background

**Grand Inquisitor Sylas** is a Kalashtar ascetic who has dedicated two centuries to hunting cosmic horrors that slip through the cracks of the Shattered Loom. Believing that visual sight is an aberration's easiest path into the mortal psyche, Sylas surgically blinded himself in his youth, replacing optical vision with an extraordinary lattice of psionic blindsight and spatial telepathy.

* **Title**: Supreme Inquisitor of the Severed Eye.
* **Habitat**: The Sanctorum of the Blind Needle, The Fractured Nexus.
* **Creature Type**: Medium Humanoid (Kalashtar), Lawful Neutral.
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Order of the Severed Eye|The Order of the Severed Eye]].
* **Associated Rules**: [[Custom Rules/The Eldritch Horror Hunting & Sanity-Harvesting Ruleset|Eldritch Horror Hunting Ruleset]].

## 🎨 Visual Appearance & Equipment

Sylas wears high-collared monastic vestments of bleached linen embroidered with silver Loom-filaments. Across his eyes is wrapped a silk blindfold stitched with lead wire and tiny silver eyes. At his hip rests **Severance**, a slender estoc forged from the crystalline spine of an astral void-horror, capable of severing psionic connections and unweaving illusions with a single flick.
