# 👁️ Grand Inquisitor Sylas the Blind
### *Supreme Inquisitor of the Severed Eye, The Un-Blinded Seer, Exorcist of the Far Void*

> *"They pitied me when I took the needle to my eyes. But when the Void-Weavers descended upon our monastery, their eyes melted in their skulls while I saw the exact silver thread connecting each beast to the dark. Sight is a prison; clarity is a weapon."*  
> — **Sylas the Blind**, addressing the Council of Needles

---

## 📜 Identity & Background

**Grand Inquisitor Sylas** is a Kalashtar ascetic who has dedicated two centuries to hunting cosmic horrors that slip through the cracks of the Shattered Loom. Believing that visual sight is an aberration's easiest path into the mortal psyche, Sylas surgically blinded himself in his youth, replacing optical vision with an extraordinary lattice of psionic blindsight and spatial telepathy.

* **Title**: Supreme Inquisitor of the Severed Eye.
* **Habitat**: The Sanctorum of the Blind Needle, The Fractured Nexus.
* **Creature Type**: Medium Humanoid (Kalashtar), Lawful Neutral.
* **Affiliated Faction**: [[Factions/The Order of the Severed Eye|The Order of the Severed Eye]].
* **Associated Rules**: [[Custom Rules/The Eldritch Horror Hunting & Sanity-Harvesting Ruleset|Eldritch Horror Hunting Ruleset]].

---

## 🎨 Visual Appearance & Equipment

Sylas wears high-collared monastic vestments of bleached linen embroidered with silver Loom-filaments. Across his eyes is wrapped a silk blindfold stitched with lead wire and tiny silver eyes. At his hip rests **Severance**, a slender estoc forged from the crystalline spine of an astral void-horror, capable of severing psionic connections and unweaving illusions with a single flick.

### Traits

* **Eldritch Mind-Shield**: Sylas is immune to madness, Dream-Fracture Lucidity drain, and any effect that would read his thoughts or sense his emotions.
* **Sever the Un-Woven**: Sylas's weapon attacks deal an extra 3d6 psychic or force damage against aberrations, fiends, and undead.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Sylas fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions

* **Multiattack**: Sylas makes three attacks with **Severance** (Estoc) and uses *Psionic Purge*.
* **Severance (Estoc +3)**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +14 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 14 (1d8 + 10) piercing damage plus 14 (4d6) psychic damage. Target must succeed on a DC 20 Wisdom save or be unable to cast spells or use psionics until the end of its next turn.
* **Psionic Purge (Recharge 5–6)**: Sylas releases a concentrated pulse of pure anti-aberrant mental energy in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a DC 20 Intelligence saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 55 (10d10) force damage, is stunned for 1 minute, and has all active illusions or mind-control effects dispelled. On a success, takes half damage.
* **Suture the Veil (Bonus Action)**: Sylas teleports up to 40 feet to an unoccupied space he can sense, leaving behind a glowing silver glyph that deals 3d8 force damage to any hostile creature that enters it.

### Legendary Actions (3 Actions/Round)

* **Sense the Fracture**: Sylas makes a Wisdom (Insight) check with advantage or senses all aberrations within 300 feet.
* **Blade Flash**: Sylas makes one attack with Severance.
* **Exorcise the Dream (Costs 2 Actions)**: Sylas touches one ally within reach, instantly curing one mental condition, charm, fright, or restoring 20 Lucidity.
