# Knot (Proprietor of The Warp & Weft)

![[knot_riftborn.jpg]]

* **Race**: Riftborn (Luminous Starlight Construct)
* **Faction**: [[The Fractured Nexus#4. The Warp & Weft (Planar Survival Supplies)|The Warp & Weft]] / Independent Merchant Syndicate
* **Role**: Merchant / Planar Survival Specialist / Information Broker
* **Appearance**: Knot manifests as a slender, faceted humanoid silhouette forged from solid, opalescent starlight. Their outer "skin" consists of interlocking geometric planes of amber and white light that gently rotate like complex polyhedral dice. Instead of hair, three orbiting halos of glowing golden wire circle their head, humming in time with the Loomforge. They wear a practical leather apron bristling with brass tension-meters, spools of filament cord, and magnifying lenses.
* **Personality**: Impossibly dry, pragmatic, and deadpan. Knot has observed mortal panic across thousands of planar collisions and finds drama thoroughly inefficient. They speak in a measured, metallic cadence with a dry sense of humor: *"Yes, the bridge is sixty percent likely to hold your bulk. If you prefer seventy percent, buy the reinforced harness."* Despite their stoic demeanor, Knot harbors a fierce loyalty to those who respect the threads.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Knot avoids physical brawling, preferring to control space, manipulate subjective gravity, and bind aggressive foes in unbreakable cords of divine filament. If attacked in their shop, Knot deploys automated geometric defenses, phase deflections, and force-tethers while effortlessly multi-tasking with floating starlight hands.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Ledger of Crossings:** Knot maintains an extensive, coded registry of everyone who enters or departs the Nexus through the major portal hubs. For the right price (usually a rare planar artifact or an intact memory vial), Knot can identify who transported a specific weapon or creature into the city.
* **The Hunt for Fray:** Knot was once close companions with [[Fray, the Unraveler|Fray]] before Fray turned renegade. Knot views Fray’s destructive sabotage not with hatred, but with deep, weary sorrow: *"A flawed knot can be re-tied; a severed cord leaves only unraveling."* Knot will pay handsomely for Fray to be brought back alive to be re-woven.
* **Merchant Bargaining:** Knot does not haggle over coin, but will immediately offer a 20% discount on all shop supplies if the party brings back authentic salvage from an unexplored or unstable outer shard.
