# Captain Seris Vael (Pilot of The Wraith)

![[attachments/captain_seris_vael.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Githyanki
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Pale Lodge/The Pale Lodge|The Pale Lodge]] (contractor — loyalty is to herself and her ship)
* **Role**: Pilot of *The Wraith* and Tactical Advisor
* **Appearance**: A lean, iron-muscled githyanki woman with the characteristic angular features of her kind — flat nose, wide jaw, deep-set eyes the color of storm clouds. She wears battered astral leather armor that has been repaired so many times the stitching forms its own pattern. Her silver greatsword — standard Githyanki issue — is always within arm's reach, even when she sleeps.
* **Personality**: Grim, cynical, and refreshingly honest. She has watched worlds end, watched the Pale Lodge manipulate well-meaning people into graves, and has no patience for mysticism, destiny talk, or the word "chosen." She says what she thinks. Always.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Seris is a master of the silver greatsword, combining psychic bursts with devastating melee combos. On her ship, she fights with the added advantage of controlling the terrain — she can vent the deck atmosphere, adjust artificial gravity, and maneuver in combat. On foot, she is a formidable powerhouse with psionic support.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Reality Check:** Whenever the Pale Lodge's agenda feels too clean or too righteous, Seris is the voice of skepticism. She will pull a player aside and say: *"You're not 'chosen.' You're useful. Figure out who benefits from that distinction."*
* **The Ship:** *The Wraith* is a refitted Githyanki patrol vessel — fast, heavily shielded, with a crew of four loyal Githyanki sailors and an astral anchor that can lock it into the Nexus. It is the party's main transport.
* **Trust & Loyalty**: Demands competence and reliability from companions before extending personal trust, watching actions far more than listening to words.
