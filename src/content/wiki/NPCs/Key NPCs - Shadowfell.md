# Key NPCs - The Shadowfell & Domains of Dread

The following figures make up the sovereign Darklords, divine masters, master investigators, and living anomalies that shape the dark tapestry of [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Shadowfell|The Shadowfell]].

* * *

## The Divine Authority

The cosmic shepherd of memories and terminal fate residing within the Fortress of Memories.

* [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/The Raven Queen|The Raven Queen]] — Mistress of Fate, Death, and Memory; Sovereign of the Shadowfell

* * *

## The Sovereign Darklords of Dread

The cursed rulers bound eternally to their domain prisons by the Dark Powers:

1. **Barovia**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Count Strahd von Zarovich|Count Strahd von Zarovich]] — The Vampire Sovereign of Castle Ravenloft
2. **Darkon**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Azalin Rex|Azalin Rex]] — The Arch-Lich and Sovereign of Castle Avernus (Currently Absent)
3. **Dementlieu**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Duchess Saidra d'Honaire|Duchess Saidra d’Honaire]] — The Porcelain Ruler of the Grand Masquerade
4. **Lamordia**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Dr. Viktra Mordenheim|Dr. Viktra Mordenheim]] — The Baroness of the Galvanic Spark
5. **Har’Akir**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Pharaoh Ankhtepot|Pharaoh Ankhtepot]] — The Sunless Mummy King of the Black Pyramid
6. **Falkovnia**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/General Vladeska Drakov|General Vladeska Drakov]] — The Iron Falcon of Lekar
7. **Borca**: 
   * [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Lady Ivana Boritsi|Lady Ivana Boritsi]] — The Black Widow of Misericordia
   * [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Ivan Dilisnya|Ivan Dilisnya]] — The Gilded Toymaker of Lechberg
8. **Valachan**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Chakuna|Chakuna the Blood Huntress]] — Alpha of the Twilight Jungle

## Key Planar Assets & Inquisitives

Notable investigators, masterworks, and rebels contesting the Darklords:

* **The Great Detectives**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Alanik Ray|Alanik Ray]] & Dr. Arthur Sedgwick — Master Consulting Investigators of Port-a-Lucine
* **The Living Masterpiece**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Elise, the Living Core|Elise, the Living Core]] — The Synthetic Spark of Lamordia
* **The Hag Matron**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Baba Lysaga|Baba Lysaga]] — Mother of Witches and Guardian of the Berez Fane (Deceased)
