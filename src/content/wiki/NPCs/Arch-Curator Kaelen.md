# Arch-Curator Kaelen (Master of the Fractured Archives)

![[attachments/arch_curator_kaelen.jpg]]

* **Race**: High Elf (Chrono-Scholar)
* **Faction**: The Keepers of Revelation / Shard-Seekers of Veilscar
* **Role**: Arch-Curator / Chief Antiquarian of Veilscar
* **Appearance**: An aristocratic elven scholar with silver hair braided with thin electrum wires. His eyes shimmer with faint, revolving geometric diagrams of forgotten epochs. He is dressed in tailored sapphire robes trimmed in silver fox fur, and he carries a crystalline chronometer-staff tipped with a pulsing memory crystal.
* **Personality**: Methodical, obsessively intellectual, and subtly melancholic. He laments the cataclysm that shattered Veilscar but is fiercely protective of whatever historical truths remain. He regards knowledge not as power, but as a solemn cosmic responsibility.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Kaelen is a master abjurer and chronomancer. He controls the battlefield by altering temporal flow, reversing hostile spells, and summoning hard-light force barriers to protect his archival relics.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Lost Secret of Veilscar:** Kaelen is convinced that the disaster which shattered Veilscar was caused by a specific forgotten thesis on planar unraveling. He will hire the party to recover intact crystal scrolls from deep fracture rifts.
* **Scholarly Patronage:** If the party shows respect to the archives and avoids damaging historical texts, Kaelen can decipher obscure dead languages, identify legendary artifacts, and provide safe haven in the High Flotilla.
* **Strict Principle:** Kaelen refuses to trade knowledge that facilitates unnecessary slaughter, such as apocalyptic biological pathogens or uncontrolled dimensional tears.
