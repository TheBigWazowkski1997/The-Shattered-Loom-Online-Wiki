# Lord Mephistopheles, Master of Cania

![[mephistopheles.jpg]]

* **Race**: Archdevil (The Alchemist of Absolute Zero & Lord of the Eighth)
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Infernal Hierarchy of Baator|The Infernal Hierarchy of Baator]] (Lord of the Void of Negation)
* **Role**: Ruler of Cania / Master of Hellfire Alchemy / Arch-Mage of the Nine Hells
* **Appearance**: A tall, devilishly handsome, diabolical archdevil scholar-mage with glowing crimson-copper skin, piercing ice-blue eyes, long pointed goatee, and elegant sweeping ram horns. Clad in high-collared scholarly robes of deep indigo, ermine fur, and blackened silk adorned with glowing arcane frost runes. In one hand he channels crackling white-blue hellfire and frost magic, while in the other he holds a spellbook bound in pit fiend leather with smoking arcane glyphs.
* **Personality**: Brilliant, theatrical, volatile, and infinitely ambitious. Mephistopheles considers himself the greatest intellect in the multiverse. He is obsessed with unlocking the ultimate secrets of the Loom and discovering the "Final Thread." He alternates between smooth, charming academic arrogance and explosive rages when his experiments are interrupted.

---

## Combat Profile

Mephistopheles is the supreme arcane caster of the Nine Hells. He wields the terrifying, contradictory power of *Hellfire*—unholy flame that burns cold and consumes soul-energy without shedding warmth.

### **Mephistopheles, Master of Cania**
*Medium Fiend (Archdevil), Lawful Evil*
* **Armor Class**: 21 (Natural Armor & Arcane Ward)
* **Hit Points**: 390 (28d8 + 264)
* **Speed**: 40 ft., fly 80 ft.

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
| :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: | :---: |
| 22 (+6) | 22 (+6) | 28 (+9) | 30 (+10) | 24 (+7) | 28 (+9) |

* **Saving Throws**: Dex +14, Con +17, Int +18, Wis +15, Cha +17
* **Skills**: Arcana +28, Deception +17, History +28, Insight +15, Perception +15
* **Damage Resistances**: Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks
* **Damage Immunities**: Cold, Fire, Poison
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Poisoned
* **Senses**: Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 25
* **Languages**: All languages, Telepathy 1,000 ft.
* **Challenge**: 27 (105,000 XP) | **Proficiency Bonus**: +8

#### **Traits**
* **Hellfire Master**: When Mephistopheles casts a spell that deals fire or cold damage, he can choose to have it deal hellfire damage instead (ignores fire resistance, treats fire immunity as resistance).
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Mephistopheles fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
* **Arcane Superiority**: Mephistopheles can maintain concentration on two spells simultaneously.
* **Spellcasting**: 20th-level spellcaster (Spell Save DC 26, +18 to hit with spell attacks). Prepared spells:
  * Cantrips (at will): *Fire Bolt, Ray of Frost, Mage Hand, Prestidigitation*
  * 1st level (4 slots): *Shield, Magic Missile, Detect Magic*
  * 2nd level (3 slots): *Misty Step, Mirror Image, Scorching Ray*
  * 3rd level (3 slots): *Counterspell, Fireball, Lightning Bolt*
  * 4th level (3 slots): *Dimension Door, Ice Storm, Wall of Fire*
  * 5th level (3 slots): *Cone of Cold, Hold Monster, Telekinesis*
  * 6th level (2 slots): *Disintegrate, Chain Lightning*
  * 7th level (2 slots): *Delayed Blast Fireball, Plane Shift*
  * 8th level (1 slot): *Maddening Darkness, Sunburst*
  * 9th level (1 slot): *Meteor Swarm, Time Stop*

#### **Actions**
* **Multiattack**: Mephistopheles casts one spell and makes one attack with his Hellfire Staff.
* **Hellfire Staff**: *Melee Spell Attack*: +18 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 19 (2d8 + 10) bludgeoning damage plus 27 (6d8) fire damage and 27 (6d8) cold damage.
* **Hellfire Blast (Recharge 5–6)**: Mephistopheles unleashes a 60-foot cone of white-blue hellfire. Each creature must make a DC 26 Dexterity saving throw, taking 70 (20d6) hellfire damage on a failed save, or half damage on a success.

#### **Legendary Actions**
Mephistopheles can take 3 legendary actions:
* **Arcane Step**: Mephistopheles teleports up to 60 feet.
* **Cast Cantrip**: Mephistopheles casts one cantrip.
* **Hellfire Nova (Costs 2 Actions)**: Mephistopheles releases a burst of flame dealing 28 (8d6) fire/cold damage to all enemies within 20 feet (DC 26 Dex save for half).

## Roleplay & DM Notes

* **The Rivalry with Asmodeus**: Mephistopheles constantly schemes to overthrow Asmodeus, but is terrified of his master's foresight. He will happily sponsor adventurers who can find lost cosmic relics before Asmodeus's inspectors claim them.
* **The Academic Patron**: Mortals who bring him genuine original magical discoveries will be treated with grand scholarly hospitality in Mephistar.
