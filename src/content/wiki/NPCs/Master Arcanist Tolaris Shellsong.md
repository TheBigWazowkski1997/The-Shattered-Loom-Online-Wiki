# Master Arcanist Tolaris Shellsong (Arch-Mage of Currents)

![[attachments/master_arcanist_tolaris.jpg]]

* **Race**: Triton
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane Of Water/Cities of Plane of Water/Tidehaven|Aquamancer’s Guild]] / The Council of Waves
* **Role**: Head of the Aquamancer’s Guild, High Researcher of Planar Resonances
* **Appearance**: An imposing triton aristocrat with silvery-blue skin, high cheekbones, and piercing turquoise eyes. He wears ceremonial crystalline plate armor that gleams with refracted prismatic light, draped in a mantlet of deep sea-silk. His arms are etched with arcane tattoos of flowing tides that glow bright azure whenever he channels magic. He carries the *Rod of the Maelstrom*, a spiral scepter carved from deep-trench black glass.
* **Personality**: Brilliant, intellectually ruthless, and haughty. Tolaris holds arcane achievement and planar knowledge above sentimentality. While polite and diplomatic in Council sessions, he views other leaders as short-sighted and believes the secrets of the Loom and the Abyssal Gate are meant to be mastered, not sealed away in fear.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]

Tolaris is a devastating 13th-level evoker and hydro-elemental wizard who excels at area-of-effect control, cold damage, and displacement spells.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Research Obsession**: Tolaris believes the **Abyssal Gate** connects directly to an ancient civilization that once mastered the Loom of the Multiverse. He seeks to siphon its power to elevate the Aquamancer's Guild into a multi-planar superpower.
* **Arrogant Patron**: He pays adventurers handsomely in gems, spell shards, and rare water-breathing items, but views them as expendable field researchers. If a party questions his ethics, he coldly reminds them of their contracts.
* **Quests**: He hires the party to venture into forbidden underwater ruins, recover crystallized memories from drowned astral wrecks, or capture live aberrant deep-sea specimens for his dissection vats.
