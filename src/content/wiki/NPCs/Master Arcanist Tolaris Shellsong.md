# Master Arcanist Tolaris Shellsong (Arch-Mage of Currents)

![[attachments/master_arcanist_tolaris.jpg]]

* **Race**: Triton
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane Of Water/Cities of Plane of Water/Tidehaven|Aquamancer’s Guild]] / The Council of Waves
* **Role**: Head of the Aquamancer’s Guild, High Researcher of Planar Resonances
* **Appearance**: An imposing triton aristocrat with silvery-blue skin, high cheekbones, and piercing turquoise eyes. He wears ceremonial crystalline plate armor that gleams with refracted prismatic light, draped in a mantlet of deep sea-silk. His arms are etched with arcane tattoos of flowing tides that glow bright azure whenever he channels magic. He carries the *Rod of the Maelstrom*, a spiral scepter carved from deep-trench black glass.
* **Personality**: Brilliant, intellectually ruthless, and haughty. Tolaris holds arcane achievement and planar knowledge above sentimentality. While polite and diplomatic in Council sessions, he views other leaders as short-sighted and believes the secrets of the Loom and the Abyssal Gate are meant to be mastered, not sealed away in fear.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Tolaris is a devastating 13th-level evoker and hydro-elemental wizard who excels at area-of-effect control, cold damage, and displacement spells.

* **Amphibious**: Tolaris can breathe air and water.
* **Control Air and Water**: Tolaris can innately cast *Fog Cloud*, *Gust of Wind*, and *Wall of Water* each once per day without material components (saving throw).
* **Sculpt Spells**: When Tolaris casts an evocation spell that affects other creatures he can see, he can choose a number of them equal to 1 + the spell’s level to automatically succeed on their saving throws, and they take no damage if they would normally take half damage.
* **Rod of the Maelstrom**: Notable combat maneuver and signature technique.
* **Abyssal Frost-Bolt (Cantrip)**: *Ranged Spell Attack:* +9 to hit, range 120 ft., one target.
* **Hydro-Burst (Recharge 5–6)**: Tolaris blasts a 60-foot line of hyper-pressurized water that is 10 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a saving throw. On a failure, a target takes significant damage and is pushed 20 feet away and knocked prone. On a success, the target takes half damage and is not pushed or knocked prone.
* **Spellcasting**: Tolaris is a 13th-level wizard. His spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save saving throw, +9 to hit with spell attacks). He has the following wizard spells prepared:.
* **Cantrips (at will):**: *Ray of Frost*, *Mage Hand*, *Prestidigitation*, *Shocking Grasp*, *Message*.
* **1st level (4 slots):**: *Shield*, *Mage Armor*, *Magic Missile*, *Detect Magic*.
* **2nd level (3 slots):**: *Misty Step*, *Mirror Image*, *Shatter*.
* **3rd level (3 slots):**: *Counterspell*, *Lightning Bolt*, *Tidal Wave*, *Slow*.
* **4th level (3 slots):**: *Ice Storm*, *Watery Sphere*, *Otiluke’s Resilient Sphere*.
* **5th level (2 slots):**: *Cone of Cold*, *Telekinesis*, *Wall of Force*.
* **6th level (1 slot):**: *Chain Lightning*, *Otiluke’s Freezing Sphere*.
* **7th level (1 slot):**: *Prismatic Spray*.
* **Flash-Freeze Ward**: When hit by a melee weapon attack, Tolaris can cause ice to encase the attacker's weapon or limb. The attacker must make a saving throw, taking significant damage on a failure, and its speed becomes 0 until the end of its turn.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Research Obsession**: Tolaris believes the **Abyssal Gate** connects directly to an ancient civilization that once mastered the Loom of the Multiverse. He seeks to siphon its power to elevate the Aquamancer's Guild into a multi-planar superpower.
* **Arrogant Patron**: He pays adventurers handsomely in gems, spell shards, and rare water-breathing items, but views them as expendable field researchers. If a party questions his ethics, he coldly reminds them of their contracts.
* **Quests**: He hires the party to venture into forbidden underwater ruins, recover crystallized memories from drowned astral wrecks, or capture live aberrant deep-sea specimens for his dissection vats.
