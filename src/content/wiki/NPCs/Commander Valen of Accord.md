# Commander Valen of Accord (Grand Marshal of Paxus Arx)

![[attachments/commander_valen_of_accord.jpg]]

* **Race**: Aasimar (Protector Aasimar)
* **Faction**: The Sentinels of Accord / High Command of Paxus Arx
* **Role**: Supreme Commander / Grand Marshal of the Accordant Legions
* **Appearance**: An imposing warrior with radiant bronze skin and eyes that glow like polished gold. Two magnificent wings of pure incandescent white light unfurl from his shoulderblades when called to battle. He is clad in *Sentinel Plate*—a suit of polished electrum and white marble etched with the seals of planar treaties. He carries the *Sun-Cleaver*, an ancient radiant greatsword whose blade hums with the chords of eternal order.
* **Personality**: Disciplined, noble, and unwavering in his duty to protect the weak. Valen believes that true peace is not the absence of conflict, but the courage to stand firm against chaos and tyranny. He speaks with calm authority, expects absolute discipline from his knights, and never asks a subordinate to face a danger he would not confront himself.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Valen is an indomitable paladin and tactical commander. He bolsters allies with radiant auras, smites chaotic fiends with blinding sunlight, and can fly across the battlefield to shield endangered comrades.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Guardian's Burden:** Valen bears the weight of defending the sanctuary of Paxus Arx against endless outside threats. He does not seek conquest, but will ruthlessly crush any faction attempting to bring war into the Plane of Unity.
* **The Peacemaker's Test:** If adventurers arrive at Paxus Arx seeking military aid, Valen will first test their moral integrity, dispatching them to resolve border skirmishes or negotiate prisoner releases without unnecessary bloodshed.
* **Respect for True Valor:** Valen respects martial prowess paired with self-restraint. He despises bullies, treacherous sellswords, and assassins who strike from the shadows.
