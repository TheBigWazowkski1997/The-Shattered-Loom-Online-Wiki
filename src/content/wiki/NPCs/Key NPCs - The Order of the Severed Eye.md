## 👤 Secondary Field Inquisitors

### 1. Sister Morwenna of the Pale Thread
* **Role**: Chief Astral Seer and Diviner.
* **Location**: Observatory of the Blind Needle, The Fractured Nexus.
* **Abilities**: Uses silver-wire scrying bowls to track aberrant migrations across the Astral Sea. Warns hunters 48 hours before an elder horror breaches reality.

### 2. Harrier-Captain Brand
* **Role**: Commander of the Lead-Plated Strike Wing.
* **Location**: Field Operative (currently deployed in the Abyssal Trench of Ry'leh).
* **Gear**: Wields the *Spoor-Harpoon of the Deep Watcher (+3)* and commands squads of blindfolded harpooners.
