# Norath of the Leaving Flame (The Chief Theologian)

![[attachments/norath_of_the_leaving_flame.jpg]]

* **Race**: Warforged (Inquisitorial Judiciar Chassis)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Ironbound Covenant|The Ironbound Covenant]]
* **Role**: High Forge Judge / Chief Theologian / Seat VI of the Sealed Conclave ("The Cast")
* **Appearance**: An austere, towering construct clad in soot-blackened liturgical robes over etched iron chassis plates. A caged iron thurible is suspended from an arch above his shoulders, releasing a steady ribbon of consecrated white incense smoke scented with sulfur and myrrh. His faceplate is stylized into a solemn, weeping iron visage. He carries the *Crosier of the Crucible*, a tall staff of tempered steel topped with an open brazier containing a living spark of Kaldryn's white flame.
* **Personality**: Somber, deeply analytical, and spiritually burdened. Norath bears the harrowing responsibility of administering the Covenant's **Severance Rulings**—the sacred, agonizing duty of putting down brothers and sisters whose Soulcores have succumbed to Vorzath's corruption. He speaks with poetic gravitas, seeing theological patterns and divine intent where others see only cold mechanics.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Seat VI of the Sealed Conclave ("The Cast"):** Norath is the chief theologian responsible for interpreting the oldest Ironscript Leaves. Unlike some on the Conclave who view the ancient leaf as a shocking revelation, Norath believes it is an ancient *warning*—a cautionary allegory indicating that if the Warforged rely solely on unyielding dogma, they will become indistinguishable from the cold, unmaking hunger of Vorzath.
* **Secret Scholarly Channel:** Norath conducts secret, encrypted correspondence with Grand Archivist Hesperia of [[../Factions/The Keepers of the Sacred Weft|The Keepers of the Sacred Weft]]. They exchange fragments of early Ironscript Leaves for pieces of the Telarian Codices. Both know the other is holding back, yet neither can resist the intellectual and theological clues the other provides.
* **The Weight of Severance:** Norath carries a ring of blackened steel prayer-beads beneath his mantle. Each bead is engraved with the true runic designation of a Covenant brother or sister he was forced to execute under a Severance Ruling. He recites these names during his Forge Dreams.
* **Interacting with the Party:** If the party encounters corrupted entities, anomalous rifts, or needs insight into the ancient theology of Kaldryn and the Shattered Loom, Norath is an indispensable, if haunting, theological authority.
