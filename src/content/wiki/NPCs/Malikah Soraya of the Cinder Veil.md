# Malikah Soraya of the Cinder Veil (The Gilded Flame)

![[attachments/malikah_soraya.jpg]]

* **Race**: Fire Genasi
* **Faction**: The Pyrefield Merchants Guild / [[../Factions/The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper|The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper]]
* **Role**: High Merchant of the Pyrefield Bazaar, Consortium Plenipotentiary
* **Appearance**: An exquisite fire genasi woman with radiant copper skin and eyes that flicker like molten brass. Her dark, obsidian-black hair is intricately woven with gold wire, fire pearls, and delicate burning ember-threads that never consume her clothing. She wears layered silks in shades of saffron, vermilion, and gold, accessorized with an ornate brass balance scale suspended from her belt and twin curved jambiyas sheathed in dragon-leather.
* **Personality**: Shrewd, charming, pragmatic, and remarkably perceptive. Unlike the cruel noble efreet, Soraya understands that the greatest power in the multiverse is not raw fire or fear, but steady commerce and leverage. She is an expert negotiator who speaks five languages fluently and can calculate exchange rates across seven planes in her head.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Soraya is an 11th-level rogue/bard multiclass archetype (formidable) who fights with fluid acrobatics, flaming daggers, and sharp psychological misdirection.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Bridge Between Worlds**: Soraya represents the commercial connection between the City of Brass and the Gilded Caliper from the Plane of Eternal Craft. She despises the erratic cruelty of the Efreet nobility and quietly funds mortal ventures that loosen the Efreet’s monopoly.
* **Trustworthy Partner**: If the party honors a contract with Soraya, she becomes one of the most reliable and resourceful allies in the Inner Planes, able to procure rare magic items, counterfeit travel papers, and safe planar transport.
* **Quests**: She hires adventurers to guard high-value trade caravans traversing the Sea of Fire, discreetly steal back intercepted ledgers, or out-negotiate rival merchants in the Pyrefield Bazaar.
