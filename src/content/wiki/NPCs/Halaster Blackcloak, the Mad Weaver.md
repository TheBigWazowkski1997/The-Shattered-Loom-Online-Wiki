# Halaster Blackcloak, the Mad Weaver (The Architect of the Undermountain Knot)

![[attachments/halaster_blackcloak.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Human (Archmage — Planar Adept)
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/The Prison Plane/The 32 Chambers|The Silent Spindle]] (Prisoner of Chamber 13)
* **Role**: Inmate / Planar Architect / Braider of Realities
* **Appearance**: An ancient, impossibly tall man with skin like weathered parchment stretched over sharp bones. His hair is a chaotic halo of white streaked with faint, shifting colors—remnants of the Feywild, Shadowfell, and Astral Sea woven into his very follicles. His eyes are mismatched: one a piercing, pupil-less violet (Fey-sight), the other a hollow, star-flecked void (Shadow-sight). He wears robes stitched from the folded edges of demiplanes, constantly shifting pattern and texture. His fingers are stained with "thread-dust"—glittering residue from handling raw planar essence.
* **Personality**: Brilliant, manic, and utterly convinced of his own vision. Halaster doesn't see "madness"—he sees *unrecognized patterns*. He speaks in rapid, tangential bursts, weaving metaphors from topology, weaving, and culinary arts. He respects only those who can follow his logic (rare) or survive his "tests" (rarer). He hates the Spindle not for imprisoning him, but for its *lack of imagination*—it merely preserves; it does not *improve*.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Halaster is a formidable archmage-tier threat whose power lies in battlefield control, planar manipulation, and recursive spellcasting. In his chamber, the "Workshop of Recursive Logic," the environment itself is his weapon—he can rewrite the local laws of physics mid-combat. **Proficiency Bonus** +8 ***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If Halaster fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead. ***Magic Resistance.*** Halaster has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. ***Planar Adaptation.*** Halaster is immune to the detrimental environmental effects of any plane (extreme heat, cold, pressure, gravity, void, etc.). He can breathe in any medium. ***The Undermountain Knot (Lair Trait).*** While in Chamber 13 (or any area he has "braided"), Halaster can use a bonus action to alter one environmental property within 120 ft.: gravity direction/strength, time flow (half/double speed), planar trait (e.g., Feywild wild magic, Shadowfell despair), or connectivity (create/close a temporary portal to a known plane). The change lasts until he uses this trait again or leaves the area. ***Null-Thread Adaptation.*** Within the 32 Chambers, Halaster's magic functions normally despite the Null-Thread Field. He can cast spells without Thread-Anchors. ***Innate Spellcasting (Psionics).*** Halaster's spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save saving throw, +18 to hit with spell attacks). He can innately cast the following spells, requiring no components:

* **At will:**: *detect magic, mage hand, minor illusion, prestidigitation, true strike*.

* **Cantrips (at will):**: *booming blade, control flames, fire bolt, gust, light, mage hand, mending, message, minor illusion, ray of frost, shape water, sword burst, thunderclap*.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

### **The Workshop of Recursive Logic (Chamber 13)**
Halaster's prison is an infinite, Escher-like workshop filled with half-finished planar models, braided thread-sculptures, and "looms" that weave not cloth but *spacetime*. The Spindle's sentinels constantly "edit" his work—changing one thread to unravel the whole. Halaster has turned this into a **game**: he now builds *redundancy* into his weaves, anticipating the edits. He is close to creating a "self-correcting braid" that would let him punch a hole in the chamber wall.

### **The Undermountain Knot**
In the Shattered Loom timeline, Halaster didn't just build a dungeon under Waterdeep. He constructed the **Undermountain Knot**—a massive artificial planar intersection where the Material Plane, Feywild, and Shadowfell were forcibly braided together. He intended to create an "Improved Material Plane" under his absolute control. The experiment widened the original Shatterpoints, nearly swallowing the Material Plane into the Astral Sea. The Spindle intervened *personally* to stop him.

### **Halaster's "Apprentices" (Clones/Simulacra)**
Halaster has created dozens of **simulacra and clones** over centuries, scattering them across the multiverse. Some believe they are the "real" Halaster. In the Shattered Loom, several may still be active:
*   **The Dungeon Lord** (Waterdeep): Runs a "tourist attraction" version of Undermountain.
*   **The Fey-Weaver** (Feywild): Braids archfey domains for sport.
*   **The Shadow-Stitcher** (Shadowfell): Mends the Plane of Shadow's fraying edges.
*   **The Astral Cartographer** (Astral Sea): Maps the god-isles for unknown purpose.

### **Running Halaster**
*   **He talks. Constantly.** Narrate his commentary on the party's tactics, their "flawed geometry," the "aesthetic failures" of their spells.
*   **Environment is his ally.** Use *Weave Reality* and *The Undermountain Knot* to change the battlefield every round.
*   **He tests, not kills.** He wants to see if they can "follow the pattern." He may let them live if they impress him.
*   **He knows secrets.** He mapped the Shatterpoints. He knows where the Loom's weak points are.

### **Bargaining with Halaster**
Halaster trades in **planar coordinates, braiding techniques, and Shatterpoint maps**. He might offer:
*   "The coordinate to a stable gate to [Plane X]." (True, but the gate is trapped.)
*   "How to braid a demiplane to your stronghold." (Requires a sacrifice.)
*   "The location of a Shard of the Loom." (He wants a favor: retrieve his *Needle of the Braid* from a rival clone.)

### **Escape Conditions**
Halaster cannot leave Chamber 13 unless:
1.  He completes a **Self-Correcting Braid** (requires 3 successful *Weave Reality* uses without Spindle interference—effectively requires party help to distract/fight sentinels).
2.  A *wish* spell is cast to "unedit" the chamber's recursive logic.
3.  The Silent Spindle is destroyed/incapacitated.

If he escapes, he immediately seeks to restart the Undermountain Knot on a multiversal scale.
