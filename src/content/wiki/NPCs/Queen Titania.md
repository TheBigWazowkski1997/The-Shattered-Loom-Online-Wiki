# Queen Titania (The Summer Queen & Sovereign of Light)

![[queen_titania.jpg]]

* **Race**: Archfey (Primordial Fey Demigod of Light, Life, and Summer)
* **Faction**: [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Factions/The Seelie Court|The Seelie Court]] (Supreme Sovereign)
* **Role**: Ruler of the Verdant Spire / Empress of the Summerlands / Arbiter of the High Revels
* **Appearance**: An ethereal figure of breathtaking, incandescent beauty. Titania stands tall and graceful, crowned with living wisteria and spun sunlight that weaves through her cascading golden-amber hair. Her gown is fashioned of living morning-glory petals, shimmering gossamer, and liquid amber that shifts hue with her temperament. Her eyes are pools of boundless, warm golden sunlight, utterly devoid of pupils. When she laughs, orchids bloom spontaneously around her; when she frowns, crops wither forty leagues away.
* **Personality**: Radiant, charismatic, captivating, yet profoundly capricious and prideful. Titania embodies the overwhelming abundance of summer—both its life-giving warmth and its merciless drought. She regards mortals as fragile, beautiful butterflies: delightful to listen to and collect, but easily crushed by accident when she gestures too enthusiastically. She never forgets a perceived slight, and a broken promise to her is punished with centuries of living imprisonment in amber.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Queen Titania rarely stoops to mundane physical combat. She fights surrounded by her Solar Eladrin champions, animated treant wardens, and choirs of singing pixies. When provoked to wrath, she commands raw solar radiance, absolute emotional charm, and the living roots of the primeval forest.

## Roleplay & Lore Notes

* **The Loom's Golden Warp**: Titania views the Shattered Loom as a magnificent instrument whose broken strings must be tuned to eternal vibrancy. She seeks to gather Weave fragments to bathe the multiverse in perpetual summer, oblivious to the fact that absolute light would scorch mortal reality into ash.
* **Courtly Diplomacy**: Titania will grant audiences to mortals who offer exquisite, original artistic creations—a song never sung, a sculpture carved from comet-ice, or a tragedy written in honest tears. Those who bore her are transformed into marble candelabras in the Grand Ballroom.
* **The Schism with Oberon**: Though married to King Oberon, their relationship is an eternal tempest of passionate reunions and furious estrangements. A party skilled in diplomacy can play upon their mutual jealousy to extract planar favors.
