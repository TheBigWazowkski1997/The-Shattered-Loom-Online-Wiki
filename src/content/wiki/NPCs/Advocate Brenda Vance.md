# Advocate Brenda Vance (The Loophole Mistress)

![[attachments/advocate_brenda_vance.jpg]]

* **Race**: Tiefling
* **Faction**: The Clause & Loophole Repository / Axiom Bar Association
* **Role**: Chief Planar Advocate, Contract Arbitrator, Legal Fixer
* **Appearance**: A sharp, immaculately dressed tiefling woman with crimson skin, swept-back horns filed into geometric points, and tailored velvet business robes embroidered with golden runes of legal binding. She wears silver-framed bifocals that glow faintly when inspecting parchment for forgery, and keeps an inkhorn filled with chimera blood at her belt.
* **Personality**: Incisive, razor-witted, and delightfully cynical. Brenda views contracts as intricate duels of intellect. She holds immense disdain for crude violence, believing that a well-placed subordinate clause can bring down an empire faster and more permanently than any army of paladins.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Brenda is a formidable enchantment and abjuration specialist who weaponizes binding geas commands, legal counter-spells, and contractual curses.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Legal Counseling**: If the party is bound by an infernal pact, an unfair planar guild indenture, or a death-debt with a crime boss, Brenda is the ultimate ally. For a hefty retainer (or a favor involving stolen municipal codices), she can rewrite their contracts and annul their obligations.
* **The Broken Courts Trial**: She enlists the party to guard her and her client during the treacherous trial in the Judgement Hall.
