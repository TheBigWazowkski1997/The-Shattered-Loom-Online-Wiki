# Hook (The Thread-Cartographer)

![[attachments/hook_eccentric_scholar.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Changeling
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Pale Lodge/The Pale Lodge|The Pale Lodge]] (Unaffiliated — Aspirant)
* **Role**: Cartographer / Planar Scholar / Loom Fragment Tracker
* **Appearance**: Hook rarely keeps the same face for more than a few minutes. Their features shift subtly mid-conversation—nose broadening, ears pointing, eyes changing color—as if trying on identities like coats. What remains constant: fingers permanently stained with charcoal, ash, and a strange **ember-red ink** that seems to writhe under the skin. They wear layers of patched scholar's robes weighted with hidden map cases, and a spectacles frame with no lenses that they tap when thinking. Their maps *hum softly* when unfolded near magical effects or Loom-threads.
* **Personality**: Manic, brilliant, and desperately eager to prove their worth. Hook speaks in excited half-sentences, often correcting themselves aloud ("No, not *ley lines*—thread-tension! *Tug*, not flow!"). They are not ambitious for power or gold—only **recognition**. They believe that if they map the Loom itself (or even one fragment perfectly), Telara will *see* them and invite them into the Pale Lodge. They treat the party as both protectors and *witnesses* to their magnum opus.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Hook is a formidable support and utility scholar. They avoid direct combat, using illusions, mobility, and their **Thread-Sense** to reposition. If forced to fight, they fight *dirty*—using environmental hazards, alchemical concoctions, and their shapechanging abilities to escape. Their true value is **navigation and lore**: they can track Loom fragments, identify planar bleeds, and stabilize wild magic surges.

* **Shapechanger**: Hook can alter their appearance and voice at will, mimicking any humanoid they have seen with uncanny accuracy.
* **Thread-Sense**: Hook can instinctively sense the tension of Loom-threads, detecting planar boundaries, portals, Shatterpoints, and Loom fragments, and can produce detailed Thread-Maps of surveyed regions.
* **Evasive & Slippery**: Skilled at dodging danger and turning lethal planar bursts into glancing blows.
* **Innate Talents & Magic**: Hook casts magic intuitively to assist in survival and research, frequently employing utility illusions and divination spells such as *detect magic*, *identify*, *comprehend languages*, *locate object*, *scrying*, *misty step*, *invisibility*, and *nondetection*.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

### **The Scholar's Journal**
Hook's prized possession. A leather-bound tome with pages that **scorch but do not burn** when opened near spellcasting. Contains:
*   Hand-drawn maps overlaid with glowing ember-red filaments tracking "energy currents that *tug* like thread under tension."
*   Notes on *heat without flame* and *fire that whispers*.
*   References to a **Resonant Anchor**—the Loom Fragment.
*   **Planar Resonance:** When near a Loom Fragment, Shatterpoint, or major planar bleed, the Journal reacts (pages turn on their own, ink rearranges, hum intensifies), offering clear guidance and clues to help locate the source.

### **Hook's Motivation: The Pale Lodge**
Hook is *unaffiliated* but does extensive work for the Pale Lodge (mapping planar bleeds, tracking fragment resonance, cataloging Shatterpoints). They believe **one perfect map** will earn them the Mark and an invitation. They are **not** a Lodge agent—they have no authority, no backup, and no safety net. This makes them *desperate* to succeed, which can lead to reckless decisions.

### **Running Hook**
*   **Constant narration.** Hook mutters observations: "Gravity's lighter here—Feywild bleed. Three percent. Maybe four. *Four*. Yes, four."
*   **Face shifts = emotional tells.** When scared, their face smooths to featureless. When excited, features sharpen. When lying, they *over*-stabilize (uncanny valley).
*   **They need the party.** Hook is fragile. They hire/attach to capable groups. If the party protects them, they are *incredibly* loyal—and will push themselves to exhaustion mapping for them.
*   **The ink is weird.** The ember-red ink on their fingers *moves* when they sleep. Sometimes it writes things Hook doesn't remember writing.

### **Hook's Fate (Campaign Arcs)**
| Outcome | Consequence |
|---------|-------------|
| **Survives & Succeeds** | Becomes a **recurring NPC**. Gains a Loom Mark (minor). Pale Lodge acknowledges them as "Associate Cartographer." Provides maps/intel between sessions. |
| **Survives & Fails** | Obsession deepens. Starts taking *risks*—hiring shadier crews, entering unstable rifts alone. May become a **rival** or **tragic figure** the party must save again. |
| **Dies Heroically** | Their Journal becomes a **Relic** (*Tome of the Thread-Cartographer*). Telara *does* notice—granting the party a vision/boon, but the Journal whispers Hook's final thoughts. |
| **Dies Pointlessly** | The Journal is just a book. The Pale Lodge files a report. A quiet tragedy. |

### **Hook's Stat Block Variants**
*   **Level 1–4 Party:** Use as-is (CR 3).
*   **Level 5–8 Party:** Increase to 7th-level wizard (CR 5). Add *counterspell*, *dimension door*, *arcane eye*. HP 82.
*   **Level 9+ Party:** Hook has found a **Loom Fragment Shard** embedded in their stylus. Gains *legendary actions* (1/round: *misty step*, *shield*, or *thread-sense pulse*). CR 7.
