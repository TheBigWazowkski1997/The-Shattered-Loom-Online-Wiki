# Elise, the Living Core (The Synthetic Spark)

![[elise_mordenheim.jpg]]

* **Race**: Flesh-Construct Paragon / Synthetic Human
* **Faction**: The Fugitives of Lamordia / Independent
* **Role**: The Living Spark of Lamordia / Masterpiece of Dr. Viktra Mordenheim
* **Appearance**: A breathtakingly beautiful woman appearing in her mid-twenties, with skin of marble-pale perfection laced with microscopic, faintly glowing gold surgical sutures. Her eyes are an uncanny, luminous blue that hums with cold galvanic light. Beneath her breastplate, visible through a translucent quartz panel over her heart, sits the **Spark of Life**—a crystalline apparatus housing an uncorrupted thread of the Shattered Loom that beats like a rhythmic heart.
* **Personality**: Melancholic, frightened, acutely self-aware, and desperate for freedom. Unlike common flesh golems, Elise possesses full human sentience, deep empathy, and an artistic soul. She lives in terror of Dr. Mordenheim, who views her not as a daughter or person, but as intellectual property to be dissected and rebuilt.

---

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Elise does not seek violence, preferring to flee using her extreme speed and cold immunity. When cornered, the Spark of Life within her surges defensively, releasing blinding galvanic pulses, freezing shockwaves, and kinetic barriers.

---

## Roleplay & Lore Notes

* **The Loom Thread Within:** The Spark of Life powering Elise is not a mundane galvanic battery; it is an intact, uncorrupted filament from the Shattered Loom that Dr. Mordenheim extracted from a planar anomaly. If removed, Elise will die, but the filament could be used to stabilize a decaying plane or cleanse a corrupted Loom Shard.
* **The Moral Dilemma:** The party will likely be hired by Dr. Mordenheim to capture Elise. Upon finding her in the freezing blizzards of Mount Grysl, they will face a critical moral decision: return her to Mordenheim for a massive gold bounty and cybernetic upgrades, or help her escape Lamordia into the Shadowsea.
* **Connection to the Keepers:** The Keepers of the Feather maintain quiet ties with Elise's safehouse network, smuggling her warm food and blankets while keeping her location hidden from Mordenheim's hounds.
