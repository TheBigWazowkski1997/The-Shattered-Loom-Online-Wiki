# Key NPCs - The Feywild & Courts of Faerie

The following legendary figures make up the sovereign Archfey, forest monarchs, shadow-stalkers, and primordial hunters that govern the radiant and savage tapestry of [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/Planes of Existence/The Material Plane/Feywild|The Feywild]].

* * *

## The Solar Sovereigns of Light (The Seelie Court)

The rulers of life, bloom, passion, and summer residing within the **Verdant Spire**:

* [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Queen Titania|Queen Titania]] — The Summer Queen & Empress of Light; Supreme Ruler of the Seelie Court
* [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/King Oberon|King Oberon]] — The Green Lord & Sovereign of Beasts; Consort King and Guardian of the Sylvan Groves

* * *

## The Shadows of Winter & Decay (The Unseelie Court)

The masters of frost, dark bargains, and nocturnal predation ruling from the **Gloaming Cradle**:

* [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/The Queen of Air and Darkness|The Queen of Air and Darkness]] — Sovereign of Night & Mistress of the Unseelie Court
* [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Prince Varyl, the Night Stalker|Prince Varyl, the Night Stalker]] — High Inquisitor and Chief Enforcer of the Unseelie Court

* * *

## The Primal & Neutral Archfey

Sovereigns of independent realms, ancient nomadic hunts, and cosmic sanctuaries:

* [[Project Shattered Loom/Project Shattered Loom/NPC's/Cernunnos, Master of the Wild Hunt|Cernunnos, Master of the Wild Hunt]] — Primeval Horned God of the Endless Chase
* **Queen Serelune, the Twilight Monarch** — Sovereign of the Twilight Glade and the Court of Stars
* **Lord Faelthorn, the Bloom King** — Archfey of the Blooming Vale, Hedonism, and the World-Hibiscus
* **Lady Marrowshade, the Wailing Widow** — Spectral Monarch of the Gloaming Mire and Lost Grief
* **Cinderheart, the Autumn Flame** — Eladrin Archfey of the Embergrove and the Hearth of Renewal
* **Kaelthas, the Winter Sovereign** — Stoic Ruler of the Frostwild Tundra and the Icebound Keep
* **Thalassia, the Briar Matron** — Mistress of the Thornmaze Court and the Living Briar Vaults

* * *

## Key Planar Assets, Merchants & Rogues

Influential brokers, outlaws, and scholars navigating the faerie borderlands:

* **Grimgog the Broker** — Master Goblin Merchant of Bramble-Cross; Buyer and Seller of True Names and Lifespans
* **Grandmother Yaga-Maeve** — Eldest Hag of the Central Cauldron; Arbiter of Market Law
* **Skitter the Quickling** — The Fastest Courier and Thief in Faerie
* **Lady Vesper of the Lily-Pads** — Eladrin Smuggler-Captain on the Lake of Shimmering Dreams
