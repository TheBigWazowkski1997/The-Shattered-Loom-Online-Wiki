# 🏹 Grand Huntmaster Torvald Blood-Tracer
### *Supreme Commander of the Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor, Slayer of the Filament-Gorgers*

> *"The beast does not care what god you pray to or what crown your grandfather wore. It smells your blood, calculates your weight, and charges. In that moment, title means nothing; only your footing and your draw-weight matter."*  
> — **Torvald Blood-Tracer**, to initiate trackers at the Hearth of the Spoor

---

## 📜 Identity & History

**Torvald Blood-Tracer** is a towering, scar-carved Goliath planar stalker who has spent over four centuries hunting apex abominations across all twenty-two planes of existence. He earned his title after single-handedly slaying the **Chasm-Gorging Behemoth** that nearly collapsed the primary filament tether of the Fractured Nexus. Under his stewardship, the scattered frontier lodges were unified into the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor**.

* **Title**: Grand Huntmaster of the Iron Spoor, High Warden of the Hearth.
* **Habitat / Domain**: The Hearth of the Spoor, The Fractured Nexus.
* **Creature Type**: Medium Humanoid (Goliath), True Neutral.
* **Associated Rules**: [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].

---

## 🎨 Visual Appearance & Gear

Torvald stands 7 feet 8 inches tall, his slate-gray goliath skin etched with glowing blue planar spoor runes. His broad shoulders are draped in the **Mantle of the Apex Stalker**, forged from the prismatic scales of an elder filament-drake and the fur of a dire winter wolf. Across his back hangs **Thread-Splitter**, a colossal +3 compound greatbow crafted from adamantine gorgon horn and spun loom-sinew.

### Traits

* **Apex Hunter's Eye**: Torvald's weapon attacks score a critical hit on a roll of 18–20.
* **Planar Spoor Senses**: Torvald cannot be surprised. He knows the exact location, creature type, and current hit points of any creature within 120 feet that has been injured in the last 24 hours.
* **Stone's Endurance (Recharge 5–6)**: When Torvald takes damage, he can use his reaction to roll 1d12 + 11 and reduce the damage by that total.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Torvald fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.

### Actions

* **Multiattack**: Torvald makes three attacks with **Thread-Splitter** (Greatbow) or four attacks with his **Slag-Tempered Hunting Machete**.
* **Thread-Splitter (Longbow +3)**: *Ranged Weapon Attack*: +13 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target. *Hit*: 18 (2d8 + 9) piercing damage plus 10 (3d6) force damage. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it must succeed on a DC 19 Strength saving throw or be pinned to the ground (speed 0 until an action is spent breaking free).
* **Slag-Tempered Machete (+2)**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +13 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit*: 15 (2d6 + 8) slashing damage plus 7 (2d6) fire damage.
* **Apex Volley (Recharge 5–6)**: Torvald fires a hail of explosive spoor-arrows into a 30-foot-radius sphere within 150 feet. Each creature in that area must make a DC 19 Dexterity saving throw, taking 45 (10d8) piercing and force damage on a failed save, or half on a success.

### Legendary Actions (3 Actions/Round)

* **Skirmisher's Reposition**: Torvald moves up to his speed without provoking opportunity attacks.
* **Target the Vitals**: Torvald makes one attack with Thread-Splitter.
* **Call of the Lodge (Costs 2 Actions)**: Torvald lets loose a thundering war-horn. Two loyal **Iron Spoor Stalkers** (CR 5) arrive to assist him at the start of the next round.
