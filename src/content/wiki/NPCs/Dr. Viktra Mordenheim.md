# Dr. Viktra Mordenheim (The Baroness of the Spark)

![[attachments/viktra_mordenheim.jpg]]

* **Race**: Human (Augmented Flesh-Artificer)
* **Faction**: The University of Ludendorf / Mordenheim Institute
* **Role**: Darklord of Lamordia / Master of Schloss Mordenheim
* **Appearance**: A severe, strikingly handsome woman in her late thirties with piercing slate-grey eyes, sharp cheekbones, and dark hair pulled tightly into a utilitarian bun. Her hands and forearms are reinforced with delicate, clockwork brass subdermal armatures and chemical injector ports. She wears a crisp, high-collared surgeon's coat of white oilcloth over tailored charcoal wool, stained with alchemical salts and conductive copper grease.
* **Personality**: Coldly rational, obsessed with scientific breakthrough, dismissive of magic and divine faith, and utterly devoid of remorse for the anatomical horrors she manufactures. Her singular curse is the pursuit of **Elise**, the living masterpiece into whom she transplanted an artificial soul—the Spark of Life—which continually flees her grasp.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Dr. Mordenheim rarely fights with bare hands; she commands hulking, lightning-powered flesh golems, constructs with integrated galvanic cannons, and automated surgical apparatus. In personal combat, she wields conductive scalpels, galvanic shockers, and pressurized chemical hypos delivering paralyzing neurotoxins.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Search for the Unbroken Thread:** Dr. Mordenheim does not believe in gods, souls, or magic. She views the "Loom" as a grand, quantum biological weave—the fundamental fabric of vitality. She believes that Elise's artificial heart houses an uncorrupted filament of this weave, explaining why Elise's body defies organic decay without needing blood or necromancy.
* **Relationship with the Party:** Should the party enter Lamordia, Mordenheim will attempt to hire them to track down Elise across the frozen wastes of Mount Grysl, promising access to her advanced flesh-grafting laboratories and experimental bio-alchemical stims.
* **Contact with Metallos:** Mordenheim secretly trades with the rogue artificers of the Gilded Crucible in Metallos on the Plane of Eternal Craft, acquiring insulated capacitors, titanium bone-pins, and conductive mercury conduits.
