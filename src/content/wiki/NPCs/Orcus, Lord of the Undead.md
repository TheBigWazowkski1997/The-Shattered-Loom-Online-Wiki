## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Orcus is the catastrophic lord of undeath whose mere presence chills the living soul. Wielding the dreaded Wand of Orcus, he commands legions of resurrected horrors, casts world-shaking necrotic death magic, and snuffs out life forces with cold indifference.

---

## **Roleplay & Lore Notes**
* **Personality**: Gluttonous, nihilistic, supremely weary of cosmic turmoil. Speaks in a low, resonant rumble that rattles teeth and bone.
* **Campaign Motivation**: Orcus is obsessed with hunting down the Raven Queen to seize control of the Shadowfell, merging it with Thanatos to freeze the multiverse's soul-threads permanently.
