# Sky Captain Kaelor Stormwing (Commander of the Skyguard)

* **Race**: Aarakocra
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane of Air/Cities of the Plane of Air/Zephyros|The Skyguard]] / The Concord of Winds
* **Role**: Supreme Commander of the Skyguard, Admiral of the Thunderhead Bastion
* **Appearance**: An imposing, broad-shouldered aarakocra warrior with feathers the color of dark storm clouds and sharp golden eyes that see with predatory clarity. He wears gleaming enchanted breastplate armor forged from skysteel and crackling with static energy. A pair of twin lightning-sheathed curved talons are strapped to his feet, and he carries *Tempest-Fang*, an electrified halberd forged in the heart of a captured hurricane.
* **Personality**: Honorable, stern, disciplined, and blunt. Kaelor has spent his life defending Zephyros from planar pirates, void predators, and elemental incursions. He despises political dithering and prefers decisive action. Despite his strict military demeanor, he cares deeply for the warriors under his command and will risk his life to ensure no soldier is left behind in the void.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

Kaelor is a devastating aerial skirmisher (formidable) who dives at blinding speed, strikes with lightning polearm attacks, and commands aerial squadrons with tactical precision.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Frustration with Politics**: Kaelor is weary of the Concord of Winds debating while external threats gather. If the party proves their tactical competence, Kaelor bypasses the bureaucrats and hires them directly for urgent military strikes.
* **Loyalty to Zephyros**: He will never betray the city, but he has no love for corrupt merchants or smugglers operating out of the Aether-Docks.
* **Quests**: He contracts the party to hunt down sky-corsair fleets, clear wild drake nests from vital flight corridors, and investigate reports of lightning drakes mutated by void energy.
