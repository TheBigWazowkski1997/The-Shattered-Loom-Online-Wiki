# The Silent Spool (Keeper of the Nexus Spire)

![[attachments/the_silent_spool.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Unknown (Ancient Construct / Eldritch Remnant)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Pale Lodge/The Pale Lodge|The Pale Lodge]]
* **Role**: Third of the Spindle Lords — Keeper of the Nexus Spire and Lodge Oracle
* **Appearance**: A hovering, cloaked figure whose "body" is a collection of twelve rotating golden rings of varying sizes, orbiting a dense core of shifting shadows. It does not speak. It has no visible face. When it focuses on a creature, the air tightens, and the rings slow, locking onto the target like a celestial mechanism fixing on a coordinate.
* **Personality**: Utterly inscrutable. It projects concepts, images, and sensations directly into the minds of those nearby — never words. It offers prophecy in the form of flashing visions and communicates urgency through the speed of its rings.

## Tactical Profile & Combat Demeanor

> [!NOTE]
> **Dungeon Master Directive**: Complete numeric statistics, Armor Class, Hit Points, spell slots, and action mechanics are restricted to the Dungeon Master's Master Vault.

The Silent Spool is an orbital weapon system as much as it is an entity. Its rings are independently mobile weapons, and it commands the space around it with gravitational and psychic force. It rarely fights — but when it does, it does so with the calm efficiency of a machine correcting an error.

---

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Hidden Truth:** The Spool is the physical shell of the entity that originally betrayed Telara — the "Silent Spindle" of legend, now bound in eternal corrective servitude. It is forced to help the Lodge fix the very thing it tried to destroy.
* **The Unpredicted Variable:** Its original betrayal was rooted in a logic even Telara could not comprehend — it believed the Loom *needed* to break to evolve. The PCs are the first "Anomalies of Potential" it did not predict. It watches them with something that approximates cautious curiosity.
* **Communicating with the Spool:** When the party interacts with it, describe the communication as feelings and images, not words. Show a flash of the party's faces. Show threads weaving together. Show a clock stopped at the moment of the Shattering. Never use dialogue.
