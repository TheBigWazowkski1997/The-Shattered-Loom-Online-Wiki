# ⚒️ Forge-Mistress Vespera Quick-Flint
### *Supreme Trophy-Smith of the Iron Spoor, Shaper of Apex Chitin and Primordial Filaments*

> *"Any blacksmith can heat iron and bang it flat. But when you are working with the chitin of an Abyssal gorgon or the lightning-plumes of a gale roc, you aren't working with dead ore—you're working with trapped planar physics. One wrong tap of the hammer and the blade detonates in your face."*  
> — **Vespera Quick-Flint**, at the Grand Crucible

---

## 📜 Identity & Background

**Forge-Mistress Vespera Quick-Flint** is a fire-blooded Azer-Dwarf master artisan who oversees all mastercraft weapon and armor commissions for the **Grand Guild of the Iron Spoor**. Based at the High Master Forge inside the Hearth of the Spoor, she has developed the secret alchemical flux formulas required to weld dragon slag, kraken chitin, and golden Loom threads into legendary armaments.

* **Title**: Supreme Trophy-Smith, Master of the Grand Crucible.
* **Habitat**: The High Master Forge, The Hearth of the Spoor (The Fractured Nexus).
* **Creature Type**: Medium Humanoid (Dwarf, Fire-Blooded), Lawful Neutral.
* **Services**: Crafts rare and very rare planar trophy relics under [[Custom Rules/The Interplanar Hunting, Harvesting & Trophy-Crafting Ruleset|The Trophy-Crafting Ruleset]].
