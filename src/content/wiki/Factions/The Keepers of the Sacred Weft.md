### Core Belief

The Keepers of the Sacred Weft believe that when the goddess Telara wove the multiverse, she did not just spin a random tapestry—she wove a **Divine Blueprint**, a master design of perfect, harmonious reality. When the Loom was shattered, the blueprint was fragmented, but not lost. 

While the **Pale Lodge** searches the planes to blindly patch together the shattered rifts, the Keepers believe that restoring the Loom without the original design is a recipe for cosmic horror. They believe that an incorrect reweaving would permanently warp the laws of nature, creating a monstrous, unstable version of reality. 

Thus, the Keepers guard the surviving fragments of the **Telarian Codices**—the closest thing to the goddess's original weaving patterns. They do not share this knowledge with outsiders, believing that mortal minds, and even the well-intentioned Pale Lodge, are too impatient and prone to misuse it.

> _“To mend a shroud in the dark is to sew a shroud for the world. We must weave by the light of the original thread.”_

---

## Presence on the Material Plane

To the common folk, the Keepers do not exist. To the local clergy, they appear to be a small, highly insular monastic order of quiet scribes and weavers.

### The Monastery of the Quiet Loom
Their primary base of operations is hidden beneath a mundane, quiet monastery on the Material Plane:
* **The Surface**: A modest stone monastery dedicated to a minor deity of craft and patience. The monks sell beautiful, hand-woven tapestries to support their quiet lives.
* **The Under-Weft Vault**: Deep beneath the monastery's crypts lies a sprawling library and magical sanctuary built into the bedrock. This sanctuary is shielded from planar divination and contains the **Skein Chamber**, where raw magic is channeled through physical looms to track planar anomalies across the multiverse.

## Organization & Structure

### The Master Weavers (The Triumvirate)
Three elders who have dedicated centuries to translating the fragmented pages of the Telarian Codices. They make all decisions regarding when to intervene in planar affairs.

### Skein-Keepers
Monks who spend their lives in the Under-Weft Vault, tending to the magical looms. They monitor the vibration of planar threads, reading them like musical strings to detect when a major rift or shard is being exploited.

### Thread-Carriers (Field Agents)
The active, secret agents of the order. They are trained in espionage, stealth, and planar survival. A Thread-Carrier is sent into the world to quietly secure fragments of the Loom or prevent others from tampering with the threads of destiny.

## Relationship to the Pale Lodge

The Keepers and the Pale Lodge have an **uneasy, conditional relationship**:

* **Different Philosophies**: The Pale Lodge acts like emergency field medics—they see a planar wound and seal it immediately. The Keepers act like surgeons with a blueprint—they believe some wounds must remain open or be mended in specific, complex ways to align with Telara's original layout.
* **Cooperation**: When a catastrophic rift threatens to collapse a region, or when the **Cult of the Broken Thread** actively poisons a Ley Line, the Keepers will send a Thread-Carrier to feed information or assistance to the Pale Lodge.
* **Secrecy**: The Keepers never reveal their base of operations or the existence of the Telarian Codices to the Lodge. When they work together, it is always through intermediaries, anonymous messages, or deniable agents.

## Magic & Aesthetic

Keeper magic is incredibly precise, structured, and visually clean:
* Spells appear as glowing, golden threads that snap perfectly into place.
* They excel in **abjuration** (shielding the tapestry) and **divination** (reading the threads of fate).
* Their symbols include a **Golden Spindle** surrounded by three concentric rings, or a simple **horizontal line intersected by three vertical threads** (the Weft).

## Key Figures

### 1. **Grand Archivist Hesperia**
**Role:** Keeper of the Codices | **Alignment:** Lawful Neutral
A stern, ancient elf whose eyes have grown milky-white from decades of reading the glowing runes of the Telarian Codices. Hesperia is extremely protective of the order's knowledge. She firmly believes that sharing the blueprint with the Pale Lodge would lead to a rushed, catastrophic attempt to rebuild the Loom.
* **Personality**: Cold, calculated, speaks in quiet, deliberate sentences.
* **DM Use**: An obstacle when the party needs deep planar lore. She will demand a high price (usually retrieving a lost artifact) before sharing even a single line of prophecy.

### 2. **Brother Malakai, the Pale Liaison**
**Role:** Active Thread-Carrier | **Alignment:** Neutral Good
Malakai is the order's primary contact with the outside world. He is younger, more practical, and recognizes that the Keepers' isolationism might cause them to lose the war before they can even begin the reweaving. He secretly respects the Pale Lodge's bravery and often pushes the boundaries of his orders to help them.
* **Personality**: Charismatic, slightly cynical, wears simple travel-stained robes that hide throwing daggers and scroll cases.
* **DM Use**: The perfect NPC to introduce the faction to the players, deliver secret missions, or rescue them when a plan goes wrong.

## Backstory Hooks for Your Player

Here are a few ways your player can be tied to the Keepers of the Sacred Weft:

1. **The Skein-Marked**: The player was born with a birthmark resembling a complex, glowing knot of thread. The Keepers found them as a child, recognizing them as "destiny-woven"—someone whose path is bound to the restoration of the Loom. They were trained as a Thread-Carrier.
2. **The Stolen Page**: The player was an initiate who discovered a forbidden truth in a page of the Telarian Codices. Realizing the elders were hiding information that could save lives *now*, the player stole the page and fled. They are now hunted by the order's silent enforcers, yet they still use the page's secrets to protect the Material Plane.
3. **The Unwitting Loom-Key**: The player was rescued from a planar rift by Brother Malakai. They carry a dormant fragment of Telara's Loom inside their soul. The Keepers are keeping a close, secret eye on them, occasionally sending anonymous aid to ensure the "key" doesn't fall into the hands of the Cult of the Broken Thread.
