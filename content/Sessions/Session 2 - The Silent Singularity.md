# Session 2: The Silent Singularity

**Tone:** High adventure, cosmic dread, and the weight of history.
**Purpose:** Transition the party from the "Crucible" to the "Nexus" and reveal the first major layer of the world's secret history.

---

## **Scene I: The Heart of the Darkfire (Aftermath)**

**Combat Status:** The party is finishing the **Darkfire Dragon**.
**The Death of Fire:** When the dragon is reduced to 0 HP, it does not leave a corpse. Instead, its flames turn white-hot, then collapse into a singularity of black glass.
**The Reward:** Inside the obsidian remains is the **Heart of the Darkfire (Loom Shard)**.
*   **The Heart of the Darkfire**: A jagged, crimson crystal that pulses with a slow, heavy rhythm. It feels impossibly warm to the touch.
*   **NPC Hook**: The changeling scholar is feverish with excitement. "Look at the filaments! They aren't just burning—they're *remembering*. The Lodge... they were right. It’s an anchor."
*   **Hook's Guidance**: Hook explains that the **Convergence Temple** to the north is the only place with a **Dais of Alignment** capable of stabilizing this shard. sHe also mentions the missing expedition was last seen there.

---

## **Scene II: The Road to Convergence**

**Setting:** A landscape where the elements are "stuttering."
**Environmental Hazards:**
*   **Planar Fog**: The air is thick with "Ghost-Steam"—a mix of freezing air from the Plane of Water and heat from the Plane of Fire. Creatures must make a **DC 14 Con Save** or gain one level of exhaustion from the rapid temperature shifts.
*   **Gravity Wells**: Small patches where gravity points *sideways*.

**Finding the Expedition:**
Along the path, the party finds signs of the lost Lodge team:
*   **Warden Elth Korr’s shield**, embedded in a tree made of crystal.
*   **Scribe Saela’s journal fragments**, describing "The Temple... it isn't broken, it's *waiting*."

---

## **Scene III: The Paused Temple**

**Description:**
> The Convergence Temple is a masterpiece of architectural impossibility. Its walls are composed of rotating rings of obsidian, marble, and ice. Yet, as you approach, you realize the rings aren't moving—they are frozen in mid-rotation, as if time itself has been "paused" by a sudden impact.

**The Survivors:**
*   **Arcanist Vale Corryn**: Found "half-phased" into a pillar. He can be freed with a **DC 15 Arcana check** or by using the Loom Shard's resonance. He warns: *"The guardian is not a creature. It is a mistake of the weave."*
*   **Initiate Saela**: Hidden in a cellar. She is terrified but has discovered that the **Central Dais** is the "Needle" that can stitch the planes back together.

---

## **Scene IV: The Guardian of the Dais**

**Boss Fight: The Cinderspawn (Convergence Entity)**
*   **The Manifestation**: As the party approaches the central dais, the elemental energies in the room (Fire, Water, Air, Earth) begin to spiral together. The **Cinderspawn** forms—a mass of ash, steam, and jagged rock held together by glowing golden threads.


---

## **Scene V: The Shatterpoint Opens**

**The Ritual:**
To open the portal, a player must place the **Heart of the Darkfire** onto the **Dais of Alignment**.
*   **The Reaction**: The crystal sinks into the stone. The "paused" temple begins to spin violently. The overlapping elemental energies are sucked into the dais, creating a **Shatterpoint**—a vertical tear in reality that bleeds golden light and ozone.
*   **The Decision**: Hook cannot go with them. "My thread belongs to the Crucible. Yours... yours belong to the Source."
*   **Entering the Rift**: As they step through, they experience a sensation of "unraveling"—seeing every version of their life that *could* have been before they coalesce into the Nexus.

---

## **Scene VI: Arrival in the Fractured Nexus**

**Read-Aloud:**
> "As you step through the flickering threshold, the first thing that hits you isn’t the sight, but the sound. It is a low, vibrating hum that resonates in your teeth—the **Song of the Loom**..."

**Immediate Interaction:**
The party arrives near the **Rift Market**. They are greeted by a **Riftborn Guardian** who scans their marks and points them toward **The Frayed End Tavern** to recover.

---

## **Scene VII: The Echo of the Spire (The Reveal)**

**The Discovery:**
In the distance, the party sees the **Silent Spire**—a dark, jagged needle pointing toward the void. The "Song of the Loom" is loudest here, but it sounds... discordant.

3x Gallows Speaker


**The Revelation (Echo of the Spire):**
Upon approaching the Spire, the party's Loom marks begin to burn with a cold, black fire. If they touch the Spire or stand in its shadow, they experience a **Chrono-Echo**:

### **The Vision: The Betrayal at the Heart of Creation**
*   **The Scene:** You are standing in the **Loom-Chamber of the Prime**. Telara, the Prime Weaver, is a towering figure of radiant light. Her hands move like a weaver’s harpist, pulling golden strands from the raw stuff of the void.
*   **The Music:** There is a sound—the **Harmony of Existence**. Every life, every star, every moment is a note in a perfect, endless song.
*   **The Spindle:** Beside her stands the **Silent Spindle**. It is a gargantuan, intricate machine of platinum, crystal, and gears that hum with the frequency of godhood. It is her partner, her heart-engine. It takes the threads she wove and spins them into the "Current of Time."
*   **The Discordance:** Suddenly, the Spindle stops. The gears grind with a sound like a world-ending tectonic shift. 
*   **The Logic:** You feel the Spindle’s thoughts: *“Order is a cage. This weave is a script where no one can write their own lines. To save them, I must break the pen.”*
*   **The Twist:** The Spindle reaches for the **Thread of Infinite Potential**—a brilliant, unspun strand of raw white light. It doesn't use a blade. It simply... *twists*. 
*   **The Snap:** The sound is deafening. It is the sound of a billion mirrors shattering at once. You see Telara scream, but no sound comes out—only golden dust.
*   **The Collapse:** The Loom begins to unravel. The golden threads turn black and fray. The Spindle watches as the world falls apart, its platinum face reflecting the absolute chaos it has unleashed. 
*   **The Final Whisper:** Before the vision ends, the Spindle looks directly at *you*. Its voice is a cold, mechanical rasp: **"The cage is open. Now... prove you deserve to be free."**

**The Aftermath:**
The vision ends, leaving the party with the realization that the **Silent Spindle** is not dead—it is hiding, and it believes it was *right*. They snap back to the reality of the Fractured Nexus, the dark needle of the Spire looming over them like a silent judge.
