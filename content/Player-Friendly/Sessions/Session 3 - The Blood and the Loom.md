# Session Plan: Session 3 — The Crimson Thread of Ravenloft

**Date:** Monday, June 8, 2026
**Location:** The Fractured Nexus → Barovia (Shadowfell)
**Primary Goal:** Retrieve the **Loom Fragment of the Crimson Weave** from Strahd von Zarovich.

---

## **Part I: The Frayed End (The Hook)**

The party begins at **The Frayed End** tavern in the **Fractured Nexus**, reeling from the vision at the Silent Spire.
*   **The Contact:** **Malphas the Broker** (a high-ranking contract devil from the Ossuary of Rebirth) or a Pale Lodge agent approach the party. They noticed the resonance flare-up at the Spire.
*   **The Intelligence:** A powerful "Loom Fragment" has been identified in a pocket-dimension of the Shadowfell known as **Barovia**. 
*   **The Conflict:** The fragment is being used as a "Metaphysical Anchor" by the domain's ruler, **Strahd von Zarovich**. Without it, Barovia would likely have been swept away by the Tide of Threads during the Shattering. Strahd uses its power to keep his "cattle" trapped and his own immortality intact.
*   **The Reward:** Malphas offers a "Planar Compass" (which detects nearby Loom fragments) if they bring the fragment back—or at least its resonance.

---

## **Part II: Crossing the Fog**

*   **The Portal:** The party must find a **Vistani Shadow-Crossing** in the Nexus's "Void-Side," shadowed by the Silent Spire.
*   **The Transition:** Describe the golden, humming filaments of the Nexus being swallowed by cold, choking grey mists. The sound of the Loomforge fades, replaced by the distant, rhythmic tolling of a funeral bell.
*   **Arrival:** The party finds themselves on the **Old Svalich Road**. The trees are skeletal, the air smells of damp earth and rot. The "Crimson Resonance" of the fragment is a faint, rhythmic pulse in their Loom-marks, like a heartbeat.




---

### **Strahd’s Monologue: The Architecture of the Cage**
Strahd stands at the head of the table, gesturing to the window where the mists of Barovia swirl against the glass.

> "You come from the Nexus. A city built on the scraps of a shattered dream. You walk streets stitched together with hope and desperation, calling it 'freedom.' But tell me, Weaver-touched... what is freedom without a foundation?
> 
> When the Silent Spindle 'liberated' the multiverse, it did not give you wings. It gave you a fall that has lasted a thousand years. My land, my Barovia, was falling with you. It was fraying at the edges, the very memories of my people bleeding into the void. 
> 
> Then, I found the Crimson Weave. To you, it is a 'fragment' to be collected. To me, it is the only honest thing left in existence. I did not use it to rebuild a broken world; I used it to *harden* a perfect one. Within these mists, there is no 'planar bleed.' There is no 'chaos of threads.' There is only my will, and the stability that comes with it.
> 
> You wish to take it? To return it to the 'Loom'? You would pull the rug from under a thousand souls just to satisfy a machine’s design. You call me a monster for keeping them here, yet you would be the ones to let them dissolve into nothingness just to see the Loom spin again. 
> 
> I am not the thief of their fate. I am the only one who bothered to tie the knot."

## **Part V:   Stability**

The party realizes that simply taking the fragment will cause Barovia to "un-anchor" and eventually be consumed by the Tide of Threads. To avoid this moral catastrophe, they have path to stabilize the domain:

### The Ritual of the Fanes (Restoring the Land)
Strahd has "choked" the land's natural spirits (The Ladies of the Fanes) to force the Loom Fragment to do all the work. To restore them, the party must visit the three ancient Fane sites, each guarded by a member of the Hag Dragon Coven led by [[Baba Lysaga]]:
1.  **The Forest Fane**: Hijacked by **Morrigan the Knot-Tangler** (Adult Hag Dragon) and her circle of corrupted druids and needle blights. Morrigan uses the forest's tangled roots and path-loop illusions to trap the party.
    *   *Tactical Mechanic (Chronal Link)*: As long as the other Fanes remain corrupted, Morrigan can channel spectral projections of her sisters to gain access to Coven Spellcasting (up to 5th-level spells).
2.  **The Swamp Fane (The Ruins of Berez)**: Hijacked by **Vervain the Spindle** (Adult Hag Dragon) and **Baba Lysaga** (their leader). Vervain accelerates decay and rot, while Baba Lysaga fights from her flying dragon skull, backed by the **Creeping Hut**.
    *   *Tactical Mechanic (Chronal Link)*: If the other two dragons are still alive, Vervain and Baba Lysaga can channel spectral projections of the sisters, allowing access to high-level Coven Spellcasting (up to 7th-level spells, or 9th-level spells if they manage to summon the physical dragons to Berez).
3.  **The Mountain Fane**: Located near the Amber Temple, guarded by **Gryla the Shear** (Adult Hag Dragon) and stone-weaving guardians. Gryla uses mountain winds to cut the future timeline of the players.
    *   *Tactical Mechanic (Chronal Link)*: Like Morrigan, Gryla can channel her sisters' spectral projections to cast Coven Spellcasting (up to 5th-level spells) as long as the other Fanes are corrupted.

*   **The Campaign Strategy**: If the party cleanses the Forest and Mountain Fanes first, they permanently sever the Chronal Link. This prevents the remaining dragons from using coven spells, leaving them with only their individual stat blocks and making the final showdown against Baba Lysaga in Berez significantly easier.
*   **The Task:** The party must visit each site, defeat the guardians, and perform a "Thread-Binding" ritual.
*   **The Cost:** This requires a high-level Druid or Nature check (DC 18) at each fane and the sacrifice of a magical item at each site to act as a permanent conduit for the land's energy.
*   **The Result:** The land becomes self-sustaining. The Loom Fragment can be removed without the domain collapsing, though Strahd will lose his "un-anchored" immortality and become vulnerable.

### **Travel: Castle Ravenloft to the Ruins of Berez**

The journey from the heights of **Castle Ravenloft** in the east to the sunken **Ruins of Berez** in the south is a perilous trek spanning approximately **20 miles** of winding mountain paths, haunted woods, and decaying swampland.

---

#### **Journey Stages & Landmarks**

1. **Stage I: The Descent (Castle Ravenloft to River Ivlis Crossroads - 4 miles)**
   - *Atmosphere:* A steep, winding path down the Pillar of Ravenloft. The shadow of the castle clings to the party. 
   - *Hazard:* The biting mountain wind. Characters must make a **DC 12 Constitution save** or have disadvantage on initiative checks for the first encounter.![[BONEYARD.pdf]]
1. **Stage II: The Old Svalich Road (Ivlis Crossroads to Luna River Crossroads - 10 miles)**
   - *Atmosphere:* Dense, oppressive woods. Skeletal trees bend over the road like ribcages. The golden threads of the Nexus are entirely gone here, replaced by thick grey fog.
   - *Key Landmark:* The Gates of Barovia, which groan open and slam shut on their own.
3. **Stage III: The Amber Haze (Luna River Crossroads to Swamp Borders - 3 miles)**
   - *Atmosphere:* The air turns warm and wet, smelling of rot. They enter the **12-mile Chronal Decay radius** and the **6-mile Amber Haze**.
   - *Mechanical Effect:* Characters feel a strange acceleration in their pulse; wounds scab over and itch, and rations decay instantly unless sealed.
4. **Stage IV: The Drowned Swamps (Swamp Borders to Berez - 3 miles)**
   - *Atmosphere:* Difficult terrain. Sunken peat, black standing water, and giant weeping willows. The swamp is quiet, save for the hum of swarming insects.
   - *Hazard:* **Vervain’s Spies**. Local wildlife has a single central eye. Any stealth check to avoid notice is made with disadvantage.
