# The Far Realm: The Un-Woven Void

In the **Shattered Loom Cosmology**, the **Far Realm** is not a plane in the traditional sense; it is the "Non-Space" that exists outside, beneath, and before the Loom. It is the realm of **Absolute Potential without Form**, a place where threads do not exist because there is no Weaver to bind them.

While the **Abyss** seeks to unravel the Loom and the **Hells** seek to freeze it, the Far Realm is simply **Incompatible** with the Loom. It is the source of the **Great Dreamer** and the home of **Pale Night**, the "Mother of Non-Existence."

---

## 1. The Nature of the Far Realm: The "Un-Spun"
If the multiverse is a tapestry, the Far Realm is the raw, chaotic wool before it was ever touched by a spindle. It is a place of "Impossible Geometry" and "Non-Linear Time."
*   **The Lack of Threads**: Entities from the Far Realm do not have **Soul-Threads**. They are "Glitches" in the design of Telara. When they enter the **Fractured Nexus**, they do not follow the laws of causality or destiny, causing the local weave to "fray" or "stutter" in their presence.
*   **The Shroud**: Telara created a barrier known as the **Shroud** to separate the Loom from this entropic void. Since the **Shattering**, the Shroud has developed "Thin-Points," allowing Far Realm influence to leak into the multiverse as **Abyssal Static** or **Eldritch Corruption**.

---

## 2. Pale Night: The Veiled Mother
**Pale Night** is the presiding consciousness of this void. She is "She-Who-Waits-Before," an entity that predates the act of weaving.
*   **Her Intent**: She does not actively "attack" the Loom; her mere attention is enough to cause reality to lose its definition. To her, the Loom is a "Beautiful Lie" of order that she intends to eventually wake from.
*   **The Great Dreamer**: Created by Pale Night as a bridge between the Far Realm and the Loom, the Dreamer is a "Living Nightmare" that feeds on the structure of the multiverse, turning ordered threads into the chaotic static of the Far Realm.

---

## 3. The Denizens: The "Anomalies" (Aberrations)
The creatures described in the ancient texts (such as the *Lords of Madness*) are viewed in the Shattered Loom as **Planar Parasites** or **Logic Errors**.

### **Aboleths: The Memory of the Un-Woven**
Aboleths claim to remember the multiverse before the Loom was ever built. They view Telara as an "Upstart Weaver" and her creation as a temporary prison.
*   **Setting Adaptation**: Aboleths seek to "Liquidize" the threads of history, returning the multiverse to a state of primordial, un-threaded memory where they were the supreme masters.

### **Mind Flayers: The Harvesters of the Future-Thread**
Mind Flayers are often whispered to be "Refugees from a Future that was Unravelled."
*   **Setting Adaptation**: They consume the "Psionic Resonance" of soul-threads, siphoning the potential of a creature's destiny to fuel their own "Elder Brains," which act as local "Anti-Spindles" that generate a signal of alien order.

### **Beholders: The Solipsistic Weavers**
Beholders are "Individual Shatterpoints." Each Beholder believes its own vision is the only "True Loom."
*   **Setting Adaptation**: Their central eye projects a "Field of Null-Reality" that temporarily un-weaves any magic or divine thread it gazes upon, asserting the Beholder’s own warped version of existence as the only reality.

---

## 4. The Waking Void: The Borderland
The region where the Far Realm touches the edges of the **Astral Sea** is known as the **Waking Void**.
*   **Dreamspawn**: Entities born from the **Great Dreamer’s** subconscious drift here. They are fragments of "Broken Possible-Realities" that hunger to be stitched into a world so they can finally become "real."
*   **The Whispers of Pale Night**: Travelers in the Waking Void often hear "The Silence of the Spindle"—a psychological phenomenon where they begin to doubt the reality of their own soul-threads, leading to madness or "Dissolution."
