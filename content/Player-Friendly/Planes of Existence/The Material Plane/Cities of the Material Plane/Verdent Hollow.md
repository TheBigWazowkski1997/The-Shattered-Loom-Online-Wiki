Verdant Hollow

**Verdant Hollow** is a hidden city built seamlessly into the canopy and roots of an ancient forest. Shrouded in the magic of nature, the city reflects its fey influences and is teeming with life. Home to druids, rangers, herbalists, and creatures aligned with nature, Verdant Hollow serves as a sanctuary for those seeking harmony with the natural world. The city's deep ties to the **Feywild** make it a place of beauty, mystery, and subtle danger, where nature itself watches over its inhabitants.

---

## **Overview**

- **Government**: Governed by the **Circle of Roots**, a council of druids, shamans, and fey emissaries who act as stewards of the forest and its balance.
    
- **Primary Exports**: Rare herbs, healing potions, enchanted wood, and fey-crafted goods.
    
- **Primary Imports**: Metal tools, arcane supplies, and luxury goods (which are traded sparingly).
    

---

## **Geography and Layout**

Verdant Hollow is built into the **Living Glade**, a forest that seems alive with sentience. Its trees are colossal, their trunks wide enough to house entire buildings. The city is divided into three layers: **The Canopy**, **The Trunk**, and **The Rootways**.

---

### **1. The Canopy (Upper City)**

- **Description**: Suspended high above the forest floor, the Canopy is a series of platforms, bridges, and walkways built into the massive branches of the ancient trees. Sunlight filters through the leaves, creating a golden-green glow. The Canopy serves as the city's cultural and administrative hub.
    
- **Key Features**:
    

- **The Elder’s Perch**: A high platform where the **Circle of Roots** convenes. The platform is adorned with hanging moss and glowing feylights.
    
- **The Glade Market**: A bustling open-air market selling fey-crafted goods, enchanted herbs, and magical plants.
    
- **The Songbough Theater**: A natural amphitheater carved into the branches, where bards and fey performers tell tales and sing songs under the stars.
    

---

### **2. The Trunk (Middle City)**

- **Description**: The trunks of the massive trees house homes, workshops, and communal spaces. The interiors are magically hollowed out, creating warm and inviting spaces filled with bioluminescent moss and glowing fungi.
    
- **Key Features**:
    

- **The Hollow Hearth**: A cozy tavern inside a massive oak tree, where travelers gather to share stories and enjoy food infused with magical herbs.
    
- **The Druid’s Enclave**: A sanctuary for druids and shamans, housing libraries of natural lore and relics from the Feywild.
    
- **The Spirit Grove**: A sacred place where visitors can commune with the spirits of the forest. The grove is watched over by a treant elder named **Oakwarden Eryndril**.
    

---

### **3. The Rootways (Lower City)**

- **Description**: Beneath the forest floor lies a network of twisting roots and caverns that make up the Rootways. It is the city's industrial and defensive layer, housing workshops, forges, and defensive outposts.
    
- **Key Features**:
    

- **The Verdant Forge**: A magical smithy where enchanted tools and weapons are crafted using rare metals and living wood.
    
- **The Lumina Caverns**: Underground caves filled with glowing crystals and rare mushrooms used in alchemical concoctions.
    
- **The Rootwatch Outpost**: A series of fortified chambers where rangers and druids stand ready to defend the forest from invaders.

## **Notable NPCs**

### **1. High Druid Caelithra Moonshade**

- **Role**: Leader of the Circle of Roots
    
- **Appearance**: A serene elven woman with long silver hair, draped in robes of green and gold. Her staff is entwined with living vines that bloom with flowers when she casts spells.
    
- **Personality**: Wise and patient, but with a fierce protective streak when the balance of the forest is threatened.
    
- **Hook**: Caelithra seeks adventurers to investigate a dark corruption spreading through the eastern edge of the Living Glade, where once-healthy trees have begun to wither.
    

---

### **2. Oakwarden Eryndril**

- **Role**: Treant elder and guardian of the Spirit Grove
    
- **Appearance**: A towering treant with bark covered in moss and leaves that shimmer with faint golden light.
    
- **Personality**: Slow to trust outsiders but deeply loyal to the Circle of Roots and the forest’s denizens.
    
- **Hook**: Eryndril believes a long-lost artifact of the forest, the **Heartseed Relic**, is hidden beneath the Rootways. He tasks the party with recovering it before it falls into the wrong hands.
    

---

### **3. Ilyana Bramblefoot**

- **Role**: Owner of the Hollow Hearth Tavern
    
- **Appearance**: A cheerful halfling woman with curly auburn hair and an apron covered in flour and dried herbs.
    
- **Personality**: Outgoing and curious, always eager to hear tales of adventure.
    
- **Hook**: Ilyana tells the party about strange lights seen in the Lumina Caverns and offers them free lodgings if they investigate.
    

---

### **4. Tharok Greenfang**

- **Role**: Leader of the Rootwatch Outpost
    
- **Appearance**: A rugged firbolg ranger with bark-like scars on his arms and a bow carved from living wood.
    
- **Personality**: Gruff but pragmatic, with a deep respect for the forest and its defenders.
    
- **Hook**: Tharok suspects poachers from a nearby settlement are stealing enchanted plants and magical creatures. He asks the party to track them down and bring them to justice.
    

---

## **Factions**

### **1. The Circle of Roots**

- **Description**: The ruling council of Verdant Hollow, composed of druids, shamans, and fey emissaries. They ensure the city operates in harmony with the forest and the Feywild.
    
- **Hook**: The Circle is divided over how to deal with increasing incursions from the Material Plane’s expanding civilizations.
    

---

### **2. The Wildvine Pact**

- **Description**: A group of fey creatures (dryads, satyrs, and sprites) who act as protectors of the forest. They maintain close ties to the Feywild and serve as the first line of defense against intrusions.
    
- **Hook**: Members of the Wildvine Pact claim that something is disrupting the Feywild’s connection to Verdant Hollow, and they ask for the party’s help in restoring the balance.
    

---

### **3. The Verdant Wardens**

- **Description**: A militant group of rangers, druids, and scouts who patrol the Living Glade to protect it from threats such as poachers, corrupted creatures, and encroaching civilizations.
    
- **Hook**: The Wardens suspect a powerful beast corrupted by shadow magic is stalking the forest, and they need skilled adventurers to hunt it.
