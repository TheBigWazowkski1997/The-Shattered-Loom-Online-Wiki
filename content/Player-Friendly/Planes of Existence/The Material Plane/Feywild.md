## **The Feywild: Overview**

### **Nature of the Plane**

The **Feywild**, or the **Plane of Faerie**, is a vibrant and chaotic reflection of the Material Plane. It is a realm where primal magic saturates the land, and emotions and desires shape reality. The Feywild is both beautiful and dangerous, where beauty can mask peril and whimsy often hides mischief.

* **Core Themes**: Chaos, beauty, and the untamed wild dominate the Feywild. It thrives on extremes—exuberance and sorrow, life and decay, light and shadow.
* **Time and Space**: Time flows strangely in the Feywild. Days may pass in the Feywild while only minutes elapse in the Material Plane—or vice versa. Travelers often find themselves lost in both time and place.
* **Influence**: The Feywild mirrors the geography of the Material Plane but with an exaggerated, fantastical twist. Forests are endless, mountains are impossibly tall, and rivers glow with magic.

---

### **Notable Features**

1. **Eternal Twilight**: Most of the Feywild exists in a perpetual state of sunrise or sunset, with soft, golden light illuminating the land.
2. **Emotional Amplification**: Visitors to the Feywild find their emotions heightened. Joy feels euphoric, fear feels paralyzing, and sorrow can be overwhelming.
3. **Wild Magic**: Magic behaves unpredictably in the Feywild. Spells might have amplified effects or cause unintended side effects.

---

## **Inhabitants of the Feywild**

1. **Eladrin**:
	
	Fey-touched elves who embody the seasons. Their emotions and physical appearance shift with the seasons they resonate with.
	
2. **Satyrs**:
	
	Mischievous and hedonistic fey creatures that embody revelry and wild abandon.
	
3. **Dryads and Treants**:
	
	Guardians of the forests and trees, tied deeply to the land’s magic.
	
4. **Pixies and Sprites**:
	
	Tiny, winged fey creatures known for their playfulness and trickery.
	
5. **Dark Fey**:
	
	Corrupted or vengeful fey beings, including hags and redcaps, who embody the darker aspects of nature.

---

### **The Courts of the Fey**

The Feywild is ruled by two major courts, but numerous minor courts exist as well. These courts represent different aspects of fey life and magic.

1. **The Seelie Court (Summer and Spring)**:
	
	Ruled by **Titania, Queen of Light**, the Seelie Court represents beauty, warmth, and growth. Its members can be benevolent but are often fickle and prideful.
	
2. **The Unseelie Court (Autumn and Winter)**:
	
	Ruled by **The Queen of Air and Darkness**, the Unseelie Court embodies decay, cold, and mischief. Its members are often cruel and manipulative.
	

## **The Seelie Court**

### **Overview**

The **Seelie Court**, also known as the **Court of Light**, is the embodiment of beauty, growth, and the vibrant energy of spring and summer. It is home to benevolent but fickle fey who value creativity, artistry, and celebration. While often viewed as the “good” court, Seelie fey are still unpredictable, and their generosity often comes with a price.

* **Alignment**: Chaotic Good and Chaotic Neutral
* **Themes**: Joy, fertility, art, and the boundless energy of life.
* **Location**: The **Verdant Spire**, a golden palace surrounded by eternal meadows and glowing forests.

---

### **Hierarchy of the Seelie Court**

1. **Titania, Queen of Light**
	* The radiant ruler of the Seelie Court, Titania is the ultimate arbiter of beauty and harmony. She governs with charm and charisma but is known for her pride and capricious decisions.
	* **Appearance**: A stunning figure with flowing golden hair, clad in a gown that shimmers with sunlight and flowers.
	* **Domains**: Life, Light, and Summer Magic.
	* **Personality**: Warm and enchanting but easily offended by slights or broken promises.
2. **Oberon, King of the Feywild**
	* Titania’s consort and the guardian of natural order in the Feywild. Oberon is the warden of beasts and forests, known for his temper and martial prowess.
	* **Appearance**: A powerful eladrin with antlered features, clad in forest-green armor.
	* **Domains**: Beasts, Strength, and Protection.
	* **Personality**: Protective and honorable but prone to jealousy and conflict.
3. **The Courtly Fey**
	* **Eladrin Nobles**: Fey elves who embody the seasons, often acting as advisors or governors of smaller realms within the Seelie Court.
	* **Fey Creatures**: Dryads, satyrs, and pixies often serve as attendants, messengers, and entertainers.

---

### **Culture and Traditions**

* **The Eternal Revel**: The Seelie Court hosts constant celebrations of art, music, and love, where mortals are often invited as honored guests—or pawns.
* **Fey Pacts**: Mortals who strike deals with Seelie fey are often required to complete creative tasks or engage in acts of charity.
* **Sacred Rites**: Seasonal ceremonies mark the transitions between spring and summer, where mortals and fey alike may receive blessings.

---
## **The Unseelie Court**

### **Overview**

The **Unseelie Court**, also known as the **Court of Shadows**, represents the darker, colder aspects of the Feywild. It embodies decay, mystery, and cruelty but also resilience and transformation. The Unseelie Court is not wholly evil, but its fey thrive on manipulation, fear, and power.

* **Alignment**: Chaotic Evil and Chaotic Neutral
* **Themes**: Shadows, trickery, death, and the beauty of decay.
* **Location**: The **Gloaming Cradle**, a palace of ice and shadow within a perpetually frozen forest.

---

### **Hierarchy of the Unseelie Court**

1. **The Queen of Air and Darkness**
	* A shadowy figure who rules the Unseelie Court with cunning and mystery. Once Titania’s sister, she was corrupted by an artifact of pure shadow and now seeks to spread her influence across the planes.
	* **Appearance**: A tall, veiled woman whose form is obscured by swirling shadows and flickering stars.
	* **Domains**: Darkness, Winter, and Secrets.
	* **Personality**: Cold, calculating, and unforgiving, but not without a strange sense of honor.
2. **Prince Varyl, the Night Stalker**
	* A malevolent eladrin who serves as the Queen’s enforcer and master of her hunts. He delights in pursuing and tormenting his prey.
	* **Appearance**: A gaunt figure with blackened antlers and glowing violet eyes.
	* **Domains**: Fear, Predation, and Night.
	* **Personality**: Sadistic and cunning, but loyal to his queen.
3. **The Courtly Fey**
	* **Shadow Fey Nobles**: Fey who specialize in manipulation and intrigue, often acting as spies or emissaries.
	* **Dark Creatures**: Hags, redcaps, and boggles frequently serve as agents and soldiers.

---

### **Culture and Traditions**

* **The Midnight Masquerade**: An annual gathering of the Unseelie Court, where identities are hidden, alliances shift, and betrayal is the norm.
* **Cursed Pacts**: Mortals who make deals with the Unseelie often find themselves cursed or bound to eternal servitude.
* **Winter’s Feast**: A grim celebration of the year’s end, where the court toasts their triumphs and punishes their failures.

---
## **Minor Courts**

Beyond the Seelie and Unseelie Courts, many smaller fey courts exist. These minor courts often align with specific elements, emotions, or natural phenomena.

### **1\. The Court of Seasons**

* **Description**: This court governs the transitions between spring, summer, autumn, and winter, led by eladrin representing each season.
* **Key Members**:
	* **Spring**: Lady Bloomwisp, a joyful pixie who heralds growth.
	* **Autumn**: Lord Emberhusk, a somber dryad who oversees harvests and decay.
* 

---

### **2\. The Court of Stars**

* **Description**: A mysterious court that resides in the night sky, ruling over dreams and cosmic magic.
* **Key Members**:
	* **Lunaris, Keeper of Dreams**: An Archfey who guides mortals through their subconscious.
	* **Stellara, the Wandering Star**: A trickster fey who grants wishes with unforeseen consequences.
* 

---

### **3\. The Wild Hunt**

* **Description**: Not a true court but a roaming band of fey hunters led by **Cernunnos**, a horned Archfey of wild savagery.
* **Key Features**: The Hunt pursues those who offend the fey, and mortals who join must prove their worth by capturing legendary creatures.
*
