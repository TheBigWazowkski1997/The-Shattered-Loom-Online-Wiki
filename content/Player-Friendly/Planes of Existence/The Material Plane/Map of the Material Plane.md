## D&D 5e Adaptation & World Geography

> [!NOTE]
> **Planar Role & 5e Connections**:
> * **The Prime Material**: Serves as the central mortal battleground and nexus of all elemental and divine influences (*DMG* Chapter 2).
> * **Planar Crossings & Shatterpoints**: Thin tears in reality allow direct crossings to the **Feywild** (via enchanted fairy circles), the **Shadowfell** (via dark tombs and the Veiled Wastes), and the **Elemental Planes** (via volcanic calderas and deep marine trenches).

## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Cities of the Material Plane/Ashara's Rest|Ashara’s Rest: The Cliffside Port]]
* **Location**: Southeastern coast, built vertically across three massive ocean tiers carved into towering sea cliffs.
* **Role**: The greatest maritime hub, merchant capital, and diplomatic center of mortal civilization.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Harbor Tier & Iron Lighthouse**: Arcane lighthouse cutting through magical fog to guide ships into the bustling Tidefall Market docks.
  * **The High Terrace**: Residential citadel of noble merchant houses, naval admirals, and the ruling Council of Tides.
  * **The Citadel of the Tide**: Coastal bastion housing naval garrisons and sea-mage academies.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The **Council of Tides**, an elected oligarchy of admirals, master merchants, and grand scholars.

### 2. [[Cities of the Material Plane/Verdent Hollow|Verdant Hollow: The Living Forest Bastion]]
* **Location**: Situated within the deep enchanted canopy of the Fractured Wilds.
* **Role**: Center of druidic lore, herbal medicine, fey diplomacy, and rangers.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Weeping Heart Tree**: A massive ancient willow whose sap heals magical wounds and purges planar blight.
  * **The Hollow Market**: Semi-subterranean bazaar trading in living seeds, silkwood bows, and tame forest beasts.
  * **The Circle of Shards**: Stone henge surrounding a floating shard of the Loom, used by druids to stabilize local wildlife.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: Archdruid Selene Green-Bough and the Circle of the Oak.

### 3. Aetherforge Citadel: The Machine City
* **Location**: Spanning the central chasm of the Iron Divide.
* **Role**: Industrial, technological, and magical powerhouse utilizing raw elemental rift energy.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Gearspire**: A titanic clockwork tower holding a pre-Shattering mechanism that taps tectonic leylines.
  * **The Rift Engine**: A controlled planar breach channeling fire and lightning to power industrial blast-furnaces.
  * **The Sky-Docks**: Gantry bridges launching dirigibles and elemental air-skiffs across the divide.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The High Mechanists and Arcane Engineers Guild.

## Major Geographic Regions & Provinces

### 1. The Fractured Wilds
* **Description**: Sprawling enchanted wilderness where residual threads of the shattered Loom cause upward-flowing rivers, bioluminescent trees, and spontaneous wild magic surges.
* **Key Locations**:
  * **The Crystal Veil**: Glade where humming shards of Telara's Loom hover above the moss.
  * **The Starlight Pools**: Mirrored forest lakes that reflect real-time scenes from the Outer Planes.

### 2. The Ashen Rift
* **Description**: A jagged volcanic canyon hundreds of miles long where molten stone bubbles to the surface from a permanent breach to the Elemental Plane of Fire.
* **Key Locations**:
  * **Emberheart Forge**: Foundry complex crafting fire-tempered steel using magma channels.
  * **The Burning Maw**: Subterranean cavern system sacred to cultists of Ignivor.

### 3. The Veiled Wastes
* **Description**: A desolate desert of pale ash where the boundary with the Shadowfell has deteriorated into transparent twilight.
* **Key Locations**:
  * **The Black Oasis**: Shadowy pool surrounded by weeping willows where restless spirits gather.
  * **The Ghost Caravan**: Spectral trade procession traversing the dunes under the cover of night.

### 4. The Verdant Archipelago
* **Description**: An island chain in the warm southern seas where proximity to the Feywild causes each island to exist in an eternal, specific season.
* **Key Locations**:
  * **Springtide Isle**: Island of eternal bloom ruled by an Archfey ambassador.
  * **Tempest Rock**: Storm-lashed crag housing a high shrine to Aurelion, the Skycaller.

### 5. The Iron Divide
* **Description**: A jagged mountain cordillera cleaved by an ancient tectonic canyon, rich in adamantine, coal, and magnetic lodestone.
* **Key Locations**:
  * **The Divide Chasm**: A bottomless canyon crossed only by massive suspension bridges.
  * **Foundry Peaks**: Alpine mines providing raw ores to Aetherforge Citadel.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Ember-Watch
* **Location**: North rim of the Ashen Rift.
* **Population**: ~1,200 (Dwarves, Fire Genasi, Mercenaries).
* **Description**: A fortified stone redoubt monitoring magma elemental incursions from the rift floor.
* **Specialty**: Heat-resistant armor, volcanic glass daggers, and fire-fighting foam.
* **Notable Feature — The Fire-Horn**: A massive bronze siren alerting the valley when lava wyrms emerge.

### 2. Shimmer-Dell
* **Location**: Southern fringe of the Fractured Wilds near a known fey crossing.
* **Population**: ~850 (Halflings, Gnomes, Half-Elves).
* **Description**: A tranquil agricultural village famous for cultivating dream-berries and glowing cider apples.
* **Specialty**: Fey-blessed cider, clover honey, and sleep-inducing pipeweed.
* **Notable Feature — The Fairy Bridge**: An arch of interwoven wild rosebushes that bloom year-round.

### 3. Salt-Spire
* **Location**: Rocky headland 40 miles north of Ashara’s Rest.
* **Population**: ~950 (Fisherfolk, Shipwrights, Coastal Rangers).
* **Description**: A rugged fishing haven anchored against ferocious coastal gale storms.
* **Specialty**: Smoked sea-bass, storm-resistant timber, and whale-oil lanterns.
* **Notable Feature — The Sea-Cave Docks**: Submerged caverns protecting fishing sloops from hurricane winds.
