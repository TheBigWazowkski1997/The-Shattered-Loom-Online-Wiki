## D&D 5e Adaptation & Canon Map References

> [!NOTE]
> **Adapted from D&D 5e Canon**:
> * **The Feywild / Plane of Faerie**: Adapted directly from official D&D 5e cosmology (*Dungeon Master's Guide*, pp. 49–50, *The Wild Beyond the Witchlight*, and *Mordenkainen's Presents: Monsters of the Multiverse*).
> * **Canon Map Reference**: The Courts of the Feywild—including the Summer Court of Queen Titania, the Autumn and Winter domains of the Unseelie Court under the Queen of Air and Darkness, and the Domain of Prismeer—provide canonical foundations for Feywild exploration and capricious planar rules.
> * **Loom Integration**: In the Shattered Loom, the Feywild represents the **Emotional Warp** of the cosmic tapestry—where the threads of reality are so supercharged with primal vitality that thought and feeling instantly reshape the geography.

## The Two Great Fey Courts & Metropolises

### 1. [[Feywild#The Seelie Court|The Verdant Spire: The Court of Light]]
* **Location**: North-central Vibrant Summerlands, set amidst eternal meadows of blooming gold and emerald orchards.
* **Role**: Seat of the Seelie Court, capital of summer revels, divine artistry, passion, and natural growth.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Verdant Spire**: A living palace of woven sunlight, flowering silverwood, and sparkling morning dew.
  * **The Hall of the Eternal Revel**: Sprawling terraces where feasts, poetry duels, and grand masquerades have continued unbroken for millennia.
  * **The Sun-Well**: A sacred spring of pure radiant water that restores youth and inspires genius in mortal artists.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Titania, Queen of Light**, alongside her consort **Oberon, the Green Lord**.

### 2. [[Feywild#The Unseelie Court|The Gloaming Cradle: The Court of Shadows]]
* **Location**: Southern frozen frontier within the Duskwood, shrouded in perpetual midnight and chilling rime.
* **Role**: Seat of the Unseelie Court, redoubt of winter intrigue, dark pacts, predatory hunts, and shadow magic.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Gloaming Cradle**: A brooding, gothic fortress carved from blue glacial ice and petrified black thorn-wood.
  * **The Hall of Broken Mirrors**: High court where the Queen of Air and Darkness receives petitioners through illusions and whispers.
  * **The Hounds' Kennels**: The starting gate for the Wild Hunt, housing shadow mastiffs and winter wolves.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **The Queen of Air and Darkness**, enforced by Prince Varyl the Night Stalker.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Moonpetal Village
* **Location**: On the southeastern shore of the Lake of Shimmering Dreams.
* **Population**: ~1,200 (Eladrin, Pixies, Satyrs).
* **Description**: A whimsical settlement of hollowed glowing mushroom houses and silk cocoons. Inhabitants brew flower wines and weave dreams into gossamer cloaks.
* **Specialty**: Moonpetal wine, dream-catching nets, and glamour-infused dresses.
* **Notable Feature — The Floating Lily-Piers**: Floating docks made of giant water-lily pads that gently drift across the lake.

### 2. Wistful Hollow
* **Location**: Western twilight woods along the River Starlight.
* **Population**: ~850 (Centaur herds, Dryads, Displaced Mortals).
* **Description**: A serene, melancholic village nested beneath weeping willows where mortal travelers who lost their way in the Feywild can live in peace.
* **Specialty**: Memory-preserving amber, herbal soothing teas, and flute carvings.
* **Notable Feature — The Time-Glass Font**: A fountain where the water droplets hang frozen in mid-air, indicating the slow passage of mortal time.

### 3. Bramble-Cross (The Goblin Market)
* **Location**: The border hedge where the Golden Leaf Forests meet the Unseelie Duskwood.
* **Population**: ~1,600 (Quicklings, Boggles, Hags, Redcaps, Goblins).
* **Description**: A chaotic, lawless fairground lit by will-o'-wisps, trading in oddities, stolen mortal years, memories, and cursed trinkets.
* **Specialty**: Fairy gold (which turns to dead leaves at dawn), true names, and riddle keys.
* **Notable Feature — The Hag's Cauldron**: A public hearth where three green hags brew soup that can grant prophecies or nightmares.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Lake of Shimmering Dreams
* **Description**: An immense body of crystal-clear water reflecting constellations that do not exist in the mortal sky.
* **Planar Trait**: Bathing in the lake restores all spell slots up to 5th level, but requires a Requires strong mental and physical fortitude to withstand.

### 2. The Golden Leaf Forests (Eternal Autumn)
* **Description**: Endless woodlands where the leaves are perpetually gold, scarlet, and amber, slowly drifting to carpet the forest floor in silence.
* **Planar Trait**: Tracking or navigating through the falling leaves has disadvantage unless guided by a native fey or possessing the Fey Ancestry trait.

### 3. Faerie Rings (Cross-Planar Crossings)
* **Description**: Concentric circles of bioluminescent mushrooms found throughout the meadows and groves.
* **Planar Trait**: Stepping into a ring under a full moon transports creatures directly to a corresponding ring on the Material Plane; stepping in during a new moon leads to the Shadowfell.

### 4. Feywild Time Warp & Memory Loss
* **Description**: The fundamental temporal distortion of Faerie.
* **Planar Trait**: Upon leaving the Feywild, creatures must roll on the *Feywild Time Warp* and *Memory Loss* tables (*DMG* p. 50), risking forgetting their experiences or discovering decades have passed.
