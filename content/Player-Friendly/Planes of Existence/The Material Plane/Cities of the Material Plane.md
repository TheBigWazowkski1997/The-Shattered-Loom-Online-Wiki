# Cities of the Material Plane

![[attachments/material_plane_map.jpg]]

> *"Across the scarred, fragmented continents of the Material Plane, great bastions of civilization stand as beacons against planar chaos. From geothermal industrial hives to verdant agrarian sanctuaries, these cities anchor mortal life."*

---

## Overview

The settlements of the Material Plane are diverse bastions of survival, culture, and commerce, each shaped by local geography, magical anomalies, and the planar bleeding caused by the Shattering.

---

## Major Settlements

### 1. [[Cities of the Material Plane/The Molten Reach|The Molten Reach]]
* **Geography**: Volcanic highlands and jagged obsidian ridges.
* **Culture**: An industrious powerhouse of smiths, metallurgists, and geothermal engineers harnessing subterranean magma veins to forge extraordinary alloys and machinery.

### 2. [[Cities of the Material Plane/Ashara's Rest|Ashara's Rest]]
* **Geography**: High mountain valley guarded by towering granite monoliths.
* **Culture**: A spiritual retreat, scholastic center, and sanctuary town where monks, planar cartographers, and weary travelers find solace and study ancient cosmic lore.

### 3. [[Cities of the Material Plane/Verdent Hollow|Verdent Hollow]]
* **Geography**: Dense, sprawling temperate river basin nestled between old-growth woodland.
* **Culture**: A bustling agrarian and trade metropolis intimately connected with seasonal rhythms and nature magic, serving as the primary breadbasket and crossroads for surrounding territories.
