## D&D 5e Adaptation & Canon Map References

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### Adapted Directly from D&D 5e Canon
> The Shadowfell within **Project Shattered Loom** directly integrates the official D&D 5e gothic horror setting of **Ravenloft** (*Curse of Strahd* & *Van Richten's Guide to Ravenloft*), adapted seamlessly into the Loom's cosmology:
> 
> * **The Valley of Barovia**: Uses the canonical **Map of Barovia** by Mike Schley (*Curse of Strahd*), covering the Svalich Woods, Lake Zarovich, Mount Baratok, Mount Ghakis, the Old Bonegrinder, and the Amber Temple.
> * **Castle Ravenloft**: Uses the canonical isometric architectural floorplans of Castle Ravenloft (*Curse of Strahd*, Appendix / Map Folio). In this campaign, the castle's **Heart of Sorrow** houses the **Loom Fragment of the Crimson Weave**.
> * **Settlements & Tactical Maps**:
>   * **Village of Barovia**: The canonical 5e map represents the town in its "Livestock Era," where human residents are marked with "Thread-Staples" and tithe blood at the Tax-Gallows.
>   * **Town of Vallaki**: Uses the canonical 5e Vallaki map (St. Andral's, Blue Water Inn, Baron's Mansion, Wachterhaus, Blinsky's).
>   * **Krezk & the Abbey of Saint Markovia**: The canonical walled hilltop abbey.
>   * **The Amber Temple**: The secret mountain redoubt of vestige-sarcophagi.
> * **The Raven Queen & Fortress of Memories**: Sourced from *Mordenkainen's Tome of Foes* and *Fizban's Treasury of Dragons*, representing the cold mistress of memory and fate who observes the unraveling of the Loom from afar.

## The Three Great Metropolises & Strongholds

### 1. [[Shadowfell#2. Castle Ravenloft — The Predator’s Eye|Castle Ravenloft: Citadel of Consumption]]
* **Location**: Perched atop a 1,000-foot pillar of sheer black basalt overlooking the eastern valley of Barovia.
* **Role**: The metaphysical throne of Count Strahd von Zarovich, harnessing the **Crimson Weave (Loom Fragment)** to defy entropy.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Heart of Sorrow**: A crystalline tower housing the stolen Loom Fragment of the Crimson Weave, pulsing with rhythmic blood-light.
  * **The Hall of Records**: The archival vault preserving the "Blood-Pedigrees" of every human bloodline in the domain.
  * **The Grand Dining Hall**: Lavish hall where vampire spawn and High-Born feast on distilled emotional vintages.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **Count Strahd von Zarovich**, Darklord of Barovia and master of Castle Ravenloft.

### 2. [[Shadowfell#4. Vallaki|Vallaki: The Walled Bastion of Paranoia]]
* **Location**: Central Barovia, along the southern shore of Lake Zarovich.
* **Role**: The largest fortified civilian population center, living in perpetual paranoia beneath enforced joy.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Blue Water Inn**: Secret resistance headquarters operated by the Martikov family (Order of the Feather wereravens).
  * **St. Andral's Church**: Hallowed refuge shielding mortals from spawn raids (reliant on the Bones of St. Andral).
  * **Baron Vallakovich’s Mansion**: Seat of the town magistrate whose weekly forced festivals mask terror.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: Baron Vargas Vallakovich (contested by Lady Fiona Wachter and the Cult of the Black Sun).

### 3. [[Shadowfell#The Raven Queen|The Fortress of Memories: The Obsidian Spire]]
* **Location**: Far western perimeter of the Shadowfell, carved into jagged peaks jutting out over the Shadowsea.
* **Role**: Seat of the Raven Queen, collecting extracted memories and soul-threads from across the multiverse.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Memory Scriptorium**: Vast chambers where Shadar-kai catalog memories sheared from mortal minds before death.
  * **The Shadow Lattice**: Ethereal webs connecting the fortress to shadow-crossings throughout the Material Plane.
  * **The Pool of Black Ice**: A freezing basin where petitioners surrender traumatic memories in exchange for dark boons.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: **The Raven Queen**, assisted by her Shadar-kai death-clerics and shadow dragons.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. The Village of Barovia (The Blood-Farm)
* **Location**: Eastern end of the Svalich Valley beneath the shadow of Castle Ravenloft.
* **Population**: ~700 (Humans under Thread-Staple bondage, Vampire Spawn overseers).
* **Description**: A bleak, rain-drenched hamlet where residents are treated as living cellars. The sound of rhythmic Blood-Mills echoes day and night.
* **Specialty**: Blood-wine barrels, preserved funeral vestments, and silver-plated wolf charms.
* **Notable Feature — The Tax-Gallows**: Central square platform where debtors who fail the Vein-Levee are exsanguinated.

### 2. Krezk (The Abbey Sanctuary)
* **Location**: Western foothills of Mount Baratok, protected by massive cyclopean stone walls.
* **Population**: ~400 (Humans, Mongrelfolk).
* **Description**: An austere, isolationist commune built beneath the Abbey of Saint Markovia, sustained by a miraculous blessed spring.
* **Specialty**: Pure mountain well-water, defensive masonry, and herbal sheep-salves.
* **Notable Feature — The Abbot's Menagerie**: The cliff-side sanitarium holding flesh-sculpted chimerae.

### 3. Argynvostholt (The Dragon's Ruin)
* **Location**: South-central mountain ridge overlooking the mist marshes.
* **Population**: ~80 (Spectral Knights of the Silver Dragon, Revenants).
* **Description**: A fortified gothic mansion built by the silver dragon Argynvost, now a melancholy fortress of fallen paladins awaiting the rekindling of the dragon's beacon.
* **Specialty**: Consecrated silver blades, dragon-scale shields, and historical tapestries.
* **Notable Feature — The High Beacon**: The lighthouse tower awaiting the skull of Argynvost to bathe the valley in holy sunlight.

### 4. Berez (The Drowned Ruins)
* **Location**: Southern marsh basin along the River Ivlis.
* **Population**: ~120 (Scarecrow Constructs, Baba Lysaga's Coven, Giant Toads).
* **Description**: An abandoned village sunken into muddy bog-water, cursed after its burgomaster executed Marina to save her from Strahd.
* **Specialty**: Creeping hut totems, swamp poisons, and corrupted magic gems.
* **Notable Feature — The Creeping Hut**: Baba Lysaga’s mobile walking cottage powered by an animated vineyard gem.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Crimson Weave (Loom Fragment)
* **Description**: A primary relic of the Shattered Loom, stolen by Strahd and bound within the Heart of Sorrow high above Castle Ravenloft.
* **Planar Trait**: The Fragment pulses with rhythmic blood-magic, granting Strahd complete control over Barovia's atmospheric mists and absorbing the first 100 points of damage dealt to him each day.

### 2. The Mists of Ravenloft
* **Description**: Semi-sentient, impenetrable grey fog surrounding Barovia and the other Domains of Dread.
* **Planar Trait**: Entering the mist causes choking suffocation (Requires strong mental and physical fortitude to withstand.

### 3. The Amber Temple
* **Description**: An ancient, snow-buried sanctuary carved into the frozen peak of Mount Ghakis.
* **Planar Trait**: Holds dozens of amber sarcophagi imprisoning vestigial Dark Powers. Touching a sarcophagus offers a Dark Gift but forces a saving throw Charisma saving throw to resist permanent moral corruption.

### 4. The Shadowsea
* **Description**: An endless, cold ocean of liquid shadow and mist connecting disparate Domains of Dread across the deeper Shadowfell.
* **Planar Trait**: Sailing the Shadowsea requires a Shadar-kai shadow-skiff; non-native creatures immersed in the waters take 4d6 cold and 4d6 necrotic damage per round.
