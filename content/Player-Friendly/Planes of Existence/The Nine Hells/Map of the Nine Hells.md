## Planar Geography & Canonical Foundations

> [!IMPORTANT]
> ### The Architecture of Absolute Law
> The Nine Hells of Baator represent the metaphysical foundation of unyielding, oppressive order—a descending spiral of nine compressed reality-shards where reality is codified with terrifying precision:
> 
> * **The Nine Layers of Baator**:
>   1. **Avernus**: The battle-scarred planar junkyard and front line of the Blood War, featuring the Bronze Citadel, the River Styx, Fort Knucklebone, and the Wandering Emporium.
>   2. **Dis**: The city of suffocating geometric perfection and iron towers ruled by Dispater.
>   3. **Minauros**: The endless sinking swamp where discarded destinies and failed threads dissolve into muck; ruled by Mammon.
>   4. **Phlegethos**: Obsidian foundries and caldera furnaces where souls are smelted into infernal iron; ruled by Belial & Fierna.
>   5. **Stygia**: The frozen sea and icebergs encasing Levistus, with the shifting ice-port of Tantlin.
>   6. **Malbolge**: The shifting vertical mountain prison and labyrinthine logic-trap ruled by Glasya.
>   7. **Maladomini**: Decaying ruins and endless quarries of failed architectural drafts ruled by Baalzebul.
>   8. **Cania**: The frozen glacial heat-sink buffering the Hells from cosmic entropy, ruled by Mephistopheles.
>   9. **Nessus**: The deepest chasm housing the counterfeit **Infernal Spindle** and Malsheem, ruled by Asmodeus.
> * **Loom Integration**: Devils do not merely collect souls for torment; they harvest **Soul-Threads** to reforge reality into the **Infernal Spindle**—a mechanical counter-loom intended to bind all existence in eternal tyranny.

## The Three Great Citadels & Strongholds

### 1. [[The Nine Hells#1. Avernus: The Shattered Frontier|The Bronze Citadel: Fortress of the First Front]]
* **Location**: Avernus, spanning a series of low basalt ridges overlooking the blood plains of the River Styx.
* **Role**: The massive headquarters of the infernal war effort in the Blood War, commanded by Archduke Zariel.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Command Spire**: Zariel’s war council chamber, hung with tactical threat-maps tracking Abyssal breaches.
  * **The Warmachine Hangars**: Enormous hangars housing heavy infernal war machines fueled by distilled soul coins.
  * **The Styx Dockyards**: Fortified river gates where infernal ironclad galleys are built and launched against demon hordes.
* **Ruler**: **Archduke Zariel**, the Fallen Weaver.

### 2. [[The Nine Hells#2. Dis: The Iron Spindle|The Iron Tower of Dis: The Sovereign Geometric Bastion]]
* **Location**: Dis, rising at the center of the labyrinthine iron city.
* **Role**: The bureaucratic data-vault of cosmic order, where universal legal statutes and planar infractions are codified.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Hall of Geometries**: Dispater’s inner sanctum, where enchanted instruments measure reality for any deviation from absolute order.
  * **The Prison of Decrees**: Subterranean vaults housing entities who violated planar compacts.
  * **The Red Iron Gates**: Massive city gates that heat to glowing white when chaotic entities approach.
* **Ruler**: **Archduke Dispater**, Surveyor of Deviance.

### 3. [[The Nine Hells#9. Nessus: The Spindle of the Dark Weaver|Malsheem: Citadel of the Infernal Spindle]]
* **Location**: Nessus, suspended above the yawning chasm of the Serpent's Coil.
* **Role**: The ultimate command throne of the Nine Hells and the site of Asmodeus’s counter-loom.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Infernal Spindle**: A gargantuan machine of blackened adamantine and cold-iron, endlessly spinning stolen mortal soul-threads into a rigid framework of absolute law.
  * **The Archive of the Pact Primeval**: The original cosmic contract inscribed on tablets of celestial ruby.
  * **The Serpent's Abyss**: The fathomless trench beneath Malsheem where Asmodeus’s primordial essence anchors the plane.
* **Ruler**: **Lord Asmodeus**, Supreme Master of the Nine Hells.

## Fortresses, Encampments & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Fort Knucklebone (The Scavenger's Garage)
* **Location**: Avernus, nestled within the protective curl of four colossal petrified skeletal fingers.
* **Population**: ~80 (Mad Maggie’s gang of redcaps, mechanics, and scrap scavengers).
* **Description**: A bustling salvage yard and repair shop where infernal war machines are built, customized, and traded.
* **Specialty**: War machine tuning, soul coin trading, and dream-divinations.
* **Notable Feature — The Dream Machine**: A magical contraption that allows mortals to experience and reshape buried memories.

### 2. The Wandering Emporium (Mahadi's Travelling Sanctuary)
* **Location**: Avernus (Mobile nomadic encampment).
* **Population**: ~200 (Merchants, entertainers, elemental cooks, and planar wanderers).
* **Description**: A luxurious oasis of silk tents, fine food, and calm baths that travels the wastes under the protection of Asmodeus's personal seal.
* **Specialty**: Rare planar delicacies, magical loans, safe lodging, and infernal bargains.
* **Notable Feature — The Infernal Contract Tent**: Mahadi’s private pavilion where travelers negotiate safe passage in exchange for favors or oaths.

### 3. Tantlin: The Floating Ice-Port
* **Location**: Stygia, constructed atop a cluster of frozen ice floes on the River Styx.
* **Population**: ~1,500 (Planar merchants, ice navigators, and frost-bitten tieflings).
* **Description**: A rough port town perpetually shifting with the tides of ice, serving as the primary trading post on the frozen layer.
* **Specialty**: Preserved ancient texts extracted from Stygian ice, Styx-skiff rentals, and cold-resistant gear.
* **Notable Feature — The Frozen Eye**: An iceberg harboring a preserved pre-Shattering scholar who whispers forgotten lore to those who thaw his lips.

### 4. Abriymoch: City of Caldera Forges
* **Location**: Phlegethos, built into the interior walls of a massive volcanic crater.
* **Population**: ~4,000 (Infernal smiths, fire elementals, and bureaucratic overseers).
* **Description**: An obsidian metropolis lit by perpetual rivers of magma, renowned as the chief industrial engine of infernal armaments.
* **Specialty**: Hellfire-tempered plate armor, soul-branded shackles, and law tablets.
* **Notable Feature — The Anvil of Mandates**: Where Belial hammers infernal statutes directly into the fabric of reality.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The River Styx
* **Description**: A dark, oily torrent flowing through all nine layers, carrying the dissolved memories and identities of mortal souls.
* **Planar Trait**: Contact with the water causes severe amnesia, wiping away memories and leaving victims in a hollow, docile stupor.

### 2. Infernal Soul-Thread Contracts
* **Description**: Metaphysical documents signed in blood that legally bind a mortal's destiny-thread directly to the devil's spindle upon death.
* **Planar Trait**: Infernal contracts cannot be dispelled by mortal magic; breaking a covenant triggers immediate soul-forfeit.

### 3. Soul Coins & War Engines
* **Description**: Heavy coins forged from infernal iron containing the compressed soul-thread of a single mortal, used as currency and fuel for infernal war machines across the wastes.
* **Planar Trait**: War machines powered by soul coins rumble across rough terrain with extraordinary speed and durability.
