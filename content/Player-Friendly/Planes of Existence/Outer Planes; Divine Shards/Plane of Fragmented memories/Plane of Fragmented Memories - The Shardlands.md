**Description**: The Shardlands is a surreal and eerie plane composed of jagged crystalline landscapes. The plane stores broken and forgotten memories, fragments of lost gods, and pieces of unfulfilled destinies. Time flows strangely, with the past, present, and future overlapping.The **Plane of Fragmented Memories** is a hauntingly beautiful and disorienting realm where the remnants of forgotten thoughts, broken dreams, and lost knowledge drift endlessly through a vast, surreal landscape. This plane is a repository of everything discarded, forgotten, or splintered across time and existence. Here, memories take on physical forms, forming the environment itself, and travelers often find themselves wandering through visions of the past, echoes of the present, or unrealized possibilities.

#### **1\. The Sea of Shards**

A vast, seemingly endless ocean of glass-like shards stretches across much of the plane. Each shard reflects fragments of memories, glowing faintly with the emotions tied to them. These fragments float above the surface or form crystalline waves that crash against jagged shores.

* **Appearance**:The Sea of Shards is a glimmering expanse of translucent fragments, each piece reflecting vivid images of moments lost to time. The waves produce a soft, chiming sound as they move, creating an eerie, melodic ambiance.
* **Key Features**:
	* **Shard Pools**: Shallow areas where clusters of memory shards swirl together. Gazing into these pools can reveal forgotten truths or lost secrets, but doing so risks becoming trapped in the memory.
	* **Shattered Isles**: Floating islands of jagged glass and stone rise from the sea, their surfaces covered with fragmented visions. These isles are often home to lingering echoes or Memory Wraiths.

---

#### **2\. The Veiled Archive**

The **Veiled Archive** is a massive, labyrinthine library composed of fragmented, disorganized knowledge. Shelves made of warped wood and twisted metal stretch into infinity, filled with books, scrolls, and artifacts that shift and shimmer with the memories they contain.

* **Appearance**:The Archive is a disorienting maze, with corridors that loop back on themselves and staircases leading to impossible places. Books and scrolls float through the air, whispering softly in languages both familiar and alien.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Index of Whispers**: A sentient, shadowy being that serves as the Archive’s caretaker. It offers guidance but always demands a price—typically a memory or piece of knowledge in return.
	* **Echoing Chambers**: Rooms within the Archive where memories play out like illusions, allowing visitors to experience lost histories.
	* **The Shelf of Broken Truths**: A restricted section filled with forbidden knowledge and secrets too dangerous to reveal.

---

#### **3\. The Fields of Echoes**

The **Fields of Echoes** are rolling landscapes where memories manifest as tangible illusions, replaying endlessly across the terrain. Travelers often find themselves walking through the past, reliving key moments from history or their own lives.

* **Appearance**:The Fields are an ever-shifting patchwork of environments—an ancient battlefield here, a joyous festival there—all fading into one another seamlessly. The air shimmers with the faint glow of emotions tied to the memories.
* **Key Features**:
	* **Timeless Monuments**: Massive statues or ruins dot the fields, each tied to a significant memory or event in the multiverse’s history.
	* **Phantom Echoes**: Ethereal figures reenact moments from their lives, sometimes interacting with travelers or dragging them into the memory.
	* **Memory Wells**: Pools of liquid light where particularly powerful memories are stored. Drinking from a well grants insight but risks overwhelming the drinker with visions.

---

#### **4\. The Dread Chasm**

The **Dread Chasm** is a terrifying abyss where the most painful and traumatic memories of the multiverse are trapped. It is a place of raw emotion and despair, and those who venture too close risk being overwhelmed.

* **Appearance**:The Chasm is a gaping wound in the plane, its edges jagged and glowing faintly with red and violet light. Black tendrils of shadow rise from the depths, whispering fragments of anguish and regret.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Abyssal Mirrors**: Reflective surfaces line the chasm’s walls, showing travelers their darkest memories or unrealized fears.
	* **The Wailing Depths**: A constant chorus of cries and whispers emanates from the chasm, growing louder the closer one approaches.
	* **Shadow Wraiths**: Malevolent entities born from the plane’s most painful memories, preying on the weak and lost.

---

#### **5\. The Thread of Remembrance**

A literal thread that winds through the Plane of Fragmented Memories, the **Thread of Remembrance** connects all memories, creating a tenuous sense of continuity amid the plane’s disjointed nature. Following the Thread often leads travelers to their most meaningful revelations.

* **Appearance**:A glowing, golden thread that twists and winds across the plane, sometimes vanishing into the air only to reappear elsewhere. It hums faintly, resonating with the emotions of those nearby.
* **Key Features**:
	* **Memory Weavers**: Ethereal beings who tend to the Thread, repairing broken connections and guiding travelers.
	* **Convergence Points**: Locations where multiple threads intersect, creating powerful nodes of memory and revelation.

### **1\. Mirrorglade: The City of Reflection**

**Mirrorglade** is a sprawling, dreamlike city built upon an endless field of mirrored glass. Each mirror reflects not just the physical form of those who walk upon it but also their memories, fears, and hopes, creating a surreal kaleidoscope of fractured reflections. The city's structures are crafted from silvery glass and crystalline memory shards, which pulse faintly with the memories trapped within them.
The inhabitants of Mirrorglade live amidst their reflections, constantly confronted with fragments of their own pasts or glimpses of alternate realities. Many travelers come to the city to uncover forgotten memories, face unresolved trauma, or retrieve pieces of themselves that were lost to time. However, not all who enter Mirrorglade leave whole—some fall into the mirrorfields, swallowed by the memories they cannot escape.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Shardspire Palace**
	* The central structure of Mirrorglade, a towering crystalline palace formed from the largest memory shards ever found. It is home to the **Shardkeepers**, enigmatic beings who claim to protect and organize the most important memories of the multiverse.
	* **Lore**: The Shardspire Palace is said to contain memories of forgotten gods, lost civilizations, and pivotal moments that shaped entire worlds. Those who enter its halls often find pieces of their own past hidden among the fragments.
2. **The Mirrored Labyrinth**
	* A vast maze of mirrors that stretches beneath the city. Each reflection within the labyrinth shows alternate versions of the traveler, memories they never lived, or choices they never made. The deeper one ventures, the more disorienting the reflections become.
	* **Lore**: It is whispered that the labyrinth has no true end and that those who lose themselves within it become one with the plane, their memories eternally trapped in the mirrors.
3. **The Reflection Bazaar**
	* A marketplace where merchants trade in crystallized memories, dreams, and echoes of past lives. Adventurers often come here seeking fragments of lost knowledge or forgotten treasures from their own pasts. However, the cost of such trades is rarely measured in gold—it often involves giving up a cherished memory of one’s own.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **Shardkeepers**: Mysterious beings who serve as the guardians of memory fragments. They appear as translucent figures, constantly shifting in shape as memories flicker within them.
* **The Echobound**: Individuals who have been partially consumed by the plane and now exist as walking memories, their forms shimmering like mirages.
* **The Mirrorborn**: Strange constructs made of reflective glass, acting as both guides and enforcers of the city's laws.

---
### **2\. Reminaria: The City of Whispers**

**Reminaria** is a floating city constructed upon a network of **memory clouds** that drift endlessly through the sky. The city’s buildings are crafted from ephemeral mist, dreamlike shapes, and solidified thoughts, giving them an otherworldly and intangible quality. Whispers echo constantly through the city’s streets, carrying snippets of conversations, secrets, and forgotten words.
The city serves as a haven for those seeking to piece together fragmented knowledge or delve into the collective subconscious of the multiverse. Its streets are filled with scholars, dreamwalkers, and memory seekers who gather in its libraries and archives. However, the city’s clouds occasionally break apart, casting portions of the city—and its inhabitants—into the void below.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Whispering Library**
	* A massive, spiraling archive filled with books that cannot be opened and scrolls that unravel into mist. Instead of reading, visitors must listen to the faint whispers emanating from the shelves to glean their contents.
	* **Lore**: The library is said to contain the memories of every word ever spoken in the multiverse, though finding a specific whisper is an arduous task.
2. **The Dreamspire Academy**
	* A towering spire where dreamwalkers and memory weavers study the art of navigating the Plane of Fragmented Memories. The spire’s interior shifts constantly, with dreamlike staircases that lead to ever-changing classrooms and chambers.
3. **The Echoing Plaza**
	* A public square where memory shards rain down from the sky like shooting stars. Locals and travelers gather to catch these shards, hoping to glimpse a fragment of lost knowledge or a fleeting dream.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **The Whispering Custodians**: Ghostly figures who tend to the Whispering Library. They guide visitors but speak only in riddles and half-truths.
* **The Cloudshapers**: Artisans who craft tools and structures from the memory clouds.
* **Fugueborn Travelers**: Lost souls who drift into Reminaria after forgetting their purpose, becoming part of the city’s strange society.

---
### **3\. Shatterpoint: The Cracked City**

**Shatterpoint** is a city built atop a massive, **cracked memory shard** that juts from the plane’s surface like a jagged mountain. The shard itself pulses with unstable energy, causing pieces of the city to phase in and out of existence. Entire districts disappear for days, only to return slightly altered or inhabited by figures from forgotten timelines.
Shatterpoint is a city of instability and peril, but also of opportunity. Adventurers, scavengers, and planar outcasts gather here to delve into the shard’s depths, seeking rare memory fragments and lost knowledge. However, the unstable nature of the shard means that reality itself is fragile within the city, and those who linger too long risk being erased or rewritten.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Fractured Spire**
	* The tallest structure in Shatterpoint, built from the sharp edges of the memory shard itself. It serves as both a watchtower and a place of research for those studying the shard’s instability.
2. **The Riftmarket**
	* A chaotic marketplace where merchants sell unstable artifacts, fractured memories, and items that flicker between different states of existence.
	* **Lore**: The Riftmarket is rumored to exist in multiple timelines simultaneously, allowing merchants to trade goods that have not yet been created.
3. **The Evershifting Warrens**
	* A labyrinth of tunnels within the shard’s depths, filled with dangerous memory echoes, unstable magic, and treasures frozen in time.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **The Shardbound**: Individuals who have been permanently altered by the shard’s energy, gaining strange powers but losing pieces of their own identity.
* **Temporal Nomads**: Planar travelers who wander the city in search of stable timelines to call home.
* **The Riftwalkers**: Adventurers who brave the shard’s depths, often returning with valuable fragments—or not returning at all.
