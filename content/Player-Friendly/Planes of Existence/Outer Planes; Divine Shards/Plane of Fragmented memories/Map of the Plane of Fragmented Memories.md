# Map & Geography of the Plane of Fragmented Memories (The Shardlands)

![[attachments/plane_of_fragmented_memories_map.jpg]]

> _"Every shard of glass underfoot reflects a life you never lived, a love you never held, or a battle you died in three ages ago. Tread lightly upon the Shardlands, lest you step on your own forgotten regret."_
> — Journal of Vexiryn the Unbowed

---

## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Plane of Fragmented Memories - The Shardlands#1. Mirrorglade: The City of Reflection|Mirrorglade: The City of Reflection]]
* **Location**: Central reflective crystalline plains, built upon a boundless field of mirrored glass.
* **Role**: Cultural and scholarly heart of memory preservation and mnemonic glasscraft.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Shardspire Palace**: A colossal crystalline palace formed from the largest memory shards, home to the ruling Shardkeepers.
  * **The Mirrored Labyrinth**: A vast maze of mirrors beneath the city reflecting alternate timelines and choices.
  * **The Reflection Bazaar**: An open market trading in crystal memories and extracted thoughts.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The High Shardkeepers, advised by master craftsmen like [[../../../NPCs/Master Rhydian Glasscarver|Master Rhydian Glasscarver]] and [[../../../NPCs/Archivist Selene Pale|Archivist Selene Pale]].

### 2. [[Plane of Fragmented Memories - The Shardlands#2. Reminaria: The City of Whispers|Reminaria: The City of Whispers]]
* **Location**: Floating aloft in the upper stratosphere upon a perpetual network of condensed memory clouds.
* **Role**: Center of dreamwalking, sensory archiving, and auditory lore retrieval.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Whispering Library**: A spiraling mist-archive containing the echoes of every word spoken in the multiverse.
  * **The Dreamspire Academy**: A towering academy studying dream-navigation and psychic defense.
  * **The Echoing Plaza**: Public square where shooting memory shards rain from the sky like celestial showers.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Whispering Custodians and Master Cloudshapers like [[../../../NPCs/Master Cloudshaper Zephyr|Master Cloudshaper Zephyr]].

### 3. [[Plane of Fragmented Memories - The Shardlands#3. Shatterpoint: The Cracked City|Shatterpoint: The Cracked City]]
* **Location**: Deep southern frontier, anchored upon a colossal cracked memory shard protruding like a mountain from the earth.
* **Role**: Frontier boomtown of temporal scavengers, chronomancers, and riftwalkers.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Fractured Spire**: The jagged peak of the shard serving as an observatory for temporal anomalies.
  * **The Riftmarket**: A chaotic bazaar operating across multiple parallel timelines simultaneously.
  * **The Evershifting Warrens**: Subterranean crystal tunnels where lost timelines and ancient ruins phase in and out.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Council of Riftwalkers, led by veterans like [[../../../NPCs/Riftwalker Vael|Riftwalker Vael]] and Chronomancer Eliphas.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Echo-Under-Glass
* **Location**: Western shore of the Sea of Shards, semi-submerged beneath crystal tides.
* **Population**: ~1,400 (Echobound, Tritons, Aquatic Elves).
* **Description**: A terraced coastal village carved out of sea-smoothed selenite crystal. Fishermen use prismatic glass nets to trawl for glowing memory-minnows and translucent glass-trout.
* **Specialty**: Glass fishing gear, preservation resin, and distilled tidal memories.
* **Notable Feature — The Singing Reef**: An offshore ridge of hollow crystal tubes that chimes with haunting melodies when tides roll in.

### 2. Oblivion's Well
* **Location**: Edge of the Dread Chasm, shrouded in permanent violet gloom.
* **Population**: ~900 (Monks, Githzerai, Penitents).
* **Description**: An austere monastery-settlement built from soundless basalt. Pilgrims travel here to voluntarily surrender unbearable memories into the chasm through guided mental rituals.
* **Specialty**: Mind-blanking teas, lead ward-scrolls, and silent meditation cells.
* **Notable Feature — The Unweeping Bell**: A massive stone bell rung to shock traumatized minds out of psychological catatonia.

### 3. Prismsight
* **Location**: At the convergence point where the Thread of Remembrance crosses the Fields of Echoes.
* **Population**: ~1,600 (Kalashtar, Gnomes, Diviners).
* **Description**: A colorful market-town of silk tents and brass scaffolding. Diviners and fortune-tellers set up shop here to catch glimpses of future threads winding through the golden leyline.
* **Specialty**: Scrying mirrors, thread-weaving spindles, and future-glimpse tarot decks.
* **Notable Feature — The Golden Loom**: An immense standing loom where Memory Weavers repair frayed connections in the Thread of Remembrance.

### 4. Fugue-Hold
* **Location**: An isolated plateau between Reminaria's shadow and the Fractured Spire.
* **Population**: ~1,200 (Amnesiac Adventurers, Fugueborn, Hermits).
* **Description**: A sanctuary for travelers who have lost their identities to the plane. The town operates entirely on collective mutual aid, assigning new names and peaceful roles to each newcomer.
* **Specialty**: Memory-grounding totems, journal archives, and sensory restoration herbs.
* **Notable Feature — The Wall of Names**: A half-mile limestone cliff carved with the original names of thousands of planar travelers who forgot who they were.

### 5. Shard-Drift Outpost
* **Location**: Mobile; drifting across the Sea of Shards on a raft of buoyant pumice and fused glass.
* **Population**: ~600 (Scavengers, Outlaws, Planar Sailors).
* **Description**: A rough, lawless floating outpost that docks at various shattered isles to salvage rare pre-Shattered Loom relics washed up on glass shores.
* **Specialty**: Salvaged relics, glass-cutting tools, and void-diving gear.
* **Notable Feature — The Harpoon Gantry**: Ballistas firing enchanted lines to lasso floating memory isles.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Sea of Shards
* **Description**: An infinite ocean of floating, translucent glass shards that chime harmonically with the tides.
* **Planar Trait**: Falling into the sea deals 4d6 slashing + 2d6 psychic damage per round; gazing into the deep water triggers a Requires strong mental and physical fortitude to withstand.

### 2. The Veiled Archive
* **Description**: An infinite, warped library where books float through the air and corridors loop through forgotten epochs.
* **Planar Trait**: Navigating requires saving throw Arcana or Investigation checks; failure results in being displaced into an illusory memory chamber for 1d4 hours.

### 3. The Fields of Echoes
* **Description**: Rolling landscapes where historical events, festivals, and battlefields continuously replay as tangible illusions.
* **Planar Trait**: Creatures within the fields have advantage on History checks, but must make Requires strong mental and physical fortitude to withstand.

### 4. The Dread Chasm
* **Description**: A jagged wound in the planar fabric echoing with screams, weeping, and traumatic memories.
* **Planar Trait**: Emits an aura of despair: creatures within 300 feet make saving throws against fear and despair with disadvantage, and nonmagical flames burn cold and violet.

### 5. The Thread of Remembrance
* **Description**: A glowing golden thread of pure memory that snakes through the sky across the entire plane.
* **Planar Trait**: Following the Thread prevents planar disorientation, grants immunity to amnesia, and allows teleportation between major convergence points.

### 6. The Mirrored Labyrinth
* **Description**: A subterranean labyrinth beneath Mirrorglade where every wall is a mirror showing alternate realities.
* **Planar Trait**: Looking into the mirrors requires a Requires strong mental and physical fortitude to withstand.
