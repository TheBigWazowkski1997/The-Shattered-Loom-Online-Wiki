## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Plane of Wild Balance Verdant Nexus#1. Viridspire: The Canopy Citadel|Viridspire: The Canopy Citadel]]
* **Location**: Perched miles above the ground in the crown branches of the **Elderbough**, in the northeastern Verdant Cradle.
* **Role**: The spiritual, philosophical, and diplomatic capital of primal balance, druidic conclaves, and shamans.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Verdant Council Grove**: A massive open-air amphitheater in the upper canopy where archdruids and spirit shamans commune with the Voice of the Tree.
  * **The Leafweaver Terraces**: Living organic bridges and platforms shaped from symbiotic vines and iron-bough leaves.
  * **The Rootheart Caverns**: Subterranean luminescent chambers at the base of the Elderbough where the tree draws vitality directly from the plane's core.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Verdant Council and the Archdruid of the Elderbough.

### 2. [[Plane of Wild Balance Verdant Nexus#2. Tidehearth: The Shifting Reef|Tidehearth: The Shifting Reef]]
* **Location**: The southwestern Azure Ocean, drifting across warm primordial currents.
* **Role**: Nomadic oceanic metropolis of living coral, aquatic trade, and maritime balance.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Coral Crown**: A towering sentient spire of glowing prism-coral that directs the migration of the reef.
  * **The Shimmering Bazaar**: A shifting aquatic marketplace trading in bioluminescent pearls, water-lungs, and sea-forged coral blades.
  * **The Abyssal Gardens**: Underwater sanctuaries of radiant kelp and ancient coral statues where predators and prey dwell without bloodshed.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Circle of Tidecallers and the Reefwardens.

### 3. [[Plane of Wild Balance Verdant Nexus#3. Stonehollow: The Cavern of Eternal Equilibrium|Stonehollow: The Cavern of Eternal Equilibrium]]
* **Location**: Southeastern mountain ranges, carved into the hollow heart of Mount Terradon.
* **Role**: Subterranean engineering capital of elemental fusion, geomancy, and volcanic metallurgy.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Elemental Wellspring**: A sacred convergence chamber where rivers of magma, glacial waterfalls, pressurized wind conduits, and mineral crystals meet.
  * **The Emberforges**: Deep thermal foundries powered by lava flows, producing tools attuned to natural leylines.
  * **The Crystal Pillars**: Enormous grounding stalagmites that absorb seismic instability and anchor the city's foundations.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Stonekeepers Guild, led by Elder Geomancers and Earth Genasi Clanlords.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Bramblegate
* **Location**: The western frontier bordering the Shifting Expanse.
* **Population**: ~1,400 (Firbolgs, Centaurs, Rangers).
* **Description**: A fortified palisade settlement grown from dense, thorn-bearing ironwood. It serves as an early-warning outpost against uncontrolled elemental surges spilling from the chaotic plains.
* **Specialty**: Heavy bramble-bows, beast-tracking pheromones, and thermal ward-salves.
* **Notable Feature — The Horn of the Wild**: A petrified hollow horn mounted atop the gate that sounds with the wind when elemental titans approach.

### 2. Spore-Haven (The Decay Bastion)
* **Location**: The southern boundary of the Silent Glades, nestled within a forest of giant bioluminescent mushrooms.
* **Population**: ~950 (Golgari-born Elves, Myconids, Hermit Necro-Druids).
* **Description**: A serene settlement dedicated to the sacred necessity of decomposition. Buildings are hollowed puffballs and petrified shelf-fungi glowing with soft lavender light.
* **Specialty**: Compost-elixirs that rapidly restore blighted soil, antitoxins, and rot-spore defensive dusts.
* **Notable Feature — The Compost Caldera**: A massive steaming peat basin where fallen matter is broken down into primal essence in seconds.

### 3. Zephyr-Roost
* **Location**: High limestone spires overlooking the Vitreous River canyon.
* **Population**: ~750 (Aarakocra, Wind-Genasi, Griffin Riders).
* **Description**: An aerie of woven wicker nests suspended between vertical rock needles, home to sky-rangers and messengers who ferry travelers between Viridspire and Stonehollow.
* **Specialty**: Feathered glider-cloaks, storm-prediction crystals, and trained celestial hawks.
* **Notable Feature — The Wind-Catchers**: Gigantic sails made of spider-silk that harvest mist to generate potable mountain water.

### 4. Coral-Crest
* **Location**: A cluster of stationary atolls on the coastal shelf where the Vitreous River flows into the Azure Ocean.
* **Population**: ~1,200 (Sea Elves, Tritons, Tortles).
* **Description**: A half-submerged harbor town built on natural basalt terraces and giant clamshells, acting as the land-to-sea transfer station for trade caravans heading to Tidehearth.
* **Specialty**: Kelp-silk sails, diving oils, and amphibious pack-beasts.
* **Notable Feature — The Tidal Basin**: Stepped seawater pools that automatically filter river silt, keeping the harbor crystalline clear.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Wildheart Nexus & Primal Colossus
* **Description**: The geographic and spiritual heart of the plane—a titanic living tree whose four main boughs represent fire, frost, stone, and wind.
* **Planar Trait**: The Nexus stabilizes natural magic across the plane. Casting druidic or elemental spells within 5 miles maximizes all healing dice and adds 1d8 elemental damage of the caster's choice.

### 2. The Shifting Expanse
* **Description**: A chaotic elemental proving ground where lava rivers crash into icy tundras and lightning strikes glass dunes.
* **Planar Trait**: Elemental affinities cycle every 6 hours. Travelers must pass saving throw Survival checks to adapt gear or suffer extreme temperature exposure and elemental hazards.

### 3. The Silent Glades
* **Description**: A solemn forest transitioning from golden blooming orchards to skeletal, moss-covered ghostwood groves.
* **Planar Trait**: In the Life Wells, non-undead creatures regain 1 hit point per minute; near the Death Wells, natural decay accelerates tenfold, allowing wounds to rot if not cleansed with holy or purified water.

### 4. The Vitreous River
* **Description**: A river of water so pure and mineral-rich that it flows like liquid beryl glass from the Primal Colossus to the Azure Ocean.
* **Planar Trait**: Drinking from the river removes one level of exhaustion and cleanses all mundane poisons and diseases.

### 5. Ghostwood Groves
* **Description**: Shimmering groves existing simultaneously in the plane and the Ethereal borderland, inhabited by ancestral animal spirits.
* **Planar Trait**: Creatures within the grove can interact with ethereal and incorporeal creatures as though they were physical; undead have disadvantage on all saving throws here.
