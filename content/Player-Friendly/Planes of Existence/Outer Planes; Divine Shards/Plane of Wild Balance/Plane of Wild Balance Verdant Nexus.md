**Description:** A sprawling plane of infinite, lush wilderness where life and death cycle seamlessly. Giant trees rise to touch the stars, rivers flow with luminescent water, and creatures of all sizes live in perfect harmony. Druids and nature deities draw power from this plane.The **Plane of Wild Balance** is a realm of constant flux, where creation and destruction, life and death, and elemental forces exist in perfect harmony. Its key features embody the themes of equilibrium, renewal, and the interdependence of opposites. Each landmark within the plane serves as a focal point for its natural forces, offering adventurers and explorers both wonder and danger.

#### **1\. The Verdant Cradle**

The **Verdant Cradle** is a sprawling region of vibrant, lush ecosystems where the forces of life thrive. Towering trees stretch into a shimmering sky, their branches forming natural bridges between miles-wide canopies. Rivers teem with life, and the air is alive with the sounds of animals and elemental spirits.

* **Features**:
	* **Flourishing Forests**: The forests are dense with giant trees, glowing fungi, and vines that pulse faintly with radiant life energy. Plants here grow rapidly and often in fantastical forms.
	* **Crystal Springs**: Hidden pools of glowing, pure water bubble with life energy. These springs heal wounds, cleanse diseases, and radiate rejuvenating warmth.
	* **Living Guardians**: Massive treants, druidic animals, and plant-elemental hybrids watch over the region, ensuring no mortal harvests more than the land can bear.

#### **2\. The Shifting Expanse**

A chaotic region where elemental forces clash, creating a constantly changing terrain. The landscape alternates between fire-scorched plains, frozen tundras, cascading waterfalls, and windswept cliffs. This is where the **elemental balance of the plane** is most visible, as the elements ebb and flow in harmony.

* **Features**:
	* **Elemental Battlegrounds**: Rivers of lava cut through icy valleys, and lightning storms dance over seas of golden sand. Travelers can witness and interact with elemental forces in their purest forms.
	* **Elemental Wells**: Hidden among the chaotic landscape are **Wells of Fire, Water, Air, and Earth**, each a source of immense elemental energy.
	* **Elemental Spirits**: Free-roaming spirits of fire, water, air, and earth patrol the expanse, often engaging in playful rivalries or offering aid to travelers who respect the balance.

#### **3\. The Silent Glades**

The **Silent Glades** are a hauntingly beautiful region dedicated to the balance between life and death. Here, vibrant groves filled with life slowly transition into eerie fields of decay. Mushrooms and moss thrive alongside withered trees, symbolizing the cycle of renewal.

* **Features**:
	* **Life and Death Wells**: The **Life Wells** sparkle with golden light, radiating energy that nurtures life, while the **Death Wells** are shadowed pools that emanate the power of decay and transformation. Both are sacred to the balance.
	* **Ghostwood Forest**: A forest of pale, spectral trees that are alive yet seem to exist partially in the ethereal plane. Spirits of animals and mortals roam here, often reflecting on their former lives.
	* **Graveward Guardians**: Spectral beings that protect the Death Wells and the natural process of decay, ensuring no mortal interferes with the balance between death and renewal

#### **4\. The Wildheart Nexus**

The **Wildheart Nexus** is the central feature of the Plane of Wild Balance—a colossal, living convergence point of all the plane’s forces. It is the beating heart of the plane, a place of raw, unfiltered primal energy where creation and destruction occur simultaneously.

* **Appearance**:
	* At the Nexus's core stands the **Primal Colossus**, a tree-like titan with branches made of fire, ice, stone, and shimmering air. Around it swirls a vortex of elemental power, feeding into and being shaped by the plane.
* **Purpose**:
	* The Nexus ensures the balance of the plane, channeling energy to areas that need renewal or decay. It is also a place where powerful rituals can be performed to influence the plane’s cycles.
* **Guardians**:
	* The **Council of Wild Spirits**—a group of powerful Primal Spirits—defends the Nexus from those who would misuse its energy.

### **1\. Viridspire: The Canopy Citadel**

**Viridspire** is a breathtaking city built high into the colossal branches of the **Elderbough**, a living, ancient tree that reaches beyond the clouds. The Elderbough is said to be one of the first trees to grow in the multiverse, its roots deep in the plane’s primal core. Viridspire exists as a series of interconnected platforms, villages, and terraces woven seamlessly into the branches and leaves of the tree, suspended high above the ever-shifting wildlands below.
The city serves as the spiritual and cultural heart of the Plane of Wild Balance, a gathering place for druids, shamans, rangers, and scholars seeking to understand and protect the harmony of nature. Despite its beauty, Viridspire is not immune to danger—the Elderbough has been known to shift or shed its branches when the natural order is threatened, forcing its inhabitants to adapt or perish.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Verdant Council Grove**
	* A massive clearing in the Elderbough’s upper canopy where the **Verdant Council** convenes. Composed of archdruids, treetop elders, and spirit shamans, the Council guides the city and ensures its actions do not disrupt the plane’s balance.
	* **Lore**: It is said the Grove is home to the **Voice of the Tree**, a sentient spirit that whispers prophecies and guides those who truly listen to the pulse of the Elderbough.
2. **The Leafweaver Terraces**
	* Intricate platforms and walkways made of living vines and branches, cultivated by **Leafweavers**—druids and architects who shape the tree without harming it. Homes and buildings are grown rather than constructed, blending perfectly with the Elderbough’s natural form.
3. **The Rootheart Caverns**
	* At the base of the Elderbough lies a network of vast, glowing caverns where the tree’s roots pulse with primal energy. The caverns serve as a place of meditation, ritual, and communion with the primal spirits of the land.
	* **Lore**: The deeper one travels into the Rootheart Caverns, the closer they come to the **Heartroot**, where it is said the Elderbough draws life from the very core of the plane.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **Leafweavers**: Druids and artisans who shape the Elderbough’s branches and tend to its well-being.
* **The Verdant Council**: Leaders and shamans who protect the harmony of the Elderbough and arbitrate disputes.
* **Spire Sentinels**: Rangers and guardians who patrol the treetop pathways and defend Viridspire from external threats.

### **2\. Tidehearth: The Shifting Reef**

**Tidehearth** is a dynamic and ever-changing city built atop a massive, living coral reef that drifts along the vast oceans of the Plane of Wild Balance. The reef, known as the **Prismreef**, constantly grows, dies, and regenerates, reshaping the city’s layout and forcing its inhabitants to adapt with it. Homes, pathways, and towers are constructed from the reef’s natural coral formations, glowing with bioluminescent light that illuminates the deep waters.
Tidehearth serves as a haven for aquatic beings, planar travelers, and seafaring nomads. Its people embrace impermanence, living in harmony with the reef’s shifting nature. The **Tidecallers**, a caste of druids and sages, oversee the reef’s health and maintain its symbiotic balance with the oceanic ecosystems.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Coral Crown**
	* A majestic spire of living coral that rises at the center of Tidehearth. It serves as the seat of power for the **Tidecallers** and pulses with the ocean’s rhythms. The Coral Crown is said to be sentient, responding to the needs of the reef and its inhabitants.
	* **Lore**: The Crown’s energy is tied to the **Primal Tide**, an elemental current that nourishes the Plane of Wild Balance’s oceans.
2. **The Shimmering Bazaar**
	* A sprawling market where traders and travelers barter in rare aquatic goods, enchanted shells, and bioluminescent pearls. The market shifts daily as the reef grows, requiring merchants to relocate their stalls constantly.
3. **The Abyssal Gardens**
	* Cavernous underwater groves of glowing seaweed, crystal-clear pools, and ancient coral sculptures. The Gardens are both a place of meditation and a sacred hunting ground where predators and prey coexist in balance.
	* **Lore**: It is said that disturbing the Abyssal Gardens will awaken the **Reefwardens**, colossal elemental beings who protect the reef.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **Tidecallers**: Druids and sages who commune with the ocean and tend to the reef’s cycles of growth and decay.
* **Prism Divers**: Skilled swimmers and scavengers who retrieve treasures and resources from the ever-shifting coral tunnels.
* **Reefwardens**: Massive, sentient coral constructs that awaken when the reef is threatened.

### **3\. Stonehollow: The Cavern of Eternal Equilibrium**

**Stonehollow** is a subterranean city carved into the heart of a vast, hollowed-out mountain that lies at the crossroads of elemental forces. Rivers of molten lava flow alongside cascading waterfalls, while luminescent crystals jut from cavern walls, casting multicolored light across the city. Stonehollow thrives where fire, water, earth, and air collide, a city built to endure and adapt amidst the chaotic interplay of primal forces.
The city’s inhabitants are expert artisans, engineers, and elemental sages who manipulate the environment to maintain its delicate balance. Stonehollow is a testament to the ingenuity and resilience of those who live within the Plane of Wild Balance, its chambers filled with forges, aqueducts, and wind tunnels that channel the plane’s raw power into productive harmony.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Elemental Wellspring**
	* A sacred chamber where all four elements converge—a molten core surrounded by flowing water, swirling air, and crystal-studded earth. Sages and elementalists gather here to study and commune with the forces of the plane.
2. **The Emberforges**
	* Massive forges powered by the rivers of magma that flow through the city. Smiths and artificers craft tools, weapons, and artifacts capable of harnessing elemental energy.
3. **The Crystal Pillars**
	* Towering crystal formations that act as conduits for elemental energy, stabilizing the cavern and preventing it from collapsing under the plane’s chaotic forces.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **Stonekeepers**: Earth genasi and dwarven engineers who maintain the cavern’s stability and balance.
* **Elemental Sages**: Scholars who study and manipulate the elemental convergence within the Wellspring.
* **Forgecallers**: Artisans and smiths who use magma and crystal to craft elemental tools and relics.

**Notable Items for Sale**:

While wearing it, you have resistance to poison and piercing damage from nonmagical attacks, and advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made in forests or jungles.

* **Predator's Fang Gauntlets (+1 Unarmed Strikes)** (*Uncommon*, 750 gp): Edged with sabertooth cat fangs. Unarmed strikes deal 1d6 + Strength modifier slashing damage, and can score a critical hit on a roll of 19–20.

**Unique Services**:

* **Hide Curing & Tailoring**: Torgar can skin and cure the pelt of any beast or monster defeated by the party, turning it into custom magical armor or cloaks (Cost: 200–1,000 gp depending on CR).
* **Bone Carving**: Sharpens natural monster horns or fangs into +1 piercing daggers or arrowheads.

## **Taverns of Viridspire**

### **1. The Roaring Canopy**

**Theme**: Soaring Open-Air Treetop Pavilion with Feast Fires  
**Location**: Built across five interconnected massive limbs of the Elderbough, 600 feet above the forest floor.  
**Owner**: **"Grizzly" Bran**, a retired werebear warrior who greets all friendly patrons with a hearty back-slap.  
**Description**: The tavern is open to the skies, sheltered only by a dense ceiling of colossal golden and green leaves. Giant spit-fires roast wild game harvested sustainably from the lower wildlands. Trapeze artists, ranger scouts, and druidic bards drink, sing, and dance along suspended rope bridges high above the clouds.  

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **Berry-Marrow Mead** *(House Special)*: Fermented from wild canopy honey and crushed spirit-berries; rich and intoxicating, grants 10 temporary hit points and +1 to melee damage rolls for 4 hours (4 sp).
* **Elderbough Cider**: Crisp, effervescent apple cider spiced with cinnamon and forest mint (2 sp).
* **Spit-Roasted Forest Boar**: Carved fresh from the spit, seasoned with smoked wild garlic and sage (8 sp).
* **Canopy Chestnut Bowl**: Roasted sweet nuts tossed in honey glaze and sea salt (3 sp).

**Entertainment**:

* **Bough Balancing Contest**: Patrons test their agility by walking narrow, swaying branches over the sheer drop; winners get their bar tab erased.
* **Primal Howling Circles**: Bards and shamans lead rhythmic drumming and wolf-howl choruses celebrating the seasonal shifts of the plane.

**Theme**: Grimy Fungal Dive in the Dark Lower Roots  
**Location**: Carved into a massive hollowed-out decaying stump at the damp base of the Elderbough.
