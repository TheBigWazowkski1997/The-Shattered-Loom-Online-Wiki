## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Plane of UnityThe Woven Citadel#1. Althera Crossing: The City of Eternal Light|Althera Crossing: The City of Eternal Light]]
* **Location**: North-central stratosphere, constructed atop the celestial river of light known as the **Luminara Flow**.
* **Role**: The diplomatic, intellectual, and cultural capital of the plane; sanctuary for planar envoys and peacemakers.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Radiant Conflux**: A towering cathedral of carved sunstone where the Luminara Flow cascades into an iridescent reservoir, dispersing discord.
  * **The Prismatic Archive**: An immense crystal library preserving knowledge in resonant Thought Crystals gifted by Archon Lumari.
  * **The Bridge of Accord**: A grand marble span lined with woven gold threads where warring factions negotiate unshakeable treaties.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Council of Stewards and the Golden Attendants, guided by [[Lumari, the Archon of Unity]].

### 2. [[Plane of UnityThe Woven Citadel#2. Verdancy Hollow: The Blooming City of Harmony|Verdancy Hollow: The Blooming City of Harmony]]
* **Location**: Western celestial platform, thriving within the branches of the colossal **Heartbloom Tree**.
* **Role**: Sanctuary of organic symbiosis, magical horticulture, living architecture, and healing arts.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Heartbloom Tree**: A miles-wide primordial tree radiating restorative vitality across the western reaches.
  * **The Verdant Bazaar**: An open-air forest market where stalls grow organically from wood and living vines.
  * **The Whispering Pools**: Sacred mirrored ponds reflecting prophetic visions of cross-planar reconciliation.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Leafcallers Conclave and Elder Botanists.

### 3. [[Plane of UnityThe Woven Citadel#3. Paxus Arx: The Bastion of Eternal Accord|Paxus Arx: The Bastion of Eternal Accord]]
* **Location**: Eastern floating plateau, surrounded by rings of concentric polished marble bastions and starlit terraces.
* **Role**: Fortress-citadel of defense, haven for displaced refugees, and garrison of the Sentinels of Accord.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Hall of Concord**: A colossal amphitheater hosting military drills focused on defense, shielding, and non-lethal pacification.
  * **The Star-Meadows**: Rolling terraces of starlit grass where refugees rest and recover from planar trauma.
  * **The Aegis Spire**: A skyward watchtower projecting a planar ward that neutralizes curses and banishes fiendish discord.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: High Justiciar Valerius and the Captains of the Sentinels of Accord.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Thread-Haven
* **Location**: At the primary convergence node of three major golden leylines south of Althera Crossing.
* **Population**: ~1,800 (Aasimar, Weavers, Githzerai).
* **Description**: A graceful community built upon suspended crystal pontoons. Inhabitants work as Weft-Spinners, repairing minor frays in the plane's anchoring filaments.
* **Specialty**: Loom-silk ropes, resonance tuning bells, and thread-stitching needles.
* **Notable Feature — The Harmonic Spool**: An immense rotating spool of solidified starlight that regulates tension along local bridges.

### 2. Mosaic-Fold
* **Location**: Nestled on the border between the desert and glacial zones of the Infinite Mosaic.
* **Population**: ~1,100 (Genasi of all four elements, Humans, Gnomes).
* **Description**: A multi-tiered architectural wonder where half the village sits on warm sandstone terraces and the other half rests on carved ice pillars, demonstrating elemental coexistence.
* **Specialty**: Thermal balance stones, steam-powered levitation gliders, and fusion crystals.
* **Notable Feature — The Equinox Arch**: An archway where fire and frost swirl together to form a warm, harmless aurora.

### 3. Gateway-Watch
* **Location**: At the foot of the Celestial Arches on the plane's southern perimeter.
* **Population**: ~1,400 (Sentinels of Accord, Planar Customs Arbiters, Monks).
* **Description**: A gleaming waystation and staging outpost where planar travelers are greeted, screened, and offered restorative baths before entering the cities.
* **Specialty**: Planar travel visas, attunement oils, and luggage stabilization crates.
* **Notable Feature — The Cleansing Gate**: An arch of liquid white fire that burns away curses and hostile magical enchantments without harming the traveler.

### 4. Whispering Glade
* **Location**: A secluded valley within the Veiled Harmony region.
* **Population**: ~600 (Hermits, Oracles, Kalashtar).
* **Description**: A quiet hamlet of modest stone domes surrounded by glowing silver mist. Silence is strictly observed, with communication occurring entirely through telepathic empathy.
* **Specialty**: Insight incenses, scrying mirrors, and psychic alignment tonics.
* **Notable Feature — The Monolith of Empathy**: A standing stone that allows any two beings holding hands against it to instantly share each other's memories and feelings.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Radiant Confluence & Nexus Spire
* **Description**: The central cosmic hub of the plane where all golden filaments anchor, housing the governing body of the Weavers.
* **Planar Trait**: Violence is magically suppressed within 2 miles of the Spire. Any creature that makes an attack roll must succeed on a saving throw Charisma saving throw or lose the action and be overcome with profound empathy.

### 2. The Luminara Flow
* **Description**: A broad river of liquid starlight and positive energy winding through the sky between floating landmasses.
* **Planar Trait**: Non-evil creatures bathed in the flow gain the benefits of a *Greater Restoration* spell and regain 10 hit points per round; fiends or creatures of pure malevolence take 4d10 radiant damage per round.

### 3. The Infinite Mosaic
* **Description**: A self-contained landscape of impossible biome juxtaposition where tundras melt into deserts and tropical seas lap against volcanic peaks without conflict.
* **Planar Trait**: Creatures traveling across the Mosaic are immune to extreme cold and extreme heat, as the environment naturally attunes to sustain all living forms.

### 4. The Celestial Arches
* **Description**: Titanic glowing colonnades rising thousands of feet into the aurora, serving as stable conduits to other planes of existence.
* **Planar Trait**: Opening an archway requires an attunement ritual requiring true cooperation between two beings of opposing alignments or elements.

### 5. The Veiled Harmony
* **Description**: A misty, reflective zone where the boundary between light and shadow is softened into gentle grey dawn.
* **Planar Trait**: Divination magic cast here functions at double range and duration, and illusions are revealed as harmless light patterns.
