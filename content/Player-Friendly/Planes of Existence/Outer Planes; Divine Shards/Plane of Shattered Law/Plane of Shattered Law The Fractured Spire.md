**Description:** Once the pinnacle of order, this plane was shattered during the war between Law and Chaos. Now, floating platforms of black marble and iron hover in an endless void, with rigid laws governing the movements of everything within.The **Plane of Shattered Law** is a realm of broken structure, fragmented rules, and chaotic echoes of what was once perfect order. It is a place where the universal laws of reality—gravity, time, logic, and morality—lie in ruin, fractured by some ancient calamity. The remnants of order still linger, but they are unstable, shifting, and vulnerable to further collapse. What remains is a chaotic, paradoxical plane where the very concept of structure rebels against itself, creating a surreal and dangerous environment.
The Plane of Shattered Law is not merely a place—it is a **conceptual battlefield**, where the ideals of law and chaos are locked in perpetual conflict. Travelers who venture here often find their perceptions, morals, and even physical forms tested by the plane’s ever-changing and paradoxical nature.

#### **1\. The Fractured Pillars**

The **Fractured Pillars** are towering remnants of the fundamental laws of reality, now shattered and cracked, radiating unstable energy. These monumental spires once anchored the multiverse’s structure, ensuring balance and harmony, but now they are symbols of the plane’s broken nature.

* **Appearance**:Massive pillars of glowing crystal, metal, and stone rise into the sky, some floating mid-air and others fractured into jagged shards. Each pillar hums with distorted energy, and faint glyphs pulse across their surfaces, flickering and fading unpredictably.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Gravity Spire**: A fractured pillar that distorts gravity in its vicinity, causing objects to float, fall sideways, or be crushed.
	* **The Chrono Obelisk**: A shattered obelisk that causes time distortions, creating pockets of accelerated or slowed time.
	* **The Ethereal Keystone**: A glowing crystal pillar that flickers between the Material and Ethereal Planes, distorting the boundary between the two realms.

---

#### **2\. The Broken Courts**

The **Broken Courts** are the remnants of a once-majestic judicial city where the laws of the multiverse were debated, codified, and enforced. Now in ruin, the courts are haunted by fragmented echoes of their former rulers and enforcers.

* **Appearance**:The Broken Courts consist of crumbling marble halls, shattered thrones, and collapsed columns. Ghostly silhouettes of judges and advocates wander the halls, endlessly repeating long-forgotten verdicts in whispers that echo through the ruins.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Judgement Hall**: A massive chamber filled with broken scales of justice and spectral echoes of past trials. The scales glow faintly, shifting wildly between extremes of punishment and mercy.
	* **The Arbiter’s Throne**: A shattered seat of power at the heart of the courts. It is said that whoever sits upon the throne can temporarily impose their will on the plane, restoring a semblance of order or further unraveling it.
	* **The Obsidian Gavel**: A powerful artifact hidden within the courts, capable of dispelling chaotic magic or enforcing temporary laws.

---

#### **3\. The Cracked Labyrinth**

The **Cracked Labyrinth** is a maze of shifting corridors and pathways, representing the fractured logic of the plane. Once a perfect geometric structure, it is now warped and unstable, with paths that loop endlessly, walls that dissolve into voids, and doors that lead to entirely different locations.

* **Appearance**:The labyrinth is made of jagged stone, glowing lines of light, and floating pathways. Its walls flicker between solidity and transparency, revealing glimpses of other parts of the plane—or other times entirely.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Gate of Paradox**: A glowing portal within the labyrinth that tests travelers with impossible riddles and contradictory tasks. Only those who embrace paradox can pass through.
	* **The Memory Mirrors**: Reflective surfaces scattered throughout the maze that show distorted memories of the viewer’s past, challenging their sense of self and purpose.
	* **The Void Corridors**: Pathways that lead into empty voids, trapping unwary travelers in pockets of endless nothingness.

---

#### **4\. The Scales of Discord**

The **Scales of Discord** is a massive, floating structure resembling a pair of scales that hover above a chaotic void. Once used to maintain balance across the multiverse, the scales are now cracked and unstable, their equilibrium lost.

* **Appearance**:The Scales are enormous, made of gleaming gold and silver, but they are tarnished and cracked. One side tilts wildly, weighed down by a massive shard of chaos, while the other is empty, glowing faintly as if longing for balance.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Chaos Shard**: A fragment of pure chaos rests on one side of the scales, distorting the plane around it. Removing or stabilizing the shard requires immense effort.
	* **The Balance Crystal**: A glowing crystal that was once used to counter the Chaos Shard has fallen into the void below. Retrieving it is a dangerous task.
	* **The Arbiter’s Shade**: A spectral figure guards the scales, testing those who seek to restore their balance.

---

#### **5\. The Lawbreaker’s Abyss**

The **Lawbreaker’s Abyss** is a swirling vortex of shattered laws and chaotic energy. It serves as a prison for those who defied the plane’s original order, now transformed into a chaotic storm of rebellion and destruction.

* **Appearance**:The Abyss is a gaping whirlpool of black and silver energy, filled with floating debris—fragments of laws, ruins of cities, and the remnants of the plane’s original structure.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Chains of Rebellion**: Massive, glowing chains drift within the abyss, binding ancient entities that once opposed the plane’s order.
	* **The Prison of Echoes**: A central structure within the Abyss that holds echoes of those who defied the laws, their voices creating an eternal cacophony.
	* **The Core Fragment**: A glowing shard of the plane’s original law lies hidden in the Abyss, guarded by chaotic spirits.

### **1\. Axiom: The City of Broken Symmetry**

**Axiom** was once the crown jewel of the Plane of Shattered Law, a city that represented perfect balance, symmetry, and order. Now, it stands as a fractured and distorted version of its former self. The city is divided into countless shards of reality, each piece floating in the air like fragments of a shattered mirror. Bridges of crystalline energy connect the fragments, flickering in and out of existence, while the streets twist and spiral in ways that defy logic. Gravity shifts unpredictably, and entire districts appear and disappear depending on the alignment of the plane’s broken laws.
Despite its shattered nature, Axiom remains a hub of power and influence. The **Wardens of the Fractured Code**, a sect of zealots who seek to restore the lost laws of the multiverse, rule the city from its central citadel. Axiom is both a sanctuary for those who revere law and a battleground for those who wish to twist its remnants to their own ends.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Fractured Spire**
	* A towering structure of broken stone and energy that serves as the seat of power for the **Wardens of the Fractured Code**. The Spire pulses with fragments of ancient law, and the laws of reality are temporarily stabilized within its immediate vicinity.
	* **Lore**: The Fractured Spire was once the **Pinnacle of Order**, where the first laws of reality were written. Its current broken state mirrors the fragmentation of the plane itself.
2. **The Paradox Market**
	* A sprawling marketplace where merchants sell fragments of laws, paradoxical artifacts, and unstable magic. Buyers must navigate carefully, as some trades result in unintended consequences, such as reversing gravity or warping time.
	* **Lore**: Many of the items sold here are shards of the original laws of reality, broken during the plane’s collapse.
3. **The Shardfield**
	* A vast expanse of floating fragments of the old city, accessible only through carefully maintained bridges of light. The Shardfield is a dangerous but rewarding place for adventurers, as the broken districts contain lost treasures and echoes of the original laws.
	* **Lore**: The Shardfield is said to hold a fragment of the **First Code**, a piece of reality’s original foundation.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **Wardens of the Fractured Code**: A militant sect that seeks to restore the original laws of reality, though their methods are often draconian.
* **Reality Tinkers**: Artisans and scholars who experiment with the broken laws of the plane, crafting paradoxical tools and spells.
* **Lawshards**: Sentient fragments of the old laws that wander the city, offering cryptic guidance—or hindrance—to those they encounter.

---

### **2\. Lex Machina: The Clockwork Ruin**

**Lex Machina** is a city built upon the remains of a **colossal clockwork machine** that once governed the laws of time and causality within the plane. The city’s inhabitants live within and upon the intricate gears, cogs, and mechanisms of the machine, which still turn and churn despite being shattered and incomplete. The city is in a constant state of motion, its streets shifting as the gears rotate, forcing its inhabitants to adapt to their ever-changing surroundings.
The machine that forms the city’s foundation is said to contain fragments of the **Prime Engine**, the mechanism that once maintained the balance of the multiverse’s laws. Many of the city’s residents worship the machine as a divine entity, believing that repairing it will restore order to the plane. However, others exploit its fractured nature, using its unstable mechanisms to manipulate time and causality for their own gain.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Prime Engine**
	* The heart of Lex Machina, a massive, partially destroyed clockwork device that radiates temporal energy. The engine is worshiped by the **Cogwrights**, a religious order dedicated to repairing and preserving it.
	* **Lore**: It is said that the Prime Engine was built by the **Architects of Law**, divine beings who first codified the rules of time and space.
2. **The Spiral Plaza**
	* A central hub where the city’s rotating gears align briefly, allowing for trade and social gatherings. The Plaza is surrounded by merchant stalls selling artifacts, fragments of time, and mysterious contraptions.
3. **The Temporal Vault**
	* A heavily guarded chamber deep within the city, where shards of frozen time and broken causality are stored. The vault is protected by **Temporal Constructs**, mechanical beings that act as the city’s enforcers.
	* **Lore**: The Temporal Vault contains glimpses of alternate timelines and futures, but accessing them comes with the risk of creating paradoxes.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **Cogwrights**: Devout engineers and clerics who maintain the Prime Engine and seek to restore its full functionality.
* **Temporal Constructs**: Clockwork guardians built from fragments of the Prime Engine, enforcing order within the city.
* **Time Thieves**: Rogues and outlaws who exploit the city’s unstable temporal energies to manipulate events to their advantage.

---

### **3\. The Lawforge: The Fractured Crucible**

**The Lawforge** is a volcanic city built around a **molten core of law**, a fiery nexus of raw energy where broken fragments of the plane’s laws are reforged into new shapes. The city itself is a vast forge, with rivers of molten stone and enchanted metal flowing through its streets. The forges burn eternally, powered by the plane’s shattered laws, and the city’s artisans shape these fragments into weapons, tools, and constructs of incredible power.
The city’s rulers, the **Iron Codex**, are a council of master smiths and lawmakers who believe that the only way to restore the plane is to forge new laws from the remnants of the old. However, their creations are often unstable, and the pursuit of perfection sometimes leads to catastrophic failures. The Lawforge is both a place of creation and destruction, where the boundaries between chaos and order blur.

---

#### **Key Features**

1. **The Crucible Core**
	* A massive molten pit at the city’s center, where fragments of broken laws are melted down and reforged. The Iron Codex oversees the process, ensuring that only the strongest laws are created.
2. **The Smelter District**
	* A sprawling network of forges and workshops where artisans craft weapons, armor, and constructs from the molten remnants of the plane’s laws.
3. **The Codex Hall**
	* A grand hall where the Iron Codex deliberates and records newly forged laws. These laws are inscribed onto enchanted metal tablets and distributed throughout the city.

---

#### **Inhabitants**

* **Iron Codex**: A council of master smiths and lawmakers who govern the city.
* **Moltenborn**: Elemental beings forged from the Crucible Core, serving as workers and enforcers.
* **Lawwrights**: Artisans who specialize in crafting new laws from the fragments of old ones.

## **Shops of Axiom**

### **1. The Axiomatic Armory**

**Specialty**: Order-Bound Weapons, Truth-Tempered Plate, and Immovable Bulwarks  
**Location**: The High Causeway beneath the Fractured Spire.  
**Owner**: **Chief Justiciar Varos**, a stern githzerai war-magistrate clad in polished obsidian and gold armor.  
**Description**: Pristine weapon racks and armor dummies line this stark, geometrically perfect hall. Every weapon is forged with axiomatic runic equations etched into the flats of the blades, humming with lawful stabilization that suppresses random entropy.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Axiomatic Greatsword (+1 Greatsword)** (*Rare*, 2,400 gp): When attacking chaotic creatures or aberrations, the weapon deals an extra 2d6 radiant damage. Furthermore, damage dealt by this weapon is never rolled; it always deals maximum flat damage (12 + Strength modifier + 2d6 max).
* **Shield of Immovable Order (+1 Shield)** (*Rare*, 1,900 gp): While holding this shield, you cannot be moved, knocked prone, or pushed against your will by any physical or magical force unless you choose to allow it. Once per long rest, can anchor in mid-air as an *Immovable Rod*.
* **Plate of Absolute Sincerity (+1 Full Plate)** (*Rare*, 2,800 gp): Grants advantage on saving throws against illusion and enchantment spells. While wearing it, you are incapable of speaking a deliberate lie, and you immediately know if any creature within 15 feet speaks an untruth.
* **Law-Bane Crossbow (+1 Heavy Crossbow)** (*Uncommon*, 850 gp): Bolts fired from this weapon anchor the target's speed; hit targets have their speed reduced by 10 feet until the start of your next turn.

**Unique Services**:

* **Axiomatic Alignment**: Varos can balance any weapon's metallurgical weight to absolute perfection, granting a +1 bonus to initiative rolls while drawing it (Cost: 300 gp).
* **Contract Weapon Bonding**: Links a weapon to an individual's palm via runic brand; unauthorized wielders take 2d8 psychic damage if they pick it up.

**Specialty**: Binding Contracts, Geas Scrolls, Immunity Writs, and Planar Passports  
**Location**: The Paradox Market, Arcade of Decrees.  
**Owner**: **Advocate Brenda Vance**, a sharp-tongued tiefling solicitor whose silver-rimmed spectacles constantly analyze fine print and verbal technicalities.  
**Description**: Stacks of iron-bound parchment, blood-quills, and wax seals fill this labyrinthine legal office. Brenda deals in enforceable contracts that leverage the plane's residual cosmic law to bind mortals, fiends, and celestial beings to their sworn word.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Contract of Unbreakable Binding (Wonder)** (*Rare*, 1,500 gp): A two-party contract written in gold ink. When both parties sign willingly, violating any specified term triggers an automatic 5th-level *Geas* spell (saving throw save) that inflicts 5d10 psychic damage each morning the violation continues.
* **Scroll of Geas** (*Rare*, 1,000 gp) & **Scroll of Zone of Truth** (*Uncommon*, 250 gp).
* **Writ of Safe Conduct**: Official legal document recognized by the Wardens of the Fractured Code granting immune passage through all city gates and bridges (Cost: 300 gp).
* **Pact-Breaker’s Scalpel** (*Rare*, 1,800 gp): A silver dagger that can slice through invisible magical bonds, ending one active contract, curse, or *Geas* upon a successful saving throw melee touch attack against the contract parchment or branded flesh.

## **Taverns of Axiom**

### **1. The Broken Gavel**

**Theme**: Prestigious High-Court Salon & Arbitrators' Guildhall  
**Location**: Suspended on an arched marble bridge overlooking the Judgement Hall ruins.  
**Owner**: **Magistrate Justinian**, a retired human judge who drinks only vintage honey wine from silver chalices.  
**Description**: An imposing dining establishment with soaring marble columns and plush royal blue seating. Disputing planar merchants and ambassadors come here to settle conflicts over multi-course meals; Justinian acts as neutral arbiter, recording binding verdicts between dishes.  

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **The Verdict Vintage** *(House Special)*: A dry white wine chilled with elemental ice; grants advantage on Insight and Persuasion checks for 2 hours (1 gp).
* **Equinox Cordial**: Perfectly sweet and tart balance of pear and citrus liqueur (5 sp).
* **Venison of Just Proportion**: Roasted venison cut to exact gram weight, served with symmetric roasted root vegetables (12 sp).
* **Symmetrical Herb Tart**: Flaky pastry baked in perfect golden squares with sage and sheep's cheese (6 sp).

**Entertainment**:

* **Moot Court Duels**: Patrons debate philosophical planar cases; the tavern votes on the winner with silver tokens.
* **Clockwork String Quartet**: Precision mechanical automatons perform flawless mathematical symphonies.

**Theme**: Chaotic, Shifting Pub Where Gravity Inverts  
**Location**: Attached to the central rotation axle of the lower Shardfield gear-district.  
**Owner**: **"Clank" O'Malley**, a cyclopean dwarf with a brass monocle and a prosthetic peg made from an iron piston.

Deep within the subterranean hollows beneath the Broken Courts, accessible through a secret passage behind the cracked scales of justice, lies **The Null Sanctum**. In this forbidden zone, the laws of the universe are completely silenced, creating a pocket where neither contract, geas, nor divine authority holds sway. Here, illegal clause forgeries, contract-breaking scalpels, and illicit poisons are peddled by planar outlaws.
