## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Plane of Shattered Law The Fractured Spire#1. Axiom: The City of Broken Symmetry|Axiom: The City of Broken Symmetry]]
* **Location**: North-central void, orbiting the jagged needle of the **Fractured Spire**.
* **Role**: The judicial and administrative capital, ruled by zealots seeking to reforge the fundamental laws of reality.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Fractured Spire**: A crystalline monument of broken law-shards that artificially stabilizes localized physics and logic within its shadow.
  * **The Paradox Market**: Sprawling bazaar where reality tinkers sell bottled paradoxes, anti-gravity compasses, and frozen moments.
  * **The Shardfield**: A ring of floating district-fragments connected by flickering, intermittent energy bridges.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The **Wardens of the Fractured Code**, commanded by Grand Inquisitor Justinian.

### 2. [[Plane of Shattered Law The Fractured Spire#2. Lex Machina: The Clockwork Ruin|Lex Machina: The Clockwork Ruin]]
* **Location**: Southwestern sector, constructed upon the titanic, slowly turning gears of the shattered **Prime Engine**.
* **Role**: Center of chronomancy, clockwork automaton engineering, and temporal logic recovery.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Prime Engine**: A colossal partially shattered clockwork heart that continues to tick, pulsing temporal energy across the layer.
  * **The Spiral Plaza**: A revolving central public square that aligns with workshop gates every seventy-two minutes.
  * **The Temporal Vault**: Heavily defended subterranean chambers storing frozen causality-shards and alternate timeline branches.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Cogwrights Synod, led by Prime Artificer Gear-Lord Chronos.

### 3. [[Plane of Shattered Law The Fractured Spire#3. The Lawforge: The Fractured Crucible|The Lawforge: The Fractured Crucible]]
* **Location**: Northeastern sector, carved into the volcanic slopes of a floating caldera of liquid axiom.
* **Role**: Industrial foundry city where broken decrees and shattered universal laws are melted down into enforceable mandates and weapons.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Crucible Core**: A churning caldera of molten white stone and glowing metal where broken laws are dissolved and recast.
  * **The Smelter District**: Rows of adamantine forges crafting weapons of binding, compliance collars, and golem soulcores.
  * **The Codex Hall**: High council chamber where the Iron Codex inscribes newly hammered decrees onto incandescent iron tablets.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Council of the Iron Codex, presided over by High Forgemaster Kaelen.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Precedent’s Fall
* **Location**: Anchored to a cluster of floating marble blocks beneath the Broken Courts.
* **Population**: ~1,200 (Legal Scholars, Githzerai, Spectral Advocates).
* **Description**: A somber town of parchment libraries and dust-choked legal archives. Scholars endlessly debate contradictory precedents established before the Loom's fall.
* **Specialty**: Legal defense scrolls, contract nullification writs, and notarized oaths.
* **Notable Feature — The Quorum Bell**: A cracked iron bell that rings automatically whenever two opposing parties reach a completely honest compromise.

### 2. Pendulum-Hold
* **Location**: Suspended directly beneath the outer rim of Lex Machina’s primary escapement wheel.
* **Population**: ~850 (Autognomes, Modron Exiles, Clockwork Tinkers).
* **Description**: A gravity-inverting settlement where buildings hang upside down. Inhabitants synchronize their heartbeats and tools to the perpetual sweep of a 300-foot pendulum.
* **Specialty**: Chrono-stabilizers, inertia compensators, and perpetual spring motors.
* **Notable Feature — The Tick-Tock Basin**: A stepped oil reservoir where mechanical beings submerge to purge temporal rust.

### 3. Null-Verdicts
* **Location**: The rocky perimeter overlooking the Lawbreaker’s Abyss.
* **Population**: ~1,500 (Outlaws, Rogue Paladins, Tieflings).
* **Description**: A lawless sanctuary perched on basalt crags where all codified laws are actively cancelled out by the vortex’s gravitational field. Fugitives gather here knowing that magical binding contracts lose all power within the town limits.
* **Specialty**: Counter-curse tattoos, geas-breaking salts, and anti-scrying talismans.
* **Notable Feature — The Broken Chain Monument**: A 60-foot monument composed of severed shackles left by escaped planar prisoners.

### 4. Pillar-Watch
* **Location**: The perimeter platform surrounding the Fractured Pillars.
* **Population**: ~700 (Monks of Equilibrium, Gravity Mages).
* **Description**: An austere monastery consisting of iron observation cages suspended in varying gravitational fields. Sentinels track the micro-fissures in reality caused by the Gravity Spire.
* **Specialty**: Gravitational anchoring boots, spatial compasses, and levitation harnesses.
* **Notable Feature — The Gyro-Chamber**: A training sphere where gravity shifts at random 90-degree angles every round.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Fractured Pillars
* **Description**: Colossal ancient obelisks representing fundamental physical constants (Gravity, Time, Dimensional Space) that pulse with volatile, fractured energy.
* **Planar Trait**: Within 1,000 feet of a pillar, physics becomes erratic. Gravity shifts on a d6 roll each round (1: Up, 2: Down, 3: East, 4: West, 5: Zero-G, 6: Crushing 2x Gravity).

### 2. The Broken Courts
* **Description**: A vast crumbling necropolis of marble halls and cracked colonnades haunted by phantom arbiters endlessly arguing dead cases.
* **Planar Trait**: Any lie spoken aloud in the Broken Courts manifests physically as a choking cloud of black smoke dealing 2d6 psychic damage to the speaker.

### 3. The Scales of Discord
* **Description**: A pair of titanic floating balances hovering over a bottomless chasm. One golden pan holds a colossal shard of raw chaos, while the other tilts empty.
* **Planar Trait**: The tilt of the scales alters planar alignment. Balancing the scales requires placing artifacts of equal lawful resonance, which temporarily dispels all wild magic in the plane.

### 4. The Lawbreaker’s Abyss
* **Description**: A churning gravitational and metaphysical maelstrom that shreds written contracts, treaties, and binding spells into glowing alphanumeric snow.
* **Planar Trait**: Any spell of the Abjuration school cast within 1 mile has a 50% chance to invert, targeting the caster instead of the intended subject.

### 5. The Prime Engine
* **Description**: The core clockwork apparatus beneath Lex Machina whose interlocking bronze gears stretch for miles into the void.
* **Planar Trait**: Attuning to the gear frequency grants advantage on initiative rolls and immunity to the *Slow* spell, but imposes disadvantage on Charisma checks against chaotic entities.
