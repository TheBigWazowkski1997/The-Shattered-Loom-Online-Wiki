# Map & Geography of the Plane of Infinite Hunger (The Spiral Abyss)

![[attachments/plane_of_infinite_hunger_map.jpg]]

> _"Unlike the layered Abyss of ancient cosmologies, this realm is an inward-spiraling vortex of pure appetite. In the Spiral Abyss, you do not simply fight starvation—the earth itself chews beneath your boots, the rivers salivate acid, and your own unfulfilled desires become hungry beasts waiting to eat you alive."_
> — Journal of Vexiryn the Unbowed

---

## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Plane of Infinite Hunger The Spiral Abyss#1. Mawhollow: The City Within the Gorge|Mawhollow: The City Within the Gorge]]
* **Location**: Carved along the vertical terraced cliffs of the boundless Chasm of Devouring, halfway along the inward spiral.
* **Role**: The primary habitable nexus, mining colony, and salvage depot of the plane.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Gullet Market**: Upper terrace bazaar where merchants trade in Feaststones, carved bone tools, and soul shards.
  * **The Ravenous Warrens**: Subterranean tunnels carved into the canyon where the scavenger clans known as the Clingers mine the "flesh-veins."
  * **The Gorgewalk**: Enormous enchanted rope-and-bone suspension bridges spanning the yawning chasm depths.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Gorgekeepers Militia and the Council of Clingers, locked in uneasy survival pacts.

### 2. [[Plane of Infinite Hunger The Spiral Abyss#2. Fleshspire: The Tower of Endless Appetite|Fleshspire: The Tower of Endless Appetite]]
* **Location**: Southeastern bile basins, rising from an acidic lake of bubbling digestive enzymes.
* **Role**: Living biological megalopolis, temple of consumption, and biomorphic mutation hatchery.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Glutton Cathedral**: The pulsating fleshy pinnacle where the Glutton Priests perform sacrifices to keep the spire from consuming its own denizens.
  * **The Maw Gardens**: Deep pits where parasitic Flesh Vines are cultivated for food, textiles, and organic weapons.
  * **The Everbirth Chambers**: Pulsing bio-vats where the tower continuously spawns mutated Flesh Servitor constructs.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: High Glutton Vorzath and the Priesthood of the Living Flesh.

### 3. [[Plane of Infinite Hunger The Spiral Abyss#3. The Hollow Expanse: The City of Endless Echoes|The Hollow Expanse: The City of Endless Echoes]]
* **Location**: Nomadic; hovering upon anti-gravitational thermals directly above the Endless Maw.
* **Role**: Mobile armada-city of scavengers, void-pirates, and cross-planar smugglers.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Chainspire**: A massive flagship carved from a leviathan's skull, serving as the admiralty center.
  * **The Void Docks**: Outrigger docking cradles where voidships tether together using colossal sinew-cables.
  * **The Hunger’s Market**: Black-market decks trading cursed planar relics, stolen food rations, and trapped void-whispers.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Chainlords, led by Void-Captain Malakor Vance.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Bone-Harbor (The Siphon Outpost)
* **Location**: The northern rim of the Devouring Wastes, overlooking the Bile Canal.
* **Population**: ~1,300 (Ghouls, Tieflings, Planar Scavengers).
* **Description**: A rough, fortified stilt-settlement constructed from massive ribcages of slain abyssal megafauna. It serves as a dredging post where dredgers sift digestive silt for undissolved metal, gold, and magical jewels.
* **Specialty**: Heat-and-acid resistant bone armor, dredging dredges, and concentrated bile vials.
* **Notable Feature — The Marrow Press**: A titanic hydraulic press extracting glowing, nutrient-dense marrow from excavated ancient fossil-roots.

### 2. Last-Crumb (Satiation Haven)
* **Location**: An isolated plateau in the Starving Expanse shielded by an ancient divine barrier.
* **Population**: ~800 (Ascetics, Starving Mortals, Githzerai).
* **Description**: A serene, austere monastery village carved from inert grey granite. The settlement is built around the single known *Well of Satiety* on the plane, which suppresses all biological and psychic hunger within its perimeter.
* **Specialty**: Satiation wafers, meditation beads, and emotional dampening draughts.
* **Notable Feature — The Unbroken Bowl**: A glowing alabaster fountain that fills each dawn with clear, thirst-quenching nectar.

### 3. Gnaw-Ridge
* **Location**: Situated between the Gnashing Plains and the central spiral.
* **Population**: ~1,500 (Gnolls, Orcs, Half-Fiends).
* **Description**: A violent, fortified barbarian encampment built on interlocking basalt teeth. The inhabitants revere the rhythm of the grinding earth, hunting predatory worm-beasts that crawl up through the cracks.
* **Specialty**: Chitinous polearms, trained hunting carnosaur beasts, and dried void-jerky.
* **Notable Feature — The Maw-Drum**: A 40-foot drum skinned with abyssal hide, struck to disrupt the rhythmic snapping of the Gnashing Plains during caravan crossing.

### 4. Bile-Gate Watch
* **Location**: The western narrows where rivers of gastric acid bottleneck through an obsidian gorge.
* **Population**: ~600 (Goblins, Kobolds, Alchemical Hermits).
* **Description**: A network of cliff-dwellings interconnected by brass pipes and acid-sluices. Residents harvest and neutralize volatile digestive fluids for export to alchemists across the multiverse.
* **Specialty**: Vitriol flasks, caustic etching acids, and fire-clay containers.
* **Notable Feature — The Sluice Dams**: Heavy iron gates regulating the flow of acid into the lower spiral.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Maw Eternal
* **Description**: The titanic singularity at the geographic center of the plane—a planet-sized mouth lined with concentric rings of jagged mountain-teeth grinding eternally downward into non-existence.
* **Planar Trait**: Falling into the Maw pulls creatures at 120 feet per round toward the crushing center; creatures take 10d10 bludgeoning and 10d10 acid damage per round upon entering the inner teeth.

### 2. The Devouring Wastes & Gnashing Plains
* **Description**: Hundreds of square leagues of undulating reddish terrain where ground-teeth rhythmically snap together like titanic jaws.
* **Planar Trait**: Traveling across the plains requires saving throw Dexterity (Acrobatics) checks every mile; a failed check catches the traveler in an opening fissure, dealing 4d10 piercing damage and pinning them.

### 3. The Starving Expanse
* **Description**: An ash-and-bone desert where the very atmosphere actively leeches calories, water, and mental resolve from living beings.
* **Planar Trait**: Rations spoil in 1d4 hours. Creatures require double water and food intake, and must make a saving throw Constitution saving throw every 8 hours or suffer 1 level of exhaustion.

### 4. The Hunger Wells
* **Description**: Swirling craters of crimson and pitch-black void energy that siphon life force from adjacent planes to fuel the plane's growth.
* **Planar Trait**: Standing within 60 feet of a Hunger Well requires a Requires strong mental and physical fortitude to withstand.

### 5. The Bone Forests
* **Description**: Groves of calcified, skeletal trees that drain vitality from the ground and cast eerie, shifting shadows.
* **Planar Trait**: Camping within a Bone Forest reduces the healing granted by a long rest by half, but grants darkvision out to 120 feet for the next 24 hours.
