# Map & Geography of the Plane of Secrets (Ocean of Whispers)

![[attachments/plane_of_secrets_map.jpg]]

> _"In the Ocean of Whispers, silence is not the absence of sound, but the terror of what is waiting to be spoken. Guard your mind, traveler, for every unvoiced thought you hold ripples across the dark tides like blood in shark-infested waters."_
> — Journal of Vexiryn the Unbowed

## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Plane of Secrets Ocean of Whispers#1. Whisperhold: The City of Echoes|Whisperhold: The City of Echoes]]
* **Location**: Subterranean deeps beneath the northern abyss, housed within an immense cavern of luminescent crystalline stalactites.
* **Role**: The archival hub, premier espionage sanctuary, and center of planar information brokerages.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Vault of Murmurs**: A colossal obsidian spire covered in shifting glyphs, containing every secret whispered, written, or thought across the multiverse, guarded by the Silent Order.
  * **The Echo Market**: Sprawling underground bazaar trading in rumors, extracted memories, true names, and shadowy oaths.
  * **The Labyrinth of Whispers**: Subterranean basalt tunnels beneath the city humming with the discarded secrets of dead gods.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Silent Order, with trade and intelligence networks dominated by information entities such as [[../../../NPCs/Chimera Veil|Chimera Veil]] and [[../../../NPCs/Master Scrivener Oras|Master Scrivener Oras Crypt-Tongue]].

### 2. [[Plane of Secrets Ocean of Whispers#2. Veilscar: The Shattered Citadel|Veilscar: The Shattered Citadel]]
* **Location**: Suspended directly above the swirling void of the Echoing Fracture.
* **Role**: Floating ruins of the Keepers of Revelation, now a boomtown for shard-seekers, scavengers, and esoteric scholars.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Echoing Fracture**: A central gaping tear in reality that radiates chaotic acoustic waves and whispers forbidden half-truths.
  * **The Fractured Archives**: Floating library modules and torn pages drifting across the void, defended by semi-sentient glyph-constructs.
  * **The Radiant Bridges**: Ethereal, shimmering walkways of pure thought connecting floating stone platforms.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: Anarchic council of Shard Seekers and Chronomantic Scavengers, loosely coordinated by [[../../../NPCs/Archivist Selene Pale|Archivist Selene Pale]].

### 3. [[Plane of Secrets Ocean of Whispers#3. Umbralith: The Shadowed City|Umbralith: The Shadowed City]]
* **Location**: The southern jagged basalt archipelago, built upon obsidian promontories jutting into the ink-black sea.
* **Role**: Autocratic citadel of conspiracy, shadow-crafting, and covert assassinations.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Veiled Spire**: A sky-piercing black tower shrouded in living darkness, seat of the ruling conspirators.
  * **The Cloaked Bazaar**: A soundless market conducted under total anonymity through hand-signs and mind-whispers.
  * **The Living Shadow Trenches**: Moats and defensive wards composed of semi-sentient, predatory shadow-matter.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The mysterious **Cabal of Veils**, an oligarchy of masked shadowcallers and faceless manipulators.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Still-Water Anchorage
* **Location**: A sheltered cove nestled among the western Obsidian Reefs.
* **Population**: ~1,100 (Tritons, Shadar-kai, Planar Sailors).
* **Description**: A harbor town built upon petrified black coral where sea-farers and astral void-skiffs dock to insulate their vessels before crossing the Ocean of Whispers.
* **Specialty**: Sound-dampened hull caulking, lead-lined sea charts, and void-diving diving bells.
* **Notable Feature — The Muffled Bell**: A massive sunken silver bell that chimes in reverse, absorbing all acoustic vibrations within a 3-mile radius.

### 2. Glyph-Haven (The Riddle Post)
* **Location**: The rocky base of the southern Shrouded Spires.
* **Population**: ~850 (Gnomes, Kenku, Cryptographers).
* **Description**: A vertical cliff-side hamlet of cantilevered timber shacks and rope bridges built directly into the rune-etched surfaces of the spires.
* **Specialty**: Decryption lenses, cipher-breaking quills, and enchanted rubbings of primordial glyphs.
* **Notable Feature — The Resonant Chimes**: Arrays of hollow glass chimes that ring whenever someone nearby speaks a deliberate falsehood.

### 3. Truth-Bloom Hollow
* **Location**: Concealed within the outer thickets of the Hidden Gardens.
* **Population**: ~650 (Firbolgs, Elves, Reformed Spies).
* **Description**: A tranquil, camouflaged commune living in symbiosis with bioluminescent truthblooms. Inhabitants live under strict vows of absolute honesty to prevent the surrounding thorn-vines from constricting their homes.
* **Specialty**: Truth-serums, calming floral teas, and memory-restoring nectar.
* **Notable Feature — The Pollen Font**: A natural basin of glowing floral sap that reveals the true alignment of any creature whose blood touches the surface.

### 4. Shroud-End (The Paranoia Outpost)
* **Location**: Bordering the abyssal drop into the Abyss of Whispers.
* **Population**: ~920 (Amnesiacs, Exiled Inquisitors, Githzerai).
* **Description**: A fortified perimeter of iron watchtowers and silence-fields. Sentinels here monitor the expansion of the deep psychic vortex to prevent entities of unmaking from climbing up.
* **Specialty**: Thought-damping helmets, sanity-anchoring charms, and anti-telepathic wards.
* **Notable Feature — The Null Beacon**: A crystal lighthouse projecting a beam of pure silence that mutes all psychic chatter in its sweep.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Whispering Labyrinth
* **Description**: A concentric, sea-level maze of floating black stone walls and mirrored waterways situated between Whisperhold and the outer ocean.
* **Planar Trait**: Travelers sailing through the labyrinth must succeed on a saving throw Wisdom saving throw every hour or be lured off course by illusory voices echoing their most shameful regrets.

### 2. The Shrouded Spires
* **Description**: Colossal obelisks of etched black glass piercing the swirling purple sky, etched with the forgotten creation codes of the Loom.
* **Planar Trait**: Climbing or flying near the spires exposes creatures to telepathic friction. Anyone casting divination spells within 1 mile gains true sight for 1 hour but takes 2d8 psychic damage.

### 3. The Abyss of Whispers
* **Description**: A bottomless oceanic whirlpool where the collective unvoiced thoughts of dead gods circulate in a crushing gyre.
* **Planar Trait**: Falling into the vortex deals 6d10 cold damage and 6d10 psychic damage per round; creatures reduced to 0 hit points have their memories permanently erased into the planar weave.

### 4. The Hidden Gardens
* **Description**: An ethereal bioluminescent floral sanctuary where emotional truths bloom into radiant petals and deceit feeds thorny, strangling briars.
* **Planar Trait**: Any creature that speaks a lie within the garden takes 2d6 piercing damage as thorny vines lash out; speaking an agonizing truth heals the speaker for 4d8 hit points.

### 5. The Stifled Vaults (Black Market)
* **Description**: Subterranean catacombs hidden beneath the labyrinth, operating beyond the reach of the Silent Order.
* **Planar Trait**: Absolute sound suppression wards cover the sector. All verbal spell components can be performed with silent somatic gestures, but deceitful contract negotiations trigger lethal glyphs of warding.
