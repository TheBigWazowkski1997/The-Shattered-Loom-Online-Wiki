# Map & Geography of the Plane of Avarice (The Gilded Eternity)

![[attachments/plane_of_avarice_map.jpg]]

> _"In the Gilded Eternity, every grain of sand is a crushed emerald, every river runs with liquid gold, and every breath you draw incurs an unpayable interest debt. Do not reach for the glittering coin, mortal—it is reaching for you."_
> — Journal of Vexiryn the Unbowed

## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Plane of Avarice The Gilded Eternity#1. Aurous Grasp: The City of Golden Chains|Aurous Grasp: The City of Golden Chains]]
* **Location**: Central molten sea, suspended directly above the deepest boiling vortex of liquid gold.
* **Role**: The financial capital, diplomatic nexus, and seat of the ruling Council of Chains.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Chains of Dominion**: Colossal golden anchor-chains tethering the floating city platforms above the lava-like molten gold.
  * **The Gilded Bazaar**: Sprawling market where magical relics, indenture contracts, and soul-bonds are traded.
  * **The Molten Expanse**: A churn of sentient liquid gold rumored to be the dissolved flesh of a betrayed god of wealth.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Council of Chains, alongside chief bullion-merchants like [[../../../NPCs/Gildmaster Vane Karr|Gildmaster Vane Karr]].

### 2. [[Plane of Avarice The Gilded Eternity#2. Emerald Maw: The Devouring City|Emerald Maw: The Devouring City]]
* **Location**: Nomadic; prowling the boundless dunes of the Gilded Wastes.
* **Role**: Mobile predatory fortress, military powerhouse, and plunder repository.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Devouring Core**: The pulsing, sickly green elemental furnace that consumes treasure and life force to drive the city's titanic walking legs.
  * **The Vaultspire**: A jagged citadel of emerald stone and gold where the dragon tyrant hoards his personal spoils.
  * **The Maw Gates**: Crushing portcullises that grind plundered caravans directly into the city's internal smelters.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The planar dragon tyrant [[../../../NPCs/Vorath Greedeater|Vorath Greedeater]], enforced by Warmaster Grash and the Plunderborn Raiders.

### 3. [[Plane of Avarice The Gilded Eternity#3. Argentum Abyss: The City Beneath the Hoard|Argentum Abyss: The City Beneath the Hoard]]
* **Location**: Subterranean deeps, excavated directly beneath the colossal mountain of treasure known as the Hoardspire.
* **Role**: Underground banking fortress, secret relic vaults, and illegal silver mining.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Vault of Ages**: Ancient adamantine chamber holding the oldest relics of dead civilizations, guarded by spectral Treasure Wraiths.
  * **The Crushing Ceiling**: Millions of tons of loose gold coin and treasure pressing down upon reinforced iron struts.
  * **The Labyrinthine Market**: A subterranean bazaar of cutpurses, scavengers, and illegal bullion-melters.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Argentum Syndicate, overseen by Mintmaster Orin Sterling and corrupt auditors.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Pyrite Shallows (Fool's Harbor)
* **Location**: On the eastern shores of the Molten Expanse, where rivers of molten gold cool into slag beaches.
* **Population**: ~1,600 (Goblins, Kobolds, Desperate Prospectors).
* **Description**: A ramshackle stilt-town built from charred ironwood and slag bricks. Prospectors use heavy drag-buckets to scoop cooling gold scum from the boiling shallows, gambling that the nuggets aren't fool's gold (pyrite).
* **Specialty**: Unrefined gold dust, heat-resistant dipping buckets, and slag-skimming dredges.
* **Notable Feature — The Quench Basin**: A bay where the molten river hits subterranean ice-water geysers, creating clouds of scalding, sulfuric steam.

### 2. The Sieve (Gilded Sifting Sluices)
* **Location**: Southern dune valley bordering the Gilded Wastes.
* **Population**: ~2,100 (Indenture Slaves, Dwarves, Tieflings).
* **Description**: A multi-tiered industrial refinery where massive mechanized sieves filter tons of windblown golden sand each hour to extract crushed rubies, sapphires, and ancient coins.
* **Specialty**: Gemstone sorting, pneumatic dust vacuums, and water-ration tokens.
* **Notable Feature — The Great Sluice Wheel**: An 80-foot water wheel powered by pumped mineral oil that drives hundreds of vibrating bronze sifters.

### 3. Coin-Fall Watch
* **Location**: Perched on a jagged basalt needle beneath the overhanging lip of the Hoardspire.
* **Population**: ~950 (Aarakocra, Kenku, Wind Scavengers).
* **Description**: A cliffside settlement built from reinforced iron awnings and chain nets. Inhabitants make their living catching the showers of loose coins that periodically spill from the overflowing vaults of the Hoardspire above.
* **Specialty**: Glider wings, magnetic salvage nets, and aerial maneuvering harnesses.
* **Notable Feature — The Catching Spire**: A massive inverted brass cone funneling falling coins directly into central collection cisterns.

### 4. Scintilla (The Opal Outpost)
* **Location**: North of the Glimmering Mire, surrounded by pools of bubbling liquefied gemstones.
* **Population**: ~1,100 (Elves, Gnomes, Gem-Artificers).
* **Description**: Built on floating pontoons of buoyant pumice stone. The village harvests rare crystallized fluids that condense from the hot vapors of the mire.
* **Specialty**: Opal lenses, liquid gemstone potions, and petrification salves.
* **Notable Feature — The Condenser Towers**: Tall glass minarets that condense iridescent mineral steam into glowing elixirs.

### 5. Debt-End
* **Location**: A desolate rock outcrop in the outer Gilded Wastes, just out of reach of the Council of Chains' patrols.
* **Population**: ~1,400 (Escaped Debt-Slaves, Smugglers, Planar Outlaws).
* **Description**: A fortified subterranean shantytown hidden beneath a canopy of camouflaged sandcloth. Here, individuals who have defaulted on contracts with the Council of Chains hide, plotting ways to escape to other planes.
* **Specialty**: False identity papers, contract-erasing acids, and planar portal guides.
* **Notable Feature — The Liberty Bell**: A cracked iron bell struck only when a slave successfully escapes the plane.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Hoardspire & The Throne of Greed
* **Description**: A towering mountain of solid treasure—coins, artifacts, crowns, and statues—rising over 10,000 feet into the golden haze.
* **Planar Trait**: The sheer psychic mass of avarice within the Hoardspire forces creatures within 1 mile to make a saving throw Wisdom saving throw every 24 hours or become afflicted with *Avaricious Madness* (compelled to guard whatever coins they carry with lethal paranoia).

### 2. The Molten Expanse
* **Description**: An endless sea of roiling liquid gold heated to incandescent temperatures.
* **Planar Trait**: Any non-enchanted matter dropped into the sea dissolves in seconds; creatures immersed take 10d10 fire damage per round and must make a Requires strong mental and physical fortitude to withstand.

### 3. The Gilded Wastes & Mirage Fortunes
* **Description**: A desert of crushed gemstones and powdered gold dust that shifts with supernatural winds.
* **Planar Trait**: Travelers who lack magical navigation fall prey to *Mirage Fortunes*, hallucinating glittering palatial cities that lure them miles off course into predatory mimic dens.

### 4. The Glimmering Mire
* **Description**: A treacherous swamp composed of molten gold quagmires and quicksands of liquefied gems.
* **Planar Trait**: Stepping into the mire requires saving throw Athletics checks; sinking causes both fire damage and restraint, and slain victims rise as Treasure Golems.

### 5. The Obsidian Bazaar
* **Description**: A neutral, shadowy marketplace of black glass pavilions floating above a dormant gold caldera.
* **Planar Trait**: The Black Auction operates under an absolute planar geas: any deal struck in blood or true name cannot be broken by mortal or divine means without disintegrating the transgressor.

### 6. The Chains of Dominion
* **Description**: Colossal forged links of *Eternal Gold* anchoring Aurous Grasp to the bedrock beneath the molten sea.
* **Planar Trait**: The chains are immune to nonmagical damage and regenerate 50 HP per turn; each chain is attuned to a member of the Council of Chains.
