## D&D 5e Adaptation & Canon Map References

> [!NOTE]
> **Adapted from D&D 5e Canon**:
> * **The Elemental Plane of Earth**: Adapted directly from official D&D 5e cosmology (*Dungeon Master's Guide*, pp. 53–55, *Manual of the Planes*, and *Planescape*).
> * **Canon Map Reference**: The classic planar geography of Earth—featuring the **Great Dismal Vale** (the Sevenfold Mazework of the Dao), the **City of Jewels**, and the boundary zones bordering Magma and Ooze—informs the deep geological structure of this expanse.
> * **Loom Integration**: In the Shattered Loom, the planar foundation is guarded by **Terridon, the Unyielding Pillar**, whose granite bones anchor the leylines that prevent the elemental spheres from collapsing into the Astral Void.

## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Cities of The Plane of Earth/Gemspire Cradle|Gemspire Citadel: The Geode Metropolis]]
* **Location**: North-central cavern vault, carved within an enormous hollow amethyst-and-diamond geode.
* **Role**: Capital of gemcraft, lapidary enchanting, banking, and planar trade with the Dao.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Prismatic Geode**: The interior dome of the city, lined with thousands of colossal amethyst crystals that emit a warm, steady golden-violet bioluminescence.
  * **The Treasury of Facets**: Deep vaults where uncut soul-gems and raw planar diamonds are stored under petrification wards.
  * **The Shimmering Promenade**: Terraced avenues carved from polished agate and rose quartz, bustling with jewelers and planar emissaries.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Lapidary Guild and Dao trade envoys, presided over by High Jeweler Balthazar Stonevein.

### 2. [[Cities of The Plane of Earth/Obsidian Cradle|Obsidian Cradle: The Volcanic Bastion]]
* **Location**: Northeastern thermal sector, constructed within a massive hollow volcanic chimney over flowing magma rivers.
* **Role**: Fortress-foundry of volcanic smithing, divine weaponsmithing, and Covenant garrison.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Black Citadel**: A colossal fortress carved from seamless black volcanic glass, virtually impervious to physical and elemental damage.
  * **The Crucible Flues**: Thermal chimneys channeling superheated magma to power titanic drop-hammers and adamantine smelters.
  * **The Hearth of Terridon**: A sacred altar where pilgrims offer worked stone and consecrated ingots to the god of endurance.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: Forge-Commandant Grothgar Ironjaw and the Council of Stonekeepers.

### 3. [[Cities of The Plane of Earth/The Deepmaw Quarry|The Deepmaw Quarry: The Chasm Hold]]
* **Location**: Southeastern sector, clinging to the vertical terraces of an infinite mineral chasm.
* **Role**: Massive industrial excavation hub, slave-freeing haven, and base of the Shadowforged Guild.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Great Winches**: Titanic mechanical cranes and cable lifts capable of hauling hundred-ton blocks of adamantine ore from the abyss.
  * **The Shadowforged Enclave**: A fortified autonomous quarter where renegade artificers craft siege equipment.
  * **The Chasm Lip**: Cantilevered stone docks extending out over the bottomless void where ore-skiffs load mineral payloads.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: Guildmaster Valen Cragwarden and the Free Miners Syndicate.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Basalt-Fold
* **Location**: In the Great Dismal Vale midway between Gemspire and Obsidian Cradle.
* **Population**: ~1,800 (Dwarves, Earth Genasi, Azer).
* **Description**: A multi-level town carved into geometric hexagonal basalt columns resembling a giant honeycomb.
* **Specialty**: Structural stone masonry, foundation keystones, and thermal heating bricks.
* **Notable Feature — The Resonant Columns**: Hexagonal pillars that hum with harmonic vibrations when subterranean tremors approach.

### 2. Xorn-Hollow
* **Location**: Northern fungal tunnels near the border of the Shattered Caverns.
* **Population**: ~950 (Xorn, Pech, Hermit Prospectors).
* **Description**: A strange, quiet subterranean settlement built around natural mineral deposits. Inhabitants trade precious gems for forged tools, living in peace with native xorn clans.
* **Specialty**: Ore-scenting divination, tunneling grease, and raw geodes.
* **Notable Feature — The Feeding Basin**: A stone trough filled daily with copper and silver ore to placate wild earth elementals.

### 3. Granite-Watch
* **Location**: Perched at the entrance to the Shattered Caverns labyrinth.
* **Population**: ~1,100 (Goliaths, Deep Gnomes, Stone Sorcerers).
* **Description**: A heavily fortified gatehouse town designed to seal the civilized vaults against aberrant burrowers and umber hulk incursions.
* **Specialty**: Heavy burrowing drills, tremorsense goggles, and seismic warding rods.
* **Notable Feature — The Stone Gate**: A single 80-foot slab of solid granite operated by counter-weight pulleys.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Shattered Caverns Labyrinth
* **Description**: A vast maze of shifting tunnels and natural limestone caves that slowly re-carve themselves over centuries.
* **Planar Trait**: Navigating without magical tremorsense or earth glide requires saving throw Survival checks every 4 hours; failure causes parties to walk into dead ends or shifting rock collapses.

### 2. The Crystal Mountains
* **Description**: Subterranean mountain ranges composed entirely of solid selenite, fluorite, and quartz projecting from the cavern floors to the ceilings.
* **Planar Trait**: Spells of the Abjuration and Transmutation schools cast within 1 mile have their durations doubled as the crystals resonate with magical matrixes.

### 3. The Magma Veins
* **Description**: Rivers of molten rock coursing through black stone canyons, providing the primary light and heat for deep settlements.
* **Planar Trait**: Approaching within 30 feet requires saving throw Constitution saving throws against extreme heat; immersion causes 10d10 fire damage per round.

### 4. The Great Dismal Vale (The Sevenfold Maze)
* **Description**: A sprawling canyon complex traditionally claimed by the Dao Khanates, filled with opulent marble pavilions and mineral slave-works.
* **Planar Trait**: Dao magic permeates the stone; spells that alter stone (*Stone Shape*, *Move Earth*) trigger Dao divination sensors.
