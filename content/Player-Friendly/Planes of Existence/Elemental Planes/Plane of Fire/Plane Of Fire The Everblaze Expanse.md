The Plane of Fire is a realm of unrelenting heat, where oceans of lava, rivers of molten metal, and infernos dominate the landscape. It is a land of creation and destruction, constantly burning yet alive with energy.

### **Geography and Features**

1. **Lava Fields**: Endless plains of molten rock that shift and harden unpredictably, creating jagged formations.
2. **Pyre Mountains**: Massive, volcano-like peaks that constantly erupt with ash and fire.
3. **The Embersea**: A vast ocean of liquid flame, dotted with floating islands of volcanic rock.

### **Key Locations**

* **The Furnace City of Cinderhold**:
	
	A fortress-city of basalt and obsidian, where Ignivor’s Emberforged disciples craft legendary weapons. The heat here is so intense that even native fire elementals tread carefully.
	
* **The Blazing Nexus**:
	
	A massive storm of fire that acts as a gateway to other planes, controlled by ancient fire djinn.
	
* **The Eternal Pyre**:
	
	A sacred flame said to hold the original spark of creation, worshipped by all beings of fire.
	

### **Unique Characteristics**

* **Infinite Light**: There is no true darkness in the Plane of Fire; everything glows with an unending orange and red hue.
* **Burning Magic**: Spells that deal fire damage are amplified, but water-based magic is significantly weakened.

### **Inhabitants**

* **Fire Elementals**: Living flames that act as protectors of the plane.
* **Efreeti**: Powerful fire genies who rule floating citadels above the Embersea.
* **Salamanders**: Serpentine creatures with molten skin who serve as builders and soldiers.

# Cities

* **1\. Cinderhold**
* **Description**:Perched atop a massive basalt plateau surrounded by rivers of molten lava, Cinderhold is a fortress-city that serves as a seat of power for Ignivor’s Emberforged disciples. Its walls glow faintly from the heat, and its forges operate continuously, filling the air with smoke and sparks.
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Pyreforge**: A massive forge fueled by an eternal flame gifted by Ignivor. Artisans and smiths create weapons and artifacts here, with Emberforged overseers ensuring their quality.
	* **Lava Bridges**: Natural stone spans that connect the city to nearby lava flows, patrolled by fire elementals and magmin.
	* **The Eternal Crucible**: A gladiatorial arena where warriors prove their worth in combat, with the blessing of Ignivor.
	

	

#### **2\. Blazeward Keep**

	**Description**:A defensive city constructed of enchanted obsidian, Blazeward Keep stands as a sanctuary for those seeking refuge from the Elemental Plane’s raging infernos. Its walls are reinforced with heat-resistant wards, and its citizens craft intricate fireglass artifacts.
	
* **Key Features**:
	* **Wardspire Tower**: A towering structure that generates protective wards shielding the city from extreme heat and firestorms.
	* **Flameglass Foundry**: A workshop where artisans create delicate, glowing fireglass sculptures and magical lenses.
		**Ember Archives**: A repository of ancient elemental lore guarded by salamanders and Azer scholars.
		
	

	

#### **3\. The Molten Reach**

	**Description**:A nomadic city of barges and floating platforms, The Molten Reach traverses the great lava seas of the Plane of Fire. It is home to traders, smugglers, and those who prefer a life of freedom amidst the chaos.
	
* **Key Features**:
	* **Lavaflow Bazaar**: A bustling marketplace where rare fire-aligned goods, creatures, and artifacts are traded.
	* **Fireweaver Guild**: An organization of mages and enchanters who specialize in binding fire elementals to items.
	* **The Emberfleet**: A fleet of fireproof ships and floating barges that serve as mobile homes for the city’s inhabitants.
