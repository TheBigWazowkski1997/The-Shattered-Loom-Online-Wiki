## D&D 5e Adaptation & Canon Map References

> [!NOTE]
> **Adapted from D&D 5e Canon**:
> * **The City of Brass & Sea of Fire**: Adapted directly from official D&D 5e cosmology (*Dungeon Master's Guide*, pp. 53–55, *Manual of the Planes*, and *Planescape*).
> * **Canon Map Reference**: The official planar layout of **The City of Brass** (including the Charcoal Palace, the Great Bazaar, the Foundry Spires, and the harbor of the Sea of Fire) functions as the canonical street-level cartography for the metropolis.
> * **Loom Integration**: In the Shattered Loom, the Plane of Fire's primal ignition is tied to **Ignivor, the Infernal Forge**, with the **Emberforged** serving as divine smiths forging stabilizing anchors against the cosmic unraveling.

## The Four Great Metropolises & Citadels

### 1. [[Cities of the Plane of Fire/The City of Brass|The City of Brass: Jewel of the Sea of Fire]]
* **Location**: Floating upon a colossal brass hemisphere dozens of miles across in the center of the Sea of Fire.
* **Role**: The supreme imperial capital of the Inner Planes, nexus of interplanar commerce, slave markets, and wish-covenants.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Charcoal Palace**: Imperial basalt stronghold of the Grand Sultan of the Efreet, surrounded by moats of liquid sunlight.
  * **The Pyrefield Bazaar**: The largest open marketplace in the multiverse, trading in elemental cores, fire-forged relics, and planar slaves.
  * **The Foundry Spires**: Magma-driven foundries operated by free Azer smiths and enslaved salamander metallurgists.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The **Grand Sultan of the Efreet** and the Court of Embers, enforced by the Brass Janissaries.

### 2. [[Cities of the Plane of Fire/Cinderhold|Cinderhold: Fortress of the Emberforged]]
* **Location**: Perched atop a titanic basalt plateau surrounded by the Ash Wastes and cascading lava rivers in the northeast.
* **Role**: Military stronghold, monastic bastion, and divine forge complex dedicated to Ignivor.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Pyreforge**: An immense caldera forge fueled by an eternal spark gifted by Ignivor.
  * **The Eternal Crucible**: A volcanic arena where warriors battle for divine blessings.
  * **Lava Bridges**: Natural arched basalt spans defended by Magmin and fire elementals.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The High Overseers of the Emberforged.

### 3. [[Cities of the Plane of Fire/Blazeward Keep|Blazeward Keep: Sanctuary of Obsidian]]
* **Location**: Northwestern jagged volcanic range, protected by permanent shimmering abjuration wards.
* **Role**: Haven for non-fire natives, mortal embassies, and fireglass artisans.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **Wardspire Tower**: A crystalline obelisk radiating thermal-damping wards that reduce environmental heat to survivable mortal levels.
  * **Flameglass Foundry**: High-end workshops blowing incandescent elemental fireglass into optics and vials.
  * **The Ember Archives**: A subterranean vault preserving fire elemental treaties and ancient Loom records.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Council of Wardwardens and Azer scholar-masters.

### 4. [[../../The Material Plane/Cities of the Material Plane/The Molten Reach|The Molten Reach: The Wandering Flotilla]]
* **Location**: Mobile; navigating the churning currents of the Sea of Fire (Cinder Sea).
* **Role**: Nomadic barge city of smugglers, free traders, and independent privateers.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Lavaflow Bazaar**: Connected flotilla decks trading smuggled planar contraband.
  * **The Fireweaver Guild**: Arcane circle binding lesser fire elementals to ship keels for propulsion.
  * **The Flagship Obsidian**: A massive heat-shielded volcanic stone galleon serving as the town hall.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: Admiral Vaelen Burn-Tide and the Captains' Guild.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Kezzar (The Azer Redoubt)
* **Location**: An obsidian needle rising from the southern shallows of the Sea of Fire.
* **Population**: ~2,200 (Azer, Fire Dwarves).
* **Description**: A bronze-domed fortress town of free azer craftsmen who refuse fealty to the Efreeti Sultan.
* **Specialty**: Flame-tempered adamantine weapons, heat-absorbing shields, and crucible ceramics.
* **Notable Feature — The Hearth-Furnace**: An open elemental flame maintained continuously for three millennia.

### 2. Ash-Hollow
* **Location**: In the lee of the Pyre Mountains within the northern Ash Wastes.
* **Population**: ~1,400 (Salamanders, Kobolds, Fire Genasi).
* **Description**: A subterranean network of lava tubes and ash-burrows where prospectors mine for volcanic sulfur and brimstone.
* **Specialty**: Pure brimstone, fire-resistant leather, and salamander spears.
* **Notable Feature — The Ash-Geysers**: Rhythmic vents blowing mineral-rich cinder clouds across the valley.

### 3. Emberspire
* **Location**: A towering basalt finger situated between Cinderhold and the Blazing Nexus.
* **Population**: ~850 (Sorcerers, Monks of the Way of the Four Elements, Efreet Exiles).
* **Description**: A vertical monastery carved with thousands of meditation niches open to the scorching planar thermals.
* **Specialty**: Pyromancy spell-scrolls, fire-attunement elixirs, and heat-endurance meditation.
* **Notable Feature — The Spire's Eye**: An open balcony at the apex where storm fire-elementals gather.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Sea of Fire (Cinder Sea)
* **Description**: An endless ocean of white-hot liquid magma and boiling brass waves stretching beyond the horizon.
* **Planar Trait**: Direct immersion is lethal to mortals without powerful elemental shielding. Water-based spells of 3rd level or lower evaporate instantly without effect.

### 2. The Blazing Nexus
* **Description**: A colossal cyclonic firestorm acting as an open interplanar hurricane connecting to the Material Plane, the Plane of Avarice, and the Abyss.
* **Planar Trait**: Flight through the Nexus requires skilled maneuvering to avoid lethal debris and elemental turbulence.

### 3. The Eternal Pyre
* **Description**: A sacred volcanic caldera in the southern reaches holding the primordial spark of the first flame.
* **Planar Trait**: The primal cosmic heat here overcomes ordinary resistance and magical shields.

### 4. The Pyre Mountains
* **Description**: A colossal range of active stratovolcanoes that continuously spew rivers of molten copper and basalt.
* **Planar Trait**: Violent planar eruptions regularly rain pyroclastic debris and volcanic ash across the region.
