**Tidehaven** is a sprawling, semi-aquatic city built atop colossal, drifting coral reefs and shimmering aquatic platforms. Unlike the enclosed sanctuary of **Pearldome**, Tidehaven is an open, living city that flows with the currents of the **Plane of Water**, constantly shifting and adapting to its surroundings. It is a bustling hub of trade, diplomacy, and piracy, where various aquatic races gather under a tenuous balance of cooperation and competition. Tidehaven is both a sanctuary and a danger—a place of opportunity for the bold and peril for the unwary.

---

## **Overview**

* **Government**: Tidehaven is ruled by the **Council of Waves**, a loose coalition of influential leaders from different factions, including merchants, pirates, scholars, and warriors. The balance of power is ever-shifting, reflecting the mercurial nature of the Plane of Water.
* **Exports**: Rare aquatic spices, enchanted seaweed, living coral constructs, and sea treasure salvaged from the depths.
* **Imports**: Terrestrial metals, magical artifacts, exotic goods, and mercenaries.

---

## **Geography and Layout**

Tidehaven consists of multiple interconnected platforms, coral isles, and floating structures. These drift across the **Infinite Ocean** of the Plane of Water, tethered together by enchanted chains of living coral and bioluminescent kelp. The city rises and falls with the ocean currents, giving it a sense of perpetual motion.
The city is divided into **five major districts**, each with its own unique character:

---

### **1\. The Saltspire Anchorage (Central District)**

* **Description**: The heart of Tidehaven, the Saltspire Anchorage is a massive floating platform fashioned from enchanted coral and driftwood. It serves as the city’s administrative and trade center, bustling with markets, taverns, and guild halls. The Anchorage is where travelers first dock their ships and where most of the city’s business is conducted.
* **Architecture**: Open-air structures of woven kelp, shell-encrusted timber, and coral, all illuminated by glowing jellyfish lanterns that float above the streets.
* **Unique Features**:
	* **The Coral Hall of Waves**: A grand, spiraling coral structure where the **Council of Waves** meets to govern the city. Its chambers echo with the sound of waves, making debates both dramatic and chaotic.
	* **The Drifting Bazaar**: A constantly shifting market where merchants trade exotic goods from across the planes. Sea treasures, magical trinkets, and enchanted aquatic mounts are common wares.
	* **The Siren’s Respite**: The largest and most famous tavern in Tidehaven, built into a hollowed-out leviathan skull. It is a haven for sailors, pirates, and adventurers looking to share stories and form alliances.

---

### **2\. The Coral Canopy (Residential District)**

* **Description**: A sprawling network of floating coral atolls and aquatic homes suspended on magical platforms of water. This is where Tidehaven’s citizens live, forming a vibrant community above and below the waves.
* **Architecture**: Residences range from woven kelp huts to elaborate coral towers, many of which extend into the water for aquatic inhabitants. Schools of brightly colored fish often swim between the homes, adding to the district’s natural beauty.
* **Unique Features**:
	* **The Living Reef**: A massive coral formation in the center of the district that grows and shifts over time, housing both aquatic creatures and citizens.
	* **The Flowing Gardens**: A network of floating gardens where seaweed, kelp, and aquatic crops are cultivated. These gardens are tended by **locathah** farmers who ensure the city’s food supply.
	* **The Tidepools**: Shallow pools scattered across the district where children play and locals gather for community events.

---

### **3\. The Leviathan’s Mooring (Pirate District)**

* **Description**: This raucous and dangerous district is home to Tidehaven’s pirates, mercenaries, and smugglers. While technically under the Council’s jurisdiction, the Mooring operates under its own loose set of rules, enforced by pirate captains and mercenary gangs.
* **Architecture**: Ramshackle buildings made from salvaged ship parts, driftwood, and scavenged materials. The district’s docks are littered with sunken shipwrecks and jury-rigged vessels.
* **Unique Features**:
	* **The Kraken’s Hold**: A notorious black market housed in the wreck of an ancient pirate ship. Anything can be bought here, from cursed treasures to stolen magical artifacts.
	* **The Scalding Cup**: A rowdy tavern and fighting pit where disputes are often settled through combat. Its arena is filled with shifting currents that challenge even the strongest fighters.
	* **The Brineforge Shipyard**: A chaotic shipyard where Tidehaven’s most daring shipwrights construct and repair vessels, including enchanted submersibles and leviathan-drawn barges.

---

### **4\. The Tidal Spire (Magical and Religious District)**

* **Description**: Rising above the waves, the Tidal Spire is a towering, crystalline structure that serves as both a magical academy and a place of worship. It is a focal point of Tidehaven’s spiritual and arcane power, attracting scholars, mages, and priests from across the planes.
* **Architecture**: The Spire is made of enchanted, translucent crystal that refracts light into shimmering rainbows. Water flows constantly through its walls, creating a serene and otherworldly ambiance.
* **Unique Features**:
	* **The Shrine of the Eternal Current**: A temple dedicated to the Elemental Lords of Water, especially **Hydrion**, the Primordial Lord of Tides. Priests perform daily rituals to bless the city’s waters.
	* **The Aquamancer’s Guild**: A prestigious academy where mages study the manipulation of water and the art of crafting spells tied to aquatic magic.
	* **The Mirror Pools**: A series of enchanted pools that act as portals to distant locations in the Plane of Water. These are heavily guarded by the Council of Waves.

---

### **5\. The Abyssal Warrens (Subterranean Depths)**

* **Description**: Beneath the surface lies the Abyssal Warrens, a labyrinth of underwater caverns and tunnels that form the foundation of Tidehaven. These depths are both a lifeline and a threat to the city, harboring rare resources and dangerous creatures.
* **Architecture**: Natural rock formations and coral growths dominate the Warrens, with carved tunnels reinforced by magical wards to prevent collapse.
* **Unique Features**:
	* **The Glowtide Mines**: Caverns rich with **luminite crystals** and **abyssal pearls**, harvested by brave miners and guarded against predatory sea creatures.
	* **The Abyssal Gate**: A mysterious, ancient portal deep within the Warrens that is rumored to connect to the **Far Realm**. It is sealed with powerful magic, but the seal is weakening.
	* **The Leviathan Caves**: Enormous caverns where aquatic titans such as krakens and leviathans occasionally dwell. The creatures are both revered and feared by the people of Tidehaven.

## **Key Figures**

### **1\. Captain Lysara Driftwave**

* **Role**: Pirate Queen and Council Member of Tidehaven
* **Appearance**: A charismatic water genasi with sea-green skin, a flowing cloak of enchanted kelp, and a rapier that glows faintly with bioluminescence.
* **Personality**: Bold, cunning, and fiercely loyal to Tidehaven, though her methods are often ruthless.

---

### **2\. High Priestess Nylaris Waveborn**

* **Role**: Spiritual Leader of the Tidal Spire and Council Member
* **Appearance**: A serene merfolk with iridescent scales and a voice that carries a hypnotic cadence. She wields a staff made of living coral.
* **Personality**: Wise and compassionate, but willing to make difficult decisions to protect Tidehaven.

---

### **3\. Master Arcanist Tolaris Shellsong**

* **Role**: Head of the Aquamancer’s Guild and Council Member
* **Appearance**: A triton mage with shimmering, crystalline armor and tattoos of shifting currents.
* **Personality**: Brilliant but arrogant, Tolaris is obsessed with unlocking the secrets of the Abyssal Gate.

---

## **Factions and Politics**

1. **The Council of Waves**: A fragile coalition of Tidehaven’s most powerful leaders, constantly shifting in power and influence.
2. **The Deepcurrent Syndicate**: A smuggling network that operates in the shadows, wielding significant influence in the Leviathan’s Mooring.
3. **The Abyssal Watchers**: A secretive group of warriors and scholars dedicated to monitoring and containing the **Abyssal Gate**.

## **Shops of Tidehaven**

### **1. The Gilded Barnacle**

**Specialty**: Salvaged Nautical Relics, Diving Apparatus, and Aquatic Mount Gear  
**Location**: The Saltspire Anchorage, anchored to an outer coral jetty.  
**Owner**: **Varek Barnacle-Back**, an eccentric, ancient crustacean-genasi whose carapace is encrusted with living sea anemones and pearls.  
**Description**: The shop is a half-submerged barge lashed to giant coral pillars. Above the waterline, brass salvage arms and pressurized diving bells hang from pulleys; below the waterline, glass tanks display glowing tridents, enchanted buoyancy bladders, and polished mother-of-pearl plate armor. The smell of brine, whale oil, and wet kelp permeates the air.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Cloak of the Manta Ray** (*Uncommon*, 500 gp): As per the DMG. Grants 60 ft. swim speed and water breathing.
* **Cap of Water Breathing** (*Uncommon*, 450 gp): As per the DMG. Allows wearer to breathe underwater while submerged.
* **Trident of Fish Command** (*Uncommon*, 800 gp): As per the DMG. Can dominate beasts with an innate swimming speed.
* **Mariner's Armor (+1 Scale Mail)** (*Rare*, 1,500 gp): Grants swimming speed equal to walking speed and floats the wearer to the surface if knocked unconscious in water.
* **Apparatus of the Crab** (*Legendary*, 12,000 gp): A sealed, claw-driven mechanical submersible kept in a pressurized deep bay behind the shop.

**Unique Services**:

* **Pressure Enchanting**: Varek can treat mundane weapons and armor with abyssal resin, rendering them immune to water resistance penalties and corrosion (150 gp).
* **Salvage Appraisals**: Evaluates recovered shipwrecks and ancient aquatic relics for a 10% appraisal cut.

**Rumor**: Varek is rumored to know the coordinates of the *Drowned Sun*, a flagship from an ancient astral fleet that crashed into the Abyssal Warrens three centuries ago.

### **2. Shellsong Arcana**

**Specialty**: Aquatic Spells, Arcane Foci, Pearls of Power, and Divination Conchs  
**Location**: At the base of the Tidal Spire, where crystal corridors meet the open waves.  
**Owner**: **Thalassa Shellsong**, a haughty triton sorceress with crystalline hair and shimmering sapphire scales.  
**Description**: Floating inside an iridescent, mother-of-pearl dome, Shellsong Arcana resonates with soft oceanic harmonies. Spells are inscribed not on parchment, but upon polished abalone plates and etched nautilus spirals. Water spheres hover in the air, suspending glowing spell components and ancient divination crystals.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Pearl of Power** (*Uncommon*, 1,000 gp): As per the DMG. Allows a spellcaster to regain one expended spell slot of up to 3rd level.
* **Spell Scroll of Water Walk** (150 gp): As per the DMG.
* **Spell Scroll of Control Water** (400 gp): As per the DMG.
* **Conch of the Roaring Deep** (*Rare*, 2,200 gp): Once per long rest, the bearer can blow this spiral conch to cast *Cone of Cold* (saving throw Constitution save) or summon a *Water Elemental* for 10 minutes.
* **Driftglobe (Bioluminescent)** (*Uncommon*, 600 gp): Emits soft green or sapphire bioluminescence, functioning equally well underwater.

**Unique Services**:

* **Abalone Spell Transcription**: Thalassa can scribe arcane spells onto water-impervious abalone tablets for wizards (50 gp per spell level).
* **Current-Divination**: For 100 gp, she reads oceanic tides to divine safe planar passage or locate underwater shipwrecks.

**Rumor**: Thalassa’s family once ruled an undersea empire in the Material Plane before it was dragged into the Plane of Water during the Shattering of the Loom.

### **3. The Coral Apothecary**

**Specialty**: Water-Breathing Draughts, Alchemical Jellies, and Bioluminescent Salves  
**Location**: The Coral Canopy, grown into the hollow of an enormous weeping staghorn coral.  
**Owner**: **Coris Bloom**, a soft-spoken locathah alchemist who wears a moisture-retaining harness filled with bubbling saltwater.  
**Description**: A vibrant, living greenhouse of aquatic flora. Glass vials are hooked to living kelp tendrils, and luminous jellyfish pulse gently in ceiling cisterns, shedding rhythmic pastel light. The air is sweet with the scent of dried sea lavender, kelp honey, and powdered coral salts.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Potion of Water Breathing** (*Uncommon*, 180 gp): As per the DMG. Duration 1 hour.
* **Potion of Healing** (50 gp) & **Potion of Greater Healing** (150 gp): Brewed in water-resistant wax-sealed flasks.
* **Siren’s Voice Tincture** (250 gp): Grants advantage on Charisma (Persuasion) checks made against aquatic creatures for 1 hour, but makes the drinker vulnerable to thunder damage.
* **Luminescent Pearl Salve** (75 gp): Rubbed onto skin or armor; causes the target to shed bright light for 30 feet in dark waters for 4 hours without washing off.
* **Vial of Moray Venom** (150 gp): Injury poison; saving throw Constitution save or take 3d6 poison damage and be poisoned for 1 minute.

**Unique Services**:

* **Gilled Respiration Tonic**: A customized brew granting terrestrial races 24 hours of natural aquatic respiration and darkvision (100 gp).
* **Toxin Extraction**: Extracts venom from captured sea beasts safely for 25 gp.

**Rumor**: Coris has perfected an elixir that allows humanoids to commune telepathically with leviathans, but refuses to sell it out of fear of what the beasts might demand.

### **4. Brine & Iron Outfitters**

**Specialty**: Harpoons, Netting, Heavy Diving Armors, and Depth-Forged Weapons  
**Location**: Beside the Brineforge Shipyard in the Leviathan's Mooring.  
**Owner**: **Captain Kormac Grin**, a gruff sea-dwarf with a peg-leg carved from narwhal horn and an anchor tattoo across his chest.  
**Description**: The shop smells of heated pitch, tarred hemp rope, and charcoal. Racks of heavy barbed tridents, weighted boarding nets, and depth-hardened iron crossbows fill the shop. A water-filled test tank sits in the center of the showroom where customers can test weapon handling under drag resistance.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Barbed Harpoon (+1 Spear)** (*Uncommon*, 500 gp): Thrown range 30/60 ft. On a hit, the target is impaled (speed halved); retrieving the line requires an Athletics contest against the wielder.
* **Weighted Sea-Net** (25 gp): saving throw Strength check to break free instead of the standard saving throw.
* **Depth-Tempered Heavy Crossbow (+1 Heavy Crossbow)** (*Uncommon*, 850 gp): Specially weighted to fire underwater without disadvantage at normal range (100 ft.).
* **Sharkskin Studded Leather** (*+1 Studded Leather*, 1,100 gp): Crafted from razor-sharp mako scales; enemies grappling the wearer take 1d4 slashing damage at the start of each turn.

**Unique Services**:

* **Anti-Corrosion Plating**: Coats steel weapons in molten brass and copper to prevent rusting in planar brine (40 gp).
* **Ballast Rigging**: Fits heavy armor with quick-release emergency flotation bladders (50 gp).

**Rumor**: Kormac lost his leg to a juvenile kraken in the Abyssal Warrens, and he keeps the harpoon that wounded the beast hanging over his forge, waiting for someone brave enough to hunt it down.

## **Taverns of Tidehaven**

### **1. The Siren’s Respite**

**Theme**: Boisterous Sailor & Pirate Haven  
**Location**: The Saltspire Anchorage, built inside the colossal, bleached skull of a deceased Leviathan.  
**Owner**: **"Salt-Tooth" Morra**, a retired merfolk privateer with a peg-tail brace, golden hoop earrings, and a booming laugh.  
**Description**: The tavern is an architectural marvel. Ribs of the leviathan form vaulted ceiling arches draped in glowing jellyfish nets. The central bar is carved from the creature's lower jaw, where Morra dispenses hearty ales and fiery rum. Salt-crusted sailors, triton duelists, and planar explorers pack the long driftwood benches, shouting sea shanties over the sound of crashing waves beneath the floorboards.  

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **Kraken's Ink Stout** *(House Special)*: A pitch-black, creamy stout brewed with roasted chicory and a drop of kraken ink; leaves a tingling, electric aftertaste on the lips (4 sp).
* **Driftwood Rum**: Sweet, oaky rum aged in sunken barrels; burns warm all the way down (3 sp).
* **Leviathan Belly-Stew**: A thick, savory chowder loaded with sea tubers, crab meat, and spicy pepper-kelp (5 sp).
* **Fried Coral-Fritters**: Crisp golden batter fried with spiced minced clams and sea onion (2 sp).

**Entertainment**:

* **Shanty Duels**: Bards and pirate crews compete in musical battles for free rounds, judged by crowd applause.
* **Pearl-Dice**: A high-stakes dice game played inside a glass basin using colored sea pearls.

**Special Features**:

* **The Submerged Lounge**: A flooded lower chamber with enchanted water-breathing fields where aquatic patrons can drink without having to leave the water.
* **The Captain’s Booth**: A secluded, curtained alcove reserved for Council members and privateer captains making back-channel agreements.

**Rumor**: Morra has a secret map etched into the roof of the leviathan skull's eye socket that reveals the location of an untamed water-vortex leading straight to the Sea of Fire.

---

### **2. The Scalding Cup**

**Theme**: Rough Pirate Dive & Current-Pit Arena  
**Location**: Suspended over a churning whirlpool in the Leviathan's Mooring.  
**Owner**: **Garrus Brinewhip**, a scarred sahuagin defector who turned his back on the blood-frenzied priestesses to make a fortune on illegal fighting wagers.  
**Description**: Raw, dangerous, and dripping with condensation. The Scalding Cup is built from the hulls of three smashed pirate sloops lashed together above a hydrothermal vent that boils the water below. At the center of the tavern floor is a 20-foot wide circular grated pit submerged in swirling, scalding currents where gladiators fight for purses of sea gold.  

**Signature Drinks and Dishes**:

* **Scald-Spit Liquor** *(House Special)*: Highly concentrated alcohol brewed from boiling hydrothermal kelp; served steaming hot (5 sp).
* **Abyssal Bitter**: A sour, murky ale that gives terrestrial drinkers temporary resistance to cold damage for 1 hour (3 sp).
* **Charred Eel Strips**: Spicy, smoked moray eel seared over volcanic steam vents (4 sp).
* **Sharkfin Bread**: Hard, salted travel cakes baked with dried ground fishmeal (1 sp).

**Entertainment**:

* **The Current Pit**: Bare-knuckle fighters and blade-dancers brawl in water with shifting vortex currents (saving throw Athletics check each round to avoid being swept into the boiling vent below).
* **Knife-in-the-Mast**: Combatants take turns throwing knives into a spinning ship's helm while dodging tossed grog mugs.

**Special Features**:

* **The Sahuagin's Parley**: Anyone drawing steel outside the pit is immediately harpooned by Garrus’s bouncers and hung over the steam vent until they apologize and buy a round.
* **Contraband Sluice**: A water trapdoor behind the bar where goods can be jettisoned into the bay if Council inspectors board the tavern.

**Rumor**: Garrus provides intelligence to the Deepcurrent Syndicate in exchange for protection against assassination squads sent by his former sahuagin clan.

## **The Black Market: The Kraken’s Hold**

Hidden deep within the hull of a colossal, petrified ghost galleon wedged into an unstable coral reef in the Leviathan's Mooring lies **The Kraken’s Hold**. The Deepcurrent Syndicate controls every gangway and water hatch, collecting tariffs from planar smugglers, black-market alchemists, and rogue privateers.

### **Black Market Shops**

### **1. The Flotsam Vault**

**Specialty**: Stolen Planar Relics, Cursed Sea Artifacts, and Counterfeit Maritime Charters  
**Location**: The captain's stateroom of the wrecked galleon, guarded by two water weirds in glass cisterns.  
**Owner**: **"Slick" Sunderfin**, an oily water genasi fence with gold-plated shark teeth and rings on every clawed finger.  
**Description**: Velvet-lined sea chests and rotting oak cabinets overflow with plundered astral jewelry, blood-stained captain’s logs, and smuggled navigational astrolabes. Sunderfin sits behind a desk fashioned from a petrified sea turtle shell, inspecting goods under a magnifying prism.  

**Notable Items for Sale**:

* **Dagger of Venom** (*Rare*, 2,000 gp): As per the DMG.
* **Stolen Council Admiralty Chart** (400 gp): Gives advantage on navigation checks through the Plane of Water and reveals secret patrol schedules of the Council of Waves.
* **Aboleth Eye Shard** (*Rare*, 1,200 gp): A petrified lens from an aboleth. Once per day, the wielder can cast *Detect Thoughts* (saving throw) or *Charm Person* (saving throw) on aquatic humanoids.
* **Counterfeit Letter of Marque** (250 gp): Perfectly forged privateering credentials recognized by the Council of Waves, allowing ships to raid rival vessels legally.
* **Necklace of Adaptation** (*Uncommon*, 1,500 gp): As per the DMG.

**Unique Services**:

* **Cargo Fencing**: Buys stolen vessels, captured cargo, and illicit pearls for 55% value in unmarked platinum pearls.
* **Identity Erasure**: Erases naval bounties or criminal records from Council archives through bribed clerks (500 gp).

**Specialty**: D&D 5e Marine Poisons, Acidic Resins, and Paralytic Slimes  
**Location**: The flooded lower ballast of the Kraken's Hold, accessible only by diving.  
**Owner**: **Scylla the Pale**, an albino sea hag with milky eyes and eel tendrils braided into her hair.  
**Description**: The air (and water) is rank with the stench of rotting pufferfish, sulfur, and ammonia. Glass jars hold squirming sea serpents, paralyzed blue-ringed octopuses, and glowing abyssal anemones. Scylla milks venom glands with bone needles into lead-stoppered bottles.  

**Notable Items for Sale (Poisons & Contraband)**:

* **Crawler Mucus (Contact)** (*DMG p. 257*, 200 gp): Harvested from abyssal worms. saving throw Constitution save or be paralyzed for 1 minute.
* **Torpor (Ingested)** (*DMG p. 258*, 600 gp): saving throw Constitution save or be incapacitated for 4d6 hours.
* **Purple Worm Poison (Injury)** (*DMG p. 258*, 2,000 gp): Refluffed as abyssal kraken-blood venom; saving throw Constitution save or take 42 (12d6) poison damage.
* **Drow Poison (Injury)** (*DMG p. 257*, 200 gp): saving throw Constitution save or be poisoned for 1 hour; unconscious on fail by 5 or more.
* **Abyssal Slime Vials (Contraband)** (150 gp): Thrown splash weapon. Covers a 10-foot area in corrosive sea sludge (saving throw Dexterity save or take 2d6 acid damage and fall prone).
* **Black-Pearl Powder (Narcotic)** (100 gp per pinch): Illegal stimulant ground from cursed pearls; grants water breathing and +2 to Dexterity checks for 1 hour, followed by a saving throw Constitution save or 1 level of exhaustion.

**Unique Services**:

* **Venom Lacquering**: Permanently treats a melee weapon with water-resistant poison coating that lasts for 10 successful strikes (cost: 200 gp + cost of poison).

## **Tidehaven Notice Board**

The Tidehaven Notice Board is an ancient driftwood mast salvaged from a sunken warship, bolted to the central pier of the Saltspire Anchorage. Waterproofed parchment sheets are pinned to it with brass fishhooks and sealed with colored wax by the Council of Waves. Adventurers routinely gather at the nearby Siren's Respite to review the postings.

---

### **Mission 1: The Abyssal Trench Incursion**

**Posted by**: High Priestess Nylaris Waveborn (The Tidal Spire)  
**Reward**: 450 gp and a *Conch of the Roaring Deep*  
**Description**: "A warband of rogue sahuagin raiders has established an illegal encampment in the Glowtide Mines within the upper Abyssal Warrens. They have slain four locathah miners, slaughtered the guard patrol, and begun harvesting the sacred luminescent pearl beds. We require seasoned combatants to descend into the trench, eradicate the raiders, and reclaim the mine before the pearls are corrupted."  

---

### **Mission 2: Ghost Ship of the Kelp Beds**

**Posted by**: Captain Lysara Driftwave (Council of Waves)  
**Reward**: 500 gp and one *Mariner's Armor* (+1 Scale Mail)  
**Description**: "The derelict merchant brig *The Wave-Cutter* was spotted drifting aimlessly through the outer kelp forests three leagues north of the city. Her lanterns burn with ghostly emerald fire, and salvage crews sent to board her have not reported back. Board the vessel, put down whatever horror has claimed her decks, and recover the ship’s captain's log for the Council."  

---

### **Mission 3: The Leviathan's Ransom**

**Posted by**: Master Arcanist Tolaris Shellsong (Aquamancer’s Guild)  
**Reward**: 400 gp and a *Pearl of Power*  
**Description**: "An invaluable astral navigation artifact—the *Chronometer of Currents*—was stolen from my personal laboratory last night. Informants in the Leviathan's Mooring report that the thief, a rogue privateer named Declan Drake, has holed up in the sea caves beneath the Brineforge Shipyard, waiting for an off-plane smuggler. Retrieve the artifact intact. Bring Drake back in chains if possible."
