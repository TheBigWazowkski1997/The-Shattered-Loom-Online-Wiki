The Plane of Water is a boundless, ever-changing ocean filled with magical marine life, towering coral reefs, and submerged ruins. It is a realm of fluidity and renewal, where the boundaries between life and magic blur.

### **Geography and Features**

1. **The Deep Abyss**: An unending chasm where the pressure is so intense that only the most powerful beings can survive.
2. **The Coral Fields**: Expansive underwater meadows of glowing coral and seaweed, home to countless aquatic creatures.
3. **The Celestial Currents**: Rivers of magical water that move faster than sound, acting as natural transportation across the plane.

### **Key Locations**

* **Pearldome**:
	
	A bubble-enclosed city of shimmering pearl and coral. Priests of Nereia, the Depthmother, maintain the protective wards.
	
* **The Sapphire Temple**:
	
	An ancient structure hidden in the depths, where the tides are said to originate.
	
* **The Wailing Trenches**:
	
	A haunted expanse filled with the spirits of those who drowned, avoided even by native elementals.
	

### **Unique Characteristics**

* **Endless Ocean**: There is no surface or solid ground; all structures are floating or submerged.
* **Breath of the Depthmother**: Non-native creatures magically gain the ability to breathe underwater while in the plane.

### **Inhabitants**

* **Water Elementals**: Sentient waves and whirlpools that act as guardians.
* **Merfolk and Tritons**: Amphibious beings who thrive in the Coral Fields.
* **Kraken and Leviathans**: Massive, ancient sea monsters that patrol the depths.

# Cities

1. #### **Pearldome**
	

	**Description**:Pearldome is an underwater city encased in a massive magical bubble. It glows softly with blue light and is built from coral, pearl, and mother-of-pearl materials. It is a cultural and spiritual center dedicated to Nereia, the Depthmother.
	
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Tidecaller Temple**: A grand cathedral dedicated to Nereia, where priests perform rituals to calm storms and heal the injured.
	* **The Aquatic Grove**: A massive underwater garden of luminescent seaweed and enchanted coral, home to rare marine creatures.
	* **The Pearl Guard**: An elite group of warriors who ride hippocampi and guard the city.
	

	

#### **2\. Tidehaven**

	**Description**:Located on the coast of a vast, shifting sea, Tidehaven is a semi-submerged city where buildings rise and fall with the tides. Its canals and aqueducts allow easy travel by small boats.
	
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Wave Markets**: Stilted shops that sell water-based magic items, potions, and aquatic mounts like giant seahorses.
	* **The Drifting Court**: A council of merfolk and tritons who oversee the city’s governance.
	* **The Tidecaller’s Bell**: A massive bell that rings to warn of incoming storms or tsunamis.
	

	

#### **3\. The Sapphire Abyss**

	**Description**:A bioluminescent underwater metropolis built within a vast coral reef. The city is a sanctuary for aquatic races, illuminated by glowing fungi and crystals.
	
* **Key Features**:
	* **The Abyssal Hall**: A meeting place for aquatic ambassadors and planar travelers.
	* **The Reefforge**: A unique workshop where items are crafted from living coral and enchanted with water magic.
	* **The Abyssal Maw**: A deep trench at the edge of the city that is believed to hold an ancient sea god’s prison.
