# Map & Geography of the Elemental Plane of Water (The Azure Expanse)

![[attachments/plane_of_water_map.jpg]]

> _"In the Azure Expanse, there is neither shore nor sky, only an endless cathedral of sunlit cerulean water. You swim along rivers of light that flow faster than arrows, rest within cities enclosed in iridescent pearls, and listen to the singing of ancient leviathans whose bodies are larger than mountains. Breathe deeply: the Depthmother's grace touches every current."_
> — Journal of Vexiryn the Unbowed

## D&D 5e Adaptation & Canon Map References

> [!NOTE]
> **Adapted from D&D 5e Canon**:
> * **The Elemental Plane of Water**: Adapted directly from official D&D 5e cosmology (*Dungeon Master's Guide*, pp. 53–55, *Manual of the Planes*, and *Planescape*).
> * **Canon Map Reference**: The classic planar geography of Water—including the **Citadel of Ten Thousand Pearls** (seat of the Great Padisha of the Marids), the **Sea of Light** (sunlit upper waters), the **Darkened Depths**, and border regions near Air (the Sea of Ice) and Earth (the Silt Pits)—informs the vertical aquatic stratigraphy of this expanse.
> * **Loom Integration**: In the Shattered Loom, the currents are tended by **Nereia, the Depthmother**, who infuses the waters with the magical "Breath of the Depthmother," allowing all living beings entering the plane to breathe and speak underwater naturally.

## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Cities of Plane of Water/Pearldome|Pearldome: The Bubble Metropolis]]
* **Location**: Central sunlit waters, enclosed within a colossal hemispherical air-bubble anchored to a dormant underwater volcano.
* **Role**: Spiritual, diplomatic, and trade capital dedicated to Nereia, the Depthmother.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Tidecaller Temple**: Grand cathedral of polished mother-of-pearl where priests perform rituals to harmonize ocean leylines.
  * **The Aquatic Grove**: Vast submerged gardens of bioluminescent kelp, sea-lilies, and medicinal anemones.
  * **The Pearl Guard Garrison**: Staging base for elite hippocampus-mounted knights guarding the city's perimeter.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The High Tidecallers, advised by Marid dignitaries.

### 2. [[Cities of Plane of Water/Tidehaven|Tidehaven: The Stilted Free-Port]]
* **Location**: Western shallow reef shelf where ancient coral atolls break the surface.
* **Role**: Commercial hub for surface-sailors, planar merchants, privateers, and amphibious races.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Wave Markets**: Stilted wooden platforms and pontoon piers selling water-breathing charms, diving rigs, and rare fish.
  * **The Drifting Court**: A forum where a council of merfolk, tritons, and mortal captains resolve harbor disputes.
  * **The Tidecaller's Bell**: An immense bronze bell rung to warn of incoming tidal bores or kraken migrations.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Port-Admirals of the Drifting Court.

### 3. [[Cities of Plane of Water/The Sapphire Abyss|The Sapphire Temple: Sanctuary of the Trench]]
* **Location**: Deep within the southern abyssal rift, glowing on the ocean floor miles below the surface.
* **Role**: Ancient pre-Shattering sanctuary of primordial magic and deep-sea wisdom.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Sapphire Font**: A pulsating core of concentrated elemental water that generates the plane's major oceanic leylines.
  * **The Trench Columns**: Titanic fluted pillars carved from solid sapphire that hold back the crushing pressures of the abyss.
  * **The Hall of Leviathans**: An open underwater plaza where ancient krakens and dragon turtles commune in peace.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Ancient Wardens of the Deep.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Shipwreck-Grove
* **Location**: A plateau of giant branch-coral north of Tidehaven where hundreds of planar ships have settled over millennia.
* **Population**: ~1,400 (Merfolk, Githzerai, Aquatic Elves, Scavengers).
* **Description**: A multi-tiered community built within the hollowed hulls of sunken astral galleons, caravels, and dreadnoughts.
* **Specialty**: Salvaged planar wood, waterproof leather tanning, and hull-breach sealants.
* **Notable Feature — The Galleon Spire**: An intact 3-masted flagship inverted and mounted atop coral branches as the town hall.

### 2. Coral-Gardens
* **Location**: Eastern reaches within the dense, glowing Coral Fields.
* **Population**: ~950 (Tritons, Locathah, Sea Druids).
* **Description**: A tranquil, living village cultivated from soft sponges and branching elkhorn coral illuminated by glowing anemones.
* **Specialty**: Sea-sponge insulation, bioluminescent pigments, and pearl aquaculture.
* **Notable Feature — The Nursery Grotto**: A protected reef hollow where rare sea mounts and giant seahorses are bred.

### 3. Trench-Edge Watch
* **Location**: The northern precipice of the Wailing Trenches.
* **Population**: ~700 (Paladins of the Depths, Shadar-kai, Water Genasi).
* **Description**: An austere fortress of carved basalt anchored with heavy iron chains to the canyon lip. Sentinels watch the dark waters for drowned undead and shadow corruption.
* **Specialty**: Deep-sea glow-lanterns, heavy harpoons, and consecrated water-flasks.
* **Notable Feature — The Sonar Array**: Brass listening horns that amplify sound waves from the abyss to detect kraken movement days in advance.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Celestial Currents
* **Description**: Glowing ribbons of super-accelerated magical water that snake through the ocean like subterranean rivers.
* **Planar Trait**: Swimmers and vessels entering a Celestial Current travel at speeds exceeding 60 miles per hour without effort, navigating safely along established inter-city corridors.

### 2. The Coral Fields
* **Description**: Boundless underwater plains of giant glowing coral, sea fans, and schools of millions of iridescent planar fish.
* **Planar Trait**: Spells that restore hit points or remove conditions cast within the Coral Fields heal an additional 1d8 hit points.

### 3. The Wailing Trenches
* **Description**: Lightless chasm depths haunted by the psychic impressions of sailors drowned across all worlds of the multiverse.
* **Planar Trait**: Emits an aura of dread; creatures entering without magical light must pass a Requires strong mental and physical fortitude to withstand.

### 4. Breath of the Depthmother
* **Description**: The innate divine blessing of Nereia suffusing the Azure Expanse.
* **Planar Trait**: Any non-water breathing creature entering the plane automatically gains the ability to breathe water, speak normally underwater, and move with a swimming speed equal to their walking speed.
