## D&D 5e Adaptation & Canon Map References

> [!NOTE]
> **Adapted from D&D 5e Canon**:
> * **The Elemental Plane of Air**: Adapted directly from official D&D 5e cosmology (*Dungeon Master's Guide*, pp. 53–55, *Manual of the Planes*, and *Planescape*).
> * **Canon Map Reference**: The traditional planar structure of the Plane of Air—featuring the Great Conflagration border near Fire, the Labyrinthine Storms, the Citadel of Ice and Steel, and the palaces of the Djinn Caliphates—informs the atmospheric layer dynamics and subjective directional gravity.
> * **Loom Integration**: In the Shattered Loom, wind and lightning currents are channeled by **Aurelion, the Skycaller**, who uses the **Windspire Monoliths** as colossal harmonic tuning forks to maintain cosmic air-circulation across the fractured multiverse.

## The Three Great Metropolises

### 1. [[Cities of the Plane of Air/Zephyros|Zephyros: The Drifting Metropolis]]
* **Location**: Center of the calm mid-atmosphere currents, drifting gently between the trade thermals.
* **Role**: Commercial crossroads, airship harbor, diplomatic hub for Djinn emirates and mortal travelers.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Gale Spire**: An immense crystalline tower at the central island that regulates weather, stabilizing air pressure and wind speeds across the city.
  * **The Nimbus Market**: Floating open-air bazaar where solidified clouds are shaped into architecture, bottled hurricanes, and featherweight silks.
  * **Windcaller Academy**: High school of aeromancy and storm-sorcery training sky-captains and elemental mages.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Sky-Senate of Zephyros, advised by Djinn Grand Viziers.

### 2. [[Cities of the Plane of Air/Stormhold|Stormhold: The Fortress of Thunder]]
* **Location**: Southeastern dark storm-front, anchored within an eternal supercell hurricane.
* **Role**: Citadel of military defense, griffon-rider garrison, and champion redoubt of Aurelion.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Stormforge**: A titanic anvil-complex powered by diverted lightning bolts, forging weapons crackling with thunderous energy.
  * **The Thunder Hall**: Vast arena where aerial knights duel in subjective three-dimensional gravity.
  * **Skyguard Eyries**: Fortified rookeries housing hundreds of armored war-griffons and hippogriffs.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: Warmaster Kaelen Storm-Eye and the Knights of the Skyguard.

### 3. [[Cities of the Plane of Air/SkySong Isles|SkySong Isles: The Singing Archipelago]]
* **Location**: Northeastern tranquil quadrant, floating among perpetual auroras.
* **Role**: Sanctuary of artistic expression, musical enchantment, and glasscraft.
* **Key Landmarks**:
  * **The Resonant Spires**: Hollow fluted glass towers that sing haunting, complex harmonies as trade winds blow through them.
  * **The Cloud Gardens**: Terraced gardens of solidified mist where floating orchids and air-ferns bloom.
  * **The Harmonic Amphitheater**: Open-sky amphitheater where bards perform symphonies heard twenty miles away.
* **Ruler / Leadership**: The Chorus of Bards, led by Maestro Lyra Windwhisper.

## Towns, Villages & Frontier Settlements

### 1. Gale-Pass (The Waystation)
* **Location**: At the southern narrow gap where the Great Trade Wind accelerates through floating rock clusters.
* **Population**: ~1,200 (Aarakocra, Air Genasi, Airship Sailors).
* **Description**: A bustling service port built on two tethered asteroids. Airships dock here to re-rig sails, hire navigators, and repair lift-enchantments.
* **Specialty**: Sky-sails, buoyancy gas canisters, and slipstream navigation charts.
* **Notable Feature — The Anchor Cable**: A 5-mile adamantine chain anchoring the two islands together against slipstream drift.

### 2. Mist-Hollow
* **Location**: Deep within the floating Cloud Forests southwest of Zephyros.
* **Population**: ~850 (Firbolgs, Wood Elves, Sprite Clans).
* **Description**: A serene treehouse village built among the branches of colossal floating ironwood trees anchored in solid cloudbanks.
* **Specialty**: Cloud-condensed healing water, feather-fall charms, and trained courier kestrels.
* **Notable Feature — The Rain-Well**: A hanging reservoir that collects dew and drops localized rain showers on demand.

### 3. Thunder-Roost
* **Location**: Atop the highest shelf of the western Thundercliffs.
* **Population**: ~600 (Cloud Giants, Goliaths, Storm Shamans).
* **Description**: A cyclopean fortress-hamlet of massive marble pillars and brass braziers, home to Cloud Giant sky-sculptors.
* **Specialty**: Giant-crafted storm javelins, lightning glass gems, and mammoth-hide sails.
* **Notable Feature — The Lightning Rod Citadel**: Colossal copper needles that channel thunderstrikes into underground power vaults.

## Special Features & Planar Wonders

### 1. The Windspire Monoliths
* **Description**: Fluted ancient stone pillars thousands of feet tall floating motionless in the northwestern sky.
* **Planar Trait**: The monoliths emit a low resonant hum. Creatures within 1 mile can cast *Levitate* or *Fly* at will with unlimited duration.

### 2. The Thundercliffs
* **Description**: Titanic floating landmasses with shear sheer vertical drop-offs from which endless waterfalls plunge into the clear sky below, evaporating into clouds.
* **Planar Trait**: The updrafts here allow creatures without flight to glide horizontally at a speed of 60 feet simply by spreading a cloak.

### 3. The Vortex (The Great Downdraft)
* **Description**: A colossal cyclonic sinkhole in the lower atmosphere pulling matter toward the border of the Plane of Water and Earth.
* **Planar Trait**: The immense gravitational vortex pulls unwary vessels inexorably toward the abyss.

### 4. Subjective Directional Gravity
* **Description**: The fundamental planar trait of the Skybound Expanse.
* **Planar Trait**: A sentient creature can choose which way is "down" with a bonus action on their turn, falling up to 60 feet per round in that direction until they reach solid footing or choose a new direction.
