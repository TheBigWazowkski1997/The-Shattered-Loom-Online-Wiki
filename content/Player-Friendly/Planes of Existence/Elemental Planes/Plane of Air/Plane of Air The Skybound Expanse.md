The Plane of Air is a realm of eternal skies, endless winds, and floating islands. It is the lightest and most open of the planes, where gravity is malleable, and clouds form solid ground.

### **Geography and Features**

1. **The Endless Horizon**: A vast, open expanse of swirling air currents and shifting weather.
2. **Cloud Forests**: Floating groves of trees and flora made from condensed mist.
3. **The Thundercliffs**: Towering, storm-shrouded plateaus where lightning constantly strikes.

### **Key Locations**

* **Zephyros**:
	
	A city of floating islands and airships, serving as a hub for merchants and planar travelers.
	
* **Stormhold**:
	
	A fortress surrounded by eternal lightning storms, home to Aurelion’s most loyal warriors.
	
* **The Windspire Monoliths**:
	
	Massive columns of air magic that channel the raw energy of the plane, sought after by storm sorcerers.
	

### **Unique Characteristics**

* **Gravity Defiance**: Gravity can be adjusted by willpower or magic, allowing creatures to float or fly.
* **Eternal Breeze**: Winds are ever-present, enhancing speed and flight capabilities.

### **Inhabitants**

* **Air Elementals**: Swift and nearly invisible beings that blend seamlessly with their environment.
* **Djinn**: Noble and proud air genies who build magnificent palaces in the sky.
* **Cloud Giants**: Towering beings who live in floating castles

# Cities

1\. Zephyros

	Description:A city of drifting islands connected by enchanted bridges and floating staircases. Zephyros is a hub for air elementals, djinn, and sky nomads.
	
* Key Features:
	* The Gale Spire: A tower that controls the winds and weather around the city.
	* The Nimbus Market: A marketplace where clouds are shaped into goods like solidified mist or bottled storms.
	* Windcaller Academy: A school for mages specializing in air-based magic.
	
	

2\. Stormhold

	Description:A fortress-city surrounded by constant thunderclouds. Stormhold serves as a stronghold for Aurelion’s most devout followers and warriors.
	
* Key Features:
	* The Stormforge: A forge powered by lightning, creating weapons imbued with storm energy.
	* The Thunder Hall: A grand arena where warriors compete in combat enhanced by lightning magic.
	* Skyguard Outpost: A watchtower network patrolled by griffon riders.
	
	

3\. The Skysong Isles

	Description:A collection of floating platforms made from clouds and enchanted wood, home to a nomadic culture of sky-dwellers.
	
* Key Features:
	* The Singing Peaks: A series of floating mountains that emit harmonious sounds when the wind passes through them.
	* The Cloud Gardens: Fields of magical flowers that only grow in the sky.
		The Aerie: A central gathering place for the sky nomads, adorned with wind chimes and kites.
