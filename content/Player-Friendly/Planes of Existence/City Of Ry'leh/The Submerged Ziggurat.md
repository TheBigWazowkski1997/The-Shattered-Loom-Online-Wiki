The **Submerged Ziggurat** is the central structure of Ryleh, a colossal, alien edifice that descends deep into the Astral Sea’s surreal, liquid essence. Constructed of green-black stone veined with glowing, pulsing runes, the Ziggurat serves as both a prison and sanctum for the **Great Dreamer**. Its tiers contain dangerous secrets, maddening insights, and warped guardians that grow more powerful and surreal the deeper one ventures.

### **Key Features**

#### **1\. The Sleeper’s Nexus** (Entrance)

* **Description**: The Nexus is the topmost chamber of the Ziggurat, a vast hall carved with alien glyphs that seem to rearrange themselves when observed. The air hums with the psychic resonance of the slumbering Great Dreamer.
* **Enemies**: Cultists of the **Dreambound**, aberrant guards, and a massive **Astral Spawn Guardian**.
* **Unique Trait**: Those entering the Nexus must succeed on a saving throw Wisdom saving throw or experience recurring nightmares of the Ziggurat for 1d4 days, taking disadvantage on Insight and Perception checks during this time.

---

#### **2\. The Chamber of Eyes**

* **Description**: A massive, circular hall lit by hundreds of glowing, unblinking eyes set into the walls. The eyes shift and track intruders, and their gazes pierce into the minds of those who look at them.
* **Hazards**:
	* **The Watchful Gaze**: Each turn, a creature in the chamber must make a saving throw Wisdom saving throw or be subjected to one of the following effects (DM’s choice or roll d4):
		1. **Psychic Screech**: The creature takes 4d8 psychic damage and is deafened for 1 minute.
		2. **Memory Fragmentation**: The creature forgets one skill or proficiency until it leaves the Ziggurat.
		3. **Paranoia**: The creature is frightened of an ally for 1d4 rounds.
		4. **Vision of the Loom**: The creature glimpses a fragment of the Shattered Loom and gains advantage on Arcana checks for 24 hours but suffers one level of exhaustion.
* **Reward**: One of the glowing eyes contains a **Loom Thread**, a fragment of primordial power.

---

#### **3\. The Labyrinth of Dreams**

* **Description**: A tier of the Ziggurat that exists simultaneously in the waking world and the dreams of the Great Dreamer. The layout shifts constantly, making navigation treacherous. Walls ripple like water, and the air is thick with strange whispers.
* **Hazards**:
	* **Dream Echoes**: Illusions of the party's past memories or future fears manifest as enemies.
	* **Time Distortions**: Hours in the Labyrinth can pass in seconds or stretch to days, potentially aging or disorienting adventurers.
* **Reward**: Hidden within the Labyrinth is the **Key of the Sleeper**, required to access the Chamber of Slumber.

---

#### **4\. The Chamber of Slumber** (Final Chamber)

* **Description**: The Great Dreamer rests here, an incomprehensible mass of eldritch power partially submerged in the astral liquid. The chamber pulses with its dreams, creating shifting landscapes and surreal environments within the same space.
* **Unique Trait**:
	* Reality is fragile in this chamber. Each round, the DM rolls a d6. On a 6, reality warps unpredictably, creating effects such as gravity reversal, planar fissures, or spontaneous illusions.
* **Enemies**:
	* The **Great Dreamer** itself, defended by **Astral Spawn**, **Deep Watchers**, and waves of Dreambound cultists.
