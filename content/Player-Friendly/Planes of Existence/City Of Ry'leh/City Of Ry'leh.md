### **Ry'leh: The Extradimensional City of the Great Dreamer**

**Ryleh** is a paradoxical, extradimensional city adrift in the **Astral Sea**, an impossible place shaped by the slumbering power of the **Great Dreamer**, a primordial and eldritch being who lies dormant within its depths. Ryleh is both a physical place and a dreamscape, existing partially in the material realm and partially in the dreams of those who come too close to its influence. Its alien geometry and warped reality defy mortal comprehension, and it is said that no two visitors experience Ryleh the same way.

---

## **Overview**

* **Nature**: A city of non-Euclidean architecture, pulsating with eldritch energy. Ryleh is partly submerged in an astral ocean of liquid thought and starlight, though its structures rise above and beneath the waves in chaotic patterns. Time flows erratically, and reality itself bends to the will of the Great Dreamer.
* **Purpose**: Ryleh serves as the prison and sanctuary of the Great Dreamer, as well as a place where cosmic secrets, forbidden knowledge, and lost souls converge. It draws those seeking ultimate power, but few leave unchanged—or alive.
* **Inhabitants**: The city is populated by aberrations, dream-echoes of beings from countless worlds, cultists of the Great Dreamer, and warped astral travelers.

---

## **Key Features of Ryleh**

### **1\. The Starlight Harbor**

* **Description**: A shimmering, semi-solid "ocean" of astral essence surrounds Ryleh. The Starlight Harbor is where planar travelers first arrive, their ships or bodies materializing out of the Astral Sea.
* **Notable Features**:
	* **The Drifting Anchorage**: Ships of all shapes and sizes—some intact, others wrecked—float in the harbor. Many are centuries or millennia old, abandoned by those who succumbed to the city’s influence.
	* **The Veil of Ripples**: A massive, semi-transparent barrier of swirling colors and strange glyphs encircles the harbor, preventing most from leaving once they’ve entered.

---

### **2\. The Dreaming Spires**

* **Description**: Towering spires of green-black stone rise above the cityscape, twisting and merging in impossible ways. These spires are both physical structures and psychic nexuses that connect to the **Dreaming Realm** of the Great Dreamer.
* **Notable Features**:
	* **The Echo Chambers**: Rooms within the spires where dreamers’ thoughts and fears manifest as physical phenomena. Visitors often encounter twisted versions of their own memories or desires.
	* **The Starbind Observatory**: An ancient structure at the tallest spire, where cultists gaze into the Astral Sea to commune with eldritch entities.

---

### **3\. The Tides of Thought**

* **Description**: Flowing rivers of astral essence run through the city, shimmering with liquid dreams and consciousness. These "Tides of Thought" are said to carry the fragmented ideas of all who have come into contact with the Great Dreamer.
* **Unique Trait**: Bathing in the Tides grants temporary insight into the multiverse’s secrets, but prolonged exposure risks madness or psychic corruption.

---

### **4\. The Submerged Ziggurat**

* **Description**: Beneath the astral waters lies the **Ziggurat of the Great Dreamer**, a massive and ancient structure where the primordial being slumbers. The Ziggurat’s upper tiers rise above the waves, but most of it remains hidden below, pulsing with alien energy.
* **Notable Features**:
	* **The Sleeper’s Nexus**: The entrance to the Ziggurat, guarded by warped aberrations and cultists. It is said that only those who have dreamed of the Great Dreamer can pass through.
	* **The Chamber of Eyes**: A massive hall lined with glowing, unblinking eyes that seem to watch intruders. Those who fail a Wisdom saving throw (saving throw) risk falling into a permanent dream-like trance.

### **5\. The Fractured District**

* **Description**: An area of the city that appears to have collapsed under the weight of its own contradictions, the Fractured District is a chaotic maze of floating ruins, broken spires, and shattered streets. Time and space warp unpredictably here, creating pockets of stillness and rapid decay.
* **Notable Features**:
	* **The Flickering Bazaar**: A strange market where shadowy figures sell incomprehensible wares, including fragments of dreams, forgotten memories, and astral artifacts.
	* **The Abyssal Wells**: Deep pits filled with liquid starlight that seem to lead to other dimensions. Some claim these wells are linked to the core of the Great Dreamer’s mind.

---

### **6\. The Waking Plaza**

* **Description**: A central square dominated by a massive, cyclopean statue of the Great Dreamer, surrounded by glowing glyphs and carvings that radiate psychic energy. The plaza is a gathering place for the city’s cultists and visitors, many of whom hold strange rituals here.
* **Rituals**: The cultists perform regular ceremonies to keep the Great Dreamer in its slumber—or, as whispered in darker corners, to one day awaken it.

---

## **Inhabitants of Ryleh**

**[[Key NPCs]]**

### **1\. The Dreambound Cultists**

* **Description**: Mortals from across the multiverse who have been drawn to Ryleh by dreams or visions of the Great Dreamer. They serve as the city’s caretakers and guardians.
* **Motivations**: Some wish to serve the Great Dreamer and awaken it, while others seek the power and knowledge it promises.

---

### **2\. Aberrations and Servants**

* **Deep Watchers**: Massive, squid-like creatures with glowing eyes that patrol the Ziggurat.
* **Warped Beasts**: Twisted animals and humanoid forms reshaped by the city’s eldritch influence.
* **The Astral Spawn**: Amorphous beings made of astral energy that act as the city’s defenders.

---

### **3\. Dream-Echoes**

* **Description**: Fragments of consciousness and memories that take on physical or semi-physical form within the city. These echoes may appear as ghostly figures, distorted images, or even temporary companions to adventurers.
* **Unique Trait**: Dream-Echoes often mimic the thoughts or fears of nearby beings, creating unsettling and surreal encounters.

---

## **[[../../Cosmology/Beings of Power/The Great Dreamer|The Great Dreamer]]**

The Great Dreamer lies dormant within the depths of the Submerged Ziggurat. Though it does not awaken, its presence shapes the city and the minds of all who enter it.

### **Influence**

* **Dream Infiltration**: Those who spend too much time in Ryleh begin to dream of impossible worlds, alien geometries, and the Great Dreamer’s gaze. These dreams can impart knowledge but often lead to madness.
* **Warping Presence**: Prolonged exposure to the Great Dreamer’s aura causes physical and mental changes, such as glowing eyes, distorted limbs, or fragmented memories.
