# **Death's Head**

*“A severed head torn from a fresh corpse, its hair knotted into membranous, leathery bat-wings, its yellowed teeth clicking in frenzied hunger as it hovers through the midnight fog.”*

---

### **Overview**
A **Death’s Head** is a horrific, mobile undead manifestation created when dark necromancy, decapitation curses, or dread darklords awaken the severed head of a freshly slain victim. Floating on wings formed of its own knotted hair or stretched skin, a Death’s Head flies through the mists of the Shadowfell seeking living flesh to bite, infect, or hypnotize.

---

### **Lore & Nature**

#### **Minions of Dread**
Death’s Heads frequently serve as terrifying scouts, skirmishers, and harassing swarms for high-ranking undead, notably **Dullahan (Headless Horsemen)**, powerful vampires like **Strahd von Zarovich**, and necromancers of Darkon. When a headless horseman strikes down a foe, the psychic trauma often causes the severed head to twist into a shrieking Death’s Head under the rider's telepathic command.

#### **Three Horrific Variants**
In the Domains of Dread, Death’s Heads manifest according to the nature of their creation:
1. **Gnashing Death’s Head**: The most common variant, propelled by wings of braided hair, which bites with infected fangs that cause blood-rot.
2. **Medusa Death’s Head**: Sprouting snakelike hair, capable of projecting a petrifying or paraylzing gaze across the mist.
3. **Beholder-Spawn Death’s Head**: An aberrant variant found near Bluetspur or corrupted tombs, shooting necrotic rays from hollow eye sockets.

#### **Campaign Connection (Session 4)**
During **Session 4**, as the party traveled down the Old Svalich Road through suffocating fog toward Barovia, they were ambushed by the **Headless Horseman**. When the horseman struck down the rogue Ethan, he summoned **three shrieking Death's Heads** from his shadowy saddle to overwhelm Charl and Daniel. The floating heads attacked with psychic shrieks and biting fangs before being destroyed by Kyle's flamethrower and Adam's reanimated thralls.
