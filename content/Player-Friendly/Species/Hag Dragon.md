# **Hag Dragon**

*“It bore the silhouette of a dragon, but the likeness ended there. In the center of its brow yawned a singular, lidless eye of burning amber, while its true eyes lay withered and blind at the sides of its skull. Its breath did not burn, nor did it freeze—it simply reft the years from us, leaving my comrades grey-haired and brittle-boned in a matter of heartbeats.”*

---

### **Overview**
A **Hag Dragon** is a horrific, draconic abomination born of dark fey magic and twisted weave corruption. Unlike true dragons, they do not belong to the natural order of Telara. Instead, they are the creation of powerful hag covens who seek to forge ultimate guardians and weapons. They are temporal predators, defying the passage of time by consuming the lifespans—the chronal filaments—of other living beings.

---

### **Lore & Nature**

#### **Fey-Warped Creation**
Hag dragons do not hatch naturally. They are created when a powerful hag coven steals a true dragon egg of any variety and subjects it to an agonizing, days-long ritual. Using rusted shears and blackened threads, the hags weave curses of decay, spite, and dark fey energy into the shell. When the egg hatches, the hatchling is born as a mottled brown-and-yellow creature, bearing only vestigial remnants of its original draconic heritage (such as shape or horn structure) but thoroughly remade into a creature of fey malignancy.

#### **The Timeless Cycle**
Unlike normal dragons who grow stronger with the natural passage of time, a hag dragon is metaphysically "frozen" at birth. It would remain a wyrmling forever if left to time alone. To grow and age, it must hunt and feed on the lifespans of others. By using its **Time Inhalation** breath weapon, the dragon literally rips the chronal filaments from a victim’s timeline, absorbing their remaining years to rapidly accelerate its own growth. 

Hags often attempt to keep hag dragons young and weak to control them easily. However, if a hag dragon manages to feed unchecked, it grows into an incredibly dangerous adult or ancient form in a matter of days or weeks, frequently turning on and consuming its creators.

#### **The Draconic Coven**
Though hag dragons are solitary by nature, they possess a strange resonance. If three hag dragons come together and agree to cooperate, they can form a **Draconic Coven**. Just as mortal hags pool their magical essence, a coven of hag dragons can cast world-shattering rituals and weave reality-warping spells by pooling their collective timelines, making them capable of bringing down entire kingdoms. 

Additionally, a powerful coven leader like [[Baba Lysaga]] can weave a **Chronal Link** between them, allowing them to pool their power and project spectral echoes of one another to cast coven spells even when separated across different fane sites, as long as the ley-line nodes remain corrupted.

### **Lair Actions & Regional Effects**
Hag dragons make their lairs in the dimmest, most decayed places: acrid marshes, hollowed-out deadwood forests, and flooded ruins. They often share a lair with the hags who created them.

#### **Lair Actions**
On initiative count 20 (losing initiative ties), the dragon takes a lair action to cause one of the following effects; the dragon can’t use the same effect two rounds in a row:
*   **Strangling Brambles**: Thorny, blackened vines jut out from the muck. Up to 3 creatures the dragon can see must succeed on a DC 13 Dexterity saving throw or become grappled (escape DC 13). A grappled creature takes 4 (1d8) piercing damage at the start of each of its turns.
*   **Visions of Fading Time**: All creatures in a 15-foot area the dragon can see are filled with a sudden, overwhelming despair as they see visions of their own lives being ripped away. They must succeed on a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw or take 7 (2d6) psychic damage.
*   **Bioluminescent Spores**: A giant, rotting mushroom cap explodes. A single target within 60 feet must succeed on a DC 15 Dexterity saving throw or have sticky, glowing spores cling to them. Attacks made against the spore-covered creature have advantage for 1 minute, or until a creature uses an action to scrape them off.

#### **Regional Effects**
The region containing a legendary hag dragon’s lair is warped by its chronal-draining magic, producing the following effects:
*   **Chronal Decay**: Living creatures within 12 miles of the lair age at twice the normal rate, with the lost lifespan automatically feeding into the dragon to sustain its immortality.
*   **The Cyclopean Curse**: Many small wild animals (frogs, snakes, ravens) within 3 miles of the lair mutate, growing a single massive eye in the center of their skull. The dragon can use an action to see through the eye of any such mutated beast.
*   **Amber Haze**: The area within 6 miles of the lair is lightly obscured by a thick, smelling orange haze.

If the dragon dies, these effects fade. The haze disperses within 24 hours, creatures return to aging at their normal rate, and any stolen lifespans are immediately returned, though animal mutations remain permanent.

### **The Coven Spellcasting & Chronal Link**
Normally, when all three members of a Hag Dragon Coven are within 60 feet of one another, they can cast the spells listed below by using an action, sharing spell slots. 

Under the **Chronal Link** ritual enacted by [[Baba Lysaga]], they can also channel their coven magic remotely when bound to Barovia's three Fanes (Forest, Swamp, and Mountain). 
*   **Active Chronal Links**: If a single dragon is engaged at a Fane and the other two Fanes remain corrupted, the dragon can project spectral echoes of her sisters, gaining access to coven spells up to **5th level**.
*   **At the Swamp Fane (Berez)**: As the coven's leader resides here, the defender (Vervain) can channel spellcasting up to **7th level** if the outer Fanes are active (or up to **9th level** if all three dragons are physically brought together).
*   **Severing the Link**: If the players cleanse a Fane, the dragon bound to it is cut from the link. If both outer Fanes are cleansed, the remaining dragon can no longer cast coven spells and must rely solely on its individual statistics.

#### **Coven Spells**
*   **1st level (4 slots)**: *Fog Cloud, Hideous Laughter*
*   **2nd level (3 slots)**: *Crown of Madness, Darkness, Locate Object, Wither and Bloom*
*   **3rd level (3 slots)**: *Animate Dead, Counterspell*
*   **4th level (3 slots)**: *Arcane Eye, Confusion, Charm Monster*
*   **5th level (3 slots)**: *Animate Objects, Cone of Cold, Mislead*
*   **6th level (2 slots)**: *Eyebite, Move Earth*
*   **7th level (2 slots)**: *Finger of Death, Forcecage*
*   **8th level (1 slot)**: *Control Weather, Horrid Wilting, Maddening Darkness*
*   **9th level (1 slot)**: *Imprisonment, Mass Polymorph, Time Stop, True Polymorph, Wish*
