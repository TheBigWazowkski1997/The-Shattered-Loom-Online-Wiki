# **Corpse Collector**

*“A colossal, creaking juggernaut of rusted iron plate, spiked grapples, and petrified bone. Impaled upon its armored back are the weeping, animate corpses of soldiers and refugees, whose agonizing moans empower its crushing advance.”*

---

### **Overview**
A **Corpse Collector** is a terrifying siege-construct forged through foul necromancy and blood-tempered iron. Originally designed by the dread warlords of Falkovnia and the necromancers of Darkon to harvest battlefields, these lumbering behemoths sweep across the Dead Fields and the fringes of the Domains of Dread, plucking corpses and wounded survivors alike to impale upon the spiked racks mounted along their carapaces.

---

### **Lore & Nature**

#### **Soul-Fueled Harvesters**
Unlike typical stone or iron golems, a Corpse Collector is not powered by an elemental spirit. Instead, it is animated by the agonized souls of those impaled upon its chassis. The construct feeds on their vital panic and necrotic decay, converting their suffering into kinetic force and poisonous graveyard miasma.

#### **Corpses of the Damned**
The bodies impaled upon a Corpse Collector do not rest peacefully. The dark magic animating the construct partially revives them as paralyzed, animate ghouls. In combat, the collector can cause these impaled corpses to animate simultaneously, clawing at nearby foes or projecting terrifying wails of psychic sorrow.
