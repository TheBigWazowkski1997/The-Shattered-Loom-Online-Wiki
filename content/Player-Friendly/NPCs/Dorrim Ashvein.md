# Dorrim Ashvein (The Crucible Champion)

![[attachments/dorrim_ashvein.jpg]]

* **Race**: Dwarf (Mountain Dwarf)
* **Faction**: The Gilded Crucible (formerly The Ironhand Brotherhood)
* **Role**: Pit Fighter / Champion of the Smelting Pits
* **Appearance**: Bulky and muscular, with a completely bald head and a long, thick, braided beard held together by iron rings. His weathered skin is covered in deep battle scars and glowing, gold-tinted runic tattoos that outline his massive arms and chest.
* **Personality**: Cruel, arrogant, and dirty-fighting. He taunts his opponents mercilessly and respects only gold and brute force. He views his defection from the Ironhand Brotherhood as a simple "business upgrade."

---
## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Dorrim is a formidable combatant who excels in close-quarters fighting, especially in arenas with environmental hazards like molten trenches. He fights dirty, using soot, spiked knuckles, and the environment to his advantage.

* **Dwarven Resilience**: Dorrim has advantage on saving throws against poison, and resistance against poison damage.
* **Arena Combatant**: Dorrim has advantage on Strength (Athletics) checks to grapple or shove a creature.
* **Brutal Strikes**: When Dorrim hits a creature with a melee weapon attack, the attack deals one extra die of the weapon's damage (included in the attacks).
* **Multiattack**: Dorrim makes three attacks: two with his Runic Battleaxe and one with his Spiked Knuckles.
* **Runic Battleaxe**: Strikes with devastating cleaving attacks using masterwork runic weaponry.
* **Spiked Knuckles**: one target. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a saving throw or bleed. While bleeding, the creature takes significant damage at the start of each of its turns. A creature can end the bleeding by using an action to make a saving throw Wisdom (Medicine) check or receiving magical healing.
* **Soot Throw (Recharge 5–6)**: Dorrim throws a handful of hot soot and metal shavings in a 15-foot cone. Each creature in that area must succeed on a saving throw or be blinded until the end of its next turn.
* **Shove to the Brim**: Dorrim attempts to shove a creature he is grappling or is adjacent to. The target must succeed on a saving throw or be pushed 5 feet. (Used to push players into molten trenches).

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Defection:** Dorrim was once a highly respected enforcer for the Ironhand Brotherhood, trained by Kazrik Goldmantle himself. He defected to the Gilded Crucible after being offered a mountain of gold and the position of champion in their illegal smelting pits.
* **The Fight :** During the showdown at the Ironhand Pits, Dorrim taunts Daine about "growing soft" after leaving Metallos. He will use every dirty trick in the book, including using the spectators to gain crowd favor .
* ** **Crucial Intel**: ** If spared after defeat, Dorrim spits blood, laughs, and reveals that both the Moonveil Accord and the Bloodchain Horde were funded by the **Cult of the Broken Thread** to hijack the Molten Veins.
