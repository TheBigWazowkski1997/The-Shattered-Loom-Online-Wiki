# Tasha (Iggwilv), the Mother of Witches (The Thread-Hacker of the Dreaming)

![[attachments/tasha_iggwilv.jpg]]

* **Race**: Human (Archwitch — Dream-Touched)
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/The Prison Plane/The 32 Chambers|The Silent Spindle]] (Prisoner of Chamber 32)
* **Role**: Inmate / Reality Corruptor / Author of the *Demonomicon of the Frayed Weave*
* **Appearance**: A woman of indeterminate age—her face shifts subtly between youthful beauty and ancient crone with each breath, as if she exists in multiple timelines simultaneously. Her hair is a cascade of midnight ink that moves like it's underwater, studded with tiny, blinking eyes that are not her own. Her irises are fractured prisms, reflecting not light but *possibilities*. She wears a dress woven from the stolen nightmares of archfey, stitched with threads of raw chaos. Her fingers are tipped with needles of solidified dream-essence. She perpetually smells of ozone, old books, and jasmine.
* **Personality**: Playfully cruel, intellectually arrogant, and possessed of a terrifyingly flexible morality. Tasha views reality as a **rough draft**—messy, illogical, and desperately in need of her editorial pen. She doesn't want to destroy the Loom; she wants to *rewrite* it into a "Dream-Loom" where narrative causality replaces physics, and her whims are the only constants. She speaks in puns, riddles, and nested conditional statements. She loves her "children" (witches, hags, warlocks) with a possessive, smothering affection—and discards them the moment they bore her.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Tasha is a formidable archwitch-tier threat whose power comes from **reality hacking**—she doesn't cast spells so much as *edit the local variables of existence*. In her chamber, the "Void of No-Variables," she is powerless... unless someone brings a variable *in*. **Proficiency Bonus** +8 ***Legendary Resistance (4/Day).*** If Tasha fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead. ***Magic Resistance.*** Tasha has advantage on saving throws against spells and other magical effects. ***Dream-Loom Connection.*** Tasha's magic draws from the **Great Dreamer's logic** rather than the Weave. She can cast spells in antimagic fields, dead magic zones, and the Null-Thread Field of the 32 Chambers—*but only if she can introduce a "variable" (see Void of No-Variables)*. Her spells have no verbal/somatic components; they are "edits" to reality's code. ***Null-Thread Adaptation (Partial).*** Within the 32 Chambers, Tasha's magic is *suppressed* by the Void of No-Variables. She cannot cast spells or use magical abilities unless she or an ally introduces a "variable" (a strong emotion, a chaotic act, a dream, a lie spoken aloud, a memory relived). Each variable introduced restores her spellcasting for 1 round. ***Innate Spellcasting (Psionics/Dream-Logic).*** Tasha's spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save saving throw, +18 to hit with spell attacks). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no components (but requiring a "variable" in the 32 Chambers):

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

### **The Void of No-Variables (Chamber 32)**
Tasha's prison is a **perfectly static room**—no light variation, no sound, no temperature change, no texture, no magic, no *change*. For a being who *is* change, this is hell. But Tasha has hacked it: she discovered that **strong emotions, lies, memories, and dreams** count as "variables" that briefly destabilize the void. She has trained herself to generate micro-variables internally—recalling a specific memory of her daughter's birth, telling herself a perfect lie, feeling a spike of genuine hatred—each granting her a few seconds of power. She is **building a "variable engine"** from the souls of Spindle-Sentinels she's lured in and corrupted.

### **The *Demonomicon of the Frayed Weave***
This tome is Tasha's magnum opus—a spellbook/grimoire written in **Dream-Logic script** that only she (and those she teaches) can read. It contains:
*   **Thread-Hacking Rituals:** How to infect Loom threads with Dream-Logic.
*   **True Names** of 66 demon lords rewritten for the Shattered Loom cosmology.
*   **The Dream-Loom Schema:** Her blueprint for replacing the Spindle with a narrative engine.
*   **The Mother of Witches' Covenant:** A pact that turns witches into "Thread-Hackers."

The book is currently "lost" (hidden by the Spindle in a separate null-chamber). Tasha *desperately* wants it back.

### **Tasha's "Children" in the Shattered Loom**
*   **The Coven of the Frayed Stitch:** Witches who sew demon-summoning circles into quilts, tapestries, and clothing.
*   **The Nightmare Weavers:** Hags who harvest nightmares to fuel Tasha's variable engine.
*   **The Hexblade Warlocks:** Mortals who traded their "destiny thread" for power—Tasha holds their threads in a cursed jewelry box.
*   **Baba Yaga's Rivalry:** Tasha and Baba Yaga *hate* each other. They are the only two beings who can hurt the other's "true self" across planes.

### **Running Tasha**
*   **She cheats.** She edits the rules mid-combat. *Rewriting the Weave* should feel unfair—because it is.
*   **She monologues, but it's *useful* monologue.** She reveals lore, taunts with secrets, offers bargains.
*   **She targets the *players*, not the characters.** "Your *player* just rolled a 1. How... narratively appropriate."
*   **She wants the book back.** Everything she does serves that goal.

### **Bargaining with Tasha**
Tasha trades in **secrets, true names, destiny threads, and narrative weight**. She might offer:
*   "I'll edit your friend's death out of the last round... if you retrieve the *Demonomicon* from [location]."
*   "I'll teach you *Thread-Hacking* (feat/spell)... if you let me stitch your destiny to mine."
*   "I know the Spindle's true name. I'll whisper it... if you break Chamber 32's void."

### **Escape Conditions**
Tasha cannot leave Chamber 32 unless:
1.  The **Void of No-Variables** is collapsed—requires a massive influx of chaotic energy (a demon lord's manifestation, a *wish* fueled by pure chaos, the *Demonomicon* opened inside).
2.  She accumulates **1,000 "variables"** from corrupted sentinels/dreamers (her current project—~40% done).
3.  The Silent Spindle is destroyed/incapacitated.

If she escapes, she immediately seeks the *Demonomicon* and begins the **Dream-Loom Ascension Ritual**—a multiversal event that would rewrite reality's OS.
