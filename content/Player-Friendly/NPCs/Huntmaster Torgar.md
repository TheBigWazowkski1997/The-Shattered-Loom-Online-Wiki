# Huntmaster Torgar (The Apex Stalker)

![[attachments/huntmaster_torgar.jpg]]

* **Race**: Longtooth Shifter
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Wild Balance/Plane of Wild Balance Verdant Nexus|Viridspire Hunters]] / Primal Fang Leatherworks
* **Role**: Chief Armorer of Viridspire, Master Monster-Hunter
* **Appearance**: A broad-shouldered, rugged shifter standing six-foot-two, with coarse bristling brown fur, feral amber eyes, and prominent predatory canines. His weathered torso and arms are crisscrossed with claw and bite scars from countless apex predators. He wears a custom breastplate crafted from black ironwood and reinforced with giant crocodile scales, carrying a massive recurve longbow strung with manticore sinew.
* **Personality**: Instinctual, fierce, but deeply respectful of the predatory balance. Torgar hunts not for slaughter or amusement, but to maintain the equilibrium between predator and prey. He detests wasteful poachers and cowardly traps, believing that every beast deserves a clean, honorable death in fair contest.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Torgar is a formidable apex ranger and tracker who commands ambush tactics, feral shifting, and lethal precision archery.

* **Shifting (Bonus Action, 1/Short Rest)**: Torgar shifts for 1 minute, gaining 12 temporary hit points. While shifted, he can make a bite attack as a bonus action (dealing 1d6 + 3 piercing damage).
* **Canopy Ambush**: During his first turn in combat, Torgar has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that hasn't acted yet. If he hits, the attack deals an extra 14 (4d6) damage.
* **Woodland Stride**: Moving through nonmagical difficult terrain costs Torgar no extra movement.
* **Multiattack**: Torgar makes three attacks with his *Manticore Sinew Longbow* or three melee attacks with his *Hunting Machete*.
* **Manticore Sinew Longbow**: *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target.
* **Hunting Machete (Shortsword)**: Unleashes swift, precision strikes infused with psychic or elemental energy.
* **Volley (Recharge 5–6)**: Torgar fires a hail of arrows at all creatures in a 20-foot-radius sphere within 120 feet. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw, taking significant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
* **Predator's Evasion**: When targeted by an area effect that allows a Dexterity saving throw to take only half damage, Torgar instead takes no damage on a success, and only half damage on a failure.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Monster Armor Crafting**: Torgar is the key artisan for players wishing to harvest scales, carapaces, and hides from slain monsters to create customized magical armor and cloaks.
* **Poaching Nemesis**: He will recruit the party to track down and dismantle illegal poaching rings operating out of The Deep Undergrowth.
