# High Priest Aethelgard (Keeper of the Conflux)

![[attachments/high_priest_aethelgard.jpg]]

* **Race**: Human (Solar-touched)
* **Faction**: Stewards of Lumari / The Radiant Conflux
* **Role**: Supreme Cleric of the Luminara Flow, Master of the Conflux Crucible
* **Appearance**: An imposing, broad-chested priest standing six-foot-four with sun-bronzed skin, a neatly trimmed white beard, and warm golden eyes that radiate quiet authority. He is clad in heavy alabaster-and-gold plate armor beneath cream-colored ceremonial vestments embroidered with the sunburst of unity. He wields *Solar Aegis*, a massive mace forged from solidified radiant glass that casts a warm glow wherever he walks.
* **Personality**: Resolute, dignified, and unwavering in his faith in cosmic reconciliation. Aethelgard is not a naive idealist; he understands that peace must often be defended with righteous martial might. He is stern toward planar warmongers and fiends who seek to poison the wellspring of harmony, but endlessly forgiving to repentant souls.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Aethelgard is a formidable holy warrior and radiant spellcaster who purges corruption, shields allies, and smites darkness with blinding celestial power.

* **Divine Eminence**: As a bonus action, Aethelgard can expend a spell slot to cause his melee weapon attacks to magically deal an extra significant damage to a target on a hit.
* **Sunlit Aura**: Hostile creatures within 10 feet of Aethelgard cannot benefit from invisibility or obscurement, and take 5 radiant damage when entering the aura or starting their turn there.
* **Spellcasting**: Aethelgard is a 9th-level spellcaster. His spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save saving throw, +9 to hit with spell attacks). He has the following cleric spells prepared:.
* **Multiattack**: Aethelgard makes two melee attacks with *Solar Aegis*.
* **Solar Aegis (Mace)**: one target. If the target is a fiend or undead, it takes an extra significant damage.
* **Radiant Cleansing (Recharge 5–6)**: Aethelgard unleashes a 30-foot burst of divine light centered on himself. Hostile creatures in the area must make a saving throw, taking significant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. Friendly creatures in the area regain 22 (4d8 + 4) hit points and are cleansed of all poisons and diseases.
* **Shield of Accord**: When an attacker hits an ally within 30 feet of Aethelgard, Aethelgard can summon an illusory barrier of golden light, granting the ally a +4 bonus to AC against that attack.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Purifying Quests**: Aethelgard commissions adventuring parties to eliminate planar rifts, eradicate abyssal blights, and protect pilgrims traveling along the Luminara Flow.
* **Moral Dilemma**: When faced with dissidents in the Frayed Undercroft, Aethelgard struggles between enforcing the sacred mandate of unity and respecting mortal autonomy. He will reward parties who find peaceful, diplomatic solutions rather than bloodshed.
