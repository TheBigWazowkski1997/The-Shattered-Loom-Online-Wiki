# Vorath Greedeater (The Devouring Maw)

* **Race**: Ancient Dragon-Construct / Planar Dragon (Avarice-bound)
* **Faction**: Master of Emerald Maw, Plunderborn Raiders
* **Role**: Tyrant of Emerald Maw, Scourge of the Gilded Wastes
* **Appearance**: A colossal serpentine dragon whose emerald-scaled hide is encrusted with thousands of fused gemstones, golden torcs, and plundered crown jewels. Where flesh meets stone, its belly glows with the sickening emerald luminescence of the city's Devouring Core. Its eyes are faceted emeralds burning with cold, predatory intelligence, and its teeth are jagged pillars of diamond and obsidian.
* **Personality**: Insatiable, megalomaniacal, and perpetually paranoid. Vorath views all matter, magic, and life across the multiverse as property that has simply not yet entered his hoard. He speaks in a booming, grinding voice that sounds like tons of coins shifting under a landslide, demanding tribute from any mortal bold or foolish enough to approach his throne.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Vorath Greedeater is a formidable planar dragon boss with devastating breath attacks that consume items and turn victims into crystalline treasure, coupled with legendary actions.

* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Vorath fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
* **Hoard-Scent**: Vorath pinpoints the precise location of any magic item or hoard of 100+ gp within 1 mile.
* **Devouring Aura**: At the start of each of Vorath's turns, each creature within 10 feet of him takes significant damage as the ambient gravity tugs at their possessions and life essence.
* **Multiattack**: Vorath can use his Frightful Presence. He then makes three attacks: one with his Bite and two with his Claws.
* **Bite**: one target. If the target is a creature, Vorath can choose to swallow up to 100 gp of non-magical coins or one unattended item the creature holds.
* **Claw**: Notable combat maneuver and signature technique.
* **Tail**: Notable combat maneuver and signature technique.
* **Frightful Presence**: Each creature of Vorath's choice within 120 feet of him and aware of him must succeed on a saving throw or become frightened for 1 minute.
* **Emerald Crystallization Breath (Recharge 5–6)**: Vorath exhales a 60-foot cone of emerald disintegration fire. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw, taking 54 (12d8) force and fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. If this damage reduces a creature to 0 HP, it is instantly petrified into a solid emerald-veined gold statue.
* **Detect**: Vorath makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
* **Tail Attack**: Vorath makes a tail attack.
* **Hoard Crush (Costs 2 Actions)**: Vorath slams the ground, spraying a hail of gemstone shrapnel in a 20-foot radius. Each creature in the area must succeed on a saving throw or take significant damage and be knocked prone.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Devouring Engine**: Vorath is symbiotically linked to the locomotive engine of the walking city of Emerald Maw. If the city starves of treasure, Vorath experiences agonizing hunger.
* **Negotiating with Greed**: He cannot be persuaded by kindness, morality, or treaties. He can only be bargained with through unique, irreplaceable treasures—artifacts, divine relics, or true names of planar archons.
* **Plot Seeds**:
* Vorath sets Emerald Maw on a collision course with a mortal settlement or planar haven, and the party must sabotage the Devouring Core from within.
* The dragon offers the party safe passage or an ancient planar key if they plunder a guarded vault in Aurous Grasp and deliver the master chest to his maw.
