# Key NPCs - The Ironbound Covenant

The following figures make up the high command, judicial inquisitors, diplomatic assets, and secret inner circle of [[../Factions/The Ironbound Covenant|The Ironbound Covenant]].

* * *

## Supreme Command

The singular authority guiding the endless crusade against [[Gods/God of the Plane of Infinite Hunger/Vorzath, the Maw Eternal|Vorzath, the Maw Eternal]].

* [[Urdakael, the Unbroken|Urdakael, the Unbroken]] — Forge-Herald of the Covenant, Living Doctrine, and Supreme Commander

* * *

## The Sealed Conclave (The Seven Seats)

The secret inner order that governs the Covenant's hidden strategies and guards the truth behind the First Forging.

* **Seat I — The Tempered**: [[Urdakael, the Unbroken|Urdakael, the Unbroken]] — Forge-Herald; supreme field authority
* **Seat II — The Annealed**: [[Thessivex the Still|Thessivex the Still]] — Master of Intelligence and Planar Strategist
* **Seat III — The Quenched**: [[Archivist Mauren Coldsteel|Archivist Mauren Coldsteel]] — Keeper of the Bound Archive and First-Forged Chronicler
* **Seat IV — The Alloyed**: [[Vexiryn the Unbowed|Vexiryn the Unbowed]] — Covenant Diplomatic Envoy to the Consortium and Pale Lodge
* **Seat V — The Drawn**: [[Halverak the Shorn|Halverak the Shorn]] — Grand Paladin of the Mandate and Conclave Enforcer
* **Seat VI — The Cast**: [[Norath of the Leaving Flame|Norath of the Leaving Flame]] — High Forge Judge, Theologian, and Administrator of Severance
* **Seat VII — The Questioned**: [[Aeldris the Dim|Aeldris the Dim]] — The Conclave Doubter and Voice of Conscience
