# Riftwalker Vael (The Horizon Strider)

![[attachments/riftwalker_vael.jpg]]

* **Race**: Shadar-kai (Elf)
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Fragmented memories/Plane of Fragmented Memories - The Shardlands#3. Shatterpoint: The Cracked City|Riftwalkers of Shatterpoint]] / The Void-Strider’s Supply
* **Role**: Veteran Temporal Scout, Master of the Evershifting Warrens
* **Appearance**: An athletic, weathered shadar-kai with chalk-white skin, raven-black hair braided with silver timeline wires, and severe cheekbones scarred by planar glass. He is draped in dark grey phase-silk climbing harnesses bristling with adamantine pitons, gravity carabinets, and dimensional compasses. Twin glowing crystalline shortswords hang crossed at his lower back.
* **Personality**: Taciturn, pragmatic, and unshakeable. Having survived dozens of expeditions into collapsed timelines and phasing reality rifts, Vael has seen companions erased from existence in the blink of an eye. He does not panic, never rushes into an unmapped tunnel, and treats temporal mechanics with the lethal respect of a bomb-disposal expert.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Vael is a formidable temporal skirmisher who phases through solid obstacles, teleports across dimensional fissures, and strikes with force-shearing blades.

* **Blessing of the Raven Queen (3/Day)**: As a bonus action, Vael magically teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see. He appears in a flurry of black feathers and gains resistance to all damage until the start of his next turn.
* **Phase Step**: Vael can move through other creatures and solid objects up to 5 feet thick as if they were difficult terrain. If he ends his turn inside an object, he takes significant damage and is shunted to the nearest unoccupied space.
* **Temporal Flank**: Vael has advantage on attack rolls against any creature that has taken damage from a temporal anomaly or force spell within the last round.
* **Multiattack**: Vael makes three attacks: two with his *Phase-Glass Shortsword* and one with his *Temporal Cleaver*.
* **Phase-Glass Shortsword**: one target. Hits ignore physical armor bonuses (+2 to hit against targets wearing nonmagical metal armor).
* **Temporal Cleaver**: one target. If the target is concentrating on a spell, it makes the concentration check with disadvantage.
* **Spatial Tear Javelin**: *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target. The target must succeed on a saving throw or be pushed 15 feet into a temporal pocket and knocked prone.
* **Slip Through Time**: When targeted by a ranged spell or weapon attack, Vael flickers half a second into the past; the attack automatically misses, and Vael can teleport up to 10 feet.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Warren Guide:** Vael is the ultimate NPC guide for parties attempting to venture into the deepest, unmapped chambers of the Evershifting Warrens. He charges 100 gp a day or a 20% cut of whatever temporal artifacts are recovered.
* **The Erased Companion:** Vael remembers an adventuring companion named Sarah who was swallowed by a temporal fissure years ago—yet no records of her existence remain anywhere in the multiverse. He is obsessed with finding the divergent shard where her timeline still exists.
* **Tactical Advice:** Before entering phasing terrain, Vael warns players never to sleep without planting a dimensional piton into bedrock, lest they wake up separated by centuries.
