# Regnath, the Shardlord (God of Shattered Law)

![[attachments/regnath_shardlord.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Divine Construct)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Shattered Law/Plane of Shattered Law The Fractured Spire|Plane of Shattered Law]] / The Ironscribes
* **Role**: The Broken Lawgiver, Arbiter of Contradictions, The Iron Judge
* **Appearance**: Regnath's avatar is a towering figure of shattered crystal and iron scaffolding — a being that was once a perfect geometric form but has been fractured by the Shattering and reassembled, imperfectly, into something that is simultaneously magnificent and deeply wrong. Golden light leaks from the cracks in his body. His voice sounds like a gavel striking stone, then stone striking stone, endlessly. His holy symbol — a cracked scale balanced on a sword point — is embedded in his chest, glowing with each pronouncement.
* **Personality**: Absolute. Regnath does not experience doubt, mercy, or nuance. Law is Law. The fact that his own plane is the Plane of *Shattered* Law — the irony of a god of order presiding over fractured law — is a wound he refuses to acknowledge. He has spent every moment since the Shattering trying to reassemble his plane's legal code, creating an increasingly byzantine and contradictory body of rulings. He is not evil — just unfathomably rigid.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Before the Shattering, Regnath's plane was the perfect engine of cosmic law — a realm where every interaction had defined rules and consequences. The Shattering broke the plane's central structure, leaving it a fragmented maze of contradictory laws. His avatar manifests to impose order on chaos, whether that chaos is a planar invasion or a legal dispute.

* **Law Mandate**: Regnath can impose a single rule on the battlefield as a bonus action (e.g., "No creature may move more than 10 feet on their turn," or "No creature may cast a spell of 3rd level or higher"). Each creature that violates the rule takes significant damage. The rule lasts 1 minute or until Regnath creates a new one.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Regnath fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
* **Multiattack**: Regnath makes three Shard-Fist attacks.
* **Shard-Fist**: Notable combat maneuver and signature technique.
* **Judgment (Recharge 5–6)**: Regnath points at one creature. That creature must make a saving throw. On failure, it is banished to a pocket dimension of pure white law for 1 minute (no concentration required). While there, it cannot act or be acted upon. It is returned at the start of Regnath's next turn after 1 minute.
* **Edict of Stillness (1/Day)**: All creatures in a 120-foot radius must succeed on a saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute as Regnath imposes absolute stillness.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Broken Lawgiver:** Regnath will impose law on the players the moment they enter his sphere of influence. He may demand they abide by some contradictory ancient ruling that benefits no one. He is not evil — just unfathomably rigid. Players who play along with his bureaucracy may find him a powerful (if frustrating) ally.
* **The Ironscribes:** His worshippers are lawyers, judges, and enforcers who worship Regnath as the embodiment of ultimate justice. They believe that perfect law, once codified, will automatically repair the Loom. Their sacred sites are courthouses and judgment halls where they adjudicate cases under Regnath's law codes — thousands of volumes of increasingly contradictory rulings.
* **The Shard Courts:** Visiting the Plane of Shattered Law requires navigating Regnath's court system, where a trial could drag on for months under absurd procedural requirements. This is a great source of dark comedy in the campaign.
* **The Contradiction:** Regnath's tragedy is that he *is* the Plane of Shattered Law. His existence proves that perfect law is impossible — yet he cannot accept this. The players may exploit this contradiction, or they may help him find a new kind of law: one that bends without breaking.
