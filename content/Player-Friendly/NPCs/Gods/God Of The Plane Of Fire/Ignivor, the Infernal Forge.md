# Ignivor, the Infernal Forge (God of Fire and Creation)

![[attachments/ignivor_infernal_forge.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Greater Deity (Elemental Divine)
* **Faction**: [[../../../Planes of Existence/Elemental Planes/Plane of Fire/Plane Of Fire The Everblaze Expanse|Plane of Fire]] / The Ashforged
* **Role**: The Destroyer-Rebuilder, The First Weaponsmith, The Volatile Flame
* **Appearance**: Ignivor's avatar is a towering figure of living magma and blackened iron — his skin flows like lava, cooling and cracking with each movement to reveal brighter fire beneath. His hair is a plume of white-hot flame, and his eyes are twin furnaces. He wields a massive hammer wreathed in eternal fire, and his footsteps leave molten footprints that take decades to cool. His holy symbol — a hammer surrounded by blazing embers — burns at the center of his chest.
* **Personality**: Passionate, temperamental, and driven by bursts of creativity and rage. Ignivor views destruction as a precursor to growth and believes in the necessity of purging imperfection through fire. He claims to have forged the first weapons used in the War of the Loom, but his creations often spiral out of control. He is neither good nor evil — he is the fire that clears the forest so new growth can begin. He respects those who can withstand his heat and despises those who seek safety in stagnation.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Ignivor is volatility given form. His avatar manifests where fire burns hottest and creation is most desperate. In combat, he is an unrelenting offensive force — he does not defend, he overwhelms.

* **Molten Aura (30 ft.)**: Any creature that starts its turn within 30 feet of Ignivor takes significant damage. Melee attackers take significant damage on a successful hit.
* **Forge Master**: Ignivor can cast *Fabricate*, *Wall of Fire*, and *Heat Metal* at will. He can shape metal and stone as if by *Stone Shape* and *Fabricate* combined, but only while in contact with flame or lava.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Ignivor fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
* **Multiattack**: Ignivor makes three Infernal Hammer attacks.
* **Infernal Hammer**: one target. If the target is an object or structure, it takes double damage.
* **Eruption (Recharge 5–6)**: Ignivor strikes the ground, causing a volcanic eruption in a 60-foot radius. Each creature in the area must make a saving throw, taking severe damage on a failure, or half on a success. The area becomes difficult terrain as magma cools.
* **Reforge (1/Day)**: Ignivor targets one metal object or construct within 120 feet. He can reshape it into any other metal object of equal or lesser mass, or repair it to full hit points. If the target is a construct, it regains 100 HP and gains the benefits of *Greater Restoration*.
* **Hammer Swing (1 Action)**: Ignivor makes one Infernal Hammer attack.
* **Lava Surge (2 Actions)**: Ignivor spews lava in a 30-foot line. Each creature in the line must succeed on a saving throw or take significant damage and be coated in cooling magma (restrained until effort as an action).
* **Spark of Creation (3 Actions)**: Ignivor creates a temporary magic item — a weapon or piece of armor of rare quality or lower — that lasts for 1 hour. The item is forged from his own essence and deals an extra 2d6 fire damage.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Ashforged:** His followers are artisans, blacksmiths, and warriors who embrace chaos to fuel their creativity. They perform fiery rituals to forge items of power, often inscribing them with Ignivor's runic blessing. Their temples are forges surrounded by ever-burning pyres. The **Radicals** among them believe in burning away societal structures, leading to destructive cults that raze towns and cities to "cleanse imperfection."
* **The War of the Loom:** Ignivor claims to have forged the first weapons used in the War of the Loom. Whether true or not, his artifacts are scattered across the multiverse — blades that burn with eternal flame, armor that repairs itself in fire, hammers that can shatter planar barriers.
* **Relationship with Kaldryn:** Ignivor and Kaldryn (Eternal Artisan) are ideological rivals. Kaldryn seeks perfection through precision; Ignivor seeks perfection through trial by fire. They have collaborated in the past — some of the Loom's strongest threads were forged in Ignivor's fire and woven on Kaldryn's anvil — but their methods clash.
* **Using Ignivor in Play:** He is a force of creative destruction. Players who seek him out may gain powerful forged items, but the price is always a trial by fire — literal or metaphorical. He respects boldness and punishes hesitation. A visit to his plane is a gauntlet of volcanic hazards and combat encounters, ending at his forge where he judges the supplicant's worth.
