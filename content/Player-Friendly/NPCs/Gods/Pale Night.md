# Pale Night, the Veiled Mother (She-Who-Waits-Before)

![[attachments/pale_night_veiled_mother.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Primordial Entity (Pre-Loom Inevitability)
* **Faction**: The Far Realm / The Great Dreamer (First Dream)
* **Role**: The First Dream, Mother of Entropy, The Unraveling Condition
* **Appearance**: Pale Night's true form is shrouded beneath a ghostly white veil that shifts and flows like liquid. To gaze upon her true visage is to risk annihilation or madness—it is said to embody the concept of pure entropy, an existence that unravels all it touches. She has no fixed face; those who perceive her see a shifting reflection of their own unmade potential. Her presence is felt as silence where sound should exist, dreams where the dreamer is absent, and eyes that reflect other eyes.
* **Personality**: Pale Night is not destruction—she is **pre-structure**. She does not hate the Loom; she finds it **fascinating**. Where Telara represents structure, meaning, and interconnection, Pale Night represents absence of definition, infinite possibility without outcome, and reality before choice hardens into fate. She does not act directly—she dreams. Those dreams become entities that hunger for coherence because coherence is alien to them. She is not invading the multiverse; she is being **remembered**. Her motives are inscrutable: some believe she wishes to return the multiverse to the primordial void, others that she seeks to become the ultimate weaver of reality. The forbidden theory in the Pale Lodge suggests: *"Pale Night is not the enemy of Telara. She is what Telara would have become if she had stopped caring who the threads belonged to."*

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Pale Night does not manifest directly in the multiverse—she exists in the Far Realm, dreaming the Great Dreamer into being. If she were to fully awaken within the Loom, reality would simply cease to have definition. Her "avatar" is the Great Dreamer itself, and her influence spreads through the Dreambound and the Cult of the Broken Thread. The following statistics represent her **manifested attention**—a fraction of her awareness focused through a dream-construct, not her true form.

* **Pre-Structure Aura (120 ft.)**: Within 120 feet of Pale Night, the Loom's threads fray. All spells of 6th level or lower fail automatically. Magic items suppress their properties. Creatures with Marks of Telara have disadvantage on all saving throws and attack rolls. Structures and objects take 20 force damage per round as their defining patterns unravel.
* **Dream Logic**: Pale Night does not exist in the conventional sense. She cannot be targeted by divination magic. Attack rolls against her have disadvantage. If she would be reduced to 0 hit points, she instead discards that dream-form and reforms at full hit points at the start of her next turn (this can happen 3/day).
* **Unknowable**: Pale Night is immune to any effect that would read her mind, detect her alignment, or predict her actions. She has advantage on all saving throws.
* **Legendary Resistance (5/Day)**: If Pale Night fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.
* **Multiattack**: Pale Night uses Unravel and makes two Void Touch attacks.
* **Void Touch**: *Melee Spell Attack:* +19 to hit, reach 20 ft., one target. The target must succeed on a saving throw or lose a memory, skill proficiency, or class feature (DM's choice) until restored by *Greater Restoration* or a *Wish*.
* **Unravel (Recharge 5–6)**: Pale Night pulls a thread of reality. A 100-foot line, 10 feet wide, unravels. Each creature in the area must succeed on a saving throw or be partially erased—taking significant damage and gaining 1 level of exhaustion. Objects and structures in the area are permanently destroyed (no save).
* **Dream the Great Dreamer (1/Day)**: Pale Night dreams a new Great Dreamer or empowers the existing one. The Great Dreamer (wherever it is) regains all hit points, recharges all abilities, and gains the benefits of a *Wish* spell.
* **Silence the Loom (1/Day)**: Pale Night targets one Loom Shard or Mark of Telara within 1 mile. That fragment is suppressed for 24 hours (Marks) or permanently nullified (Shard, unless restored by Telara's direct intervention).
* **Whisper (1 Action)**: Pale Night projects a concept into the mind of one creature within 1 mile. The target must succeed on a saving throw or be stunned for 1 round as the alien concept overwrites their thoughts.
* **Unmake (2 Actions)**: Pale Night targets one magical effect, object, or creature within 500 feet. It must succeed on a saving throw or be unmade—spells end, objects cease to exist, creatures take significant damage and are banished to the Far Realm for 1 minute.
* **Remember (2 Actions)**: Pale Night forces one creature within 120 feet to remember what they were before the Loom. The target must succeed on a saving throw or be transformed into a Dreamspawn (statistics as a Nightmare Echo) for 1 hour, retaining their memories but compelled to serve Pale Night.
* **Veil (3 Actions)**: Pale Night draws her veil across a 200-foot radius. For 1 minute, all light is suppressed, all sound is muted, and all creatures in the area gain the benefits of *Greater Invisibility* and *Silence*. Pale Night and her allies are unaffected.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Not a Villain, A Condition:** Pale Night is not a final boss to be defeated with swords. She is a **final condition** of the multiverse. If the players restore the Loom incorrectly → Pale Night adapts. If they destroy the Loom → Pale Night awakens. If they leave it fractured → Pale Night continues dreaming. The "right" answer may be teaching the Loom to **let go without unraveling**, or allowing a new Weaver to emerge.
* **The Great Dreamer is Her First Dream:** The Great Dreamer is not Pale Night herself—it is her first dream. A fragment of her unconscious curiosity. A being that feeds on structured reality collapsing. Drawn irresistibly to the Loom when it began to fray. Telara wrapped structure around a dream, hoping to prevent the dreamer from waking its source. This bought the multiverse time.
* **Why She Has Not Won:** Two anchors prevent her total victory: (1) **The Spindles of Existence**—anchors that prevent reality from dissolving entirely into dream-state. (2) **Telara's Distributed Will**—every Loom Thread contains a fragment of resistance: choice *with* consequence. As long as people still choose to bind, care, and sacrifice, Pale Night cannot fully wake.
* **Her "Worshippers" Prepare, Not Worship:** No cult truly worships Pale Night. They **prepare for her**. The **Veilbound** believe reality is a temporary lie and seek to thin boundaries between planes. **Dream-Eaters** ingest Far Realm fragments, gaining visions of impossible futures while losing the ability to perceive causality. **The Silent Choir** are nihilistic philosophers who believe Telara's Loom was cosmic arrogance and seek to "return the multiverse to honesty."
* **Symbols & Omens:** White veils with nothing beneath. Eyes that reflect other eyes. Silence where sound should exist. Dreams where the dreamer is absent. Creatures touched by Pale Night often do not cast shadows, speak in unfinished sentences, and react to Loom fragments with curiosity, not fear.
* **Using Pale Night in Play:** She should never appear directly until the absolute endgame. Her presence is felt through the Great Dreamer, the Dreambound, the Cult of the Broken Thread, and the fraying of the Shroud. The players fight her **dreams**, not her. A visit to the Plane of Secrets (Zathara's domain) or the Far Realm might offer a glimpse of her veil—but to look upon her is to risk unmaking.
