# Master Rhydian Glasscarver (The Prismatic Shaper)

![[attachments/master_rhydian_glasscarver.jpg]]

* **Race**: Echobound Human
* **Faction**: Shard-Forged Glassworks / Mirrorglade Artisan Guild
* **Role**: Master Smith of Memory Glass, Proprietor of the Shard-Forged Glassworks
* **Appearance**: An athletic craftsman whose forearms and hands have completely crystallized into translucent, rainbow-refracting quartz. He wears a heavy leather smith's apron studded with diamond dust and protective brass goggles. When he moves, faint acoustic harmonics chime around him like crystalline tuning forks.
* **Personality**: Soft-spoken, deeply introspective, and passionate about mnemonic art. Rhydian treats every shard of memory glass as a living piece of someone's soul, believing that weapons and armors forged from memories inherit the courage and conviction of those who lived them. He despises those who desecrate memories for base cruelty.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Rhydian is a formidable resonance combatant who wields sound, psychic reverberation, and razor-sharp crystal shards.

* **Resonant Body**: When Rhydian takes damage from a melee weapon attack, the attacking creature takes 5 (1d10) thunder or radiant damage as his crystalline flesh chimes violently.
* **Mnemonic Weapon Master**: Rhydian's weapon attacks are magical and deal an extra significant damage on a hit (included in the attack).
* **Multiattack**: Rhydian makes two attacks with his *Prismatic Glass Greatsword* or his *Sonic Shard Burst*.
* **Prismatic Glass Greatsword**: one target. If the target is a creature concentrating on a spell, it makes the concentration check with disadvantage.
* **Sonic Shard Burst (Recharge 5–6)**: Rhydian claps his crystalline hands together, discharging a 30-foot cone of ultrasonic sound and flying razor glass. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes 21 (6d6) piercing and thunder damage and is deafened for 1 minute. On a success, it takes half damage and is not deafened.
* **Mirror Deflection**: When targeted by a ranged spell attack, Rhydian can raise his crystalline forearm to impose disadvantage on the attack roll. If the attack misses, he reflects the beam harmlessly into the sky.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Crafting Quests**: Rhydian can forge wondrous items (such as *Sun Blades*, *Rings of Spell Storing*, or *Glamoured Studded Leather*) if adventurers bring him virgin memory shards harvested from the Sea of Shards or the heart of the Shardspire Palace.
* **The Lost Memory**: Rhydian traded his own memories of his birthplace to save his apprentice from being swallowed by the Mirrored Labyrinth. He secretly hires trustworthy parties to search for his lost childhood memory shard.
