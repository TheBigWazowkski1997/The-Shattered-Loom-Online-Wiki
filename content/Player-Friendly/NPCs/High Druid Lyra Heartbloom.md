# High Druid Lyra Heartbloom (Voice of the Living Canopy)

![[attachments/high_druid_lyra_heartbloom.jpg]]

* **Race**: Firbolg (Archdruid of Unity)
* **Faction**: The Leafcallers of Verdancy Hollow / Guardians of the Heartbloom
* **Role**: High Druid / Hierophant of the Living Canopy
* **Appearance**: An imposing yet gentle firbolg standing over seven feet tall, with moss-green skin and long silver-white hair interwoven with fresh jasmine blossoms and ivy leaves. She is dressed in soft robes woven from living willow bark and starlight silk. In her hand, she carries the *Heartwood Scepter*, a blooming staff grown directly from a dormant branch of the world-tree.
* **Personality**: Serene, motherly, and deeply attuned to the natural rhythms of life. She speaks with a soft, resonant voice that calms agitated beasts and pacifies angry mortals. While she prefers peace and healing, she will fiercely defend the sacred groves against planar blights and industrial despoilers.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Lyra channels the boundless vitality of the Heartbloom Tree, conjuring grasping briars, commanding awakened wildlife, and healing mortal wounds with radiant life energy.

* **Firbolg Magic**: Lyra can cast *Detect Magic* and *Disguise Self* at will, using Wisdom as her spellcasting ability.
* **Hidden Step (3/Day)**: As a bonus action, Lyra can turn magically invisible until the start of her next turn or until she attacks, makes a damage roll, or forces someone to make a saving throw.
* **Aura of Vitality**: Friendly creatures that start their turn within 20 feet of Lyra regain 5 hit points if they have at least 1 hit point, and have advantage on saving throws against poison and disease.
* **Multiattack**: Lyra makes two attacks with the *Heartwood Scepter* or casts one spell and makes one staff attack.
* **Heartwood Scepter**: one target. If the target is an aberration or fiend, it takes an extra significant damage.
* **Briar Burst (Recharge 5–6)**: Living briars erupt from the ground in a 20-foot radius centered on a point within 60 feet. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes significant damage and is restrained. On a success, it takes half damage and isn't restrained. A restrained creature can use an action to make a effort to free itself.
* **Spellcasting**: Lyra is a 12th-level spellcaster. Her spellcasting ability is Wisdom (spell save saving throw, +9 to hit with spell attacks). She has the following druid spells prepared:.
* **Nature's Ward**: When an ally within 30 feet takes damage, Lyra can invoke living roots to interpose, reducing the damage by 20.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Voice of the Canopy:** Lyra does not govern by force; citizens of Verdancy Hollow bring their grievances to her at the roots of the Heartbloom Tree, trusting her to guide them toward harmonious compromise.
* **Planar Healer:** If the party brings afflicted comrades suffering from planar diseases, curses, or abyssal corruption, Lyra can cure them using consecrated sap from the world-tree—in exchange for their aid in cleansing blighted borderlands.
* **Steward of Life:** She views all life as a single interwoven tapestry, warning that cutting down forests or slaughtering beasts carelessly damages the metaphysical fabric of the Plane of Unity.
