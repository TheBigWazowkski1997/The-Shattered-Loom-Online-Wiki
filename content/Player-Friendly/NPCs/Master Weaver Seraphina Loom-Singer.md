# Master Weaver Seraphina Loom-Singer (The Concordant Weaver)

![[attachments/seraphina_loom_singer.jpg]]

* **Race**: Protector Aasimar
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Unity/Plane of UnityThe Woven Citadel|The Resonance Loom]] / Althera Artisans
* **Role**: Master Weaver of Unity Threads, Proprietor of The Resonance Loom
* **Appearance**: A breathtaking aasimar woman with luminous golden skin, hair that shimmers like spun dawn, and eyes of pure liquid topaz. When she weaves, her fingertips trail physical filaments of pure golden light that hum with crystalline chords. She wears an elegant chiton woven from starlight and solar silk, adorned with geometric mandalas representing the interconnected planes.
* **Personality**: Compassionate, deeply intuitive, and patient. Seraphina believes that every soul in the multiverse is a unique thread in a grand, divine tapestry; when one thread snaps or frays, the whole tapestry suffers. She treats travelers of all backgrounds—even those from dark lower planes—with gracious hospitality, seeking to understand and mend rather than condemn.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Seraphina is a formidable radiant battlefield controller and abjuration master who binds enemies with glowing threads and shields allies with shared resonance.

* **Aura of Harmony**: Friendly creatures within 15 feet of Seraphina have advantage on saving throws against being charmed or frightened, and gain +2 to all saving throws.
* **Shared Resonance**: Whenever an ally within 30 feet takes damage, Seraphina can use a reaction to magically absorb half of that damage, granting the ally temporary hit points equal to the absorbed amount.
* **Multiattack**: Seraphina makes two attacks with her *Luminara Thread Lash* or casts a spell and makes one attack.
* **Luminara Thread Lash**: *Melee Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. If the target is a creature, it is restrained by golden threads (strength check to escape).
* **Weave of Concordance (Recharge 5–6)**: Seraphina throws an expanse of shimmering light threads in a 30-foot cone. Each hostile creature in that area must make a saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes significant damage and is incapacitated until the end of its next turn as harmony overwhelms its aggressive impulses. On a success, it takes half damage and is not incapacitated.
* **Radiant Soul (1/Day)**: Seraphina unleashes her divine energy. Two luminous, incorporeal wings sprout from her back for 1 minute. During this time, she has a flying speed of 30 feet, and once on each of her turns when she deals damage, she can deal an extra 8 radiant damage to one target.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Weaving Relics**: Seraphina can mend damaged artifacts, severed attunement bonds, or weave enchanted cloaks for parties who bring her pure filaments from the Luminara Flow or pristine dream-threads.
* **The Dissonant Under-Trade**: While Seraphina values unity, she recognizes that total conformity can become tyrannical. She quietly monitors the Frayed Undercroft, intervening only to prevent open violence or planar infection.
