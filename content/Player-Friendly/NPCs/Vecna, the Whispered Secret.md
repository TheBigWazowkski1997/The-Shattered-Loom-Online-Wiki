# Vecna, the Whispered Secret (The Archlich of Secrets)

![[attachments/vecna_whispered_secret.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Undead (Lich — Ascended Deity)
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/The Prison Plane/The 32 Chambers|The Silent Spindle]] (Prisoner of Chamber 7)
* **Role**: Inmate / Cosmic Usurper / God of Secrets
* **Appearance**: A desiccated, skeletal figure draped in rotting robes of midnight blue, cinched at the waist with a belt of preserved human fingers. His left eye socket is empty, a void that drinks light; his right hand is missing, severed at the wrist. Where his left hand should be rests the **Hand of Vecna**—a withered, clawed artifact of immense necrotic power. An aura of whispered voices surrounds him, audible only to those he permits.
* **Personality**: Coldly patient, infinitely calculating, and utterly devoid of mercy. Vecna views all existence as a puzzle to be solved, a secret to be hoarded, or a thread to be cut. He speaks in riddles and half-truths, for even his lies contain kernels of dangerous knowledge. He respects only those who seek power through knowledge, and he despises the Spindle not for imprisoning him, but for *hiding* the Mother-Thread from him.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Vecna is a formidable deity-tier threat. Even as a "prisoner," his connection to the Mother-Thread grants him reality-warping power within his chamber. He avoids direct combat, preferring to manipulate, corrupt, and erase. If forced to fight, he alters the battlefield's fundamental laws. **Proficiency Bonus** +8 ***Legendary Resistance (5/Day).*** If Vecna fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead. ***Rejuvenation.*** If Vecna's body is destroyed, his essence returns to the Hand of Vecna (or the Eye of Vecna if the Hand is unavailable). He gains a new body in 1d10 days, appearing within 5 feet of the artifact. Only destroying both artifacts simultaneously while casting *wish* to sever his link to the Mother-Thread can permanently kill him. ***Turn Immunity.*** Vecna is immune to effects that turn undead. ***Mother-Thread Awareness.*** Vecna knows the location of any creature that has ever spoken a secret, written a hidden truth, or deliberately concealed knowledge within 10 miles. He can scry on any such creature without components (save saving throw). ***Null-Thread Adaptation.*** Within the 32 Chambers, Vecna's magic functions normally despite the Null-Thread Field. He can cast spells without Thread-Anchors. ***Innate Spellcasting (Psionics).*** Vecna's spellcasting ability is Intelligence (spell save saving throw, +18 to hit with spell attacks). He can innately cast the following spells, requiring no components:

* **At will:**: *detect magic, detect thoughts, disguise self, mage hand, message, nondetection, silent image, thaumaturgy*.
* **3/day each:**: *arcane eye, clairvoyance, counterspell, dominate person, modify memory, scrying, seeming, telekinesis, tongue of the sun and moon*.
* **1/day each:**: *antimagic field, foresight, mind blank, plane shift (self only), power word stun, true seeing, weird*.
* **Cantrips (at will):**: *chill touch, mage hand, minor illusion, poison spray, ray of frost, toll the dead, vicious mockery*.
* **1st level (4 slots):**: *comprehend languages, detect magic, identify, shield, silent image, sleep*.
* **2nd level (3 slots):**: *detect thoughts, invisibility, knock, misty step, suggestion*.
* **3rd level (3 slots):**: *counterspell, dispel magic, fear, hypnotic pattern, nondetection*.
* **4th level (3 slots):**: *arcane eye, banishment, dimension door, greater invisibility, Mordenkainen's private sanctum*.
* **5th level (3 slots):**: *dominate person, modify memory, scrying, seeming, telekinesis, wall of force*.
* **6th level (2 slots):**: *chain lightning, circle of death, disintegrate, globe of invulnerability, true seeing*.
* **7th level (2 slots):**: *finger of death, forcecage, plane shift, symbol, teleport*.
* **8th level (1 slot):**: *antimagic field, mind blank, power word stun*.
* **9th level (1 slot):**: *foresight, power word kill, true polymorph, wish*.
* **Whispered Insight**: Vecna targets one creature within 120 ft. The target must succeed on a saving throw or reveal its true intentions, current hit points, and one secret it is keeping to Vecna.
* **Necrotic Bolt**: Vecna makes one Hand of Vecna attack.
* **Rewrite Reality (Costs 2 Actions)**: Vecna casts *counterspell*, *dispel magic*, or *telekinesis* without expending a spell slot.
* **Sever Destiny (Costs 3 Actions)**: Vecna uses *Sever the Thread* if available, or casts *power word kill* (save saving throw).

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

### **The Chamber of Total Transparency (Chamber 7)**
Vecna's prison is a crystalline sphere where every thought manifests as glowing script on the walls. The Spindle intended this as torture—for a god of secrets, total exposure is agony. But Vecna has *adapted*. He now uses the chamber as a **universal scrying lens**. Every secret thought projected on the walls is a secret *he now owns*. He has turned his punishment into the ultimate intelligence network.

### **The Mother-Thread**
Vecna discovered the Mother-Thread—the foundational strand anchoring all destinies—before the Shattering. He nearly severed it to become the new Spindle. The Silent Spindle imprisoned him *specifically* because he knows the Spindle's internal geometry better than any being in existence. He is the only entity who could theoretically *replace* the Spindle.

### **The Hand and Eye of Vecna**
These artifacts are not mere items—they are **fragments of Vecna's divine essence**, physically separated from him by the Spindle during his capture. They are currently "lost" in the multiverse (likely in the hands of mortal cultists or hidden in other Chambers). If reunited with Vecna, his CR increases to 28 and he gains *Divine Intervention* (1/day, automatic success).

### **Vecna's Cults in the Shattered Loom**
*   **The Whispered Court**: Mortal cultists who cut out their own tongues to "speak only in secrets." They seek the Hand and Eye.
*   **The Thread-Cutters**: A radical sect believing Vecna *should* sever the Mother-Thread, ending all destiny and granting true free will (and chaos).
*   **The Silent Archivists**: Scholars who hoard forbidden knowledge, believing Vecna will reward them with godhood.

### **Running Vecna**
*   **Never fight fair.** Vecna uses minions, traps, revealed secrets, and environmental manipulation.
*   **Information is his weapon.** Before combat, he should know the party's secrets, fears, and weaknesses.
*   **He offers bargains.** "I will tell you how to save your dying friend... if you retrieve the Hand of Vecna from [location]."
*   **He fears only one thing:** The Spindle discovering he has turned his prison into a scrying network.

### **Escape Conditions**
Vecna cannot leave Chamber 7 unless:
1.  The Hand *and* Eye of Vecna are both brought to him within the chamber.
2.  A *wish* spell is cast to sever the Spindle's ward on the chamber.
3.  The Silent Spindle is destroyed or incapacitated (effectively impossible).

If all three occur, Vecna reforms at full power and immediately attempts to seize the Mother-Thread.
