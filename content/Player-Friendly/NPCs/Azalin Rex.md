# Azalin Rex (The Arch-Lich of Darkon)

![[attachments/azalin_rex.jpg]]

* **Race**: Lich (Undead Humanoid, formerly Human)
* **Faction**: The Crown of Darkon / The Kargat
* **Role**: Darklord of Darkon / Master of Castle Avernus
* **Appearance**: An imposing skeletal monarch clad in tattered crimson and gold royal robes bordered in ermine fur, crowned by the spiked iron coronet of Darkon. Within his hollow eye sockets burn twin pinpoints of icy violet flame. His bony hands, adorned with heavy signet rings carved of planar obsidian, crackle with unmaking necrotic arcana.
* **Personality**: Brilliant, tyrannical, deeply paranoid, and profoundly arrogant. Unlike other Darklords who wallow in self-pity or romance, Azalin views his domain purely as an infuriating prison to be mathematically dismantled. His curse is the inability to learn new magic, driving him to endless, catastrophic experiments to bypass his planar confinement.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Azalin is an arch-mage of world-shattering potency who commands legions of undead, wields forbidden necromantic spells, and bends the geography of Castle Avernus itself. He avoids direct melee, preferring to annihilate opposition from afar while shielding himself behind prismatic wards, wall of force barriers, and summoned wraiths.
* **Damage Resistances**: Cold, Lightning, Necrotic.
* **Damage Immunities**: Poison; Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from Nonmagical Attacks.
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Exhaustion, Frightened, Paralyzed, Poisoned.
* **Senses**: Truesight 120 ft., Passive Perception 20.
* **Languages**: Abyssal, Celestial, Common, Draconic, Infernal, Undercommon.
* **Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Azalin fails a saving throw, he can choose to succeed instead.
* **Master of the Undead**: Any undead creature created by Azalin or under his command gains a +4 bonus to attack and damage rolls and has advantage on saving throws against turn undead.
* **Rejuvenation**: If Azalin is destroyed, his spirit returns to his phylactery—hidden within the shifting vaults of Castle Avernus—reforming his body in 1d10 days.
* **Turn Resistance**: Azalin has advantage on saving throws against any effect that turns undead.
* **Spellcasting**: Azalin is an 18th-level spellcaster (high spell save difficulty, expert accuracy with spell attacks). Prepared spells:.
* **Paralyzing Touch**: *Melee Spell Attack*: expert accuracy, reach 5 ft., one creature. *Hit*: significant damage plus significant damage. The target must succeed on a saving throw or be paralyzed for 1 minute.
* **Disrupt Life (Costs 3 Legendary Actions)**: Each non-undead creature within 20 feet of Azalin must make a saving throw, taking significant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
* **Cantrip**: Azalin casts a cantrip.
* **Paralyzing Touch (Costs 2 Actions)**: Azalin uses his Paralyzing Touch.
* **Frightening Gaze (Costs 2 Actions)**: Azalin fixes his gaze on one creature within 10 feet. saving throw or frightened for 1 minute.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Hour of Ascension & The Empty Throne:** In recent times, Azalin attempted to shatter Darkon's borders during the **Hour of Ascension**, triggering a planar cataclysm that split Darkon and left Castle Avernus fractured. Though he is currently absent from his physical throne, his arcane echo permeates Castle Avernus and the city of **Necropolis**.
* **Obsession with Loom Fragments:** Azalin was among the first planar scholars to theorize the existence of the Shattered Loom. His private notes in the Grand Arcaneum reveal he sought to harvest a Loom Fragment to rewrite the laws of the Dark Powers.
* **Rivalry with Strahd:** Azalin despises Strahd as a petty, romantic fool whose obsession with a single mortal woman holds him back from true cosmic godhood. Should Azalin learn Strahd lost his immortality at the hands of adventurers, he would exploit the opportunity to annex Barovia into Darkon's borders.
