# Lady Seraphina Aurum (Grand Caliper of Light & Prism)

![[attachments/lady_seraphina_aurum.jpg]]

* **Race**: High Elf (Prism Shaper)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper|The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper]]
* **Role**: Grand Caliper of Light & Prism — Crystalis Representative of the Triumvirate
* **Appearance**: An elegant, ethereal elf who wears a gown made of woven glass threads that refract light in a shifting rainbow pattern. Her hair is platinum white, and she speaks in a melodic, quiet whisper that carries further than it should. Every motion trails faint prismatic light, as if reality itself is catching up to her.
* **Personality**: Dramatic, perceptive, and highly philosophical. She believes that the physical world is merely a canvas for the soul's light. She becomes visibly distracted by anything she finds emotionally or philosophically resonant — which is everything.

## Spellcasting

Seraphina is a 14th-level Illusionist/Evoker. Her spellcasting ability is Charisma (Spell Save saving throw, +10 to hit with spell attacks).

* **Cantrips (at will):** *Light*, *Dancing Lights*, *Ray of Frost*
* **1st Level (4 slots):** *Color Spray*, *Faerie Fire*, *Charm Person*
* **2nd Level (3 slots):** *Blur*, *Mirror Image*, *Shatter*
* **3rd Level (3 slots):** *Hypnotic Pattern*, *Daylight*
* **4th Level (3 slots):** *Hallucinatory Terrain*, *Confusion*
* **5th Level (2 slots):** *Wall of Light*, *Dominate Person*
* **7th Level (1 slot):** *Prismatic Spray*

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Seraphina fights through prismatic magic — blinding, shattering, and refracting light into destructive beams. She is never aggressive but is devastatingly effective when forced to defend herself or her research. |8 (-1)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|16 (+3)|22 (+6)|

* **Prismatic Aura**: At the start of each of Seraphina's turns, each creature within 10 feet of her must succeed on a saving throw or be blinded until the start of their next turn.
* **Prism Shaper**: Seraphina's spell attacks have advantage against creatures that are in direct line of sight with a light source.
* **Emotional Resonance**: Seraphina can sense the emotional state of any creature within 60 feet. She has advantage on Insight checks and cannot be surprised by emotional manipulation.
* **Multiattack**: Seraphina casts one spell and makes one Crystal Shard attack.
* **Crystal Shard**: *Ranged Spell Attack:* +10 to hit, range 60 ft., one target. The target must succeed on a saving throw or be blinded until the end of their next turn.
* **Prismatic Spray (3/Day)**: Seraphina casts *Prismatic Spray* (saving throw). Each creature in the 60-foot cone must make a Dexterity saving throw, suffering a different effect depending on which of the eight colors strikes them.
* **Refraction Shield (Recharge 5–6)**: Seraphina bends light around herself. She gains half cover (+2 AC, +2 Dex saves), and any attack that misses her due to this cover bounces back as a Crystal Shard attack against the attacker.
* **Light Step**: Seraphina teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space she can see, leaving a blinding flash of light at her origin point. Creatures within 5 feet of the origin must succeed on a saving throw or be blinded until the end of their next turn.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Memory Cataloguer:** Seraphina wants to record every emotion and memory experienced on the Plane of Eternal Craft within crystalline arrays. She views this as the plane's greatest possible contribution to the multiverse — a perfect emotional record.
* **Fascinated by the Marks:** She has heard rumors of the players' Marks of Telara and is professionally *obsessed*. She will find a reason to study them — how divine weaving interacts with prismatic light — and may ask (or beg, in her oblique way) to take "just a small reading."
* **Distracted in a Crisis:** She is noticeably unfocused during the Metallos lockdown, finding the emotional resonance of the crisis "profoundly beautiful in its tragedy." She is not uncaring — she just processes everything philosophically first.
