# Grand Storm-Marshal Vaelen Gale (The Maelstrom Exile)

![[attachments/vaelen_gale.jpg]]

* **Race**: Air Genasi (Storm-Touched)
* **Faction**: Exiled Tempest Blades / The Eye's Shadow Underworld
* **Role**: Former Military Commander of Stormhold / Underworld Black Market Boss
* **Appearance**: An imposing, weathered air genasi with stormy gray-blue skin, silver hair that crackles with static electricity, and cold, electric-blue eyes that glow behind a copper-rimmed eye-glass. He wears tarnished thundersteel plate armor wrapped in weather-beaten black cloud-silk cloaks, and wields *Tempest-Cutter*, a serrated thundersteel greatsword that hums with supersonic vibrations.
* **Personality**: Cynical, sharp-tongued, and fiercely independent. Having been cast out of the Thundering Assembly after refusing to sacrifice a scout squadron to an elemental tempest, Vaelen operates in the shadows. He respects tactical cunning, direct honesty, and those who can hold their ground against hurricane-force gales.

---
## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Vaelen is a formidable aerial combat master and tempest warrior who manipulates lightning arcs, wind barriers, and concussive thunder strikes.

* **Unending Breath**: Vaelen can hold his breath indefinitely while not incapacitated.
* **Eye of the Tempest**: Ranged attacks against Vaelen have disadvantage while he is flying or in wind exceeding 20 mph.
* **Thunderous Strike**: When Vaelen hits with a melee weapon attack, the attack deals an extra 2d8 thunder damage (included in attacks).
* **Multiattack**: Vaelen makes three attacks with *Tempest-Cutter* or two ranged Lightning Javelins.
* **Tempest-Cutter**: one target. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it must succeed on a saving throw or be pushed 10 feet back and knocked prone.
* **Lightning Javelin**: *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target.
* **Lightning Surge (Recharge 5–6)**: Vaelen discharges an arc of lightning in a 60-foot line that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a saving throw, taking significant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
* **Gale Repulsion**: When hit by a melee attack, Vaelen unleashes a concussive blast of wind. The attacker takes significant damage and must make a saving throw or be pushed 15 feet away.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Exile's Grudge:** Vaelen despises the current Thundering Assembly, believing their obsession with martial glory will drive Stormhold into ruin. He provides secret intelligence and black-market military gear to parties willing to undermine the Assembly’s corrupt warlords.
* **The Shattered Loom:** Vaelen has noted that the Great Maelstrom’s winds have begun spinning backward at certain lunar phases, indicating an extraplanar tear anchored directly to the plane's atmospheric core.
* **Dialogue & Demeanor:** *"The Assembly preaches about honor while sitting behind three leagues of hurricane winds. In the Eye's Shadow, we deal in reality. What do you need, and what are you willing to bleed for it?"*
