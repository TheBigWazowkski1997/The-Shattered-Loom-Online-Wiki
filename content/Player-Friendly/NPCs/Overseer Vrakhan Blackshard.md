# Overseer Vrakhan Blackshard (Lord of the Maw)

![[attachments/overseer_vrakhan_blackshard.jpg]]

* **Race**: Earth Genasi
* **Faction**: The Mining Collective / The Deepmaw Syndicate
* **Role**: Supreme Overseer of Deepmaw Quarry, Syndicate Enforcer
* **Appearance**: A hulking, stern earth genasi standing well over six and a half feet tall. His skin resembles polished black obsidian, veined with glowing fractures of molten amber. He wears heavy armor plates of dark stone bolted directly to a thick leather harness, and carries *Earth-Splitter*, a massive two-handed warhammer carved from solid quartz that hums with low seismic vibrations.
* **Personality**: Cold, pragmatic, and unyielding as granite. Vrakhan respects strength, productivity, and hard results. He detests incompetence and has zero tolerance for thievery or labor strikes. Yet, unlike the cruel Dao, he is a man of his word—contracts signed with Vrakhan in stone are strictly honored.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Vrakhan is a formidable earth-shattering juggernaut. He commands stone-meld movement, tremors that knock entire parties prone, and punishing bludgeoning strikes.

* **Earth Glide**: Vrakhan can burrow through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone. While doing so, he doesn't disturb the material he moves through.
* **Siege Monster**: Vrakhan deals double damage to objects and structures.
* **Stone Camouflage**: Vrakhan has advantage on Dexterity (Stealth) checks made to hide in rocky terrain.
* **Multiattack**: Vrakhan makes three melee attacks with *Earth-Splitter*.
* **Earth-Splitter (Warhammer)**: one target. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a saving throw or be pushed 10 feet and knocked prone.
* **Seismic Slam (Recharge 5–6)**: Vrakhan slams his hammer into the stone floor, creating a localized earthquake in a 30-foot cone. Each creature on the ground in that area must make a saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes significant damage, is knocked prone, and is stunned until the end of its next turn. On a success, the creature takes half damage and is not knocked prone or stunned.
* **Stone Shield**: When a creature hits Vrakhan with an attack, he can summon a jagged slab of stone from the ground to grant himself a +4 bonus to AC against that attack.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Balance of Power**: Vrakhan walks a razor's edge between maintaining the Mining Collective's independence and resisting the complete economic takeover by Khanum Zoraya and the Dao cartels.
* **Mercenary Employer**: He views outside adventuring parties as expendable scalpels. He hires them for dangerous subterranean incursions that his own syndicates cannot officially handle.
* **Quests**: He contracts parties to root out saboteurs in the lower rings, eliminate subterranean xorn nests, and investigate the ominous whispers emanating from the Abyssal Roots.
