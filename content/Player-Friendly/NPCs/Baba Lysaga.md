# Baba Lysaga (Mother of the Swamp Fane)

![[attachments/baba_lysaga.jpg]]

* **Race**: Human (Fey-Corrupted Witch / Hag-Mother)
* **Faction**: The Coven of the Loom-Rot / Cult of Mother Night
* **Role**: Adoptive Mother of Strahd / Guardian of the Swamp Fane & The Crimson Weave
* **Appearance**: An ancient, withered crone with skin like cracked swamp mud, wild tangles of greasy grey hair decorated with raven skulls and rotting lily-pads, and milk-white eyes that blaze with virulent amber when channeling coven witchcraft. She rides through the murky mists inside a flying, hollowed-out giant skull of a dead dragon, draped in rotting velvet robes stained with goat blood and swamp bile.
* **Personality**: Delusional, zealously maternal, and pathologically devoted to Count Strahd von Zarovich. She truly believes herself to be Strahd's real mother, nursing a bitter hatred for Queen Ravenovia. She is patient, sadistic, and utterly fanatical in using the stolen Loom Fragment to elevate Strahd into an eternal god.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Baba Lysaga is a devastating arch-witch and master of fey-tainted chronal magic. In battle, she flies high above her enemies in her floating giant dragon skull, raining down swarms of insects, chain lightning, and blight spells while commanding her terrifying animated construct, the **Creeping Hut**. She orchestrated the **Chronal Link** that bound Barovia's three ancient Fanes to three young [[../Species/Hag Dragon|Hag Dragons]] (Vervain the Spindle, Morrigan, and Gryla). By drawing upon the corrupted Loom Fragment—the **Crimson Weave**—she channeled world-altering 9th-level coven rituals to sustain Strahd's absolute immortality.
* **Damage Resistances**: Acid, Necrotic, Poison.
* **Condition Immunities**: Charmed, Frightened, Poisoned.
* **Senses**: Truesight 60 ft., Passive Perception 17.
* **Languages**: Abyssal, Common, Draconic, Sylvan.
* **Shapechanger**: Baba Lysaga can use an action to polymorph into a swarm of insects (flies or mosquitoes) or back into her true form.
* **Blessing of Mother Night**: Baba Lysaga is shielded from divination magic. She can't be targeted by such magic or perceived through scrying sensors.
* **Master of the Creeping Hut**: As long as Baba Lysaga is conscious, she can telepathically command the Creeping Hut to attack, move, or use root-spikes as a bonus action.
* **Spellcasting**: Baba Lysaga is a 16th-level spellcaster (high spell save difficulty, expert accuracy with spell attacks). Prepared spells:.
* **Multiattack**: Baba Lysaga makes two quarterstaff attacks or casts a spell and makes one attack.
* **Rusted Spindle-Staff**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: expert accuracy, reach 5 ft., one target. bludgeoning damage plus significant damage.
* **Summon Swarms of Insects (Recharge 5–6)**: Baba Lysaga summons 1d4 swarms of biting swamp flies or rot grubs in unoccupied spaces she can see within 60 feet.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Climax at the Ruins of Berez:** In the sodden marshes of Berez, Baba Lysaga made her final stand against the party. Drawing upon the pulsating energy of the **Crimson Weave (Loom Fragment)**, she initiated the summoning incantation to call forth all three [[../Species/Hag Dragon|Hag Dragons]] into a devastating draconic coven that would have sealed Barovia's borders permanently.
* **The Party's Triumph & The Planar Anchor:** The party successfully interrupted the ritual, slew Baba Lysaga before the three dragons could unite, and recovered the corrupted Loom Fragment. To prevent the unmoored region from collapsing into the bottomless Astral void, the adventurers hammered an enchanted **Planar Anchor** into the heart of the Swamp Fane, tethering southern Barovia firmly to material reality.
* **The Severed Immortality:** Baba Lysaga's demise shattered the three-way chronal link. Because she was the metaphysical anchor sustaining Strahd’s invulnerability, her death stripped Count Strahd of his planar immunity. Strahd remains completely unaware that his adoptive "mother" was killed by the party—believing she simply remains secluded in Berez.
