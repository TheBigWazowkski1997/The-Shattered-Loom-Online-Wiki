# Khanum Zoraya of the Seven Veins (Mistress of the Dismal Delve)

![[attachments/khanum_zoraya.jpg]]

* **Race**: Dao (Noble Earth Genie)
* **Faction**: The Seven-Vein Concessions / The Great Dismal Delve
* **Role**: Sovereign Gem-Magnate of Deepmaw Quarry, High Slavelord
* **Appearance**: A colossal, imposing noble dao standing nearly ten feet tall, with polished bronze-brown skin and long hair adorned with strands of woven diamonds, emeralds, and gold chains. Her thick, muscular frame is draped in heavy cloth-of-gold and layered velvet embroidered with powdered gemstones. She wears a torc of uncut star sapphires and wields an ornate two-handed maul fashioned from a single gigantic dark amethyst.
* **Personality**: Greedy, imperious, cruel, and boastful. Zoraya loves precious gems with an all-consuming passion, viewing mortals and elementals alike merely as tools to unearth more treasures for her vaults. She is easily flattered by displays of opulence and deference, but swift to petrify anyone who cheats or disrespects her contracts.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Khanum Zoraya is a Noble Dao warlord (formidable) combining devastating bludgeoning force with petrifying earth magic and spell-like planar abilities.

* **Earth Glide**: Zoraya can burrow through nonmagical, unworked earth and stone without disturbing the material she moves through.
* **Elemental Demise**: If Zoraya dies, her body disintegrates into crystalline powder, leaving behind only the equipment she was wearing or carrying.
* **Sure-Footed**: Zoraya has advantage on Strength and Dexterity saving throws made against effects that would knock her prone.
* **Innate Spellcasting**: Zoraya’s innate spellcasting ability is Charisma (spell save saving throw, +8 to hit with spell attacks). She can innately cast the following spells, requiring no material components:.
* **At will:**: *Detect Evil and Good*, *Detect Magic*, *Stone Shape*.
* **3/day each:**: *Passwall*, *Move Earth*, *Tongues*, *Wall of Stone*.
* **1/day each:**: *Conjure Elemental* (earth elemental only), *Flesh to Stone*, *Gaseous Form*, *Plane Shift*.
* **Multiattack**: Zoraya makes two attacks with her *Amethyst Maul* or hurls two boulders.
* **Amethyst Maul**: one target. If the target is a Large or smaller creature, it must succeed on a saving throw or be pushed up to 15 feet away and knocked prone.
* **Boulder**: *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +10 to hit, range 60/240 ft., one target.
* **Petrifying Breath (Recharge 5–6)**: Zoraya exhales calcifying dust in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes significant damage and begins to turn to stone, restrained. It must repeat the save at the end of its next turn. On a second failure, the creature is petrified.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Gem Hoard**: Zoraya measures respect by the carat. She can be bribed or placated by exceptionally rare or foreign gemstones (especially those from the Plane of Eternal Craft or the Astral Sea).
* **Ruthless Employer**: She frequently hires outside adventurers to hunt down runaway indentured workers, seize rogue mines, or assassinate rival Dao cartel agents who infringe on her concessions.
* **Quests**: She will pay enormous sums in diamonds and Ioun stones to any party that retrieves ancient pre-Shattering relics from the Abyssal Roots or eliminates rival cartels in Deepmaw Quarry.
