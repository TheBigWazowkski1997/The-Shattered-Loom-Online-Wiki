# Aeldris the Dim (The Conclave Doubter)

![[attachments/aeldris_the_dim.jpg]]

* **Race**: Warforged (Echo-Seeker Chassis)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Ironbound Covenant|The Ironbound Covenant]]
* **Role**: The Questioned / Conclave Doubter / Seat VII of the Sealed Conclave ("The Questioned")
* **Appearance**: Noticeably younger and more slender than their veteran peers, Aeldris is built of brushed silver-blue alloy with clean, delicate joinery that has seen barely two centuries of service. Unlike the roaring kiln-fire visible in senior paladins, the Soulcore lattice in Aeldris's chest emits only a pale, flickering azure ember—like a candle in an autumn wind. Their eyes hold an unusually soft, contemplative glow, devoid of the aggressive zeal typical of the crusaders.
* **Personality**: Quiet, curious, gentle, and profoundly existential. Aeldris asks questions that most Covenant Warforged are conditioned to avoid: *"If my thoughts were shaped by Kaldryn's hammer, are they truly my own?"* *"Is the pain we feel in war a necessary component of existence, or merely an uncalibrated error?"* Despite their faint flame and deep existential doubt, Aeldris fights with graceful valor—motivated not by divine dogma, but by genuine empathy for their comrades.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Aeldris is a swift skirmisher and elusive scout who uses mobility, rapier precision, and psychic resonance. Rather than overpowering foes with brute weight, Aeldris projects an aura of disorienting resonance that forces enemies to hesitate and doubt their own actions.

* **Resonance of Doubt**: Any hostile creature that starts its turn within 15 feet of Aeldris must make a saving throw. On a failure, the creature is stricken by sudden hesitation, suffering disadvantage on attack rolls and ability checks until the start of its next turn.
* **Flickering Core**: Aeldris can take the Disengage or Dash action as a bonus action on each of their turns. In dim light or darkness, attacks against Aeldris have disadvantage unless the attacker has blindsight or truesight.
* **Living Construct**: Aeldris has advantage on saving throws against poison and spells, and does not need to eat, sleep, or breathe.
* **Multiattack**: Aeldris makes two attacks with their *Flickerblade* and uses *Echo Pulse* if available.
* **Echo Pulse (Recharge 5–6)**: Aeldris taps into their faint, unconventional Soulcore ember, releasing an acoustic-spiritual wave in a 20-foot radius. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw. On a failure, a creature takes significant damage and cannot take reactions until the end of its next turn. On a success, it takes half damage and retains reactions.
* **Empathetic Respite (1/Day)**: Aeldris touches a creature. The target regains 20 (4d8 + 2) hit points and is freed from any effect that caused it to be charmed or frightened.
* **Slip the Blow**: When hit by a melee weapon attack, Aeldris can roll 1d8 and add it to their AC against the triggering attack. If the attack still hits, Aeldris can immediately move up to 10 feet without provoking opportunity attacks.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Seat VII of the Sealed Conclave ("The Questioned"):** The Conclave intentionally preserves a seat for a Doubter. Aeldris was chosen because their Soulcore flame dimmed not through corruption or cowardice, but through deep, honest contemplation. In conclave meetings, Aeldris serves as the designated devil's advocate, preventing the council from falling into blind, self-justifying fanaticism.
* **The Dead-Drop in Metallos (Current Investigation):** Aeldris has established an anonymous dead-drop in the artisan quarter of Metallos. They are reaching out to independent adventurers (the party) to investigate Vault 7 of the Bound Archive and discover the truth of the oldest Ironscript Leaf: *Did Kaldryn truly create Vorzath?* Aeldris wants unbiased, external witnesses to find the truth before the Conclave decides whether to destroy or weaponize the tablet.
* **Philosophical Bridge:** Unlike Urdakael or Halverak, Aeldris relates deeply to mortals. They love watching mortal painters, musicians, and bakers in free cities, marveling at how fleeting, fragile lives create enduring beauty without divine blueprints.
* **Voice & Demeanor:** Soft-spoken, polite, earnest. Aeldris frequently tilts their head when observing organic creatures, asking disarming questions with utter sincerity: *"When you dream, do you choose the faces you see, or do they visit you unbidden?"*
