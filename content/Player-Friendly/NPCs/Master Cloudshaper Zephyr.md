# Master Cloudshaper Zephyr (The Mist Architect)

![[attachments/master_cloudshaper_zephyr.jpg]]

* **Race**: Air Genasi
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Fragmented memories/Plane of Fragmented Memories - The Shardlands#2. Reminaria: The City of Whispers|Reminaria Guild of Cloudshapers]] / The Cloud-Spun Atelier
* **Role**: Chief Cloud-Weaver of Reminaria, Aerial Armorer
* **Appearance**: A tall, slender air genasi with pale cerulean skin and hair that constantly billows like silvery cumulus clouds, sparkling with faint static electricity. His eyes are swirling pools of lavender vapor that lack pupils. He wears weightless robes of condensed memory fog trimmed with mother-of-pearl clasps, and floats a few inches above the ground wherever he walks.
* **Personality**: Dreamy, gentle, and softly spoken. Zephyr speaks as though listening to a distant melody carried on the wind. He perceives thoughts and memories not as rigid historical facts, but as shifting atmospheric currents. He is deeply protective of the floating city of Reminaria, treating the memory clouds as fragile living organisms that must be tended with care.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Zephyr is a formidable aerial skirmisher and battlefield controller who weaves fog to blind foes, manipulates wind shears, and strikes with concussive sonic thunder.

* **Unending Breath**: Zephyr can hold his breath indefinitely while not incapacitated.
* **Mistsight**: Magical fog, clouds, and vapor do not obscure Zephyr's vision.
* **Wind Stride**: Ranged weapon attacks against Zephyr have disadvantage while he is flying.
* **Multiattack**: Zephyr makes three attacks with his *Vapor Lash* or uses *Mist Barrage*.
* **Vapor Lash**: *Melee Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 15 ft., one target. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it must succeed on a saving throw or be pushed 10 feet and knocked prone.
* **Mist Barrage**: *Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, range 90 ft., one target.
* **Vortex of Solid Fog (Recharge 5–6)**: Zephyr creates a 20-foot radius sphere of dense, solidifying memory fog centered on a point within 60 feet. The area is heavily obscured. The fog is difficult terrain, and each foot of movement through it costs 4 feet of movement. Each creature in that area when it appears must succeed on a saving throw or be restrained until the start of Zephyr's next turn.
* **Cloud Shift**: When an attacker hits Zephyr with a melee attack, Zephyr turns into pure vapor and teleports up to 30 feet to an unoccupied space he can see, halving the triggering attack's damage.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Cloud Crafting:** Zephyr can tailor custom cloaks and boots for the party that grant *Feather Fall*, *Fog Cloud*, or walking on liquid surfaces.
* **The Sinking Cloud Crisis:** When residential clouds in Reminaria destabilize, Zephyr enlists the adventurers to fly out to the anchor spires to secure the buoyancy runes before hundreds plummet into the void.
* **Memory Dreams:** If a player brings Zephyr a rare memory crystal, he can weave it into a wearable talisman that grants proficiency in a chosen forgotten skill.
