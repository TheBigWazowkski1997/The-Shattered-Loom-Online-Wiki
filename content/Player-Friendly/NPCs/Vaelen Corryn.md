# Vaelen Corryn (The Stitcher)

![[attachments/vaelen_corryn.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Half-Elf
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Pale Lodge/The Pale Lodge|The Pale Lodge]]
* **Role**: Primary Handler, Planar Navigator, and Loom Compass Interpreter
* **Appearance**: A lean, anxious half-elf in his late thirties with ink-stained fingers and perpetually disheveled hair. He wears layered arcanist robes covered in hand-written notes, cross-references, and reminders. His eyes are quick and observant, and he carries a battered satchel overflowing with planar charts, compressed scroll cases, and a leather-bound Loom Compass of his own design.
* **Personality**: Nervous, brilliant, and deeply grateful. He talks fast when excited (which is often) and speaks slowly when frightened (which is also often). He is the "good cop" of the Pale Lodge — the only one of their agents who genuinely cares about the players as people rather than instruments.

* **Cantrips (at will):** *Force Bolt*, *Mage Hand*, *Detect Magic*

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Vaelen is not a combatant by nature or preference. He is a utility arcanist — a researcher who can set up magical barriers, read magical auras in real time, and fire off a handful of focused blasts if cornered. He will stay behind the frontline and provide support. |8 (-1)|14 (+2)|14 (+2)|18 (+4)|14 (+2)|12 (+1)|

* **Loom Compass Reader**: As an action, Vaelen can attune to the Loom Compass to identify the direction and distance to the nearest Loom Shard within 100 miles. He can also identify the "color" of a Loom fragment's corruption.
* **Aura Detection**: Vaelen can cast *Detect Magic* at will as a bonus action.
* **Multiattack**: Vaelen casts one cantrip and uses Arcane Shield if available.
* **Force Bolt (Cantrip)**: *Ranged Spell Attack:* +7 to hit, range 60 ft., one target.
* **Arcane Disruption (1/Day)**: Vaelen targets a magical effect within 60 feet. He casts *Dispel Magic* (as a 3rd-level slot, saving throw check for higher-level spells).
* **Arcane Shield (3/Day)**: When Vaelen or a creature within 5 feet of him is hit by an attack, he conjures a shield of force, granting +4 AC against that attack.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Grateful One:** Vaelen was rescued by the party in Session 2. He views them with profound, genuine gratitude — not just as assets but as people who saved his life when they didn't have to. He will go out of his way to help them, sometimes at his own risk.
* **The Black Thread:** He has noticed a growing "black thread" in the party's Loom marks — a sign of corruption. Aelira told him to ignore it and document it quietly. He is deeply conflicted about this. If the players confide in him or he perceives them in genuine danger from their marks, he will break rank and tell them.
* **The Compass Briefings:** Use Vaelen to deliver complex planar lore and Loom mechanics in a way that feels natural. He gets visibly excited about discoveries and often says: *"Wait, wait — I read something about this — hang on—"* while frantically rummaging through his satchel.
