## The Mark of Telara: Mechanics & Lore

The PCs don't fully understand their marks, which leads to the following "Symptoms":

1. **The Resonance Hum**: When near a Loom Shard, the mark vibrates. To the PCs, it's a painful headache. To the Lodge, it's a tracking beacon.
2. **Thread-Sight (Passive)**: The PCs occasionally see the "threads" of fate. This manifests as a +2 bonus to Initiative or Perception, but also causes "Vision Fatigue" (Disadvantage on the next check) as they see too many possible futures.
3. **The Fraying**: When a PC drops to 0 HP, their "thread" begins to unravel. They don't just bleed; they leak silver light. This makes them impossible to ignore by entities from the **Far Realm**.
