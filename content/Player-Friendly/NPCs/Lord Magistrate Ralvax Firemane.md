# Lord Magistrate Ralvax Firemane (The Magma Sovereign)

![[attachments/ralvax_firemane.jpg]]

* **Race**: Fire Genasi
* **Faction**: The Infernal Council of Cinderhold
* **Role**: Leader of the Infernal Council / Planar Magistrate of Mount Khaldrath
* **Appearance**: A tall, commanding fire genasi with crimson skin, sharp obsidian cheekbones, and flaming hair that cascades like roaring embers down his back. He wears ceremonial plate armor forged from molten adamantine, quenched in volcanic bile, and carries *Cinder-Brand*, a broadsword radiating superheated golden light.
* **Personality**: Pragmatic, calculating, and fiercely proud of Cinderhold’s autonomy. He scorns the decadent Efreeti of the City of Brass as arrogant slavers, yet negotiates with cold precision. He believes survival in the Everblaze Expanse demands iron discipline and unbending resolve.

---
## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Ralvax is a formidable martial commander and pyromantic warrior who controls the battlefield with localized magma fissures, blinding heat bursts, and crushing tactical strikes.

* **Aura of the Caldera**: Any hostile creature that starts its turn within 10 feet of Ralvax takes significant damage.
* **Adamantine Bastion**: Critical hits against Ralvax become normal hits.
* **Heated Weapons**: Ralvax's melee weapon attacks deal an extra 2d6 fire damage (included in attacks).
* **Multiattack**: Ralvax makes three attacks with *Cinder-Brand* or two attacks with his Magma Javelins.
* **Cinder-Brand**: one target. If the target is a creature, it must succeed on a saving throw or ignite; while burning, it takes significant damage at the start of each of its turns until an action is used to extinguish the flames.
* **Magma Javelin**: *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +9 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target.
* **Fissure Strike (Recharge 5–6)**: Ralvax slams *Cinder-Brand* into the earth, opening a 30-foot line of erupting magma that is 5 feet wide. Each creature in that line must make a saving throw, taking significant damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
* **Molten Parry**: Ralvax adds +3 to his AC against one melee attack that would hit him. If the attack misses, the attacker takes significant damage from backsplashing magma.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Council Authority:** Ralvax maintains order across Cinderhold by keeping the volatile factions—the ambitious Furnace Guild, the zealot Flamebound Cult, and the proud Azer artisans—in perpetual diplomatic tension.
* **The Shattered Loom:** Ralvax has noticed that the magma veins beneath Mount Khaldrath have begun vibrating with harmonic, clockwork-like resonances, suspecting that an unstable thread from the Loom has anchored into the volcano's primordial roots.
* **Dialogue & Demeanor:** *"The Efreet offer wishes wrapped in barbed chains; we offer steel tempered in truth. Choose wisely who you bargain with upon this stone, traveler."*
