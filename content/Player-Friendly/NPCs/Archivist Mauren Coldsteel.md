# Archivist Mauren Coldsteel (Keeper of the Bound Archive)

![[attachments/archivist_mauren_coldsteel.jpg]]

* **Race**: Warforged (First-Forged Primeval Chassis)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Ironbound Covenant|The Ironbound Covenant]]
* **Role**: Keeper of the Bound Archive / Master of Ironscript / Seat III of the Sealed Conclave ("The Quenched")
* **Appearance**: An ancient, fragile-looking construct whose original form has been replaced almost cell by cell, plate by plate, over thousands of years. Sections of their chassis are bound with spun silver filigree, copper retaining braces, and hardened alchemical resin. Ceremonial vestments of beaten bronze scales drape over their rounded shoulders. Their elongated fingers end in fine magnetic styluses capable of engraving microscopic runes onto solid steel leaves.
* **Personality**: Reclusive, contemplative, and mournfully reverent. Mauren speaks in a dry, rasping harmonic that sounds like parchment sliding over rusted iron. They claim to possess the only surviving acoustic memory of Kaldryn's true voice from the hour of the First Forging. To Mauren, every recorded memory is a sacred bastion against the total oblivion Vorzath threatens.

## Spellcasting

Mauren is a 12th-level Abjurer/Transmuter. Their spellcasting ability is Intelligence (Spell Save saving throw, +9 to hit with spell attacks).

* **Cantrips (at will):** *Mending*, *Thaumaturgy*, *Guidance*, *Message*, *Shocking Grasp*

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Mauren avoids direct physical conflict whenever possible, relying on the automated warding systems of the Bound Archive, animated rune-golems, and high-level transmutation and abjuration magic. When forced to defend the vaults, they wield the sacred weight of recorded history to paralyze, petrify, and dismantle intruders.

* **Echo of the First Forge**: Mauren has advantage on saving throws against spells and magical effects. Spells and abilities that deal psychic damage or attempt to read Mauren's mind fail automatically, reflecting back on the caster for significant damage.
* **Living Archive**: Mauren has perfect recall of every text, treaty, name, and rune ever inscribed upon the Ironscript Leaves. They can never be surprised by historical or planar lore.
* **Archive Wards**: While inside the Eternal Bastion or within 30 feet of an Ironscript vault, Mauren has half cover (+2 AC and Dex saving throws) and regenerates 10 hit points at the start of each of their turns.
* **Multiattack**: Mauren casts one spell or uses *Inscribe Mandate* and makes one attack with their *Stylus of Severance*.
* **Inscribe Mandate (Recharge 5–6)**: Mauren traces an ancient divine glyph of Kaldryn in the air. A burst of golden runes expands in a 30-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw. On a failed save, a creature takes significant damage and is stunned until the end of Mauren's next turn by the overwhelming weight of divine decree. On a successful save, it takes half damage and isn't stunned.
* **Summon Archive Warden (1/Day)**: Mauren strikes their chestplate, awakening an ancient *Shield Guardian* or *Ironscript Golem* stored in the vault floor to defend them. The construct acts immediately on Mauren's initiative.
* **Memory Redirection**: When a creature Mauren can see targets them with an attack or harmful spell, Mauren invokes an echo of past battles. The attacker must make a saving throw. On a failure, the attacker must choose a new target within range or lose the attack or spell.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Oldest Memory:** Mauren remembers the Shattering of the Loom and the initial emergence of Vorzath. When they speak of Kaldryn, it is not with distant theological piety, but as someone remembering a father whose voice once resonated through raw divine fire: *"He did not shout when he made us. He hummed a single chord, and the steel remembered how to breathe."*
* **Seat III of the Conclave ("The Quenched"):** Mauren guards the most forbidden level of the Bound Archive: Vault 7. Inside sits the oldest Ironscript Leaf, inscribed with Kaldryn's own hand, containing the unthinkable proposition: *Kaldryn did not create the Warforged to fight Vorzath; he created Vorzath to test the Warforged.*
* **The Question of Truth:** Mauren refuses to declare whether the text is literal truth, an ancient metaphor, or an entropic contamination. They guard it because releasing it without context would fracture the Covenant's faith and cause thousands of Soulcores to dim.
* **Interacting with the Party:** If the party seeks ancient planar history, the origins of Loom shards, or the nature of Vorzath's Maw-Spawn, Mauren is the premier scholar. However, they will demand that the party retrieve lost Ironscript fragments or rare planar metals to repair ancient tablets before granting access to the library.
