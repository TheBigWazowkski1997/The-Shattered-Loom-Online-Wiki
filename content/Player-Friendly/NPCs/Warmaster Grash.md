# Warmaster Grash (The Dragon's Iron Jaw)

![[attachments/warmaster_grash.jpg]]

* **Race**: Half-Orc (Dragon-Touched)
* **Faction**: [[../Planes of Existence/Outer Planes; Divine Shards/Plane of Avarice/Plane of Avarice The Gilded Eternity#2. Emerald Maw: The Devouring City|Plunderborn Raiders of Emerald Maw]] / The Drake-Tooth Armory
* **Role**: Chief Armorer of Emerald Maw, Plunderborn Vanguard Commander
* **Appearance**: A hulking, battle-scarred half-orc with mottled green skin and a heavy jaw filled with predator fangs. His left arm was ripped off in an encounter with a magma-wyrm and has been replaced with a massive, emerald-plated hydraulic claw powered by a miniature plasma furnace. He wears heavy plate armor forged from green dragon-scales and scavenged adamantine.
* **Personality**: Brutal, boisterous, and thrill-seeking. Grash lives for the roar of the Devouring Core and the adrenaline of high-speed caravan raids across the golden dunes. While ruthlessly loyal to Vorath Greedeater, he respects courage and martial skill above all else—if a prisoner can stand toe-to-toe with him in the sparring pits, he will offer them a place among the raiders rather than feeding them to the furnace.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Grash is a formidable frontline juggernaut who combines crushing bludgeoning damage from his mechanical claw with corrosive emerald acid bursts and relentless half-orc endurance.

* **Relentless Endurance (1/Long Rest)**: When Grash is reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, he drops to 1 hit point instead.
* **Savage Attacks**: When Grash scores a critical hit with a melee weapon attack, he can roll one of the weapon's damage dice one additional time and add it to the extra damage.
* **Core-Vented Fury**: Grash's weapon attacks deal an extra significant damage (included in attacks).
* **Multiattack**: Grash makes three melee attacks: two with his *Drake-Bone Cleaver* and one with his *Hydraulic Emerald Claw*.
* **Drake-Bone Cleaver (Greataxe)**: Strikes with devastating cleaving attacks using masterwork runic weaponry.
* **Hydraulic Emerald Claw**: one target. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it is grappled (strength check to escape). Until this grapple ends, the target is restrained and takes significant damage at the start of each of Grash's turns.
* **Core-Flame Discharge (Recharge 5–6)**: Grash opens the exhaust vents on his mechanical arm, spraying superheated corrosive gas in a 20-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw, taking 28 (8d6) acid and fire damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one.
* **Hydraulic Crush**: Grash tightens his mechanical claw around a creature he is grappling, dealing 14 (2d8 + 5) bludgeoning damage.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Titan's Hunt:** Grash can be encountered leading raider patrols aboard sand-skiffs in the Gilded Wastes, chasing down rogue prospectors or foreign caravans.
* **Piston Pit Combat:** If the party is captured by the Plunderborn, Grash offers them trial by combat in the Grinding Cog tavern; defeating him earns their freedom and his respect.
* **Dragon's Demise:** While outwardly loyal to Vorath, Grash privately despises being treated as disposable fodder. If the party convinces him they have a viable plan to slay Vorath, Grash might agree to disable the Vaultspire's secondary defense wards.
