# Warden-Captain Jaxen Vance (Lead Caravan Escort)

![[attachments/warden_jaxen_vance.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Human (Planar Ranger / Caliper Warden)
* **Faction**: [[../Factions/The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper|The Consortium of the Gilded Caliper]]
* **Role**: Lead Caravan Escort and Border Patrol
* **Appearance**: A weather-beaten man with a scarred face from decades of planar survival. He wears a cloak made of camouflage-enchanted displacement beast hide, whose edges blur and shimmer as if reality cannot quite settle on his silhouette. He carries a composite longbow that glows with subtle druidic runes, and a pair of serrated shortswords hang crossed at his back.
* **Personality**: Sarcastic, street-smart, and highly pragmatic. Having survived decades in the planar wilds, he has seen almost everything and trusts his gut over guild politics. He speaks in short, deadpan observations and has a dark sense of humor about close calls with death.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Jaxen is a mobile skirmisher who excels at planar terrain navigation, rapid repositioning, and multi-target suppression. He uses his displacement hide to misdirect attackers and strikes from unexpected angles.

* **Displacement Cloak**: Attack rolls against Jaxen have disadvantage until he is hit. When he is hit, the cloak deactivates until the start of his next turn.
* **Favored Terrain (Planar Rifts)**: Jaxen has advantage on Perception, Survival, and Stealth checks in extraplanar environments and cannot be tracked by magical means.
* **Uncanny Dodge**: When an attacker that Jaxen can see hits him with an attack, he can use his reaction to halve the attack's damage.
* **Multiattack**: Jaxen makes three attacks: two with his Runic Composite Bow or two with his Serrated Shortswords and one with the other.
* **Runic Composite Bow**: *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, range 150/600 ft., one target.
* **Serrated Shortsword**: one target. If the target is bleeding (below half HP), it takes an additional significant damage.
* **Smoke Grenade (3/Day)**: Jaxen throws a canister of planar smoke at a point within 30 feet. A 20-foot sphere of obscuring smoke fills the area for 1 minute. Jaxen can see through this smoke.
* **Cunning Action**: Jaxen can Dash, Disengage, or Hide as a bonus action.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Guide:** When the party needs to travel between the three cities of the Plane of Eternal Craft, Jaxen is the best escort available. He knows the planar routes, the hazard timing (molten storm windows, clockwork rust-monster migration patterns, crystal rifts), and every tavern worth sleeping in.
* **The Secret Bribe:** Jaxen has been taking bribes from a Timberforge smuggling ring to let shipments of "unregulated" enchanted wood through Metallos checkpoints. He only does it to fund an orphanage in the outer slums of Metallos. He is not proud of it and will shut down the operation if confronted with genuine proof, not blackmail.
* **Earning His Respect:** Jaxen trusts competence. If a player demonstrates field awareness (checking exits, watching for tails, preparing for hazards), he respects them quietly. If they blunder into an obvious trap, he will say: *"Yep. Saw that coming."*
