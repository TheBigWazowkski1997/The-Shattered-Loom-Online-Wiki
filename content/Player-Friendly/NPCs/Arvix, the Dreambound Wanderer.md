# Arvix, the Dreambound Wanderer (Tragic Antihero)

![[attachments/arvix_dreambound.jpeg]]

* **Race**: Human
* **Faction**: None (self-imposed exile; former Pale Lodge operative)
* **Role**: Reluctant Ally, Antihero, Potential Betrayer
* **Appearance**: A scarred human warrior whose haunted eyes glow with a faint, pulsing silver light — the residue of the Great Dreamer's psychic corruption. He wears armor forged from astral essence, dark as the void but shot through with silver veins that pulse like a slow heartbeat. His sword — **Dreamrender** — is a massive two-handed blade that hums with contained psychic energy, its edge leaving a faint after-image when swung.
* **Personality**: Quiet, guarded, and volatile in a controlled way. He speaks only when he has something worth saying, and everything he says carries the weight of grief. He despises the Great Dreamer with the white-hot intensity of someone whose family was consumed by it. He does not want to be anyone's hero.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Arvix is a devastating frontline fighter who channels residual psychic energy through Dreamrender. He fights with the focused aggression of someone who has nothing left to lose — but holds back just enough to stay alive for his goal. His biggest weakness is his vendetta: if he sees an opportunity to damage the Dreamer directly, he will take it regardless of tactical consequences.

* **Dream Corruption**: Arvix has been psychically touched by the Great Dreamer. He has advantage on saving throws against enchantment and illusion spells but has disadvantage on Wisdom saving throws against fear effects tied to the Dreamer.
* **Astral Armor**: While wearing his armor, Arvix can use his reaction to reduce the damage of any single attack by 7 (2d6) once per short rest.
* **Bitter Purpose**: Arvix has advantage on all attack rolls against creatures directly serving the Great Dreamer (Dreambound, Dreamspawn, Xelthar, etc.).
* **Multiattack**: Arvix makes three attacks with Dreamrender.
* **Dreamrender**: one target. On a critical hit, the target must succeed on a saving throw or be stunned until the end of its next turn as psychic energy overloads its mind.
* **Psychic Sundering (Recharge 5–6)**: Arvix plunges Dreamrender into the ground. A wave of psychic force erupts in a 20-foot radius. Each creature of his choice must succeed on a saving throw, taking significant damage on a failure, or half on a success.
* **Vendetta Rush**: If Arvix can see a creature linked to the Great Dreamer, he can move up to 30 feet toward it without triggering opportunity attacks from other creatures.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **The Reluctant Ally:** Arvix approaches the party because they appear capable of reaching the Dreamer — not because he trusts them. He will help, guard their backs, and share tactical information about the Ziggurat's layout (from prior failed incursions).
* **The Betrayal Trigger:** If the party hesitates, negotiates with the Dreambound, or suggests that the Dreamer might be useful or salvageable, Arvix's patience breaks. He may try to go ahead alone, which will almost certainly kill him. The DM should use this as a "clock" to create urgency.
* **What He Lost:** His family — a wife and two daughters — were consumed by a Dreamspawn incursion on a small coastal town. He has their names tattooed on his forearm. If a player notices and asks, he will answer with a single quiet sentence and change the subject. He never discusses it again unless he trusts the person completely.
* **The Sword's Secret:** Dreamrender was forged using a fragment of the Dreamer's nightmare-energy, harvested at great cost by a Pale Lodge artificer. It can sever psychic links and dismiss Dreamspawn with a successful strike. The Dreamer is dimly aware of it and has a faint, instinctive "dislike" of Arvix.
