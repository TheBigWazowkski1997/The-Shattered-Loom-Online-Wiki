# Forge-Mistress Skraka Bone-Grinder (The Carapace Smith)

![[attachments/skraka_bone_grinder.jpg]]

* **Race**: Bugbear (Abyssal-touched)
* **Faction**: The Clingers / Mawhollow Forge Guild
* **Role**: Chief Armorer of Mawhollow, Chitin & Bone Artisan
* **Appearance**: A hulking, battle-scarred bugbear female standing seven feet tall. Large segments of her arms and shoulders are grafted with shimmering, iridescent black chitin plates harvested from apex abyssal predators. She wears a soot-stained leather apron fastened with predator jawbones and wields a massive two-handed bone-crushing sledgehammer called *Marrow-Sunder*.
* **Personality**: Gruff, fiercely practical, and relentless. Skraka respects physical resilience and honest craftsmanship. In a plane where starvation drives most creatures to madness, she values stability and self-sufficiency above all else. She has no patience for squeamish planar travelers who whine about the macabre materials she works with.

## Known Abilities & Combat Style

Skraka is a formidable bruiser who combines crushing bludgeoning damage with acidic bone-shrapnel and relentless endurance.

* **Brute**: A melee weapon deals one extra die of its damage when Skraka hits with it (included in the attack).
* **Acidic Retaliation**: When a creature within 5 feet of Skraka hits her with a melee attack, jagged shards of acidic carapace burst from her armor, dealing 7 (2d6) acid and piercing damage to the attacker.
* **Surprise Attack**: If Skraka surprises a creature and hits it with an attack during the first round of combat, the target takes an extra 10 (3d6) damage from the attack.
* **Multiattack**: Skraka makes three melee attacks with *Marrow-Sunder* or two ranged attacks with *Acid Bile Javelins*.
* **Marrow-Sunder (Maul)**: one target. If the target is a Medium or smaller creature, it must succeed on a saving throw or be knocked prone.
* **Acid Bile Javelin**: *Ranged Weapon Attack:* +8 to hit, range 30/120 ft., one target.
* **Bone-Shrapnel Roar (Recharge 5–6)**: Skraka vents superheated marrow steam and pulverized bone from her armor vents in a 20-foot radius. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw, taking 24 (7d6) piercing and acid damage on a failed save, or half as much damage on a successful one.

## Lore & Roleplay Notes

* **Forge Inquiries**: Skraka will gladly forge exotic weapons and armor from any rare monster parts or divine bones brought to her by the party, often discounting her labor if she gets to keep a fraction of the harvest.
* **Gorge Navigation**: She knows safe climbing routes down into the lower Chasm and can provide the party with climbing advice or sturdy climbing gear.
* **Quest Hook**: Skraka needs an intact carapace from an ancient Gorger-Beast in the Gnashing Plains to complete an experimental set of acid-immune dread-plate.
