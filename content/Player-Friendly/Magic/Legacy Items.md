##  **Ability Descriptions**

### **Woven Ward**

While holding the Codex, you gain a **+1 bonus to AC** and saving throws. This bonus increases to **+2 at 10th level** and **+3 at 15th level**.

### **Sentinel’s Prayer**

As a bonus action, you can invoke a prayer of protection. You cast **Shield of Faith** without expending a spell slot or requiring concentration. The spell lasts for 1 minute. You can use this feature a number of times equal to your **proficiency bonus** per long rest.

### **Resilience of the Loom**

You gain **resistance to force damage**. Additionally, you have advantage on saving throws against being moved against your will or knocked prone.

### **Threaded Shield**

When a creature you can see within **30 feet** is hit by an attack, you can use your **reaction** to manifest a web of golden threads around them. The target gains **resistance** to the damage of that attack.

### **Bastion of the Weave**

As an action, you can slam the Codex shut, releasing a pulse of stabilizing energy. All friendly creatures within **20 feet** gain **temporary hit points** equal to twice your character level. While they have these hit points, they are immune to the **frightened** condition. You can use this once per long rest.

### **Aura of the Iron Covenant**

The threads of the Loom bind your allies to your resolve. Friendly creatures within **10 feet** of you gain a bonus to all saving throws equal to your **spellcasting ability modifier** (minimum of +1). If you already have the *Aura of Protection* feature, the range of that aura increases by 10 feet instead.

### **Planar Anchor**

You are a fixed point in a shifting multiverse. You are immune to any effect that would teleport you or banish you to another plane against your will. As an action, you can extend this immunity to all allies within **10 feet** for 1 minute (1/day).

### **Avatar of the Weaver (1/day)**

As an action, you undergo a celestial transformation. For 1 minute, your form becomes semi-translucent, woven from threads of pure light:

* You gain **resistance to all damage**.
* Hostile creatures that start their turn within **20 feet** of you must succeed on a **Strength saving throw (DC = your spell save DC)** or be **restrained** by golden threads.
* You can cast **Lesser Restoration** as a bonus action on each of your turns without expending a spell slot.

### **Eternal Sanctuary**

The Codex can manifest a "Shatter-Proof" zone. Once per long rest, you can cast **Globe of Vulnerability** (centered on yourself) or **Temple of the Gods**. When cast this way, the structure is made of reinforced Loom-threads and cannot be dispelled by anything short of a *Wish* spell.

### **Mantle of the Prime Weaver**

At 17th level, you radiate the authoritative grace of Telara. You gain **Truesight** out to 60 feet. Furthermore, you can cast **Holy Aura** once per long rest without expending a spell slot. While the aura is active, any creature of your choice that starts its turn within the aura has its soul-threads "tightened," granting them advantage on all attack rolls and saving throws until the start of their next turn.

### **The Unbroken Reality**

At 19th level, you become a living anchor for the multiverse. You are immune to being erased from existence or having your form altered against your will (such as by the *Polymorph* or *Petrified* condition). Once per long rest, you can use an action to invoke the "Unbroken Reality." For 1 minute, you and all allies within 60 feet cannot have your hit point maximum reduced, and if any affected creature is reduced to 0 hit points, they instead drop to 1 hit point and become immune to all damage until the end of their next turn. This immunity can only trigger once per creature per use of this ability.

## **Ability Descriptions**

### **Forge's Breath**
The axe glows with the inner heat of the forge, casting dim light in a 10-foot radius. The wielder can suppress or resume this light at will (no action required).

### **Smoldering Strike**
On a hit, the axe deals an additional **1d4 fire damage**. 

### **Ember's Warmth**
While holding the axe, you gain **resistance to fire damage** and are unaffected by extreme heat environments.

### **Sundering Blow**
Once per turn, when you hit a creature with the axe wielded in two hands, you can choose to make a sundering strike. The target's Armor Class is reduced by 1 until the start of your next turn (this effect does not stack with itself).

### **Molten Cleave**
When you hit a creature with a melee attack using Cinderheart, you can choose to cause molten metal to splash from the wound. One creature of your choice within 5 feet of the target takes fire damage equal to your Strength modifier.

### **Burning Edge**
The intense heat within the blade intensifies. The extra fire damage dealt by the weapon increases:
* **+1d6** at 10th level.

# **Grudgestone Knuckles**

_Legacy Weapon (knuckles), requires attunement by a creature proficient with unarmed strikes_

> _"Carved from the deep roots of a mountain that refused to fall, these knuckles were worn by the dwarf vanguard-captain Orel Flintskin. When his fortress was breached, he stood at the gates alone, his stone fists shattering iron shields and crushing plate armor until the mountain itself collapsed upon the siege. The stone did not break, and neither did his grudge. Since then, the knuckles find their way into the hands of those who refuse to budge, those who fight with raw, unyielding mechanics rather than magic."_

## **Weapon Properties**

* **Weapon Type**: Knuckles (Unarmed Strike enhancement; counts as a light, finesse weapon for the purpose of class features, feats, and two-weapon fighting)
* **Damage**: 1d8 bludgeoning (or your unarmed strike damage, whichever is higher)
* **Appearance**: Thick straps of iron-reinforced leather wrapped around heavy plates of dark, basalt stone. Runes of anchoring are etched deep into the stone, glowing only with the dull red heat of friction when struck.

## **Ability Descriptions**

### **Awakening of Stone**
You gain a **+1 bonus** to attack and damage rolls made with your unarmed strikes. Your unarmed strikes count as magical for the purpose of overcoming resistance and immunity to nonmagical attacks and damage.
Additionally, your unarmed strike damage increases by one die step (e.g., `1 -> 1d4 -> 1d6 -> 1d8 -> 1d10 -> 1d12 -> 2d6`). If your unarmed strike damage is already scaling (such as through Monk levels), you instead add a flat **+1 bonus to damage rolls** with your unarmed strikes.

### **Rooted Stance**
Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can attempt to knock the target prone. The target must succeed on a Strength saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + the ability modifier used for the attack roll) or be knocked prone. This requires no resource or ki.

### **Meteor Weight**
Your fists strike with the momentum of falling boulders. Once per turn, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can deal an additional **1d4 bludgeoning damage**. At 12th level, this extra damage increases to **1d6**.

### **Break the Line**
When you score a critical hit with an unarmed strike, your opponent's momentum is utterly shattered:
* The target’s speed becomes 0 until the start of your next turn.
* The target has disadvantage on the next attack roll it makes before the start of your next turn.

### **Grudge Mark**
Once per long rest, when a creature deals damage to you, you can mark them as your Grudge (no action required). For 1 minute, or until the target drops to 0 hit points, you gain the following benefits against them:
* You deal an additional **2 damage** on each hit with your unarmed strikes against the marked target.
* You have **advantage on saving throws** against spells, features, and effects created by the marked target.

### **Gatebreaker Blow**
Once per short rest, when you hit a creature with an unarmed strike, you can channel earth-shattering force into the blow. The target must make a Constitution saving throw (DC = 8 + your proficiency bonus + the ability modifier used for the attack roll):
* **On a failure**, the target is **stunned** until the end of your next turn.
* **On a success**, the target takes an additional **2d8 bludgeoning damage** as the shockwave ripples through them anyway.

### **Enduring Stone**
Once per long rest, when you are reduced to 0 hit points but not killed outright, you can choose to drop to 1 hit point instead. This represents pure refusal to fall.

### **Legacy of the Breach**
Once per week, you can use your action to make a single, devastating unarmed strike against a creature within your reach. If the attack misses, the weekly use is not expended.
If the attack hits:
* It deals maximum damage (all damage dice are treated as having rolled their highest possible value).
* It ignores resistance to bludgeoning damage.
* It automatically counts as a critical hit if the target is Large or larger.

To unlock the knuckles' true power, the wielder must undergo three grueling trials of endurance and resolve:

### **Ritual of the Crushed Earth (Level 5)**
> _"To shatter iron, you must first become harder than the stone."_
* **Requirements**: The wielder must spend 24 hours carrying a stone of at least 100 lbs on their back without letting it touch the ground, then shatter it with a single unarmed strike.
* **Cost**: 1,500 gp and 120 XP.

### **Ritual of the Iron Root (Level 10)**
> _"The mountain does not move because the wind blows."_

* **Cost**: 13,000 gp and 1,000 XP.

### **Ritual of the Granite Grudge (Level 15)**
> _"The stone remembers every strike. So must you."_
* **Requirements**: The wielder must carve the name of a sworn enemy into a slab of granite using only their bare knuckles, and then defeat that enemy (or a creature of CR 10 or higher that has harmed them or their allies) in single combat.
* **Cost**: 40,000 gp and 3,000 XP.

The Clockwork Curio is a renowned workshop and store located in a bustling city or magical hub, catering to adventurers, scholars, and explorers seeking reliable, customized clockwork companions. Owned by the eccentric and ingenious Master Tinkerer Cylix Gremlincoil, this shop is a wonderland of mechanical marvels, filled with the soft whirring of gears, the ticking of intricate devices, and the occasional chirp or squeak of a newly crafted automaton.
From loyal guardians to utility-focused assistants, Cylix designs each companion to fit the specific needs of their clients, making the Clockwork Curio a favorite among adventurers who desire something unique to aid them on their journeys.
The Storefront
The exterior of the Clockwork Curio is a spectacle that draws the eye of any passerby. The building itself is an elaborate piece of craftsmanship, featuring:
Gleaming brass-and-steel architecture with clockwork gears embedded into its walls, constantly turning in mesmerizing patterns.
A rotating sign depicting a mechanical owl holding a gear in its talons, with the store’s motto: “Precision. Loyalty. Ingenuity.”
Large glass windows showcasing an array of small clockwork creatures in motion—mechanical birds hopping on perches, tiny automaton dogs wagging their tails, and intricate arachnid scouts weaving webs of copper thread.
Inside the Store
The interior of the Clockwork Curio is a chaotic-yet-cozy workshop, combining retail space with a working laboratory. Visitors are greeted by:
Rows of display cases filled with miniature and medium-sized clockwork creations, each labeled with their name, purpose, and features.
Shelves stacked with components: brass gears, crystal cores, enchanted screws, and shimmering metal plates etched with runes.
A central counter cluttered with tools, half-finished projects, and small mechanical arms that occasionally pass Cylix a needed tool or clean up the workspace.
Chirping and buzzing companions scuttle and hover throughout the shop, performing tasks or simply observing the customers.
The Owner: Cylix Gremlincoil
