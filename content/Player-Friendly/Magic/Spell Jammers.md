### **1\. The Aether Gale**

* **Design**: A sleek, crystalline vessel shaped like a falcon in mid-flight, with wings made of shifting energy that glow with faint, colorful auroras. The hull is composed of enchanted quartz that reflects starlight and enhances its magical propulsion.
* **Fuel Source**: **Aether Crystals**
	* Aether Crystals are mined from rare pockets of raw magic in the Astral Sea. When channeled, they release bursts of **aetheric wind**, which propel the Spelljammer at incredible speeds.
	* **Fuel Perk**: Provides bursts of speed for short-term travel or a powerful energy shield in emergencies.
	* **Drawback**: Aether Crystals burn out quickly, and excessive use risks shattering the crystal and causing localized planar disruptions.
* **Crew Consideration**: Requires an **Aether Weaver**—a skilled mage or artificer attuned to controlling the magical winds generated by the crystals.

### **2\. The Emberwing**

* **Design**: A massive phoenix-shaped vessel forged from blackened steel and enameled in fiery gold. Its engine glows like a furnace, and its wings leave a trail of ember sparks as it flies.
* **Fuel Source**: **Phoenix Ashes**
	* Phoenix Ashes are imbued with a fragment of the rebirth cycle of phoenixes. When burned, they release a self-renewing fire that powers the ship’s furnace-like core.
	* **Fuel Perk**: Provides incredible power for long journeys and allows the ship to briefly phase through hostile environments, such as fiery realms or volcanic zones.
	* **Drawback**: Phoenix Ashes are incredibly rare, and harvesting them often angers phoenix spirits, potentially cursing the crew.
* **Crew Consideration**: The Emberwing thrives under the care of **Firekin Engineers**, often fire genasi or salamanders, who are immune to heat and specialize in maintaining the furnace core.

### **3\. The Verdant Voyager**

* **Design**: A living, organic ship resembling a massive, hollowed-out tree entwined with vines, leaves, and glowing spores. Its sails are massive, bio-luminescent leaves, and its hull pulses faintly with life.
* **Fuel Source**: **Planar Sap**
	* Planar Sap is a viscous, glowing liquid harvested from **worldtrees** or plants with deep ties to the natural magic of the multiverse. The sap acts as a regenerative fuel, both powering and healing the living vessel.
	* **Fuel Perk**: The ship heals itself over time, reducing repair costs, and its connection to nature allows it to navigate through wild, entangled planar realms.
	* **Drawback**: The sap requires constant care and replenishment, and neglecting the ship’s living ecosystem causes it to weaken or even wither entirely.
* **Crew Consideration**: Requires **Druids or Botanists** who can tend to the ship’s living systems and cultivate additional sap-producing growths onboard.

### **4\. The Starbound Colossus**

* **Design**: A massive, mechanical leviathan resembling a humanoid knight with wings of steel. The ship's hull is constructed from interlocking gears and polished star-metal, and its propulsion system resembles a heart-like core surrounded by glowing, spinning rings.
* **Fuel Source**: **Primordial Cores**
	* Primordial Cores are condensed essences of elemental energy, harvested from defeated elementals or natural disasters. These cores release controlled bursts of elemental power to propel the ship.
	* **Fuel Perk**: Depending on the elemental core used (fire, air, water, or earth), the ship gains different benefits, such as increased speed, durability, or the ability to bypass specific planar barriers.
	* **Drawback**: Mixing elemental cores can destabilize the core’s containment, causing explosive damage to the ship.
* **Crew Consideration**: **Elementalists** or artificers are required to manage and stabilize the Primordial Core’s energy, and the ship often attracts hostile elementals seeking vengeance.

### **5\. The Whispering Wraith**

* **Design**: A spectral, ethereal ship made of shadow and bone, with glowing runes etched into its skeletal hull. The ship seems to phase in and out of existence, leaving behind ghostly trails of light.
* **Fuel Source**: **Souls of the Forgotten**
	* This macabre Spelljammer is powered by the **residual essence of forgotten souls**, collected from realms of death or shadows. These souls fuel the ship’s spectral engines, allowing it to phase between planes and move undetected.
	* **Fuel Perk**: The ship becomes incorporeal for short bursts, bypassing physical barriers and avoiding detection by enemies.
	* **Drawback**: Using the souls of the forgotten angers entities tied to death, such as liches, gods of the afterlife, or the souls themselves, which can revolt and sabotage the ship.
* **Crew Consideration**: Requires a **Soulbinder**—a necromancer or cleric adept at managing the restless spirits powering the ship.
