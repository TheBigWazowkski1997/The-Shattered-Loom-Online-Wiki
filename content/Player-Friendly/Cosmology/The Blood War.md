# **The Blood War: The Weaver’s Conflict**

In the wake of the **Shattering of the Loom**, the multiverse is caught in an eternal, binary struggle between two extreme versions of existence. This is not a war over territory or resources, but over the **fundamental state of reality**. The Blood War is the friction between the absolute rigidity of the **Nine Hells** and the absolute entropy of **The Abyss**. 

In the Shattered Loom cosmology, this conflict is known as the **Weaver’s Conflict**. It is a war that can never end, for if one side were to truly triumph, the multiverse would either freeze into a sterile, unchanging cage or dissolve into a sea of non-existent static.

---

## **The Two Fronts: Rigidity vs. Entropy**

### **1. The Stitchers (The Nine Hells)**
*   **Mission**: To "Hard-Fix" reality. The Devils believe the Loom failed because it allowed for the "Slack" of free will and chaotic potential. Their goal is to bind every remaining thread into a rigid, permanent structure—the **Iron Spindle**.
*   **Strategy**: **Calculated Consolidation**. They operate like a massive, industrial machine, slowly claiming Material Plane shards, "Stitching" them into the Hells' logic, and fortifying them against the Abyss.
*   **The Price of Victory**: A "Frozen Reality" where no new stories can be woven, and every soul is a fixed, unchanging cog in Asmodeus’s machine.

### **2. The Unravelers (The Abyss)**
*   **Mission**: To "Shred" existence. The Demons are the personification of the Loom’s failure. They believe that any structure is a prison. Their goal is to pull at every loose thread until the entire multiverse unravels back into the primal chaos of the **Great Unraveling**.
*   **Strategy**: **Entropic Surge**. They do not use tactics so much as they use "Planar Bleed." Where the **Great Dreamer** passes, the Abyss follows, flooding reality with "Abyssal Static" that causes matter and identity to simply fall apart.
*   **The Price of Victory**: Absolute non-existence—a state of infinite, un-spun potential where nothing ever becomes real.

---

## **The Primary Battlefields: Shatterpoints**

The Blood War is fought primarily at **Shatterpoints**—regions where the boundaries between the Hells, the Abyss, and the Material Plane have become dangerously thin.

### **1. Avernus: The Bulwark of Law**
Avernus is the front line. It is a "Planar Scar" where the Hells’ rigidity directly meets the Abyss’s entropy. 
*   **The Bronze Citadel**: The Hells’ primary defensive hub. It is a moving fortress that "Seals" Abyssal rifts as soon as they appear. 
*   **The Frayed Wastes**: The literal border where threads are being pulled in two directions at once. Landscapes here "glitch," flickering between scorched iron and dissolving grey static.

### **2. The Iron Spindle’s Reach**
The Hells often launch "Stitch-Incursions" into the Abyss, seeking to "Harden" specific Fracture-Zones to stabilize them. These are suicide missions intended to "anchor" the Abyss, preventing it from spreading.

### **3. Material Shard Proxy Wars**
Both sides seek to claim "Uncommitted Shards" of the Material Plane. 
*   **Devils** offer "Pacts of Stabilization" to dying worlds, promising to stop the unraveling in exchange for the world's "Future-Threads."
*   **Demons** simply accelerate the rot, feeding on the fear and chaos caused by the world's collapse.

---

## **Key Figures & Their Roles**

### **Zariel: The Fallen Weaver**
Zariel is the architect of the Hells' defense. She views the Blood War as a **"Repair Project."** Every Abyssal rift she seals is a thread she has "Stitched" back into the Iron Spindle. She is loathed by the Abyss and pitied by the **Pale Lodge**, who see her as someone trying to save the world by turning it into a prison.

### **The Lords of the Fray (Demon Lords)**
The Demon Lords do not coordinate; they are "Entropic Storms." 
*   **Demogorgon** seeks to split reality so it can never be stitched.
*   **Orcus** seeks to rot the threads so they are too brittle to bind.
*   **Lolth** uses the war as a distraction, "Snaring" threads from both sides to weave her own dark web.

### **Pale Night: The Architect of Entropy**
Pale Night is the "Whisperer" who ensures the war continues. She knows that as long as the Hells and the Abyss are locked in conflict, the **Great Dreamer** can continue to feed on the rest of the multiverse undisturbed.

---

## **The Role of the Soul-Thread**

In the Blood War, souls are the primary currency and ammunition:
*   **Devils** harvest **Soul-Threads** to provide the raw material for the Iron Spindle. A soul bound by contract is a thread that can be perfectly aligned.
*   **Demons** consume **Soul-Threads** to fuel their own chaotic manifestations. A consumed soul is a thread that is permanently unraveled, removed from the Loom forever.

---

## **The Stance of Other Factions**

*   **The Pale Lodge**: They maintain the **"Policy of Necessary Friction."** They hate the Blood War, but they know that if either side won, the multiverse would end. They often intervene to prevent one side from gaining a decisive advantage, effectively "Stitching" or "Unraveling" just enough to keep the conflict in equilibrium.
*   **The Cult of the Broken Thread**: They revere the Blood War as the **"Grand Unmaking."** They often act as saboteurs, helping the Abyss bypass the Hells' defenses to ensure reality continues to fracture.
