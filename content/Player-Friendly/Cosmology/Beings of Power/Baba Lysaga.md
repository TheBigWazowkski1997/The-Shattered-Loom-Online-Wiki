# **Baba Lysaga, the Weaver of Withered Threads**

*“You think you understand the Weave, child? You think because you carry the Weaver’s touch you can simply pull at our threads and make us unravel? I was stitching Strahd’s greatness into the stars while your ancestors were still crawling through the dirt. Here in Berez, we do not mend the Loom. We let it rot, and in the rot, we find eternity.”*

---

### **Overview**
**Baba Lysaga** is an ancient, fanatical arch-hag and one of the most terrifying **Beings of Power** in the Shadowfell. Believing herself to be the true architect of Count Strahd von Zarovich's destiny, she operates from the drowned, plague-ridden **Ruins of Berez**. While Strahd anchors the domain with the **Crimson Weave**, Baba Lysaga acts as his hidden shield. She has choked the **Swamp Fane** (one of the three ancient ley-energy hubs of Barovia) and spun its power into the **Weft of Rot and Rebirth**, weaving the very mists that isolate Barovia and protect it from the unraveling multiverse.

---

### **History: The Mother of the Crimson Knot**
*   **The Nursemaid's Obsession**: Long before the Loom shattered, Baba Lysaga was the midwife and nursemaid to a young Strahd. Her fanatical devotion to the boy bordered on madness. She performed dark rituals in secret, placing a spark of ancient fey magic within him and sowing the seeds of his inevitable ascension. When Strahd’s mother banished her for her dark practices, Lysaga did not despair; she merely watched from the shadows, convinced that she alone was his true mother.
*   **The Shattered Shield**: When the Loom of Telara broke and the Shadowfell began to dissolve, Baba Lysaga was not caught off guard. As Strahd claimed the Crimson Weave, she gathered the drifting, corrupted fey threads and took over the ruins of Berez. By twisting the Swamp Fane, she locked the southern borders of the domain in a stasis of decay.
*   **The Fane-Wardens**: To defend the land's spiritual nodes and secure Strahd's immortality, she stole three chromatic dragon eggs and subjected them to the ritual of the fey-shadow fray. She raised her three "darling daughters"—a coven of Adult Hag Dragons—and bound each to one of Barovia's three ancient Fanes, with herself as their matriarch and leader.

---

### **Appearance**
Baba Lysaga is a hunched, repulsive crone whose skin is the color of bruised clay. Her hands are constantly in motion, her long, claw-like fingers weaving invisible or spectral grey threads that rise from the swamp water.
*   **The Dragon-Skull Chariot**: She travels through the swamp by flying inside a giant, hollowed-out skull of an ancient black dragon—the very mother of the eggs she stole to create her coven.
*   **The Cloak of Shrouds**: Her tattered, mud-stained cloak is woven from the funeral shrouds of Berez's dead, humming with the faint, mournful whispers of the soulless.
*   **The Amber Eye**: At her throat, she wears a heavy iron pendant holding a single large, orange orb—an eye taken from her first hag dragon wyrmling, allowing her to share the sight of her coven.

---

### **The Ruins of Berez: The Swamp-Engine**
In Baba Lysaga's hands, the swamp has become a metaphysical defense system and the center of her power.
*   **The Swamp Fane**: The ancient Swamp Fane is located beneath her hut, guarded by her daughter **Vervain the Spindle**. It has been wrapped in thick, rotten roots and silver thread, pumping a slow, chronal decay into the land to feed the dragons.
*   **The Creeping Hut**: Her home is a rotting wooden hovel built upon the stump of a giant, ancient tree. Animated by a corrupted weave node (a stolen seed of power from the winery), the hut can rise on massive, root-like legs to crush intruders.
*   **The Veil of Mist**: By draining the lifespans of those who perish in the swamp, Baba Lysaga fuels the grey mists of Barovia, reinforcing Strahd’s "Firewall" against the Tide of Threads.

---

### **The Hag Dragon Coven: The Three Sisters**
To maximize their defensive footprint, Baba Lysaga has distributed her three Adult Hag Dragons to the three Fanes of Barovia. Through a ritual known as the **Chronal Link**, they remain linked in a single magical consciousness. Together, they form a **Draconic Coven** that shares spell slots and can project echoes of themselves to cast powerful magic (see [[../../Species/Hag Dragon|Hag Dragon]]):
1.  **Vervain the Spindle (Swamp Fane - Berez)**: Yellow-mottled scales dripping with acidic slime. Vervain accelerates decay, rotting wood, iron, and flesh in a matter of seconds.
2.  **Morrigan the Knot-Tangler (Forest Fane - Yester Hill)**: Pale yellow scales that shift and shimmer like swamp gas. Morrigan weaves illusions and distorts space, trapping intruders in recursive path-loops.
3.  **Gryla the Shear (Mountain Fane - Mt. Ghakis)**: Mottled brown scales with a crown of jagged horn-shears. Gryla focuses on chronal manipulation, slowing enemies down and stripping away their future actions.

---

### **Personality & Philosophy**
*   **Fanatical Matriarch**: Baba Lysaga’s entire existence is dedicated to Strahd. She views him as a god and herself as his divine mother. She is fiercely protective of him, even though she keeps her presence hidden from him to avoid his wrath.
*   **Cynical Weaver**: She mocks those who wish to mend the Loom. She believes that a whole, orderly multiverse is a cage, and that the only true freedom is to rot and reform in the dark corners of the Nexus.
*   **Cruel but Maternal**: While she is monstrously cruel to outsiders, she treats her hag dragons and her creeping hut with unsettling motherly affection, speaking to them in sweet baby-talk while ordering them to tear travelers apart.

---

### **Plot Hooks**
1.  **Cleansing the Swamp Fane**: If the players wish to strip Strahd of his re-weaving invulnerability (Option A in [[../../Sessions/Session 3 - The Blood and the Loom|Session 3 - The Blood and the Loom]]), they must travel to Berez, defeat Baba Lysaga, and dismantle her coven of hag dragons to cleanse the Swamp Fane.
2.  **The Stolen Years**: A vital ally of the players (such as a Martikov scout or a Pale Lodge agent) has been caught by the coven. Their lifespan has been reduced to a single day by the dragons’ breath. The players must hunt the dragons in Berez to kill them and reclaim the stolen years before it is too late.
3.  **The Weaver's Gambit**: Baba Lysaga contacts the players in secret. She offers them safe passage and information on the Crimson Weave's vulnerabilities, but only if they retrieve a "Loom Fragment of the Verdant Weave" from the wereravens of the Wizard of Wines winery and deliver it to her to strengthen the Swamp Fane.
