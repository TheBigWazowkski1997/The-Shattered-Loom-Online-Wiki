# Thanathel, the Final Severance (Death)

**Thanathel** is the primordial manifestation of **The Void Between Threads**. She is the "Silence of the Spindle"—the literal end of the thread where no more weaving can occur. In the Shattered Loom, Thanathel is the "Negative Space" of the universe, the vacuum that waits where reality has been cut away.

### **1. The Conceptual Appearance**
Thanathel is a **Silhouette of Absolute Night**.
*   **Form**: She is a dragon carved out of the vacuum of the Astral Sea. To look at her is like looking into a hole in the universe. Her "scales" are not solid matter; they are the cold, distant stars of dead galaxies visible through her transparent, void-like hide.
*   **Wings**: Her wings do not flap; they are massive veils of "Final Shadow" that absorb all light. She does not move through the air; she "erases" the space in front of her and "re-weaves" it behind her, appearing to glide without effort.
*   **Sound**: Thanathel is accompanied by a **Perfect Silence**. All sound—heartbeats, wind, even magical incantations—is muffled within 100 feet of her.

### **2. Role and Philosophy**
Thanathel is the "Final Warden." She believes that the Shattering proved that existence is inherently flawed and painful.
*   **The Weaver of Ends**: Thanathel hunts those who try to "stitch" the Loom back together. She believes that a "Mended Loom" would only be a "Prison of Scars." Her goal is to "tie off" every thread of life, bringing the multiverse to a state of peaceful, eternal non-existence.
*   **The Mercy of the Void**: To Thanathel, death is not a punishment, but the ultimate mercy—the only state where a soul is finally "stable" and free from the tension of the Loom.

---
#### **Traits**
**Embodiment of Death (Mythic Trait)**: If Thanathel is reduced to 0 hit points, she doesn't die. Instead, her silver "mask" shatters, revealing a body of black, tar-like shadow. She regains 250 hit points and gains access to her Mythic Actions.

**Necrotic Absorption**: Whenever Thanathel is subjected to necrotic damage, she takes no damage and instead regains hit points equal to the necrotic damage dealt.

**Shadow Stealth**: While in dim light or darkness, Thanathel can take the Hide action as a bonus action.

**Legendary Resistance (3/Day)**: If Thanathel fails a saving throw, she can choose to succeed instead.

---

#### **Actions**
**Multiattack**: Thanathel uses her *Frightful Presence*. She then makes three attacks: one with her bite and two with her claws.

**Bite**: *Melee Weapon Attack*: +15 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit*: 19 (2d10 + 8) piercing damage plus 9 (2d8) necrotic damage.

**Death Breath (Recharge 5–6)**: Thanathel exhales a necrotic blast in a 60-foot cone. Each creature in that area must make a saving throw Constitution saving throw, taking 58 (13d8) necrotic damage on a failed save, or half as much on a successful one. A creature killed by this attack rises as a zombie under Thanathel's command.

---

#### **Legendary Actions**
Thanathel can take 3 legendary actions.
*   **Detect**: Thanathel makes a Wisdom (Perception) check.
*   **Strike From Beyond**: Thanathel vanishes and reappears within 5 feet of a creature within 120 feet and makes a bite attack.
*   **Cast a Spell (2 Actions)**: Thanathel casts *Finger of Death* or *Power Word Kill* (1/day).

---

#### **Mythic Actions**
If Thanathel's Mythic trait is active, she can use the options below as legendary actions.
*   **Call of Death (2 Actions)**: Thanathel empowers herself. For 1 minute, all necrotic damage she deals reduces the target's hit point maximum by an equal amount.
*   **Swift Strikes**: Thanathel makes two attacks: one with her bite and one with her tail.
