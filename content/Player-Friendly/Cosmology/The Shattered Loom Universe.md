In the Shattered Loom, the multiverse was once a single, harmonious creation spun by the goddess Telara, known as the Loom Weaver. Telara's great loom was shattered, scattering the planes into distinct but interconnected realms. The remnants of her loom now form the Spindles of Existence, which anchor this multiverse.
Core Themes

**Fragmentation and Connection**: While the planes are distinct, there are hidden paths and secret threads that link them, representing the lingering effects of Telara's followers to weave the universe back together.
**Tension Between Law and Chaos**: The multiverse is caught in a perpetual struggle between forces seeking to restore unity (Law) and those embracing the freedom of its shattered state (Chaos).
**Planar Scars**: Evidence of the Loom’s shattering exists throughout the planes, from unstable rifts to strange hybrid realms.

# **Unique Features of the Universe**

* Telara’s Loom Fragments:

Mystical artifacts scattered throughout the planes,  the loom holds immense power. Collecting these could allow mortals to influence the structure of the multiverse.

* The Riftborn:

A unique race of planar beings born from the scars of the Loom’s destruction. They are nomadic wanderers who can traverse planar boundaries naturally.

* Planar Knots:

Rare zones where multiple planes overlap, creating unique and unstable environments (e.g., a jungle with fiery rivers and airless voids).

# **Overarching Adventure**

**Levels 5-20 Campaign**This campaign intricately weaves the **Silent Spindle** into the story as a central antagonist and thematic reflection of Telara. The players will navigate the complex relationship between the **Loom of Creation**, the **Great Dreamer and Pale Night**, and the **Silent Spindle**, whose motivations, schemes, and existential philosophy challenge their understanding of the multiverse. By the campaign’s end, the party must make a climactic decision about whether to restore the Loom, destroy the Spindle, or embrace a chaotic new reality.

---

## **Campaign Themes**

1. **Betrayal and Legacy**: The Silent Spindle was once Telara’s greatest creation and closest ally, but its betrayal shattered the multiverse. The campaign explores themes of redemption, purpose, and the costs of divine ambition.
2. **Creation vs. Destruction**: The party grapples with whether the multiverse needs the Loom’s restoration, the Spindle’s unmaking, or an entirely new paradigm.
3. **Sanity and the Unknown**: With the **Great Dreamer** stirring and the **Far Realm** encroaching, the party must face increasingly surreal and horrifying truths about reality.
