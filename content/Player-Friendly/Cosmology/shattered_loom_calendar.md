# The Shattered Loom: Custom Calendar System

Welcome to the official calendar of **The Shattered Loom** (also known as the *Tapestry of Fate*). This system tracks time across a reality bound by three static moons, where the planes of existence hold fixed positions around the celestial spheres.

---

## 🌙 The Celestial Anchor (The Moons)
Unlike dynamic worlds, time and tide here are not dictated by moving celestial bodies. **Three stationary moons** anchor the night sky permanently. 
* **The Crimson Eye (The Red Moon):** Represents the static plane of physical matter and blood.
* **The Silver Loom:** Represents the plane of magic and thread.
* **The Void Sphere:** Represents the plane of shadow and transition.

Because each plane of existence sits in a static position around these moons, seasons change as the world vibrates or shifts its energy matrix, matching the four familiar cycles of Earth.

---

## 📅 System Overview

* **Days in a Week:** 10 Days
* **Months in a Year:** 12 Months
* **Days per Month:** 32 Days (Standard)
* **Total Days in a Standard Year:** 384 Days
* **Leap Year Cycle:** Every 4 years, the 12th month receives **1 extra day** (33 days total) to realign the structural boundaries of the planes.

## 🍂 The 12 Months & Seasons
Each month spans exactly 32 days, categorized cleanly across the four primary seasonal shifts.

### 🌸 The Season of Rebirth (Spring)
1. **Prime-Weave (Month 1):** 32 Days. The thawing of the threads; new life begins.
2. **Verdant-Rise (Month 2):** 32 Days. Rapid growth across the earthly plane.
3. **Bloom-Anchor (Month 3):** 32 Days. The seasonal peak where nature stabilizes.

### ☀️ The Season of High Zenith (Summer)
4. **Solar-Thread (Month 4):** 32 Days. Longest days, matching high energy from the planes.
5. **Crimson-Heat (Month 5):** 32 Days. The Red Moon glows brightest against the summer dust.
6. **Harvest-Knot (Month 6):** 32 Days. The transition from growth to collection.

### 🍁 The Season of Unraveling (Autumn)
7. **Rust-Shear (Month 7):** 32 Days. The falling away of unnecessary growth.
8. **Fade-Loom (Month 8):** 32 Days. Twilight lingers longer; magic thins out.
9. **Gloom-Anchor (Month 9):** 32 Days. Preparation for structural stillness.

### ❄️ The Season of Stasis (Winter)
10. **Frost-Braid (Month 10):** 32 Days. The absolute freezing of physical planes.
11. **Void-Chill (Month 11):** 32 Days. The quietest month of deep spiritual reflection.
12. **The Shattered Loom (Month 12):** 32 / 33 Days. The final month of execution. *During Leap Years, a 33rd day known as **The Intercalary Thread** is woven at the very end to reset the cycle.*
