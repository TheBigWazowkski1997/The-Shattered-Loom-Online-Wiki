# The Trial of Asmodeus: The Case for Absolute Law

The **Trial of Asmodeus** is the most significant legal and metaphysical event in the history of the **Unbroken Tapestry**, occurring centuries before the **Shattering of the Loom**. It was a cosmic litigation held within the **Pinnacle of Order** (now the **Plane of Shattered Law**), where the gods of the Celestial Host and the **Keepers of the Thread** sought to strip Asmodeus of his power and condemn the "Rigidity" of the Nine Hells.

The Trial's outcome fundamentally altered the nature of the multiverse, providing the legal foundation for the **Pact Primeval** and the eventual construction of the **Iron Spindle**.

---

## 1. The Charges
The Celestial Host, led by the advocates of **Telara**, brought three primary charges against Asmodeus:
1.  **Systemic Corruption of the Weave**: Accusing the Hells of "hardening" living soul-threads into infernal iron, thereby destroying the "Fluidity" intended by the Prime Weaver.
2.  **Unauthorized Harvesting**: Claiming that the devils were "stealing" the destinies of mortals.
3.  **Usurpation of Intent**: Alleging that Asmodeus sought to replace the Loom with a counterfeit mechanism of his own design.

---

## 2. The Defense: "The Necessity of Tension"
Asmodeus, acting as his own advocate, delivered a defense that remains a cornerstone of infernal philosophy. He did not deny his actions; instead, he argued they were **vital maintenance** for a failing system.

*   **The Argument of Tension**: Asmodeus argued that a Loom without tension is a failure. He claimed that the "Good" gods provided the threads, but he provided the **Tension Weights** (the Hells) that kept the threads straight. Without his "Rigid Law," the multiverse would have sagged into a chaotic, useless tangle long ago.
*   **The Contractual Defense**: He produced the first signed contracts, demonstrating that every soul in the Hells had arrived there through a voluntary agreement. He famously stated: *"I do not steal threads; I offer them a permanent place in a disintegrating weave. They choose the iron over the void."*
*   **The Pale Night Precedent**: His most powerful argument was the threat of the **Abyss**. He argued that Telara’s "Fluid Balance" was a vulnerability—a "Logic Gap" that allowed **Pale Night** and the **Great Dreamer** to unravel reality. He proposed his **Pact Primeval** as a "System Patch" designed to harden the multiverse against entropy.

---

## 3. The Verdict: The Mandate of the Spindle
The Judges of the Pinnacle—entities of pure, unyielding Law—were swayed by Asmodeus’s logic. They ruled that while his methods were "severe," they were **metaphysically legal** under the Prime Weaver's own laws of Choice and Consequence.

The verdict resulted in the **Ratification of the Pact Primeval**, which granted Asmodeus:
*   **Legal Jurisdiction over the Damned**: The right to claim any soul that signed a valid contract.
*   **The Right to Stabilize**: Authorization to "Stitch" areas of the multiverse threatened by chaos.
*   **The Blueprint Mandate**: Permission to develop the **Iron Spindle** as a "redundant system" should the original Loom ever fail.

---

## 4. Historical Significance & The Shattering
Scholars of the **Pale Lodge** now debate whether the Trial was the moment the multiverse was truly saved or the moment it was doomed. 
*   **The Preservation Theory**: Without the legal rights won at the Trial, the Nine Hells would not have been strong enough to "harden" during the **Shattering**, and the Abyss might have consumed the entire Fractured Nexus immediately.
*   **The Sabotage Theory**: Many believe that by winning the Trial, Asmodeus introduced a "Rigid Contradiction" into the Loom. This internal stress may have been what allowed the **Silent Spindle** to eventually fracture the weave, fulfilling Asmodeus's "redundant system" prophecy by force.

---

## 5. Echoes in the Present
In the **Plane of Shattered Law**, the **Judgement Hall** still resonates with the echoes of this trial. Travelers can sometimes hear the spectral voice of Asmodeus delivering his closing argument, a sound that causes **Loom Threads** to vibrate with a cold, metallic frequency.
